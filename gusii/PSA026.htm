Ogosaba kw’omonto omuya
(Ogoteera gwa Daudi)
Ⅰ Aye Omonene, ing’a oboronge bwane;
ekiagera nabeire ntari na komocha;
naboigo ngosemeretie aye Omonene ntari ne chitang’u‐tang’u.
Ⅱ Omonene, inteeme na ondenge;
orenge enkoro yane na ebirengererio biane.
Ⅲ Nigo ndigereretie obwanchani bwao bogendererete botambe
na gotaara ase obwegenwa bwao.
 
Ⅳ Tindi gosanga nabanyaborimo,
gose tindi kogosoria nabang’ainereria.
Ⅴ Inche nigo ngechete gosanga nabaanto bagokora amabe,
na tingwancha goikaransa amo nabaanto abakayayu.
 
Ⅵ Nigo ngoisaba amaboko aane ase okoorokia ng’a tindi na komocha,
erio naetanana egesasiimero kiao, aye Omonene,
Ⅶ imba ngoteera neriogi rinene gokoiraneria ng’a mbuya mono
na koraria ogokora kwao kw’ogokumia.
Ⅷ Aye Omonene, inche nigo nyanchete obomenyo bwao,
aase obonene bwao bore.
Ⅸ Tosiria omoyo one ekero ogosiria abanyabibe;
gose obogima bwane ekero ogosiria abaiti.
Ⅹ Abanto abwo bagokora emeroberio y’obonyaka,
na amaboko abo a borio nigo aichire namaaki.
 
Ⅺ Inche nigo inde norogendo rotari na komocha;
indorere amaabera na omboorie.
Ⅻ Amagoro aane nigo agotenena aase ateremu;
nintogie Omonene ase egati y’omosangererekano omonene.
