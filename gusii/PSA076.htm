Nyasae ore omobui
(Ogoteera gw’Asafu)
Ⅰ Nyasae nigo amanyekanete ase Abayuda,
erieta riaye rire rinene mono ase Abaisraeli.
Ⅱ Obomenyo bwaye Obochenu nigo bore agwo Salemu,
ere nigo amenyete agwo Sayoni.
Ⅲ Aroro agwo nabunete chinsara chi’amata chikomesa,
boigo naende akabutorana chinguba, na emioro na ebirwanero binde bi’esegi.
 
Ⅳ Aye nigo okomeka‐meka oichire
nobonene kogocha korwa ase ebitunwa biababisa kwabuire.
Ⅴ Abare ne chinkoro chimwenu baurirwe ebinto konya basakoire,
bono bararire chitoro chindito.
Abarwani esegi bonsi basinyirwe korwana namaboko abo.
Ⅵ Aye Nyasae o Yakobo, ekero gwatogonyerete abanto bariinete chibarasi,
barabwo nigo bagwete inse, bakaba buna nigo bakwete, amo ne chibarasi chiabo.
 
Ⅶ Aye bweka nigo ore oyo bw’okoondokia mono.
Ning’o oranyare gotenena ase obosio bwao,
ekero kwabeire nendamwamu?
Ⅷ Korwa agwo igoro, nigo kwamanyetie ense ebiina biao;
na ense ekoboa egakira‐kiri,
Ⅸ ekero aye Nyasae gwatenenete konacha ekiina
na kobatooria abwo bonsi bagokorerwa bobe.
Ⅹ Ekeene, endamwamu ya Mwanyabaanto nenyare kogera aye otogigwe;
eyeratigare noyieboyie buna orokini.
 
Ⅺ Inwe karie chiira ase Omonene, Nyasae oino, na mochiikeranie.
Abanto bonsi bamoetanaine, tiga bamorentere ebiegwa,
ere oria ogwenerete koirokwa.
Ⅻ Ere nigo agokeyia abare abaenenu ase ense,
abarwoti bonsi b’ense nigo bamwoboete.
