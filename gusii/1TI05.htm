Buna togwenerete gokorera abaande
Ⅰ Totogonyera omogaka, korende omosorore buna iso, abamura abake buna bamura bamino, Ⅱ abakungu abagotu buna abaanyoko, na abakungu abasubati buna basubati bamino, ase obochenu bwonsi.
Ⅲ Osike abatakanwa abare abataakanwa bori. Ⅳ Korende onye omotakaanwa ore nabana gose abaachokoro, tiga beorokererie ritang’ani ayabagwenerete bakorere abamwabo abanyene, na koiraneria abaibori babo ng’a mbuya mono ase ebi banyorire, ekiagera ring’ana eri ndiancheire ase obosio bwa Nyasae. Ⅴ Oyore omotaakanwa bori, na otigire bweka, oyo nigo amosemeretie Nyasae, na kogeenderera ase okoboria na ogosaba obotuko na omobaso. Ⅵ Korende ere, oy’otari gwetanga ase okogania kwaye, okuure nonya ore moyo. Ⅶ Obachiike ayio, erinde babe batari na komocha. Ⅷ Onye omonto takoreenda abare abamwabo, mono baria be enyomba yaye, okaanire okwegena, naende nomobe kobua oy’otegeneti.
Ⅸ Omotakaanwa anyare koriikwa onye okoorire emiaka emerongo etano nomo, naende onye orenge omokungu bw’omosaacha oyoomo. Ⅹ Nemogwenerete gwatorwa ng’a nare nogokora okuya, buna oyo okinirie abana, oroisirie abageni, osibirie abachenu amagoro, okoonyire abachaandegete, na bweruete omonyene ase kera ogokora okuya. Ⅺ Korende toriika abatakanwa batari nemiaka emenge, ekiagera ekero baranyore ebirengererio ebire ao na Kristo, nigo baraganie konyuomwa. Ⅻ Barabwo nabagaambigwe, ekiagera baatigire eira yabo ye ritang’ani. ⅩⅢ Amo nayio beorokereirie koba aboro, bagoetanana enyomba ase enyomba; tibari aboro rioka, korende nabatora‐toria, bakwerosia namang’ana y’abanto bande, bagokwana amang’ana atagwenereti. ⅩⅣ Ase igo, nanchire abatakanwa batari nemiaka emenge banyuomwe, baibore abana, barende chiinka chiabo, na tibaa moengereria onde ribaga ri’ogokwana bobe igoro ase tore. ⅩⅤ Inaki abaande bairanire magega, na gotunyana Saitani. ⅩⅥ Onye omosacha gose omokungu, oyobwegenete, ore nabatakaanwa bamwabo, tiga abakoonye, ekanisa terarerigwa, erinde ebakonye baria bare abatakanwa bori.
ⅩⅦ Abagaaka abakogaamba buya baabarerwe ng’a mbagwenerete amasikani ara kabere, kobua mono abwo bagokora emeremo y’okorandia na kworokereria. ⅩⅧ 5:18: a Okoir 25:4; b Mat 10:10; Luk 10:7Inaki amariiko nigo agoteeba, “Tobaisa kobekera entang’ana ekeokio ekero ekoora endaagera”, na “Omokori emeremo nagwenerete koegwa eng’eria yaye.” ⅩⅨ 5:19: Okoir 17:6; 19:15Tibwancha ogosoerwa igoro y’omogaka otatiga, ase amang’ana y’abarori babere gose batato. ⅩⅩ Abwo bagokora ebibe, obatogonyere ase amasio y’abanto bonsi, erinde abaande bairoke. ⅩⅪ Nagochiikire ase obosio bwa Nyasae, na obwa Kristo Yeso, na obw’abamalaika abachoore, obwate amang’ana ayio, na tonacha ring’ana ritang’ani otaramanya buna rire; tokora ing’ana rinde rionsi ase ogwanchereria. ⅩⅫ Tobekera omonto amaboko bwango, gose tosanga nebibe bi’abaanto bande. Bwerende aye omonyene, obe omochenu.
ⅩⅩⅢ Korwa bono tonywa amaache oka, korende onywe edivai nke, ase engencho y’enda yao na amarwaire agokonyora ekero ase ekero.
ⅩⅩⅣ Ebibe bi’abaanto baande nigo bire maiso marore, biatang’anire gochia ase ekiina, korende ebi’abaande nigo bikobatunyana korwa magega. ⅩⅩⅤ Naboigo, ogokora okuya nigo kore maiso marore, na koria gotari maiso marore tigokogenderera kobisekana.
