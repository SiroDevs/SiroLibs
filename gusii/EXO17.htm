Amache akorwa egetare
(Omob 20:1-13)
Ⅰ 17:1: Omob 20:2-13Omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli okaimoka korwa erooro ya Sinai. Barabwo bakabwatia orogendo rwabo, bakana‐gotoora buna Omonene abachiigete, Bagaika Refidimu, bagatoora aroro, korende mache tarengeo abanto baare konyara konywa. Ⅱ Erio abanto bakomania Musa, bagoteeba, “Toe amaache tonywe!”
Musa akabateebia, “Nase ki mokonyomania? Nase ki mogoteema Omonene?.”
Ⅲ Agwo bakaba nerang’o enene y’amaache, bakemurunganeria Musa, bakaboora, “Ninki gwatoruseria Misiri gocha aa, amo nabana baito ne chitugo chiaito, togokwa ase erang’o?”
Ⅳ Musa akarera ase Omonene, akabora, “Ninki ngokorera abanto aba? Barabwo nigo bare ang’e kong’aka amagena.”
Ⅴ Omonene akairaneria Musa, “Oete bosio bwa abanto aba, oire abagaaka bande b’Abaisraeli, oire boigo nenyimbo yao, eria kwaagete orooche, motang’ane abanto mogende. Ⅵ Nondore inteneine agwo ase egetare kia Horebu, nkoganyete. Oake egetare, na amaache arwe aroro, erinde abanto banyore banywe.” Musa agakora bo ase amasio ’abagaaka b’Abaisraeli.
Ⅶ Akaroka aase aria Masa*17:7 Masa: Masa engencho yaye: ogoteema na Meriba,†17:7 Meriba: Meriba engencho yaye: okwomana ase engencho y’eriomana ri’Abaisraeli, naende ekiagera abanto bamoteemete Omonene, bakoboora, “Inee! Omonene nare aa ase egati yaito, gose taiyo?”
Esegi n’Abaameleki
Ⅷ Erio Abaameleki bakaoroka na korwania Abaisraeli aaria Refidimu. Ⅸ Erio Musa agateebia Yoshua, “Tochorere abanto bagende korwana n’Abaameleki ankio; inche ninche ’ntenene ekegoro igoro, na enyimbo ya Nyasae in’yebwate koboko kwane.” Ⅹ Yoshua agakora buna Musa amotebetie, akarwania Abaameleki, na Musa, na Aroni, na Huri bagatiira gochia ekegoro igoro. Ⅺ Ekaba Musa kaimokirie amaboko aye igoro, rirorio Abaisraeli babua, na kayairanirie inse, Abaameleki babua. Ⅻ Ekero amaboko a Musa arosire, Aroni na Huri bakaimokia rigena, bakaritereka inse, na Musa akariikaransera. Erio bakaba bagosiira amaboko a Musa, oyomo okoboko gwo korio, na oyonde ogwokobee. Naboigo bakamosiira goika erioba rikagwa. ⅩⅢ Na igo Yoshua akabua Abaameleki kegima, akabaita nemioro.
ⅩⅣ 17:14: Okoir 25:17-19; 1 Sam 15:2-9Omonene agateebia Musa, “Yarike amang’ana aya ase egetabu, abe amang’ana ’okoinyorwa, oteebie Yoshua ng’a ninsirie Abaameleki erio bebwe kegima, tibaabaisa koinyorwa inse y’erioba.” ⅩⅤ Musa akaagacha egesasiimero agwo, agakeroka “Yahwe Nisi”, engencho yaye, “Omonene nebendera yane y’esegi”, ⅩⅥ agateeba, “Ninyimokie okoboko kwane igoro, kobe ekemanyererio ki’Omonene, na gotiana ng’a Omonene nigo aragenderere korwania Abaameleki ebiaare ase ebiaare.”
