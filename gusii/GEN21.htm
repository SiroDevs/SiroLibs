Okoiborwa gwa Isaka
Ⅰ Omonene akamotaarera Sara, akamokorera buna konya amoriereire eira. Ⅱ 21:2: Abaibr 11:11Erio Sara akaba morito, akaiborera Aburaamu omwana omoisia ase obogotu bwaye, ase engaki eria Nyasae amotebeetie. Ⅲ Omwana oyio Sara aiborete, Aburaamu ise akamoroka Isaka. Ⅳ 21:4: Omoch 17:12; Ogok 7:8Ekero Isaka akoorire amatuko atano na atato, Aburaamu akamwarokia, buna Nyasae konya amochikiire. Ⅴ Aburaamu nigo areenge nemiaka rigana erimo ekero Isaka, omwana oye, aiboretwe. Ⅵ Sara akabora: “Nyasae onkoreire amaseko, na onde bwensi orayaigwe nansegisie.” Ⅶ Naende agateeba: “Ning’o nanga ore komoteebia Aburaamu ng’a Sara nagonkie abana? Na bono namoiboreire omwana ase obogotu bwaye.”
Ⅷ Omwana oria agakina, agatiga okogonka. Ase rituko riria Isaka atigete kogonka, Aburaamu agakora omoyega omonene.
Ogoserigwa gwa Isumaeli na Hagari
Ⅸ Korende Sara akarora omwana oria o Hagari Omomisiri, oyio aiborerete Aburaamu, ogochecheria Isaka, omwana oye. Ⅹ 21:10: Abagl 4:29,30Ase ayio agateebia Aburaamu: “Moserie omokungu oyo omosomba amo nomwana oye, ekiagera omwana bw’omokuungu oyo omosomba takoria omwando amo nomwana one Isaka.” Ⅺ Ring’ana erio tiriamogwenera Aburaamu nonya ng’ake, ase engencho y’omwana oye. Ⅻ 21:12: Abar 9:7; Abaibr 11:18Korende Nyasae agateebia Aburaamu: “Ring’ana eri tiriba riibe asore, ase engencho y’omwana oyo, na ase engencho y’omokuungu oyo, omosomba oo. Rinde rionsi Sara akogoteebia, riigwere, ekiagera korwa ase Isaka ororeeria rwao narwatorwe. ⅩⅢ Naboigo, omwana bw’omokuungu oyo omosomba nimokore abe egesaku ekenene, ekiagera nere oyio nomwana oo.”
ⅩⅣ Erio Aburaamu akaimoka mambia chuni, akaimokia omogati na ebirandi biroiseirie amasankwa, bire n’amaache, akabiaa Hagari, akamobibekera mareko. Akamorentera na omwana, akamoteebia agende amo nere. Akagenda, akana‐koong’anya ase erooro ya Beeri‐Seba.
ⅩⅤ Ekero amaache aria areenge ebirandi ime aerire, akaareria omwana inse y’egetutu. ⅩⅥ Erio akagenda, agaikaransa are ake, ase oboare bw’okoruta ensara, ekiagera nigo atebeete: “Tinkonyara korigereria omwana one ekero ogokwa.” Ekero aikaransete omorigereretie, agaaka eriogi riaye, akareera.
ⅩⅦ Nyasae akaigwa okoreera kw’omwana, na omomalaika o Nyasae akarangeria Hagari korwa igoro, akamoteebia: “Hagari, inki okoreera? Tibwoboa, ekiagera Nyasae oigure okoreera kw’omwana agwo ase araire. ⅩⅧ Tenena, oimokie omwana, omobwate koboko kwao; naki nimokore abe egesaku ekenene.” ⅩⅨ Erio Nyasae akaramokia Hagari amaiso, na ere akarora ensoko y’amaache, akagenda, akabuucha, agaichoria ekerandi amaache, akaa omwana oria erio akanywa. ⅩⅩ Nyasae akaba amo nomwana oria. Ere akamenya ase erooro, akaba omomanyi bw’okorasa nemegwe. ⅩⅪ Akamenya ase erooro ya Parani, na ng’ina akamonyuomeria omokungu korwa ase ense ya Misiri.
Aburaamu na Abimeleki bagokora okobwatana
ⅩⅫ 21:22: Omoch 26:26Ase engaki eyio Abimeleki na Pikoli, omochiki omonene bw’abarwani baye, bagateebia Aburaamu: “Nyasae nare amo naye ase ande onsi ogokora. ⅩⅩⅢ Ase ayio, bono karie eira na gotiana ase Nyasae ng’a togonkorera obong’ainereria inche, na abana baane, na abachokoro baane, korende koreng’ana buna inche nagokorerete aye, obwanchani obo naye onkorere nainche boigo, amo nense eye aye omenyete ime.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Aburaamu agateeba: “Ee, ninkoriere eira.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Aburaamu akamwemurunganeria Abimeleki ase engencho y’ensoko eria abasomba b’Abimeleki bamourete. ⅩⅩⅥ Abimeleki akamoteebia: “Inche timanyeti ning’o okorete ring’ana erio, gose tikwana gonteebia; nonya nokoigwa, tinana koyaigwa otatiga reero rioka.” ⅩⅩⅦ Erio Aburaamu akaimokia etugo yaye, ching’ondi ne chiombe, akaa Abimeleki, na barabwo babere bagakora okobwatana gati yabo. ⅩⅩⅧ Aburaamu agaatanana chimanwa chisigiti isano na ibere, agachibeeka ensemo. ⅩⅩⅨ Abimeleki akabooria Aburaamu: “Inee, chimanwa echi chisigiti isano na ibere gwatanana, engencho yaye inki?”
ⅩⅩⅩ Aburaamu akamoiraneria: “Chimanwa echi chisigiti isano na ibere nigo nagochieire korwa ase okoboko kwane, erinde chibe kirori ase ’nde, ng’a ensoko eye ninche nayeremete.” ⅩⅩⅪ Ase ayio bakaaroka ase agwo Beeri‐Seba*21:31 Beeri‐Seba: engencho yaye: chinsoko isano na ibere, gose: ensoko y’emuma, ekiagera barabwo babere mbariereranete emuma aroro.
ⅩⅩⅫ Ekero bakorire okobwatana aria Beeri‐Seba, Abimeleki na Pikoli, oria omochiki omonene bw’abarwani baye, bakaimoka korwa agwo, bakairana ase ense y’Abafilisti. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Aburaamu agasimeka omote onga buna ekerachuoki aria Beeri‐Seba, na agwo akarangeria erieta ria Nyasae Omonene o kare na kare ase okomosasiima. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Aburaamu akamenya buna omomenyi ase ense y’Abafilisti amatuko amange.
