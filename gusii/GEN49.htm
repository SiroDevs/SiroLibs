Yakobo ogosesenia abana baye
Ⅰ Erio Yakobo akarangeria abamura baye, akabateebia: “Sangererekana aa ase ’nde, mbateebie amang’ana arabanyore ase amatuko agochi‐gocha.
Ⅱ “Inchuo montegerere, inwe abana ba Yakobo; montegerere inche Israeli iso.
Ⅲ “Rubeni, aye nomwana one omotangi,
aye nokobua kwane, na ogotongora gwe chinguru chiane.
Noetanetie abamino ase okobua,
nobaetanetie ase chinguru.
Ⅳ Aye nkwaberete buna amaache;
korende togoetania abande,
ekiagera nkwarinete borere bwa iso, okabosaria, ase igo okarenta obosooku.
 
Ⅴ “Simioni na Lawi naba ng’ina omo,
ebirwanero biabo nebi’obotindi.
Ⅵ Omoyo one tosoa ase obobisi bwabo,
gose enkoro yane tesanga ase omosangererekano obo;
naki ase endamwamu yabo mbaitete abanto,
na ase obomwenu bwabo bakanacha chinteng’e chie chintang’ana.
Ⅶ Endamwamu yabo eragererigwe, ekiagera nigo yareenge entindi,
na ekeririanda kiabo, ekiagera tikiareenge namaabera.
Nimbaatanane ase abana ba Yakobo,
nimbasiarere ase abana ba Israeli.
 
Ⅷ “Aye Yuda, abamino inabagotogie;
okoboko kwao inakobwate ebigoti biababisa bao;
abamura ba iso ng’a batugame bosio bwao na amasikani.
Ⅸ 49:9: Omob 24:9; Okom 5:5Yuda, aye nendo ensae.
Omwana one, gokorwa gotwara, nigo oratiire,
na korara, na gotimoka buna endo,
buna endo eaberi, ning’o orabe noboremu kogotura?
Ⅹ Enyimbo y’obogaambi tekorua o Yuda,
gose omobeeki amachiiko ase ororeeria rwaye,
goika achiche oyo orabogorie obogaambi,
oyo abanto b’ebisaku bionsi baraigwere.
Ⅺ Nigo arasibe etigere yaye ase omote bw’omosabibu,
na emori y’etigere yaye ase chinsagia chi’omosabibu omuya.
Nigo arasibie egetambaa kiaye akwambara nedivai,
ne chianga chiaye n’amaache amonyinga y’amasabibu
Ⅻ Amaiso aye nigo arabe amabariri ase okonywa edivai,
na amaino aye abe amarabu ase okonywa amabeere.*49:12 okonywa amabeere: engencho yaye, nigo babwate obotenenku bw’endagera
 
ⅩⅢ “Sabuloni nigo aramenye ase enyancha mbarabare,
agwo ase chimeeri chigotenenigwa,
orobebe rwaye rosinyane norw’Abasidoni.
 
ⅩⅣ “Isakaru netigere ere ne chinguru, ekorara obweri bwe chitugo.
ⅩⅤ Akarora ng’a aase agotimokera naagiya,
na ense eria nero ekomogwenera.
Ase igo, agekumba abogorie egurube,
akerua kogenderera nemeremo emekong’u.
 
ⅩⅥ “Dani nagambere abanto baye,
buna egesaku ekemo ki’Abaisraeli.
ⅩⅦ Dani nabe eng’iti ekweriba inchera,
ring’ere ase oboeto,
rikoroma egetingiro ki’ebarasi,
goika oyoyeriinete agwe masogota.
ⅩⅧ Aye Omonene, ninganyete ogotooreka kwao nogosemeria!
 
ⅩⅨ “Gadi namigwe nemeganda y’abauri,
korende nere nabamige korwa magega.
 
ⅩⅩ “Aseri natware endagera y’obonoru,
narue endagera engiya eragwenere abarwoti.
 
ⅩⅪ “Nafutali nengabi esiboire ekominyoka mono,
nigo ekobiara chimanwa chingiya.
 
ⅩⅫ “Yusufu nomote omorere okwama ang’e nensoko;
chinsagia chiaye nigo chikoranda enyasi igoro.
ⅩⅩⅢ Abaruti emegwe bakomoimokera ase okomogwera n’bororo,
bakomoruta emegwe na komoorosia,
ⅩⅩⅣ korende obota bwaye tibogosukigwa.
Amaboko aye nigo are ne chinguru, naende namaango,
goetera ase amaboko ’Omonyene Chinguru o Yakobo,
oyore Omorisia na Egetare gia Israeli.
ⅩⅩⅤ Nyasae o iso, nere oragokonye,
Nyasae Omobui, nere oragosesenie
nogosesenigwa gokorwa igoro,
na ogosesenigwa gokorwa chindiba inse,
na ogosesenigwa gokorwa inda na ase chimbeere.
ⅩⅩⅥ Ogosesenigwa gwa iso nkore ne chinguru
ngoetanetie okw’ebitunwa bia kare,
na obonene bw’ebigoro bia kare na kare.
Ogosesenigwa okwo kobe egutwa ase omotwe o Yusufu,
igoro y’egiototeri ki’oyore omogambi ase egati y’abamwabo.
 
ⅩⅩⅦ “Benjamini n’eseese y’orosana ekoria mono,
engaki ya mambia yaria ebi’obotwari bwaye,
na engaki ya mogoroba bwatanana ebiasakoire.”
ⅩⅩⅧ Ebi nabio ebisaku bia Israeli ikomi na bibere; naya naro ise abateebetie ekero are kobasesenia na koa kera oyomo ogosesenigwa kwaye.
Ogokwa kwa Yakobo
ⅩⅩⅨ Erio Israeli akabachiika, akabora: “Inche nkoirwa inde gosangererekana amo nabaanto baane. Montindeke ase chisokoro chiane chiatindegetwe ase rikuruma ri’oboremo bware bw’Efuroni Omohiti, ⅩⅩⅩ 49:30: Omoch 23:3-20rikuruma eri rire ase oboremo agwo Makipela, moocha ya Mamure, ase ense ya Kaanani. Oboremo boria Aburaamu agorete korwa ase Efuroni Omohiti bobe aase ogotindeka. ⅩⅩⅪ 49:31: a Omoch 25:9,10; b Omoch 35:29Agwo nao Aburaamu atindegetwe ase Sara mokaye ensemo; agwo nao Isaka na Rebeka mokaye batindegetwe, na agwo inche omonyene natindegete Lea. ⅩⅩⅫ Oboremo obwo amo ne rikuruma riria nigo biagoretwe korwa ase Abahiti.” ⅩⅩⅩⅢ 49:33: Ogok 7:15Ekero Yakobo akoorete gochiika abamura baye amang’ana aya, akaimokia amagoro aye, akayabeeka borere igoro; erio agakwa, erio agasangererekana amo nabaanto baye.
