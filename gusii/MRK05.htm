Yeso okogwenia Omonto obwatire nebirecha
(Mat 8:28-34; Luk 8:26-39)
Ⅰ Bagaika ng’umbu y’enyancha, goika ense y’Abageraseni. Ⅱ Yeso agachia goika korwa obwato; agwo akaumerana nomonto okorwa ase chimbeera obwatire nekerecha ki’eubi. Ⅲ Obomenyo bwaye nigo bwarenge aria ase chimbeera. Monto onde tarenge ore konyara komosiba nonya nase ebinyuri, Ⅳ ekiagera nigo arenge konya osibirwe ara aange ne chingataare na ebinyuri, abibutora ebinyuri biria, na kobuna‐buna chingataare chiria, na onde tarenge ore ne chinguru chi’okomobua. Ⅴ Na botambe, botuko na mobaso, nao arenge ase chimbeera na ebitunwa, okona‐korera na gwekeba‐keba namagena. Ⅵ Agachia korora Yeso korwa are, akaminyoka chimbero, akamosasiima; Ⅶ agaaka eriogi, okobora, “Inaki inde naye aye Yeso, Omwana o Nyasae Ore Igoro Mono? Nagotianirie ase Nyasae ng’a tonchanda”, Ⅷ ekiagera Yeso konya ogetebirie, “Aye ekerecha ki’eubi, karwe ase omonto oyo!” Ⅸ Yeso akamobooria, “Erieta riao naki okorokwa?” Akairaneria, “Erieta riane n’Elegioni abasigari chilifu isano nemo na goetani erombe y’ abanto, ekiagera nabange tore.” Ⅹ Erio ere agasorora Yeso mono tabiira isiko y’ense eria. Ⅺ Aria ase egetunwa ndiarengeo riicho rinene rie chimbeche riare korisia. Ⅻ Ebirecha bionsi bikamosorora, bikaboora, “Toire ase chimbeche, tonyare gosoa ase chirochio.” ⅩⅢ Akabia ribaga. Ebirecha biria bi’eubi bigasoka, bigasoa ase chimbeche chiria; riicho riria rigatirimboka bwango gochia ase egeturunkano, goika chikagwa enyancha ime. Igo chiarenge koreng’ana chilifu ibere, chikarimera nyancha ime.
ⅩⅣ Abarisia bagatama, bakararia amang’ana aya ase omochie na ebinyoro. Abanto bagasooka barore amang’ana aorokire. ⅩⅤ Bagachiera Yeso, bakarora omonto oria obwatire nomoganda bw’ebirecha oikaransete, bweboyetie chianga, ore nebirengererio ebironge bakairoka. ⅩⅥ Na baria abayaroche bakaorokia amang’ana amonyorire oria obwatire nebirecha, na amang’ana ye chimbeche. ⅩⅦ Bagachaaka gosorora Yeso arue ase ense yabo. ⅩⅧ Ekero Yeso achia koriina obwato ime, oria obwatire nebirecha akamosorora ng’a abe amo nere. ⅩⅨ Yeso tamwanchera, korende akamoteebia, “Genda nka bwoo ase abamino, obateebie amang’ana amanene ay’Omonene agokoreire, naende buna akororeire amaabera.” ⅩⅩ Akagenda bwoye, agachaaka koraria ase Dekapoli amang’ana aya Yeso amokoreire. Abanto bonsi bagakumia.
Omoiseke o Yairo na omokungu ore noborwaire bw’okorua amanyinga bakogwenigwa
(Mat 9:18-26; Luk 8:40-56)
ⅩⅪ Yeso kambokire naende koirana ng’umbu nobwato boria, omoganda omonene okamosangererekanera; ere nigo arenge ensemo y’enyancha. ⅩⅫ Omonto oyomo bw’abanene b’esinagogi agacha, erieta riaye Yairo. Agachia komoorora, akagwa ase amagoro aye, ⅩⅩⅢ akamosorora mono, akabora, “Omonene, omoiseke one omoke oisire gokwa. Nagosabire, oche obeke okoboko kwao igoro yaye, anyare kogwena, abe moyo.” ⅩⅩⅣ Bono Yeso akaimoka akagenda amo nere.
Omoganda omonene okamotunyana, okamomiga‐miga korwa ase chinsemo chionsi. ⅩⅩⅤ Narengeo omokungu oyomo orenge noborwaire bw’okorua amanyinga ase engaki y’emiaka ikomi na ebere. ⅩⅩⅥ Ere nigo achandegete mono ase amaboko y’abarwaria abange. Ere konya osiareire ebinto bionsi arenge nabio, tagwenigwa nonya ng’ake, korende obogima bwaye bokamentekana koba obobe kobua. ⅩⅩⅦ Konya oigure amang’ana igoro ya Yeso, agaeta ase omoganda korwa magega, agakuna eanga yaye, ⅩⅩⅧ ekiagera nigo ateebete, “Kondakune chianga chiaye chioka, ningwene.” ⅩⅩⅨ Erio kegima ensoko y’amanyinga aye egakama: akaigwa omobere oye ng’a ogwenire korwa ase oborwaire bwaye. ⅩⅩⅩ Erio agwo Yeso akamanya ase omoyo oye ng’a chinguru chiarure asare, akeonchora gati ase omoganda, akabora, “Ning’o okuna chianga chiane?” ⅩⅩⅪ Aborokigwa baye bakamoteebia, “Norooche omoganda oyo buna okomigete, na aye gokoboria: Ning’o onkuna?” ⅩⅩⅫ Korende Yeso akarigereria chinsemo chionsi, erinde arore oria okora ring’ana erio. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Na omokungu oria agasogwa nomoondoko na okoiguswa, akamanya eriamonyorire, agacha akamogwera, akamoorokeria ekeene gionsi. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Yeso akamoteebia, “Omoiseke one, okwegena kwao gwakogwenirie, genda nomorembe, ogwene korwa ase oborwaire bwao.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Ekero Yeso agendererete gokwana, abanto korwa ase enyomba y’omotang’ani bw’esinagogi bagaacha, bakaboora, “Enyaroka yao yakuure. Inki okona‐koroseria Omworokia rende?” ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Korende Yeso tancha gotegerera ring’ana riria rigotebwa, agateebia omotang’ani oria bw’esinagogi, “Toiroka, egene rioka.” ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Gose tanchete monto onde amotuny’ane, otatiga Petero, na Yakobo, na Yohana momura omwabo Yakobo. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Bagaika enyomba y’omonene oria bw’esinagogi; akarora rirorooche, na abanto bakorera nokweumia okonge. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Agachia gosoa, akabateebia, “Inaki mogokora rirorooche na korera? Omwana takweti, korende nigo araire.” ⅩⅬ Bakamosekerera. Korende gaikire kobasokia isiko bonsi, akaira ise omwana, na ng’ina, na aborokigwa baye batato abwo barenge amo nere, agasoa ime ase omwana oria arenge. ⅩⅬⅠ Akamobwata okoboko omoiseke oria omoke, akamoteebia, “Talitha kumi”, okoonchorwa kwaye, “Tenena aye enyaroka.” ⅩⅬⅡ Erio agwo omoiseke oria omoke akaimoka akagenda, ere konya okoorire emiaka ikomi na ebere. Erio bagakumia ogokumia okonene. ⅩⅬⅢ Yeso akabakuurera ng’a monto onde tamanya amang’ana ayio, akabachiika bae omoiseke oria endagera arie.
