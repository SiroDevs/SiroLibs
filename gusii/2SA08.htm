Daudi okonyora obobui ase esegi
(1 Amg 18:1-17)
Ⅰ Magega y’ayio Daudi akarwania Abafirisiti, akababua, akabua nomochie obo omonene akayobeeka inse y’obogaambi bwaye.
Ⅱ Naende akabua Abamoabu. Akabachiika barare inse ase chindwaro. Erio akarengia*8:2 akarengia: engencho yaye agapima chindwaro ibere chi’abaanto, agachiika abwo baitwe bakwe, na abanto b’orwaro oromo agachiika batigwe babe moyo. Bono Abamoabu bakaba abasomba ba Daudi bakaba bakomorentera ebango.
Ⅲ Naende Daudi akabua Hadadeseri mosinto o Rehobu, omorwoti o Soba, ekero ere agendete kogareyia obogaambi bwaye gochia ase orooche rw’Eufarati. Ⅳ Daudi agoosa abariini chibarasi elifu eyemo, na amagana atan’abere (1,700), na abarwani abaare kogendera amagoro chilifu emerongo ebere (20,000). Erio akabutora chibarasi chinteng’e, korende agatiga chibarasi rigana erimo chibe chikong’usa chigari.
Ⅴ Na ekero Abasiiria ba Damesiki baachete gokoonya Hadadeseri, omorwoti o Soba, Daudi akabarwania, agaita abanto chilifu emerongo ebere na ibere (22,000). Ⅵ Erio akabeeka abarwani ase egati y’Abasiiria agwo Damesiki, na Abasiiria abwo bakaba abasomba ba Daudi bakaba bakomorentera ebango. Omonene akaa Daudi obobui aande onsi ase are kogeenda korwana. Ⅶ Daudi agasakora chinguba chi’echitaabu, echio abasomba ba Hadadeseri babogoretie, agachiira Yerusalemu. Ⅷ Naende omorwoti Daudi akaira chitaai chinyinge mono korwa Bera na Berotai, emechie Hadadeseri arenge kogaambera.
Ⅸ Toi, omorwoti o Hamati, agachia koigwa ng’a Daudi obuire abarwani bonsi ba Hadadeseri, Ⅹ agatoma Yoramu, momura oye, ase omorwoti Daudi amokwanie na komosesenia, ekiagera ere konya arwanirie Hadadeseri omobuire. Hadadeseri nigo abeete okona‐korwania Toi. Boigo Yoramu akairera Daudi ebinto bi’etaabu, na ebi’efeta, na ebi’etai. Ⅺ Ebinto ebio omorwoti Daudi akabiatananera Omonene, buna akorete ne chitaai ne chitaabu chiria arusetie korwa ase abanto b’ebisaku biria abuete: Ⅻ Abaedomu, na Abamoabu, na Abaamoni, na Abafirisiti, na Abaameleki. Boigo agaatananera Omonene ebisakorwa arusetie korwa ase Hadadeseri, mosinto o Rehobu, omorwoti o Soba.
ⅩⅢ 8:13: Zab 60Daudi akenyorera erieta rinene ekero arenge koirana, gaitire Abaedomu chilifu ikomi na isano’isato (18,000) ase Riranya ri’Omonyo. ⅩⅣ Daudi akabeeka abarwani aande onsi ase egati y’Abaedomu, na barabwo bonsi bakaba abasomba ba Daudi, n’Omonene akaa Daudi obobui aande onsi ase arenge kogeenda korwana.
ⅩⅤ Bono Daudi akaba okogaambera Abaisraeli bonsi, akaba ogokorera abanto baye bonsi oboronge na ekeene. ⅩⅥ Yoabu, omomura bw’omokuungu oria Seruya, nere orenge omochiki bw’emegaanda y’abarwani, na Yehosafati, mosinto bw’Ahiludi, nere orenge omoriki bw’amang’ana are gokoreka. ⅩⅦ Zadoki, mosinto bw’Ahitubu, na Ahimeleki, mosinto bw’Abiatari, nabwo barenge abakuani, na Seraya nere orenge omoriki; ⅩⅧ Benaya, mosinto o Yehoyada, nere orenge omoteneneri bw’Abakereti, na Abapeleti, na basinto ba Daudi nigo barenge Abakuani nabwo barenge abasemia b’oborwoti.
