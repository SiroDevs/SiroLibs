Omoyio omwoge Omonene
Ⅰ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: Ⅱ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, rigereria gochia Yerusalemu, orarie ring’ana rire mamincha y’Aase Aachenu, na obaane mamincha y’ense y’Abaisraeli. Ⅲ Oteebie abanto b’ense eyio ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Rora inche nigo inde mamincha yao, ninsomore omoyio one korwa ase ekeraaro, ninsirie korwa ase ore abanyaboronge amo nabakori amabe. Ⅳ Ekiagera ninsirie korwa ase ore abanyaboronge amo nabakori amabe, omoyio one nosomorwe korwa ase ekeraaro kiaye, oite abanto bonsi korwa Irianyi goika sugusu. Ⅴ Erio abanto bonsi mbamanye ng’a ninche Omonene nasomoire omoyio one korwa ase ekeraaro kiaye, tinkoyoirania ime naende.
Ⅵ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, ichaana, ee, ichaana obe buna oyo obunekire chibachwa, obe nobororo ase amasio y’ abanto abwo. Ⅶ Ekero barakoborie: Nase ki oichaanete iga? Obairanerie, obateebie: N’ase engencho y’amang’ana amabe agochia gocha. Ekero araache, kera omonto nairianyeke ase enkoro yaye, na amaboko y’ abanto bonsi naborwe chinguru; kera omonto nakwe omoyo, na amaru y’ abanto bonsi nororobe abe buna amaache. Naama! Amabe ayio ngocha are, goika aikeranigwe. Nabo Omonene ateebire.”
Ⅷ Naende ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: Ⅸ Aye Mwanyabaanto, bana oteebe ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Oteebe iga:
Omoyio otieriigwe, ee, otieriigwe na goseenigwa;
Ⅹ otieriigwe, erinde oite ogoita okonene,
oseeniigwe, erinde omese buna okomesa gw’enkoba.
Inee! Inatoyogokere?
Enyimbo y’omwana one nigo yachaayete kera omote.
Ⅺ Ase ayio omoyio oyio orurwe otieriigwe,
erinde obekwe ang’e okorerwe emeremo;
otieriigwe na goseenigwa, obekwe ase okoboko kw’omoiti.
Ⅻ Aye Mwanyabaanto, rera na goichaana!
Omoyio oyio orentirwe oite abanto bane,
oite na abagaambi Abaisraeli.
Barabwo barurwe baitwe nomoyio amo nabaanto bane.
Ase ayio ita egekoe.
ⅩⅢ Okorengwa nkoreo.
Inee! Naki gwachaayete enyimbo?
Tekonyara koba buya ase ore.
Nabo Omonene ateebire.
ⅩⅣ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, bana oake chinkobi. Tiga omoyio oyio ogochia goita omentwe chinguru ara kabere, nonya ng’ara gatato; oyio nomoyio bw’ogoita bw’oyio omonene, na omoyio oyio obaetananire ase chinsemo chionsi. ⅩⅤ Nabekire ensonga y’omoyio oyio agwo ase ebiita biabo bionsi, erinde chinkoro chiabo chirianyeke, na abange babo bagwe. Omoyio oyio otierigwe bwogee na komesa, osomoirwe ase ogoita. ⅩⅥ Ebeeke ang’e oetere, orenge ensemo ya borio na eya bomosi, aande onsi ase obwoge bochigete. ⅩⅦ Inche boigo ning’ake chinkobi, ing’isanekie ekeririanda kiane. Ninche Omonene nakwanire.”
Omoro bw’omorwoti o Babiloni
ⅩⅧ Naende ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: ⅩⅨ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, eroiserie chinchera ibere chikworokia ase omoro bw’omorwoti o Babiloni oraetere. Echio ibere nigo chirarwe ase ense eyemo. Naende otenenie ekemanyererio ase amatabekania y’omochie. ⅩⅩ Bworokie enchera ase omoro oraetere, eyemo gochia ase Raba ase ense y’Abaamoni, na eyende ase Yerusalemu, omochie ogiteire chindwaki, ase ense y’Abayuda. ⅩⅪ Omorwoti o Babiloni nigo ateneine ase amatabekania ye chinchera chiria ibere ogokora oboringoria; nigo agotierania chinsara, na okoboria korwa ase ebinyasae biaye, naende nigo akorigereria riini.*21:21korigereria riini: engencho y’Abaababeli y’okomanyera amabe na amaya ⅩⅫ Ase okoboko kwaye gw’okorio obomera nigo bokogwera Yerusalemu, erinde abeekeo ebikorero bi’ogotagorera, arere okorera kw’ogoita†21:22okorera kw’ogoita: engencho yaye ekeririanda na goaka eriogi ri’eseegi‡21:22 eriogi ri’eseegi: engencho yaye egeturi. Nigo arabeekeo ebikorero bi’ogotagorera ebiita, na koagacha chiburi ne chindwaki chi’ogwekengera. ⅩⅩⅢ Korende ase abanto ba Yerusalemu amang’ana ayio nigo ararorekane koba oboringoria bw’oborimo, ekiagera bariereranete eira y’ogotianania. Korende nigo aragere ebibe biabo biinyorwe, erinde babwatwe. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Ekiagera mwagerire ebibe biaino biainyoirwe, na ogosaria kwaino gwakorirwe kororekane maiso marore, na ase ayio onsi mwakorire, ebibe biaino biarorekanire, ee, ekiagera mwainyoirwe iga, inamobwatwe n’okoboko gwe chinguru.
ⅩⅩⅤ “Aye otachenegeti, aye omokori amabe ore omogambi bw’Abaisraeli, rituko ria’o ri’okonyora egesusuro ki’omoerio ki’ebibe biao riaikire. ⅩⅩⅥ Rusia egetambaa kia motwe amo n’eguutwa. Amang’ana takogenderera koba buna are bono. Ayio konya akeeigwe naimokererigwe igoro, na ayio konya aimokererigwe igoro nakeeigwe. Nabo Omonene agoteeba. ⅩⅩⅦ Ning’onchorane oborwoti obo, ee, nimboonchorane, nimboonchorane; tibobao nonya ng’ake goika oyio oborwoti obo bore obwaye achiche, oyio nere ndaboe.
Omoyio na Abaamoni
ⅩⅩⅧ 21:28: Yer 49:1-6; Ezek 25:1-7; Amos 1:3-15; Sef 2:8-11“Aye Mwanyabaanto, bana oteebe ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba igoro y’Abaamoni, na igoro y’okorama kwabo, oteebe:
“Omoyio, ee, omoyio osomoirwe ase ogoita,
otierigwe na gosenigwa,
nigo okomesa buna enkoba.
ⅩⅩⅨ “Nigo bakobarorera inwe amaroro y’oborimo, na kobaringoreria oborimo, bateeba ng’a monyare koba abamogotacha ebigoti bi’abakori amabe abwo batachenegeti, ekiagera rituko riabo riachire baraegwe egesusuro ki’omoerio ki’ebibe biabo.
ⅩⅩⅩ “Irania omoyio ase ekeraaro kiaye! Agwo ase ense mwatongeretwe, ense y’obobiare bwaino, nao ’ndabanachere ekiina. ⅩⅩⅪ Nimbaumorere ekeririanda kiane, na mbautere omorero bw’endamwamu y’ane; nimbabeeke ase amaboko y’ abanto abatindi, abamanyete goita na gosiria kegima. ⅩⅩⅫ Inwe mobe chinko chigouterwa omorero; amanyinga aino naiterwe igoro ase ense. Timokoinyorwa nonya ng’ake. Ninche Omonene nakwanire ayio.”
