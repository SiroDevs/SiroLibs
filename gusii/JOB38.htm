ⅩⅩⅩⅧ
Omonene okoiraneria Ayubu
Ⅰ Omonene akairaneria Ayubu korwa ase ekembugu‐mbugu ime, akamoteebia:
Ⅱ Aye naye ng’o okorenta omosunte ase ogosemia
ase ogokwana amang’ana atari ay’obong’aini?
Ⅲ Tenena okong’e buna omosacha,
inche ninkoborie, naye ong’iranerie.
Ⅳ Ng’ai kwarenge ekero nabegete oboroso bw’ense?
Ntebie onye aye kore omong’aini.
Ⅴ Ning’o obegete ebirengo biaye? Ntebie onye komanyete.
Gose ning’o oramborete engori y’okoyerenga?
Ⅵ Ng’ai amaroso aye aterekereretwe?
Gose ning’o obegete rigena riaye ri’ekoona,
Ⅶ ekero ching’enang’eni chia mambia chiaterete amo,
na abana bonsi ba Nyasae baagete eriogi ri’omogooko?
 
Ⅷ 38:8: Yer 5:22Ning’o osiegete ebisieri bi’enyancha, ekero yarindoretie buna
ndiria yarenge ime ase enda y’ense?
Ⅸ Ekero keria naroisetie amare abe chianga chiaye,
na nkayeboyia omosunte ekerima,
Ⅹ naende nkabeekera enyancha chimbebe chiaye,
boigo nkayebekera emekorogoto na ebisieri,
Ⅺ ngateba: Aa nao oraikere, korende togoetania,
naiga nao emerindo yao eimokererete igoro eratangerwe.
Ⅻ Inee! Ase obogima bwao bwonsi kwana gochika emambia ebeo?
Kwana kworokia amasase a mambia aase akomoreka,
ⅩⅢ erinde ense yonsi ekiamoke, ebwatwe ase chinsinyo chiaye,
na abakori amabe banyegerigwe barueo?
ⅩⅣ Ense nigo ekoonchorwa yarorekana buna amaroba abeire inse y’ebonyi*38:14 Ebonyi: engencho yaye n’ekemanyererio ere nekemanyererio,
na ebinto bionsi nigo bikororekana buna chianga chire n’ebieni ao ao.
ⅩⅤ Oborabu nigo bogotangwa korwa ase abakori amabe,
ne chinguru chiabo chi’okweenenia nigo chikobunwa chiasirigwa.
 
ⅩⅥ Inee! Kwana gosoa ime ase chinsoko chi’enyancha,
gose kwana gotaara ase enkoro ye chindiba?
ⅩⅦ Kwana kworokigwa ebiita bi’amakweri,
gose korora ebisieri bi’ekiriri ki’amakweri?
ⅩⅧ Kwana korengereria igoro y’obogare bw’ense?
Onye komanyete ayio onsi, yararie!
 
ⅩⅨ Enchera ekogenda ase oborabu bore, ng’ai ere?
Nomanyete ase omosunte omenyete,
ⅩⅩ erinde ogende oyoire ase ribebe riaye?
Naende nomanyete ebinchera bigochia ase enyomba yaye?
ⅩⅪ Ee, aye nomanyete, ekiagera nigo kwaiboretwe kare,
na emiaka yao nemenge.
 
ⅩⅫ Inee! Kwana gosoa ime ase ebigachero bi’obokeng’entu,
gose ebigachero bi’amagena ’embura?
ⅩⅩⅢ Amagena ayio ngachete goika ase engaki y’emechando,
amatuko aria arabe ay’esegi na okorwana konde.
ⅩⅩⅣ Inchera ki ekogenda aase oborabu bokoruerwa,
gose ekogenda aase omwaga okorwa moocha bwesiarera ense yonsi?
 
ⅩⅩⅤ Ning’o oremete omotaro ase chiooro chi’embura chigoeta,
gose enchera ase chinkoba chikogukura goeta,
ⅩⅩⅥ echio chikogera embura yatwa aase monto atamenyeti,
ase erooro monto ataiyo?
ⅩⅩⅦ Embura eyio nigo ekoigotia ense ere bosa etamenyiri,
yagera yamera obonyansi.
ⅩⅩⅧ Inee! Embura nebwate ise?
Gose ning’o okoibora amatoima e riime?
ⅩⅩⅨ Nkorwa ase enda ya ng’o obokeng’entu bokorwa?
Ning’o okoibora obokendu bokorwa igoro?
ⅩⅩⅩ Amache nigo akobwatana oma pa buna rigena,
ne chinyancha chioma pa chiaba buna egetare.
 
ⅩⅩⅪ 38:31: Ayubu 9:9; Amos 5:8Inee! Nonyare gosiberania egetuse gie ching’enang’eni gekorokwa Abaiseke batano na babere bamwabo?
Gose bwororobie egetuse keria gekorokwa Orioni?
ⅩⅩⅫ Nonyare koraa ching’enang’eni chioroke ase chingaki chiabo?
Gose nonyare koraa egetuse gie ching’enang’eni gekorokwa Edubu ne chimanwa chiaye?
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Nomanyete emeroberio ya igoro echiikire?
Nonyare koroberia okogamba kwabo igoro ase ense?
 
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Nonyare goaka eriogi riao riike ase amare
ogere embura enyinge egotwere?
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Nonyare gotoma chinkoba chigende aase gete,
erio chigoteebie: Ee, nao tore aa?
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Ning’o okobeeka obong’aini ase enkoro ime,
gose okomanya ase omoyo ime?
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Ning’o oranyare koba nobong’aini abare amare?
Gose ning’o oranyare koberereka chinyongo chi’amaache chia igoro,
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ ekero riroba riabwatanire riomire pa,
na amagakara abwatanire amo.
 
ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Inee! Nonyare gotwarera endo ebi ekoria,
gose koigotia chindo chinsae chiganetie endagera,
ⅩⅬ ekero chire ase amakuruma abo chieringanetie ase okoboera,
gose gochiramaime agwo ase chiebisete?
ⅩⅬⅠ Ning’o okoa ekegonkoru endagera yaye,
ekero amage aye akorera ase Nyasae
na goetanana onsi korigia endagera?
