ENCHIRI BUNA ERIIKIRE NA
MATAYO
Ogochaakera
Enchiri ya Matayo nigo egototeebia Amang’ana Amaya ng’a Yeso nere omotoria oria orieretwe eira n’Omonene. Goetera ase ere, Nyasae oikeranirie chiira chiria arierete abanto baye ase okobwatana ogokoro. Amang’ana aya Amaya tari rioka ase Abayahudi aria ase Yeso aiboreretwe na gokiinera, korende nase ense engima. Egetabu eke nigo kere n’omooroberio omoikeranu. Nigo kegochaakera omogano bw’okoiborwa kwa Yeso, okobatiswa kwaye n’ogoteemwa boigo kobogoria emeremo yaye yokoraria amang’angana y’Enchiri, okworokereria aria Galili, amo n’okogwenia. Agwo kegima, Enchiri yatoa omogano bw’orogeendo rwa Yeso korwa Galili goika Yerusalemu, boigo amang’ana y’echuuma yomoerio goika okobambwa na okobokigwa. Enchiri eye nigo ekomworokia Yeso koba omworokia omonene obwate chinguru chiogotegora amachiiko Omonene naboigo kworokereria oborwoti bwa Nyasae. Amorokererio aye amange nigo asangereirie ase ebireenga bitano; (1) okorandia ase egetunwa okwo kobogoretie amakora, emeremo, ebiegwa bia bosa, na omoerio bwa baria bagacheire omwando bw’obogima bw’oborwoti bwa igoro (Egesieri 5-7); (2), amorokererio ase abatomwa baria Ikomi na babere ase emeremo yabo (Egesieri 10); (3), emereng’anio y’oborwoti bwa igoro (Egesieri 13); (4), amorokererio igoro yengeencho yokoba omotunyani (Egesieri 18); boigo (5), amorokererio igoro yomoerio bwamakia aya, nogoocha kwoborwoti bwa igoro (Egesieri 24-25).
Okobagwa kw’egetabu ekee
Ororeria rwa Yeso Kristo n’okoiborwa kwaye 1:1—2:23
Emeremo ya Yohana Omobatisi 3:1-12
Okobatiswa kwa Yeso n’ogoteemwa kwaye 3:13—4:11
Yeso okoranderia abanto oborwoti bwa Nyasae na gokora emeremo yaye agwo Galili 4:12; 18:35
Korwa Galili goika Yerusalemu 19:1—20:34
Ayakoregete agwo Yerusalemu na ang’e aroro ase echuma y’omoerio 21:1—27:66
Okobokigwa kw’Omonene na okoorokigwa kwaye 28:1-20
Ⅰ
Chisokoro ne chisokoororia chia Yeso
(Luk 3:23-38)
Ⅰ Oyo noro omoroberio bw’echisokoro chia Yeso Kristo korwa ase ororeria rwa Daudi, oyio orenge korwa ase ororeria rwa Aburaamu.
Ⅱ Korwa ase Aburaamu goika omorwoti Daudi akaibora Isaka, na Isaka akaibora Yakobo, na Yakobo akaibora Yuda na abamwabo, Ⅲ na Yuda akaibora Peresu na Zera ase Tamari, na Peresu akaibora Hezironi, na Hezironi akaibora Aramu, Ⅳ na Aramu akaibora Aminadabu, na Aminadabu akaibora Nashoni, na Nashoni akaibora Salimoni, Ⅴ na Salimoni akaibora Boazi ase Rahabu, na Boazi akaibora Obedi ase Rutu, na Obedi akaibora Yese, Ⅵ 1:6: 2 Abar 24:14,15; 2 Amg 36:10; Yer 27:20na Yese akaibora omorwoti Daudi.
Na Daudi akaibora Sulemani ase oyio orenge omokungu o Uria, Ⅶ na Sulemani akaibora Rehoboamu, na Rehoboamu akaibora Abiya, na Abiya akaibora Asa, Ⅷ na Asa akaibora Yehosafati, na Yehosafati akaibora Yoramu, na Yoramu akaibora Usia, Ⅸ na Usia akaibora Yotamu na Yotamu akaibora Ahasi, na Ahasi akaibora Hezekia, Ⅹ na Hezekia akaibora Manase, na Manase akaibora Amoni, na Amoni akaibora Yosia, Ⅺ na Yosia akaibora Yekonia na abamwabo ase chingaki chiria Abaisraeli basakoretwe na kong’anyigwa gochia Babiloni.
Ⅻ Na magega ko Abaisraeli basakoirwe na kong’anyigwa gochia Babiloni Yekonia akaibora Salatieli, na Salatieli akaibora Serubabeli, ⅩⅢ na Serubabeli akaibora Abihudi, na Abihudi akaibora Eliakimu, na Eliakimu akaibora Azoro, ⅩⅣ na Azoro akaibora Zadoki, na Zadoki akaibora Akimu, na Akimu akaibora Eliudi, ⅩⅤ na Eliudi akaibora Eleazari, na Eleazari akaibora Matani, na Matani akaibora Yakobo, ⅩⅥ na Yakobo akaibora Yusufu, omosacha o Mariamu oyo oiborete Yeso, oyio okorokwa Kristo.
ⅩⅦ Na ebigori bionsi korwa Aburaamu goika Daudi nigo biarenge ebigori ikomi na bine: na korwa Daudi goika ogosakorwa na okong’anyigwa kwa Abaisraeli gochia Babiloni, n’ebigori ikomi na bine: na korwa ogosakorwa na okong’anyigwa kwa Abaisraeli gochia Babiloni goika Kristo, nebigori ikomi na bine.
Okoiborwa kwa Yeso
(Luk 2:1-7)
ⅩⅧ 1:18: Luk 1:27Okoiborwa kwa Yeso Kristo nigo kwarenge iga: Ekero Mariamu ng’ina konya ariereirwe boko na Yusufu, na konya bataramenyana buna omosacha n’omokuungu, Mariamu akarorekana ore morito ase chinguru chi’Omoika Omochenu. ⅩⅨ Yusufu nigo arenge omonto omooronge, na tarigetie komoa obosooku, maiso marore, ase igo akaroberia amotige bobisi. ⅩⅩ Korende ekero arenge orengereretie aya, rora, omomalaika bw’Omonene akamochiera ase chindoto akamoteebia. “Aye Yusufu, korwa ase ororeria rwa Daudi, toiroka koira Mariamu abe mokao, ekiagera nigo abeire morito ase okobua kw’Omoika Omochenu. ⅩⅪ 1:21: Luk 1:31Ere naibore omwana omoisia, nomoroke erieta riaye Yeso, ekiagera nere oratoorie abanto baye korwa ase ebibe biabo.” ⅩⅫ Aya onsi nigo abeire, erinde riikerane ring’ana riria riakwanetwe n’Omonene goetera ase omonwa bw’omobani:
ⅩⅩⅢ 1:23: Isaya 7:14“Rigereria, omoiseke omwekungi nabe morito,
erio aibore omwana omoisia,
na erieta riaye narokwe Imanueli”
(engencho yaye: Nyasae nare naintwe). ⅩⅩⅣ Na Yusufu agachia kobooka korwa ase chitoro, akanywoma Mariam buna omomalaika bw’Omonene konya amoteebirie akore. ⅩⅩⅤ 1:25: Luk 2:21Korende konya tibaramanyana kemobere nyuma ataraibora omwana oye omoisia. Naende Yusufu akamoroka erieta riaye Yeso.
