Miriamu Okonyora Egesusuro
Ⅰ Miriamu na Aroni bagakwana amang’ana arenge mamincha ya Musa, ekiagera Musa konya onyuomire omokungu korwa ase Abaetiopia. Ⅱ Bagakwana, bagateeba, “Inee! Omonene nigo agokwana na Musa bweka? Tari gokwana naintwe?” Omonene akaigwa amang’ana ayio. Ⅲ Omonto oyio Musa nigo arenge omoitongo mono kobua abanto bonsi barenge ase ense.
Ⅳ Erio agwo Omonene agakwana na Musa, na Aroni, na Miriamu, akabateebia, “Nimbaganetie inwe batato mochiche agwo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano.” Abwo batato bakageenda aroro. Ⅴ Omonene agaika korwa igoro ore ime ase riire riria riare buna esiiro, agatenena agwo ase egesoero ki’Eema eria, akarangeria Aroni na Miriamu. Barabwo bagasuka gochia bosio, Ⅵ Eri’Omonene akabateebia, “Igwa amang’ana ane: Onye omobani ore ase egati yaino, inche Omonene nigo ’nkwemanyekania ase are ase okorora, naende nakwana nere ase chindoto. Ⅶ 12:7: Abaibr 3:2Korende ase Musa, omosomba one, taibo, ere nomwegenwa ase enyomba yane yonsi. Ⅷ Nigo ’ngokwana nere obosio ase obosio, gose tindi gokwana nere ase emerabaaro; ere ororire ekieni kiane. Nase ki inwe motabeete n’obwoba gokwana mamincha ya Musa, omosomba one?”
Ⅸ Endamwamu y’Omonene ekaba enene ase bare, akarua aroro agwo, akageenda. Ⅹ Ekero riire riria riaruete ase Eema eria, omobere o Miriamu konya obeire noborwaire bwa ukoma, okaraba se. Aroni akaonchoka komorigereria, akarora ng’a omobere oye obeire noborwaire bwa ukoma. Ⅺ Erio Aroni agateebia Musa, “Aye omonene one, koranche, tobaisa gotoa egesusuro, ekiagera intwe nigo twakorire egento ki’oboriri, na twakorire ebibe. Ⅻ Totiga Miriamu abe buna oyokuure, buna omonto oiboretwe ne chinyama ekeng’ese, chianiarokeire inda, chiaerire.”
ⅩⅢ Musa akarera ase Omonene, “Aye Nyasae, nagosoroire, koranche ogwenie Miriamu!”
ⅩⅣ 12:14: Omob 5:2,3Korende Omonene akairaneria Musa, “Onye rende ise arenge komotwera amate ase obosio bwaye, Inee, tagwenerete anyore obosooku ase engaki y’amatuko atan’abere? Tiga aatananwe korwa ase egetwori abekwe ase aao ase engaki y’amatuko atan’abere, na magega y’ayio airanigwe ase egetwori.” ⅩⅤ Ase ayio Miriamu agasokigwa isiko y’egetwori, akabeekwa ase aao ase engaki y’amatuko atan’abere, na abanto tibaimogete kogeenda goika ekero Miriamu airanete ase egetwori. ⅩⅥ Magega y’ayio abanto bakaimoka kogeenda korwa ase Haseroti, bagaika ase erooro ya Parani, bagatoora aroro.
