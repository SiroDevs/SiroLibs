Ⅻ
Yoashi omorwoti bw’Abayuda
(2 Amg 24:1-16)
Ⅰ Ase omwaka o gatano na kabere bw’okogaamba kwa Yehu, Yoashi agachaaka kogamba, na akagaamba emiaka emerongo ene agwo Yerusalemu. Ng’ina nigo arenge korokwa Sibia korwa Beeri‐Seba. Ⅱ Yoashi agakora ayare amaronge ase amaiso ’Omonene ase amatuko aye onsi, ekiagera Yehoyada Omokuani abeete okomosemia. Ⅲ Korende nonya naboigo, aase aimokererete igoro agosasimerwa tatagoretwe. Abanto bakagenderera korua ebing’wanso na gosambera ubani aroro.
Ⅳ 12:4: Okong’ 30:11-16Yoashi agateebia abakuani, “Chibesa chionsi chi’ebinto ebichenu chikorentwa ase enyomba y’Omonene, ne chitaabu kera omonto arageire korua, ne chitaabu chikoruegwa ase okobooria abanto koreng’ana ne rigori richikiire, ne chitaabu omonto akorua ase ogwancha, Ⅴ tiga abakuani bachibogorie, kera oyomo korwa ase abanto baye. Erio barabwo baroiserie enyomba aande onsi eganeirie koroisigwa.”
Ⅵ Korende ase omwaka bw’emerongo ebere na etato bw’okogaamba kwa Yoashi omorwoti, abakuani tibaroisetie enyomba y’Omonene ase yatagogete. Ⅶ Ase ayio Yoashi omorwoti akarangeria Yehoyada Omokuani na abakuani bande, agakwana nabarabwo agateeba: “Ninki kiagera motari koroisia enyomba aase yatagokire? Bono timobaisa korusia chitaabu chinde chionsi korwa ase abanto, korende morente chitaabu chiria mobwate, erinde eroiserigwe ase yatagokire.” Ⅷ Abakuani bagancha ng’a tibagochia korusia chitaabu chinde korwa ase abanto, naende ng’a tibagochia koroisia enyomba ase yatagokire.
Ⅸ Erio Yehoyada Omokuani akarenta esanduki enkong’u akayebochora akabao ase ekemumeko kiaye, akayebeeka mbarabare y’egesasiimero ensemo y’okorio, ase abanto bare gosoera enyomba y’Omonene. Na abakuani baria barendete agwo ase egesieri ki’enyomba, akabeeka ime ase esanduki eria chitaabu chionsi chiarentetwe ase enyomba y’Omonene. Ⅹ Engaki ende yonsi chitaabu chiarenge koba chinyinge ime ase esanduki omoriki bw’omorwoti na Omokuani omonene nigo baarenge gocha kobara chitaabu chiria chiare korentwa ase enyomba y’Omonene na gochibeeka ime ase emebuko, naende bayeboa. Ⅺ Ekero chitaabu chiria chiarengirwe, nigo chiare koruegwa ase amaboko ’abaanto baria batenenerete emeremo y’enyomba y’Omonene. Nabarabwo nigo baarenge goakana ching’eria ase abasenia be chibao na abaagachi baarenge gokora emeremo ase enyomba y’Omonene, Ⅻ na abaagachi b’amagena na abaabaachi amagena. Naende nigo baarenge kogora chibao, na amagena asenirie, ase okoroiseria enyomba y’Omonene aase yatagogete. Chibesa echio nigo chiaruetwe ase kera egento kiaganeirie ase okoroiseria enyomba eria. ⅩⅢ Korende korwa ase chitaabu chiria chiarentetwe ase enyomba y’Omonene tibaroisetie chisani chi’echifeta gose ebinachi, gose ebitaabo, gose ebitureeri, gose ebikorero binde bie chitaabu, gose ebie chifeta, ase enyomba y’Omonene, ⅩⅣ ekiagera chionsi chiaruetwe ase abakori emeremo abaarenge koroisia enyomba ase echio. ⅩⅤ 12:15: 2 Abar 22:7Abanto abwo baarenge koegwa chitaabu chiria tibaarenge koborigwa koorokia omobaro, ekiagera baarenge abegenwa. ⅩⅥ 12:16: Abal 7:7Chibesa chiarenge koruegwa ase okorongeyia okomocha, na echio chiarenge koruegwa ase ekeng’wanso ki’ebibe tichiarentetwe ase enyomba y’Omonene; echio nigo chiabeete echi’abakuani.
ⅩⅦ Ase engaki eria Hasaeli, omorwoti bw’Abasiiria, akagenda akarwania Gati, akayobua. Korende ekero Hasaeli aonchogete kogenda korwania Yerusalemu, ⅩⅧ Yoashi, omorwoti bw’Abayuda, akaimokia ebiegwa bionsi biria Yehosafati, na Yehoramu, na Ahasia, chisokoro chiaye, abarwoti b’Abayuda, baatananete koba ebichenu, amo nebiegwa biaye omonyene bi’ogwancha, ne chitaabu chionsi chianyoretwe ase chikungo chi’enyomba y’Omonene na ey’omorwoti. Erio agatoma ebinto biria bionsi ase Hasaeli, omorwoti bw’Abasiiria. Erio Hasaeli agatiga Yerusalemu.
ⅩⅨ Bono amang’ana onsi igoro ya Yoashi, na aya onsi akorete, nigo ariikire ase Egetabu ki’ogokora kw’Abarwoti b’Abayuda.
ⅩⅩ Abasomba baye bagakora omooroberio bw’okomoita, bakamoitera ime ase enyomba yaye agwo Milo, agwo ase enchera ekogenda Sila. ⅩⅪ Abasomba baye, Yosakari, mosinto o Simeati, na Yehosabadi, mosinto o Someri, aba nabwo bamoitete agakwa. Yoashi agatindekwa ase chisokoro chiaye chiatindegetwe agwo ase Omochie o Daudi, na Amasia, omwana oye omomura, akaba okogaamba ase ribaga riaye.
