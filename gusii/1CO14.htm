ⅩⅣ
Ebiegwa bi’obobani na ebi’ogokwana ase emenwa
Ⅰ Rigia obwanchani, teema mono konyora ebiegwa bi’Omoika; kobua mono ase ebi, monyare kobana. Ⅱ Naki oyogokwana ase omonwa otamanyekaneti, tari gokwana nabaanto, korende na Nyasae, ekiagera onde tari okomoigwa, korende nigo agokwana amang’ana abobisi ase Omoika. Ⅲ Korende ere oyokobana, nigo agokwana ase abanto amang’ana ay’okobaagacha, na kobaremia, na kobabeeka chinguru. Ⅳ Oyogokwana ase omonwa otamanyekaneti nigo akweagacha ere omonyene; korende oyokobana nigo akoagacha ekanisa. Ⅴ Inche nigo ndigetie inwe mwensi mokwane ase emenwa, korende kobua mono ng’a monyare kobana. Ere oyokobana nomonene kobua oyogokwana ase emenwa, otatiga omonto aonchore, erinde ekanisa eagachwe.
Ⅵ Gaki baminto, kondache ase more na gokwana ase emenwa, inki ndabagwenere, onye aya ngokwana takoba amang’ana ’okomaanorerwa gose obong’aini, gose obobani, gose okworokereria? Ⅶ Nonya n’ebinto bitari nobogima, buna ekeroria, gose obokano, onye tibikorua eriogi rire nogwatananekana, naki eramanyekane ngotera ki gokobugigwa ase ekeroria gose obokano? Ⅷ Naende onye egetureeri gekobugigwa na gosokia egeturi getamanyekaneti, ning’o orabe ang’e ase esegi? Ⅸ Naboigo nainwe motakwaneti ring’ana rikoigweka, ring’ana riria rigokwanwa, naki riramanyekane? Naki igo morakwane ase embeo bosa. Ⅹ Inyora ng’a nere emenwa ere ne chingencho chinyinge ase ense, gose onde tari nonya noyomo, otari nengencho yaye. Ⅺ Onye tinkomanya engencho y’omonwa oria, nimbe buna omogeni asare ere oyogokwana, na ere nabe buna omogeni ase ’nde. Ⅻ Naboigo nainwe, ekiagera morigetie mono koba abanto bare nebiegwa bi’Omoika, rigia mono momente koba nabio, erinde moagache ekanisa.
ⅩⅢ Ase ayio, ere oyogokwana ase omonwa otamanyekaneti tiga asabe aegwe ekeegwa gi’okoonchora. ⅩⅣ Naki kondasabe ase omonwa otamanyekaneti omoika one nigo ogosaba, korende obong’aini bwane tibori kwama. ⅩⅤ Inki rende ekio? Nindigetie nsabe ase omoika, naende nindigetie nsabe ase obong’aini. Nindigetie ntere ase omoika, naende nindigetie ntere ase obong’aini. ⅩⅥ Aande onye aye ogotogia Nyasae ase omoika, nere oyoikaransete otamanyeti, naki araitabe “Amina” ase ogoaka kwao ng’a mbuya mono, na ere tamanyeti aya ogokwana? ⅩⅦ Naki nekeene, aye gwatogirie buya, korende oria oyonde taagachiri. ⅩⅧ Namoakeire Nyasae ng’a mbuya mono, ekiagera ngokwana ase emenwa kobua inwe mwensi; ⅩⅨ korende nanchire gokwana amang’ana atano ase ekanisa ase obong’aini bwane, inyare kworokereria abande, kobua gokwana amang’ana chilifu ikomi ase emenwa etamanyekaneti.
ⅩⅩ Baminto, timoba buna abana abake ase ebirengererio biaino; ase obobe mobe abana ebing’werere, korende ase okomanya kwaino mobe abanto abanene. ⅩⅪ 14:21: Isaya 28:11,12Ase amachiiko nigo ariikire, “Omonene oteebire: Ase abanto b’emenwa etamanyekaneti, na ase ebikoba bi’emenwa y’abageni ninkwane nabaanto aba. Nonya nabo tibakonyigwera.” ⅩⅫ Gaki emenwa eyio nekemanyererio, tari ase abegenete, korende ase abategeneti. Korende obobani nekemanyererio, tari ase abategeneti, korende ase abegenete. ⅩⅩⅢ Gaki onye ekanisa yonsi, egosangererekana amo, na mwensi mogokwana ase emenwa, erio abanto ba isiko bagocha, gose abategeneti, Inee, tibagoteeba ng’a nigo more chibarimo? ⅩⅩⅣ Korende onye bonsi bakobana, omonto otegeneti ogocha, gose omonto o isiko, orokigwe na bonsi ng’a nomomochi are, agambigwe na bonsi; ⅩⅩⅤ naboigo obobisi bw’enkoro yaye bobe maiso marore; na igo aragwe maumama, na gosasiima Nyasae, atore ng’a ekeene Nyasae nigo are ase egati yaino.
Omoroberio omuya bw’ogosasiima
ⅩⅩⅥ Naki rende baminto? Komogosangererekana amo, kera oyomo nare nogoteera, gose okworokereria, gose okomaanorerwa, gose gokwana ase omonwa, gose koonchora, amang’ana onsi tiga akoreke ase okoagacha ekanisa. ⅩⅩⅦ Onye omonto ogokwana ase omonwa otamanyekaneti, bakwane babere gose batato boka, korende kera omonto ase ribaga riaye, na oyomo aonchore. ⅩⅩⅧ Korende onye oyokoonchora taiyo, tiga oyogokwana ase omonwa akire ase ekanisa, ekwanere bweka omonyene naende akwane na Nyasae. ⅩⅩⅨ Abaabani bakwane babere gose batato, na abande barenge amang’ana aya bakwana. ⅩⅩⅩ Korende oyonde oikaransete karaigorerwe ring’ana, oria bw’eritang’ani tiga akire. ⅩⅩⅪ Naki inwe mwensi namonyare kobana oyomo ase oyomo, erinde bonsi banyare kweorokereria, na bonsi baremigwe. ⅩⅩⅫ Emeika y’ababani nigo ekoigwera ababani. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Naki Nyasae tari Nyasae bw’eburuburu, korende oyo bw’omorembe.
Buna ere ase chikanisa chionsi chi’abachenu, ⅩⅩⅩⅣ abakungu bakire ase chikanisa. Naki tibari ne ribaga ri’ogokwana, korende baigwere buna amachiiko agoteeba. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Onye barigetie kweorokereria ring’ana, tiga baborie abasacha babo inka. Naki nobosooku omokungu gokwana ase ekanisa. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Inee! Ring’ana ria Nyasae nasemore riarwete, gose ninwe mweka riaikerete?
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Omonto karerore ng’a nomobani are, gose noyo bw’Omoika, tiga amanye ng’a aya nabarikeire namachiiko ’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Onye omonto tarigeti koyamanya, tiga abe omoriri. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Ase ayio baminto, rigia mono okobana, gose timokana ogokwana kw’emenwa. ⅩⅬ Amang’ana onsi akoreke ase obuya na ase omooroberio.
