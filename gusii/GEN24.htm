ⅩⅩⅣ
Isaka okorenterwa Rebeka abe mokaye
Ⅰ Bono Aburaamu konya obeire omogotu, akoorire emiaka emenge; n’Omonene konya omosesenirie ase amang’ana onsi. Ⅱ Aburaamu akarangeria omosomba oye oyo konya omokoreire ase emiaka emenge mono, na omorenderete enibo yaye yonsi, akamoteebia: “Beka okoboko kwao inse y’ebinama biane, Ⅲ na inche ninkoboorie otiane ase Omonene Nyasae o igoro na ense, ng’a tokorigeria omwana one omokungu korwa ase abaiseke b’Abakaanani, aba imenyete ase egati yabo. Ⅳ Korende ogende ase ense yane na ase eamate yane, orentere omwana one Isaka omokungu korwa aroro abe mokaye.”
Ⅴ Omosomba oria akamoiraneria: “Aande, omokungu oria atancheraneti gocha amo nainche goika ase ense eye; Inee, nigo ndaire omwana oo goika ase ense eria kwarwete?”
Ⅵ Aburaamu akamoiraneria: “Bwerende, tobaisa koira omwana one aroro. Ⅶ Omonene, Nyasae o igoro na ense, oyondusetie o tata korwa ase ense eria naiboreretwe, ere nigo akwanete nainche na gotiana, ogoteeba: Ning’ee ororeeria rwao ense eye, ere natome omomalaika oye agotang’ane; aye norentere omwana one omokungu korwa aroro. Ⅷ Na omokungu oria atancheti moche komo, rirorio nosiborwe korwa ase eira eye okoria ase ’nde, korende tobaisa koira omwana one gochia aroro.” Ⅸ Erio omosomba oria ababeeka okoboko kwaye inse y’ekebono kia Aburaamu, omonene oye, akerama ase ring’ana erio.
Ⅹ Erio omosomba oria akarusia chingamia ikomi chi’omonene oye, akaimokia na ebiegwa ebinge ebiya; akaimoka, akagenda gochia Mesopotamia, agaika omochie oria Nahori amenyete. Ⅺ Agwo agatimokeria chingamia isiko y’omochie ang’e nensoko y’amaache engaki ya mogoroba, ekero abakungu bagocha kobuucha amaache. Ⅻ Ere agasaba, agateeba: “Aye Omonene, Nyasae bw’omonene one Aburaamu, nagosabire, onsesenie ase amang’ana aane a reero, okorere omonene one Aburaamu obwanchani. ⅩⅢ Rora, ninteneine aiga ase ensoko y’amaache, na abaiseke b’omochie oyo ngocha bare kobuucha amaache. ⅩⅣ Tiga ebe iga; Kondaborie oyomo obo: Koranche obeke enyongo yao inse, inche inywe amaache, na ere karang’iranerie: Ee, kanywe aye, na nimbuuchere chingamia chiao nachirochio; otige oyio abe nere gwachoreire omosomba oo Isaka, na inche erio nimanye ng’a aye Omonene one gwankoreire amaya ase obwanchani.”
ⅩⅤ Ritang’ani atarakoora ogosaba kwaye, akarora Rebeka ogocha obogoretie enyongo yaye mareko. Ere nomoiseke o Betueli, na Betueli nomwana o Milka, moka Nahori, momura omwabo Aburaamu. ⅩⅥ Na omoiseke oria nigo areenge omonyakieni, naende nomwekuungi, otaraba na mosacha onde. Agatirimboka gochia ase ensoko, agaichoria enyongo yaye amaache, agatiira. ⅩⅦ Erio omosomba oria akaminyoka bwango gochia komoorotota, akamosaba: “Koranche, ong’e amaache inywe.”
ⅩⅧ Ere akamoiraneria akamoteebia: “Kanywe, omonene one!” Agatoora enyongo yaye bwango, akayebeeka koboko kwaye, akamoa, na ere akanywa. ⅩⅨ Gakoorire komoa amaache, akabora: “Ne chingamia chiao boigo ninchibuuchere, na chirochio chinywe.” ⅩⅩ Akaayerera, akaumora amaache aria areenge ase enyongo ase orotuba ime, akaminyoka bwango ase ensoko naende, agachibuuchera chingamia chiaye chionsi. ⅩⅪ Erio omonto oria akamobeta amaiso, akaba okirete kiri, erinde amanye gose Omonene osesesenirie orogendo rwaye, gose taibo.
ⅩⅫ Ekero chingamia chiakoorire konywa amaache, omonto oria akamoruseria ekegera gi’etaabu, kiare noborito bwe chigiramu isano na isato, na ebitange bibere bie chitaabu ase amaboko aye, ebiare noborito bwe chigiramu rigana erimo, na emerongo etano. ⅩⅩⅢ Akamobooria: “Nomoiseke o ng’o ore? Naende onteebie gose ribaga ndire mwa iso toranyare koraara botuko.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Ere akamoiraneria: “Inche nomoiseke o Betueli, oyo Milka aiborerete Nahori.” ⅩⅩⅤ Naende akamoteebia: “Seito obonyansi mboreo, na aase tokorisia aisaine, na enyomba y’abageni bakorara nereo.”
ⅩⅩⅥ Erio omonto oria agatugama inse, agasasiima Omonene, ⅩⅩⅦ agateeba: “Omonene, Nyasae bw’omonene one Aburaamu, atogigwe! Ere tatigeti koorokia omonene one obuya na obwegenwa. Omonene ondaaire enchera, naikire enka y’abamwabo omonene one.”
ⅩⅩⅧ Erio omoiseke oria akaminyoka, agaika inka sobo, agateebia abanto ba nyomba mwa ng’ina amang’ana ayio. ⅩⅩⅨ-ⅩⅩⅩ Rebeka nabwate momura omwabo ore korokwa Labani. Ekero Labani arooche ekegera na ebitange ase amaboko ’omoiseke omwabo, na kaigure amang’ana omonto oria ateebetie Rebeka, agasoka, akaminyoka gochia ase ensoko ase omonto oria areenge, akamorora oteneine ase chingamia chiaye, ⅩⅩⅪ akamoteebia: “Inchuo, aye osesenirie n’Omonene! Naki oteneine isiko aa? Inche nakoroiseirie enyomba ase ogochia koraara, na aase e chingamia chiao.”
ⅩⅩⅫ Erio omonto oria agasoa nyomba, na Labani agasibora chingamia chigurube chiabo, agachia obonyansi, na chirochio chikaria. Naende akarentera omonto oria amaache, akarentera na abwo baarenge komo, bagesibia amagoro. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Erio akaariganigwa ase endagera, korende agateeba: “Tinkoria endagera, goika ndateeba amang’ana ane.”
Erio Labani akairaneria: “Gaki, yakwane!”
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Erio agateeba: “Inche nomosomba o Aburaamu. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Omonene osesenirie omonene one mono, na ere obeire omotenenku. Omoeire amaicho e ching’ondi, na aye chiombe, chifeta ne chitaabu, na abasomba, abamura na abaiseke, chingamia ne chitigere.” ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Na Sara, moka omonene one, akamoiborera omwana omomura ekero amenyire omogotu; na omonene one oeire omwana oyio bionsi ebi abwate. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Omonene one akamboria ntiane, aganteabia: “Torigeria omwana one omokungu korwa ase abaiseke b’Abakaanani, aba imenyete ase ense yabo; ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Korende ogende ase omochie o tata, ase eamate yane, orentere omwana one omokungu korwa aroro. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Na inche ngateebia omonene one: Aande omokungu oria atancheraneti gocha amo nainche. ⅩⅬ Korende aganteebia: Omonene oyo ngendererete ase obosio bwaye, natome omomalaika oye amo naye, asesenie orogendo rwao. Naye orentere omwana one omokungu korwa ase eamate yane, korwa ase omochie o tata; ⅩⅬⅠ erio nosiborwe korwa ase eira eyio nakoboririe otiane. Ekero oraike ase eamate yane, onye tibagokoa omoiseke oyio, aye rirorio nobe gwasiboirwe korwa ase ogotiana kwao.”
ⅩⅬⅡ “Inche igo nacha reero ase ensoko, nasaba iga: Aye Omonene, Nyasae bw’omonene one Aburaamu, koranche onsesenie bono ase orogendo rwane nkogenda. ⅩⅬⅢ Rora ninteneine aiga ase ensoko y’amaache. Tiga ebe iga: Omoiseke onde omo orache kobuucha amaache, na inche imosabe oyio: Koranche ong’e amaache make korwa ase enyongo yao inywe. ⅩⅬⅣ Onye ere okong’iraneria: Ee, kanywe, na nimbuuchere chingamia chiao nachirochio; otige oyio abe nere omokungu Omonene achoreire omwana bw’omonene one.”
ⅩⅬⅤ “Ritang’ani ntarakoora ogosaba ase omoyo one, narora Rebeka ogocha obegete enyongo yaye mareko, ogotirimboka gochia ase ensoko kobuucha amaache.” Na inche namosaba: “Koranche, ong’e amaache inywe.” ⅩⅬⅥ Ere orusia enyongo yaye bwango korwa ase amareko aye, oyebeeka inse, onteebia: Kanywe, ne chingamia chiao ninchibuchere boigo. Erio ong’a, nanywa, oa ne chingamia, nachirochio chianywa. ⅩⅬⅦ Erio namobooria: “Nomoiseke o ng’o ore?” Ere ong’iraneria: “Inche nomoiseke o Betueli oyo Milka aiborerete Nahori.” Agwo namobekera ekegera chimioro chiaye, na ebitange ase amaboko aye. ⅩⅬⅧ Erio natugama inse nasasiima Omonene. Naende namotogia Omonene, Nyasae bw’omonene one Aburaamu, oyontang’anete ase enchera endonge, erinde imorentere omwana bw’omonene one omokungu korwa ase eamate yaye. ⅩⅬⅨ Bono rende, onye morigetie gokorera Omonene one amaya, na ayare boronge, monteebie; onye taibo, monteebie, ngende ensemo ende eng’ao korigia.
Ⅼ Erio Labani na Betueli bakamoiraneria, bakamoteebia: “Amang’ana aya nigo arure ase Omonene; intwe titokonyara gokwana ande, amabe gose amaya. ⅬⅠ Rora, Rebeka oyio ore ase obosio bwao, moire, mogende komo, achie koba omokungu bw’omwana bw’omonene oo, buna Omonene konya ateebire.”
ⅬⅡ Ekero omosomba o Aburaamu aigwete amang’ana abo, agatugama inse ase obosio bw’Omonene. ⅬⅢ Erio omosomba oria akarusia ebinto bi’ogwechabera bie chitaabu, na ebie chifeta, amo ne chianga, akaa Rebeka. Naende akaa momura omwabo amo na ng’ina ebiegwa bi’erigori rinene.
ⅬⅣ Ere na abanto baria baarenge komo bakaria na bakanywa, erio bakaraara aroro. Mambia kobwachia gokia akabateebia: “Ing’a ribaga ng’irane ase omonene one.”
ⅬⅤ Momura omwabo Rebeka na ng’ina bakamoteebia: “Tiga omoiseke oyo arabeere aiga koreng’ana amatuko ikomi; magega y’ayio mogende komo.”
ⅬⅥ Ere akabateebia: “Timobaisa goteebania, ekiagera Omonene onsesenirie ase orogendo rwane; ing’a ribaga ngende ase omonene one.”
ⅬⅦ Bakamoteebia: “Tiga torangerie omoiseke, tomoboorie, toigwe buna agoteeba.” ⅬⅧ Bakarangeria Rebeka, bakamobooria: “Inee! Inabwanche ogende nomonto oyo?”
Akairaneria: “Ee, nanchire, ngende nere.”
ⅬⅨ Erio bakaa Rebeka ribaga amo nomoreri oye bagende nomosomba o Aburaamu na abanto baye. ⅬⅩ Bagasesenia Rebeka, bakamoteebia,
“Oyominto, obe ng’ina abanto chilifu ne chilifu;
ororeeria rwao inarobue emechie y’ababisa bao, royegambere!”
ⅬⅪ Erio Rebeka akaimoka amo nabaiseke baye abasomba, bakariina chingamia, bagatunyana omonto oria. Naboigo omosomba oria akaira Rebeka, akagenda nere sobo.
ⅬⅫ Bono Isaka konya orure ase ensoko ya Beeri‐Lahai‐Roi, ekiagera nigo amenyete ase ense ya Negebu. ⅬⅩⅢ Isaka nigo achiete mogondo ekero kia mogoroba, akaba ore nebirengererio. Agwo akaimokereria amaiso aye igoro, akarora chingamia chigocha. ⅬⅩⅣ Rebeka nere akaimokereria amaiso aye igoro, akarora Isaka; erio agaika bwango korwa ase engamia igoro, ⅬⅩⅤ akabooria omosomba oria: “Monto ki oria ogotaara ase omogondo, ogocha koumerana naintwe?”
Omosomba oria akamoiraneria: “Oria nere omonene one.” Erio omoiseke oria akabwata egietubo kiaye, agetuba.
ⅬⅩⅥ Na omosomba oria agateebia Isaka amang’ana onsi, buna akorete. ⅬⅩⅦ Isaka agasoyia Rebeka ase eema ya ng’ina ime, akaba omonywomire, naende akaba omwanchete. Ase ogi, Isaka akaremigwa korwa ase omoichano bw’ogokwa kwa ng’ina.
