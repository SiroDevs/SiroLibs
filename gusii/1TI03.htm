Buna abatang’ani b’ekanisa bagwenerete koba
Ⅰ Ring’ana eri neri’ekeene, onye omonto origetie koba omotang’ani bw’ekanisa, igo aganetie emeremo emiya. Ⅱ 3:2: Tito 1:6-9Omotang’ani bw’ekanisa nemogwenerete abe omonto otari gokwanwa bobe, omonto ore nomokuungu oyomo, omweriiti, na omwechiiki omwegaamberi ase onsi, abe, omooroisia abageni, na oy’omanyete kworokereria buya, Ⅲ tari omonto onarete edivai, gose omonto bwanchete goaka abanto, abe omonto omoremereria, otari gwancha eriomana, gose otari omwegimeria. Ⅳ Naende nemogwenerete abe omonto omanyete kogambera enyomba yaye omonyene buya, na kworokereria abana baye baigwere ase ogosika gwonsi, Ⅴ ekiagera onye omonto tamanyeti gotenenera enyomba yaye omonyene, inaki aranyare korenda ekanisa ya Nyasae? Ⅵ Ere taaba omonto oonchoka reero iga, abaise goocha koenena na kogwa ase okogaambigwa gwa Saitani. Ⅶ Nagwenerete gwatorwa ng’a nomuya nabaanto bare isiko, erinde tabaisa kogwa ase ogosoerwa, na ase omoeto o Saitani.
Buna abakoonyi b’ekanisa bagwenerete koba
Ⅷ Naboigo nabakoonyi b’ekanisa babe abanto bateneneine boronge ase ogokora okuya, batari nemenwa ebere, abanto batari konywa edivai enyinge, abanto batari gwancha okonyora kw’oborianania. Ⅸ Mbagwenerete babwate obobisi bw’okwegena ase chinkoro chitakobagaambia. Ⅹ Barabwo boigo barengwe ritang’ani: onye bakonyorekana ng’a tibari ne chingeencho chimbe, erio baegwe emeremo y’obokonyi ase ekanisa. Ⅺ Naboigo nabakungu babo bateneine boronge ase ogokora okuya, tari abang’achi, babe aberiiti na abegenwa ase amang’ana onsi. Ⅻ Omokonyi bw’ekanisa abe omonto ore nomokuungu oyomo, omanyete kogaambera abana baye buya, nenyomba yaye omonyene. ⅩⅢ Inaki abwo bagokora emeremo yabo y’obokonyi buya benyoreire amasikani na oboremu obonge ase okwegena kore ime ase Kristo Yeso.
Obobisi bwe chingeencho chi’obonyasae
ⅩⅣ Nigo nakoriikeire amang’ana aya, inde nogosemeria ng’a ninchiche ase ore bwango. ⅩⅤ Korende inse goteebana, erio omanye buna abanto bagwenerete kogeenderera ase enyomba ya Nyasae, ey’ere ekanisa ya Nyasae ore moyo, naende ere esiiro na oboroso ebire bi’ekeene. ⅩⅥ Naende ne ring’ana ritari koamereranigwa, ng’a obobisi bwe chingencho chi’obonyasae nobonene:
Ere oyoorogetigwe ase omobere o Mwanyabaanto,
akaorokigwa n’Omoika koba omonyene oboronge,
akarorwa nabamalaika,
akarandigwa ase egati y’abanto b’ebisaku,
akegenwa ase ense,
naende akabogorigwa gochia igoro.
