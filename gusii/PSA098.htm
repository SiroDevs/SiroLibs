Nyasae nere Omogambi bw’ense engima
Ⅰ Moterere Omonene ogoteera okoyia,
ekiagera okorire amang’ana ay’ogokumia;
obuire nokoboko kwaye gw’okorio,
ee, nokoboko kwaye ogochenu.
Ⅱ Omonene okorire ogotooreka kwaye komanyekane,
oorokirie oboronge bwaye ase amasio ’abaanto b’ebisaku;
Ⅲ oinyoire obwanchani bwaye bogendererete botambe, na obwegenwa bwaye,
obwo aorogetie ase Abaisraeli.
Chinsinyo chionsi chi’ense chiarorire ogotooreka kwa Nyasae oito.
Ⅳ Aye ense yonsi, aka eriogi ri’omogooko ase Omonene,
tera ogoteera kw’omogooko na motogie ase amariogi amanene.
Ⅴ Terera Omonene ase okobugia amakano,
ee, ase okobugia amakano tera namariogi amaya.
Ⅵ Moake amariogi ’omogooko ase Omonene ore omorwoti
ase okobugia ebirombeta na ebitureeri.
Ⅶ Tiga enyancha ebe ekoruma amo n’ebinto bionsi bire ime yaye,
boigo nense na abanto bonsi bamenyeteo.
Ⅷ Chindooche tiga chiake chinkobi,
na ebitunwa tiga bitere nomogooko amo,
Ⅸ ase obosio bw’Omonene, ekiagera ngocha are konachera ense ekiina.
Ere nanachere ense ekiina ase oboronge,
na abanto nabanachere ekiina atari gwanchereria.
