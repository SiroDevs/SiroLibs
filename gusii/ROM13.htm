Igoro y’okoigwera abare n’okobua
Ⅰ Tiga kera omonto abe omoigweri ase abare n’okobua, ekiagera okobua konde tikori gotarueti ase Nyasae, na koria koreo nokobekire na Nyasae. Ⅱ Naboigo oy’otari koigwera oyore n’okobua, nigo akoamererania naya Nyasae achiikire; na barabwo abakoamererania inabenyorere ekiina. Ⅲ Inaki abakogaamba tibari kogosa abanto ase engencho y’ogokora okuya, korende nase engencho y’ogokora okobe. Inee! Torigeti komoiroka oyore n’okobua? Kora ayare amaya, na aye nonyore ogotogigwa ase amaya aria. Ⅳ Inaki oyore n’okobua, ere n’omokoreri o Nyasae ase ogokonyorera amaya. Korende korakore amabe nekogwenerete oiroke; inaki ere tabogoreti omoro bosa, ekiagera n’omokoreri o Nyasae ase ogoakana egesiomba ase omokori amabe na komoa egesusuro. Ⅴ Ase ayio negwenerete kera oyomo aigwere, tari ase engencho y ’endamwamu ya Nyasae yoka, korende aigwere boigo, erinde enkoro yaye temogaambia. Ⅵ 13:6: Mat 22:21; Mar 12:17; Luk 20:25Ase engencho eyio naende nakio mokoruera ebango, ekiagera abare n’okobua nigo bare abakoreri ba Nyasae, ase ogokora emeremo eyio eyio. Ⅶ Bae bonsi ebiaboronge biabo: omonto bw’ebango, ebango; na omonto bw’ogochooga, ogochooga; oyogwenerete koirokwa, tiga airokwe; na oyogwenerete gosikwa, tiga asikwe.
Mobe nobwanchani inwe ase inwe
Ⅷ Timoba n’esira ase onde bwensi otatiga ogwanchana, ekiagera oy’omwanchete omogisangio oye oikeranirie amachiiko. Ⅸ 13:9: a Okong’ 20:14; Okoir 5:18; b Okong’ 20:13; Okoir 5:17; c Okong’ 20:15; Okoir 5:19; d Okong’ 20:17; Okoir 5:21; e Abal 19:18Inaki amachiiko aya, “Totomana, Toita, Toiba, Toba nokogania okobe”, na gakorabeo gochiika konde gwonsi, kwabeekeraniigwe amo ase ring’ana eri, “Ancha omogisangio oo buna bweanchete.” Ⅹ Obwanchani tibogokorera omogisangio oo bobe; naboigo, obwanchani nabwo bogoikerania amachiiko.
Ogocha kw’erituko ria Kristo
Ⅺ Kora ayio, ekiagera mwamanyire ng’a amatuko aikire, na engaki yaino y’okoboka korwa ase chitoro yabeire ang’e. Inaki bono ogotooreka nigo kore ang’e naintwe kobua ekero keria twachaagete kwegena. Ⅻ Obotuko bwaisire koera, omobaso obeire ang’e. Torusie ogokora kw’omosunte; tweboyie ebirwanero bi’omobaso. ⅩⅢ Totaare buna egwenerete ase omobaso; tari ase okoria bobe na gotinda, tari ase obotomani na ase obonyaka, tari ase eriomana ne ribero. ⅩⅣ Korende mweboyie Omonene Yeso Kristo, timoa omobere ribaga goikerania okogania kwaye kw’ebibe.
