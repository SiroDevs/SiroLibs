Okweng’ata gw’ekeene
Ⅰ Aka eriogi rinene, tobaisa gokira, aka eriogi buna eri’egetureeri! Teebia abanto baane ogosaria kwabo, bateebie abwo bare ab’ororeeria rwa Yakobo ebibe biabo. Ⅱ Abanto abwo nigo bakondigia kera rituko, na nigo bagokirie bamanye chinchera chiane. Nigo banga buna egesaku kegokora oboronge getana gotiga amachiiko a Nyasae obo. Barabwo nigo bakomboria mbagambie ebiina bi’oboronge, nigo baganetie Nyasae abaike ang’e.
Ⅲ Abanto abwo nigo bagoteeba: Nase ki tokweng’ata, na aye Omonene tori korengereria amang’ana ayio? Nase ki togwekeyia, na aye tori korora ayio? Omonene nigo agoteeba: Rora rituko eri mokweng’ata nigo mokwerigeria aya akobagokia inwe abanyene, naende mwachanda abakori emeremo baino bonsi. Ⅳ Rora, inwe nigo mokweng’ata ase okwomana na okoamererania. Nigo mogoakana chinkundi chi’ogokora amabe. Okweng’ata kwaino buna okwe rituko ria reero nigo kogotanga ogosaba kwaino tikoigugwa igoro. Ⅴ Inee! Oko nakwo okweng’ata inche nganetie? Inee! Eye nero enchera endonge omonto agwekeyia? Ogwekumba omotwe buna risakwe, na kweboyia chigunia amo nokweaka ribu? Inee! Ayio naro mokoroka okweng’ata ne rituko rikomogwenera Omonene?
Ⅵ Okweng’ata inche nanchire noko: Mosibore chingataare chi’obobe; mosibore chinkini chi’amakorogoto, bae abachandegete ribaga bagende, na mobune kera rikorogoto. Ⅶ 58:7: Mat 25:34Mwatananere abare nenchara endagera yaino; abanto abataka batabwati chinka, mobarente ase chinyomba chiaino; ekero mokorora omonto ore getirianda, momoboyie. Timobaisa kwanga gokonya abare nomobere na amanyinga buna inwe.
Ⅷ Korakore igo, omobaso oo nobare buna ensakia ya mambia, na okogwenigwa kwao nkogoikere bwango; oboronge bwao mbogotang’ane, na obonene Omonene nabogotunyane korwa magega, bobe bokorendete. Ⅸ Erio norangerie, n’Omonene nakoiranerie, norere, nere nateebe iga: Nao inde aa.
Koratige kobekera abande amakorogoto, na kobaraga nekiara, na kobakwana bobe; Ⅹ korakonye abare nenchara na oe oyochandegete eki’aremeire, rirorio omobaso oo nobare ase omosunte, na obotuko bwao mbobe buna mobaso gati. Ⅺ Igo Omonene nakoraae kogenderera botambe, nagoisanekie n’ebinto ebiya, nakong’ie amauga ’omobere oo, naye obe buna omogondo ogoitororerwa amaache, na buna ensoko y’amaache etari gokama. Ⅻ Abanto bao mbaagache naende amatombe aria a kare, noagache amaroso ’ebiaare ebinge; aye norokwe “Omoroisia bw’aase atagogete”, na “Oyokoroisia chinchera chibe chinyia, erinde ense emenywe”.
Obuya bw’okorenda Esabato
ⅩⅢ “Koratange okogoro kwao ase rituko ri’Esabato na bwetange tokora amang’ana ao agokogokia ase rituko riane richenu; koraroke Esabato ebe omogooko oo, rituko riria richenu ri’Omonene rituko ri’ogosikwa; korarisike otari kobwatia chinchera chiao omonyene, gose kwerigeria aya agokogokia, na tokwana amang’ana atagwenereti, ⅩⅣ rirorio nonyore omogooko ase ’nde inche Omonene, nainche ningoetie ase ebitunwa bi’ense igoro, nkoe omwando o Yakobo, sokoro yao, oyorie.” Omonene nere okwanire amang’ana ayio korwa ase omonwa oye.
