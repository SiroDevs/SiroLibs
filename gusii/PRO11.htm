Ⅰ Egepimo*11:1 Egepimo: nkerokio naende gekorokwa “Ekerengero” ki’oborito ki’oborianania nigo kere okogechia ase Omonene, korende egi’ekeene nigo gekomogokia.
Ⅱ Ekero oboenenu bwachire, nobosooku nario bogocha, korende obong’aini nigo bokonyorekana ase abanto abaitongo.
Ⅲ Obwegenwa bw’abanyene oboronge nigo bokobaraa, korende obokorekanu bw’abatari abegenwa nigo bokobasiria.
Ⅳ Chinibo tichiri gokonya kende ase rituko ri’endamwamu, korende oboronge nigo boratoorie korwa ase amakweri.
Ⅴ Oboronge bw’omonto omoikeranu nigo borarongeyie chiinchera chiaye, korende omokori amabe, obobe bwaye ’mbomoiyerie.
Ⅵ Oboronge bw’abaanto bare ne chinkoro chindonge mbobatoorie, korende abasaria nigo barabwatwe n’okogania kwabo okobe.
Ⅶ Ekero omokori amabe agokwa, ogosemeria kwaye nigo kogokwa amo nere, ee, aya abwo batamoirogeti Nyasae basemeretie nigo agosira.
Ⅷ Omonyene oboronge nigo akorusigwa ase emechando, erio omokori amabe osoa ase emechando ribaga riaye.
Ⅸ Oyo otamoirogeti Nyasae nigo agosiria abanto bande n’amang’ana agokwana korwa ase omonwa oye, korende goetera ase obong’aini abanyene oboronge ’mbatoorigwe.
Ⅹ Ekero abanyene oboronge bageendererete koba buya, abanto b’omochie nigo bakogoka, na ekero abakori amabe bagosira nigo bagoaka amariogi y’omochengo.
Ⅺ Goetera ase ogosesenia kw’abanyene oboronge omochie nigo okoimokererigwa igoro, korende goetera ase chinkwana chi’abakori amabe nigo okoiyerigwa.
Ⅻ Omonto ogochaaya omonto onde, oyio nigo aborire obong’aini, korende omonto omong’aini nigo agokira‐kiri.
ⅩⅢ Omonto okona‐goeta‐etia obogenki nigo akoorokia obobisi, korende oyobwate omoika bw’obwegenwa tari koorokia obobisi.
ⅩⅣ Ase ogoseemia okuya gotari, egesaku nigo gekogwa, korende ase abasemia abange abaya bare, oborendi obuya ’mboreo.
ⅩⅤ Omonto okwerua koba intenenera ase omonto omoao nigo agwesoyia ase emechando, korende oyo okwanga koba intenenera nigo erendete buya.
ⅩⅥ Omokungu omwesiki nigo akogeenderera gosikwa botambe. Abanto abatindi nabwo bakobogoria chinibo ase chinguru.
ⅩⅦ Omonto ore n’amaabera nigo akwerentera amaya, korende omonto omotindi nigo agwechaanda ere omonyene.
ⅩⅧ Omokori amabe nigo akwenyorera eng’eria y’oborianania, korende oyo ogosimeka oboronge nigo akwenyorera eng’eria y’ekeene.
ⅩⅨ Omonto ore omwegenwa, ase oboronge bwaye nabe moyo, korende oyo okorigia obobe na kobobwatia nakwe.
ⅩⅩ Abanto abakorekanu ase chinkoro chiabo nigo bare okogechia ase Omonene, korende abare n’orogendo ororonge nigo bakomogokia.
ⅩⅪ Ekeene, Omonto omobe goika anyore egesusuro, korende oroiboro rw’abanyene oboronge ’ndotoorigwe.
ⅩⅫ Omokungu omonyakieni otari nechiseemi nigo anga buna ebonyi y’etaabu ere ase chimioro chi’embeche.
ⅩⅩⅢ Okogania kw’abanyene oboronge nigo gokorenta amaya, korende okw’abakori amabe nigo gokobarentera egesusuro.
ⅩⅩⅣ Ere omonto okorua ebinto ebinge, nigo akomenteranigwa ebinge goetania, na nere oria omogooko otari korua, nigo akogeenderera kona‐koremerwa.
ⅩⅩⅤ Omonto okorua n’omoyo bw’ogwancha natenenkigwe; nere oyokorua amaache agokendia erang’o, nere boigo ’naegwe amaache agokendia erang’o yaye.
ⅩⅩⅥ Omonto oria okwegimeria endagera ase obong’iti, nigo akoragererigwa nabaanto, korende ogosesenigwa nigo kore igoro ase omotwe bw’oria okoyeoonia.
ⅩⅩⅦ Omonto ogokora omokia korigia ayare amaya, oyio nigo akonyora ayare amaya, korende oyokorigia ay’obobe, obobe nigo bokomonyoora.
ⅩⅩⅧ Omonto osemeretie chinibo chiaye nigo aragwe, korende abanyene oboronge ’mbareerete buna amato amarere y’emete.
ⅩⅩⅨ Omonto okorenta emechando ase enyomba yaye nigo aranyore ayare buna embeo bosa; oyore omoriri nigo arabe omosomba bw’omonto omong’aini.
ⅩⅩⅩ Okwama kw’omonyaboronge nigo kore omote bw’obogima, nere oyokong’usa emioyo y’abanto n’omonto omong’aini.
ⅩⅩⅪ 11:31: 1 Petr 4:18Onye omonyene oboronge okonyora eng’eria yaye aiga ase ense, Inee, omokori amabe na omonyabibe, abwo tibakoegwa eng’eria yabo goetania?
