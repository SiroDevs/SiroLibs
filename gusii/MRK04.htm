Omobayeeno bw’omobusuri
(Mat 13:1-9; Luk 8:4-8)
Ⅰ 4:1: Luk 5:1-3Erio Yeso agachaaka kworokereria naende ase enyancha mbarabare, na omoganda omonene okamosangererekanera, goika ere akariina obwato, agaikaransa imeo igoro y’enyancha. Omoganda bwonsi nao orenge ase ense enyomo enyancha mbarabare. Ⅱ Akaborokereria amang’ana amange ase emebayeeno, akabateebia ase okworokereria kwaye, Ⅲ “Tegerera! Omobusuri akamanora kogenda kobusura. Ⅳ Agachia koba nkobusura are, chimbusuro chinde chikagwa ase enchera ensemo, chinyoni chigaacha chigachiria. Ⅴ Na echinde chikagwa aase egetare kere, aase amaroba are amake; chikamera bwango, ekiagera amaroba arenge amake. Ⅵ Omobaso ogachia kobara, chigasusana, na ekiagera chitarenge nemeri, chikooma. Ⅶ Na echinde chikagwa ase amagwa ime, na amagwa aria agakiina, agachimiga, tichiama amatunda. Ⅷ Na echinde chikagwa ase amaroba amaya, chikaama endagera, chikamera na gokina, na kwama; echinde emerongo etato, na echinde emerongo etano nomo, naende echinde rigana.” Ⅸ Akabora, “Oyore namato akoigwera tiga aigwe.”
Ekerenga ki’ogokwana ase emebayeeno
(Mat 13:10-17; Luk 8:9-10)
Ⅹ Ekero arenge bweka, abanto baria bamoetanaine amo nabaria ikomi na babere bakamobooria amang’ana y’emebayeeno eria. Ⅺ Yeso akabateebia, “Inwe mwaeirwe komanya obobisi bw’oborwoti bwa Nyasae, korende ase abanto bare isiko, amang’ana onsi nigo are emebayeeno ase bare, Ⅻ 4:12: Isaya 6:9,10erio okorigereria nabarigererie, ko tibakorora, na okoigwa nabaigwe, ko tibakomanya; tibacha koonchoka, baaberwe ebibe biabo.”
Ogotebereria kw’omobayeeno bw’omobusuri
(Mat 13:18-23; Luk 8:11-15)
ⅩⅢ Erio Yeso akabateebia, “Timomanyeti omobayeeno oyo? Gaki emebayeeno yonsi naki morayemanye? ⅩⅣ Omobusuri nering’ana akobusura. ⅩⅤ Abanto bande nigo bare buna embusuro eyio ekogwa enchera ensemo; na barabwo kobaikire koriigwa ring’ana, bwango Saitani ocha orusia ring’ana riria riabusuirwe ase emioyo yabo ime. ⅩⅥ Abanto bande nigo banga buna chimbusuro chiria chiaguete ase egetare, ekiagera kobaigure ring’ana riria, erio kegima baribwata nomogooko; ⅩⅦ na abwo tibari nemeri ime yabo abanyene, korende bagenderera ekero egeke, na ekero emechando gose okorosigwa bikooroka ase engencho ye ring’ana riria, bwango bagwa. ⅩⅧ Naende abande nabwo baria babusuirwe ase amagwa, aba nabwo bakoigwa ring’ana, ⅩⅨ naende okorengereria gw’ense na obong’ainereria bw’eniibo, na okogania kw’amang’ana ande kogosoa, biamiga ring’ana riria, riaba tirikwama. ⅩⅩ Naende aba nabwo babusuirwe ase amaroba amaya, abwo n’abanto baria bakoigwa ring’ana riria, na koribwata, na kwama okwama, oyomo emerongo etato, oyomo emerongo etano nomo, na oyonde rigana.”
Okworokereria korwa ase etaya na korwa ase embusuro
(Mat 13:31-35; Luk 8:16-18; 13:18-19)
ⅩⅪ 4:21: Mat 5:15; Luk 11:33Yeso akabateebia, “Inee! Etaya nigo ekorentwa erinde yamumekwa negetonga gose yabekwa nyaro y’oborere? Teri kobekwa igoro ase ekebekerero? ⅩⅫ 4:22: Mat 10:26; Luk 12:2Inaki ring’ana tiriri ria bobisi ritache koorokigwa, gose eribisegete ritache koorokigwa maiso marore. ⅩⅩⅢ Onye omonto ore n’amato akoigwera, tiga aigwe.” ⅩⅩⅣ 4:24: Mat 7:2; Luk 6:38Akabateebia, “Rigereria aya mokoigwa; na ekerengo keria mokorengera nakio morache korengerwa inwe, naende namomenterwe. ⅩⅩⅤ 4:25: Mat 13:12; 25:29; Luk 19:26Inaki oyobwate naegwe; korende oyotabwati naurwe nonya nekio abwate.”
Omobayeeno korwa ase embusuro egokina
ⅩⅩⅥ Erio Yeso akabora, “Oborwoti bwa Nyasae nigo bogwekaine nomonto obusurete embusuro ase riroba. ⅩⅩⅦ Akaba okoraara botuko na koboka, mobaso, na embusuro eyio ekamera na gokiina, ere atamanyeti buna egokoreka. ⅩⅩⅧ Inaki ense nigo ekwama enyene, ritang’ani rinyansi, naende ekegara, erio enyeke ase ekegara. ⅩⅩⅨ 4:29: Yoel 3:13Na endagera koyamire, omonto oyio bwango nigo akoira omoyio bwokogesa, ekiagera rigesa riaikire ang’e.”
Omobayeeno bw’ententere y’omoaruni
ⅩⅩⅩ Erio Yeso akagenderera koboora, “Ninki torabogwekanie oborwoti bwa Nyasae? Gose tobobeke ase mobayeeno ki? ⅩⅩⅪ Nigo bonga entetere y’omoaruni, ekero egosimekwa ase amaroba nenke kobua chimbusuro chionsi ase ense, ⅩⅩⅫ korende koyasimekirwe, yakiina, yaba enene kobua emete yonsi y’amato, yaba ne chinsagia chinene; goika chinyoni chia igoro chinyare gokenderera ase ekiriri kiaye.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Yeso akaranderia abanto ring’ana riaye ase emebayeeno emenge ere buna eye; nigo arenge gokwana ring’ana ase bare, koreng’ana buna baare konyara koigwa, ⅩⅩⅩⅣ gose taare kobateebia ring’ana otatiga ase omobayeeno, korende ekero arenge ensemo n’Aborokigwa baye nigo arenge kobateebia obobisi bwonsi.
Yeso ogotoereria omwaga
(Mat 8:23-27; Luk 8:22-25)
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Ase rituko riria, rigachia koba mogoroba, akabateebia, “Twamboke gochia ng’umbu.” ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Bagatiga omoganda, bakamoira amo nabarabwo, buna arenge, ase obwato boria. Na amaato ande narenge amo nere ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Ekembugu‐mbugu ekenene ki’omwaga gekaoroka, emerindo egaaka‐aka obwato goika amaache agachaaka goichora obwato ime. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Ere omonyene nigo arenge ase obwato ime magega oraire igoro ase omoito. Bakamotura, bakamoteebia, “Omworokia, tari gento ase ore gatogosira?” ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Yeso akaboka, agatogonyera omwaga, agateebia enyancha, “Kira, otoerere!” Omwaga okaera; gokaba ogotoerera okonene. ⅩⅬ Erio Yeso agateebia aborokigwa baye, “Inaki mokwoboa inwe? Timori nokwegena?” ⅩⅬⅠ Bagasogwa nomoondoko omonene, bagateebania, “Oyo ning’o rende, goika nonya nomwaga na enyancha bikomoigwera?”
