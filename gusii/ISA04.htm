Ⅰ Ase engaki eyio abakungu batano na babere mbabwate omosacha oyomo, mbeteebie: “Intwe abanyene ntwenyorere endagera tokoria ne chianga tokobeeka, korende tiga rioka torokwe abakungu bao, erinde otoruserie obosooku.”
Yerusalemu noiranerigwe amaya aye naende
Ⅱ Ase engaki eyio Omoseboka bw’Omonene noseboke ore omoseera na ore nobonene, na okwama gw’ense nkobe obonene na obosiani bw’Abaisraeli abaratooreke. Ⅲ Erio neebe ng’a kera omonto otigarete ase Sayoni, na ase Yerusalemu, narokwe omochenu, kera oyomo oyorikire ase egati y’abwo bare moyo ase Yerusalemu. Ⅳ Ase omoika bw’okonacha ekiina na gochena, Omonene nasibie obobe bw’abakuungu ba Sayoni, naende achene abanto ba Yerusalemu korwa ase okomocha kwabo kw’ogoitera amanyinga. Ⅴ 4:5: Okong’ 13:21; 24:16Igoro y’egetunwa gia Sayoni gionsi, na igoro y’abanto bonsi basangererekaneteo, Omonene natonge riire na erioki bibeo mobaso, na omorero okomesa obeo botuko; na igoro y’obonene obwo bwonsi egietubo gi’okorenda nkebekweo. Ⅵ Ekiriri ’nkebeo ki’ogokenderera korwa ase omobaso, naende kebe obotamero na oborendi bw’ogwekengera korwa ase embura y’omoubera.
