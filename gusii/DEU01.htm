OKOIRANERERA KW’AMACHIIKO
Ogochaakera
Egetabu eke nigo kebogoretie obosemia bwa Musa obwo aete Abaisraeli aase ense ya Moabu agwo ase batimokerete magega y’orogeendo orotambe korwa aase erooro gochia ense ya Kaanani. Ime y’obosemia obo, Musa akairanerera naende ase okobainyoria aria abateebetie ase amachiiko y’Omonene. Eke nakio gekogera egetabu eke kebwate erieta “Okoiranerera kwa Amachiiko.”
Aiga Musa naorogetie amakora amanene ase ebiro biria biemiaka emerongo ene ase erooro. Boigo, ere Musa nigo okobakuurera Abaisraeli bamotunyane Omonene na kobwatia amachiiko aye. Ase ogochika kw’eritang’ani, Musa akairorera engencho yokomosasiima Omonene bweka, boigo ase okoiranerera amachiiko ande, akabaa amorokererio ye chingeencho chi’Obomenyo bwabo ase ense eria ye chiira. Musa boigo ase okobainyoria engencho ye chiira chi’ogotianania amo n’Omonene, ere akabamanyia engencho y’ogochiiranerera na kwerua gochikorera chiira echio. Yoshua agatenenigwa koba omorai bwa Abaisraeli kobwatia Musa. Magega y’ogoteera kwogotogia amo n’omoyega bwokoiraneria ing’a mbuya mono ase obwegenwa bwa Nyasae, ebisaku bia Abaisraeli bionsi bigasesenigwa ase obomo, erio Musa agakwa ase ense eria ya Moabu moocha y’orooche rwa Yorodani.
Amorokererio amanene ase egetabu eke naria ing’a Omonene nigo agotooria na gosesenia abachoore baye abwo anchete. Nigo boigo bakoinyorigwa bagenderere komwancha na komoigwera Omonene erio ere agenderere boigo kobaa obogima na amasesenigwa.
Okobagwa kw’egetabu eke:
Ogokwana kw’eritang’ani kwa Musa 1:1—4:49
Ogokwana gwa kabere kwa Musa 5:1—26:19
a. Amachiiko aria ikomi 5:1—10:22
b. Amachiiko, na ogoteebereria, na ogokuurera 11:1—26:19
Ogoteebereria kw’ogosoa Kaanani 27:1—28:68
Okobwatana kw’Ogotianania gokoiranererigwa 29:1—30:20
Amang’ana ’omoerio a Musa 31:1—33:29
Ogokwa kwa Musa 34:1-12
Ⅰ
Obweng’e bw’Egetabu eke
Ⅰ Egetabu eke nigo kebwate amang’ana Musa akwanete n’Abaisraeli ekero barenge ase erooro, moocha y’orooche rwa Yorodani. Nigo barenge ase Ekerubo Ekiomo*1:1 Ekerubo Ekiomo: engencho yaye ase aomo ime ye enyancha embariri atari komera kende kerigereretie Sufu, obogati bw’omochie o Parani ase ensemo eyemo, na emechie ya Tofeli na Labani, na Haseroti, na Disahabu ase ensemo ende. Ⅱ Nigo rore orogendo rwa amatuko ikomi na rimo korwa ase egetunwa kia Horebu goetera ase ense y’ebitunwa bia Edomu goika Kadeshi‐Barinea. Ⅲ Ase rituko rie ritang’ani ri’omotienyi o ikomi nomo, omwaka bw’emerongo ene, korwa barwete Misiri, Musa akabateebia amang’ana onsi ayi’Omonene amochiigete abateebie. Ⅳ 1:4: Omob 21:21-35Amang’ana aya nigo abeete ekero Omonene konya abuire Sihoni, omorwoti bw’Abaamori, oyio ogambete ase omochie o Hesiboni, amo n’omorwoti Ogi o Basani, oyio ogambete ase emechie ya Asitaroti na Edirei. Ⅴ Nigo yarenge ekero abanto barenge ase ense ya Moabu, moocha y’orooche rwa Yorodani, Musa agachaaka kworokereria na kwegeria amachiiko a Nyasae. Ogoteeba, Ⅵ Ekero twarenge ase egetunwa gia Sinai, Omonene, Nyasae oito, agakwana naintwe, agatoteebia, “Mwabereire aiga ase egetunwa eke ase egeka geisaine. Ⅶ Tagora ebigutu imoka mochaake orogeendo rwaino. Genda gochia ase ense y’ebitunwa y’Abaamori, na ase abanto b’echinse chietanaineo-gochia ase Ekerubo kia Jorodani, na ase ense y’ebitunwa, na ase ense omweya, na ase ense ere ang’e n’Enyancha Enene. Mogende ase ense y’ Abakaanani gochia ase egetunwa kia Lebanoni, na goikera ase orooche roria oronene rw’Eufarati. Ⅷ Eyio yonsi n’ense inche Omonene nariete eira koa chisokoro chiaino Aburaamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo, chisokoro chiaino, amo n’abanto b’ororeeria rwabo. Genda moyeire.”
Musa ogochoora abagaambi
(Okong’ 18:13-27)
Ⅸ Musa agakwana n’abanto, Ase engaki eria twarenge egetunwa gia Sinai nigo nabateebetie ng’a inche bweka tinkonyaara kobaraa. Ⅹ Omonene Nyasae oino, obamentekanirie mono, na bono inwe nigo more abange mono buna ching’enang’eni chia igoro. Ⅺ Tiga Omonene, Nyasae bw’echisokoro chiaino, abamentekanie ara elifu, mobe abange goetania buna more bono, naende abasesenie buna ariete eira. Ⅻ Korende naki ndakore inyare inche bweka kobogoria egurube enene y’okogaayana emechando yaino ⅩⅢ chora abanto abang’aini, abare abamanyi, abanto bamanyekanete buya korwa ase kera egesaku, na inche nimbabeeke babe abataang’ani baino.1:13 Okong’ 18:21-22 ⅩⅣ Inwe nigo mwancheranete buna ayio namaya na buna moyakore. ⅩⅤ Ase ayio nkabeeka abanto baria abang’aini na bamanyekanete buya, abwo mwachorete korwa ase ebisaku biaino nkababeeka koba abaraai baino. Abande ase chilifu, na abande ase amagana, na abande ase emerongo etano, na abande ase ikomi. Naboigo nkababeeka abateneneri bande b’ebisaku biaino bionsi.
ⅩⅥ Ase engaki eria ngachiika abagaambi baino, nkabateebia: Motegerere ebiina bire ase egati y’Abaisraeli bamino. Monache ekiina kia boronge, keraabe ase egati y’omonto n’oyomwabo, gose ase egati yaye n’omomenyi omenyete bwoye. ⅩⅦ Timoba n’ogwanchereria komokonacha ebiina; mokore boronge, abe omonto omoke gose omonene. Timoiroka omonto onde bwensi, ekiagera okonacha mogokora nigo gokorwa ase Nyasae. Onye kiina kende gionsi kere egekong’u ase more, mokendetere, na inche ninkenache. ⅩⅧ Naboigo ase engaki eria nabachiigete igoro y’amang’ana onsi mogwenerete gokora.
Abanto chirooti bagatomwa korwa ase Kadeshi‐Barinea
(Omob 13:1-33)
ⅩⅨ Intwe togakora buna Omonene, Nyasae oito, atochiigete. Tokarua ase egetunwa gia Sinai, tokageenda, togaeta ase egati y’erooro enene ekwoboyia mono, eria mwarooche ase ense y’ebitunwa bia Abaamori; erio togaika Kadeshi‐Barinea. ⅩⅩ ’Nkabateebia: Bono mwaikire ase ense y’ebitunwa y’Abaamori, eyi’Omonene, Nyasae oito, agochia gotoa. ⅩⅪ Rigereria, Omonene Nyasae oino, obaeire ense eria. Genda moyeire ebe eyaino, buna Omonene, Nyasae bw’echisokoro chiaino, abateebirie. Timoiroka, gose timokwa emioyo.
ⅩⅫ Erio inwe mwensi mogaacha ase ’nde, moganteebia: Totome abanto bagende koroota ense eria, erinde batorentere amang’ana buna ense eria ere, barore enchera togochia goetera, naende emechie eria togochia koira, buna ere.
ⅩⅩⅢ Amang’ana ayio akarorekana ase ’nde ng’a namaya, na inche ngachoora abanto ikomi na babere, oyomo korwa ase kera egesaku. ⅩⅩⅣ Abanto abwo bakageenda gochia ase ense eria y’ebitunwa, bagaika ase ense omweya y’Esikoli, bakaroota ense eria. ⅩⅩⅤ Bakanyora okwama gw’ense eria, bagakorenta ase tore, naende bagatorentera amang’ana aya barooche agwo ase ense eria, bagakwana: Ense eri’Omonene Nyasae oito, agochia gotoa nigo ere entobu mono.
ⅩⅩⅥ 1:26: Okoir 9:23; Abaibr 3:16Korende timwanchete kogeenda ase ense eria, mokaanga koigwera ogochika Omonene Nyasae oino, abaete. ⅩⅩⅦ Mokemurungania ekero mwamenyete ase chiema chiaino, mogateeba: Omonene nigo atogeechete, nakio kiagerete agatorusia korwa ase ense ya Misiri, erinde atobeeke ase amaboko ya Abaamori, nabarabwo batoite na gotosiria.
ⅩⅩⅧ Nase ki toragende aroro? Abaminto batwoboirie, ekiagera bateebire ng’a abanto bare ase ense eria n’abanene naende n’abatambe gotobua. Emechie yabo n’emenene, naende nigo egiteire chindwaki chigete igoro. Naende ntwarora amamonto aroro.
ⅩⅩⅨ Korende nkabateebia: Timoiroka abanto baria. ⅩⅩⅩ Omonene Nyasae oino, oria okobaraa, nere orabarwanere esegi buna mwarooche ay’abakorerete ekero mwarenge Misiri, ⅩⅩⅪ 1:31: Ogok 13:18naende ekero mwarenge ase erooro. ’Mwarooche buna abaraaete, na ase chinchera chionsi echio mwaetete goika mogaika aase aa: ere nigo ababogoretie buna omonto akobogoria omwana oye. ⅩⅩⅫ 1:32: Abaibr 3:19Korende nonya okorete ayio onsi, timwegenete Omonene, ⅩⅩⅩⅢ nonya oyorenge kobatang’ana botambe na kobarigeria aase mware gochia gotoora. Ase obotuko nigo arenge kobaraa n’omorero, na ase omobaso obaraa ne riire, koborokia aase mware goetera.
Omonene okoa Abaisraeli egesusuro
(Omob 14:20-35)
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ 1:34: Abaibr 3:18Omonene akaigwa okwemurungania kwaino, akaba n’endamwamu, agatiana na goteeba: ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Ekeene, onde tari ase abanto b’ekegori eke ekebe oraike ase ense eria entobu narierete chisokoro chiaino eira ng’a nimbayee, ⅩⅩⅩⅥ otatiga Kalebu mosinto o Yefune. Ere nasoe ense eria. Ere obeire omwegenwa ase inde nimoyee ere na ororeeria rw’aye, ense eyio arotire. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Ase engencho yaino Omonene nigo abeete n’endamwamu ase inde agateeba “Nonya naye Musa tokonyaara gosoa ase ense eria.” ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Korende Yoshua, mosinto o Nuni, omokonyi oo, nere orasoe aroro. Mobeeke chinguru, omoremie, ekiagera nere oraraae Abaisraeli bagende koira ense eria.
ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Naende Omonene agatoteebia twensi: Abana baino abake abwo mwateebete ng’a ’nabooswe, abwo ase rituko ria reero bataramanya ayaare amabe gose amaya, nabwo baraike ase ense eria, inche nimbayee ebe eyabo. ⅩⅬ Korende inwe onchoka, mogende gochia ase erooro, gochia ase enchera ekogeenda ase omosomo bwa Akaba.
ⅩⅬⅠ Erio mokang’iraneria moganteebia: Musa twakorire ebibe ase Omonene, korende bono ’nkogeenda tore korwana esegi buna Omonene Nyasae oito, atochiigete. Kera omonto ase more akabogoria ebirwanero biaye bi’eseegi, akarengereria ng’a namang’ana amagusu kogeenda koira ense eria y’ebitunwa.
ⅩⅬⅡ Omonene aganteebia: Bakanie, obateebie ng’a tibaabaisa kogeenda korwana esegi, ekiagera inche Omonene tindi amo nabarabwo. Kobaragende nigo bagochia kobugwa n’ababisa baabo. ⅩⅬⅢ ’Nkabateebia amang’ana ayi’Omonene akwanete, korende mokaanga koigwera, mogasaria amang’ana Omonene abachiigete, mokageenda n’obomwenu†1:43 obomwenu: nakwo ogoteeba, oboenenu. ase ense eria y’ebitunwa. ⅩⅬⅣ Abaamori abamenyete ase ense eria bagaacha bakabaumokera buna chinchoke. Bakabaseria goika Horima erio bakababua agwo ase ense y’ebitunwa ya Edomu. ⅩⅬⅤ Erio inwe, mokarera ase Omonene abakoonye, korende Omonene taigwete ekerero kiaino gose tabategererete.
Emiaka Abaisraeli bamenyete ase erooro
ⅩⅬⅥ Ekio gekagera mokabeera agwo Kadeshi ase amatuko amaange.
