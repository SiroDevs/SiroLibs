Yeso okwegeria igoro y’ogotigana
(Mar 10:1-12; Luk 16:18)
Ⅰ Ekero Yeso achia gokoora gokwana amang’ana ayio, akarua Galili, agasoa ensemo ya Boyahudi ng’umbu ya Yorodani; Ⅱ na emeganda emenene ekamotunyana, akabagwenia aroro.
Ⅲ Abafarisai bakamochiera, bakamoteema ase okomoboria, “Inee! Mboronge omonto atige mok’aye ase ring’ana rinde rionsi?” Ⅳ 19:4: Omoch 1:27; 5:2Akabairaneria, “Timwana gosoma buna ere oyobatongete korwa omochakano, nere obatongete omosacha na omokungu, Ⅴ 19:5: Omoch 2:24agateeba: Ase engencho eyio omonto natige ise na ng’ina, abwatane na mok’aye, na abwo babere nigo barabe omobere oyomo? Ⅵ Ase igo tibakoba babere naende, korende omobere oyomo. Naboigo, ebi Nyasae abwatanirie, Mwanyabaanto tabiatanana.” Ⅶ 19:7: Okoir 24:1-4; Mat 5:31Bakamobooria, “Nase ki rende Musa achiigete komoa omokungu riruube ri’ogotigana, na komotiga?” Ⅷ Akabateebia, “Nase engencho y’obokong’u bwe chinkoro chiaino Musa abaete ribaga gotiga abakungu baino, korende ayio tarenge bo korwa omochakano. Ⅸ 19:9: Mat 5:32; 1 Abakr 7:10,11Korende inche nabateebirie, oyoratige mok’aye, otatiga ase engencho y’obotomani, na konyuoma oyonde, oyio otomanire; naende oy’oranyuome omokungu oyotigirwe, otomanire.”
Ⅹ Aborokigwa bakamoteebia, “Amang’ana igoro y’omonto na mok’aye k’arabe engencho eye, tegwenereti konywoma.” Ⅺ Korende akabateebia, “Tari bonsi baranyare gwancha ring’ana eri, otatiga abwo baeirwe. Ⅻ Inaki mbare abagomba abaiboretwe bo, naende mbare abagomba aba abanto bagombaetie, naende mbare abagomba abekorete abanyene koba abagomba ase engencho y’oborwoti bwa igoro. Oyoranyare gwancha ring’ana eri, tiga arianche.”
Yeso ogosesenia abana abake
(Mar 10:13-16; Luk 18:15-17)
ⅩⅢ Erio abana abake bakarentwa ase Yeso, erinde ababeekere amaboko aye na kobasabera. Aborokigwa baye bagatanga abanto, ⅩⅣ korende Yeso agateeba, “Batige abana bache ase ’nde, timobaisa kobatanga, ekiagera abare buna aba, oborwoti bwa igoro n’obwabo.” ⅩⅤ Akababeekera amaboko aye, akaimoka korwa aroro, akagenda.
Omomura omonda
(Mar 10:17-22; Luk 18:18-23)
ⅩⅥ Na rora, omonto oyomo akamochiera, akamobooria, “Omworokia ’mboseera ki ndakore ’nyore obogima bwa kare na kare?” ⅩⅦ Akamoteebia, “Nase ki okomboria ninki kere ekiya? Nareo oyomo bweka oyore omuya. Onye korigetie gosoa ase obogima, bwata amachiiko.” ⅩⅧ 19:18: a Okong’ 20:13; Okoir 5:17; b Okong’ 20:14; Okoir 5:18; c Okong’ 20:15; Okoir 5:19; d Okong’ 20:16; Okoir 5:20Akamoboria, “Ayare buna arari?” Yeso akamoiraneria, “Toita, Totomana, Toiba, Toteba oborimo, ⅩⅨ 19:19: a Okong’ 20:12; Okoir 5:16; b Abal 19:18sika iso na nyoko; naende: Ancha omogisangio oo buna bweanchete19:19 Abal 19:18; Mar 12:33; Abar 13:9; Yakb 2:8.” ⅩⅩ Omomura oria akamoteebia, “Ayio onsi nayabwatire; ing’ana ki rimochete?” ⅩⅪ Yeso akamoteebia, “Onye korigetie koba omoikeranu, gachie, oonie ebio ore nabio bionsi, oe abataka, na aye n’obe neniibo igoro; erio oche ontunyane.” ⅩⅫ Ekero achia koigwa ring’ana riria, omomura oria akagenda nobororo, ekiagera arenge neniibo enyinge.
ⅩⅩⅢ Erio Yeso agateebia aborokigwa baye, “Nekeene nabateebirie: Nabe aakong’u ase omonto omonda gosoa ase oborwoti bwa igoro. ⅩⅩⅣ Naende nabateebirie inwe, naororo engamia goeta ase ekebao gi’esindani, kobua omonto omonda gosoa ase oborwoti bwa Nyasae.” ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero aborokigwa bachia koigwa aya, bagakumia mono, bagateeba, “Ning’o rende oranyare gotooreka?” ⅩⅩⅥ Korende Yeso akabarigereria, akabateebia, “Aya takonyarekana ase Mwanyabaanto, korende ase Nyasae onsi nigo akonyarekana.” ⅩⅩⅦ Erio Petero akamoiraneria, “Rora, intwe twatigire bionsi, twagotunyanire, inki rende toranyore?” ⅩⅩⅧ 19:28: a Mat 25:31; b Luk 22:30Yeso akabateebia, “Nekeene nabateebirie: Ase ense enyia, ekero Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto agochi‐gocha gaikaransete ase ekerogo kiaye ki’obonene, inwe abamwantunyanire inamoche goikaransera ebirogo bi’obonene ikomi na bibere, mokogaambera ebisaku ikomi na bibere bi’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅩⅨ Na onde bwensi otigete chinyomba, gose bamura bamwabo, gose basubati bamwabo, gose ise, gose ng’ina, gose abana, gose emegondo ase engencho y’erieta riane, namenteranigwe ara rigana, na obogima bwa kare na kare nabonyore. ⅩⅩⅩ 19:30: Mat 20:16; Luk 13:30Korende abange abare ab’eritang’ani inababe abamagega, na abamagega inababe ab’eritang’ani.
