ⅩⅣ
Ogokora gwa Daudi ase Yerusalemu
(2 Sam 5:11-16)
Ⅰ Hiramu, omorwoti o Turo, agatoma abatomwa ase Daudi, akamotomera ebingese bi’emete y’emecharake na abaagacheri amagena, na abasenia be chibao, erinde baagachere Daudi enyomba. Ⅱ Erio Daudi akamanya ng’a Omonene omokong’irie ase okoba omorwoti bw’Abaisraeli, na omoimokereirie igoro ase engencho y’abanto baye Abaisraeli.
Ⅲ Daudi akanyuoma abakungu bande agwo Yerusalemu, na abwo bakamoiborera abana abange, abamura na abaiseke. Ⅳ Aya naro amarieta ’abana baiboretwe asare agwo Yerusalemu: Samua na Sobabu, Nathani na Sulemani, Ⅴ Ibuhari na Eliasama, na Elifeleti, Ⅵ Noga na Nefegi, na Yafia, Ⅶ Elisama na Beliada, na Elifeleti.
Abafirisiti bakobugwa
(2 Sam 5:17-25)
Ⅷ Ekero Abafirisiti baigwete ng’a Daudi oakirwe amaguta obeire omorwoti igoro ase Abaisraeli bonsi, bagatiira gochia komorigia. Daudi akaigwa ayio, akagenda koumerana nabarabwo ase okorwana esegi. Ⅸ Abafirisiti bagacha gosakora agwo ase ense omweya*14:9 omweya: engencho yaye se arengaine y’Abarefai. Ⅹ Na Daudi akaboria Nyasae “Inee! Ndagenda korwania Abafirisiti?” Nobabeeke ase amaboko aane gose?
Omonene akamoiraneria, “Ee, genda, nimbabeeke ase amaboko ao.”
Ⅺ Daudi akagenda goika Baali‐Perasimu, akababua agwo. Erio akabora, “Nyasae okoroorire ababisa baane goetera ase okoboko kwane, buna chiooro chi’amaache are nemerindo chigokoroora.” Ase ayio bakaroka aase aria Baali‐Perasimu. Ⅻ Abafirisiti bagatiga chinyasae chiabo aroro, na Daudi agachiika abanto, bachisambe nomorero.
ⅩⅢ Naende Abafirisiti bakagenda aroro eria kabere bagasakora. ⅩⅣ Ekero Daudi aboretie Nyasae naende, Nyasae akamoiraneria, “Tobarwania esegi korwa aa, korende oetanane ogende obatubokere korwa magega ase emete ekorokwa balisamu ere. ⅩⅤ Na ekero oraigwe ebititiro bigoeta ase emete eyio igoro, erio ogende korwana esegi; omanye ng’a Nyasae ogotang’aneire kobua abarwani b’Abafilisti.” ⅩⅥ Daudi agakora buna Nyasae amochigeete, na barabwo bagaita abarwani b’Abafilisti korwa Gibeoni goikera Geseri. ⅩⅦ Chinkuma chia Daudi chigasarera ase chinse chionsi; n’Omonene akarentera abanto b’ebisaku bionsi obwoba, bakamoiroka.
