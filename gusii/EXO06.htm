Ⅰ Eri’Omonene agateebia Musa, “Bono norore aya ndakorere Farao. Naki ase okoboko kwane gwe chinguru nimobetererie abae abanto baane ribaga bagende, ee, ase okoboko kwane gwe chinguru nabetererigwe abaserie korwa ase ense yaye.”
Okorangerigwa kwa Musa naende
Ⅱ 6:2: Omoch 17:1; 28:3; 35:11; Okong’ 3:13-15Nyasae agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, “Inche ninche Omonene. Ⅲ Naorokerete Aburaamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo buna Nyasae Omobui. Korende ase erieta riane Yahwe, gose Omonene, tinamanyekanete ase bare. Ⅳ Naende ninche nakorete okobwatana kw’ogotianania nabarabwo ng’a nimbae ense ya Kaanani, ense eria barenge buna abamenyi. Ⅴ Na bono naigure okwerera kw’Abaisraeli abwo Abamisiri babegete inse y’obosomba, naende ning’inyorete okobwatana koria kwane. Ⅵ Ase igo oteebie Abaisraeli ng’a, Inche ninche Omonene, ndabarusie korwa ase emeremo emerito inse y’Abamisiri. Nimbatoorie korwa ase obosomba bwaino. Ase okoboko kwane koramboire, na goetera ase ebisusuro ebinene, nimbaboorie. Ⅶ Nimbakore mobe abanto baane, na inche nimbe Nyasae oino. Inwe namomanye ng’a ninche Omonene, Nyasae oino, oyobarusirie korwa ase emeremo emerito inse y’Abamisiri. Ⅷ Nimbaire goika ense eria nariete eira koa Aburaamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo. Nimbayee ebe omwando oino. Inche ninche Omonene.”
Ⅸ Musa agateebia Abaisraeli amang’ana ayio, korende barabwo tibancha gotegerera, ekiagera konya emioyo yabo y akuure, ase engencho y’emeremo y’obosomba obotindi.
Ⅹ Eri’Omonene agakwana na Musa akamoteebia, Ⅺ “Genda okwane na Farao, omorwoti o Misiri, omoteebie abae Abaisraeli ribaga bagende, barue ase ense yaye.”
Ⅻ Musa agakwana n’Omonene, akamoteebia. “Rora Abaisraeli baangire konyigwera, naki rende Farao ere arakore anyigwere, inche omonto ntamanyeti gokwana.?”
ⅩⅢ Korende Omonene agakwana na Musa na Aroni, akabachika bagende bateebie Abaisraeli na Farao, omorwoti o Misiri, ng’a batomirwe barusie Abaisraeli korwa ase ense ya Misiri.
Omoroberio bw’ororeria rwa Musa na Aroni
ⅩⅣ Abanto aba nabwo barenge emetwe ye chiamate chiabo. Abana abamura ba Rubeni, omomura omotangi o Israeli, nabwo aba: Hanoki, na Palu, na Hezironi, na Karimi. Aba nabwo chiamate chia Rubeni. ⅩⅤ Abana abamura ba Simioni nabwo aba: Yemueli, na Yamini, na Ohadi, na Yakini, na Sohari, na Saulo, omwana bw’omokuungu Omokanani. Aba nabwo chiamate chia Simioni. ⅩⅥ 6:16: Omob 3:17-20; 26:57,58; 1 Amg 6:16-19Aya naro amarieta ’abamura ba Lawi, buna batunyaine ase okoiborwa kwabo: Gerisoni, na Kohati, na Merari. Lawi nigo akoorete emiaka rigana erimo, na emerongo etato, na etano na ebere (137). ⅩⅦ Abana abamura ba Gerisoni nabwo Libini na Simei, ne chiamate chiabo. ⅩⅧ Na abana abamura ba Kohati nabwo aba: Amuramu, na Isari, na Heburoni, na Usieli. Kohati nigo akoorete emiaka rigana erimo, na emerongo etato, na etato (133). ⅩⅨ Na abana abamura ba Merari nabwo Mali na Musi. Aba nabwo chiamate chia Lawi, buna batunyaine ase okoiborwa kwabo.
ⅩⅩ Amuramu akanyuoma Yokebedi, omoiseke omwabo ise; na ere akamoiborera Aroni na Musa. Amuramu nigo akoorete emiaka rigana erimo, na emerongo etato, na etano na ebere (137). ⅩⅪ Na abana abamura ba Isari nabwo aba: Kora, na Nefegi, na Sikiri. ⅩⅫ Na abana abamura ba Usieli nabwo aba: Mishaeli na Elisafani, na Sitiri.
ⅩⅩⅢ Aroni akanyuoma Elisheba, omoiseke bw’Aminadabu, omoiseke omwabo Nashoni. Ere akamoiborera Nadabu, na Abihu, na Eleazari, na Itamari. ⅩⅩⅣ Na abana abamura ba Kora nabwo aba: Asiri, na Elikana, na Abiasafu. Aba nabwo chiamate chia Kora. ⅩⅩⅤ Eleazari, omwana o Aroni, akanyuoma omoiseke oyomo bw’abaiseke ba Putieli, na ere akamoiborera Finehasi. Abanto aba nabwo barenge emetwe ye chinyomba chi’Abalawi, eamate ase eamate.
ⅩⅩⅥ Aroni amo na Musa nabwo bari’Omonene atebeetie, “Barusie Abaisraeli ase ense ya Misiri, omoganda ase omoganda.” ⅩⅩⅦ Aba nabwo baria Musa na Aroni bateebeetie Farao ng’a barusie Abaisraeli korwa Misiri.
Omonene ogochika Musa na Aroni
ⅩⅩⅧ Rituko riri’Omonene akwanete na Musa ase ense ya Misiri ⅩⅩⅨ akamoteebia, “Inche ninche Omonene. Otebie Farao, omorwoti o Misiri, amang’ana onsi nkogoteebia.” ⅩⅩⅩ Korende Musa akairaneri’Omonene akamoteebia, “Inche nomonto ntamanyeti gokwana, naki rende Farao arakore anyigwere?”
