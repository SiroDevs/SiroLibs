Esanduki y’Okobwatana ekoirwa Yerusalemu
Ⅰ Daudi akeagachera chinyomba agwo ase Omochie o Daudi. Akaroisia aase ase Esanduki ya Nyasae yare koganchwa, naende akayeteneneria eema aroro. Ⅱ 15:2: Okoir 10:8Erio Daudi akabora, “Onde takobogoria Esanduki eria ya Nyasae otatiga Abalawi boka, ekiagera Omonene nigo achorete Abalawi babe bakobogoria Esanduki y’Omonene, naende bamokorere botambe.” Ⅲ Daudi agasangereria Abaisraeli bonsi agwo Yerusalemu gochia korenta Esanduki eria y’Omonene gocha aase aye ayeroiseretie. Ⅳ Naende agasangereria amo abanto b’ororeria rwa Aroni na Abalawi. Ⅴ Korwa ase eamate ya Kohati: Urieli, omotang’ani obo, amo nabamwabo rigana erimo, na emerongo ebere (120); Ⅵ korwa ase eamate ya Merari: Asaia, omotang’ani obo, amo nabamwabo amagana abere, na emerongo ebere (220); Ⅶ korwa ase eamate ya Gerisomu: Yoel, omotang’ani obo, amo nabamwabo rigana erimo, na emerongo etato (130); Ⅷ korwa ase eamate y’Elisafani: Semaya, omotang’ani obo, amo nabamwabo amagana abere (200); Ⅸ korwa ase eamate ya Heburoni Elieli, omotang’ani obo, amo nabamwabo emerongo etano na etato (80); Ⅹ korwa ase eamate ya Usieli: Aminadabu, omotang’ani obo, amo nabamwabo rigana erimo, na ikomi na babere (112).
Ⅺ Erio Daudi akarangeria abakuani Zadoki na Abiatari, naende Abalawi: Urieli na Asaia, Yoel na Semaya, Elieli na Aminadabu, Ⅻ akabateebia, “Inwe nabateneneri be chiamate chi’Abalawi, echene inwe abanyene amo nabaanto bamino, mochie kwoyia Esanduki eria y’Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli, moyebeke aase agwo inche nayeroiseirie. ⅩⅢ Ekiagera inwe timwayebogoretie engaki eria entang’ani, Omonene, Nyasae oito, atoete egesusuro; titwamorigetie buna atochigeete.”
ⅩⅣ Ase ayio abakuani na Abalawi bagechena abanyene, erinde bachie korenta Esanduki eria y’Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅤ 15:15: Okong’ 25:14Abalawi bakabogoria Esanduki eria ya Nyasae nemiaro igoro ase amareko abo, buna Musa abachigete koreng’ana ne ring’ana ri’Omonene.
ⅩⅥ Boigo Daudi agachiika abatang’ani b’Abalawi bachore abanto bamwabo babe abateri, baranyare kobugia ebinto bigoterera ase amariogi, ababugia amakano, na amakano ’ebirigisa, ne chisani chigoakanigwa na kwoga ase ogoteera nomogooko. ⅩⅦ Ase igo Abalawi bagachora Hemani, mosinto o Yoel, na ase abanto bamwabo Asafu, mosinto o Berekia, na ase ororeria rwa Merari abanto bamwabo Etani, mosinto o Kusaya.
ⅩⅧ Ase okobakonya baria bachorire abanto bamwabo b’omooroberio o kabere: Zakaria na Yasieli, Semiramoti na Yeieli, Uni na Eliabu, Benaya na Maaseya.
Naende bagachora Matitia na Elifelehu, na Mikineya, na abarendi b’egeita Obedi‐Edomu na Yeieli.
ⅩⅨ Abateri baria Hemani na Asafu, na Etani babe bakobugia chisani chi’etai chikobugigwa na kwoga.
ⅩⅩ Zakaria na Yasieli, Semiramoti na Yehieli, Uni na Eliabu, Maaseya na Benaya, aba babe bakobugia amakano koreng’ana buna amariogi a igoro ay’Alamoti.*15:20 Alamoti: ring’ana rigwatora eriogi ri’ogoteera
ⅩⅪ Korende Matitia na Elifelehu, Mikineya na Obedi‐Edomu, Yeieli na Asasia babe abaraai ase okobugia amakano koreng’ana buna amariogi a inse a Seminiti.†15:21 Seminiti: ring’ana rigwatora eriogi ri’ogoteera
ⅩⅫ Kenania, omoraai bw’Abalawi ase amatera, abe omoteria, ekiagera namanyete emeremo eyio. ⅩⅩⅢ Berekia na Elikana babe abarendi b’egeita ase Esanduki eria y’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅣ Sebania na Yehosafati, Netaneli na Amasai, Zakaria na Benaya, na Eliezeri, abakuani, babe bakobugia ebitureeri bosio bw’Esanduki eria ya Nyasae. Obedi‐Edomu na Yehia barabwo babe abarendi b’egeita ase Esanduki eria.
Esanduki y’Okobwatana ekorentwa Yerusalemu
ⅩⅩⅤ Ase ayio Daudi, na abagaaka b’Abaisraeli, na abatang’ani b’abarwani chilifu bakagenda kwoyia Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana kw’Omonene, korwa ase enyomba y’Obedi‐Edomu, bagachenga nomogooko omonene mono. ⅩⅩⅥ Ekiagera Nyasae akonyete Abalawi baria babogoretie Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana kw’Omonene, bakarua ebing’wanso bie chieri isano na ibere, ne chimingichi isano na ibere. ⅩⅩⅦ Daudi akabeeka eanga engiya y’egatani, boigo n’Abalawi baria bonsi babogoretie Esanduki eria bakabeeka, na abateri boigo, na Kenania, omoteria bw’abateri. Daudi akabeeka negesibao ki’egatani. ⅩⅩⅧ Ase ayio Abaisraeli bonsi bakarenta Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana kw’Omonene. Bagaaka amariogi ’omochengo, bakabugia chingoma na eirombeta, ne chisani chigoakanigwa chikwoga, naende bakabugia mono namakano, na amakano ’ebirigisa.
ⅩⅩⅨ Ekaba ekero Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana kw’Omonene yare korentwa ase Omochie o Daudi, Mikali, mosubati o Saulo, akarigereria korwa ase ekebao, akarora Daudi omorwoti ogocharoka‐charoka na gotenga ore nomogooko, akamochaya ase omoyo oye ime.
