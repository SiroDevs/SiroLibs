Ogochandwa na ogoserebanigwa kw’Abakristo
Ⅰ Ase rituko riria emechando emenene egaacha igoro ase ekanisa ya Yerusalemu; bonsi bagaserebanigwa gochia chinse chia Boyahudi na Samaria, otatiga abatomwa bataserebaanetigwe. Ⅱ Abanto abeegeni bagatindeka Stefano, bakamoreera okorera okonene. Ⅲ 8:3: Ogok 22:4,5; 26:9-11Korende Saulo agasaria ekanisa, agasoa kera enyomba akaba ogokurura abasacha na abakungu, na kobaira ase echela.
Filipo ase Samaria
Ⅳ Na baria baserebaanetigwe bakagenda chinsemo ne chinsemo, bakarandia ring’ana ria Nyasae. Ⅴ Filipo agatirimboka, agasoa omochie o Samaria, akabaranderia Kristo. Ⅵ Emeganda yonsi ase omoyo oyomo bagategerera amang’ana onsi Filipo are gokwana, ekero bamoigwete na korora ebikone ebiarenge gokora. Ⅶ Ekiagera abange babwatire nebirecha bi’eubi, na ebirecha ebi bikarwa ase bare, bikarera ase egeturi ekenene; abanto abarenge amagata, na baria barenge ebirema bakagwenigwa. Ⅷ Ase igo omogooko omonene okaba ase omochie oria.
Simioni omoorogi
Ⅸ Omonto oyomo ore korokwa Simioni, agwo ritang’ani nigo arenge gokora oborogi ase omochie oria; abanto ba Samaria bakamokumia, ekero are goteeba ng’a ere omonyene nomonto omonene. Ⅹ Bonsi, abake na abanene, bakamotegerera, bagateeba: “Omonto oyo nigo are okobua koria kwa Nyasae gokorokwa Okobua Okonene.” Ⅺ Bakamotegerera, ekiagera ase amatuko amange nigo barenge gokumia ase oborogi bwaye. Ⅻ Korende ekero bachia kwegena Filipo, gakorandia amang’ana amaya igoro y’oborwoti bwa Nyasae na erieta ria Yeso Kristo, bakabatiswa, abasacha na abakungu. ⅩⅢ Nonya n’Simioni omonyene akeegena, na magega kabatisirwe akagenderera koba amo na Filipo. Ekero aroche ebikone na ogokora okonene gwe chinguru biakoretwe, agakumia.
Petero na Yohana ase Samaria
ⅩⅣ Na ekero abatomwa ba Yerusalemu bachia koigwa ng’a Abasamaria banchire ring’ana ria Nyasae, bakabatomera Petero na Yohana. ⅩⅤ Barabwo bagatirimboka, bakabasabera, erinde banyore Omoika Omochenu, ⅩⅥ ekiagera konya torabaikera nonya noyomo obo. Barabwo nigo baabatisetwe rioka ase erieta ri’Omonene Yeso. ⅩⅦ Erio Petero na Yohana bakababeekera amaboko abo, na barabwo bakanyora Omoika Omochenu. ⅩⅧ Ekero Simioni achia korora ng’a abanto nigo bakoegwa Omoika ase okobeekerwa amaboko nabatomwa, akabarentera chitaabu, ⅩⅨ agateeba: “Ing’a nainche okobua oko, ng’a kera omonto ndabeekere amaboko aane anyore Omoika Omochenu.”
ⅩⅩ Korende Petero akamoteebia: “Chifeta chiao chisire amo naye, ekiagera okagete ng’a nonyore ekeegwa kia Nyasae ase chitaabu! ⅩⅪ Tori negetari gose nensemo ase ring’ana eri, ekiagera enkoro yao teri boronge ase obosio bwa Nyasae. ⅩⅫ Ase ayio onchoka korwa ase obobe bwao obo, osabe Omonene, erinde, keranyarekane, bwaberwe ebirengererio bi’enkoro yao. ⅩⅩⅢ Naki nakororire ng’a nore nobororo buna endorwe, naende nigo ore ase egesibo ki’ebibe.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Simioni akairaneria: “Insabera inwe ase Omonene, ng’a amang’ana aya mwakwanire tabaisa gocha konyora nonya nerimo riabo.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Erio kabaorokirie oborori na korandia ring’ana ri’Omonene, bakairana Yerusalemu, bakorandia amang’ana amaya ase ebichie ebinge bi’Abasamaria.
Filipo na Omoetiopia omogomba
ⅩⅩⅥ Omomalaika Omonene agateebia Filipo: “Imoka ogende ensemo ya Irianyi goika enchera eria egotirimboka korwa Yerusalemu ekogenda Gasa.” Eye nenchera y’erooro. ⅩⅩⅦ Akaimoka, akagenda. Na rora, ekero are inchera, akarora omonto bw’Ethiopia, omokoreri, oyobegetwe igoro y’enibo yonsi ya Kandake, enting’ana ya Ethiopia. Ere nigo agendete Yerusalemu gosasiima. ⅩⅩⅧ Ekero arenge koirana na oikaransete ase egari yaye, nigo arenge gosoma egetabu y’omobani Isaya. ⅩⅩⅨ Erio Omoika ogateebia Filipo: “Genda ang’e negari eyio, ogende nero.” ⅩⅩⅩ Filipo akaminyoka gochia asare, akamoigwa ogosoma egetabu y’omobani Isaya, akamobooria: “Inee! Kwamanyire buya aya ogosoma?”
ⅩⅩⅪ Akamoiraneria: “Naki ndanyare komanya otatiga omonto antang’ane?” Agateebia Filipo ariine, aikaranse amo nere. ⅩⅩⅫ 8:32: Isaya 53:7,8Amang’ana e ririiko ayarenge gosoma naro aya:
“Akairwa buna eng’ondi egochia konyenywa,
na buna emanwa etari kwana ase omonachi bw’amarionya,
naboigo tamaanorete omonwa oye.
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Ase ogwekeyia kwaye ekiina kiaye gekarusigwa.
Ning’o oranyare gototeebia ororeria rwaye?
Ekiagera obogima bwaye bwarusigwe ase ense.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Omogomba oria akaboria Filipo: “Koranche onteebie igoro ya ng’o omobani oyo agokwana, igoro yaye omonyene gose igoro y’omonto onde?”
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Filipo agachaaka gokwana; agachakera korwa ase ririiko eri, akamoranderia amang’ana amaya igoro ya Yeso. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Ekero bagendererete inchera, bagaika aase amaache arenge. Oria omogomba akabora: “Rora amaache aya! Inki kegontanga timbatiswa?”
[ ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Filipo akamoteebia: “Koregene nenkoro yao yonsi, nenyarekane.” Akairaneria: “Negenire ng’a Yeso Kristo nere Omwana o Nyasae.”] ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Agachiika egari eria etenene, na bonsi babere, Filipo na oria omogomba, bagatirimboka gochia ase amaache ime, erio Filipo akamobatisa. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Ekero bachia korwa ase amaache, Omoika bw’Omonene ogataboria Filipo; omogomba oria tamorora naende, korende nigo agendete enchera yaye ore nomogooko. ⅩⅬ Korende Filipo akarorekana ase Asoto, na ekero arenge goeta, akarandia enchiri ase emechie yonsi, goika agaika Kaisaria.
