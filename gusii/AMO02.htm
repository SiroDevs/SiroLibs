Moabu.
Ⅰ 2:1: Isaya 15:1—16:14; 25:10-12; Yer 48:1-47; Ezek 25:8-11; Sef 2:8-11Aya naro Omonene ateebire: “Ase engencho y’ogosaria kw’ abanto ba Moabu ara gatato, ee, ara kane, tingotanga egesusuro tikebanyora. Barabwo nigo basambete amauga ’omorwoti o Edomu akaba ribu. Ⅱ Ase ayio nintome omorero ase ense ya Moabu; oro nosambe osirie chinyomba chinkong’u chia Kerioti. Abamoabu mbakwe ase ebikuuro bi’eseegi, na ase eriogi ri’ebitureri bi’eseegi na ebi’abarwani esegi. Ⅲ Ninsirie omogambi ebiina o Moabu nabagaambi b’aroro.” Nabo Omonene ateebire.
Yuda.
Ⅳ Aya naro Omonene ateebire: “Ase engencho y’ogosaria kw’Abayuda ara gatato, ee, ara kane, tingotanga egesusuro tikebanyora. Barabwo nigo bachaayete amachiiko ’Omonene, gose tibabwatete emeroberio yaye echiikire, korende amang’ana ’obong’ainereria bwabo, ayio chisokoro chiabo chiarenge kobwatia abasiririe. Ⅴ Ase ayio nintome omorero ase ense y’Abayuda; nisambe insirie chinyomba chinkong’u chia Yerusalemu.”
Nyasae okonachera Abaisraeli ekiina
Ⅵ Aya naro Omonene ateebire: “Ase engencho y’ogosaria kw’Abaisraeli ara gatato, ee, ara kane, tingotanga egesusuro tikebanyora. Barabwo nigo baoonetie abanyene oboronge ase rigori ri’efeta ase engencho batare konyara goakana singo, na abataka bakabaonia ase engencho batare konyara goakana rigori ri’ebikoroto bibere. Ⅶ Abanto abwo nigo bagotiringera emetwe y’abataka nabwo batari n’echinguru ase orotu rw’ense, naende nigo bagokorekania amang’ana ’omonto omoitongo. Omomura na ise nigo bagokora obonyaka n’omosomba omoiseke oyomo, na igo bakora erieta riane riaba eritachenegeti. Ⅷ Mbarabare ase kera egesasiimero barabwo nigo bakorara inse igoro ye chianga chikobekwa kebwaterero. Ase enyomba y’enyasae yabo banywa edivai y’abwo bagambetigwe bakarusigwa chitaabu.
Ⅸ 2:9: Okoir 3:8-11“Korende ase engencho y’Abaisraeli, inche Omonene ngasiria Abaamori abwo barenge abatambe buna emecharake, naende abakong’u buna Omoaluni. Ⅹ Ninche nabarusetie inwe korwa ase ense ya Misiri, erio nkabaraa ase erooro ase engaki y’emiaka emerongo ene, na inwe mokaira ense y’Abaamori ekaba eyaino. Ⅺ 2:11: Omob 6:1-8Erio ngachora abana baino bande bakaba ababani, na abamura bande nkabaatanana bakaba Aberwa bane. Inwe Abaisraeli, Inee, amang’ana ayio tari bo are?” Nabo Omonene ateebire. Ⅻ “Korende inwe mokaa Aberwa abwo beritete edivai bakanywa, mogakania ababani tibaabana. ⅩⅢ Rora nimbaramatere gochia inse mokeme buna egari eichire chibando. ⅩⅣ Ekero ekio abanto bamanyete kominyoka chimbero, mbasinywe gotama, na abwo bare ne chinguru, mbaborwe chinguru, na abarwani esegi bare chituoni, tibagotooria obogima bwabo. ⅩⅤ Omonto omanyete koruta chinsara, tagotenena arwane, gose oyomanyete kominyoka chimbero, tagwetooria, na omoriini ebarasi tagwetooria. ⅩⅥ Ase rituko erio omonto ore omoremu mono ase egati y’abarwani chituoni, natugute ebirwanero biaye atame ore getirianda.” Nabo Omonene ateebire.
