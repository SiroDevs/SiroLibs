Ⅴ
Amachiiko ikomi
(Okong’ 20:1-7)
Ⅰ Musa akarangeria Abaisraeli bonsi goocha amo, erio akabateebia, Inwe Abaisraeli, igwa amachiiko na emeroberio echiikire ’nkobateebia rituko ria reero, moyieorokererie, mobe n’obomaene ng’a ’moyakore. Ⅱ Omonene Nyasae oito, nakorete okobwatana kw’ogotianania amo naintwe agwo Horebu. Ⅲ Omonene takorete okobwatana okwo ne’chisokoro chiaito, korende nigo akorete okobwatana okwo naintwe abanyene, intwe twensi abatore moyo rituko ria reero. Ⅳ Agwo ase egetunwa, korwa ase egati-gati y’omorero, Omonene nigo akwanete nainwe obosio ase obosio. Ⅴ Ase engaki eria inche nabeete ’nteneine ase egati y’Omonene nainwe, nareenge gokwana nainwe ring’ana ri’Omonene, ekiagera inwe nigo mwarenge n’obwoba ase engencho y’omorero oria, timwatiirete goocha ase egetunwa.
“Omonene agakwana: Ⅵ Inche ninche Omonene Nyasae oino, nabarusetie korwa ase ense ya Misiri, ense y’obosomba.
Ⅶ “Toba ne chinyasae chinde otatiga inche.
Ⅷ 5:8: Abal 26:1; Okoir 4:15-18; 27:15“Tobaisa gwekorera omogwekano bw’ogosasiimwa, omogwekano o getongwa kende gionsi kere igoro, gose ase ense, gose ime ase amaache inse y’ense. Ⅸ 5:9: Okong’ 34:6,7; Omob 14:18; Okoir 7:9,10Tobaisa gotugama gose gosasiima emegwekano, ekiagera inche Omonene, Nyasae oo, inche ’Nyasae bwe risemi*5:9 risemi: engencho yaye endamwamu ya Nyasae etagwanchera nyasae ende esasiimwe. Ase engencho y’amabe y’abaibori nigo ’nkobaa abana egesusuro goika ekiaare kia gatato na egia kane, abwo bangeechete. Ⅹ Korende nigo ’nkoborokia obwanchani bwane botari koera goika ebiaare chilifu, ase abwo banyanchete na babwatete amachiiko ane.
Ⅺ 5:11: Abal 19:12“Toriatora bosa na korikorera bobe erieta ri’Omonene, Nyasae oo, ekiagera Omonene tagotiga komoa egesusuro oria okoriatora bosa na korikorera bobe erieta riaye.”
Ⅻ 5:12: Okong’ 16:23-30; 31:12-14“Bwata rituko ri’Esabato, oriatanane ribe richenu, buna Omonene Nyasae oo, agochiigete. ⅩⅢ 5:13: Okong’ 23:12; 31:15; 34:21; 35:2; Abal 23:3Ase amatuko atano na rimo okore emeremo yao yonsi. ⅩⅣ Korende rituko ria gatan’akabere, ne’rituko ri’Esabato riatananwe ase Nyasae oo. Ase rituko erio tokora emeremo ende yonsi, aye gose omwana oo, omomura gose omoiseke, nonya n’omosomba oo, omomura gose omoiseke, gose eng’ombe yao, gose etigere yao, gose etugo yao ende yonsi egokora emeremo, gose omomenyi oyomenyete mwao. Omosomba oo, omomura gose omoiseke, nabarabwo batimoke buna aye ogotimoka. ⅩⅤ Inyora ng’a aye omonyene nigo kwarenge omosomba ase ense ya Misiri, n’Omonene, Nyasae oo, agakorusia korwa aroro n’okoboko kore ne chinguru koramboire. Ase ayio Omonene, Nyasae oo, nigo agochiigete orende rituko ri’Esabato.
ⅩⅥ 5:16: a Okoir 27:16; Mat 15:4; 19:19; Mar 7:10; 10:19; Luk 18:20; Abaef 6:2; b Abaef 6:3“Sika iso na nyoko, buna Omonene, Nyasae oo, agochiikire; nario oramenye amatuko amaange, naende er’abe buya ase ore ase ense eri’Omonene, Nyasae oo, agochia gokoa.
ⅩⅦ 5:17: Omoch 9:6; Abal 24:17; Mat 5:21; 19:18; Mar 10:19; Luk 18:20; Abar 13:9; Yakb 2:11“Toita.
ⅩⅧ 5:18: Abal 20:10; Mat 5:27; 19:18; Mar 10:19; Luk 18:20; Abar 13:9; Yakb 2:11“Totomana.
ⅩⅨ 5:19: Abal 19:11; Mat 19:18; Mar 10:19; Luk 18:20; Abar 13:9“Toiba.
ⅩⅩ 5:20: Okong’ 23:1; Mat 19:18; Mar 10:19; Luk 18:20“Tomoteebera omogisangio oo oborimo.
ⅩⅪ 5:21: Abar 7:7; 13:9“Tobaisa kogania omokungu bw’omogisangio oo. Tobaisa kogania enyomba y’omogisangio oo, nonya n’omogondo oye, gose omosomba oye, omomura gose omoiseke, gose eng’ombe yaye, gose etigere yaye, gose gento kende gionsi abwate.
ⅩⅫ 5:22: Abaibr 12:18,19“Amang’ana aya nar’Omonene akwanete ase omosangererekano oino bwonsi agwo ase egetunwa, ekero akwanete korwa ase egati‐gati y’omorero, ne riire, na omosunte ekerima. Akayakwana n’eriogi rinene, na tamentete gento kende gionsi. Akayariika ase chibao ibere chi’amagena, erio agachiing’a.
Abanto bakoba n’obwoba
(Okong’ 20:18-21)
ⅩⅩⅢ “Ekero egetunwa keria kiagendererete gwoka omorero, mokaigwa eriogi korwa ase egati‐gati y’omosunte oria, nainwe mwensi, abamore abatang’ani na abagaaka ase ebisaku biaino, mogaacha ase ’nde. ⅩⅩⅣ Mokabora: Naama, Omonene Nyasae oito, otworokirie obonene bwaye na obonguru bwaye, naintwe twaiguure eriogi riaye korwa ase egati‐gati y’omorero. Rituko ria reero twarorire Nyasae ogokwana na Mwanyabaanto, na Mwanyabaanto oyio ogendererete koba moyo. ⅩⅩⅤ Bono nase ki togwenerete gokwa? Omorero oyo omonene nigo ogochia gotosamba na gotosiria, onye naende togochia koigwa eriogi ri’Omonene Nyasae oito, goika tokwe. ⅩⅩⅥ Inee! Omonto nareo obeire okoigwa Nyasae ore moyo kagokwana korwa ase egati‐gati y’omorero, buna intwe twamoiguure, na omonto oyio ogendererete koba moyo? ⅩⅩⅦ Naende moganteebia: Aye genda ang’e, oigwe amang’ana onsi Omonene Nyasae oito, agokwana. Erio oche ototeebie onsi ayio Omonene Nyasae oito, aragoteebie, naintwe ’natoyaigwe na koyakora.
ⅩⅩⅧ “Omonene nigo aigwete amang’ana aino ekero keria mware gokwana nainche, erio aganteebia: Naiguure amang’ana abanto abwo bakwanire naye. Ayio onsi bagoteebirie, mbuya bakwanire. ⅩⅩⅨ Onye ndiria baare koba n’omoyo oyio botambe, na kong’iroka na korenda amachiiko ane onsi, nanga kera egento nigo kiare koba buya ase bare, na ase ororeeria rwabo, kare na kare. ⅩⅩⅩ Genda obateebie bairane ase chiema chiabo. ⅩⅩⅪ Korende aye tigara aa ase ’nde, na inche ngoteebie amachiiko onsi, na emeroberio echiikire, na onsi aya nachiikire, ayio ogochia koborokia, erio bayarende na koyakora ase ense eria ’ngochia kobaa, ebe eyabo.
ⅩⅩⅫ “Ase ayio morende na gokora ayio Omonene Nyasae oino, abachiikire. Timoyatiga na koonchoka gochia aande, ensemo y’okorio gose ey’okobee. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Mobwatie chinchera chionsi Omonene Nyasae oino, abachiikire, erio nario mor’abe moyo, naende nario er’abe buya ase more, na inwe momenye amatuko amaange ase ense eria mogochia koira ebe eyaino.
