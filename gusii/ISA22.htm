Obobani igoro ya Yerusalemu
Ⅰ Aya naro amang’ana igoro ye Rikura ri’Amaroro. Mang’ana ki abeire inwe mwensi mwariinire gochia birama igoro? Ⅱ Aye omochie oichire neburu‐buru na okwogana, aye ore nomochengo bw’obobe, abanto bao abaitirwe tibaitiri nemioro, gose tibakweti ase esegi. Ⅲ Abagaambi bao bonsi batamire bare amo, nigo babwatirwe batararaswa chinsara. Abanto bao bonsi banyoretwe nigo babwatetwe, nonya konya batamire gochia aare. Ⅳ Ase ayio inche ngateeba: “Tobaisa kondigereria, ntige ndere nobororo ing’iyerie amariga; tobaisa gwechanda kondemia ase engencho y’ogosarigwa kw’omochie bw’abaanto baane.” Ⅴ Omonene, Nyasae bw’Emeganda, ochorire engaki ere nobotantanu, na ogotiringerwa inse, na omoichano, na engaki eyio nigo erache igoro ye Rikura ri’Amaroro. Ase engaki eyio chindwaki nchitagorwe chigwe inse, na ebirero mbiigweke gochia ase ebitunwa.
Ⅵ Abaelamu nigo babogoretie chinsara, bagacha amo nabariini chigari na abe chibarasi; na abanto ba Kiri bagasokia chinguba chiabo. Ⅶ Amakura aino are amaya mono aichoirwe ne chigari, na abariini chibarasi mbateneine ase ebiita. Ⅷ Egekengero ki’Abayuda nigo kiarusetigweo kegima.
Ase engaki eyio mokarigia ebirwanero biarenge kogachwa ase Enyomba ye Rinani. Ⅸ Erio mokanyora ng’a obotandoku bwe chindwaki chi’Omochie o Daudi bwabeire obonge; inwe mogatanga amaache tarua ase Ritibo ria Maate. Ⅹ Mokabara chinyomba chia Yerusalemu, na mogachitagora ase okoroisia orwaki robe orokong’u. Ⅺ Mokaagacha etangi y’amaache agwo ase egati ye chindwaki chiria ibere, erinde monyore amaache akorwa ase ritibo rikoro, korende timwamorigeretie Oyio okorete ayio, gose timwamorengereretie Oyio okorete omooroberio oyio agwo kare.
Ⅻ Ase engaki eyi’Omonene, Nyasae bw’Emeganda, nigo abateebeetie ng’a morere na kobokia amagombo, na mokigite chitukia chi’emetwe yaino, na kweboyia chigunia. ⅩⅢ 22:13: 1 Abakr 15:32Ribaga ri’ogokora igo mokagoka na gochenga; mokanyenya chiombe ne ching’ondi, mokaba mokoria chinyama na konywa edivai, mogateeba: “Tiga torie na konywa, ekiagera ankio natokwe.”
ⅩⅣ Omonene bw’Emeganda nere onyorokirie amang’ana ayio na gonteebia, “Ekeene, timokwaberwa ebibe ebio nonya ng’ake.” Omonene bw’Emeganda oteebire ayio.
Sebuna ogokuurerwa
ⅩⅤ Aya nar’Omonene, Nyasae bw’Emeganda, agoteeba: “Inchuo ogende ase Sebuna, oyio ore omogachi na omoteneneri bw’enyomba y’obogaambi, omoteebie: ⅩⅥ Aye ninki ogokora aiga, na monto ki obwate aa? Nase ki ogwekunyera oboina aase are igoro mono? Inee! Nigo okwebaachera oboina aiga ase egetare bobe obomenyo bwao? ⅩⅦ Aye nonya kore omonto bw’echinguru, Naama, Omonene nakobwate bokong’u, akorute gochia aare mono. ⅩⅧ Nigo arakoborongose buna omopira, akorute gochia ase ense ere engare; agwo nao orakwere. Naende nao chigari chiao chi’obonene chirabe, ekiagera aye kwabeire omonto bw’obosooku ase enyomba y’omonene oo. ⅩⅨ Inche Omonene ninkorusie orue ase emeremo yao, naende nkoiyerie korwa aase ao.
Obobani igoro y’Eliakimu
ⅩⅩ “Ase engaki eyio nindangerie omosomba one Eliakimu, mosinto o Hilikia, ⅩⅪ imobekere eganchwa yao y’obogaambi, naende imoboerie orokini rwao. Nimbeeke obogaambi bwao ase okoboko kwaye, ere nabe ise bw’abaanto ba Yerusalemu na Abayuda. ⅩⅫ 22:22: Okom 3:7Nimbeeke ekeigorero ki’enyomba ya Daudi ase amareko aye; ere nabe okoigora, na onde tari oranyare gosieka; nabe ogosieka na onde tari oranyare koigora. ⅩⅩⅢ Nimokong’ontere akong’e buna omosumari okong’onteire ase enyasi enkong’u, na ere nabe ekerogo ki’amasikani ase enyomba ya ise.
ⅩⅩⅣ “Abanto bonsi b’enyomba ya ise, na abana baye, ne chinderia chiabo, mbabe bakomosemeria abakoonye. Ebikorero bionsi, ebisinini na ebinene, mbimosung’unane. ⅩⅩⅤ Korende ase engaki eyio omosumari oria okong’onteretwe ase enyasi enkong’u nosimorwe korwao ogwe inse, ne chigurube chiasungireo nchigwe, chisamunyokane.” Nab’Omonene bw’Emeganda atebiire.
