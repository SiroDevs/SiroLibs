Yerusalemu okogambigwa
Ⅰ Omoika okanyimokereria igoro, okang’ira ase egeita ki’enyomba y’Omonene kiarochie moocha. Na rora, agwo ase egesieri ki’egeita mbarengeo abasacha emerongo ebere, na batano, na ase egati yabo nkarora Yasania, mosinto bw’Asuri, na Peletia, mosinto o Benaya, abwo barenge abagaambi b’ abanto.
Ⅱ Omonene aganteebia, “Aye Mwanyabaanto, abanto aba nabwo bagokora emeroberio y’ogokora ebibe na korua ogoseemia kw’ogokora amabe ase omochie oyo. Ⅲ Barabwo nigo bagoteeba ng’a engaki teraika ang’e tokoagacha chinyomba; omochie oyo n’enyang’eni ekoiyeka, na intwe nechinyama. Ⅳ Ase ayio, aye Mwanyabaanto, bana ayare mamincha, yabo; ee baabaanere.”
Ⅴ Omoika bw’Omonene ogaacha igoro ase inde, oganteebia, “Bateebie ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Inwe Abaisraeli, naboigo mogokwana; inche nigo imanyete ayare ase chinkoro chiaino ime. Ⅵ Mwaitire abanto abange goetania, ase omochie oyo, mwaichoirie chinchera chiaye nebitondo.
Ⅶ “Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Abwo mwaitire baraire ase omochie ime nabwo chinyama, na omochie oyo n’oro enyang’eni ekoiyeka; korende inwe abanyene nimbasokie korwa ase omochie oyo. Ⅷ Inwe nigo moirogete ogoitwa nemioro, nindete emioro ebaite. Nab’Omonene ateebire. Ⅸ Nimbarusie ase omochie oyo, mbabeeke ase amaboko y’ abanto abaao, na ’mbanachere ekiina. Ⅹ Inwe imoitwe nemioro. Nimbanachere ekiina agwo ase ribebe ri’Abaisraeli. Erio momanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene. Ⅺ Omochie oyo tori gochia koba buna enyang’eni ase more, na inwe timori koba chinyama ime yaye; inche nimbanachere ekiina agwo ase ribebe ri’Abaisraeli. Ⅻ Erio momanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene. Inwe mwangire kobwatia emeroberio y’ane echiikire, gose timori gokora aya nachiikire, korende nigo mogokora koreng’ana namachiiko y’ abanto b’ebisaku ebio bibaetanaine.”
ⅩⅢ Ekero nagendererete kobana, Pelatia, mosinto o Benaya, agakwa. Erio ’ngatung’ama inse maumama, ’nkarera neriogi rinene, ’nkabora, “Aye Omonene, Inee, nigo ogochia goita osirie kegima abatigari b’Abaisraeli?”
Eira ya Nyasae ase abooswa
ⅩⅣ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia, ⅩⅤ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, abanto bamenyete Yerusalemu nigo bagokwana igoro y’ abanto bamino b’egesaku kiao, ee, igoro y’Abaisraeli bonsi, bagoteeba: Barabwo nigo bare aare korwa ase Omonene, ense eye nintwe twaeirwe ebe eyaito.
ⅩⅥ “Ase ayio obateebie ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Nonya nabaseririe gochia aare ase egati y’ abanto b’ebisaku na kobasiarera gochia ase chinse chionsi, inche omonyene nabeire inde obomenyo bwabo obochenu ase engaki eyio enke, agwo ase chinse echio baseretigwe gochia.
ⅩⅦ “Ase ayio obateebie ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Nimbasangererie korwa ase abanto b’ebisaku, na korwa ase chinse echio ase nabasiarerete gochia, erio mbairanie inwe Abaisraeli, ’mbae ense yaino. ⅩⅧ Ekero barabe bairanire ase ense eyio, mbatugute korwa aroro emegwekano egosasiimwa y’obosooku, na okogechia gwonsi. ⅩⅨ 11:19: Ezek 36:26-28Ekero ekio nimbae enkoro eyemo, naende mbeke omoika omoyia ime yabo. Nindusie korwa ase bare chinkoro chire chinkong’u buna rigena, erio mbae echire chinyororo buna chinyama. ⅩⅩ Erio nario barabwatie emeroberio y’ane echiikire, babwatie na amachiiko ane na koyaigwera. Barabwo mbabe abanto bane, na inche nimbe Nyasae obo. ⅩⅪ Korende abanto abwo chinkoro chiabo chikobwatia emegwekano bagosasiima y’obosooku na okogechia, barabwo imbaakane ogokora kwabo kobairanere.” Nabo Omonene agoteeba.
Obonene bwa Nyasae bogotiga Yerusalemu
ⅩⅫ 11:22: Ezek 43:2-5Erio ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba bikarambora chimbaaba chiabo, na amagoro arenge mbarabare yabo; na obonene bwa Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli bokaba igoro yabo. ⅩⅩⅢ Erio obonene bw’Omonene bokaimokerera igoro korwa ase omochie oria, bokagenda bogatenena igoro ase egetunwa kere moocha y’omochie. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase okorora naetwe, Omoika o Nyasae okanyimokereria igoro, okang’ira ase ense y’Abakalidayo, ase abanto abwo boosetwe barenge. Nyuma y’ayio okorora koria narooche gokarua asende. ⅩⅩⅤ Erio ngateebia abanto abwo bonsi boosetwe amang’ana onsi ay’Omonene konya anyorokirie.
