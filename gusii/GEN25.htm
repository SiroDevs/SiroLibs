Ororeeria ronde rwa Aburaamu
Ⅰ Aburaamu akanyuoma omokungu onde, ore korokwa Ketura. Ⅱ Nere akamoiborera Simurani, na Yokisani, na Medani, na Midiani, na Isibaki, na Sua. Ⅲ Yokisani akaibora Seba na Dedani. Na basinto ba Dedani nabwo aba: Ashurimu, na Letusi, na Leumi. Ⅳ Basinto ba Midiani nabwo aba: Efa, na Eferi, na Hanoki, na Abida, na Elidaa. Abwo bonsi nabwo baarenge abana ba Ketura.
Ⅴ Aburaamu akaa Isaka ebinto bionsi ebi abwate. Ⅵ Korende abana b’abakuungu baria ba sere‐sere Aburaamu akabaa ebiegwa. Ekero are moyo, konya atarakwa, akabateebia barue ase omwana oye Isaka, bagende gochia moocha, ase ense ya Moocha.
Ogokwa kwa Aburaamu
Ⅶ Aburaamu nigo amenyete emiaka rigana erimo, na emerongo etano na ebere, na etano (175), ase obogima bwaye. Ⅷ Nigo asogetie omoyo ase obogotu n’obuya, ore omogaka, oisanekire nemiaka emenge; agasangerekana amo nabaanto baye. Ⅸ Isaka na Isumaeli bakamotindeka ase rikuruma agwo Makipela ase oboremo bwa Efuroni, omwana o Sohari, Omohiti, moocha ya Mamure, Ⅹ 25:10: Omoch 23:3-16ase oboremo boria Aburaamu agorete korwa ase Abahiti. Agwo nao Aburaamu atindegetwe ase Sara mokaye nere atindegetwe. Ⅺ Magega y’ogokwa kwa Aburaamu, Nyasae agasesenia Isaka, omwana oye. Na Isaka akamenya ang’e nensoko yaare korokwa Beeri‐Lahai‐Roi.
Ororeeria rwa Isumaeli
Ⅻ Oro narwo ororeeria rwa Isumaeli, omwana o Aburaamu, oyo Hagari, omosomba o Sara, amoiborerete. ⅩⅢ Aya naro amarieta ’abana ba Isumaeli, ase omooroberio bw’okoiborwa kwabo: Nebayoti, nere omwana oye omotangi, na Kedari, na Adibeeli, na Mibusamu, ⅩⅣ na Misima, na Duma, na Masa, ⅩⅤ na Hadadi, na Tema, na Yeturi, na Nafisi, na Kedema. ⅩⅥ Aba nabwo abana ba Isumaeli, na aya naro amarieta abo ase emechie yabo na ebitwori biabo, na barabwo nigo baarenge abagaambi ikomi na babere, ase ebisaku biabo. ⅩⅦ Isumaeli nigo aigetie emiaka rigana erimo, na emerongo etato, na etano na ebere (137), erio erio agakwa; agasangererekana amo nabaanto baye. ⅩⅧ Abana ba Isumaeli nigo baamenyete bagachaakera Havila, bagaika Suri moocha ya Misiri, ase enchera ekogenda Asuri; bakamenya babebekaine n’abamwabo bonsi.
Okoiborwa gwa Esau na Yakobo
ⅩⅨ Oro narwo ororeeria rwa Isaka, mosinto o Aburaamu. Aburaamu nere oiborete Isaka. ⅩⅩ Isaka nigo areenge nemiaka emerongo ene ekero anyuomete Rebeka, omoiseke o Betueli, Omoaramu o Padan‐Aramu, omoiseke omwabo Labani, Omoaramu. ⅩⅪ Isaka agasabera mokaye ase Omonene, ekiagera nigo areenge omogomba. Omonene akaigwa ogosaba kwaye, na Rebeka mokaye akaba morito. ⅩⅫ Abana bakaba bakomigana enda yaye ime. Ere akabora: “Onye nabo iga, okoba moyo kwane ninki korangwenere?” Akagenda koboori’Omonene.
ⅩⅩⅢ 25:23: Abar 9:12Omonene akamoiraneria,
“Ebisaku bibere mbire enda yao ime,
abanto babere orache koibora,
na barabwo mbaatananekane korwa okoiborwa kwabo;
oyomo nabe ne chinguru kobua oria onde,
oria omonene omosomba bw’oria omoke.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Ekero amatuko aye ay’okoibora achia goikerana, bakanyora ne chibarongo chiare enda yaye ime. ⅩⅩⅤ Oyo bw’eritang’ani agasooka, ere nigo areenge omobariri, omobere bwonsi nigo orenge namarionya; bakamoroka Esau.*25:25 Esau: engencho yaye: ore namarionya. Erieta riaye rinde Edomu. Engencho yaye: omobariri (ekebungu 30) ⅩⅩⅥ Magega yaye oyomwabo agasooka, okoboko kwaye kobwatete egetingiro gia Esau na ere bakamoroka Yakobo.†25:26 Yakobo: engencho yaye: okobwata egetingiro, gose: omong’ainereria Isaka nigo areenge nemiaka emerongo etano nomo (60), ekero mokaye abaiborete.
Esau okoonia omwando oye bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi
ⅩⅩⅦ Ekero abana abwo bakinire, Esau akaba omotwari bwe ching’iti, korende Yakobo nigo areenge omonto omotoereru, ore kobeera ase chiema. ⅩⅩⅧ Isaka nigo amwanchete Esau, ekiagera areenge komorentera chinyama chi’obotwari bwaye; korende Rebeka nigo amwanchete Yakobo.
ⅩⅩⅨ Rituko erimo Yakobo naiyegete omosori gete. Esau agacha korwa orosana, konya orosire mono, ogoyire ase engencho y’enchara. ⅩⅩⅩ Na Esau agasaba Yakobo: “Koranche, ong’e omosori oyio omobariri inywe, ekiagera n akuure enchara.” Ase ayio bakamoroka Edomu (engencho yaye, omobariri).
ⅩⅩⅪ Yakobo akamoiraneria: “Ritang’ani ng’oonerie omwando oo bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi.”
ⅩⅩⅫ Esau agateeba: “Rora, inche nigo inde ang’e gokwa, omwando oyo bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi inki orankonye?”
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ 25:33: Abaibr 12:16Yakobo akamoteebia: “Karatiana ritang’ani ase ’nde.”
Esau agatiana, erio akaooneria Yakobo omwando oye bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Erio Yakobo akaa Esau omogati na omosori bwe ching’ende, na ere akaria na konywa; erio akaimoka akagenda. Naboigo Esau achayete omwando oye bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi.
