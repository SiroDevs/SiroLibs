Ekiina ki’omoerio ase Abaisraeli
Ⅰ Ekero Abaefraimu baarenge gokwana, abanto nigo baarenge koiguswa; nigo beimokereretie igoro ase egati y’Abaisraeli; korende bakarenta okomocha ase ogosasiima enyasae Baali, na ase ayio bagakwa. Ⅱ Nigo bagendererete gokora ebibe goetania, bakaroisia emegwekano ye chifeta bagosasiima, eyio babombete nobong’aini. Eyio yonsi nemeremo yaroisirie nabaroisia b’ebinto. Nigo bagoteeba igoro y’abwo: “Abwo bakorua abanto baba ebing’wanso, nigo bakonyunyunta emegwekano eyio ye chimori.” Ⅲ Ase ayio abanto abwo nabasire buna ritaturi ria mambia, gose buna riime rigosira bwango, na buna omoiriro okoirurukigwa nomwaga korwa ase ekeooreri, gose buna erioki rigoetera ase ekebao riasira.
Ⅳ “Inche ninche Omonene, Nyasae oino, korwa ekero mwarenge ase ense ya Misiri. Timwabeete momanyete enyasae ende yonsi otatiga inche; omotooria onde tari otatiga inche. Ⅴ 13:5: Okoir 8:11-17Ninche nabarendete ase erooro, ense eria enyomo pa egosamba. Ⅵ Korende ekero banyorete endagera eisaine, bakaria bakaigota, erio bakaenena na kweimokereria igoro; ase ayio bakanyeba. Ⅶ Ase engencho eyio nkaba buna endo ase bare, nkababoera ase chinchera chiabo buna engo ekoboera. Ⅷ Nimbaumokere buna edubu yaurirwe chimanwa chiaye, mbatandore ebikuba biabo, erinde mbaite mbarie buna endo ekoria, ee, nimbe buna eng’iti y’orosana mbatandorane.
Ⅸ “Inwe Abaisraeli mwesiririe inwe abanyene ase okoba mamincha yane, inche oyo narenge ooboseemiaka kwaino. Ⅹ 13:10: 1 Sam 8:5,6Bono ng’ai omorwoti oino are, erinde abatoorie ase emechie yaino yonsi? Ng’ai abagaambi ebiina baino bare, inwe abamwateebete: Toe omorwoti na abagaambi batogambere? Ⅺ 13:11: a 1 Sam 10:17-24; b 1 Sam 15:26Nkabaa omorwoti inde nendamwamu, naende namorusirie inde nekeririanda.
Ⅻ “Obobe bw’Abaefraimu nigo bosibire amo, na ebibe biabo nigo birikire bigachire. ⅩⅢ Obororo bonga buna obw’omokuungu okobina aibore mbobachere, nigo bonga buna omwana otari nokomanya osinyirwe gosoka korwa ase enda ya ng’ina ekero engaki yaye yaikire. ⅩⅣ 13:14: 1 Abakr 15:55Nimbaboorie korwa ase okobua gw’aase abakuure, na nimbatoorie korwa ase amakweri. Aye amakweri, amaako ao ng’ai are? Aye aase abakuure, ng’ai ogosiria kwao kore? Ogweitia gwasirire korwa ase amaiso aane? ⅩⅤ Abaefraimu nabaisa koibora babuche ase egati y’abamwabo, omwaga korwa moocha noche, ee, omwaga o Omonene noche korwa ase erooro. Erio ebisima biabo bi’amaache biome, ne chinsoko chiabo chikame. Omwaga oyio nosirie kegima chinibo chiabo banchete. ⅩⅥ Abanto ba Samaria mbaegwe egesusuro, ekiagera basaririe ase Nyasae obo, mbaitwe nemioro, na abana babo banachanwe ebing’ese. Abakungu babo bare barito mbatandorwe chinda.”
