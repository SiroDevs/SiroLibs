Okobokia amagombo kw’Abaisraeli agwo ase ense ya Babiloni
Ⅰ Nigo twarenge goikaransa ase chingegu chie chindooche chia Babiloni,
twaba tokorera ekero twarenge koinyora Sayoni.
Ⅱ Aroro agwo nigo twasungete amakano aito ase emete enga buna emesobeti.
Ⅲ Abanto baria batwosete bakagania tobaterere,
abachandi abwo baito bakagania tobagokie, bagateeba,
“Toterere ogoteera gwa Sayoni.”
 
Ⅳ Naki torakore totere ogoteera kw’Omonene ase ense engeni?
Ⅴ Aye Yerusalemu, inse gokweba,
rirorio okoboko kwane gw’okorio kogatae.
Ⅵ Onye tingokoinyora aye Yerusalemu,
erinde obe omogooko one omonene mono,
rirorio tiga oromeme rwane robwatane namang’ang’ura.
 
Ⅶ Aye Omonene, inyora amabe aya abanto b’Edomu bakorete
ase rituko riria Yerusalemu obwatetwe;
buna bateebete, “Yotagore kegima!
Yotagore amo noboroso bwaye!”
 
Ⅷ 137:8: Okom 18:6Inwe abanto ba Babiloni nigo mogochia gosirigwa.
Nagoke oyio ogochia kobaakana
amabe aya mwabeire mogotokorera.
Ⅸ Nachenge omonto oyio ogochia koira abana baino abake
na kobaakera ase amagena y’Ebitare.
