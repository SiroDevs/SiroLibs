Okomotogia Omonene
Ⅰ Motogie Omonene!
Motogie Nyasae agwo ase obomenyo bwaye obochenu,
motogie agwo igoro eyio yatongetwe ase okobua kwaye!
Ⅱ Motogie ase engencho y’ogokora kwaye okonene;
motogie koreng’ana nobonene bwaye bore ne’chinguru chinyinge goetania!
 
Ⅲ Motogie ase okobugia ebitureeri;
motogie ase okobugia amakano onsi!
Ⅳ Motogie ase okobugia chinchigiri na gotenga;
motogie ase okobugia amakano na ebiroria!
Ⅴ Motogie ase ogoakania chisani chikwoga amariogi are inse;
motogie ase ogoakania chisani chikwoga amariogi are igoro!
Ⅵ Tiga ebitongwa bionsi bire moyo bitogie Omonene!
 
Motogie Omonene!
