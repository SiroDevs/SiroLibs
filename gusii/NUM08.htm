Okobekwa Gwe Chitaaya
Ⅰ 8:1: Okong’ 25:31-40; 37:17-24Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, Ⅱ “Teebia Aroni ng’a, ekero arabeeke chitaaya isano na ibere ase ekebekerero gie chitaaya, achibeeke erinde chimoreke gochia bosio.” Ⅲ Aroni agakora bo, akabeeka ekebekerero gi’echitaaya echio, erinde chimoreke gochia bosio bw’ekebeekerero ekio, buna Omonene achiigete Musa. Ⅳ Ekebekerero gie chitaaya na enterekerero yaye, amo n’emegwekano yaye y’ebisicha nigo biaroisirie korwa ase chitaabu chiturire. Nabo Musa aroisetie ekebeekerero ekio buna Omonene konya amochiikire.
Abalawi Bagwatananwa Na Gochenwa
Ⅴ Omonene agateebia Musa, Ⅵ “Atanana Abalawi korwa ase Abaisraeli na obachene. Ⅶ Ekero okobachena nigo orakore iga: Onyororokie amaache y’ogwesibia igoro yabo, na barabwo bakigite emebere yabo yonsi na basibie chianga chiabo. Erio babe bachenekire. Ⅷ Naende barente eeri ensae amo n’okoruegwa kw’endagera kw’obosie obonuke bwochoganirie n’amaguta. Naye oire eeri ende ensae ebe ekeng’wanso ki’ebibe. Ⅸ Osangererie omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli na orente Abalawi agwo bosio bw’Eema y’Omosangererekano. Ⅹ Ekero kwarentire Abalawi ase obosio bwane, inche Omonene, Abaisraeli goika babeeke amaboko abo igoro ase emetwe yabo. Ⅺ Naende Aroni arente Abalawi ase obosio bwane, babe buna ekeng’wanso ki’ogosungusigwa korwa ase Abaisraeli. Naboigo Abalawi baatananwe, babe ekeegwa ase ogonkorera inche Omonene. Ⅻ Magega yaye Abalawi goika babeeke amaboko abo igoro ase emetwe ye chieri chiria, naye orusie eeri eyemo ebe ekeng’wanso ki’ebibe, na eyende ebe ekeng’wanso ki’ogosambwa ase ’nde inche Omonene, na igo okore okobwatanigwa ase Abalawi.
ⅩⅢ “Obeeke Abalawi babe bagokonya Aroni na abamura baye, obaatanane babe buna ekeng’wanso ki’ogosungusigwa ase ’nde inche Omonene. ⅩⅣ Ase enchera eyio obaatanane Abalawi korwa ase Abaisraeli, barabwo ’mbabe abaane. ⅩⅤ Ekero kwabachenire na kobaatanana babe buna ekeng’wanso ki’ogosungusigwa, barabwo basoe ase Eema y’Omosangererekano babe bagokora emeremo aroro. ⅩⅥ Barabwo baruegwe kegima korwa ase Abaisraeli babe abaane, ekiagera nabachorire ribaga ri’abana abatangi b’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅦ 8:17: Okong’ 13:2Abana abatangi bonsi b’Abaisraeli n’abaane, ne ching’iti chintangi boigo. Rituko riria naitete abana abatangi bonsi amo ne ching’iti chintangi ase ense ya Misiri, nigo neatananerete abana abatangi b’Abaisraeli amo ne ching’iti chintangi. ⅩⅧ Na bono nachorire Abalawi babe abaane ribaga ri’abana abatangi bonsi b’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅨ Narure Abalawi babe ekeegwa ase Aroni na abamura baye korwa ase Abaisraeli, erinde bakore emeremo ase engencho y’Abaisraeli, agwo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. Boigo bakore okobwatanigwa ase bare, erinde nintange amaakwa tabanyora ekero bagoika ang’e n’Aase Aachenu.”
ⅩⅩ Naboigo Musa, na Aroni, amo n’omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli bakaba bagokorera Abalawi. Abaisraeli bakabakorera kera ring’ana Omonene konya achiikire Musa baabakorere. ⅩⅪ Abalawi bagechena korwa ase ebibe na bagasibia chianga chiabo, na Aroni akabaatanana bakaba buna ekeng’wanso ki’ogosungusigwa ase obosio bw’Omonene, naende agakora okobwatanigwa ase bare, na kobachena. ⅩⅫ Magega yayio Abalawi bakageenda ase obokoreri bwabo ime ase Eema y’Omosangererekano inse y’oborendi bwa Aroni na abamura baye. Bun’Omonene konya achiikire Musa igoro y’Abalawi nabo Abaisraeli baabakorerete.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omonene agakwana naende na Musa, akamoteebia, ⅩⅩⅣ “Eye nero emeremo y’Abalawi: Abwo bakoorire emiaka emerongo ebere na etano, na goetania, nigo barabe bagokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero bakoorire emiaka emerongo etano batige batimoke korwa ase emeremo eria. ⅩⅩⅥ Barabwo ’mbabe ne ribaga ri’ogokonya baria bamwabo bare abakoreri ase Eema y’Omosangererekano, korende tibaabaisa gokora emeremo eyio abanyene. Naboigo oraroberie Abalawi ase emeremo yabo.”
