Ⅳ
Ⅰ Naende nkarigereria nkarora ogokora okobe kware gokorwa inse y’erioba. Nkarora abwo bagokorerwa bobe bakorera, na monto onde tarengeo okobaremia, ekiagera abwo barenge kobakorera bobe nigo barenge nokobua, na onde tarengeo okobaremia. Ⅱ Nkarora ng’a nigo ere buya ase abakuure kobua ase abare moyo. Ⅲ Korende ase oyo otaraiborwa, oyio otararora ogokora okobe kore aiga inse y’erioba, nigo ere buya asare kobua ase abwo bonsi bare moyo na abakuure.
Ⅳ Erio nkarora emeremo eye emekong’u egokorwa, na omokia bwonsi bw’ogwechanda ase ogokora emeremo, ayio nigo agokorwa, ekiagera omonto akoba ne ribero*4:4 Ribero: koba nokogania gokora egento goetania oyonde gose ogotwara okogania ase ebinto bitari ebiao ng’a abue oyonde. Ayio boigo mbosa, nigo are buna okominyokia embeo. Ⅴ Omoriri nigo agwetumata, na bweria omobere oye orea. Ⅵ Mbuya koba negento geichire oroe oromo na koba nomorembe, kobua koba n’ebinto biichire chindoe ibere na koba nogwechanda na kominyokera konyora ebire buna embeo bosa.
Ⅶ Naende nkarora obosa kegima bore inse y’erioba: Ⅷ Nare omonto otabwate omwana gose omwabo; ere nigo agendererete gwechanda gokora emeremo emekong’u, na amaiso aye tari goisaneka ne chinibo; omonto oyio tana kweboria, “Ning’o ngochandekera gokorera na kwerina omogooko bwonsi?” Aya naro mbosa, na ogwechanda gotari nomogooko.
Ⅸ Nigo ere buya koba babere kobua oyomo, ekiagera mbanyore eng’eria engiya ase emeremo yabo bagokora. Ⅹ Oyomo karagwe, oyonde namogororokie; korende obobe nobwaye oria ore bweka ekero aragwe, na onde tari oramogororokie. Ⅺ Abanto babere kobararaare amo mbaigwe riberera korende naki oyomo arakore aigwe riberera kare bweka? Ⅻ Omonto aise korwania oyomo ore bweka, namobue, korende onye bare babere, takobabua. Engori esisire nemegoye etato teri kobutoka bwango.
ⅩⅢ Omwana omosae omotaka ore nobong’aini niigo’are omuya kobua omorwoti omogotu ore omoriri, otari gwancha gosemigwa; ⅩⅣ neba ng’a omosae oria nigo aruete echela akabeekwa oborwoti, gose nigo aiboretwe ore omotaka ase obogambi bw’omorwoti oria. ⅩⅤ Nkarora abanto bonsi abamenyete inse y’erioba bakobwatia omosae oria ogochia kobeekwa oborwoti ribaga ri’oria orengeo. ⅩⅥ Abanto abwo arenge koraa tibare kobareka. Nonya naboigo abwo barachiche magega y’abwo tibakomogokera. Ekeene, aya naro mbosa, na aya akominyokerwa konyorwa nigo are buna embeo bosa.
