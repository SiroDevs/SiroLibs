Ⅰ Naki twamanyire ng’a enyomba yaito ere buna eema eyere ey’ense eye, ekero erasarigwe ntore noboagacho borwete ase Nyasae, enyomba etaagachiri namaboko, eyere igoro kare na kare. Ⅱ Naki intwe ase enyomba eye iga togwechanda, ntore namaiga koboyigwa obomenyo bwaito aaria igoro. Ⅲ Ekero twaboyigwe titokororekana tore getirianda. Ⅳ Naki intwe, abatore ase enyomba eye ere buna eema, nigo togwechanda, toritoerwa, tari ng’a igo torigetie korusigwa ase enyomba eye, korende ntorigetie okoboyigwa konde, erinde egekonyorekana namakweri kemerwe nobogima. Ⅴ Gaki ere oyotoroisetie ase amang’ana aya nere Nyasae, oyotoete Omoika buna ekebwaterero giantenenera.
Ⅵ Gaki amatuko onsi nigo tore nomoyo bw’oboremu, naende twamanyire ng’a ekero tore aa ase omobere nigo tomenyete aare korwa ase Omonene, Ⅶ ekiagera nigo togotaara ase okwegena, tari ase okorora. Ⅷ Ntore nomoyo bw’oboremu, na twarorire buya kobua korua ase omobere na komenya amo n’Omonene. Ⅸ Ase ayio, onye tore aa, gose onye titori aa, nigo okogania gwaito kore ng’a tomogwenere ere. Ⅹ 5:10: Abar 14:10Naki netogwenerete intwe twensi koorokigwa ase obosio bw’ekerogo gi’ekina gia Kristo; erio kera omonto anyore koreng’ana buna are gokora ase omobere, onye bire ebiya gose onye bire ebibe.
Abasani amo na Nyasae goetera ase Kristo
Ⅺ Nabo gatwamanyire komoiroka Omonene, twateemire kong’usa abanto; korende twamanyekanire ase obosio bwa Nyasae, na inche nasemeirie ng’a twamanyekanire ase okomanya gwe chinkoro chiaino ime boigo. Ⅻ Titogwetogia intwe abanyene ase obosio bwaino naende, korende twabaeire ribaga ri’ogwetogia ase engencho yaito erinde monyare koba nokoiraneria ase abagwetogia ase amang’ana a isiko oka, gose tari ase amang’ana y’enkoro ime. ⅩⅢ Naki onye twasirerigwe nobong’aini bwaito, ayio nigo are ase Nyasae; gose onye tore nobong’aini bwaito, ayio nigo are ase engencho yaino. ⅩⅣ Naki obwanchani bwa Kristo nigo bogotobetereria, ekiagera twamanyire ekeene ng’a onye oyomo okuure ase engencho ya bonsi, nabo bonsi bakuure. ⅩⅤ Ere agakwa ase engencho ya bonsi, erio abare moyo tibaaba moyo ase engencho yabo boka abanyene, korende nase engencho yaye ere oyokwete, naende akaboka ase engencho yabo.
Obogima oboyia ime ya Kristo
ⅩⅥ Ase ayio, korwa bono intwe titomanyeti monto onde koreng’ana buna Mwanyabaanto bamomanyete; nonya intwe twamomanyete Kristo ritang’ani buna Mwanyabaanto bamomanyete, korende bono titokomomanya buna igo. ⅩⅦ Ase ayio, omonto karabe ime ya Kristo nigo are egetongwa ekeyia; ebia kare biaetire, rora bionsi biabeire ebiyia. ⅩⅧ Korende bionsi nigo bikorwa ase Nyasae, oyotobwatanirie intwe amo nere omonyene ase Kristo, nere otoeire obosomba bw’okobwatanigwa, ⅩⅨ ekiagera Nyasae nigo arenge ime ya Kristo, akabwatania ense amo nere omonyene, tabaabarera ogosaria kwabo; ere obekire ase tore ring’ana ri’okobwatanigwa.
Amang’ana y’okobwatanigwa
ⅩⅩ Nabo tore abatomire na Kristo; Nyasae nigo akobasorora inwe goetera ase emenwa yaito. Twabasoroire ase engencho ya Kristo: Mobwatanigwe na Nyasae. ⅩⅪ Nyasae omokorire ere, oyotamanyeti ebibe, koba ebibe ase engencho yaito, erio asare intwe tobe noboronge ase Nyasae.
