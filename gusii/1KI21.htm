Omogondo bw’emesabibu o Naboti
Ⅰ Naboti Omoyesireeli nabwate omogondo bw’emesabibu agwo Yesireeli, ang’e nenyomba y’Ahabu, omorwoti bw’Abasamaria. Ⅱ Ahabu agateebia Naboti: “Ing’a omogondo oyo oo bw’emesabibu, erinde nsimeke chinyeni, ekiagera nigo ore ang’e na mwane, nainche ninkoe omogondo onde omuya mono ribaga riaye, gose onye gokorora ng’a mbuya, ninkoe chitaabu.”
Ⅲ Korende Naboti akamoteebia: “Tiga Omonene atange amang’ana ayio, timbaisa gokoa omwando one naetwe ne chisokoro chiane.”
Ⅳ Ahabu akagenda inka bwoye nenkoro y’obororo, naende akagechigwa mono, ekiagera Naboti, Omoyesireeli oria, akanire ng’a takomoa omwando oye aetwe ne chisokoro chiaye. Ahabu akaraara borere bwaye, akaonchora obosio bwaye gochia ase enyasi, akaba taria gento. Ⅴ Korende Yesebeli mokaye agacha asare, akamobooria: “Nase ki enkoro yao ere nomoichano goika nonya nendagera otari koria?”
Ⅵ Ere akairaneria mokaye: “Nekiagera nakwana na Naboti Omoyesireeli, nkamoteebia ng’a ang’e omogondo oye bw’emesabibu, na inche imoe omogondo onde bw’emesabibu ribaga ri’oyoye, gose onye ogwancha imoe chitaabu, korende akaanga, tancha kong’a omogondo oye bw’emesabibu.”
Ⅶ Yesebeli mokaye akamoteebia: “Inee! Naye okogaambera Abaisraeli, gose tari aye? Imoka! Orie endagera, tiga enkoro yao ebe nomosasoko; ninkonyorere omogondo bw’emesabibu o Naboti Omoyesireeli.”
Ⅷ Ase ayio omokungu oria akarika amarube, akabeeka erieta ri’Ahabu na ekemanyererio kiaye ase amarube ayio, akayatoma gochia ase abagaaka, na ase abanene baria bamenyete amo na Naboti ase omochie oye. Ⅸ Omokungu oria akarika ase amarube aria: “Raria okweng’ata gokorwe, na mobeke Naboti ase amasio ’abaanto ase okomoa amasikani. Ⅹ Naende mobeke abanto babere abatagwenereti, bamosoere oborimo, bateebe ng’a oragereirie Nyasae na omorwoti. Erio momosokie isiko, momoake amagena goika akwe.”
Ⅺ Na abanto b’omochie, na abagaaka, na abanene b’omochie oyio, bagakora buna Yesebeli abateebeetie. Buna koreng’ana ariikire ase amarube aria atomete ase bare, Ⅻ bakararia okweng’ata, na bakabeeka Naboti ase amasio ’abaanto ase okomoa amasikani. ⅩⅢ Abanto babere abatagwenereti bagacha, bagaikaransa kororia asare, bakamosoera ase amasio y’abanto, bagateeba: “Naboti oragereirie Nyasae na omorwoti.” Ase igo abanto bakamosokia isiko y’omochie, bakamoaka amagena goika agakwa. ⅩⅣ Erio bagatoma amang’ana ase Yesebeli, bagateeba: “Naboti oakirwe amagena okuure.”
ⅩⅤ Ase obwango ekero Yesebeli aigwete ng’a Naboti oakirwe amagena okuure agateebia Ahabu: “Imoka, oire omogondo bw’emesabibu o Naboti, Omoyesireeli, oria angete gokoa omoe chitaabu, ekiagera bono tari moyo, okuure.” ⅩⅥ Erio kegima, ekero Ahabu aigwete ng’a Naboti okuure akaimoka akagenda ase omogondo oria bw’emesabibu o Naboti Omoyesireeli, akayoira okaba oyoye.
ⅩⅦ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha ase Elija, Omotisibi, rikamoteebia, ⅩⅧ “Imoka, otirimboke oumerane n’Ahabu, omorwoti bw’Abaisraeli, oyio omenyete Samaria. Rora nao are ase omogondo bw’emesabibu o Naboti, agwo ogendire koyoira obe oyoye. ⅩⅨ Omoteebie ng’a aya nar’Omonene atebiire: Aye nigo gwaita Naboti, naende kwairire omogondo oria obeire oyoo. Naende omoteebie ng’a aya nar’Omonene atebiire: Aase agwo chisese chiamenerete amanyinga a Naboti, nao chiramenere nayao.”
ⅩⅩ Ahabu agateebia Elija: “Inee! Aye omobisa one, nkwanyorire.”
Elija akairaneria: “Ee, nakonyorire, ekiagera aye kweoonirie aye omonyene gokora ayare amabe ase obosio bw’Omonene. ⅩⅪ Rora, inche nindete amabe igoro yao; ningosirie kegima orueo, ninsirie korwa asore Ahabu kera omonto omosacha, omosomba na oyotari omosomba. ⅩⅫ Ninkore enyomba yao ebe buna eya Yeroboamu, mosinto o Nebati, naende ebe buna enyomba ya Baasa, mosinto bw’Ahiya, ekiagera kwangechirie goika nabeire nendamwamu, naende kwagerire Abaisraeli bakorire ebibe. ⅩⅩⅢ 21:23: 2 Abar 9:36Omonene oteebire boigo igoro ya Yesebeli; Chisese inachiriere Yesebeli agwo ase oboremo bwa Yesireeli. ⅩⅩⅣ Omonto onde bwensi bw’enyomba y’Ahabu orakwere ase omochie ime, chisese nachimorie; na onde bwensi orakwere isiko aarabu, chinyoni chia igoro nchimorie.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Omonto tari nonya noyomo oreenge buna Ahabu, ere oyio bweoonetie ere omonyene gokora amabe ase obosio bw’Omonene, ekero Yesebeli mokaye amoseegetete. ⅩⅩⅥ Agakora kera okogechia ase ogotunyana emegwekano y’ogosasimwa, buna Abaamori bakorete, bari’Omonene aseretie korwa ase amasio ’Abaisraeli.
ⅩⅩⅦ Ekero Ahabu aigwete amang’ana aria, agatandora chianga chiaye, na akeboyia chigunia, na kweng’ata. Akaraarera chigunia, akaba omokanyeku ore nomoichano.
ⅩⅩⅧ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha ase Elija, Omotisibi, rigateeba, ⅩⅩⅨ “Kwarorire buna Ahabu ekanyire ere omonyene, na obeire omoitongo ase obosio bwane? Ekiagera akorire igo, tinkorenta amabe ayio ase amatuko aye, korende nase amatuko ’omwana oye omomura ndarente amabe ayio ase enyomba yaye.”
