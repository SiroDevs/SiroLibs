ⅩⅣ
Oboremereria ase abanto batari abaikeranu
Ⅰ 14:1: Abakol 2:16Moroisie ere oy’otari na nguru ase okwegena, motari koamererania. Ⅱ Omonto oyomo bwegenete ng’a nanyare koria bionsi, korende ere oy’otari na nguru nigo akoria chinyeni. Ⅲ Oyokoria tachaaya oria otari koria, na oria otari koria tagaambia oria okoria, ekiagera Nyasae omwancheire. Ⅳ Aye naye ning’o okogaambia omosomba bw’omonto onde? Ase omonene oye omonyene nigo ateneine gose nigo akogwa. Ee, natenene, ekiagera Omonene nere ore n’okobua gw’okomotenenia.
Ⅴ Omonto oyomo narengereretie ng’a rituko erimo nerinene kobua erinde, na oyonde arora ng’a amatuko onsi nigo areng’aine. Tiga kera omonto aisaneke ase aya amanyete omonyene. Ⅵ Ere oyokorenda rituko, nigo akorirenda ase Omonene, na ere oyokoria, nigo akoria ase Omonene, ekiagera nigo agoakera Nyasae ng’a mbuya mono; ere oy’otari koria, nigo agokora bo ase Omonene, na aakera Nyasae ng’a mbuya mono. Ⅶ Ekiagera onde tari ase egati yaito ore moyo ase engencho yaye omonyene; na onde tari ogokwa ase engencho yaye omonyene. Ⅷ Inaki onye tore moyo, nigo tore moyo ase Omonene; gose gatorakwe, nigo torakwe ase Omonene. Naboigo, onye tore moyo, gose gatorakwe, nigo tore aba Omonene. Ⅸ Inaki Kristo nigo akwete, naende akaba moyo, erinde abe Omonene ase abare moyo na ase abakuure boigo.
Ⅹ 14:10: 2 Abakr 5:10Korende aye, nase ki okogaambia oy’omino? Gose aye, nase ki ogochaaya oy’omino? Inaki intwe twensi inatoche gotenena bosio bw’ekerogo gi’ekina kia Nyasae. Ⅺ 14:11: Isaya 45:23Inaki neriikire,
“Omonene oteebire: Amaene buna inde moyo,
kera riiru inaribekwe inse ase obosio bwane,
na kera oromeme ’naromobeere Nyasae kirori.”
Ⅻ Ase igo, kera omonto ore ase egati yaito naache gwekwanera ere omonyene ase obosio bwa Nyasae.
ⅩⅢ Ase ayio, titoba nkona‐kogaambania tore, korende anga mbuya kobua togaambe ng’a onde tabeekera oy’omwabo egento gekomogocha gose gekomotanga. ⅩⅣ Namanyire, naende naenekirie ase Omonene Yeso, ng’a gento kende tikeri ekebe ekenyene, korende nigo kere ekebe ase oyo okeroche ng’a negento ekebe. ⅩⅤ Onye oy’omino okoba n’ogwechanda ase engencho y’endagera okoria aye, gwatigire gotaara ase obwanchani. Ase engencho y’endagera yao tomobeyia omonto oria, ere Kristo akwete ase engencho yaye. ⅩⅥ Timotiga ayare amaya ase more atorwe ng’a n’amabe. ⅩⅦ Inaki oborwoti bwa Nyasae tari okoria na okonywa, korende oboronge, na omorembe, na omogooko bikorwa ase Omoika Omochenu. ⅩⅧ Ere oy’ogokorera Kristo ase amang’ana aya onsi nigo akogwenera Nyasae, naende nigo agwancherwa na Mwanyabaanto. ⅩⅨ Ase igo, tiga torigie chingencho chi’omorembe ne chingencho chigwenerete ase okoagachana. ⅩⅩ Ase engencho y’endagera tosaria emeremo ya Nyasae. Bionsi nebichenu; korende ’mbobe omonto oragere abande bagochwe ase engencho y’ebiakoria. ⅩⅪ Mbuya gotaria enyama, nonya ’nkonywa edivai, gose gokora gento kende gionsi keragoche oy’omino. ⅩⅫ Okwegena okwo ore nakwo, obe nakorokwo ase egati yao na Nyasae. Nasesenirie omonto oria otenachereti ekiina ase ring’ana riria anchete. ⅩⅩⅢ Korende oyore ne chitang’u‐tang’u onye okoria, onacheirwe ekiina, ekiagera tarieti ase okwegena; na ogokora konde gwonsi gotarueti ase okwegena nebibe.
