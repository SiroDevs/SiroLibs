ⅩⅣ
Abanto Bakwomania Musa na Aroni
Ⅰ Erio omoganda bwonsi bw’ abanto bagaaka eriogi rinene, bakaba bakorera ase obotuko boria. Ⅱ Abaisraeli bonsi bakaba bakwomania Musa na Aroni; bonsi bagakwana “Nigo anga yaba buya onye ’ntwakwerete Misiri, gose ase erooro eye. Ⅲ Ninki gekogera Omonene agotoira ase ense eria tochie goitwa ase esegi? Abakungu baito, na abana baito, nigo bagochia gwoswa n’ababisa baito. Naki yare koba buya onye ’ntwairana gochia Misiri.” Ⅳ Barabwo bagateebania, “Tochore omonto oyomo abe omoraai oito, toirane Misiri.”
Ⅴ Musa na Aroni bagatung’ama inse bakaumama ase obosio bw’omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli osangererekanete. Ⅵ Erio Yoshua, mosinto o Nuni, na Kalebu, mosinto o Yefune, abwo barenge amo ne chirooti*14:6 chirooti: engencho yaye omotuki ase okomanya amang’ana bobisi chiria chiarootete ense eria, bagatandora chianga chiabo, Ⅶ bagateebia omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli, “Ense eria twataarete ase okoyeroota nigo ere ense engiya mono. Ⅷ Onye Omonene okogokigwa naintwe, na gotoyea toyeire, ense eria n’entenenku ere namabeere naamooke. Ⅸ 14:9: Abaibr 3:16Timobaisa kwanga komoigwera Omonene, na timobaisa koboboa ababiare b’ense eria; abwo nigo barabe buna endagera enyororo ase tore. Baurirwe okorendwa kwabo; Omonene nigo are amo naintwe; timobairoka.” Ⅹ Korende omoganda bwonsi ogaaka eriogi, ogateeba ng’a baitwe n’amagena. Erio obonene bw’Omonene bokaoroka mosiabanoagwo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano ase Abaisraeli bonsi.
Musa ogosabera abanto
Ⅺ Omonene agateebia Musa, “Ngoika ririri abanto aba barabe bagonchaaya? Na ngoika ririri barabe batari konyegena, nonya nakorire ebikone ebinge ase egati yabo? Ⅻ Nkobaaka inde noborwaire bogoita erinde imbasirie, na aye ’ngokore obe egesaku ekenene, naende kere nokobua goetania abanto aba.”
ⅩⅢ 14:13: Okong’ 32:11-14Korende Musa akamoiraneria, “Aye nigo kwarusetie abanto aba korwa ase ense ya Misiri ne chinguru chinene. Ekero Abamisiri bonsi baraigwe aya gwakorire ase abanto aba, ⅩⅣ barabwo ’mbatebie abanto b’ense eye amang’ana ayio. Barabwo konya baigure ng’a aye Omonene nigo ore ase egati y’Abaisraeli, kwarorekanire ase bare obosio ase obosio, ne riire riao nigo riteneine ase bare, naende naye okobatang’ana, omobaso ase riire rire buna esiiro, na obotuko ase omorero ore buna esiiro. ⅩⅤ Bono onye aye kogoita abanto aba bonsi amo, erio abanto b’ebisaku abaraigwe chinkuma chiao ’mbateebe: ⅩⅥ Omonene nigo asinyetwe koira abanto aba ase ense eria konya arire eira akobaa; ase ayio obaiteire ase erooro. ⅩⅦ Bono nagosabire, tiga okobua kwao, aye Omonene, kobe okonene buna aye kwarire eira, kogokwana: ⅩⅧ 14:18: Okong’ 20:5,6; 34:6,7; Okoir 5:9,10; 7:9,10Inche Omonene tindi omwango bw’okoba n’endamwamu, nigo inde omonyene amaabera, naende nigo inde n’obwanchani botari koera. Nigo ’nkwabera amamocho na ogosaria, korende tingotiga koa abana egesusuro goika ekiaare kia gatato, na egia kane, ase engencho y’amabe y’abaibori baabo. ⅩⅨ Ase igo nagosabire, bwabere ebibe bi’abaanto aba koreng’ana nobonene bw’obwanchani bwao botari koera, koreng’ana buna aye kwabaaberete korwa ekero barenge Misiri goika reero iga.”
ⅩⅩ Eri’Omonene agateeba, “Nabaabeire koreng’ana buna gwansabire. ⅩⅪ 14:21: Abaibr 3:18Korende ekeene, buna inche inde moyo, naende buna ense yonsi eraichorwe nobonene bwane, ⅩⅫ onde tari ase abanto aba orasoe ense eria. Aba barorire obonene bwane na ebikone biria nakorete aaria Misiri, na ase erooro eye, korende nonya naboigo babeire bagonteema ase chingaki echi chionsi, nonya ng’ara ikomi, bangire konyigwera. ⅩⅩⅢ Ase barabwo onde tari nonya noyomo, oranyare korora ense eria narierete chisokoro chiabo eira ng’a nimbayee. Onde tari ase abwo banchaayete oranyare koyerora. ⅩⅩⅣ 14:24: Yosh 14:9-12Korende Kalebu, omosomba one, ekiagera abwate omoika ore nogwatananekana korwa ase oyo bwa baria bande, naende obeire okonyigwera buya ase amang’ana onsi, nimoire ase ense eria agendete koroota naende ning’ee abanto b’ororeria rw’aye ense eria ebe eyabo. ⅩⅩⅤ Na bono ekiagera Abaameleki na Abakaanani bamenyete ime ase amakura, inwe ankio moonchoke, mochake koirana gochia ase enchera ekogeenda ase omosomo bw’Enyancha Embariri.”
Omonene akaa abanto abwo bamwemurunganeretie egesusuro
ⅩⅩⅥ Omonene agakwana na Musa na Aroni, akabateebia, ⅩⅩⅦ “Inee! Ngoika ririri omoganda oyo omobe oremurunganie ase ’nde? Naigure Abaisraeli banyemurunganeirie. ⅩⅩⅧ Obateebie ng’a inche Omonene nigo ngoteeba iga: Buna inche inde moyo, naigure amang’ana ayio mwakwanire, ayio naro ndabakorere. ⅩⅩⅨ 14:29: Abaibr 3:17Ekiagera mwemurunganirie ase ’nde, inwe mwensi abamwabarirwe na korikwa, abamwakoorire emiaka emerongo ebere na goetania, nigo morakwe, na emebere yaino ekwete ebeere aiga ase erooro. ⅩⅩⅩ Ase ense eria nabarierete eira na gotiana ng’a nimbayee, onde tari ase more orachie komenyao, otatiga Kalebu, mosinto o Yefune, na Yoshua, mosinto o Nuni. ⅩⅩⅪ Korende abana baino, abwo mwateebete ng’a nabooswe, aba nabwo ndaire aroro, na barabwo mbamenye ase ense eria inwe mwangire. ⅩⅩⅫ Korende inwe nigo morakwere aiga ase erooro, na emebere yaino esirere aiga. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ 14:33: Ogok 7:36Abana baino nababe abarisia ase erooro eye ase engaki y’emiaka emerongo ene, nabarabwo nabachandeke, ekiagera inwe motabeete abegenwa ase ’nde, mbabe bagochandeka goika omonto bw’omoerio bw’ekegori kiaino akwe, na omobere oye osirere ase erooro eye. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Inwe nigo morachandeke ase emiaka emerongo ene, omwaka oyomo ase kera rituko mwarenge koroota ense eria, nainwe namonyare komanya ng’a inki gekoba ekero nkoba mamincha yaino. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Ninche Omonene nakwanire. Ekeene ninkore egento eke ase omoganda oyo omobe osangererekanire amo, na obeire mamincha y’ane. Aiga ase erooro ninsirie abanto aba, aa ase erooro nao barakwere.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Abanto baria Musa atomete gochia koroota ense eria barentete amang’ana amabe igoro y’ense eria, bakagera omoganda bwonsi okemurungania ase Omonene. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Ase ayio Omonene akabaaka noborwaire bogoita, na barabwo bagakwera agwo ase obosio bwaye. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Korende Yoshua, mosinto o Nuni, na Kalebu, mosinto o Yefune, barabwo boka bagatigwa bare moyo korwa ase abanto baria bagendete koroota ense eria.
Abaisraeli bagoteema gosoa Kaanani engaki entang’ani
(Okoir 1:41-46)
ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Musa agateebia Abaisraeli amang’ana onsi Omonene akwanete, na abanto bakarera mono nobororo. ⅩⅬ Ase emambia ye rituko ria kabere bakaimoka, bagatiira gochia ase ense y’ebitunwa, bagakwana, “Rora twachire, nao tore aa, bono twanchire kogeenda ase ense eri’Omonene atoteebetie. Intwe twakorire ebibe.”
ⅩⅬⅠ Korende Musa akabateebia, “Nase ki bono mogosaria ogochika kw’Omonene. Timokonyara konyora ogosesenigwa ase okogeendao. ⅩⅬⅡ Timobaisa kogeenda; moise gokora igo, rirorio nigo mogochia goitwa n’ababisa baino, ekiagera Omonene tari amo nainwe. ⅩⅬⅢ Aroro agwo bosio bwaino Abaameleki na Abakaanani mbareo. Nigo bagochia kobarwania baababue na kobakenya, ekiagera inwe mwaonchokire, mwangire komoigwera Omonene; nere takonyara koba amo nainwe.”
ⅩⅬⅣ Korende bagekora abaremu, bagatiira gochia ase ense y’ebitunwa, nonya Esanduki eria yare ne chibao chiriikire okobwatana kw’Omonene yatigarete ase egetwori, na Musa nao atigarete. ⅩⅬⅤ Erio Abaameleki na Abakaanani abamenyete ase ense y’ebitunwa, bagatirimboka bakarwania Abaisraeli abwo, bakababua na kobaseria, bakababwatia goika Horima.
