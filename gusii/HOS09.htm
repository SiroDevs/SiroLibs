Hosea okoraria egesusuro ase Abaisraeli
Ⅰ Inwe Abaisraeli, timobaisa kogoka gose gochenga buna abanto b’ebisaku bagokora, ekiagera inwe mwakorire obotomani, mwatigire Nyasae oino. Mwagokeire eng’eria y’obotomani ase kera ekeooreri gi’okoorera. Ⅱ Korende ebiooreri bi’okoorera na ebimitero bi’edivai tibigoisanekia abanto, na edivai enyia tekonyorekana ase bare. Ⅲ Barabwo tibakogenderera komenya ase ense y’Omonene; Abaefraimu mbairane gochia ase ense ya Misiri, na agwo Asuri babe bakoria endagera etachenegeti. Ⅳ Tibakorua okoruegwa gw’ekenyugwa gi’edivai ase Omonene, ere takogokera ebing’wanso bararue asare. Okoruegwa okwo kw’endagera bararue asare nigo korabe buna endagera y’ebirero; na omonto onde bwensi orayerie nigo arabe oyo otachenegeti, ekiagera endagera eyio erabe egoisanekia oyore nenchara rioka, tekorentwa ase enyomba y’Omonene. Ⅴ Ninki morakore ekero Rituko Rinene ri’omoyega o Omonene riraike? Ⅵ Rora, abanto abwo batamire ng’a tibaabaisa gosirigwa; ense ya Misiri nebasangererie amo, na omochie o Memifisi nobatindeke. Amasa namere aase babeegete chinibo chiabo chi’echifeta, chiria chianchire, na amagwa namere ase chinyomba chiabo ime.
Ⅶ 9:7: Luk 21:22Amatuko ’egesusuro aikire, ee, amatuko ay’ogoakanera ogokora kw’abaanto aikire. Abaisraeli mbamanye ayio. Omobani obeire omoriri, na omonto ore n’Omoika obeire ebarimo, ekiagera obobe bwaino bwabeire obonge, na okogecha kwaino kwabeire okonene. Ⅷ Nyasae ontomire buna omobaabi gochia ase abanto baye Abaisraeli. Korende kera ekero ngotema kogenda nigo babeire emeeto y’obotegi ase chinchera chiabo chionsi; obobisa bwabo nigo bore ime ase enyomba ya Nyasae obo. Ⅸ 9:9: Abag 19:1-30Besaririe kegima buna yabeete ase amatuko aria a kare agwo Gibea. Omonene nainyorete ebibe biabo, abae egesusuro.
Ebibe bi’Abaisraeli na aya abanyorete
Ⅹ 9:10: Omob 25:1-5“Inche Omonene nigo nanyorete Abaisraeli banga buna emesabibu ase erooro; nigo naroche chisokoro chiaino chinga buna okwama ogotang’ani kw’emeko. Korende bakagenda Baali‐Peori, bakerua abanyene gosasiima enyasae eria y’obosooku, bakaba okogechia buna enyasae eria banchete ere. Ⅺ Obonene bw’Abaefraimu mboiruruke buna enyoni bogende. Okoibora tigokoba ase bare, gose abakungu tibakoba barito, bonsi mbagombae. Ⅻ Baise gokinia abana, ning’ite abana abwo, onde tagotigara ore moyo nonya noyomo. Obobe nobwabo abanyene, ekero inche ndabatige.
ⅩⅢ “Nkarora Abaefraimu banga buna omogondo omuya buna Turo orenge, korende ase rituko erimo mbabe bakoira abana babo gochia goitwa. ⅩⅣ Aye Omonene, bae eki ogwenerete kobaa, ogombayie chinda chiabo, ne chimbeere chiabo chiome pa.
ⅩⅤ “Obobe bwabo bwonsi nigo bore Giligali, agwo nao nachakerete kobagecha. Nimbaserie barue ase enyomba yane ase engencho y’ogokora kwabo okobe. Tinkogenderera kobancha; abagaambi babo bonsi nigo bare abasaria. ⅩⅥ Abaefraimu baakirwe, omori obo bwomire, tibakonyara kwama okwama. Nabaisa koibora, goika ing’ite abana abwo banchete.”
Ekiina kia Nyasae ase Abaisraeli
ⅩⅦ Nyasae one nabaange, ekiagera baangete komoigwera; mbabe bakonagotayaya ase egati y’abanto b’ebisaku.
