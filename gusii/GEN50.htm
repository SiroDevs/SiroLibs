Ogotindekwa kwa Yakobo
Ⅰ Erio Yusufu akagwera ise ase obosio bwaye, akarera na komonyunyunta. Ⅱ Agachiika abarwaria abaare komokorera, ng’a baake Israeli ise amafuta, tabaisa kogunda; naboigo abarwaria bamokorerete, Ⅲ goika amatuko emerongo ene agaikerana. Ase amatuko ang’ana buna ayio naro egetondo kiare goakwa amariogo. Abamisiri bakamoreera Israeli amatuko emerongo etano na ebere.
Ⅳ Ekero amatuko okomoreera Israeli aerete, Yusufu agakwana nabaanto b’enka ya Farao, akabora: “Onye nkonyora ogwancherwa ase amaiso aino, komoranche monteeberie Farao Ⅴ 50:5: Omoch 47:29-31ng’a tata nandierete omwando ogoteeba: Ng’ang’e inde gokwa, ong’ire ontindeke ase oboina boria naremete ase ense ya Kaanani. Momonsabere ribaga ngende gotindeka tata, erio ning’irane.”
Ⅵ Farao akairaneria: “Tiira ochie gotindeka iso, buna akorierete omwando.”
Ⅶ Erio Yusufu agatiira gochia gotindeka ise. Abasomba bonsi ba Farao bakagenda amo nere, na abagaaka bonsi b’enka yaye, na abagaaka bonsi ba Misiri, Ⅷ na bonsi ab’enka ya Yusufu, amo nabamwabo, na ab’enka ya ise. Abana babo boka bagatigara Goseni amo ne ching’ondi ne chiombe chiabo. Ⅸ Naende Yusufu akaira amo nere chigari na abariini chibarasi; bakaba omoganda omonene mono.
Ⅹ Ekero baigete ase ekeooreri gi’Atadi ng’umbu y’orooche rwa Yorodani, bakaba bakorera ebirero ebinene mono. Agwo Yusufu akagenderera komoreera ise ase engaki y’amatuko atano na abere. Ⅺ Na Abakaanani abamenyete ase ense eria, ekero bachia korora ebirero agwo Atadi ase ekeooreri, bakaboora: “Ebirero ebi bi’Abamisiri nebinene mono.” Ase ayio, aase aria ng’umbu ya Yorodani akarokwa Abeli‐Misiri.*50:11 Abeli‐Misiri: engencho yaye: Ekerero ki’Abamisiri
Ⅻ Naboigo basinto ba Yakobo bakorete buna ise abachiigete. ⅩⅢ 50:13: Ogok 7:16Bakamoimokia, bakamoira goika ase ense ya Kaanani. Erio bakamotindeka ase rikuruma riria riare, ase oboremo agwo Makipela, moocha ya Mamure, oboremo boria Aburaamu agorete korwa ase Efuroni Omohiti abe aase aye agotindeka abakuure. ⅩⅣ Yusufu, ekero akoorire gotindeka ise, akairana Misiri amo nabamwabo na bonsi abatiirete amo nere.
Yusufu okoremia abamwabo
ⅩⅤ Ekero abamwabo Yusufu barooche ng’a ise okuure bagateebania. “Aande Yusufu nigo aratorege, arusie egesiomba ase amabe aria twamokorerete.” ⅩⅥ Ase ayio bagatoma abanto ase Yusufu komoteebia ng’a: “Iso nigo ateebete iga ritang’ani atarakwa. ⅩⅦ Moteebie Yusufu iga: Nagosabire obaabere abamino amamocho abo na ebibe biabo bagokorerete. Nimanyete ng’a mbagokorerete bobe. Ase igo, bono twagosabire, otwabere ebibe biaito, naki intwe nabasomba ba Nyasae o iso.” Yusufu akarera ekero abamwabo bamoboretie amang’ana ayio.
ⅩⅧ Erio abamwabo bagacha, bagatung’ama inse ase obosio bwaye, bakaboora: “Intwe iga nabasomba bao tore.”
ⅩⅨ Korende Yusufu akabateebia: “Timoiroka! Inee! Inche inde ribaga ria Nyasae? ⅩⅩ Ekeene, inwe nabo mwarengereretie gonkorera amabe, korende Nyasae akayaonchora akaba amaya, na buna mwarorire reero iga, otooririe obogima bw’abaanto abange. ⅩⅪ Gaki, bono timoiroka! Nimbaragerie inwe amo nabana baino.” Naboigo akaba okobaremia, agakwana nabarabwo amang’ana amaya.
Ogokwa kwa Yusufu
ⅩⅫ Yusufu akamenya Misiri, ere amo nabaanto bamwabo, agakoora emiaka rigana erimo, na ikomi (110). ⅩⅩⅢ Agwo akarora abana b’Efraimu na abachokoro baye goika ekiaare kia gatato, akarora na abana ba Makiri, omwana o Manase, baiboreretwe ase Yusufu. ⅩⅩⅣ Yusufu agateebia abamwabo: “Ng’ang’e inde gokwa, korende amaene Nyasae nabanyorere enchera y’okobarusia ase ense eye, abatiirie mogende ase ense eria abarierete eira na gotiana, ng’a nae Aburaamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo.”
ⅩⅩⅤ 50:25: Okong’ 13:19; Yosh 24:32; Abaibr 11:22Yusufu agatiania abana ba Israeli agwo, akabora: “Bono, ekero Nyasae arabachere abakoonye, goika moire amauga aane; mogende naro.” ⅩⅩⅥ Erio Yusufu erio agakwa ore n’emiaka rigana erimo na ikomi (110). Bakamoaka amafuta, tabaisa kogunda, bakamobeeka ase esanduki ime aaria Misiri.
