Ororeria rwa Rubeni
Ⅰ 5:1: Omoch 35:22; 49:3,4Aba nabwo abana abamura ba Rubeni oyio orenge omwana omomura omotangi o Israeli. (Korende ekiagera naraarete na moga ise, omwando oye bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi okaegwa abana abamura ba Yusufu, mosinto o Israeli. Korende Yusufu tarikiri ase omooroberio bw’okonyora omwando bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi. Ⅱ 5:2: Omoch 49:8-10Nonya Yuda obete ne chinguru ase egati y’abamura bamwabo, na korwa asare nao omogambi arwete, omwando bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi nigo obete oyo o Yusufu.) Ⅲ Abana abamura ba Rubeni, omwana omotangi o Israeli, nabwo aba: Hanoki na Palu, Hezironi na Karimi.
Ⅳ 5:4: 2 Abar 15:29Ororeria rwa Yoel narwo oro: Yoel akaibora Semaya, na Semaya akaibora Gogi, na Gogi akaibora Simei.
Ⅴ Simei akaibora Mika, na Mika akaibora Reaya, na Reaya akaibora Baali. Ⅵ Baali akaibora Beera oria Tiligati‐Pileseri omorwoti bw’Abaasuri, aosete*5:6 aosete: engencho yaye okoirwa obosomba, akamoira ase obosomba. Beera orenge omoteneneri bw’abaanto ba Rubeni.
Ⅶ Na abamwabo ase chiamate chiabo buna barigetwe ase omooroberio bwe chinderia chi’ebiare biabo nabwo aba: Yeieli, nere orenge omoteneneri obo, na Zakaria, Ⅷ na Bela, mosinto bw’Asasi o Sema, o Yoel, Barabwo nigo bamenyete agwo Aroeri goikera orobebe rwa Nebo na Baali‐Meoni. Ⅸ Ensemo ya moocha bakamenya goikera egesoero ki’erooro egoikera orooche rw’Eufarati, ekiagera amaicho abo nigo abuchete mono ase ense ya Gileadi.
Ⅹ Ase engaki eria Saulo arenge kogamba bakarwana esegi n’Abahagari, bakabaita, na barabwo bakamenya ase chiema chiabo agwo ase ense yonsi moocha ya Gileadi.
Ororeria rwa Gadi
Ⅺ Abanto b’ororeria rwa Gadi bakamenya sugusu y’abanto b’orwa Rubeni agwo ase ense ya Basani, bagaikera omochie o Saleka. Ⅻ Yoel nere orenge omonene, na Safamu oyo o kabere, naende Yanai na Safati bakaba agwo Basani. ⅩⅢ Na abamwabo be chiamate chiabo nabwo aba: Mikaeli na Mesulamu, Seba na Yorai, Yakani na Sia, na Eberi, bonsi mbatano na babere. ⅩⅣ Aba nabwo barenge basinto b’Abihaili, mosinto o Huri o Yaroa, o Gileadi, o Mikaeli, o Yesisai, o Yado, o Busi. ⅩⅤ Ahi, mosinto bw’Abidieli, o Guni, nere orenge omoteneneri bwe chiamate chiabo. ⅩⅥ Barabwo bonsi bakamenya agwo Gileadi na Basani, na ase emechie y’aroro na ase oborisia bwonsi bwa Saroni goikera chinsinyo chiaye. ⅩⅦ Aba bonsi nigo baabaretwe na korikwa ekero omobaro bwe chinderia chiabo okoretwe ase amatuko ’okogamba kwa Yotamu, omorwoti bw’Abayuda, na aya okwa Yeroboamu, omorwoti bw’Abaisraeli.
Abarwani esegi b’ebisaku bia moocha
ⅩⅧ Abanto ba Rubeni, na Abagadi, na ekeng’ese ki’egesaku kia Manase, nigo babwate abanto chituoni barenge kobogoria chinguba na emioro, naende nigo bamanyete korasa nemegwe abanto abang’aini ase esegi. Abwo nigo barenge chilifu emerongo ene na inye, na amagana atano na abere, na emerongo etano nomo (44,760), nigo barenge bebegete ang’e korwana esegi. ⅩⅨ Bakaba bakorwana esegi n’Abahagari abarwete ase Yeturi na Nafisi, na Nodabu. ⅩⅩ Ekiagera bamwegenete Nyasae bakarera ase okonyora ogokonywa, na Nyasae akaigwa ogosaba kwabo, na barabwo bakabua Abahagari na abanto bonsi barenge amo nabarabwo. ⅩⅪ Bagasakora chitugo chiabo: Chingamia chilifu emerongo etano (50,000), ne ching’ondi chilifu amagana abere na emerongo etano (250,000) ne chitigere chilifu ibere (2,000). Naende bagoosa abanto chilifu rigana erimo (100,000), ⅩⅫ na bagaita abanto abange, ekiagera esegi yarenge eya Nyasae. Erio bakamenya aase abo goika engaki y’okong’anyigwa.
Abanto ba Manase abamenyete moocha
ⅩⅩⅢ Abanto ekeng’ese ki’egesaku kia Manase bakamenya ense eria; bakamentekana mono, bakamenya korwa orobebe rwa Basani goika Baali‐Herimoni, na Seniri, na egetunwa kia Herimoni. ⅩⅩⅣ Aba nabwo barenge abateneneri be chiamate chiabo: Eferi na Ishi, Elieli na Asirieli, Yeremia na Hodawia, na Yadieli. Abwo nabaanto chituoni bachiete nkuma ase okorwana esegi.
Ebisaku bia moocha bigwoswa
ⅩⅩⅤ Korende abanto b’ebisaku bia moocha bagakora ogosaria ase Nyasae bwe chisokoro chiabo, bagakora obotayayi, bakabwatia chinyasae chi’abaanto b’ense eria, abwo Nyasae asiretie korwa ase bare. ⅩⅩⅥ 5:26: a 2 Abar 15:19; b 2 Abar 15:29; c 2 Abar 17:6Ase ayio Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli agaseegeta omoika o Puli, omorwoti bw’Abaasuri (nere Tiligati‐Pileseri), na Puli oyio akaira abanto b’ororeria rwa Rubeni, na ab’orwa Gadi, na ekeng’ese ki ’egesaku kia Manase goika Hala na Habori, na Hara, na goika ase orooche rwa Goseni; nao bare goika reero iga.
