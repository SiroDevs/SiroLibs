Ⅺ
Paulo na’bamotomwa b’oborimo
Ⅰ Anga yaba buya onye mwandemereria ake ase oboriri bwane. Ee, namanyire ng’a mondemererie. Ⅱ Naki nabaroreire ribero, na eri neribero ria Nyasae; naki nabariereire boko ase omosacha oyomo, erinde nyare kobarenta buna omoiseke omwekungi omochenu ase Kristo. Ⅲ 11:3: Omoch 3:1-5,13Korende nairokire ng’a buna eng’iti eria, ase obong’ainereria bwaye, yang’ainete Hawa, naboigo ebirengererio biaino birasareke, na mobaise gotiga obwegenwa bwaino bw’ekeene more nabwo ase Kristo. Ⅳ Naki onye omonto ogocha okorandia Yeso onde omoao, otatiga oyo twarandetie intwe, gose mwanche omoika onde omoao oyomotanyorete, gose enchiri ende eng’ao eyemotanchete, igo moremereretie buya nayio. Ⅴ Naki igo nkagete ng’a inche tindemeiri gento eki abatomwa baria abanene bare nakio. Ⅵ Nonya timanyeti gokwana buya, korende obong’aini nimbwate, na ase kera enchera twaorokirie amang’ana onsi ase more.
Ⅶ Inee! Nebibe nakorire ase okweirania inse, erio mokiinigwe, ekiagera nabarandeirie enchiri ya Nyasae ntari neng’eria? Ⅷ Naurire abanto be chikanisa chinde enibo yabo ase okorusia eng’eria yane ase bare, erinde mbakorere inwe. Ⅸ 11:9: Abafl 4:15-18Inche ekero narenge amo nainwe ekero naremeretwe, timorarereti monto onde, ekiagera abaminto bagachia gocha korwa Makedonia bagankonya ase okoremerwa kwane. Ase amang’ana onsi nkerenda inche omonyene timbaraarereria nonya ng’ake, naende ningenderere gokora bo. Ⅹ Naende buna ekeene gia Kristo kere ime ase ’nde, omonto tari oranyare gontanga ase ogwetogia okwo ase chinsemo chi’Akaya. Ⅺ Nase ki? Ekiagera ntabancheti inwe? Nyasae namanyete ng’a nimbanchete!
Ⅻ Korende eri ngokora, ningenderere korikora, erinde mbatange abakorigia ribaga tibanyora ribaga ri’ogwetogia barorekane koba buna intwe tore. ⅩⅢ Naki abwo nabatomwa b’oborimo, bagokora emeremo ase obong’ainereria, bakweonchora babe omogwekano bw’abatomwa ba Kristo. ⅩⅣ Nabo tari ogokumia, ekiagera Saitani omonyene nigo akweonchora bwekora buna omomalaika bw’omobaso. ⅩⅤ Ase ayio tari ring’ana rigeni ng’a abasomba baye nabarabwo bakweonchora bekora buna abasomba b’oboronge. Omoerio obo nobe koreng’ana nemeremo yabo.
Ogochandeka gwa Paulo buna omotomwa
ⅩⅥ Nakwanire naende, omonto takaga ng’a inche nomoriri inde; korende namonkagera bo, monyanche buna omonto omoriri, erinde nainche boigo ninyare gwetogia ake. ⅩⅦ (Eri ngokwana tindi korikwana ase okobua kw’Omonene, korende buna omoriri ase oboremu obo bw’ogwetogia ⅩⅧ ekiagera abange bagwetogia ase engencho yabo abanyene, na inche boigo ninyetogie). ⅩⅨ Inwe, ekiagera more nobong’aini, nigo mokoremereria nabariri ase omogooko. ⅩⅩ Naki iga mokoremereria nomonto, karabakore mobe abasomba, kararie ebiaino, abasakore, kareimokererie igoro, karabaake inwe ase obosio. ⅩⅪ Ase obosooku bwane nigo ngokwana ng’a nigo twarenge abareu ase ogokora amang’ana aya.
Emechando ya Paulo na obokong’u bwaye
Korende onye omonto ore noboremu bw’ogwetogia ase rinde rionsi (nakwanire ase oboriri), na inche ninde noboremu boigo bw’ogwetogia. ⅩⅫ Barabwo n’Abaiberania? Nainche boigo. Barabwo n’Abaisraeli? Nainche boigo. Barabwo noroiboro rwa Aburaamu? Nainche boigo. ⅩⅩⅢ 11:23: Ogok 16:23Inee! Barabwo nabasomba ba Kristo? (Bono nigo ngokwana buna ebarimo). Inche mbuya kobua; ase emeremo kobua mono, ase ogosibwa kobua mono, ase ogoakwa goetania ekerengo, ang’e namakweri ebiro ebinge. ⅩⅩⅣ 11:24: Okoir 25:3Ase Abayahudi ara gatano nkanyora ogoakwa emerongo ene otatiga okomo. ⅩⅩⅤ 11:25: a Ogok 16:22; b Ogok 14:19Ara gatato ngaakwa chinyimbo, erimo ngaakwa amagena. Ara gatato ngasarekerwa nemeeri, obotuko obomo na omobaso oyomo ngaikaransa igoro y’endiba; ⅩⅩⅥ 11:26: a Ogok 9:23; b Ogok 14:5nkaba ase orogendo ekero ase ekero, ase emechando ye chindooche, ase ogochandwa nabauri, ase ogochandwa neamate yane, ase ogochandwa nabaanto b’Ebisaku, ase emechando emechie ime, ase emechando y’erooro, ase emechando y’enyancha, ase ogochandwa nabaminto b’oborimo; ⅩⅩⅦ ase emeremo na obokong’u, ase okogeesa ara aange, ase enchara na erang’o, ase ogotanyora endagera ara aange, ase embeo na ase okoba getirianda. ⅩⅩⅧ Kobua ase amang’ana onsi ayare korwa isiko naro okorigereria gwe chikanisa chionsi, ayare gonchichera kera rituko. ⅩⅩⅨ Ning’o ore omoreu, na inche timba omoreu? Ning’o ogerire ogwe, na inche tingechigwa?
ⅩⅩⅩ Onye ngwenerete gwetogia, ninyetogerie amang’ana ’oboreu bwane. ⅩⅩⅪ Nyasae, Ise Omonene Yeso, oyorabakwe goika kare na kare, omanyire ng’a tindi gokwana oborimo. ⅩⅩⅫ 11:32: Ogok 9:23-25Ekero narenge aaria Damesiki gabana, oyorenge inse y’omorwoti Areta, nigo arendete omochie o Damesiki, erio ambwate. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Korende nkabekwa ase egetonga ime, ngaikerigwa ase ekebao ase orwaki, ngatooreka korwa ase amaboko aye.
