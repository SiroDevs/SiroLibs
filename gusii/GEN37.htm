Chindoto chia Yusufu
Ⅰ Yakobo akaba okomenya ase ense y’Abakaanani, eyio ase ise nere areenge omenyete buna omomenyi. Ⅱ Oyo noro omogano bw’ororeeria rwa Yakobo.
Yusufu, ekero aikire emiaka ikomi, na etano na ebere, nigo areenge korisia amaicho amo na bamura bamwabo. Omosae oyio akaba ore amo nabamura bamwabo, abana ba Bilia na Silpa, abakungu ba ise. Yusufu akaba okorentera ise amang’ana igoro y’ogokora kwabo okobe.
Ⅲ Bono Israeli nigo anchete Yusufu kobua abana baye bande bonsi, ekiagera nigo areenge omwana oye bw’obogotu. Akamoroiseria eanga engiya entambe. Ⅳ Abamwabo ekero barooche ng’a ise nigo amwanchete ere kobua abamwabo bonsi, barabwo bakamogecha, tibare gokwana amang’ana y’omorembe amo nere.
Ⅴ Yusufu akaroota chindoto, na ekero ateebetie abamwabo chindoto echio, barabwo bakamenta mono komogecha. Ⅵ Yusufu akabateebia: “Montegerere, moigwe okoroota oko narootete. Ⅶ Nkarora ng’a intwe nigo twareenge mogondo tokoboa emesaima y’endagera, na omosaima one okaimoka ogatenena boronge, na emesaima yaino ekaba ekoyoetanana, egotugama inse ase omosaima one.”
Ⅷ Abamwabo bakamobooria: “Inee! Aye nigo orengereretie ng’a nigo orabe omogambi oito, otogambere?” Ase igo, barabwo bakamenta komogecha mono ase engencho y’okoroota kwaye, na ase engencho y’amang’ana aye.
Ⅸ Naende akaroota konde, na okwo agateebia abamwabo, akabora: “Moigwe! Inche nigo narootete okoroota konde. Nkarora erioba, na omotienyi, ne ching’enang’eni ikomi nemo bigotugama inse ase obosio bwane.”
Ⅹ Korende ekero ateebetie ise na bamura bamwabo amang’ana ayio, ise akamotogonyera, akamobooria: “nkoroota ki okwo okoroota? Inee! Inche na nyoko, na bamura bamino, amaene, nigo torache togotugamere togosasime aye?” Ⅺ 37:11: Ogok 7:9Abamwabo bakamobwaterwa ribero, korende ise ere akagacha ogokwana okwo ase enkoro yaye ime.
Yusufu okoonigwa nabamwabo na koirwa Misiri
Ⅻ Erio abamwabo bakagenda korisia amaicho a ise ang’e na Sekemu. ⅩⅢ Na Israeli akarangeria Yusufu, akamoteebia: “Inee! Abamino tibakari korisia amaicho aaria Sekemu? Inchuo bono ngotome gochia ase bakare.”
Yusufu agaitaba: “Nao inde aa.”
ⅩⅣ Ise akamoteebia: “Genda bono orore gose abamino mbuya bare amo namaicho, erinde ondetere amang’ana.” Ase igo, akamotoma korwa ase omweya o Heburoni, akageenda agaika Sekemu; ⅩⅤ omonto oyomo akamonyora, ekero areenge kona‐goetanana ase erooro, na omonto oyio akamobooria: “Inki okorigia?”
ⅩⅥ Akamoiraneria: “Nabaminto nkorigia; koranche nteebie aase bakorisia amaicho.”
ⅩⅦ Omonto oria akabateebia: “Barure aa, bachiire, naki nigo nabaigwa bagoteebania: Tiga togende Dotani.” Erio Yusufu akababwatia korwa magega, akabanyora Dotani.
ⅩⅧ Barabwo bakamorora ere okare egeka, ritang’ani konya ataraika ang’e asebare, bakagaamba buna bamoite. ⅩⅨ Barabwo bagateebania: “Omonyandooto oyio ogocha. ⅩⅩ Bono ntore ne ribaga; tiga tomoite, tomorute ase engoro eyemo ye chingoro chire aa, erio toteebe ng’a neng’iti y’orosana yamoriete, na torore chindoto echio chiaye buna chirabe.”
ⅩⅪ Rubeni ekero ayaigwete, akarigia komotooria korwa ase amaboko abo, akabakania: “Yaya! Titomoita akwe.” ⅩⅫ Naende akabateebia: “Timoitera amanyinga aye; morute ase engoro eyemo y’echi chire aa ase erooro, korende timorambora amaboko aino gochia asare ase okomoita.” Ere nigo abateebetie igo, erinde anyare komotooria korwa ase amaboko abo, amoiranie ase ise. ⅩⅩⅢ Ekaba ekero Yusufu achia goika ase abamwabo, barabwo bakamorusia eanga eria engiya entambe abeegete, ⅩⅩⅣ bakamobwata, bakamoruta engoro ime. Engoro eyio nigo yareenge bosa, mache tareengeo.
ⅩⅩⅤ Erio bagaikaransa inse koragera; bagachia koimokia amaiso abo korigereria, bakarora omoganda bw’Abaisumaeli ogocha korwa Gileadi. Barabwo nigo baare ne chingamia chiabo, chibogoretie ubani, na amaguta akogwenia, na manemane, ase orogendo rwabo baare kogenda Misiri. ⅩⅩⅥ Agwo Yuda agateebia abamwabo: “Inki eratogwenere toite oyominto na kobisa ogokwa kwaye? ⅩⅩⅦ Tiga tomoonie ase Abaisumaeli aba, erinde titobaisa komoita namaboko aito, ekiagera noyominto, toiboire komo.” Na abamwabo bagancherana nere. ⅩⅩⅧ 37:28: Ogok 7:9Erio Abamidiani bagaeta aroro, baare gokora oboonchoreria; abamwabo bakarusia Yusufu korwa ase engoro eria, na komooneria Abaisumaeli ase chisekeli chi’echifeta emerongo ebere; barabwo bakamoira goika Misiri.
ⅩⅩⅨ Rubeni agachia koirana korigereria ase engoro eria, akanyora Yusufu taiyo ase engoro, ere agatandora chianga chiaye, ⅩⅩⅩ akeonchora gochia ase abamwabo, akabateebia: “Omwana timonyoreti ase engoro. Ng’ai rende nkogenda?”
ⅩⅩⅪ Erio bakaimokia eanga ya Yusufu, na bakanyenya embori, bakaimokia eanga eria, bakayetobeka ase amanyinga; ⅩⅩⅫ bakagenda n’eanga eria entambe bakayeirera ise, bakamobateebia: “Yemanye eanga eye gose ney’omwana oo gose tari ero.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Yakobo akayemanya, akabora: “Neanga y’omwana one, amaene neching’iti chi’orosana chiamoitire; Yusufu orirwe!” ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Erio Yakobo agatandora chianga chiaye, akeboyia chigunia kenema kiaye, akaba okomoreera omwana oye engaki entambe. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Abana baye, abamura na abaiseke bonsi, bakaimoka gochia komoremia, korende akaanga, taremigwa, agateeba: “Yaya, goika inkwe, ngende inse ase oboina nkona‐komoreera omwana one.” Ise akaba okona‐kweumia.
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Na Abamidiani baria bakamoonia aaria Misiri ase Potifari, omoteneneri bw’enka ya Farao na omotang’ani bw’abarendi baye bande.
