ⅩⅩⅩ
Ⅰ Ekero Raeli arooche ng’a tari koiborera Yakobo abana, akarorera mosubati omwabo ribero, agateebia Yakobo: “Ing’a abana! Otabang’eti, rirorio nigo ndakwe!”
Ⅱ Yakobo akabea Raeli mono, akamobooria: “Inee! Ninche inde ribaga ria Nyasae, oyogotangete tikwaegwa abana?”
Ⅲ Erio akamoiraneria: “Nimbwate omosomba one omoiseke, Bilia. Motware, erinde inyore abana korwa asare, barokwe abana baane, na goetera asare na inche inyore oroiboro.” Ⅳ Naboigo Yakobo akaegwa Bilia, omosomba omoiseke o Raeli, abe mokaye, na Yakobo akaba nere. Ⅴ Bilia akaba morito, akaiborera Yakobo omwana omoisia. Ⅵ Erio Raeli akabora: “Nyasae ong’eire oboronge bwane, amaene, oigure ekerero kiane, ong’eire omwana omoisia.” Ase ayio akamoroka Dani.*30:6 Dani: engencho yaye: ong’eire oboronge bwane Ⅶ Bilia, omosomba o Raeli, akaba morito naende, akaiborera Yakobo omwana omoisia o kabere. Ⅷ Raeli akabora: “Ase okoamererania okonene namenyire nkoamererania na mosubati ominto, nabuire.” Ase igo, akamoroka Nafutali.†30:8 Nafutali: engencho yane: okoamere rania
Ⅸ Ekero Lea achia korora ng’a Bilia okoorire koibora abana, akaimokia Silpa, omosomba oye omoiseke, akamomoa Yakobo, abe mokaye. Ⅹ Erio Silpa, omosomba omoiseke o Lea, akaiborera Yakobo omwana omoisia. Ⅺ Lea akabora: “Ninde negesio.” Akamoroka Gadi.‡30:11 Gadi: engencho yaye aande: egesio ekiya Ⅻ Silpa, omosomba o Lea, akaiborera Yakobo omwana omoisia o kabere. ⅩⅢ Lea akabora: “Inche ninde nomogooko omonene, naki abakungu nabandoke Oyore n’omogooko.” Ase igo, akamoroka Aseri.§30:13 Aseri: engencho yaye: omogooko
ⅩⅣ Ase engaki ye rigesa ri’engano Rubeni akagenda akanyora amasooni ng’ombe *30:14 Amasooni ng’ombe: amatunda amarabu ko nigo anga buna chinyanya getii, akayatwa, akayarentera Lea ng’ina.
ⅩⅤ Erio Raeli agasaba Lea amwatanere amasooni ng’ombe y’omwana oye, korende Lea akamoiraneria: “Inee! Nering’ana rike kwang’urire omosacha one, na bono korigetie koira amasooni ng’ombe ay’omwana one boigo?”
Raeli akamoiraneria: “Reero nache araare naye, korang’e amasooni ng’ombe y’omwana oo.”
ⅩⅥ Ekero Yakobo areenge koirana korwa mogondo mogoroba, Lea akagenda komoorotota, akamoteebia: “Reero goika oraare mwane, nakogorire namasooni ng’ombe ay’omwana one.” Ase obotuko Yakobo akaba nere.
ⅩⅦ Nyasae akaigwa Lea, na ere akaba morito, akaiborera Yakobo omwana omoisia o gatano. ⅩⅧ Lea akabora: “Nyasae ong’eire eng’eria yane, ekiagera naete omosacha one omosomba one omoiseke.” Ase igo, Akaaroka omwana oria Isakaru†30:18 Isakaru: engencho yaye aande: eng’eria. ⅩⅨ Lea akaba morito naende, akaiborera Yakobo omwana omoisia o gatano na rimo. ⅩⅩ Erio Lea akabora: “Nyasae ong’eire ekeegwa ekiya; bono omosacha one nansike, ekiagera namoiboreire abana abaisia batano nomo.” Ase igo, akamoroka Sabuloni.‡30:20 Sabuloni: engencho yaye aande: okomanya, gose: amasikani ⅩⅪ Magega yaye akaibora omwana omoiseke, akamoroka Dina.
ⅩⅫ Korende Nyasae akamoinyora Raeli, akaigwa ogosaba kwaye, na akaigora enda yaye; ⅩⅩⅢ ere akaba morito, akaibora omwana omoisia, akabora: “Nyasae onduseirie ogochaywa kwane.” ⅩⅩⅣ Akaroka omwana oria Yusufu§30:24 Yusufu: engencho yaye: amente oyonde, akabora: “Tiga Omonene karanche amentere omwana onde omoisia.”
Yakobo Okogomba na Labani
ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero Raeli aiboire Yusufu, Yakobo agateebia Labani: “Ing’a ribaga ngende seito, ase ense yane. ⅩⅩⅥ Ing’a abakungu baane na abana baane, aba namenyire nkogokorera, ontige ’ngende seito; naki nomanyete emeremo nabeire nkogokorera.” ⅩⅩⅦ Labani akamoiraneria: “Oise kong’anchera nkwane iga, rigereria namanyire boronge ng’a Omonene onsesenirie mono ase engencho yao. ⅩⅩⅧ Bono nteebia eng’eria yao ey’oganetie, na inche ninkoyee.” ⅩⅩⅨ Yakobo akamoiraneria: “Aye omonyene nomanyete buna nabeire omokoreri oo, na amaicho ao buna abeire ase oborendi bwane. ⅩⅩⅩ Naki enibo yao nigo yareenge enke ritang’ani ekero ntaracha aa, korende bono yamentekanire mono kegima. Omonene ogosesenirie aye, aande onsi ase nabeire nkogenda kogokorera emeremo. Ko bono, inche indi ndabe nkoroisia amang’ana abwone inche omonyene?” ⅩⅩⅪ Labani akamobooria: “Ninki ndakoe rende?” Yakobo akamoiraneria: “Tinganeti ong’e kende. Oise gonkorera ring’ana eri ndagoteebie rioka, rirorio naende ningenderere korisia amaicho ao, na koyarenda. ⅩⅩⅫ Tiga ng’ete reero ase amaicho ao onsi, ndusie korwao kera eng’ondi emechege na egesembe, na emaanwa y’eng’ondi emwamu, na kera embori nyankororo na nyankanga. Erinde echio nachio chirabe eng’eria yane. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Ase igo, amatuko agochia bosio, ekero orache korora eng’eria yane, oboronge bwane nabombeere kirori. Kera embori etari nyankanga gose nyankororo, na kera emaanwa y’eng’ondi etari emwamu, onye ekonyorekana ase ’nde, oyebare ng’a eyio nigo eibire.” ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Labani agancha amang’ana ayio, agateeba: “Tiga abe buna gwateebire.” ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Ase rituko erio erio Labani akarusia chigoree chiimarera ne chinkororo, ne chimbori chiaberi, echiare nyankanga ne chire nyankororo, na kera eyare nebimoa ebirabu ase omobere oye, na kera eng’ondi eyare emwamu. Echio chionsi agachia abana baye abamura bachirende. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Erio ageatanana korwa ase Yakobo oboare bw’orogendo rw’amatuko atato; na Yakobo agatigara okorisia riicho ria Labani eriatigarete.
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Yakobo agatwa ebite ebibese biomopopla*30:37 Omopopla: Ebite bi’emete gete enga buna omosobeti, na omwalozi, na omouru. akabisata chinsemo, erinde emeng’ororia emerabu ekarorekana ase ebite biria. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Erio akabeeka ebite biria ase chintuba chi’amaache ase amaicho are gocha konywera. Na ekiagera ching’iti chiria chiare koa chiaberi amarieme, ekero chigocha konywa amaache, ⅩⅩⅩⅨ chirochio chikanyora amarieme bosio bw’ebite ebio, chikabiara chimanwa chire chimarera, ne chinkanga, ne chire nyankororo. ⅩⅬ Na Yakobo agaatanana ching’ondi chimingichi, ne ching’ondi chiaberi agachisang’anania ne chimingichi chie riicho ria Labani echiareenge namagwari, na echiare chimwamu. Naboigo akenyorera amaicho aye ere omonyene, ayio atabegete amo naya Labani. ⅩⅬⅠ Kero kende gionsi chimbori gose ching’ondi echiare ne chinguru gochiaganirie amarieme, Yakobo nigo are kobeeka ebite biria bosio bwe ching’iti ase chintuba chiria chinyare konyora amarieme gochikorigereria ebite ebio. ⅩⅬⅡ Korende echiare chinyoro tare gochibekera ebite ebio. Ase igo, echiare chinyoro nigo chiare koba echia Labani, na echiare ne chinguru chiba echia Yakobo. ⅩⅬⅢ Ase igo, Yakobo akaba okomenteka koba omotenenku, akaba namaicho amange, na abasomba, abaiseke na abamura, ne chingamia, ne chitigere.
