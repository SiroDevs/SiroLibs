Ogosororwa gw’okoonchoka
Ⅰ Ekero ekio mbachete aroro abanto bamotebetie amang’ana igoro y’Abagalili, baria Pilato asang’ananetie amanyinga abo nebing’wanso biabo. Ⅱ Akabairaneria, akabateebia, “Mokagete ng’a Abagalili abwo nigo barenge abanyene ebibe kobua Abagalili bande bonsi, ekiagera banyorete emechando buna eyio? Ⅲ Nabatebirie, taibo; korende motaonchogeti mwensi namosire boigo. Ⅳ Gose baria ikomi na batano na batato bagweretwe norwaki orotambe ase Siloamu, rokabaita, mokagete ng’a nigo barenge abasaria kobua abanto bonsi abamenyete Yerusalemu? Ⅴ Nabatebirie, taibo; korende motaonchogeti mwensi, namosire boigo.”
Omoreng’anio bw’omoko omogomba
Ⅵ Agakwana omoreng’anio oyo, “Omonto oyomo narenge nomoko osimekire ase omogondo oye bw’emesabibu; akagenda korigia amatunda ase omoko oria, korende tanyora gento. Ⅶ Agateebia omooroiseria bw’omogondo bw’emesabibu: Rora, emiaka eye etato nachire korigia amatunda ase omoko oyo, na tindanyora gento. Yonache! Nase ki ogosaria riroba? Ⅷ Akamoiraneria: Omonene, yotige omwaka oyo naende, goika nyoburugere na koyobekera obwoyo. Ⅸ Noro korame amatunda, mbuya; onye taibo, erio oyonache.”
Omokungu ogwenetigwe rituko ri’Esabato
Ⅹ Akaba okworokereria ase esinagogi eyemo ase rituko ri’Esabato. Ⅺ Naende rora, narengeo omokungu obwatire nekerecha ki’oboreu ase emiaka ikomi na etano na etato, na ere konya okogotaire, taare konyara kwerambora nonya ng’ake. Ⅻ Ekero Yeso achia komoorora, akamorangeria, akamoteebia, “Aye omokungu, gwasiboirwe korwa ase oboreu bwao.”
ⅩⅢ Akabeka amaboko aye igoro asare, na ere akaramboka erio kegima, akamotogia Nyasae. ⅩⅣ 13:14: Okong’ 20:9,10; Okoir 5:13,14Korende omotang’ani bw’esinagogi eria akabea, ekiagera Yeso agwenia omonto ase rituko ri’Esabato, agateebia abanto, “Nare amatuko atano na rimo agwenerete Mwanyabaanto bakore emeremo; ase ayio, inchwo ase amatuko ayio mogwenigwe, korende tari ase rituko ri’Esabato.”
ⅩⅤ Korende Omonene akamoiraneria, “Inwe abang’ainereria, Inee, kera oyomo oino tari gosibora eng’ombe yaye gose etigere yaye korwa bweri ase rituko ri’Esabato, ogenda nero koyenyoisia? ⅩⅥ Oyo nenyaroka ya Aburaamu; ekiagera Saitani omosibire emiaka eye ikomi na etano na etato, temogwenereti asiborwe korwa ase egesibo eke ase rituko ri’Esabato?”
ⅩⅦ Naende ekero are gokwana aya, abanto bonsi abare koamererania nere bagasookigwa. Abanto bonsi bakagoka ase engencho y’amang’ana onsi amanene akorire.
Omoreng’anio bw’entetere y’omote onga buna omosabisabi na oyo bw’ememera
(Mat 13:31-33; Mar 4:30-32)
ⅩⅧ Naende akabora, “Oborwoti bwa Nyasae nigo boreng’anirie naki? Ninki nkoboreng’ania? ⅩⅨ Mboreng’anie nentetere y’omote onga buna omosabisabi omonto abogoretie, akayesimeka ase omogondo oye; egakina, ekaba omote. Chinyoni chia igoro chikaroisia ebiswa ase chinsagia chiaye.”
ⅩⅩ Agakwana eria kabere, “Ninki nkoreng’ania oborwoti bwa Nyasae? ⅩⅪ Igo boreng’anirie nememera omokungu abogoretie, akayebeka ase ebirengo bitato bi’obosie, goika bionsi bikabota.”
Egesieri ekegote gochia ase obogima
(Mat 7:13-14,21-23)
ⅩⅫ Ekero arenge kogenda Yerusalemu, agaeta ase emechie na ebichie okworokereria. ⅩⅩⅢ Omonto oyomo akamobooria, “Omonene, barabwo abaratoorigwe nabake?”
Akabateebia, ⅩⅩⅣ “Teema mono gosoa ase egesieri kere ekegote, ekiagera nabateebirie: Abanto abange nabarigie gosoa, na barabwo tibakonyara. ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero omonyene enyomba araimoke na gosieka egesieri, inwe namochake gotenena isiko na gotura gesieri, mokobora, Omonene, toigorere, na ere nabairanerie: Timbamanyeti aase mokorwa. ⅩⅩⅥ Erio namochake goteeba: Nigo tware koria na konywa ase obosio bwao, naye nkworokereria kwarenge ase chinchera chiaito. ⅩⅩⅦ 13:27: Zab 6:8Korende ere natebe: Nabatebirie, timbamanyeti aase mokorwa; karue ase ’nde, inwe mwensi abakori amabe. ⅩⅩⅧ 13:28: Mat 22:13; 25:3013:28: Mat 8:11,12Erio namobe nokorera na okoriania amaino, ekero morarore Aburaamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo, na ababani bonsi ase oborwoti bwa Nyasae, na inwe abanyene namotugutwe isiko. ⅩⅩⅨ Naende mbache abanto korwa moocha na bosongo, na korwa sugusu na Irianyi, na barabwo mbaikaranse ase oborwoti bwa Nyasae. ⅩⅩⅩ 13:30: Mat 19:30; 20:16; Mar 10:31Na rora, mbare ab’omoerio barache koba ab’eritang’ani, naende mbare ab’eritang’ani barache koba ab’omoerio.”
Obobisa bwa Herode
ⅩⅩⅪ Ekero keria abande b’Abafarisai bakamochiera, bakamoteebia, “Imoka korwa aa, ogende, ekiagera Herode narigetie kogoita.”
ⅩⅩⅫ Akabateebia, “Genda moteebie ekebwe ekio: Rora ngoseria nde ebirecha, ngokora nde emeremo y’okogwenia abanto reero na ankio, ne rituko ria gatato ninkoore emeremo yane. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Korende ningwenerete kobwatia enchera yane reero, na ankio, na ankio ende, naki temogwenereti omobani asirere isiko ya Yerusalemu.
Ogoichanerwa kwa Yerusalemu
(Mat 23:37-39)
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ “Aye Yerusalemu, Yerusalemu, ogoita ababani na kobaaka amagena abatomirwe ase ore! Ng’ara karenga narigirie gosangereria abana bao buna engoko egosangereria ebichuchu biaye aase chimbaaba chiaye ime, korende timwancha! ⅩⅩⅩⅤ 13:35: Zab 118:26Rora, enyomba yaino mwatigeirwe ere ritombe; na inche nabateebirie ng’a timokondora nonya ng’ake, goika ekero keria morache goteeba: Nasesenirie oyogocha ase erieta ri ’Omonene.”
