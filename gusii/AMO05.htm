Ⅴ
Okorangerigwa gw’okoonchoka
Ⅰ Inwe Abaisraeli, igwa amang’ana ’okorera kwane gw’okobokia amagombo ase engencho yaino:
Ⅱ “Abaisraeli banga buna omoiseke omwekungi bagure,
tibakonyara koboka naende;
batigirwe bagwete ase ense yabo,
monto onde tari orabagororokie batenene.”
Ⅲ Aya naro Omonene agoteeba: “Omochie bw’Abaisraeli otomete abarwani esegi elifu eyemo, bono nigo otigarete ne rigana erimo, na oyotomete rigana erimo, bono nigo otigarete na ikomi.”
Ⅳ Aya nar’Omonene agotebia Abaisraeli: “Ndigie inche, erio nario morabe moyo. Ⅴ Korende timogenda Beteli, gose timosoa Giligali, gose timogenda goika Beeri‐Seba, ekiagera abanto ba Giligali mbairwe ase obosomba, na Beteli nosirigwe kegima.”
Ⅵ Morigie Omonene, nario morabe moyo; motakoreti bo, Omonene nigo arachere ororeria rwa Yusufu arosirie buna omorero ogosiria, na onde tari orayorimie atoorie Beteli. Ⅶ Inwe nigo mokoonchorana ayare amaronge aba ay’obororo, na mwatuguta oboronge inse ase riroba.
Ⅷ 5:8: Ayubu 9:9; 38:31Oria otongete egetuse gie ching’enang’eni chiria isano na ibere,
na ekio gekorokwa Orioni,
oria okoonchora omosunte ekerima oba emambia,
naende oonchora omobaso oba obotuko,
oria okorangeria amaache ’enyancha oyaumora igoro ase ense,
Omonene, nario erieta riaye.
Ⅸ Ere nigo akorenta ogosiria kwa mobosokano ase abwo bare ne chinguru,
boigo nagere ogosareka gochere
chiburi chinkong’u.
Ⅹ Abanto nigo bageechete oria okorwanerera ekeene agwo ase ekegambero kere geita, na bagechigwa noyo ogokwana ay’ekeene. Ⅺ Ase ayio, buna koreng’ana mwatindacheire abataka inse na kobaura engano yabo ebe ebango, nebe ekero moraagache chinyomba chi’amagena abaachire, timogochimenya, na ekero morasimeke emesabibu ase emegondo emiya, timokonywa edivai yaye. Ⅻ Inche nimanyete ogosaria kwaino ng’a nokonene na ebibe biaino ng’a nebinene mono. Nigo mogochanda omonyaboronge, boigo mokoegwa riaaki, erio mwaseria abataka korwa ase ekegambero kere geita tibakonyora oboronge bwabo. ⅩⅢ Ase engencho eyio, omonto ore omong’aini nakire‐kiri ase engaki buna eyio, ekiagera eyio nengaki embe.
ⅩⅣ Rigia ayare amaya, na tari ayare amabe, erio nario morabe moyo. Komorakore bo, Omonene, Nyasae bw’Emeganda, nabe amo nainwe, buna inwe mwatebiire. ⅩⅤ Gecha ayare amabe, na mwanche ayare amaya. Motenenere oboronge agwo ase ekegambero kere geita. Komorakore bo, ande nabo Omonene, Nyasae bw’Emeganda, ararorere abatigari ba Yusufu amaabera.
ⅩⅥ Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene, Nyasae bw’Emeganda, oyore Omonene, atebiire: “Ebirero mbibe ase chiraaro chionsi chi’omochie, na ase chinchera chiaye chionsi abanto barere bakobuga: Obee! Obee! Mbarangerie abaremi bache korera, na abamanyete kobokia amagombo bache kweng’ereria. ⅩⅦ Ebirero mbibe ase emegondo yonsi y’emesabibu. Ayio onsi nigo arabe, ekiagera inche Omonene ning’ete ase egati yaino mbaite.” Nabo Omonene atebiire.
ⅩⅧ Obobe nobwaino inwe abamoganetie rituko ri’Omonene riike! Nase ki moganetie rituko riria ri’Omonene? Rituko erio nigo rirabe eri’omosunte, gose tirikoba eri’omobaso. ⅩⅨ Nigo erabe buna ekero omonto agotama korwa ase endo, aumerane nedubu, gose ekero agosoa mwaye atoorera okoboko kwaye ase enyasi, na eng’iti emorome. ⅩⅩ Rituko riria ri’Omonene nigo rirabe eri’omosunte, tirikoba eri’oborabu, ee, neri’omosunte ekerima, na omobaso tokobara nonya ng’ake.
ⅩⅪ 5:21: Isaya 1:11-14“Inche nigo ngechete na gochaaya Amatuko aino Amanene. Tingokiri nemesangererekano yaino y’ogosasiima. ⅩⅫ Namoisa kondetera ebing’wanso biaino bi’ogosambwa, na okoruegwa kw’endagera, tinkobianchera; na tingwancha nonya nokorigereria ndigererie ching’iti chiaino chinoru chikoruegwa chibe ebing’wanso bi’omorembe. ⅩⅩⅢ Rusia korwa asende okwoga kwaino kw’amatera, tingwancha ntegerere amariogi ’amakano aino. ⅩⅩⅣ Korende tiga ekeene kegenderere buna amaache amaange, na oboronge bobe buna orooche amaache aye atari koera nonya ng’ake.
ⅩⅩⅤ 5:25: Ogok 7:42,43“Inwe Abaisraeli, Inee, ase engaki y’emiaka emerongo ene mwarenge ase erooro, mwandeterete ebing’wanso amo nokoruegwa konde? ⅩⅩⅥ Inwe mobogorie Sikuti, omogwekano oyio mogosasiima buna omorwoti oino, na Kiyuni, enyasae yaino ye ching’enang’eni mweroiseretie. ⅩⅩⅦ Ase engencho eyio inche nimbaire ase obosomba ase ense ere ng’umbu ya Damesiki.” Nabo Omonene ateebire. Nyasae bw’Emeganda, nario erieta riaye.
