ⅩⅬ
Chindoto chi’abasibwa babere
Ⅰ Ase engaki gete, magega y’amang’ana ayio, omoteneneri bw’ebinyugwa bia Farao na omoteneneri oye bw’abaroisia emegati bakamoochera omonene obo, omorwoti o Misiri. Ⅱ Ase igo, Farao akabeigwa n’abateneneri abwo babere, omoteneneri bw’ebinyugwa na omoteneneri bw’abaroisia emegati. Ⅲ Akababeeka barendwe ase enyomba yomotang’ani bw’abarendi, ase echela eyio Yusufu nere asibetwe. Ⅳ Omotang’ani bw’abarendi akamobeka Yusufu abe okobarigereria na kobakorera, na barabwo bakabeera ase egesibo ase engaki enke.
Ⅴ Ase obotuko obomo bonsi babere, omoteneneri oria bw’ebinyugwa na omoteneneri bw’abaroisia emegati b’omorwoti o Misiri, abwo basibetwe echela, bakaba bakoroota chindoto. Kera oyomo akaroota okoroota kwaye okware nengencho eng’ao korwa ase okw’oyonde. Ⅵ Ekero Yusufu agendete mambia kobarigereria akabanyora nigo baichanete. Ⅶ Ase igo, akabooria abateneneri abwo ba Farao, abaarenge amo nere ase egesibo ase enyomba y’omonene oye, akabora: “Naki moichaanete iga reero?”
Ⅷ Barabwo bakamoiraneria: “Nigo twarootete chindoto, na onde tari oranyare kogoteebia engencho ye chindoto chiaito.”
Yusufu akabateebia: “Nigoro ya Nyasae kobateebia engencho yaye. Komoranche, mochinteebie.”
Ⅸ Omoteneneri bw’ebinyugwa agateebia Yusufu okoroota kwaye, akabora: “Ase okoroota kwane nigo narooche omosabibu ore ase obosio bwane, Ⅹ na omosabibu oyio nigo orenge ne chinsagia isato; bwango ogasomoria, ogasokia ebisicha, na okaama ebisago bi’amasabibu amatobu. Ⅺ Na egekombe kia Farao nigo nakebwate ase okoboko kwane; nkabogoria amasabibu aria, nkayamitera ase egekombe kia Farao, nkaa Farao egekombe eke koboko kwaye.”
Ⅻ Yusufu akamoteebia: “Engencho y’okoroota okwo nero eye: Chinsagia echio isato, ayio namatuko atato. ⅩⅢ Magega y’amatuko atato Farao nakorusie aiga, akoe ogosika, akoiranie ase emeremo yao, na aye nobe gokomoa egekombe koboko kwaye, buna kware komoa agwo ritang’ani, ekero kwareenge omoteneneri bw’ebinyugwa biaye. ⅩⅣ Korende ekero oranyore amaya, ong’inyore nainche, ondorere amaabera. Koranche nagosabire onyatore ase Farao, ondusie ase enyomba eye, ⅩⅤ ekiagera nigo naibetwe korwa ase ense y’Abaiberania, nonya naa tinkoreti ing’ana rinde riibe riagwenerete kombeeka ase echela.”
ⅩⅥ Ekero omoteneneri bw’abaroisia emegati arooche ng’a okoonchora okwo nigo kwabeete okuya, agateebia Yusufu: “Nainche boigo nigo narootete. Nkarora ng’a inyetwegete chinteru isato chire nemegati emerabu ase omotwe one. ⅩⅦ Ase oroteru rwa igoro kegima yarengeo emegati y’ebisaku ao ao yaroiseretigwe Farao, korende chinyoni chigaacha chikayeria korwa ase oroteru igoro, oro netwegete ase omotwe one.”
ⅩⅧ Na Yusufu akamoiraneria: “Engencho y’okoroota okwo nero eye: Chinteru echio isato, ayio namatuko atato. ⅩⅨ Magega y’amatuko atato Farao nakoimokie, agosunge ase omote igoro ne chinyoni nachirie chinyama chi’omobere oo.”
ⅩⅩ Rituko ria gatato, erio riaare ri’okoinyora okoiborwa gwa Farao, ere agakorera abasomba baye bonsi omoyega, akabarusia echela baria babere, omoteneneri bw’ebinyugwa na omoteneneri bw’abaroisia emegati, ase egati y’abasomba baye. ⅩⅪ Akairania omoteneneri bw’ebinyugwa ase emeremo yaye y’obonyoisia, na ere akaba okomoa Farao egekombe ase okoboko kwaye; ⅩⅫ korende omoteneneri bw’abaroisia emegati ere akamosunga ase omote igoro, buna Yusufu abanorete chingencho chie chindoto chiabo. ⅩⅩⅢ Nonya nabo oyio omoteneneri bw’ebinyugwa taba okoinyora Yusufu, korende akamweba.
