Ogochorwa kwa baria batano na babere
Ⅰ Bono ase amatuko aria, ekero aborokigwa barenge komentekana omobaro obo, nkwabeete okwemurungania kw’Abayahudi abarenge gokwana Ekegiriki igoro ase Abayahudi baria barenge gokwana Ekearamaiki, ekiagera ababoraka baabo bebetwe ase okogaberwa gwa kera rituko. Ⅱ Baria ikomi na babere bakarangeria omoganda bw’aborokigwa, bagateeba: “Tetogwenereti intwe gotiga ring’ana ria Nyasae na gokora emeremo ye chimesa. Ⅲ Gaki baminto, chora abanto batano na babere korwa ase egati yaino, abamanyekanete ng’a nabaya, baichire n’Omoika na obong’aini, erinde tobabeeke ase emeremo eye. Ⅳ Korende intwe inatogenderere ase ogosaba na gokorera ring’ana ria Nyasae.”
Ⅴ Ring’ana eri rikagwenera omosangererekano bwonsi. Bagachora Stefano, omonto oichire nokwegena na Omoika Omochenu, na Filipo, na Prokoro, na Nikanora, na Timoni, na Parmena, na Nikolao bw’Antiokia, omonto oonchogete. Ⅵ Bakababeeka bosio bw’abatomwa, na barabwo bakabasabera na kobabeekera amaboko.
Ⅶ Ring’ana ria Nyasae rikaranda, na omobaro bw’aborokigwa okamentekana mono ase Yerusalemu, na abakuani abange bakaigwera okwegena.
Ogosoerwa gwa Stefano
Ⅷ Na Stefano, oichire obuya na okobua, nigo arenge gokora amakumia amanene na ebikone egati y’abanto. Ⅸ Korende abanto bande b’esinagogi yare korokwa Esinagogi y’Abasibore, aba nigo barenge Abakuurene na Abaaleksanderia, na baria korwa ase Kilikia na Asia, bakaimoka bakaamererania na Stefano. Ⅹ Korende tibanyara kobua obong’aini na Omoika oyo are gokwana noro. Ⅺ Goika bakagenka abanto bateebe: “Ntwaigwa omonto oyo ogokwana amang’ana ’oborami igoro ya Musa na Nyasae.” Ⅻ Bagaseega abanto na abagaaka na aborokia b’amariiko, bagachiera Stefano bakamobwata, bakamoira bosio bw’ekegambero. ⅩⅢ Bagatenenia kirori b’oborimo abaare goteeba: “Omonto oyo tatigeti gokwana bobe igoro y’aase Aa Aachenu na igoro y’amachiiko. ⅩⅣ Naki ntwamoigwa ogoteeba ng’a Yeso oyio o Nazareti inasarie aase aa, na koonchora chingencho echi twaetwe na Musa.” ⅩⅤ Abanto bonsi, abaikaransete ase ekegambero eke, bakamobeta amaiso, bakarora obosio bwaye ng’a nigo bwabeire buna obosio bw’omomalaika.
