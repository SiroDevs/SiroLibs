Yeso okogwenia omonto orenge rigata
(Mat 9:1-8; Luk 5:17-26)
Ⅰ Magega y’amatuko amake Yeso akairana gochia Kapernaumu, abanto bakanyora amang’ana ng’a nao are nyomba. Ⅱ Erio abanto abange bagasangererekana amo, tiriatigara ribaga ase bare, nonya ngoika gesieri; na Yeso akaba ogokwana ring’ana ase bare. Ⅲ Abanto bagaacha; bakamorentera omonto orenge rigata obogoirie nabaanto bane. Ⅳ Ekiagera batanyarete komoikia ang’e ase engencho y’omoganda, bakabochora ekerama aase aaria Yeso arenge. Kobakoorire kogetagora, bagaikia oborere boria oria orenge rigata araire. Ⅴ Yeso agachia korora okwegena kwabo agateebia oria orenge rigata, “Omwana one, ebibe biao biabeirwe.” Ⅵ Abande ase aborokia b’amariiko baikaransete aroro, bakeboria ase chinkoro chiabo, Ⅶ “Inaki oyo agoteeba iga? Nkorama are! Ning’o oranyare kwabera ebibe, otatiga oyomo, nere Nyasae?” Ⅷ Erio Yeso namanyete ase omoika oye ime buna baare korengereria ase chinkoro chiabo, akabateebia, “Inaki mokorengereria iga ase chinkoro chiaino? Ⅸ Ndiriri rire eriango, goteebia oyore rigata: Ebibe biao biabeirwe, gose goteeba: Imoka, bwetweke oborere bwao, ogende? Ⅹ Korende monyare komanya ng’a Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto nare nokobua ase ense gw’okwabera ebibe.” Erio agwo agateebia oria orenge rigata, Ⅺ “Nagotebirie, imoka, bwetweke oborere bwao, ogende bwoo.” Ⅻ Erio kegima akaimoka, agetweka oborere bwaye, akarua ase amasio abo bonsi; goika bagakumia bonsi, bagatogia Nyasae, bakoboora, “Ayare buna aya, titwana koyarora rinde nonya ng’ake!”
Yeso okorangeria Lawi
(Mat 9:9-13; Luk 5:27-32)
ⅩⅢ Yeso naende akairana gochia ensemo y’enyancha. Omoganda bwonsi okamochiera, ere agachaaka koborokereria. ⅩⅣ Ekero achia goeta, akarora Lawi, omwana bw’Alufayo, oikaransete aase bakoruseria ebango, akamoteebia, “Ntunyana!” Akaimoka, akamotunyana.
ⅩⅤ Ekaba ekero Yeso aikaransete nyomba mw’omonto oria, okoria endagera, abarusia ebango abange na abanyene ebibe bagaikaransa amo na Yeso na aborokigwa baye; inaki barenge abange bamotuny’anete. ⅩⅥ Aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai bagachia komoorora okoria amo n’abanyene ebibe na abarusia ebango, bagateebia aborokigwa baye, “Nase ki akoria na konywa amo nabarusia ebango na abanyene ebibe?” ⅩⅦ Yeso agachia koigwa aya, akabateebia, “Abare buya tibari korigia omogwenia, otatiga abarwarete; tincheti korangeria abanyaboronge, korende abanyene ebibe.”
Riborio igoro y’okweng’ata
(Mat 9:14-17; Luk 5:33-39)
ⅩⅧ Aborokigwa ba Yohana na Abafarisai nigo barenge kweng’ata. Abanto bande bagaacha, bakamobooria, “Nase ki aborokigwa ba Yohana na aborokigwa b’Abafarisai bakweng’ata, ko aborokigwa bao tibari kweng’ata?” ⅩⅨ Yeso akabateebia, “Inee! Abatuunyire enyangi inabanyare kweng’ata ekero omonywomi are amo nabarabwo? Ekero omonywomi are amo na barabwo, tibakonyara kweng’ata. ⅩⅩ Korende amatuko naache baraache kourwa omonywomi, erio nario baraache kweng’ata ase rituko riria. ⅩⅪ Gose tari monto onde okobareera ekeraka ki’egetambaa ekeyia ase eanga engotu ekiagera keria kiabekwa nigo kegotandora eanga eria, keria ekeyia kiagendereria eanga eria engotu etandoke kobua. ⅩⅫ Gose onde tari okobeeka edivai enyia ase ebirandi ebigotu biroisirie korwa ase amasankwa, gose edivai eria nebiate ebirandi, edivai eiteke, na ebirandi bitandoke; korende edivai enyia nigo ekobekwa ase ebirandi ebiyia.”
Riborio igoro y’okorenda Esabato
(Mat 12:1-8; Luk 6:1-5)
ⅩⅩⅢ 2:23: Okoir 23:25Yeso agachia koba ngoeta arenge ase emegondo y’engano ase rituko ri’Esabato; aborokigwa baye bagachaaka kobutora ebigara kobakogenda. ⅩⅩⅣ Abafarisai bakamoboria, “Rora, naki bagokora eritari boronge gokorwa ase rituko ri’Esabato?” ⅩⅩⅤ Akabairaneria, “Timwana gosoma aya Daudi akorete ekero aremeretwe endagera, akaba nenchara, ere na abwo barenge amo nere, ⅩⅩⅥ 2:26: Abal 24:92:26: 1 Sam 21:1-6buna asoete ase enyomba ya Nyasae, ekero Abiatari arenge Omokuani omonene, akaria emegati yarenge korugwa ase Nyasae, etare boronge koriegwa nonde otatiga abakuani, naende akaa abarenge amo nere?” ⅩⅩⅦ Akabateebia, “Esabato nigo yakoretwe ase engencho ya Mwanyabaanto, tari Mwanyabaanto ase engencho y’Esabato; ⅩⅩⅧ naki Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto nere omonene bw’Esabato.”
