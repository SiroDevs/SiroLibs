Omonto ekeoru ase ritibo ria Betiseda
Ⅰ Magega ase aya ndiarenge Rituko Rinene ri’Abayahudi; Yeso agatiira kogenda Yerusalemu.
Ⅱ Naende aaria Yerusalemu ase Egeita gie Ching’ondi ndiarengeo ritibo rikorokwa ase Ekeiberania Betiseda, na rirorio ndiabwate ebisoero bitano. Ⅲ Ime ase ebio mbarenge abanto abange abarwaire baare koraara, emeuko, ebirema, na amagata, [baganyete amaache aturubanigwe. Ⅳ Naki ekero gete omomalaika ngoika arenge osoa ase ritibo riria, oturubania amaache. Nabo ere oyorenge gosoa ritang’ani magega ase amaache gaturubaniigwe, nigo arenge kogwena oborwaire bonde bwonsi obwamonyorete.] Ⅴ Narenge aroro omonto gete oyorenge noborwaire bw’emiaka emerongo etato, na etano na etato. Ⅵ Yeso agachia komorora oraire, konya omanyire ng’a obeire engencho eye amatuko amange, akamobooria, “Norigetie koba omogima?” Ⅶ Omorwaire oria akamoiraneria, “Omonene, tindi nomonto okombeka ase ritibo, ekero amaache agoturubanigwa; korende ekero ngochicha inche, omonto onde asoa ritang’ani ase ’nde.”
Ⅷ Yeso akamoteebia, “Tenena, etweke egesero kiao, ogende.”
Ⅸ Erio kegima omonto oria akaba omogima, agetweka egesero kiaye, akagenda.
Nabo yarenge Esabato rituko riria. Ⅹ 5:10: Neh 13:19; Yer 17:21Abayahudi bagateebia omonto oria ogwenetigwe, “Reero n’Esabato, tari boronge ase ore gwetweka egesero kiao.”
Ⅺ Akabairaneria, “Ere oyonkora koba omogima nere onteebia: Etweke egesero kiao, ogende.”
Ⅻ Bakamobooria, “Oria ogoteebia: Etweke egesero kiao ogende, nere ng’o?”
ⅩⅢ Korende oria ogwenetigwe tamomanyete gose ning’o. Yeso nigo erusananetie, ekiagera abanto abange barenge aase aria. ⅩⅣ Magega ase aya Yeso akamonyora ore Hekalu ime, akamoteebia. “Rora, kwabeire omogima; tokora ebibe naende, ring’ana rire riibe kobua tiricha gokonyoora.”
ⅩⅤ Omonto oria akagenda, agateebia Abayahudi amang’ana, ng’a Yeso nere omokorete koba omogima. ⅩⅥ Ase ayio Abayahudi bagachaaka gochanda Yeso, bakarigia komoita, ekiagera akora aya ase rituko ri’Esabato. ⅩⅦ Yeso akabairaneria, “Tata ngokora are emeremo nonya ngoika bono, na inche ngokora nde emeremo.”
ⅩⅧ Nabo ekiagera ase aya Abayahudi bakamenta korigia komoita, ekiagera tasaretie Esabato yoka, korende amo naya narogete Nyasae ng’a n’Ise, ogwekora koreng’ana buna Nyasae.
Buna Ise agokora na Omwana naboigo agokora
ⅩⅨ Yeso akabateebia, “Ekeene, ekeene, nabateebirie: Omwana takonyara gokora ring’ana ere bweka, otatiga riria aroche Ise agokora; naki onsi aya agokora ere, ayio naro n’Omwana agokora. ⅩⅩ Tata nigo amwanchete Omwana, na komworokia onsi ayagokora omonyene; na emeremo emenene kobua eye iga namworokie, erinde inwe monyaare gokumia. ⅩⅪ Na buna Tata akobokia ebitondo obia obogima, naboigo n’Omwana akobaa obogima aba arigetie. ⅩⅫ Naki Tata tari kogambia monto onde bwensi, korende oeire Omwana obogambi bwonsi, ⅩⅩⅢ erinde bonsi basike Omwana buna basigete Tata. Oyotasigeti Omwana tasigeti Tata oyo omotomete. ⅩⅩⅣ Ekeene, ekeene, nabateebirie: Oyokoigwa ring’ana riane, na komwegena ere oyontomete nare nobogima bwa kare na kare, gose tagoika ase ekiina, korende oetire korwa ase amakweri, oikire ase obogima.
ⅩⅩⅤ “Ekeene, ekeene, nabateebirie: Ensa ngocha ere, na bono nereo, ebitondo biraigwe eriogi ri’Omwana o Nyasae, na baria barariigwe nababe moyo. ⅩⅩⅥ Naki buna Tata are nobogima ime yaye omonyene; naboigo oeire na Omwana koba nobogima ime yaye omonyene. ⅩⅩⅦ Omoeire okobua kw’ogokora ekiina, ekiagera ere n’Omwana‐o‐Mwanyabaanto. ⅩⅩⅧ Timokumia ase aya! Naki ensa ngocha ere, ekero abanto bonsi bare ase chimbeera ime baraigwe eriogi riaye, ⅩⅩⅨ 5:29: Dan 12:2na barabwo inabasoke, baria abaare gokora amaya ase okobokigwa kw’obogima, na baria abaare gokora amabe ase okobokigwa gw’ekiina.
Oborori igoro ya Yeso
ⅩⅩⅩ “Inche omonyene tinkonyaara gokora kende gionsi. Buna nkoigwa nabo nkogamba, na ekiina kiane nigo kere boronge, ekiagera tindi korigia ogwancha kwane, korende nogwancha gwa Tata oyontomete. ⅩⅩⅪ Inche kondeorokie omonyene, oborori bwane tibori obw’ekeene. ⅩⅩⅫ Nare oyonde oy’okombera kirori; na inche namanyire ng’a oborori bwaye agonteeba nobw’ekeene. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ 5:33: Yoh 1:19-27; 3:27-30Inwe mwatomete abanto ase Yohana, na oborori bwaye nobw’ekeene. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Korende inche tindi konyora oborori korwa ase abanto; korende nigo ngokwana aya, erinde inwe motooreke. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Ere nigo arenge etaaya egwoka na komoreka, na inwe mwanchire gosasokera omobaso oye ase ekero egeke. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Korende oborori inde nabwo nobonene kobua boria bwa Yohana, naki emeremo eria naetwe na Tata inyekoore. Emeremo eyio ngokora, yambereire kirori, ng’a Tata nere ontomete. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ 5:37: Mat 3:17; Mar 1:11; Luk 3:22Nere Tata oyontomete obeire kirori ase ’nde. Eriogi riaye timwana koriigwa kero kende gionsi, gose ekieni kiaye timwana gokerora ⅩⅩⅩⅧ naende ring’ana riaye timori narirorio ime yaino, ekiagera timomwegeneti ere oyotomirwe nere. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Igo mokorigarigia mono amariiko, ekiagera mokagete ng’a nigo more nobogima bwa kare na kare ime y’aro; na ayio naro are kirori ase ’nde, ⅩⅬ ko mwangire gocha ase ’nde monyare koba nobogima. ⅩⅬⅠ Tindi konyora obonene korwa ase abanto. ⅩⅬⅡ Korende nabamanyire inwe ng’a timori nobwanchani bwa Nyasae ime yaino. ⅩⅬⅢ Inche nachire ase erieta ria Tata one, na inwe timori konyancha; oyonde karaache ase erieta riaye omonyene, oyio namomwanche. ⅩⅬⅣ Naki moranyare kwegena, inwe abamokoenana obonene inwe ase inwe, na obonene boria bokorwa ase Nyasae ore bweka, timori koborigia? ⅩⅬⅤ Timokaga ng’a inche nimbasoere ase Tata; nare oyo okobasoera, nere Musa, oyomosemeretie inwe. ⅩⅬⅥ Naki onye mwamwegenete Musa anga mwanyegena nainche, ekiagera nere origete amang’ana igoro yane. ⅩⅬⅦ Korende onye timwegeneti amariiko aye, naki moregene amang’ana aane?”
