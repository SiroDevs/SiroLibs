Omoreng’anio bw’omosabibu
Ⅰ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: Ⅱ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, Inee, naki omote bw’omosabibu gose orosagia rw’aye robuete emete ende yonsi, ase ogotogigwa, ase egati y’emete ende ere ase rinani? Ⅲ Inee! Omote oyio nonachwe okorerwe moremo onde? Gose abanto mbaroisie egekongo korwa ase omote oyio basungere egento kende gionsi. Ⅳ Nigo okoba chinko orutwa riko. Ekero omorero osambire chinsinyo chiaye chionsi, na obogati bwaye bwarurirwe, Inee, bono moremo ki orakorerwe? Ⅴ Naama! Onye ekero omosabibu oyio orenge omogima togwenerete gokorerwa moremo onde bwensi, bono ekero oyire na korurwa nomorero, ’moremo ki orakorerwe?
Ⅵ “Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Buna koreng’ana naruure omote bw’omosabibu ore ase egati y’emete ende ere ase rinani outerwe omorero oyie, naboigo ndatugute abanto ba Yerusalemu. Ⅶ Nigo ndababee; nonya bagochia gotooreka korwa ase omorero, naende omorero n’oro orabasirie. Erio nario moramanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene, nababeeire. Ⅷ Ninsirie ense eyio ebe bosa, ekiagera bakorire aya atari ay’obwegenwa. Nabo Omonene ateebire.”
