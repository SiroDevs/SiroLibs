ⅩⅬⅡ
Orogendo rwe ritang’ani rw’abamwabo Yusufu gochia Misiri
Ⅰ Ekero Yakobo aigwete ng’a endagera nekare ase ense ya Misiri, akabooria abamura baye: “Inaki morigereranetie iga?” Ⅱ 42:2: Ogok 7:12Naende akabateebia: “Naigure ng’a endagera nekare Misiri, tirimboka gochiao, motogorere endagera toche koria, tobe moyo, titokwa.” Ⅲ Ase igo, bamura bamwabo Yusufu ikomi bagatirimboka gochia Misiri kogora endagera. Ⅳ Korende Yakobo tamwancherete Benjamini, omwabo Yusufu, kogende amo nabarabwo, ekiagera nigo aoboete ng’a aande nabaise gochia konyorwa n’amabe.
Ⅴ Nabo basinto ba Israeli bakagenda amo nabaanto bande abachiete kogora endagera, ekiagera enchara yarenge ase ense ya Kaanani. Ⅵ Yusufu nere orenge gabana igoro y’ense yonsi ya Misiri, naende nere orenge kooneria abanto bonsi b’ense eria endagera. Na abamwabo Yusufu, ekero baigete asare, bagatung’ama inse ase obosio bwaye. Ⅶ Yusufu, ekero abarora, akabamanya, korende agekora buna tabamanyeti, agakwana nabarabwo amang’ana amakong’u. Akababooria: “Abanto aba, ng’ai mwarure?”
Barabwo bakamoiraneria: “Nigo twarure ase ense ya Kaanani, twachire kogora endagera.”
Ⅷ Nonya Yusufu omanyete abamwabo, barabwo tibamomanyete ere. Ⅸ 42:9: Omoch 37:5-10Erio Yusufu akainyora chindoto chiria areenge kobarootera, akabateebia: “Inwe nechirooti more, nigo mwachire koroota ense eye, na korora ase ere bosa etari na getango.”
Ⅹ Bakamokania: “Tari bo, Omonene, intwe abasomba bao nigo twachire kogora endagera. Ⅺ Intwe twensi nabana b’omonto oyomo. Intwe abasomba bao nigo tore abanto abaronge, titori chirooti.”
Ⅻ Akabakania: “Yaya, nigo mwachire koroota ense, na korora ense ase ere bosa etari na getango.”
ⅩⅢ Barabwo bakamoiraneria: “Intwe abasomba bao nigo tore abamura ikomi na babere, abana b’omonto oyomo, ase ense ya Kaanani; na bono omoke ominto tari aa, inka akare ase tata, na oyomo ere takaiyo.”
ⅩⅣ Korende Yusufu akabateebia “Nabo ere buna konya naikire kobateebia, ng’a inwe nechirooti more. ⅩⅤ Ase amang’ana aya nimbateeme: Amaene, buna Farao are moyo, timokorua aa mogende, oria omino omoke ataracha aa. ⅩⅥ Toma oyomo oino agende korenta oyio omino, na inwe abande motigare echela. Nabo morateemwe gose namaene mogoteeba. Onye taibo, amaene buna Farao are moyo, inwe nechirooti more.” ⅩⅦ Erio agasiekera bonsi amo echela ime ase amatuko atato.
ⅩⅧ Rituko ria gatato Yusufu akabateebia: “Mokore iga buna ndabateebie, nario morabe moyo; naki inche nomonto imoirogete Nyasae. ⅩⅨ Onye inwe more abanto abaronge, tiga oyomo oino atigare aa ase echela, na inwe abande moirere abwo mwatigete inka endagera, ase engencho y’enchara; ⅩⅩ erio morente oria omino omoke ase ’nde. Igo amang’ana aino aenekigwe ng’a namaene are, na inwe timobaisa gokwa. Barabwo bagakora bo.”
ⅩⅪ Erio bagateebania barabwo ase barabwo: “Amaene, ntwamocherete oyominto. Nigo twarooche ogochandeka kw’omoyo oye, na ekero atosororete titwamoigwete. Ase ayio nakio kiagerire obokong’u obo bwatonyorire.”
ⅩⅫ 42:22: Omoch 37:21,22Rubeni akabairaneria, “Tinabakanetie ng’a timomochera omosae oyo? Korende inwe mokanga, timwanyigwera. Ase igo, bono amanyinga aye aganigwe aakanwe.” ⅩⅩⅢ Barabwo tibamanya ng’a Yusufu nayaigwete, ekiagera omonogi nareengeo ore komoonchorera. ⅩⅩⅣ Erio akarua ase bare, akagenda ensemo, akarera amariga; naende akairana, agakwana nabarabwo. Akarusia Simioni korwa ase bare, akamosiba korwa ase amasio abo. ⅩⅩⅤ Yusufu agachiika abanto baichorie chigunia chiabo endagera, na kera oyomo airanerigwe chitaabu chiaye, chibekwe egunia yaye ime, na aegwe endagera agochia koria inchera. Naboigo baabakorerete.
Abamwabo Yusufu bakoirana gochia Kaanani
ⅩⅩⅥ Erio bakaberekia chitigere chiabo endagera, bakagenda. ⅩⅩⅦ Ekero baigete ase bakorara botuko, oyomo obo agasansora egunia yaye arusie endagera ae etigere yaye, akarora chitaabu chiaye igoro y’endagera ase egunia yaye ime, ⅩⅩⅧ agateebia abamwabo: “Ayee! Chibesa chiane nigo chiairanetigwe, rigereria, nao chire aa egunia yane ime!” Chinkoro chiabo chikagoyaana, bakarigererania na koiguswa, bagateeba: “Inki eke Nyasae atokoreire iga?”
ⅩⅩⅨ Ekero baigete ase Yakobo ise, ase ense ya Kaanani, bakabateebia ayio onsi abanyorete, bakaboora, ⅩⅩⅩ “Omonto oria omonene bw’ense eria nigo akwanete naintwe amang’ana amakong’u agatoroka ng’a intwe nechirooti, tokoroota ense. ⅩⅩⅪ Korende tokamoteebia: Intwe nigo tore abanto abaronge, titori chirooti. ⅩⅩⅫ Nigo tore abamura ikomi na babere, tata noyomo. Oyomo oito takaiyo, na omoke kegima nere okare ase tata inka, ase ense ya Kaanani. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Omonto oyio, omonene bw’ense eria, agatoteebia: Ase amang’ana aya naro ndamanyere gose nabaanto abaronge more: Tiga oyomo oino aiga ase ’nde, inwe abande genda, moire endagera ase engencho y’enchara ekare inka seino. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Genda mondetere oyio omino omoke, erio nario ndamanye ng’a timori chirooti, korende nabaanto abaronge. Erio nimbairanerie oyomino, na inwe inamonyore ribaga mobe mokoonchoreria ase ense eye.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Ekaba ekero baumorete endagera korwa ase chigunia, bakanyora kera oyomo esaro ye chitaabu chiaye nao ere egunia yaye ime. Na ekero barabwo amo na ise barooche chisaro chi’echitaabu chiabo, bakaondoka. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Yakobo ise akabateebia: “Mwang’urire abana baane, Yusufu taiyo, na Simioni taiyo, na bono morigetie koira Benjamini nere. Amang’ana aya onsi anyorire inche.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Erio Rubeni agateebia ise “Oite abana baane babere abamura onye tinkomoirania gocha asore. Mobeke ase amaboko aane na inche nimoiranie.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Akamokania: “Omwana one tagotirimboka amo nainwe, ekiagera oyomwabo okuure na oyo nere otigarete bweka. Amabe aise gochia komonyoora agwo inchera, nigo moragere ngende ase oboina ne chimbuche chiane inde nomoichano.”
