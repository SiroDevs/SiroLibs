Abana na abaise
Ⅰ 6:1: Abakol 3:20Inwe abana, moigwere abaibori baino ase Omonene, ekiagera obo nabwo oboronge. Ⅱ 6:2: Okong’ 20:12; Okoir 5:16“Sika iso na nyoko” (ogochika oko nakwo ogochika kw’eritang’ani kore neira), Ⅲ “erinde ebe buya ase ore, nario oramenye amatuko amange ase ense.” Ⅳ 6:4: Abakol 3:21Na inwe abaise, timochegia abana baino baabaise kobeeigwa, korende mobarere ase amasikani na ase okworokereria kw’Omonene.
Abasomba na abanene babo
Ⅴ 6:5: Abakol 3:22-25Inwe abasomba, moigwere abanene baino ase ense eye buna mokoigwera Kristo, ase obwoba na okoiguswa, na ase oboitongo bw’enkoro, Ⅵ gose tari ase obosomba bw’okwegwenia ase amaiso ya Mwanyabaanto, korende buna abasomba ba Kristo, mogokora ogwancha kwa Nyasae ase chinkoro chiaino; Ⅶ ase omoyo omuya mogokorera Omonene, gose tari Mwanyabaanto. Ⅷ Naende momanye ng’a ring’ana rigiya omonto agokora, naegwe erio erio n’Omonene, k’arabe omosomba, gose omosibore. Ⅸ 6:9: a Abakol 4:1; b Okoir 10:17; Abakol 3:25Na inwe abanene, bakorere ayio ayio, motige kobairokia. Momanye ng’a ere oyore Omonene obo, nere oyoino ore igoro, na ase are gwanchereria tikoiyo.
Esegi igoro y’amabe
Ⅹ Omoerio, motenene bokong’u ase Omonene, na ase chinguru chi’okobua kwaye. Ⅺ Eboyie ebirwanero bionsi bia Nyasae, erinde monyaare gotenena korwania obong’ainereria bwa Saitani. Ⅻ Inaki okoamererania gwaito tikori ase abare n’amanyinga ne chinyama, korende igoro y’abagaambi na abare nokobua, igoro y’abanene b’omosuunte bw’ense eye, na igoro y’emegaanda y’ebirecha ebibe bire igoro. ⅩⅢ Ase ayio, bogoria ebirwanero bionsi bia Nyasae, erinde monyaare koamererania ase rituko ri’obobe, na komwaikeranirie onsi, motenene. ⅩⅣ 6:14: a Isaya 11:5; b Isaya 59:17Tenena gaaki, mweboyie ebinema biaino nekeene, na kobeeka oboronge buna engobo y’ekioma ase ebikuba biaino, ⅩⅤ 6:15: Isaya 52:7na gosiberwa amagoro aino engencho y’okoba ang’e eyio twanyorire ase enchiri y’omorembe. ⅩⅥ Kobua onsi bogoria enguba y’okwegena, ase eyio namonyaare korimia emegwe yonsi y’omorero y’oria omobe. ⅩⅦ 6:17: Isaya 59:17Naende bogoria ekegogwa gi’ekioma ki’ogotooreka, na omoro bw’Omoika, n’oro ring’ana ria Nyasae. ⅩⅧ Mosabe botambe ase Omoika ase ogosaba gwonsi na ase okoboria kw’ogosorora, na gotagesia ase kera ring’ana. Mogenderere gosabera abachenu bonsi. ⅩⅨ Monsabere nainche, ng’egwe amang’ana ase ogokwana n’oboremu, erinde ndandie obobisi bw’enchiri. ⅩⅩ Ase ayio, nigo inde oyo otometwe nsibire ebinyuri, inyore oboremu bw’ogokwana amang’ana buna ngwenerete gokwana.
Amang’ana y’ogokoorera
(Abakol 4:7-8)
ⅩⅪ 6:21: Ogok 20:4; 2 Tim 4:126:21: Abakol 4:7,8Erinde monyaare komanya buna inche inde, naende ninki ngokora, Tukiko, oyominto omwanchwa, omosomba omwegenwa ase Omonene, nabateebie amang’ana onsi. ⅩⅫ Namotomire ase more ase engencho eyio eyio, erinde monyaare komanya buna tore; na ere naremie chinkoro chiaino.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omorembe obe ase abaminto, na obwanchani amo nokwegena bikorwa ase Nyasae Tata, na ase Omonene Yeso Kristo. ⅩⅩⅣ Obuya bobe amo na bonsi abamwanchete Omonene oito Yeso Kristo n’obwanchani botari gosareka.
