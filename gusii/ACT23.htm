ⅩⅩⅢ
Ⅰ Paulo akarigereria mono abanto b’ekegambero, akabora: “Baminto, nabeireo ase obosio bwa Nyasae goika reero iga, na enkoro yane tekongambia.”
Ⅱ Anania, Omokuani omonene, agachiika baria bateneine ang’e nere bamoake omonwa oye. Ⅲ 23:3: Mat 23:27,28Erio Paulo akamoteebia: “Nyasae nagoake aye, enyasi eakire echooka! Aye gwaikaransire konachera inche ekiina boronge koreng’ana buna ogochika, naende kogoteeba mamincha y’amachiiko ng’a ng’akwe?”
Ⅳ Baria bateneine ang’e nere bakaboora: “Inee! Naye okorama Omokuani omonene o Nyasae?”
Ⅴ 23:5: Okong’ 22:28Paulo agateeba ng’a: “Tinamanyete baminto ng’a ere n’Omokuani omonene, naki nariikire: Tokwana bobe omogambi bw’abaanto bao.”
Ⅵ 23:6: Ogok 26:5; Abafl 3:5Korende ekero Paulo achia komanya ng’a ensemo eyemo n’Abasadukai, na ensemo ya kabere n’Abafarisai, agaaka eriogi ase ekegambero keria, agateeba: “Abaminto, inche n’Omofarisai, omwana bw’Abafarisai; inche nigo nkogambigwa ase engencho y’ogosemeria na ase okobokigwa kw’abakuure.”
Ⅶ Erio gakwanire aya, ogwatananekana gokaba egati y’Abafarisai na Abasadukai, na omosangererekano ogaatananekana. Ⅷ 23:8: Mat 22:23; Mar 12:18; Luk 20:27Naki Abasadukai nigo bagokana ng’a kobokigwa tikori, nonya nabamalaika, nonya nemeika, korende Abafarisai nigo bagwancha onsi. Ⅸ Bikaba ebituri ebinge; aborokia b’amariiko b’ensemo y’Abafarisai bagatenena, bakamobuna, bagoteeba: “Titorochi bobe bonde ase omonto oyo. Aande onye n’Omoika gose omomalaika okwanire nere?”
Ⅹ Ekero eriomana rinene riachia kooroka, omotang’ani oria omonene akoboa ng’a Paulo nabaise kobutoranwa nabarabwo, agachiika abarwani baike na komotaboria korwa ase amaboko abo, na komorenta ase enyomba enene y’abarwani.
Ⅺ Obotuko bori’Omonene agatenena ang’e nere, akamoteebia: “Remia enkoro, ekiagera buna kwamberete kirori Yerusalemu, nekogwenerete kombera boigo Rumi.”
Okobwatana kw’Abayahudi komoboera Paulo
Ⅻ Bogachia gokiamoka, Abayahudi bakabwatana komoboera Paulo, bagatianania ng’a tibakoria gose tibakonywa goika bamoitire. ⅩⅢ Abwo batiananetie nigo barenge abanto kobua emerongo ene. ⅩⅣ Barabwo bakagenda ase abakuani abanene na abagaaka, bakaboora: “Ntwatiananetie bokong’u ng’a titoria gento goika twaitire Paulo. ⅩⅤ Gaki bono inwe amo nab’ekegambero moteebie omotang’ani oria omonene amotirimbokie gocha ase more, buna inwe morigetie komanya chingencho chiaye buya kobua; na intwe nigo tore ang’e komoita ritang’ani ataraika ase more.”
ⅩⅥ Omoigwa o Paulo akaigwa amang’ana igoro y’okoboera kwabo, akagenda, agasoa ime ase enyomba enene, akamanyia Paulo amang’ana ayio. ⅩⅦ Paulo akarangeria oyomo bw’abatang’ani b’abarwani rigana, akamoteebia: “Ira omomura oyo ase omotang’ani omonene, ekiagera nabwate ring’ana akomoteebia.” ⅩⅧ Erio akamoimokia, akamoira ase omotang’ani oria omonene, akamoteebia: “Paulo, oria omosibwa nandangeria, omboria nkorentere omomura oyo, ekiagera nare ne ring’ana akogoteebia.”
ⅩⅨ Omotang’ani oria omonene akamobwata okoboko, akagenda nere ensemo, akamobooria bobisi: “Ing’ana ki obwate origetie gonteebia?”
ⅩⅩ Akabora: “Abayahudi babwatanire kogosaba otirimbokie Paulo gochia bosio bw’ekegambero ankio, buna igo barigetie komanya chingencho chiaye buya kobua. ⅩⅪ Gaki aye tibwancha, ekiagera abanto kobua emerongo ene bamoboeire, batiananirie ng’a tibakoria gose tibakonywa goika bamoitire; na barabwo nigo bare ang’e baganyete eira korwa ase ore.”
ⅩⅫ Erio omotang’ani oria omonene agatiga omomura oria agende, akamoteebia: “Toteebia monto onde ng’a kwamanyirie aya.”
Paulo okoirwa goika Kaisaria
ⅩⅩⅢ Magega y’ayio akarangeria babere b’abatang’ani b’abarwani rigana, akabateebia: “Beka ang’e abarwani amagana abere, na abarwani abariini chibarasi emerongo etano na ebere, na abare namatimo amagana abere ase okogenda Kaisaria chinsa isato chia botuko. ⅩⅩⅣ Naende beka ang’e chibarasi bariinie Paulo, momoire buya ase Feliki, gabana.” ⅩⅩⅤ Akarika riruube iga:
ⅩⅩⅥ “Ase omosike gabana Feliki. Inche Klaudio Lisia, nagokwanirie. ⅩⅩⅦ Omonto oyo nigo abwatwa n’Abayahudi, na barabwo bakaba ang’e komoita. Erio ngacha nomoganda bw’abarwani, nkamotooria, konya nanyorire amang’ana ng’a n’Omorumi are. ⅩⅩⅧ Naende nkarigia komanya engencho yaye bakomosoera, nkamotirimbokia na komobeka bosio bw’ekegambero kiabo. ⅩⅩⅨ Nkanyora ng’a nigo asoeirwe ase engencho y’amaborio ’amachiiko abo, korende tasoeiri ase ring’ana rigwenerete agoitwa nonya ngosibwa. ⅩⅩⅩ Ekero nanyora amang’ana ng’a Abayahudi bamoboereire omonto oyo, ngachora komotoma ase ore erio kegima, ngachiika abamosoeri bache bakwane amang’ana igoro yaye ase obosio bwao.”
ⅩⅩⅪ Erio abarwani baria bakaimokia Paulo buna bachiigetwe, bakamoira gochia Antipatiri ase obotuko boria. ⅩⅩⅫ Ekero bwakia mambia bagatiga abarwani be chibarasi bagenderere norogendo na Paulo, na barabwo abanyene bakairana ase enyomba enene y’abarwani. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Abarwani baria be chibarasi bagachia goika Kaisaria, bakaa gabana riruube riria, bakabeeka na Paulo ase obosio bwaye. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Ekero arisomire, akaboria: “Omonto oyo noyo bw’ense ki?” Ekero aigwete ng’a nomonto o Kilikia, agateeba, ⅩⅩⅩⅤ “Ninche nyigwe amang’ana ao ekero abagosoeri bao barache.” Agachiika arendwe ase enyomba y’ekegambero kia Herode.
