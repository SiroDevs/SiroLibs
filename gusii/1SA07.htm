Ⅰ 7:1: 2 Sam 6:2-4; 1 Amg 13:5-7Abanto ba Kiriati‐Yearimu bagaacha, bakaira Esanduki eria y’Omonene, bakayebeka ase enyomba y’Abinadabu, ase ekegoro igoro; bagachoora na gwatanana Eleazari, omwana oye omomura, abe okorenda Esanduki eria.
Samweli okogaambera Abaisraeli
Ⅱ Korwa engaki eyio Esanduki eria y’Omonene ekabeera agwo Kiriati-Yearimu ase engaki entaambe, emiaka emerongo ebere. Ase engaki eyio yonsi, Abaisraeli bonsi bakaba bakorera ase Omonene ng’a abakoonye.
Ⅲ Erio Samweli agateebia Abaisraeli bonsi: “Onye mogochia koonchoka na koirana ase Omonene ne chinkoro chiaino chionsi, morusie korwa ase more chinyasae chingeni chionsi, amo n’Asitaroti, mokorere Omonene bweka; erio nario arabatoorie korwa ase amaboko yabafilisti.” Ⅳ Erio Abaisraeli bakarusia korwa ase bare chinyasae chiria Baali na Asitaroti, bakamokorera Omonene bweka.
Ⅴ Samweli agateeba, “Sangereria Abaisraeli bonsi agwo Misipa, erinde imbasabere aroro ase Omonene.” Ⅵ Abanto bagasangererekana agwo Misipa, bakabuucha amaache, bakayaumora agwo ase obosio bw’Omonene, bakeng’ata ase rituko erio, bagakwana aroro agwo, “Twakorire ebibe ase Omonene.” Erio Samweli akaba okogaambera Abaisraeli agwo Misipa.
Ⅶ Ekero Abafirisiti baigwete ng’a Abaisraeli basangererekanire agwo Misipa, abanene b’Abafilisti bakagenda gochia kobarwania. Abaisraeli bagachia koigwa amang’ana ayio, bakabairoka, Ⅷ bagateebia Samweli, “Tobaisa gotiga kogenderera gotosabera ase Omonene, Nyasae oito, ng’a atotoorie korwa ase amaboko y’Abafilisti.” Ⅸ Erio Samweli akaimokia emaanwa y’eng’ondi ekogonka, akayerua ase Omonene ekaba ekeng’wanso ki’ogosaambwa ekegima, agasabera Abaisraeli ase Omonene, n’Omonene akaigwa ogosaba kwaye. Ⅹ Ekero Samweli areenge korua ekeng’wanso keria ki’ogosaambwa, Abafirisiti bagaacha korwania Abaisraeli, korende Omonene akabarentera okogukura okonene gwe chinkoba korwa igoro, akabataantania, na Abaisraeli bakabua Abafirisiti. Ⅺ Abaisraeli bakaimoka korwa agwo Misipa, bakabwatia Abafirisiti goika Beti‐Kari, bakabaitera ’nchera.
Ⅻ Erio Samweli akaimokia rigena, akaritenenia gati ya Misipa na Seni, akariroka Ebeni‐Eseri,*7:12 Ebeni‐Eseri: engencho yaye rigena ri’ogokonya akabora: “Omonene otokonyire twaikire aiga.” ⅩⅢ Naboigo Abafirisiti bakabugwa, tibanyora ribaga naende gosoa ase ense y’Abaisraeli. Okoboko kw’Omonene nigo kwareenge mamincha y’Abafilisti ase amatuko onsi Samweli areenge moyo. ⅩⅣ Emechie yonsi egati y’Ekironi na Gati, eyio Abafirisiti konya baurire Abaisraeli, ekairanigwa amo ne chingongo chiaetanaineo. Agwo boigo omorembe okaba ase egati y’Abaisraeli na Abaamori.
ⅩⅤ Samweli akaba omogambi ebiina bw’Abaisraeli ase amatuko onsi areenge moyo. ⅩⅥ Kera omwaka akaba okogenda goika Beteli, na Giligali, na Misipa, akaba okogaambera Abaisraeli ebiina ase emechie eyio yonsi. ⅩⅦ Erio akaba okoirana Rama, ekiagera agwo nao bwoye yarenge, na agwo akaba okogaamba ebiina bi’Abaisraeli, naende akaagachera Omonene egesasiimero.
