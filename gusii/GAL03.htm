Amachiiko gose okwegena
Ⅰ Inwe Abagalatia abariri! Ning’o obaarogete inwe? Amang’ana igoro y’okobambwa kwa Yeso Kristo nigo aorogetigwe ase more maiso marore. Ⅱ Tiga mbaborie ring’ana eri rioka: Igo mwanyorete Omoika ase ogokora kw’amachiiko, gose ase okwegena goetera ase okoigwa? Ⅲ Nabo more abariri buna iga? Mogachaaka ase Omoika, na bono morigetie goikeranigwa ase chingencho chiaino abanyene? Ⅳ Inee! Igo mwanarete emechando emenge enga buna eye bosa? Aande gose tiyarenge bosa. Ⅴ Ere oyokobaa inwe Omoika na gokora ebikone bie chinguru egati yaino, Inee, igo agokora ayio ase ogokora kw’amachiiko gose ase okwegena goetera ase okoigwa?
Ⅵ 3:6: Omoch 15:6; Abar 4:3Nabo “Aburaamu nigo amwegenete Nyasae, na ase engencho eyio akabarwa koba omonyene oboronge.” Ⅶ 3:7: Abar 4:16Gaki manya iga, ng’a baria bare nokwegena, abwo nabwo bare abana ba Aburaamu. Ⅷ 3:8: Omoch 12:3Korende ayio amariiko aroche korwa kare, ng’a Nyasae nae abanto b’Ebisaku oboronge ase okwegena. Ase ayio akaranderia Aburaamu enchiri kare, akabora, “Asore aye abanto b’ebisaku bionsi nabasesenigwe.” Ⅸ Naboigo abwo bare nokwegena mbasesenirie amo na Aburaamu oyorenge omwegeni.
Ⅹ 3:10: Okoir 27:26Naki baria bonsi basemeretie ogokora kw’amachiiko, nigo bare inse y’okoragererigwa, ekiagera neriikire, “Kera omonto oyotari kobwata onsi ayarikire ase egetabu y’amachiiko na koyakora oragererigwe.” Ⅺ 3:11: Habk 2:4Bono nigo are maiso marore, ng’a onde tari oranyore oboronge ase obosio bwa Nyasae ase amachiiko; ekiagera nigo ariikire, “Omonyene oboronge nabe moyo ase okwegena.” Ⅻ 3:12: Abal 18:5Amachiiko nigo are ao ase okwegena, ekiagera nigo ariikire, “Omonto oyorakore ayio nabe moyo ase aro.” ⅩⅢ 3:13: Okoir 21:23Kristo natobooretie korwa ase okoragererigwa kw’amachiiko, ekero akoretwe koba okoragererigwa ase engencho yaito, ekiagera neriikire, “Kera omonto oyobambirwe igoro ase omote nigo are inse y’okoragererigwa kwa Nyasae.” ⅩⅣ Igo ogosesenigwa okwo Aburaamu anyorete goikera abanto b’Ebisaku goetera ase Kristo Yeso, erinde tonyare konyora Omoika ase enchera y’okwegena, buna twarieretwe eira.
Ogochika n’echiira
ⅩⅤ Abaminto, ngokwana ’nde ase omobwekano bw’amang’ana a kera rituko. Ekero okoriera kw’omwando bw’omonto kogoikerana, onde tari oranyaare gokoonchora gose komenta ring’ana. ⅩⅥ Chiira chiria chiarieretwe Aburaamu na omoiborwa oye. Biblia teri gokwana amang’ana aya, “Ase oroiboro” buna nase abange; korende eira iga erengete ase oyomo, “Ase omoiborwa oo”, nere Kristo. ⅩⅦ 3:17: Okong’ 12:40Aya ngokwana naya: Okobwatana koria gwakoretwe ritang’ani na Nyasae; na amachiiko aria akarentwa magega y’emiaka amagana ane, na emerongo etato, tagosaria okobwatana koria, erinde eira eria ebe eya bosa. ⅩⅧ 3:18: Abar 4:14Naki omwando korabe ase amachiiko, tokoba naende ase eira; korende Nyasae akayoa Aburaamu ase eira.
ⅩⅨ Amachiiko rende, inki agwenerete? Akamentwa ase engencho y’amamocho, goika omoiborwa oria orieretwe eira anyare gocha. Amachiiko aria nigo aruetwe goetera ase abamalaika ase okoboko kw’omobwatania. ⅩⅩ Oyore omobwatania tari omobwatania ase oyomo, korende Nyasae noyomo.
Engencho y’amachiiko
ⅩⅪ Inee! Amachiiko nigo are mamincha yechiira chia Nyasae? Yaya, taibo! Naki onye amachiiko aruetwe ayaranyare koa abanto obogima, anga oboronge nigo bwanyorekanete ase amachiiko. ⅩⅫ Korende amariiko asiekeire bonsi inse y’okobua kw’ebibe, erinde abegenire baegwe ayio ariereretwe eira ase enchera y’okomwegena Yeso o Kristo.
ⅩⅩⅢ Korende ekero okwegena gotaracha, nigo twabwatire inse y’amachiiko, twasibire goika okwegena goche koorokigwa. ⅩⅩⅣ Naboigo amachiiko abeire omotebereria oito agotoira goika ase Kristo, erinde tonyore oboronge ase okwegena.
ⅩⅩⅤ Korende bono okwegena kogwachire, titori naende inse y’omotebereria. ⅩⅩⅥ Naki ime ase Kristo Yeso inwe mwensi mwabeire abana ba Nyasae ase enchera y’okwegena. ⅩⅩⅦ Ekiagera inwe mwensi, abamwabatisetwe ase Kristo, mwamweboire Kristo. ⅩⅩⅧ Aiga tari Omoyahudi gose Omogiriki, tari omosomba gose omosibore, tari omosacha gose omokungu, ekiagera inwe mwensi nigo more oyomo ime ase Kristo Yeso. ⅩⅩⅨ 3:29: Abar 4:13Naki onye inwe naba Kristo, rirorio mwabeire oroiboro rwa Aburaamu, na abanyamwando ase eira.
