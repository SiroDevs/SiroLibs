Abakori emeremo b’Obomenyo bwa Nyasae
(Okong’ 35:30—36:1)
Ⅰ Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, Ⅱ “Rora, nachorire Besaleli, oyore mosinto o Uri, mochokoro o Huri, bw’egesaku kia Yuda. Ⅲ Inche Nyasae namoichoirie omoika one, namoeire obong’aini ne chisemi amanye gokora emeremo ende yonsi y’oboroisia, Ⅳ abe oyomanyete koroberia na koroisia ebinto bie chitaabu, ne chifeta, ne chitaai. Ⅴ Ere abe okobaacha amagena e rigori rinene na koyabeka ase ebikonde, na kobaacha emete, na gokora emeremo ende yonsi y’oboroisia. Ⅵ Nachorire Oholiabu, mosinto bw’Ahisamaki bw’egesaku kia Dani amokonye ase emeremo eyio. Nabaeire abaroisia bande bonsi obong’aini, erinde banyare gokora ebinto bionsi ebi nagochikiire. Ⅶ Barabwo baroisie Eema y’Omosangererekano, na Esanduki ebe ne chibao chiriikire oborori, na Oboikaranso bwa amaabera, na ebinto bionsi bi’Eema eyio, Ⅷ emesa na ebinto birabeo, ekebekerero gie chitaaya gi’etaabu echenire na ebikorero biaye na egesasiimero ase ubani erasamberwe, Ⅸ egesasiimero ase ebing’wanso birasamberwe na ebikorero biaye, na egetuba amo nenterekerero yaye, Ⅹ ne chianga chingiya chinchenu chia Aroni Omokuani, na echi’abamura baye, echi barabeeke ekero bagokora emeremo y’obokuani, Ⅺ na amaguta akweakwa, na ubani ere ne rimisu rigiya esamberwe Aase Aachenu ime. Ase ogokora ebinto ebi bionsi goika bakore buna nagochikiire kegima.”
Esabato, rituko ri’ogotimoka
Ⅻ Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia. ⅩⅢ Kwana n’Abaisraeli aye omonyene obateebie “Goika morende Chisabato chiane, ekiagera echio nekemanyererio egati yane nainwe ebiaare ase ebiaare, erinde momanye ng’a ninche Omonene oyokobachena. ⅩⅣ Ase igo morende rituko ri’Esabato, ekiagera ne rituko richenu ase more. Onde bwensi aise korisaria, nigo araitwe akwe. Onde bwensi aise gokora emeremo ase rituko erio nigo araatananwe korwa ase egesaku ki’abaanto b’Omonene. ⅩⅤ 31:15: Okong’ 20:8-11; 23:12; 34:21; 35:2; Abal 23:3; Okoir 5:12-14Mokore emeremo amatuko atano na rimo, korende rituko ria gatano na kabere n’Esabato Enchenu, rituko ri’ogotimoka, richenu ase Omonene. Onde bwensi aise gokora emeremo ase rituko erio goika aitwe akwe. ⅩⅥ Nabo Abaisraeli goika babwate na korenda rituko ri’Esabato ebiaare ase ebiaare, ekiagera oko nokobwatana koragenderere botambe. ⅩⅦ 31:17: Okong’ 20:11Rituko erio ribe ekemanyererio keragenderere egati yane na Abaisraeli, ekiagera ase amatuko atano na rimo inche Omonene natongete igoro na ense, korende ase rituko ria gatano na kabere ngatiga emeremo yane yonsi, na gotimoka.”
Chibao ibere chi’Amachiko
ⅩⅧ Ekero Omonene akoorire gokwana na Musa agwo ase egetunwa gia Sinai, akamoa chibao ibere chi’amagena chiriikire oborori nekiara kia Nyasae.
