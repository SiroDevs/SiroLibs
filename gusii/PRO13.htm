ⅩⅢ
Ⅰ Omwana omong’aini nigo akoigwera amorokererio a ise, korende oyore omochaayi tari gwancha ogokuurerwa.
Ⅱ Okwama kw’amang’ana omonto agokwana nigo gokomorentera amaya, korende okogania kw’abatari abegenwa nigo gokorigia obotindi.
Ⅲ Omonto orendete omonwa oye, oyio nigo arendete obogima bwaye, korende oyogokwana amang’ana ateriteti, goika asoe ase obosiru.
Ⅳ Omonto omworo nigo agosanera ebinto, korende takonyora kende, ere o’yore omonyamokia nigo akonyora amagania aye onsi.
Ⅴ Omonyene oboronge nigo ageechete oborimo, korende omokori amabe nigo agokora echaayo n’obosooku.
Ⅵ Oboronge nigo bokorenda omonto omosike, korende ebibe nigo bikomosiria omokori amabe.
Ⅶ Omonto nare ogwekora ng’a n’omonda are, korende nigo are omotaka, na oyonde bwekora ng’a nomotaka are, korende nigo are omonda mono.
Ⅷ Chinibo nachio chikobooria obogima bw’omonto omonda, korende omonto omotaka tari n’onde okomogosa.
Ⅸ Oborabu bw’abanyene oboronge nigo bokomoreka n’omogooko, korende etaya y’abakori amabe nigo enga ekebeya gekorima.
Ⅹ Oboenenu nigo bokorenta okoamererania, korende baria bakoigwera ogoseemigwa nigo bakoba n’obong’aini.
Ⅺ Enibo enyorire ase obwango ase enchera y’obobe nigo egosira, korende ey’ekonyorekana ase okoyekorera emeremo ’nementekane.
Ⅻ Egesemeirie kogiatebanire nigo gekogera enkoro yarwara, korende ogosemeria kogwaikeraniigwe nigo kore omote bw’obogima.
ⅩⅢ Omonto ogochaaya Ring’ana nigo arasirigwe narirorio, korende oyokoiroka ogochika nanyore eng’eria y’obuya.
ⅩⅣ Amorokererio y’omonto omong’aini nigo are ensoko y’obogima, ekiagera nigo agokoonya omonto tabwatwa n’omotego bw’amakweri.
ⅩⅤ Okomanya okuya nigo gokorenta ogwancherwa, korende enchera y’abatari abegenwa nigo ekoraa gochia ase obosiru.
ⅩⅥ Omonto omong’aini nigo agokora emeremo yaye ne chiseemi, korende omonto omoriri nigo akoraria oboriri bwaye.
ⅩⅦ Omotomwa otari omwegenwa nigo akogwa ase emechando, korende oyore omwegenwa nigo akogwenia.
ⅩⅧ Omonto okwanga koigwera amorokererio akomoorongeyia, obotaka na obosooku nigo biramonyore, korende oyokoigwera ogokuurerwa nanyore amasikani.
ⅩⅨ Okogania gwaikeranigwe nigo kore ogwansu ase omoyo, korende okoonchoka gotiga amabe, okwo nigo kore okogechigwa ase abariri.
ⅩⅩ Omonto ogotaara n’abanto abang’aini nigo arabe omong’aini, korende oyo ogosanga nabariri nanyorwe n’amabe.
ⅩⅪ Amang’ana amabe nigo agotunyana abanyene ebibe, korende abanyene oboronge ’nabaegwe amaya.
ⅩⅫ Omonto omuya nigo agotiga omwando ase abana baye, na ase abachokoro baye, korende obotenenku bw’abanyene ebibe nigo bogacheire abanyene oboronge.
ⅩⅩⅢ Oboremo oboyia bw’omonto omotaka nigo bokwama endagera enyinge, korende ase engencho y’obobe, enyinge yasiara‐siarerwa yasira.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omonto okorina omwana oye ekeranya nigo amogechete, korende oy’omwanchete tari goteebana komoorongeyia.
ⅩⅩⅤ Omonyene oboronge nigo abwate endagera ekomoisanekia, korende enda y’omokori amabe nigo egotigara n’okogania.
