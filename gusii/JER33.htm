Eira ende ey’ogosemeria
Ⅰ Ekero Yeremia agendererete kobeera ase egoora y’enyomba y’abasibwa agwo ase enyomba y’oborwoti, ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha asare eria kabere, rikamoteebia, Ⅱ “Aya nar’Omonene oyotongete ense, oyo oyebombete akayekora egenderere kobao, ere oyio erieta riaye n’Omonene, agoteeba: Ⅲ Ndangerie, nainche ninkoiranerie, ninkoorokerie amang’ana amanene abisekanete, ayio aye otana komanya. Ⅳ Naende aya naro inche Omonene bw’Abaisraeli ngoteeba igoro ye chinyomba chi’omochie oyo, na igoro ye chinyomba chi’abarwoti b’Abayuda, chiria chiatagoretwe nabwo baagachete chindwaki chi’ogwekengera, naende bakorwana esegi: Ⅴ Abanto ngocha bare korwania Abakalidayo baichorie chinyomba nemebere ekwete y’abanto abwo ndaite nendamwamu yane enene na ekeririanda kiane. Nabisire obosio bwane ase omochie oyo ase engencho y’obobe bwabo. Ⅵ Korende nonya naboigo ningwenie abanto b’omochie oyo, babe nemebere emiya. Nimbagwenie mbae omorembe na obotoereru ase obonge. Ⅶ Ning’iranerie Abayuda na Abaisraeli amaya abo, mbaagache babe buna barenge agwo ritang’ani. Ⅷ Nimbachene korwa ase okomocha kwabo, na korwa ase ebibe bionsi bakorire ase ’nde; nimbaabere ebibe biabo bionsi na ogosaria bakorire ase ’nde. Ⅸ Omochie oyio nobe okongokia ase ogontogia na okombaka ase amasio ’ebisaku bionsi bire ase ense, ebio biraigwe chinkuma chi’amang’ana nakoreire abanto b’omochie oyio. Abanto b’ebisaku mbairoke na koiguswa ase obwoba, ekiagera nakoreire omochie oyio amaya, naende nayosesenirie.” Ⅹ Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba, “Abanto nigo bagokwana ng’a aase aiga asarekire, abeire buna erooro, abanto tibaiyo, gose ching’iti, ee, amaene emechie y’Abayuda ne chinchera chia Yerusalemu biatigaire bosa; abanto tibaiyo, gose ching’iti. Ⅺ 33:11: 1 Amg 16:34; 2 Amg 5:13; 7:3; Ezra 3:11; Zab 100:5; 106:1; 107:1; 118:1; 136:1Korende aase agwo eriogi ri’omogooko na eri’omochengo nariigugwe naende, eriogi ri’omonywomi na eri’omoriakari, na amariogi ’abateri abwo bagotera ekero bakorenta okoruegwa gw’okoiraneria ng’a mbuya mono ase enyomba y’Omonene. Mbabe bagotera iga:
“Iraneria ng’a mbuya mono ase Omonene bw’Emeganda,
ekiagera Omonene nomuya,
obwanchani bwaye nigo bogendererete botambe.
Inche Omonene ning’iranerie ense obuya bwaye bw’eritang’ani.” Nab’Omonene atebiire.
Ⅻ Aya nar’Omonene bw’Emeganda agoteeba, “Aase aiga are buna erooro ase abanto batamenyeti, gose ching’iti, korende emechie yaye yonsi nemenywe nabarisia bakorisia ching’ondi naende. ⅩⅢ Ase emechie y’ense y’ebigoro, na ey’ense omweya, na eya Irianyi, na ey’orogongo rwa Benjamini, na ase chinsemo chionsi chia Yerusalemu, na ase emechie y’Abayuda, amaicho nabe agoeta inse y’okoboko kw’oyomo okoyabara.” Nab’Omonene agoteeba.
ⅩⅣ 33:14: Yer 23:5,6Omonene oteebire, “Naama! Amatuko ngocha are ndaikeranie eira narierete chiamate chi’Abaisraeli na echi’Abayuda. ⅩⅤ Ase amatuko ayio na engaki eyio ninkore Omoseboka bw’oboronge oseboke korwa ase ororeria rwa Daudi. Oyio nanache ebiina bi’ekeene na oboronge ase ense eye. ⅩⅥ Ase amatuko ayio Abayuda mbatoorigwe, na abanto ba Yerusalemu bamenye ase obotoereru. Na Omoseboka oyio nigo orarokwe: Omonene Nere Oboronge Bwaito.” ⅩⅦ 33:17: 2 Sam 7:12-16; 1 Abar 2:4; 1 Amg 17:11-14Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba, “Omonto takobora ase ororeria rwa Daudi oraikaranse ase ekerogo ki’oborwoti bw’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅧ 33:18: Omob 3:5-10Boigo Omokuani takobora korwa ase egesaku ki’Abalawi orarue ebing’wanso bi’ogosambwa ase obosio bwane, na ogosamba okoruegwa kw’endagera, na korua ebing’wanso binde kogenderera botambe.”
ⅩⅨ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha ase Yeremia, rikamoteebia, ⅩⅩ “Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba: Nakorete okobwatana kwane igoro y’omobaso na obotuko, erinde bibe bikororekana ase chingaki chiabo chichikiire, na okobwatana okwo tikogosareka. ⅩⅪ Boigo okobwatana kwane nakorete na Daudi, omosomba one, ngotenene. Omonto takobora ase ororeria rwaye oraikaranse ase ekerogo kiaye ki’oborwoti. Boigo nokobwatana kwane nabakuani b’ororeria rwa Lawi ngotenene. ⅩⅫ Buna koreng’ana emeganda ye ching’enang’eni chia igoro na omokenye ore nyancha bitanyare kobarwa, naboigo ororeria rwa Daudi, omosomba one, na orw’Abalawi abakuani bagonkorera, rotanyare kobarwa, ekiagerainche nindomente na korobuchia.”
ⅩⅩⅢ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha ase Yeremia, rikamoteebia, ⅩⅩⅣ “Inee! Kwaigure amang’ana abanto aba bagokwana? Nigo bagoteeba: Omonene bwangire ebisaku biria bibere achorete. Nigo bachayire abanto baane, gose tibaababareti koba egesaku. ⅩⅩⅤ Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba: Inche nakorete okobwatana igoro y’omobaso na obotuko, na emeroberio echikeire igoro na ense. Onye ntakong’etie okobwatana okwo, ⅩⅩⅥ nanga nangete nororeria rwa Yakobo, na orwa Daudi omosomba one, na tinchora omonto bw’ororeria rwaye agambere oroiboro rwa Aburaamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo. Nimbairanerie amaya abo na kobarorera amaabera.”
