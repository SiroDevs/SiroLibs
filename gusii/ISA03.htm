Yerusalemu ogotantanigwa
Ⅰ Naama! Omonene, oyore Omonene bw’Emeganda, narusie korwa ase Yerusalemu, na korwa ase Abayuda, kera egento basemeretie na ebi begenete; narusieo endagera yonsi, na amaache onsi. Ⅱ Narusie korwao abanto bare chituoni na abarwani bande b’esegi, na abagaambi ebiina, na ababani, na abaringoria, na abagaaka, Ⅲ na abatang’ani b’abarwani emerongo etano, na abanto abasike, na abasemia, na abaroisia abang’aini, na abaragori bamanyete oborogi mono. Ⅳ Omonene nabeeke abasae babe abanene babo, na abana abake baabagambere. Ⅴ Abanto mbachandane, kera omonto nabe ogochanda oyonde, na kera omonto nabe ogochanda omogisangio oye; omwana omoke naenenere omonto omogotu, na omonto otaroochi nchera nachaye omonto osikire.
Ⅵ Engaki eyio omonto nabwate oyomwabo baiboire komo amoteebie, “Aye nobwate chianga okobeeka, ase ayio kabe omogambi oito, otenenere obogaambi obo bwanyegerigwe.”
Ⅶ Korende ase rituko erio oyio nakane ateebe: “Inche tinkonyara koba omogwenia; ase enyomba yane endagera teiyo, gose eanga, ase ayio timokonyara kombeeka imbe omoraai bw’abaanto.”
Ⅷ Yerusalemu otagoirwe, na Abayuda bagure, ekiagera chinkwana chiabo na ogokora kwabo biabeire mamincha y’Omonene, basiereiri, Obonene bwa Nyasae gati yabo. Ⅸ Okororekana kw’amaiso abo kwababereire kirori; nigo bakoraria ebibe biabo buna Abasodomu bakorete, tibari kobibisa. Obobe nobwabo! Barabwo abanyene berenteire obobe obwo.
Ⅹ Teebia abanyene oboronge ng’a amang’ana nabe amaya ase bare, barabwo mbarie okwama kw’ogokora kwabo. Ⅺ Obobe nobwabo abakori amabe! Amang’ana nabe amabe ase bare, ekiagera aya amaboko abo abeire agokora, naro barakorerwe abanyene.
Ⅻ Abanto baane nigo bagochandwa nabana, na abakungu nabwo bakobagaambera. Inwe abanto baane, abaraai baino baabatantanirie enchera, babasiririe ase enchera mogoeta.
Omonene okonachera ebisaku ekiina
ⅩⅢ Omonene obeire ang’e korenta ekiina, otenenire konachera ebisaku ekiina. ⅩⅣ Omonene nachake kogambia abagaaka na abagaambi b’abaanto baye, ateebe, “Ninwe mwasaririe omogondo one bw’emesabibu, na ebisakorwa bi’abataka nigo bire ase chinyomba chiaino. ⅩⅤ Inche Omonene bw’Emeganda, nigo nkoboria iga: Ninki mokorengereria goika mwasunyuntire abanto baane na gosia amasio ’abataka?”
Ogokuurerwa kw’abakuungu ba Yerusalemu
ⅩⅥ Omonene nigo agoteeba, “Abakungu ba Sayoni nigo bare aberori, nigo bagotaara basongorete amagoti, na bagoaka‐akania amaiso are nokogania okobe; nigo bagotira‐tiria kobakogenda na kwogia chinchigiri chire amagoro abo. ⅩⅦ Ase ayio Omonene nae abakungu ba Sayoni oborwaire bw’ogosunyora chitukia chiabo, naende abaasiorie chianga abatige bare getirianda.”
ⅩⅧ Ase rituko eri’Omonene nabaure ebinto bionsi bechaberete: ebitange bi’amagoro, na ebitinti, ne chisonoi chi’amagoti, ⅩⅨ na ebigera bi’amato, ne chitaai chi’amaboko, na ebiechabero bia metwe, ⅩⅩ na ebinyuri bi’amagoro, ne chinkini chi’okweboeria, ne chichuba chire namaguta agotioka, na ebite bi’ogwekong’era, ⅩⅪ ne chibonyi chire nebimanyererio, na ebigera bie chimioro, ⅩⅫ ne chiganchwa chi’emeyega, ne chigoti chi’okwambara, na ebiambaro bi’amareko, na emebuko ya koboko, ⅩⅩⅢ na ebirore, ne chianga chi’egatani chie rigori rinene, na ebigogwa, na ebietubo.
ⅩⅩⅣ Ribaga ri’ogotioka okuya nobogundo borabeo, ne ribaga ri’orokini nengori eraabeo, ne ribaga ri’etukia eroisirie buya nerisio rirabeo, ne ribaga ri’okobeeka chiganchwa chi’emeyega mweboyie chigunia, obuya bwabo mboonchoke bobe obosoku.
ⅩⅩⅤ Abanto baino mbaitwe nemioro, na abarwani baino chituoni mbakwere ase esegi. ⅩⅩⅥ Ebiita bi’omochie oyio mbirere na kobokia amagombo; n’omochie oyio nobe buna omokungu ourirwe kera egento oinkaransete amaroba igoro.
