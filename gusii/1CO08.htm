Obokong’u bw’ebing’wanso bikoruegwa nabaanto batamomanyeti Nyasae
Ⅰ Ase amang’ana ’ebinto bikoruegwa ebing’wanso ase emegwekano y’ogosasimwa, twamanyire ng’a “intwe twensi ntore ne chisemi.” “Chisemi” nigo chikorenta oboenenu, korende obwanchani nigo bokoagacha. Ⅱ Omonto karekage ng’a namanyete ring’ana rinde rionsi, oyio taramanya nonya mbono buna emogwenerete amanye. Ⅲ Korende omonto kamwanchete Nyasae, oyio omanyirwe nere.
Ⅳ Gaki ase amang’ana ay’okoria ebinto bikoruegwa ebing’wanso ase emegwekano y’ogosasimwa, twamanyire ng’a “omogwekano bw’ogosasimwa tai gento ase ense”, ekiagera twamanyire ng’a “Nyasae onde tari aande, otatiga oyomo bweka.” Ⅴ Gaki, nonya chire echinde chikorokwa chinyasae igoro gose ense (buna chire “chinyasae” chinyinge, na “abanene” abange) Ⅵ korende ase tore intwe Nyasae noyomo bweka, oyore Tata, na ebinto bionsi nigo bikorwa asare, na intwe nigo tore moyo asare. Naende nare Omonene oyomo, nere Yeso Kristo, na ebinto bionsi nere obitongete, naboigo naintwe nere ototongete.
Okorenda chinkoro chi’abaminto echi chikobugwa bwango
Ⅶ Korende obong’aini obo tibori ime ase abanto bonsi. Abande, ekiagera bakwenaria emegwekano y’ogosasimwa goika bono, bakoria endagera buna egento kiarurwe ekeng’wanso ase omogwekano bw’ogosasiimwa, na ekiagera chinkoro chiabo chikobugwa bwango, nigo chikobekwa ogotacheneka. Ⅷ Korende endagera tegotorenta ang’e ase Nyasae. Totarieti, titogosiria gento, na toisa koria, tegotomenta gento. Ⅸ Korende rigereria ng’a obosibore boria bwaino tibocha kobagocha baria bare abareu. Ⅹ Naki omonto karakorore aye, omonto ore nobong’aini, goikaransete ase endagera ime ase Hekalu y’emegwekano y’ogosasimwa, Inee, onye enkoro yaye ekobugwa bwango, takonyora oboremu goika arie ebinto bikoruegwa ebingwanso ase emegwekano y’ogosasimwa? Ⅺ Ase igo oria omoreu nigo agosirigwa ase engencho y’obong’aini bwao, nere oyominto Kristo akwete ase engencho yaye. Ⅻ Ase ayio, komorakorere abamino ebibe na goichania chinkoro chiria chikobugwa bwango, igo mwakoreire Kristo ebibe. ⅩⅢ Ase ayio, endagera keragere oyominto agwe, rirorio tinkoria enyama nonya ng’ake, timbaisa kogera oyominto agwe.
