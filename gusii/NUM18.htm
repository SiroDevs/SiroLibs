Emeremo y’Abakuani na Abalawi
Ⅰ Omonene agateebia Aroni, “Aye na abamura bao amo n’abanto b’eamate yaino nigo morabarerwe okomocha konde gwonsi koraabe kogokorerwa ase obokoreri ime y’Aase Aachenu, naende aye na abamura bao inamobarerwe okomocha konde gwonsi koraabe kogokorwa ase obokuani bwaino. Ⅱ Renta Abalawi bamino, egesaku gia iso, barabwo bakore emeremo aroro amo naye. Abwo bagokorere aye amo nabamura bao, ekero mogokora emeremo agwo bosio bw’Eema eria ere noborori. Ⅲ Barabwo ’nabagokorere, naende bakore emeremo yonsi ase Eema eria, korende tibaabaisa goika ang’e ase ebikorero ebichenu bi’Aase Aachenu, gose tibaika ang’e ase egesasiimero. Baise gokora igo, aye amo nabarabwo igo morakwe. Ⅳ Nigo barabwatane naye na gokora emeremo yabo yonsi y’obokoreri ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. Omonto onde omoao otachiikiri tabaisa goika ang’e akore emeremo amo nainwe. Ⅴ Aye amo nabamura bao, ninwe mweka morakore emeremo ime y’Aase Aachenu na ase egesasiimero, erio nario endamwamu yane etabaise koba igoro y’Abaisraeli naende. Ⅵ Ninche nachorire Abalawi bamino korwa ase Abaisraeli, babe ekeegwa ase more. Barabwo baatananirwe ase ’nde inche Omonene, bakore emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. Ⅶ Aye amo nabamura bao, ninwe mweka morakore emeremo yonsi y’obokuani, na emeremo yonsi ase egesasiimero, na eria ere moino y’etoto enachete Obomenyo Obochenu. Nabaeire obokuani buna ekeegwa kebe ekiaino. Onye omonto onde omoao otachiikiri ogocha ang’e ase obokoreri obwo, goika aitwe akwe.”
Ebitari bi’abakuani
Ⅷ Omonene agateebia Aroni, “Rora, nakoeire okoruegwa konde gwonsi gokoruegwa ase ’nde, obe gokorenda. Ebinto bionsi ebichenu Abaisraeli bakorua, ebio nakobieire bibe egetari kiao amo neki’ororeria rwao kogenderera botambe. Ⅸ Ase ebinto bire ebichenu mono, ebiratigare, bitari gosambwa nomorero, ebiraabe ebiao, nabio ebi: Kera okoruegwa kw’endagera, na kera ekeng’wanso ki’ebibe, na ekeng’wanso gi’okorongeyia okomocha. Ebinto bionsi barabwo barabe bakorua ase ’nde, nabibe ebichenu mono ase ore, na ase abamura bao. Ⅹ Noriere ebinto ebio aase gete are aachenu mono. Kera omonto omosacha ore ase more nanyare koria ebinto ebio. Ebio n’ebinto ebichenu ase ore.
Ⅺ “Boigo nakoeire okoruegwa Abaisraeli barabe bakorenta ase ’nde buna ebing’wanso bi’ogosungusia. Nakoeire aye amo nabana bao, abamura na abaiseke, ’nabibe ebiaino kogenderera botambe. Kera omonto ochenegete ore ase enyomba yao nanyare koria ebinto ebio.
Ⅻ “Nakoeire kera ekere ekiya mono ki’ogotongora oko Abaisraeli bakondetera: Amaguta y’amazeituni, na edivai, n’endagera. ⅩⅢ Okwama gwonsi kw’eritang’ani ase ebinto bionsi bigokina ase ense yabo, okwo bakondetera, ’nakobe okwao. Kera omonto ochenegete ore ase enyomba yao nanyare koria ebinto ebio.
ⅩⅣ 18:14: Abal 27:28“Kera egento Abaisraeli baatananire ase ’nde ’nakebe ekiao.
ⅩⅤ “Kera omwana omotangi bakorua ase ’nde ’nabe oyoo, na kera eng’iti entangi boigo, korende nonya naboigo bwanche embooria ase omwana omotangi o Mwanyabaanto, na ase chintangi chie ching’iti chiria chitachenegeti. ⅩⅥ Abana baboorigwe ekero bakoorire omotienyi oyomo, ne ching’iti boigo. Aye nobeke rigori ri’okobooria, ribe chisekeli isano chi’echifeta, koreng’ana nesekeli egokorerwa ase Obomenyo Obochenu. (Esekeli eyemo nechigera ibere.) ⅩⅦ Korende ching’iti chintangi chi’echiombe, gose ching’ondi, gose chimbori, tichikoboorigwa. Echio nchiatananwe ase ’nde. Amanyinga abo oyanyororokererie igoro ase egesasiimero, na ebitinya biabo obisambe bibe ekeng’wanso kegosambwa nomorero kere ne rimisu rikongwenera. ⅩⅧ Korende chinyama chiabo amo ne ritaana rigosungusigwa, na ekenama kia borio, echio nachio echiao.
ⅩⅨ “Ebiegwa bionsi ebichenu Abaisraeli barandetere inche Omonene nakobieire aye amo nabana bao, abamura na abaiseke, bibe ebitari biaino kera ekero. Okwo nokobwatana kw’ogotianania kw’omonyoo gwa kare na kare ase obosio bwane inche Omonene, okwo nakorire naye na ororeria rwao.”
ⅩⅩ Naende Omonene agateebia Aroni, “Aye tokonyora omwando bw’orogongo ase egati y’Abaisraeli bande. Tokoegwa ensemo ende yonsi ase egati yabo. Inche ninche ensemo yao, naende omwando oo, ase egati y’Abaisraeli.
Egetari Ki’Abalawi
ⅩⅪ 18:21: Abal 27:30-33; Okoir 14:22-29“Naeire Abalawi ensemo ya ikomi, eye Abaisraeli bakorua, ebe eyabo, ebe eng’eria ase emeremo eye barabe bagokora agwo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. ⅩⅫ Korwa bono na kogenderera Abaisraeli bande tibaabaisa goika ang’e n’Eema eria. Baise gokora bo, nigo barabe nebibe, erio bakwe. ⅩⅩⅢ Korende Abalawi barabwo nigo barakore emeremo ase Eema y’omosangererekano, okomocha kw’ebibe gokoraabeo nakobarwe igoro ase bare. Okwo nakwo koraabe ogochika ase bare kogenderera ebiaare ase ebiaare. Barabwo tibakoba nomwando ase egati y’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅩⅣ Ensemo ya ikomi eye Abaisraeli bakondetera buna okoruegwa kwabo nayeire Abalawi ebe eyabo. Ase ayio, nabateebirie ng’a tibakoba nomwando ase egati y’Abaisraeli bande.”
Ensemo ya ikomi y’Abalawi
ⅩⅩⅤ Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, ⅩⅩⅥ “Kwana n’Abalawi, obateebie: Ekero mokonyora ensemo ya ikomi korwa ase Abaisraeli, eri’Omonene abaeire ebe eyaino, erio morusie ensemo ya ikomi y’okoruegwa okwo, moyerentere Omonene. ⅩⅩⅦ Okoruegwa okwo inwe morarue, nigo korabarwe ase more buna n’okoruegwa kw’endagera korwa ase ebiooreri biaino, na ogw’edivai na amaguta korwa ase ekemitero, okwo abande bakorua. ⅩⅩⅧ Ase enchera eye nainwe morentere Omonene okoruegwa kwaino, okwo mokorusia korwa ase ensemo ya ikomi eria mokonyora korwa ase Abaisraeli. Na okoruegwa okwo gw’enseemo ya ikomi okore kw’Omonene, moe Aroni Omokuani. ⅩⅩⅨ Korwa ase ebiegwa ebio bionsi moranyore korwa ase Abaisraeli, mwatananere Omonene biria bire ebiya mono. ⅩⅩⅩ Ase ayio otebie Abalawi: Ekero moraabe mwarure ebinto ebiya mono korwa ase ensemo ya ikomi eria, erio ebinto ebio nigo birabarwe ase more, inwe Abalawi, buna okoruegwa kwaino kw’endagera, na okomitwa gw’edivai, na okw’amaguta. ⅩⅩⅪ Inwe amo n’abanto b’echinka chiaino namoriere ebinto ebio aase aande onsi, ekiagera eyio neng’eria yaino moraakanwe ase emeremo mogokora agwo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. ⅩⅩⅫ Ekero mwarure ensemo engiya kobua korwa ase okoruegwa okwo, timokoba nokomocha ase okoria eria entigari. Timobaisa gokora ebinto ebichenu bi’Abaisraeli bibe ebitachenegeti, erinde timokwa.”
