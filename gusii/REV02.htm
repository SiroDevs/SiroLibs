Amang’ana ase Efeso
Ⅰ Ase omomalaika bw’ekanisa y’Efeso riika: Amang’ana aya naro agokwana, ere oyobwate ching’enang’eni chiria isano na ibere ase okoboko kwaye gw’okorio, ere oyogotaara gati‐gati ase ebibekerero biria bie chitaaya bitano na bibere bie chitaabu.
Ⅱ Namanyire ogokora kwao, na ogwechanda kwao, na okoremereria kwao. Namanyire ng’a tokonyara koremereria abanto ababe, na ogateema abwo bakweroka abatomwa, ko tibari bo, okabarora ng’a nabanyaborimo. Ⅲ Naende okaremereria na kwebogoreria amange ase engencho y’erieta riane, na toroseti. Ⅳ Korende ninde ne ring’ana eri igoro ase ore: Gwatigire obwanchani bwao bw’eritang’ani. Ⅴ Gaki, inyora nkorwa ase ki ogwete. Onchoka, okore ogokora kwao kw’eritang’ani! Otakoreti bo, ninchiche asore, ndusie ekebekerero kiao gie chitaaya korwa aase aye. Ⅵ Korende ndire ring’ana erimo rire rigiya ase ore, nario eri: Nogechete ogokora kw’Abanikolaiti, okwo nainche ngechete. Ⅶ 2:7: a Omoch 2:9; Okom 22:2; b Ezek 28:13; 31:8Ere oyore namato akoigwera, tiga aigwe ring’ana eri Omoika ogoteebia chikanisa. Ere oyorabue nimoe ribaga arie amatunda y’omote bw’obogima, oyio ore ase Paradiso ya Nyasae.
Amang’ana ase Sumurina
Ⅷ 2:8: Isaya 44:6; 48:12; Okom 1:17; 22:13Na ase omomalaika bw’ekanisa ya Sumurina riika: Amang’ana aya naro agokwana, ere oyore bw’eritang’ani na oyo bw’omoerio, oyo konya okuure, naende akaba moyo.
Ⅸ Namanyire emechando yao, na obotaka bwao (korende nomonda ore). Namanyire buna ogokwanwa bobe nabwo bagoteeba ng’a n’Abayahudi bare, ko tibari bo, korende barabwo n’ekeombe gia ya Saitani. Ⅹ Toiroka amang’ana ayio arache kogochanda. Rigereria, Saitani narute abande baino ase echela monyare goteemwa, na inwe namochandwe ase engaki y’amatuko ikomi. Obe omwegenwa goika ogokwa na inche ninkoe egutwa y’obogima. Ⅺ 2:11: Okom 20:14; 21:8Ere oyore namato akoigwera, tiga aigwe ring’ana eri Omoika ogoteebia chikanisa. Ere oyorabue takonyora obororo bw’amakweri a kabere nonya ng’ake.
Amang’ana ase Perigamo
Ⅻ “Na ase omomalaika bw’ekanisa ya Perigamo riika: Amang’ana aya naro agokwana, ere oyore nomoyio o birende ore nobwoge chinsemo ibere.
ⅩⅢ “Namanyire aase omenyete, nagwo aase ekerogo ki’obonene gia Saitani kere. Aye nobwatete mono erieta riane, na tokaneti okwegena ore nakwo ase inde, nonya nase amatuko Antipa kirori one, omwegenwa one, oyio oiteretwe ase egati yaino agwo aase Saitani amenyete. ⅩⅣ 2:14: a Omob 22:5,7; 31:16; Okoir 23:4; b Omob 25:1-3Korende ninde namang’ana make igoro ase ore: Nore nabaanto agwo babwatete okworokereria kwa Baalamu, ere oyio bworokereretie Balaki abeeke ekegocha ase Abaisraeli, ng’a barie ebinto biare korugwa ebing’wanso ase emegwekano y’ogosasimwa, na gokora obotomani. ⅩⅤ Naboigo naye nore nabaanto babwatete okworokereria kw’Abanikolaiti. ⅩⅥ Onchoka rende! Otaonchogeti, ninchiche ase ore bwango, nkore esegi igoro ase bare nomoyio o birende bw’omonwa one. ⅩⅦ 2:17: a Okong’ 16:14,15; 16:33,34; Yoh 6:48-50; b Isaya 62:2; 65:15Ere oyore namato akoigwera, tiga aigwe ring’ana eri Omoika ogoteebia chikanisa. Ere oyorabue nimoe ensemo ya mana ebisire, naende nimoe rigena rirabu, na ase rigena erio ndirikireo erieta riyia, eritamanywe na monto onde otatiga ere oyo orariegwe.
Amang’ana ase Tiatira
ⅩⅧ “Na ase omomalaika bw’ekanisa ya Tiatira riika: Amang’ana aya naro Omwana o Nyasae agokwana, ere oyore namaiso agwoka buna ekebemberi ki’omorero, na amagoro aye nigo anga buna etai esenirie mono.
ⅩⅨ “Namanyire ogokora kwao, na obwanchani bwao, na obwegenwa bwao, na obokoreri bwao, na okoremereria kwao. Naende namanyire ng’a ogokora kwao kw’omoerio kwabuire koria kw’eritang’ani. ⅩⅩ 2:20: 1 Abar 16:31; 2 Abar 9:22,30Korende ninde ne ring’ana eri igoro asore: Tibwangeti omokungu oria Yesebeli ere oyokweroka omobani o Nyasae. Ere nigo akworokereria na kong’aina abasomba baane bakore obotomani, na koria ebinto bikoruegwa ase emegwekano y’ogosasimwa. ⅩⅪ Inche namoeire ribaga anyare koonchoka, korende tancheti koonchoka na gotiga obotomani bwaye. ⅩⅫ Rigereria, nimorute borere arware, na abwo batomanire amo nere banyore emechando emenene, bataonchogeti korwa ase ogokora kwaye. ⅩⅩⅢ 2:23: a Zab 7:9; Yer 17:10; b Zab 62:12Inche ning’ite abana baye bakwe. Na abanto be chikanisa chionsi mbamanye ng’a inche nigo nkoriga‐rigia ebirengererio bie chinkoro, na inche nimoe kera oyomo oino koreng’ana nogokora kwaye. ⅩⅩⅣ Korende inwe abande abamore Tiatira, inwe mwensi motari nokworokereria okwo, inwe abamotamanyeti eri abande bakoroka obobisi bwa Saitani, nabateebirie ng’a tinkobatweka egurube ende. ⅩⅩⅤ Korende eki more nakio kebwate goika nchiche. ⅩⅩⅥ 2:26: Zab 2:8,9Ere oyorabue na gokora ogokora kwane goika omoerio, oyio nimoe okobua igoro y’abanto b’ebisaku, ⅩⅩⅦ ere nabagambere nenyimbo y’okobua y’ekioma, na kobaata buna chinyongo. ⅩⅩⅧ Naende nimoe eng’enang’eni ya mambia. ⅩⅩⅨ Ere oyore namato akoigwera, tiga aigwe ring’ana eri Omoika ogoteebia chikanisa.
