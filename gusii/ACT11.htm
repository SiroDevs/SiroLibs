Petero ogwekengera ase ogokora kwaye
Ⅰ Naende abatomwa na abaminto abarenge Boyahudi bakaigwa ng’a abanto b’Ebisaku nabarabwo banyorire ring’ana ria Nyasae. Ⅱ Erio ekero Petero achete ase Yerusalemu, baria abare ensemo y’okwaroka bakaamererania nere, Ⅲ bakamoteebia: “Aye ngwasoa ase abanto batarogeti, okaragera amo nabarabwo!” Ⅳ Korende Petero agachaaka kobateebia amang’ana onsi buna abeete, Ⅴ “Nigo narenge ase omochie okorokwa Yopa, ngosaba nare, nkaba buna oyobwatire nobogondereria, nkarora ase okorora egento kere buna egetambaa kiabwatire emekoba ene, kegoikigwa korwa igoro gocha ase ’nde. Ⅵ Ngakerigereria mono, nkarora ching’iti chire n’amagoro ane, ne ching’iti chi’orosana, amo n’echikwagura, n’chinyoni chia igoro. Ⅶ Nkaigwa eriogi rigonteebia: Petero, imoka, onyenye orie! Ⅷ Korende nkabora: Yay’Omonene, ekiagera egento getachenegeti gose ekere neubi, tibiana gosoa ase omonwa one nonya ng’ake. Ⅸ Eriogi riganteebia eria kabere korwa igoro: Eki Nyasae achenire tokeroka ng’a tikeri egechenu. Ⅹ Ring’ana eri rigakoreka ara gatato, naende ebinto biria bionsi bikang’uswa gochia igoro. Ⅺ Ani! Ekero ekio abanto batato baikire ase enyomba eria namenyete, na barabwo nigo batometwe ase ’nde korwa Kaisaria. Ⅻ Omoika Omochenu oganteebia ngende amo nabarabwo, timbaisa koba ne chitang’u‐tang’u. Na abaminto aba batano nomo bakagenda amo nainche, togasoa ase enyomba y’omonto oria; ⅩⅢ ere agatoteebia buna aroche omomalaika oteneine mwaye, na komoteebia: Toma abanto bagende Yopa, barangerie Simioni, oyokorokwa Petero. ⅩⅣ Nere oragoteebie amang’ana ayarakorentere ogotooreka, aye na ab’enyomba yao yonsi. ⅩⅤ Ekero nachaagete gokwana, Omoika Omochenu okabaikera buna otoikerete intwe agwo ritang’ani. ⅩⅥ 11:16: Ogok 1:5Nkainyora ring’ana riria ri’Omonene, buna ateebete: Yohana nigo arenge kobatisa ase amaache, korende inwe inamobatiswe ase Omoika Omochenu. ⅩⅦ Gaki, onye Nyasae obaeire barabwo ekeegwa keria keria atoete intwe, ekero twamwegenete Omenene oito Yeso Kristo, inche ninche ng’o, ndanyaare koamereria Nyasae?”
ⅩⅧ Ekero bachia koigwa amang’ana aya, bagakira‐kiri. Na barabwo bagatogia Nyasae, bagoteeba: “Gaki, Nyasae oeire abanto b’Ebisaku boigo okoonchoka okwo gokorenta obogima.”
Ekanisa y’abanto b’Ebisaku ase Antiokia
ⅩⅨ 11:19: Ogok 8:1-4Baria baatananegete magega y’emechando eria yabeete igoro ya Stefano bakagenda Foiniki na Kuporo, na Antiokia, bakarandia ring’ana ase Abayahudi boka, tari ase abanto bande. ⅩⅩ Korende mbarengeo ase egati yabo abanto ba Kuporo na Kurene, barabwo ekero bachia goika Antiokia bagakwana n’Abagiriki, bakabaranderia amang’ana amaya igoro y’Omonene Yeso. ⅩⅪ Okoboko kw’Omonene nigo kwarenge amo nabarabwo; abanto abange bakamwege n’Omonene, na komoonchokera. ⅩⅫ Abanto b’ekanisa ya Yerusalemu bakaigwa amang’ana aya, bagatoma Barnaba agende Antiokia. ⅩⅩⅢ Ere, ekero achia goika na korora buna obuya bwa Nyasae bwakorire, akagoka, agasorora bonsi babe abegenwa ase Omonene ase okogania gwe chinkoro. ⅩⅩⅣ Ere nigo arenge omonto omuya, oichire n’Omoika Omochenu na okwegena. Abanto abange bakamenteka ase Omonene. ⅩⅩⅤ Barnaba akarua agwo, akagenda Tariso korigia Saulo, ⅩⅩⅥ na ekero amonyorete akamorenta Antiokia. Ase engaki y’omwaka omogima bagasangererekana amo ase ekanisa na kworokereria abanto abange. Agwo Antiokia nao aborokigwa batang’anete korokwa Abakristo.
Obotomwa bwa Barnaba na Saulo gochia Yerusalemu
ⅩⅩⅦ Ase amatuko aria ababani bagatirimboka korwa Yerusalemu gochia Antiokia. ⅩⅩⅧ 11:28: Ogok 21:10Oyomo obo, ore korokwa Agabo, agatenena akabana ase Omoika Omochenu, ng’a enchara enene nechiche ase ense engima; ero ekaba ekero Klaudio are kogamba. ⅩⅩⅨ Na aborokigwa baria, kera oyomo koreng’ana buna okonyara kwaye, bakang’ea gotoma ogokonywa ase abamwabo abamenyete Boyahudi. ⅩⅩⅩ Bagakora bo, bagatoma ebiegwa ase abagaaka ase amaboko a Barnaba na Saulo.
