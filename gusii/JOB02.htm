Saitani ogotema Ayubu naende
Ⅰ Naende ekaba ng’a rituko erimo, ekero abamura ba Nyasae bachete kweorokia ase obosio bw’Omonene, Saitani nere agacha agwo ase barenge kweorokia ase obosio bw’Omonene. Ⅱ Erio Omonene akaboria Saitani, “Naye ng’ai okorwa?”
Saitani akamoiraneria, “Nigo nkorwa ase etaaro yane, nkona‐goetanana ase ense yonsi, naende nabo nkonagotiira na gotirimboka aroro.”
Ⅲ Agwo Omonene akaboria Saitani, “Ayubu, omosomba one, kwarorire buna are? Monto onde tari ase ense onga buna ere; nigo are omwegenwa na obwate enkoro endonge; ere nigo anyirogete inche Nyasae, naende nigo etangete korwa ase ogokora amabe. Nonya kwang’ebereretie komochanda ngencho ende etaiyo, ere nigo agendererete koba omwegenwa.”
Ⅳ Erio Saitani akairaneri’Omonene, akamoteebia, “Risankwa ase risankwa; omonto nigo ararue gento kende gionsi abwate ase ogotooria obogima bwaye. Ⅴ Bono aye rambora okoboko kwao, okune amauga ’omobere oye, amo ne chinyama, omokore bobe, ekeene, ere nigo arakoragereria agwo ase obosio bwao.”
Ⅵ Naende Omonene agateebia Saitani ayio namaya, “Rora, Ayubu nigo are inse y’okobua kwao, korende omotige abe moyo.”
Ⅶ Erio Saitani akarua agwo ase Omonene, akageenda, akarentera Ayubu amagumba, gochakera chimbaratero goikera ase omotwe igoro. Ⅷ Ayubu akaba ogoikaransa ase ribu, akaimokia orogio, akana‐kweageria amaote aye. Ⅸ Erio mokaye akamobooria, “Inee, nonya ngoika bono nigo ogendererete koba omwegenwa ase Omonene? Ragereria Nyasae, erio okwe!”
Ⅹ Korende Ayubu akairaneria mokaye, akamoteebia, “Aye nigo ogokwana buna omokungu onde bwensi omoriri agokwana. Onye tokonyora amaya korwa ase Nyasae, Inee, titokonyora amabe naro korwa asare?”
Ase amang’ana ayio onsi Ayubu takorete ebibe ase ogokwana kwaye.
Abasani b’Ayubu bagocha
Ⅺ Ayubu nabwate abasani batato: Elifasi korwa Temani, na Bilidadi korwa Sua, na Sofari korwa Naama. Ekero barabwo baigwete igoro y’emechando eria Ayubu anyorete, bagachikana ng’a kera oyomo arue bwoye, baumerane amo, bagende komwechandera na komoremia. Ⅻ Bagachia komoorora korwa aare, tibanyara komomanya gose nere. Bagachaaka goaka amariogi bakorera; bagatandora chianga chiabo, bagesiarera ebuse gochia igoro na ase emetwe yabo. ⅩⅢ Abanto abwo bagaikaransa inse agwo amo nere ase engaki y’amatuko atano na abere, omobaso na obotuko; agwo onde tabeete ogokwana ing’ana rinde rionsi ase Ayubu, ekiagera baroche ng’a emechando yaye nigo yarenge emenene mono.
