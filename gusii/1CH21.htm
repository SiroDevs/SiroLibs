ⅩⅪ
Daudi ogokora omobaro na kobarika
(2 Sam 24:1-5)
Ⅰ Saitani akagania gochanda Abaisraeli, agasukia enkoro ya Daudi ase okobara Abaisraeli. Ⅱ Ase igo Daudi agateebia Yoabu na abatang’ani b’abasikari, “Genda mobare Abaisraeli korwa Beeri‐Seba goika Dani, erio mondetere amang’ana imanye omobaro obo.”
Ⅲ Yoabu agateeba, “Omonene amente abanto baye bamentekane ara rigana ase omobaro obo. Inee! Aye omorwoti, omonene one, abanto aba bonsi tibari abasomba bao? Nase ki aye omonene one okogania gokora ayio? Nase ki ogochia korenta okomocha igoro ase Abaisraeli?” Ⅳ Korende ring’ana ri’omorwoti rikaba ne chinguru igoro ya Yoabu. Erio Yoabu akaimoka akagenda ase Abaisraeli bonsi, erio akairana gocha Yerusalemu. Ⅴ Yoabu akaa Daudi omobaro bw’abaanto. Ase Abaisraeli bonsi abwo barenge komanya korwana esegi nemioro nigo barenge abanto emilioni eyemo, ne chilifu rigana erimo (1,100,000), na ase Abayuda abanto chilifu amagana ane na emerongo etano na ebere (470,000). Ⅵ Korende Yoabu tabarete Abalawi na Ababenjamini, ekiagera ogochika koria omorwoti aruete nigo kwamogechetie.
Ⅶ Amang’ana ayio akoregete akagechia Nyasae; ere akaa Abaisraeli amaakwa. Ⅷ Erio Daudi agateebia Nyasae, “Nakorire ebibe ebinene ase aya nakorire. Korende bono nagosabire, onyabere inche, omosomba oo, ogosaria kwane, naki nigo nakorire egento ki’oboriri obonene.”
Ⅸ Omonene agakwana na Gadi, omoorori o Daudi, akamoteebia, Ⅹ “Genda okwane na Daudi, omoteebie ng’a aya naro inche Omonene ngoteeba: Nabekire asore amang’ana atato; ase ayio chora erimo, eri ngochia kogokorera.”
Ⅺ Erio Gadi akagenda ase Daudi akamoteebia, “Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba: Chora erimo eri oranche. Ⅻ Noganetie enchara echiche ase emiaka etato; gose nigo oganetie oserigwe nababisa bao ase engaki y’emetienyi etato, babe bakobabuna‐buna na kobaita nemioro; gose mochandwe n’omoro Omonene ase amatuko atato, ekero oborwaire obobe bw’ogoita abanto borache igoro ase ense eye yaino, na omomalaika bw’Omonene ogosaria ense y’Abaisraeli? Bono nteebie aya ngochia koiraneria oria ontoma.”
ⅩⅢ Daudi akairaneria Gadi, “Inche nasoire ase emechando emenene. Gaki bono, tiga ingwe ase okoboko kw’Omonene, ekiagera amaabera aye namanene mono; korende ase amaboko a Mwanyabaanto tintageti ingweo.”
ⅩⅣ Eri’Omonene akarentera Abaisraeli oborwaire obobe bw’ogoita; Abaisraeli chilifu emerongo etano na ebere (70,000) bagakwa. ⅩⅤ Nyasae agatoma omomalaika oye gochia ase Yerusalemu koyosaria; korende ekero omomalaika oria abeete ang’e koyosaria, Omonene akaywechandera, akaonchoreria ebirengererio biaye igoro y’obobe boria, agateebia omomalaika oria ogendererete gosaria omochie, “Bono aisanire, ororobia okoboko kwao!” Omomalaika oria konya oikire ang’e nekeooreri ki’Orinani*21:15 Orinani: erieta riaye rinde: Arauna (rora boigo ebibungo 18-8) Omoyebusi.
ⅩⅥ Daudi akarigereria akarora omomalaika oria bw’Omonene oteneine gati y’ense na igoro; ere nabwate omoyio o birende ase okoboko kwaye okoramborete gochia igoro ya Yerusalemu, na Daudi na abagaaka babegete chianga chi’echigunia, bagatung’ama inse bakaumama. ⅩⅦ Erio Daudi agateebia Nyasae, “Inee! Tari inche nachigete abanto bakabarwa? Ninche nakorire ebibe; nakorire amang’ana amabe mono. Korende ching’ondi echi, ninki chiakorire? Nagosabire aye Omonene, Nyasae one, tiga okoboko kwao kong’ake inche amo neamate ya tata; korende oborwaire tibobaisa goita abanto bao.”
ⅩⅧ Erio omomalaika bw’Omonene agachiika Gadi ateebie Daudi ng’a agende atenenie egesasiimero ase Omonene agwo ase ekeooreri ki’Orinani, Omoyebusi. ⅩⅨ Ase ogochika kw’Omonene koria Gadi amotebetie Daudi, Daudi agatiira. ⅩⅩ Bono Orinani nigo arenge koora engano, na agwo akeonchora akarora omomalaika, na abana baye abamura baane abarenge amo nero bakebisa. ⅩⅪ Daudi agacha ase Orinani, na ekero Orinani amorooche akarua agwo ase ekeooreri, agacha agatung’ama, akaumama ase obosio bwa Daudi. ⅩⅫ Daudi agateebia Orinani, “Ing’a ensemo eyio y’ekeooreri, ing’agachere Omonene egesasiimero, erinde oborwaire botangwe korwa ase abanto. Inche ningakane rigori riaye rionsi.”
ⅩⅩⅢ Erio Orinani agateebia Daudi, “Ira ekeooreri ekio! Aye omonene one omorwoti, kora eki oranche. Rora, narure chiombe chibe ebing’wanso bi’ogosambwa na ebikorero ebi bi’okoorera bibe chinko, na engano ebe okoruegwa kw’endagera. Nakobieire bionsi.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Omorwoti Daudi agakania Orinani, akamoteebia, “Yaya taibo, korende nigo ndabiakanere rigori riikeranu; tinkoira ekere ekiao koa Omonene gose okorua ekeng’wanso ki’ogosambwa eke ntaakanereti.” ⅩⅩⅤ Ase igo Daudi akaa Orinani etaabu y’oborito bwe chisekeli amagana atano na rimo agoakanera ekeooreri keria. ⅩⅩⅥ Daudi akaagachera Omonene egesasiimero aroro, akarua ebing’wanso bi’ogosambwa, na ebi’omorembe, agasaba Omonene, nere akamoiraneria ase ogotoma omorero korwa igoro gocha ase egesasiimero keria gi’ekeng’wanso ki’ogosambwa.
ⅩⅩⅦ Eri’Omonene agachiika omomalaika oria, na ere akairania omoyio oye ime ase ekegoocho†21:27 ekegoocho: engencho yaye n’ekeraro ki’omoyio. ⅩⅩⅧ Korwa ase engaki eria, ekero Daudi aroche ng’a Omonene oiraneirie ogosaba kwaye ase ekeooreri ki’Orinani Omoyebusi, akarua ebing’wanso biaye aroro agwo. ⅩⅩⅨ Korende ase engaki eria Eema y’Omonene, eria Musa atenenetie agwo ase erooro, na egesasiimero gi’ekeng’wanso ki’ogosambwa nigo kiarenge agwo aase aimokererete igoro aaria Gibeoni. ⅩⅩⅩ Korende Daudi tagendete ase egesasiimero keria koboria Nyasae, ekiagera nigo oboete omoyio oria bw’omomalaika bw’Omonene.
