Obobani bore mamincha y’Abaamoni
Ⅰ 25:1: Yer 49:1-6; Ezek 21:28-32; Amos 1:13-15; Sef 2:8-11Naende ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: Ⅱ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, onchoka origererie Abaamoni, obaane mamincha yabo. Ⅲ Obateebie Abaamoni baigwe ring’ana ri’Omonene, oteebe: Aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Inwe nigo mwagogete, mogateeba: Ahaa! Ahaa! Ekero Aase ane Aachenu akoretwe akaba atachenegeti, na ekero ense y’Abaisraeli yatigarete bosa, na ekero Abayuda bairetwe ase obosomba. Ⅳ Ase ayio, nimbarue mbabeeke ase amaboko y’ abanto ba moocha, mobe ababo. Barabwo mbabeeke chiema chiabo ase egati yaino, naende mbaagache obomenyo bwabo aroro; mbarie okwama kwaino na banywe amabeere aino. Ⅴ Nindue omochie o Raba obe oborisia bwe chingamia, na emechie y’Abaamoni ebe ameeri y’amaicho. Erio nario moramanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.
Ⅵ “Aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Inwe nigo mwaagete chinkobi, naende mogatinera amagoro aino inse, mokagoka ne chinkoro chiaino chionsi ase engencho y’amabe anyorete ense y’Abaisraeli. Ⅶ Ase ayio, rora, nindambore okoboko kwane igoro ase more mbae egesusuro, nimbarue mwoswe nebisaku binde. Nimbaite mbasirie korwa ase abanto, ee, mbasirie kegima, morue ase chinse echio. Erio nario moramanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.
Obobani bore mamincha y’Abamoabu
Ⅷ 25:8: Isaya 15:1—16:14; 25:10-12; Yer 48:1-47; Amos 2:1-3; Sef 2:8-11“Aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Abamoabu na Abaseiri nigo bagokwana ng’a Abayuda nigo banga buna abanto b’ebisaku binde bionsi. Ⅸ Ase engencho eyio ninyasiore ense ya Moabu, agwo ase egetirimboko ase emechie ere, eyio ere obonene bw’ense eria, nero Beti‐Yesimoti, na Baali‐Meoni, na Kiriataimu. Ⅹ Nimbeke ense eria amo nense y’Abaamoni ase amaboko y’ abanto ba Moocha, chibe echiabo, na Abaamoni abwo tibakoba bakoinyorwa nabaanto b’ebisaku binde nonya ng’ake. Ⅺ Ninachere Abamoabu ekiina, erio bamanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.
Obobani bore mamincha y’Abaedomu
Ⅻ 25:12: Isaya 34:5-17; 63:1-6; Yer 49:7-22; Ezek 35:1-15; Amos 1:11,12; Obad 1:1-14; Malk 1:2-5“Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba: Abaedomu bakoreire Abayuda bobe ase okorusia egesiomba, na babeire nokomocha ase okobakorera igo. ⅩⅢ Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Nindambore okoboko kwane mamincha y’Abaedomu; ning’ite nsirie kera omonto, na kera eng’iti. Ninsarie ense eyio ebe bosa. Abanto mbakenywe nemioro korwa Temani goika Dedani. ⅩⅣ Nindusie egesiomba kiane ase ense y’Abaedomu goetera ase amaboko y’Abaisraeli, abanto bane. Barabwo mbakorere Abaedomu koreng’ana nendamwamu y’ane, na ekeririanda kiane. Erio Abaedomu mbanyare komanya egesiomba kiane. Nabo Omonene ateebire.
Obobani mamincha y’Abafilisti
ⅩⅤ 25:15: Isaya 14:29-31; Yer 47:1-7; Yoel 3:4-8; Amos 1:6-8; Sef 2:4-7; Zak 9:5-7“Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba: Abafirisiti barusirie egesiomba bare nobobisa ase chinkoro chiabo, na ase okogecha kogendererete botambe basiririe ababisa babo kegima. ⅩⅥ Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Nindambore okoboko kwane mbae Abafirisiti egesusuro, ning’ite Abakereti, mbasirie kegima, naende nsirie kegima abwo baratigare ase chinse chia ng’umbu mbarabare y’enyancha. ⅩⅦ Nindusie egesiomba ekenene mono ase bare, nimbe nendamwamu y’okobaa egesusuro. Erio bamanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene, ekero ndarusie egesiomba ase bare.”
