Abanto ba Omonene omonyene
(Okong’ 34:11-16)
Ⅰ 7:1: Ogok 13:19“Omonene Nyasae oino, nabaikie ase ense eria, mogochia koira ebe eyaino, na aseerie ebisaku ebinge birue ase amasio aino, ebisaku bitano bibere bire ebinene, naende bire nechinguru kobabua. Ebisaku ebio nabio ebi: Abahiti, na Abagirigasi, na Abaamori, na Abakaanani, na Abaperisi, na Abahivi, na Abayebusi. Ⅱ Ekero Omonene Nyasae oino, arabeeke abanto abwo ase amaboko aino, nainwe mobabue, goika mobaite na kobasiria kegima, timobaisa gokora okobwatana kw’ogotianania nabarabwo, gose kobarorera amaabera. Ⅲ Timobaisa konyuomerana nabarabwo; timorua abana baino abaiseke ase abamura babo, gose timonyuomeria abana baino abamura ase abaiseke babo, Ⅳ ekiagera barabwo nigo baraonchore abana baino abamura batige gosasiima Omonene, erio bakorere chinyasae ching’ao. Eri’Omonene abaise koba n’endamwamu ase more, abasirie ara rimo rioka. Ⅴ 7:5: Okoir 12:3Aya naro morabakorere: Motagore ebisasimero biabo, mobutore chisiiro chiabo bagosasiima, monachane emete y’Asera, enyasae yabo enkuungu, na mosambe n’omorero emegwekano yabo bagosasiima, Ⅵ 7:6: Okong’ 19:5; Okoir 4:20; 14:2; 26:18; Tito 2:14; 1 Petr 2:9ekiagera inwe n’abanto mwatananetwe koba abachenu ase Omonene Nyasae oino. Ere obachoorire mobe abanto baye omonyene kobua abanto bande bonsi bare ase ense.
Ⅶ “Omonene tang’usetwe nainwe, gose tabachoorete ekiagera mwarenge abange kobua abanto bande (inwe nigo mwarenge abake mono kobua abanto bonsi), Ⅷ korende ekiagera nigo abanchete, naende akagania goikerania eira eria arierete chisokoro chiaino. Ayio akagera Omonene akabang’anyia n’okoboko kwaye kore nechinguru, na kobabooria korwa ase ense y’obosomba korwa ase okoboko gwa Farao, omorwoti o Misiri. Ⅸ 7:9: Okong’ 20:5,6; 34:6,7; Omob 14:18; Okoir 5:9,10Gaki manya ng’a Omonene Nyasae oino, nere bweka Nyasae bw’ekeene, naende Nyasae bw’obwegenwa, oyokorenda okobwatana kwaye kw’ogotianania, na koorokia obwanchani bwaye botari koera goika ebiaare chilifu ase baria bamwanchete na babwatete amachiiko aye. Ⅹ Korende ere nigo akobaakana obobe baria bamogeechete, na kobasiria. Tari goteebana koa omonto oria omogeechete egesusuro. Ⅺ Bono morende na koigwera amachiiko, na emeroberio echiikire, mokore ayio onsi nabachiikire ase rituko ria reero.
Ogosesenigwa ase abaigweri
(Okoir 28:1-14)
Ⅻ 7:12: Okoir 11:13-17“Komoraigwere emeroberio eye echiikire na koyereenda, na koyekora, rirori’Omonene Nyasae oino, naikeranie na gokong’ia okobwatana kwaye kw’ogotianania, okwo akorete nainwe, na kobaorokeria obwanchani bwaye botari koera, buna arierete chisokoro chiaino eira ase ogotiana. ⅩⅢ Ere nabanche na kobasesenia, nabamentekanie. Boigo nasesenie oroiboro rwe chinda chiaino, na okwama kw’emegondo yaino, na endagera yaino, na edivai yaino, na amaguta aino y’amazeituni; chiombe chiaino ’nchimentekane, amo ne ching’ondi ne chimbori chiaino, ase ense eria arierete chisokoro chiaino eira ng’a n’abayee. ⅩⅣ ’Namosesenigwe kobua ebisaku binde bionsi. Ase egati yaino onde tari omosacha gose omokungu, oraabe omogomba, gose etugo ende yaino tekogombaa. ⅩⅤ Omonene narusie amarwaire onsi korwa ase more; nonya nobomo bw’amarwaire aria amabe arenge Misiri, aria ase mwamenyete, tagoocha igoro ase more kobachaanda, korende nigo arayarente ase abanto baria bonsi babageechete. ⅩⅥ Abanto abwo bonsi Omonene Nyasae oino, arabeeke ase amaboko aino, mobasirie, timobarorera amaabera. Timobaisa gokorera chinyasae chiabo, ekiagera ase ogokora igo, chirochio ’nachinyaare kobatiga.
ⅩⅦ “Moise goteeba ase chinkoro chiaino ime: Ebisaku ebi nigo bire ebinene gotobua, naki toranyaare kobiseeria birueo? ⅩⅧ Timobairoka, korende moinyore buna Omonene Nyasae oino, akorerete Farao na abanto bonsi ba Misiri. ⅩⅨ Moinyore amaakwa amanene aria mwarooche, na ebikone, na amakumia, na buna Omonene Nyasae oino, abarusetieo n’okoboko kwaye kore nechinguru, koramboire. Naboigo arakore abanto bonsi baria moirogete. ⅩⅩ Naende Omonene natome ebiaramba ase egati yabo bibarase, baria abatigari barebise korwa ase more, basire kegima. ⅩⅪ Timoondoka ase engencho yabo, ekiagera Omonene Nyasae oino, nigo are egati yaino, ere nere Nyasae omonene, naende oyo bw’okoondokia. ⅩⅫ Omonene Nyasae oino, naseerie ebisaku ebio ng’orang’ora, birue ase more. Timobaisa kobiseeria biere kegima ase ekero ekemo gioka. Moise gokora bo, ching’iti chi’orosana nigo chiramentekane chibachaande. ⅩⅩⅢ Korende Omonene Nyasae oino, nabeeke ababisa baino ase amaboko aino, erio abatantanie, nainwe goika mobasirie kegima. ⅩⅩⅣ Nabeeke abarwoti babo ase amaboko aino, erio mobaite na kobasiria nonya n’amarieta abo tainyorwa ase ense. Omonto onde tari, nonya n’oyomo, oranyaare korwana nainwe na kobabua; goika mobasirie kegima. ⅩⅩⅤ Emegwekano yabo bagosasiima moyesambe n’omorero. Timogania koira chifeta gose chitaabu chire ase emegwekano eyio. Moise gochigania nigo chiraabe chikobatega. Ayio onsi nigo arabe okogeechia ase Omonene Nyasae oino. ⅩⅩⅥ Timobaisa korenta okogeechia buna okwo ase chinyomba chiaino. Komorakore igo, nigo moraragererigwe. Mokogeeche na kogochaaya, ekiagera korokwo nigo koragereirie.”
