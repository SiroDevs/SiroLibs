Ekiina kia Nyasae
Ⅰ 2:1: Mat 7:1; Luk 6:37Ase ayio, aye onde bwensi, tori naya ogotaamera, ekero okogaambia oyonde ekiina, ekiagera ase okomogaambia oyonde ekiina, naye okwegaambia omonyene. Inaki okomogaambia, na aye naro ayio ogokora? Ⅱ Intwe twamanyire ng’a ekiina kia Nyasae nigo kere boronge igoro ase abwo bagokora ayio. Ⅲ Aye omonto oyokogaambia baria bagokora ayio, naye omonyene naro ayio ogokora, Inee, nokagete ng’a aye notame korwa ase ekiina kia Nyasae? Ⅳ Gose ngwachaayire obonge bw’obuya bwaye, na oboremereria bwaye, na okoganya kwaye, na tomanyeti ng’a obuya bwa Nyasae nigo borigetie gokoraa gochia ase okoonchoka? Ⅴ Korende ase obokong’u bwao na enkoro yao etari nokoonchoka, kwegacheire endamwamu ase rituko riria ri’endamwamu, ekero ekiina kia boronge kia Nyasae kerache koorokigwa, Ⅵ 2:6: Zab 62:12; Emeb 24:12ekiagera ere naache komoa kera omonto koreng’ana nogokora kwaye; Ⅶ nabae obogima bwa kare na kare baria bakorigia obonene, na ogosika, na ogotasareka, na gokora amaya ase oboremereria. Ⅷ Korende endamwamu na ekeririanda inabibe igoro ase baria beanchete abanyene, batari koigwera ekeene, korende bakoigwera ayatari boronge. Ⅸ Emechando na obororo inabibe igoro ase kera oyomo bw’abana ba Mwanyabaanto oyogokora amabe, ritang’ani ase Omoyahudi, na ase Omogiriki boigo; Ⅹ korende obonene na ogosika, na omorembe nigo bire ase kera oyomo oyogokora amaya, ritang’ani ase Omoyahudi, na ase Omogiriki boigo. Ⅺ 2:11: Okoir 10:17Ekiagera gwanchereria tikori ase Nyasae.
Ⅻ Inaki Abagiriki bakorire ebibe batari namachiiko a Musa, inabasire batari namachiiko; na Abayahudi bakorire ebibe bare namachiiko, nabo baranacherwe ekiina ase amachiiko. ⅩⅢ Inaki tari abwo bakoigwa amachiiko bare abanyene oboronge ase obosio bwa Nyasae, korende mbaria bagoikerania amachiiko, nabwo baraegwe oboronge. ⅩⅣ Abanto b’Ebisaku batari namachiiko, ekero bagokora buna engencho ya Mwanyabaanto ere aya amachiiko are korigia, nonya abwo tibari namachiiko, babeire amachiiko boka abanyene. ⅩⅤ Abwo nigo bakoorokia ng’a aya amachiiko are korigia ariikirwe ime ase chinkoro chiabo. Okomanya gwe chinkoro chiabo ime nigo gokobabera kirori, na ebirengererio biabo nigo bikobasoera gose kobaegerera. ⅩⅥ Nabo arache koba ase rituko riria, koreng’ana nenchiri eye nkorandia, Nyasae kagochi‐gocha nanachere obobisi bwa Mwanyabaanto ekiina, goetera ase Kristo Yeso.
Abayahudi n’amachiiko
ⅩⅦ Korende aye okweroka Omoyahudi, na gosemeretie amachiiko, na gwetogia ase Nyasae. ⅩⅧ Kwamanyire ogwancha kwaye, naende nomanyete buya amang’ana ayare amaya, ekiagera kworokererigwe amachiiko. ⅩⅨ Nore nobomaene ng’a aye omonyene nigo ore omoraai bw’emeuko, na omobaso bw’abare ase omosunte ime. ⅩⅩ Omotebereria bw’abariri, na omworokia bw’abana abake, ekiagera kwanyorire okworokereria okuya gwe chisemi na ekeene bire ime ase amachiiko. ⅩⅪ Aye okworokereria abande, Inee, tokweorokereria aye omonyene? Aye okorandia ng’a omonto taiba, tori koiba aye omonyene? ⅩⅫ Aye ogoteeba ng’a omonto tatomana, tori gotomana aye omonyene? Aye ogechete emegwekano y’ogosasimwa, aye tori koiba korwa ase chi Hekalu? ⅩⅩⅢ Aye ogwetogeria amachiiko, Inee, nigo ogosareria Nyasae ogosika ase ogosaria kwao kw’amachiiko? ⅩⅩⅣ 2:24: Isaya 52:5Inaki amariiko nigo agoteeba, “Erieta ria Nyasae nigo rikoramwa ase egati y’ abanto b’Ebisaku ase engencho yaino.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Okwaroka nigo kogwenerete onye kore omoigweri bw’amachiiko, korende onye kore omosaria bw’amachiiko, okwaroka kwao kwabeire ogotaaroka. ⅩⅩⅥ Karabe ere oyotaarogeti okobwata amachiiko, Inee, ogotaaroka kwaye tigokobarwa ng’a kwabeire okwaroka? ⅩⅩⅦ Ere oyotaarogeti ase omobere, onye obwatete amachiiko, Inee, tagokonachera ekiina aye, oyore namachiiko na okwaroka, naende osaririe amachiiko? ⅩⅩⅧ Inaki ere oyore Omoyahudi ase isiko oka, tari Omoyahudi bw’ekeene, gose okwaroka koria gokorire isiko ase omobere, tikori okwaroka gw’ekeene. ⅩⅩⅨ 2:29: Okoir 30:6Korende ere n’Omoyahudi bw’ekeene oyore Omoyahudi o ime, na okwaroka gw’ekeene nogw’enkoro. Eye nemeremo y’Omoika, teri ey’amariiko. Ogotogigwa kwaye tikorueti ase Mwanyabaanto, korende nase Nyasae.
