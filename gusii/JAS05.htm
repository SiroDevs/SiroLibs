Ogokuurerwa kw’abanda
Ⅰ Igwa bono, abamore abanda, rera na goaka egeturi ase engencho y’emechando yaino eyekobacheera. Ⅱ 5:2: Mat 6:19Chinibo chiaino chiagundire, ne chianga chiaino chiarirwe nebigwankwa. Ⅲ Chitaabu ne chifeta chiaino chiasoirwe noboboosu, na oboboosu obwo bwabeire kirori igoro yaino, na borobwo naborie emebere yaino buna omorero okoria. Mwegacheire emonga amatuko aya ay’omoerio. Ⅳ 5:4: Okoir 24:14,15Rigereria, mwabarinire abakoreri, abagesire emegondo yaino, eng’eria yabo. Bono eng’eria eria ekorera, na okorera kw’abagesi baria gwaikire ase amato ’Omonene bw’Emeganda. Ⅴ Igo momenyete ase ense eye ase okwegokia, na timwetangeti ase okogania kwaino. Mwenoririe ase chinkoro chiaino goika rituko ri’okonyenya. Ⅵ Mwagambirie omonyene oboronge na komoita, na ere tabaamerereti inwe.
Oboremereria na ogosaba
Ⅶ Ase ayio, baminto, remereria goika Omonene achiche. Rigereria, omoremi nigo akoganya okwama kw’omogondo okore ne rigori. Igo akoremereria ase ayio, goika anyore embura ye ritang’ani na ey’okwamia endagera. Ⅷ Naboigo, nainwe remereria, kong’ia chinkoro chiaino, ekiagera ogocha kw’Omonene kwabeire ang’e. Ⅸ Abaminto, timwemurunganerania, timocha kogambigwa. Rigereria, Omogambi nigo ateneine gesieri. Ⅹ Abaminto, bwatia ababani baria bakwanete ase erieta ri’Omonene, babe ekiorokererio gi’okobogoria obokong’u, na eki’oboremereria. Ⅺ 5:11: a Ayubu 1:21,22; 2:10; b Zab 103:8Rigereria, abwo baremereretie twabaabarire ng’a mbasesenirie. Mwaigwete amang’ana ’Ayubu buna aremereretie, na buna Omonene amokorerete ase omoerio. Naki Omonene nomonyene amabeebe amange na amaabera.
Ⅻ 5:12: Mat 5:34-37Korende abaminto, kobua ase onsi timotiana, ase igoro, gose ase ense, gose koria muma ende yonsi, korende “ee” yaino ebe “ee”, na “yaya” yaino ebe “yaya”, timocha kobekwa inse y’okogambigwa.
ⅩⅢ Omonto onde oino karanyorekane nemechando, tiga asabe. Onde omo oino, onye ore nomoyo bw’ogosasoka, tiga atogie ase ogoteera. ⅩⅣ 5:14: Mar 6:13Onye omonto onde oino obeire omorwaire, tiga arangerie abagaaka b’ekanisa bamosabere na bamoake amaguta ase erieta ri’Omonene. ⅩⅤ Na ogosaba koria gw’okwegena nagotoorie omorwaire oria, n’Omonene namogwenie. Onye omonto oria okorire ebibe naaberwe. ⅩⅥ Orokerania ebibe biaino inwe ase inwe, na gosaberana, monyore okogwenigwa. Ogosaba kw’omonyene oboronge nigo kore nengencho enene mono gokorasabwe nomokia. ⅩⅦ 5:17: 1 Abar 17:1; 18:1Elija nigo arenge omonto bwe chingencho echimo naintwe, agasaba nomokia ng’a embura tetwa. Na embura tiyatwa igoro ase ense ase engaki y’emiaka etato na emetienyi etano nomo. ⅩⅧ 5:18: 1 Abar 18:42-45Agasaba naende, igoro ekarenta embura, na ense ekama okwama kwaye.
ⅩⅨ Abaminto, onye omonto onde oino oteanire korwa ase ekeene, na monto onde amoiranie, ⅩⅩ 5:20: Emeb 10:12; 1 Petr 4:8tiga ere amanye ng’a oyokoirania omonyene ebibe korwa ase enchera y’ogosira, ere natoorie omoyo oye korwa ase amakweri na atube obonge bw’ebibe.
