Okoonchoka ekieni kwa Yeso
(Mar 9:2-13; Luk 9:28-36)
Ⅰ Na nyuma y’amatuko atano na rimo Yeso akaira Petero, na Yakobo, na Yohana momura omwabo Yakobo, bakagenda nabarabwo igoro ase egetunwa egetambe barabwo boka. Ⅱ Akaonchoka ekieni kiaye ase bare, obosio bwaye bokagirigenta buna erioba, ne chianga chiaye chikaraba buna omobaso. Ⅲ Na rora, Musa na Elija bakabaorokera, bagakwana nere. Ⅳ Petero agateebia Yeso, “Omonene, mbuya intwe koba aiga; onye ngwancha, ninkore aiga ebigutu bitato, ekemo ekiao, ekemo ekia Musa, na ekemo eki’Elija.” Ⅴ 17:5: a Omoch 22:2; Zab 2:7; Isaya 42:1; Mat 3:17; 12:18; Mar 1:11; Luk 3:22; b Okoir 18:1517:5: 2 Petr 1:17,18Ekero arenge gokwana, rora, riire rirabu rikababisia, na eriogi rikarwa ase riire riria, rigateba, “Oyo n’Omwana one omwanchwa, ngokirie nere; moigwere ere.” Ⅵ Ekero aborokigwa bachia koigwa aya, bakagwa inse maumama, bakabwatwa nomoondoko omonene. Ⅶ Korende Yeso agacha akabakuna, akabora, “Imoka, timobaisa kwoboa.” Ⅷ Ekero bachia koimokia amaiso abo, tibarora monto otatiga Yeso bweka.
Ⅸ Kobagotirimboka korwa ase egetunwa, Yeso akabatianera, akabora, “Timoteebia monto onde okorora oko goika Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto aboke korwa ase abakuure.” Ⅹ 17:10: Malk 4:5Aborokigwa baye bakamobooria, “Nase ki rende aborokia b’amariiko bagoteeba ng’a negwenerete Elija ache ritang’ani?” Ⅺ Yeso akabairaneria, “Nekeene, n’Elija ogochia gocha ritang’ani, na ere naroisie onsi. Ⅻ 17:12: Mat 11:14Korende nabateebirie ng’a Elija oikire gocha; na barabwo tibamomanyete, korende bakamokorera onsi aya barigetie. Naboigo n’Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto nachie konyora emechando ase amaboko abo.” ⅩⅢ Erio aborokigwa baye bakamanya ng’a ere nigo are kobateebia igoro ya Yohana Omobatisi.
Yeso okogwenia omomura obwatire nekerecha
(Mar 9:14-29; Luk 9:37-43)
ⅩⅣ Ekero bachia goika ase omoganda, omonto oyomo akamochiera agwo, akamotung’amera, akabora, ⅩⅤ “Omonene, ororere omomura one amaabera, ekiagera nare nendurume, nigo achandegete mono. Inaki ere, kero kende nigo akogwa ase omorero ime, na kero kende ase amaache ime. ⅩⅥ Namorenta ase aborokigwa bao, tibanyara komogwenia.” ⅩⅦ Yeso akairaneria, “Inwe ekegori getari nokwegena, naende ekebe, nimbe amo nainwe goika ririri? Nindemererie nainwe goika ririri? Mondetere aiga.” ⅩⅧ Yeso agatogonyera ekerecha, na kerokio kegatiga omwana oria na ere akagwenigwa ekero keria. ⅩⅨ Erio aborokigwa bagachiera Yeso bobisi, bakaboora, “Ninki kiagera intwe titwanyara korusia ekerecha keria?” ⅩⅩ 17:20: Mat 21:21; Mar 11:23; 1 Abakr 13:2Akabateebia, “Nase engencho y’okwegena kwaino kore ogoke. Ekeene nabateebirie: Moise koba nokwegena koreng’aine buna entetere y’Omosirapi, inamoteebie egetunwa eke: Karue agwo, ogende aaria, na kerokio inakeruo, tirikoba ring’ana ritanyarekane ase more. [ ⅩⅪ Korende ekerecha kenga buna eke tikegosoka otatiga ase ogosaba na okweng’ata.”]
Yeso ogokwana naende igoro y’ogokwa kwaye
(Mar 9:30-32; Luk 9:43-45)
ⅩⅫ Ekero basangererekanete Galili, Yeso akabateebia, “Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto ngochia are kobekwa ase amaboko y’ abanto. ⅩⅩⅢ Barabwo inabamoite, ne rituko ria gatato nabooke.” Bagaichana mono.
Yeso ogoakana ebango ye Hekalu
ⅩⅩⅣ 17:24: Okong’ 30:13; 38:26Kobaikire Kapernaumu, abaare korusia ebango ye Hekalu bagachiera Petero, bakamobooria, “Inee! Omworokia oino nkorua are ebango?” ⅩⅩⅤ Akabairaneria, “Ee, nkorua are.” Goika agasoa nyomba, Yeso agatang’ana gokwana na Petero, akamobooria, “Inki okorengereria Simioni? Nase ng’o abarwoti b’ense bakorusia ebango n’echoogo? Nkorwa ase abana babo, gose nase abanto bande?” ⅩⅩⅥ Na ekero Petero amoiraneria, “Nkorwa ase abande,” Yeso akamoteebia, “Rirorio abana babo barabwo nabasibore. ⅩⅩⅦ Korende titobaisa kobagechia, genda nyancha orute endobio na enswe eraache ritang’ani oyebwate; na ekero okoyiasamia omonwa oye norore egesamunye kiebesa kereo, okerusie, obae abwo ase engencho y’ane amo naye.”
