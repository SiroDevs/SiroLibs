Obomo bw’omobere
Ⅰ Ase ayio, inche omosibwa ase engencho y’Omonene nabasoroire mogenderere buna egwenerete okorangerigwa kwaino mwarangereretigwe Ⅱ 4:2: Abakol 3:12,13ase obokanyeku bwonsi na obwororo, na ase oboremereria mokobogorerania ase obwanchani. Ⅲ Mokore omokia konyora obomo bw’Omoika ase egesibo ki’omorembe. Ⅳ Omobere n’oyoomo, na Omoika n’oyoomo, buna mwarangeretigwe ase ogosemeria okomo gw’okorangerigwa kwaino. Ⅴ Omonene oyomo, okwegena okomo, ebatiso eyemo, Ⅵ Nyasae oyomo, ore Tata o bonsi, ere ore igoro ase bonsi, goetera ase bonsi, na ime ase bonsi. Ⅶ Korende kera oyomo oito naetwe obuya koreng’ana n’ekerengo gi’ekeegwa gia Kristo. Ⅷ 4:8: Zab 68:18Ase ayio nigo agoteeba.
“Ekero ageendete igoro, akaira abasakore,
akaa Mwanyabaanto ebiegwa.”
Ⅸ (Gaki, ring’ana eri, “Ageendete igoro”, engencho yaye inki otatiga naboigo aigete nonya ngoika ensemo yainse y’ense? Ⅹ Oyoigete nere oyio ogendete boigo igoro ya igoro, erinde ebinto bionsi biichorwe nere.) Ⅺ Ere akarua abande babe abatomwa, na abande ababani, na abande abarandia, na abande abarisia, na abande aboorokia. Ⅻ Agakora bo ase ogoikerania abachenu ase emeremo y’obosomba, goika omobere o Kristo oagachwe. ⅩⅢ Nabo goika intwe twensi tonyaare goikera obomo bw’okwegena, na komanya mono Omwana o Nyasae, na koba omonto omoikeranu, goika ekerengo ki’ogokiina kw’ogoichorwa gwa Kristo; ⅩⅣ erinde titoba naende abana ebing’werere, bagotumatumigwa aaria na aaria, na koirurukigwa ase kera embeo y’okworokereria, ase obong’ainereria bw’abaanto, ekero bakobang’aina kobwatia chiinchera chi’okobasiria. ⅩⅤ Korende tokwane ekeene ase obwanchani, tokiine goika toikere Kristo ase chiinchera chionsi; nere ore omotwe. ⅩⅥ 4:16: Abakol 2:19Ase are ere omobere bwonsi onyare gokiina ogokiina kw’omobere na kweagaacha omonyene ase obwanchani, korasang’ananigwe na kobwatanigwa ase ogokoonywa gwa kera ekiimo koreng’ana n’emeremo ya kera ensemo.
Obogima obokoro na boria oboyia
ⅩⅦ Bono ’ngokwana nde ring’ana eri, na koba kirori ase Omonene; korwa bono timogeenda buna abanto b’Ebisaku bakogeenda ase ebirengorerio biabo bitari nengeencho. ⅩⅧ Obong’aini bwabo bwabekirwe omosunte, barusananiigwe korwa ase obogima bwa Nyasae ekiagera ase ogotamanya koria kore ime ase bare ase engencho y’obokong’u bwe chinkoro chiabo. ⅩⅨ Chinkoro chiabo chiabeire n’amagandi, bebeekire ase chingeencho chiimbe chi’omobere, erio ase obwegimeria banyaare gokora kera engencho y’eubi. ⅩⅩ Korende inwe timworokereretigwe komomanya Kristo ase enchera eyio. ⅩⅪ Inwe mwamoigwete na kworokererigwa nere, buna ekeene kere ime ase Yeso. ⅩⅫ 4:22: Abakol 3:9Rusia chingeencho chiaino chi’eritang’ani chi’oboMwanyabaanto bwa kare, obwasariigwe ase okobwatia okogania kw’obong’ainereria; ⅩⅩⅢ mokorwe abayia ase emeika na ebirengererio biaino, ⅩⅩⅣ 4:24: a Abakol 3:10; b Omoch 1:26mweboyie omonto omoyia, oyotongetwe ase omogwekano o Nyasae ase oboronge na obochenu bw’ekeene.
Emeroberio y’obogima oboyia
ⅩⅩⅤ 4:25: Zak 8:16Gaki aboria oborimo, mokw’ane ekeene kera omonto nomogisangio oye, inaki nigo tore ebiimo ebimo intwe ase intwe. ⅩⅩⅥ 4:26: Zab 4:4Mobee, korende timokora ebibe; erioba tiriigwa na inwe more n’endamwamu, ⅩⅩⅦ gose timoa Saitani ribaga. ⅩⅩⅧ Omoibi taiba naende; mbuya akore emeremo ase omokia namaboko aye na gokora ayare amaya, erinde abe negento akomwatanera oyoremeire. ⅩⅩⅨ Ogokwana okobe tikorua ase emenwa yaino, korende okore okuya na okoranyaare kobaa obuya abwo bagokoigwa na koagaacha abaganeirie koagaachwa. ⅩⅩⅩ Gose timoichania Omoika Omochenu o Nyasae, ase are ere oyomwabegetwe ekemanyererio goika rituko ri’okoboorigwa. ⅩⅩⅪ Obororo bwonsi, na endamwamu, na okobea, na eriomana, na emerumi birusigwe ase more, amo n’obobe bwonsi. ⅩⅩⅫ 4:32: Abakol 3:13Mobe nobwororo inwe ase inwe, mororerane amabeebe, na kwaberana inwe ase inwe, buna Nyasae ase Kristo abaaberete inwe.
