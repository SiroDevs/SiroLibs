Okoonchoka gwa Saulo
(Ogok 22:6-16; 26:12-18)
Ⅰ Korende ase chingaki echio Saulo akamenta kobagosa na korengereria goita aborokigwa bw’Omonene. Akagenda ase Omokuani omonene, Ⅱ akamobooria amoe amarube ariikire ase chisinagogi chia Damesiki, erinde onye okonyora abanto b’Enchera eye, abasacha gose abakungu, abasibe na kobarenta Yerusalemu. Ⅲ Ekero arenge kogenda, gaikire ang’e na Damesiki, erio mobasokano amasase akarwa igoro, akamomesera korwa chinsemo chionsi. Ⅳ Akagwa inse, akaigwa eriogi rikomoteebia: “Saulo, Saulo, nase ki ogonchanda iga?”
Ⅴ Akamoboria: “Naye ng’o Omonene?”
Akairaneria: “Ninche Yeso, oyo gwachandire aye, Ⅵ korende imoka osoe omochie, noteebigwe ayarakogwenere okore.”
Ⅶ Abanto abagendete amo nere bagatenena, bagakira‐kiri; bakaigwa eriogi, korende tibarora monto. Ⅷ Saulo akaimoka korwa inse, korende ekero achia koramokia amaiso aye tarora gento. Bakamobwata okoboko, bakamoira Damesiki. Ⅸ Akaba omouko amatuko atato, atari koria, nonya nkonywa.
Saulo ase omochie o Damesiki
Ⅹ Ase Damesiki narengeo omworokigwa ore korokwa Anania. Omonene akamoteebia ase chindoto: “Anania!” Ere akairaneria: “Omonene nao inde aiga.” Ⅺ Omonene akamoteebia: “Imoka ogende enchera eria Endonge, origie ime ase enyomba ya Yudasi omonto okorokwa Saulo o Tariso; rora, nao are agosaba. Ⅻ Na ere ase okorora ororire omonto okorokwa Anania ogosoa, okomobekera amaboko, anyare korora.”
ⅩⅢ Korende Anania akairaneria: “Omonene, naigure korwa ase abanto abange igoro y’omonto oyo, buna akoreire abachenu bao Yerusalemu amabe amange. ⅩⅣ Aiga nigo are nokobua korwa ase abakuani abanene asibe abanto bonsi baria bakorangeria erieta riao.”
ⅩⅤ Korende Omonene akamoteebia: “Genda aye rioka, ekiagera oyio nomokoreri one omochore, aire erieta riane gochia ase abanto b’Ebisaku, na ase abarwoti, na ase Abaisraeli. ⅩⅥ Inche nimworokie onsi ayaramogwenere gochandwa ase engencho y’erieta riane.”
ⅩⅦ Anania akagenda, agasoa nyomba ime. Akamobekera amaboko aye, akabora: “Oyominto Saulo, Omonene Yeso, ere oyokoorokerete ase enchera kware gochera, ontomire, erinde onyare korora naende, na oichorwe n’Omoika Omochenu.” ⅩⅧ Erio kegima ebinto buna amasabo bikagwa korwa ase amaiso aye, akanyora okorora, agatenena, akabatiswa, ⅩⅨ akaragera, akanyora chinguru.
Akaba agwo amatuko atari make amo naborokigwa abarenge Damesiki. ⅩⅩ Erio agwo kegima akamorandia Yeso ase chisinagogi, okobora: “Ere n’Omwana o Nyasae.” ⅩⅪ Na bonsi abamoigwete bagakumia, bagateeba: “Tari ere oyo orenge Yerusalemu goteema goita abare korangeria erieta eri? Naende aiga, erinde abasibe, abaire ase abakuani abanene?” ⅩⅫ Korende Saulo akagenderera komenteka koba ne chinguru, agatantania Abayahudi abamenyete Damesiki, ase okoorokia ekeene ng’a Yeso nere Kristo.
ⅩⅩⅢ 9:23: 2 Abakr 11:32,33Ekero amatuko amange aetire, Abayahudi bakamogamba, erinde banyare komoita, ⅩⅩⅣ korende okogamba kwabo gokamanyekana na Saulo. Bakamoboera ase ebiita omobaso na obotuko, erinde banyare komoita. ⅩⅩⅤ Korende aborokigwa baye bakamoira botuko na komoikeria ase orwaki, ore egetonga ime.
Saulo ore ase Yerusalemu na Tariso
ⅩⅩⅥ Ekero achia goika Yerusalemu agateema kobwatana naborokigwa; na barabwo bonsi bakamoiroka, ekiagera tibanchete ng’a ere nomworokigwa arenge. ⅩⅩⅦ Korende Barnaba akamobwata okoboko, akamoira ase abatomwa, akabateebereria buna Saulo aroche Omonene nchera, naende ng’a Omonene nakwanete nere, na buna arandetie noboremu ase Damesiki ase erieta ria Yeso. ⅩⅩⅧ Saulo akaba amo nabarabwo ase Yerusalemu, agasoa na gosoka, ⅩⅩⅨ akarandia noboremu ase erieta ri’Omonene. Agakwana na koamererania n’Abayahudi abarenge gokwana Ekegiriki, korende barabwo bakarigia komoita. ⅩⅩⅩ Ekero abamwabo bachia konyora amang’ana aya, bakamoira goika Kaisaria, bakamotoma agende Tariso.
ⅩⅩⅪ Erio ekanisa ekanyora omorembe ase chinsemo chionsi chia Boyahudi, na Galili, na Samaria. Ekagenderera koagachwa ase okoiroka kw’Omonene, na ase okoremia kw’Omoika Omochenu ekamentekana.
Okogwenia gwa Petero ase Lida
ⅩⅩⅫ Ekero Petero are goetanana ase chinsemo chionsi, agatirimboka gochia ase abachenu abamenyete Lida. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Akanyora omonto oyomo aroro ore korokwa Ainea. Omonto oyio konya obeire ekeoru*9:33 ekeoru: engencho yaye omonto omorwaire mono borere ase engaki y’emiaka etano na etato, are rigata. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Petero akamoteebia: “Ainea, Yeso Kristo okogwenirie; imoka bware oborere bwao!” Erio kegima akaimoka. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Na abanto bonsi abamenyete Lida na Saroni bakamorora, bakaonchokera Omonene.
Dorika okoiranerigwa obogima
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Ase Yopa narengeo omworokigwa oyomo ore korokwa Tabitha, engencho yaye Dorika, gose Engabi. Omokungu oyio nigo arenge oichire nogokora okuya na ebiegwa ebi arenge korua. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Ase amatuko aria akarwara, agakwa. Ekero bamosibirie bakamobeka akanyomba ka irongo. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Ekiagera Lida nigo orenge ang’e na Yopa, na aborokigwa konya baigure ng’a Petero nao are aria, bagatoma abanto babere asare komosorora na komoteebia: “Koranche, totebana gocha ase tore.” ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Petero akaimoka, akagenda amo nabarabwo. Ekero aikire aroro, bakamoira akanyomba ka irongo. Ababoraka bonsi bagatenena ang’e nere bakorera, na komworokia chiganchwa ne chianga echi Dorika arenge koroisia ekero arenge amo nabarabwo. ⅩⅬ Petero akabasokia bonsi isiko, agatung’ama, agasaba. Akeonchora gochia ase egetondo, agateeba: “Tabitha, imoka!” Na ere akaramokia amaiso aye; ekero arora Petero, akaboka, agaikaransa. ⅩⅬⅠ Petero akamoa okoboko kwaye, akamoimokia. Erio akarangeria abachenu baria na ababoraka, akamorenta ase bare ore moyo. ⅩⅬⅡ Aya akamanyekana ase Yopa, ase omochie omogima, na abanto abange bakamwege n’Omonene. ⅩⅬⅢ Petero akamenya Yopa amatuko atai make ase enyomba y’omonto oyomo ore korokwa Simioni, omooroisia bw’amao.
