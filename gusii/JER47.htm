Obobani Omonene igoro y’Abafilisti
Ⅰ 47:1: Isaya 14:29-31; Ezek 25:15-17; Yoel 3:4-8; Amos 1:6-8; Sef 2:4-7; Zak 9:5-7Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha ase Yeremia igoro y’Abafilisti ekero Farao atarabua Gasa, rikamoteebia,
Ⅱ “Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba:
Naama! Amache naimokerere igoro korwa sugusu,
naichore aminyoke buna emegoko emenene,
naichore na gotuba ense na bionsi ebire aroro;
namere emechie na bonsi abamenyeteo.
Erio abanto mbarere,
na abamenyi bonsi b’ense eyio mbarere na kweng’ereria.
Ⅲ Ekero baraigwe amariogi ’ebititiro bi’ebitaago bie chibarasi,
na okwoga kw’amagoro e chigari.
Abaise tibakorigia magega barore ase abana babo bare;
ekiagera amaboko abo nigo araborwe chinguru.
Ⅳ Rituko ngocha rire ekero Abafirisiti bonsi barasirigwe,
na abatigarete gokonya Turo na Sidoni basirigwe boigo.
Omonene nasirie Abafirisiti,
abare abatigari ase egesinka gia Kafitori.
Ⅴ Gasa yatigaire bosa,
Asikeloni osirire kegima.
Inwe abatigari ase rikura riria,
ngoika ririri moragenderere gwekeba?
Ⅵ Aye omoyio o birende Omonene,
ngoika ririri orabe kogendererete otari gotoerera?
Irana ime ase ekegocho kiao, okire na gotimoka.
Ⅶ Naki omoyio orakore okire‐kiri na gotimoka,
ekero Omonene ayochikiire gokora emeremo?
Oyochikiire orwanie Asikeloni ne chinse chire ang’e nenyancha.”
