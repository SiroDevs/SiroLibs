Motosabere
Ⅰ Omoerio, baminto, tosabere erinde ring’ana ri’Omonene rirande na gotogigwa buna riakorire ase egati yaino, Ⅱ ng’a totoorigwe korwa ase abanto batari abaronge, na abare ababe, ekiagera tai bonsi bare nokwegena. Ⅲ Korende Omonene nomwegenwa, nere orababeeke chinguru na kobarenda korwa ase oria omobe. Ⅳ Ase Omonene ntore nogosemeria igoro yaino, ng’a ngokora more ayatwabachiigete, naende mogenderere koyakora. Ⅴ Omonene atang’ane chinkoro chiaino, erinde momanye obwanchani bwa Nyasae, na oboremereria bwa Kristo.
Ogokuurera kw’ogokora emeremo
Ⅵ Bono baminto, twabachikiire ase erieta ri’Omonene Yeso Kristo, mweatanane korwa ase oyomino oyore omworo, oyotabwateti chingencho echio mweorokereretie korwa ase tore. Ⅶ Naki inwe abanyene momanyete buna ebagwenerete motwege; titwabeete aboro ekero twarenge amo nainwe, Ⅷ gose titwariete endagera ya monto onde bwensi bosa, totaakaneti, korende nase obokong’u amo nogwechanda togakora emeremo obotuko na omobaso, erinde titoraarereria monto onde oino. Ⅸ Tari ng’a eyio tiyatotungetie, korende igo twekorete abanyene koba ekiorokererio ekiya ase more, erinde motwege. Ⅹ Ekiagera nonya nekero keria twarenge amo nainwe ntwabachiigete ring’ana eri, ng’a onye omonto tarigeti gokora emeremo, taegwa endagera. Ⅺ Naki twaigure ng’a mbare abanto ase more bare aboro, batari gokora emeremo, korende nigo bakona‐gotuka amang’ana y’abaanto bande. Ⅻ Nabo abanto banga buna abwo twabachikiire na kobasorora ase Omonene Yeso Kristo, ng’a bakore emeremo ase obotoereru, na koria endagera yabo abanyene. ⅩⅢ Korende baminto, timorosa gokora amaya.
ⅩⅣ Onye monto onde bwensi okwanga koigwera ring’ana eri tokwanete ase riruube eri, erusananie korwa ase omonto oyio, na timosanga nere; tiga arore obosooku. ⅩⅤ Timomobara buna omobisa, korende momokuurere buna oyomino.
Amang’ana omoerio
ⅩⅥ Bono Omonene bw’omorembe omonyene abae omorembe ase chingaki chionsi, na ase chinchera chionsi. Omonene abe amo nainwe mwensi.
ⅩⅦ Oko nogokwania kwane inche Paulo ase okoboko kwane omonyene. Eke nakio ekemanyerio ase kera riruube riane; naboigo nkoriika. ⅩⅧ Obuya bw’Omonene oito Yeso Kristo bobe amo nainwe mwensi.
