ⅩⅫ
Amachiiko igoro ase ogoakana
Ⅰ Omonto karaibe eng’ombe gose eng’ondi na koyenyenya, gose ayeoonie, nigo araakane chiombe isano ribaga ri’eng’ombe eyemo, ne ching’ondi inye ribaga ri’eng’ondi eyemo. Ⅱ-Ⅳ Igo emogwenerete aakane chionsi. Onye tabwati gento, ere omonyene goika aoonigwe ase engencho y’oboibi bwaye. Onye etugo eyio aiba ekonyorekana ase amaboko aye ere moyo, aakane ara kabere, onye neng’ombe, gose etigere, gose eng’ondi.
Omoibi karanyorekane ogotagora enyomba botuko ase okoiba, nere aitwe akwe: omonto oria omoita takobarerwa oboiti obwo. Korende onye nigo amoita kobwakiire, rirorio nigo arabarerwe oboiti obwo.
Ⅴ “Omonto kararisie etugo yaye ase omogondo bw’endagera, gose oyo bw’emesabibu, na gotiga riicho riaye ririe endagera y’omonto onde, nere goika aakane endagera enoru korwa ase omogondo oye, gose eyere engiya korwa ase emesabibu yaye.
Ⅵ “Onye omonto ogosamba omorero, na omorero oyio bwesiarere gochia ase amagwa, na emesaima y’endagera ere ase omogondo eyie, omonto oria goika aakane yonsi pi.
Ⅶ “Omonto karae omoiri oye chitaabu, gose ebinto binde biaye amogachere, na birobio biibwe korwa nyomba mwaye, omoibi karanyorekane goika aakane ara kabere. Ⅷ Korende atanyorekaneti, oria omonyene enyomba arentwe ase obosio bw’ekegambero, erinde amanywe gose nere obiibete.
Ⅸ “Eriomana rinde rionsi riise gotuboka, onye igoro y’eng’ombe, gose etigere, gose eng’ondi, gose eanga, gose gento kende gionsi kerasire, na omonto oyomo aamererie ng’a nekiaye, ekiina ekio ki’abaanto abwo babere nigo keraarentwe ase obosio bw’egesasiimero. Na oria Nyasae araorokie ng’a nomomochi, goika aakane oria onde ara kabere.
Ⅹ “Omonto karae omoiri oye etigere, gose eeri, gose eng’ondi, gose etugo ende yonsi ebe sagare, na etugo eria eitwe gose eakwe, gose eirwe monto tarora, Ⅺ omoiri oria goika atenene bosio bw’ekegambero atiane ase Omonene ng’a takuneti enibo aetwe sagare. Omonyene etugo eria goika anche ng’a ayio atianwa namaene, na tagoakanwa. Ⅻ Korende onye nigo yaibwa korwa asare ere goika aakane omonyene oria. ⅩⅢ Onye yariegwa nenyang’au, arente chinyama chintigari chibe kirori, taakana ase eye enyang’au yaitete.
ⅩⅣ “Omonto karasabe na koegwa etugo sagare korwa ase omoiri oye, na etugo eyio keraakwe gose ekwe, na omonyene tayerochi, omonto oria goika ayeakane. ⅩⅤ Onye omonyene abao ekero egoakwa gose egokwa, rirorio tagoakanwa. Onye etugo eyio yarenge eyogoakanerwa rirerio, ogoakanerwa okwo kobe kogoisanera ogosirerwa okwo.
Amachiiko y’ogotanga obonyaka na obobe bonde
ⅩⅥ 22:16: Okoir 22:28,29“Omonto karamoege na gosaria omoiseke omwekungi otararierwa boko, goika aakane enibo eraisane erio amonyuome. ⅩⅦ Korende onye ise omoiseke okwanga, tamomoa, omonto oyio goika aakane chitaabu chireng’aine rigori ri’omoiseke omwekungi.
ⅩⅧ 22:18: Okoir 18:10,11“Totiga omokungu omoorogi abe moyo.
ⅩⅨ 22:19: Abal 18:23; 20:15,16; Okoir 27:21“Omonto oraraare neng’iti goika aitwe akwe.
ⅩⅩ 22:20: Okoir 17:2-7“Omonto oraruere enyasae ende yonsi ekeng’wanso, otatiga Omonene bweka, goika asirigwe kegima.
ⅩⅪ 22:21: Okong’ 23:9; Abal 19:33,34; Okoir 24:17,18; 27:19“Tobaisa gokorera omomenyi bobe, gose komochegia, ekiagera nainwe abanyene mwarenge abamenyi ase ense ya Misiri. ⅩⅫ Tochanda omoboraka onde bwensi, gose omwana entakana oyo abaibori baye bakuure. ⅩⅩⅢ Oise kobachanda abwo, na barabwo kobaraarere ase ’nde, amaene, inche ninyigwe ekerero kiabo. ⅩⅩⅣ Endamwamu yane inayoke, na inche mbaite n’omoro, na abakungu baino nababe ababoraka, na abana baino babe chintakaana batari nabaise.
ⅩⅩⅤ 22:25: Abal 25:35-38; Okoir 15:7-11; 23:19,20“Oise komoa singo omonto onde one omotaka ore ase egati yaino, tobaisa gokora buna abwo bakoa abanto singo na koboria ebitoki ebinge; tobaisa komoboria ebitoki.
ⅩⅩⅥ 22:26: Okoir 24:10-13“Onye kogosaba omonto onde egetambaa kiaye akwambara kebe intenenera, goika omokeiranerie erioba ritaragwa, ⅩⅩⅦ ekiagera nakio gioka abwate akwebisia. Eke nakio abwate gekomobekera riberera ase omobere oye. Inee! Otakemoeti, inki rende araraarere? Ere karaarere ase ’nde, ekeene, nimoigwe, ekiagera inche nomonyamaabera.
ⅩⅩⅧ 22:28: Ogok 23:5“Tobaisa korama Nyasae, gose koragereria omorai bw’abaanto bao.”
Ogotongora, na omwana omotangi, ne ching’iti chintangi
ⅩⅩⅨ “Totebana kondetera ogotongora kwao kwe rigesa ri’endagera na eri’emesabibu. Ong’e omwana oo omomura omotangi ase abana bao, abe oyone. ⅩⅩⅩ Ong’e boigo chimori chintangi chi’echiombe, ne chimaanwa chintangi chi’eching’ondi. Ochitige chikoore amatuko atano na abere, ko rituko ria gatano na gatato ochindetere.
ⅩⅩⅪ 22:31: Abal 17:15“Mobe abanto baane abachenu; timobaisa koria eng’iti ende yonsi eyaitirwe nenyang’au ase orosana, motugutere chisese chinyama echio.”
