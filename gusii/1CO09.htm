Omotomwa ore omogwekano omuya ase ogotarigia ebimotungetie
Ⅰ Inee! Inche tindi omosibore? Inche tindi omotomwa? Tinaroche Yeso, Omonene oito? Inwe timori okwama kw’emeremo yane ase Omonene? Ⅱ Naki onye inche tindi omotomwa ase abande, korende nomotomwa inde ase more inwe. Inwe nigo more ekemanyererio ki’obotomwa bwane ase Omonene.
Ⅲ Oko nakwo okoiraneria kwane ase baria bakomboria. Ⅳ Inee! Titori ne ribaga ri’okoria na konywa? Ⅴ Titori ne ribaga koira amo naintwe omokungu ore oyominto ase okwegena buna abatomwa bande, na abamwabo Omonene, na Kefa bakorete? Ⅵ Gose ninche bweka na Barnaba totari ne ribaga ri’ogotiga emeremo ya kera rituko? Ⅶ Morwani ki okogenda ase esegi kero kende gionsi na kweakanera ere omonyene? Moremi ki otakoria amasabibu ’omogondo oye bw’emesabibu? Gose ning’o okorisia riicho, otakonywa amabeere aye?
Ⅷ Inee! Nigo ngokwana aya buna Mwanyabaanto? Gose amachiiko tari goteeba boigo? Ⅸ 9:9: Okoir 25:4; 1 Tim 5:18Naki ase amachiiko a Musa nigo ariikire, “Tobaisa kobekera entang’ana ekeokio ekero ekoora endagera.” Inee! Nyasae narengereretie amang’ana e chintang’ana? Ⅹ Inee! Tateebete ayio ase engencho yaito yoka? Ee, nigo ariikire ase engencho yaito; ekiagera oyokorema nagwenerete korema ase ogosemeria, na ere oyokoora nigo akoora ase ogosemeria konyora ensemo yaye y’endagera. Ⅺ 9:11: Abar 15:27Onye intwe twabasimekeire ebinto bi’omoika, Inee, nering’ana rinene gatoragese ebinto biaino bi’ogokonya ase obogima bw’ense eye? Ⅻ Onye abande banyorire ensemo ebatungetie korwa ase more, Inee, intwe titokoyenyora kobua mono?
Korende titorakorera oboronge obo; korende nigo tokoremereria amang’ana onsi, titobaisa gotanga enchiri ya Kristo. ⅩⅢ 9:13: Okoir 18:1Timomanyeti ng’a baria bagokora emeremo ase Hekalu nigo bakoria endagera korwa ase Hekalu, na baria bagokorera ase egesasiimero mbare nensemo yabo ase ebinto bikoruegwa ebing’wanso? ⅩⅣ 9:14: Mat 10:10; Luk 10:7Na Omonene naboigo ochikiire ng’a baria bakorandia enchiri banyore ebibagwenerete korwa ase enchiri.
ⅩⅤ Korende tindakorera amang’ana aya nonya ndimo. Gose tinyarigeti aya erinde abe boigo ase ’nde; naki mbuya ninkwa kobua omonto onde bwensi kong’ura ogwetogia kwane. ⅩⅥ Naki onye nkorandia enchiri, tindi neri ingwetogeria, ekiagera okobetererigwa kwabekirwe ase ’nde. Obobe nobwane ntarandeti enchiri! ⅩⅦ Naki onye ngokora emeremo eye ase ogwancha kwane, ninde neng’eria; korende onye tari ase ogwancha kwane, nonya nabo negenirwe noborendi. ⅩⅧ Gaki eng’eria yane ninki? Neye, ng’a ndandie enchiri ya Kristo ntari konyora ebintungetie, na tinsaba ebiaboronge biane ase okorandia enchiri.
Omokia o Paulo bw’okorenta abanto ase Kristo
ⅩⅨ Naki nonya inde nobosibore korwa ase abanto bonsi, nekorire omosomba o bonsi, erinde ndete abanto abange kobua. ⅩⅩ Ase Abayahudi nkaba buna Omoyahudi, erinde mbarente Abayahudi; ase baria bare inse y’amachiiko nkaba buna oyore inse y’amachiiko (nonya inche omonyene tindi inse y’amachiiko) erinde mbarente baria bare inse y’amachiiko. ⅩⅪ Ase baria batari namachiiko nkaba buna oyo otari namachiiko (tari buna tindi namachiiko ase obosio bwa Nyasae, korende nigo inde inse y’amachiiko a Kristo) erinde mbarente baria batari namachiiko. ⅩⅫ Ase abare abareu nkaba omoreu, erinde mbarente abareu. Igo nkaba n’echingencho chionsi ase abanto bonsi, erinde inyare kobatooria abanto ase chinchera chionsi. ⅩⅩⅢ Na inche nigo ngokora aya onsi ase engencho y’enchiri, erinde inyare koba oyomo obo bw’abakonyora ogosesenigwa kwaye.
Omokia ogwenerete ase obobui
ⅩⅩⅣ Timomanyeti ng’a abwo bakoamererania ase okominyoka chimbero, nigo bakominyoka bonsi, korende noyomo bweka okonyora eng’eria? Minyoka boigo, erinde moyenyore. ⅩⅩⅤ Na kera oyokoamererania ase chigosori nigo agwetanga ase onsi. Na abwo nigo bagokora bo, erinde banyore egutwa egosareka, korende intwe tonyore eye etagosareka. ⅩⅩⅥ Nonya ninche nkominyoka nde chimbero ntari gotiting’ara; naboigo inkorwanera chinkundi, tari buna oyokorwana nembeo. ⅩⅩⅦ Korende nigo ngochanda omobere one na koyokoria buna omosomba, erinde konarandeirie abande, inche timba omonto ogocha kwangwa.
