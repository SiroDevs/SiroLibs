Okorora kwa Danieli igoro y’emingichi na egoree
Ⅰ Ase omwaka o gatato bw’okogamba kwa Belisasari omorwoti, inche Danieli nkarora okorora konde magega ya koria kw’eritang’ani. Ⅱ Ase okorora kwane ’nkarora ng’a nigo narenge ase eburi y’omochie o Susa, agwo ase orogongo rwa Elamu. Ekero narengeo ase okorora kwane, nkarora inde ang’e n’orooche rwa Ulai. Ⅲ Nkarigereria nkarora emingichi yabwate ching’unchara ibere eteneine ase engegu y’orooche. Ching’unchara chionsi ibere nigo chiarenge chintambe, korende oromo nigo rwarenge orotambe kobua oronde, na orwo orotambe nigo rwamerete korwa magega y’orwo ronde. Ⅳ Nkarora emingichi eria egotwoma gochia ensemo ya bosongo, na eya sugusu, na eya Irianyi; eng’iti ende tiyarenge konyara gotenena ase obosio bwaye, gose onde tarenge konyara gotooria korwa ase okobua kwaye. Nigo yarenge gokora buna yanchete, naende ekaba egokora amang’ana amanene.
Ⅴ Ekero narenge ndengereretie amang’ana ayio, nkarora egoree egocha korwa bosongo, egaeta ase ense engima etari gotacha inse. Nigo yabwate orogunchara rw’ogokumia gati‐gati y’amaiso aye. Ⅵ Egachiera emingichi eria yabwate ching’unchara ibere, eria narooche eteneine ase engegu y’orooche, ekarindokera emingichi eria ere nekeririanda ekenene. Ⅶ Nkarora egosuka gochia ang’e ase emingichi eria; egatwoma emingichi eria, ere nekeririanda ekenene ekayeiyeria inse, ekabuna ching’unchara chiaye ibere. Emingichi eria tiyarenge ne chinguru chi’okorwana negoree eria, korende ekaiyerigwa inse na gotiringwa negoree eria. Onde tarenge konyara gotooria emingichi eria korwa ase okobua kw’egoree eria.
Ⅷ Erio egoree eria egakora amang’ana amanene mono. Ekero yabete ne chinguru chinyinge, orogunchara rw’aye oronene rokabuneka, na ase ribaga riaye ching’unchara inye chi’ogokumia chikamerao chiarochie gochia ase chinsemo inye chia igoro. Ⅸ Korwa ase oromo rw’echio orogunchara ronde rokamera korwao; orwo rogakiina mono gochia Irianyi, na gochia moocha, na gochia ase ense eria engiya mono. Ⅹ 8:10: Okom 12:4Orogunchara orwo rogakiina mono, rogaika ase emeganda ya igoro, rokaiyeria emeganda ende ne ching’enang’eni gocha inse aa, rogachitiringerao. Ⅺ Ee, orogunchara roria rogekinia na gwetogia igoro y’Omogaambi bw’emeganda eria, rokarusia korwa ase Omogambi oria ekeng’wanso ki’ogosambwa gia kera rituko, rogatagora Obomenyo bwaye Obochenu. Ⅻ Omoganda oria amo nekeng’wanso ki’ogosambwa gia kera rituko bikaruegwa na kobekwa ase okobua kw’orogunchara roria ase engencho y’ogosaria. Ekeene kegatugutwa inse, na orogunchara roria rokagenderera koba nokobua.
ⅩⅢ Erio nkaigwa omochenu oyomo ogokwana, na omochenu onde akaboria oria orenge gokwana, “Nengaki eng’ana naki okorora okwo igoro y’ekeng’wanso ki’ogosambwa gwa kera rituko gokworokia? Nengaki eng’ana naki ogosaria kw’ogosiria korabe, na okoruegwa kw’Obomenyo Obochenu na okw’omoganda oria biratiringerwe inse korabe?”
ⅩⅣ Omochenu oria bw’eritang’ani akang’iraneria, “Nase engaki ye chimogoroba ne chimambia chilifu ibere, na amagana atato (2,300); magega y’ayio Obomenyo Obochenu mbochenwe.”
Omomalaika Gabirieli okoorokia engencho y’okorora koria
ⅩⅤ Ekero inche Danieli narooche okorora koria na nkarigia imanye engencho yaye, ’nkarora oyomo ogwekaine Mwanyabaanto oteneine ase obosio bwane. ⅩⅥ 8:16: Luk 1:19,26Nkaigwa eriogi ri’omonto rikorangeria korwa gati‐gati y’orooche rwa Ulai, rigoteeba, “Aye Gabirieli, momanyie omonto oyio engencho y’okorora okwo!” ⅩⅦ Ase ayio agacha ang’e ase nateneine; ekero aigete ase ’nde nkaondoka, nkagwa inse maumama.
Korende omonto oria aganteebia, “Aye Mwanyabaanto, manya ng’a okorora koria nigo gokworokia chingaki chi’omoerio.” ⅩⅧ Ekero agendererete gokwana nainche, chitoro chinene chikambwata gontung’aime inse ng’umaime, korende agankuna, agantenenia, na inche ngatenena boronge. ⅩⅨ Nyuma y’ayio aganteebia, “Rora, ningoteebie aya agochia koba ase omoerio bw’endamwamu y’Omonene, ekiagera okorora okwo nigo gokworokia igoro y’engaki y’omoerio.
ⅩⅩ “Emingichi eria kwarooche yabwate ching’unchara ibere, abwo nabarwoti b’Abamedi na Abaayemi, ⅩⅪ na egoree eria yabwate amanya, oyio nomorwoti bw’Abagiriki, na orogunchara roria oronene rwarenge gati‐gati y’amaiso aye, oyio nomorwoti obo bw’eritang’ani. ⅩⅫ Orogunchara roria rwabunegete na aase aye ching’unchara inye chikamera aroro, engencho yaye nere eye: Amarwoti ane naoroke korwa ase egesaku ekio, korende takoba nokobua buna koria kwa roria rwe ritang’ani.
ⅩⅩⅢ “Ase engaki y’omoerio bw’amagaambi ayio, ekero abasaria barabe baikeire ekerengo kiabo ki’omoerio, omorwoti omotindi mono oichire nobong’ainereria naoroke. ⅩⅩⅣ Ere nabe nokobua okonene mono, korende tari ase chinguru chiaye ere omonyene. Narente ogosiria okonene gw’okoondokia, nabue ase kende gionsi arakore. Nasirie abanto bare nokobua amo nabaanto abachenu. ⅩⅩⅤ Ase obong’aini bwaye bw’obong’ainereria nagenderere konyora okobua ase kende gionsi arakore, na ase ebirengererio biaye ere omonyene nekinie na koenena. Naite abanto abange mobosokano batakuureiri. Naimokerere igoro koamererania n’Omogaambi bw’abagaambi. Erio nasenyentwe, korende tari nokoboko kwa Mwanyabaanto. ⅩⅩⅥ Okorora okwo igoro ye chimogoroba ne chimambia gwakwanetwe nogw’ekeene. Kogache okobise, ekiagera nigo koraire amatuko amaange komanye goikeranigwa.”
ⅩⅩⅦ Inche Danieli ’nkaborwa chinguru, ’nkaba omorwaire ase amatuko amake. Nyuma y’ayio nkaimoka gochia gokora emeremo ase omorwoti, korende ngaichanigwa ase engencho y’okorora okwo, na onde tarenge omanyete engencho yaye.
