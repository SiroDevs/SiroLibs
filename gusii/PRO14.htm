ⅩⅣ
Ⅰ Omokungu omong’aini nigo akoagaacha mwaye, korende oyore omoriri nigo agotagora mwaye na maboko aye omonyene.
Ⅱ Omonto omoirogete Omonene nigo akobwatia enchera y’oboronge, korende oyo omochaayete nigo are omokorekanu ase chiinchera chiaye.
Ⅲ Aya omoriri agokwana nigo are enyimbo y’oboenenu, korende aya abang’aini bagokwana nigo akobarenda.
Ⅳ Ase chiombe chi’okorema chitari, ebiage nigo bikoba bosa, korende ase okong’usa gwe chintang’ana, endagera enyinge nigo ekonyorekana.
Ⅴ Kirori ore omwegenwa tari gokwana oborimo, korende oy’otari omwegenwa nigo akoroka oborimo bwoka.
Ⅵ Omonto ore omochecheria narigia obong’aini takobonyora, korende chiseemi nigo chikonyorekana bwango ase omonto ore omong’aini.
Ⅶ Karue ase omonto omoriri, ekiagera tokonyora obong’aini ase aria agokwana.
Ⅷ Obong’aini bw’omonto ore n’okomanya nigo bokomokonya omanya buna chiinchera chiaye chire, korende obosooku bw’abaanto abariri nigo bokobang’aina.
Ⅸ Abanto abariri nigo bagokora echeche n’ebibe, korende abanyene oboronge nigo bagwancherana barabwo ase barabwo.
Ⅹ Enkoro enyene nigo emanyete obororo bwaye, na omonto omoao takonyaara gosanga ase omogooko bw’enkoro eyio.
Ⅺ Enyomba y’omokori amabe ’nesirigwe, korende ekegutu ki’omonyene oboronge ’nkegeenderere gosesenigwa.
Ⅻ 14:12: Emeb 16:25Enchera nere ekororekana ase omonto ng’a nendonge, korende omoerio bw’enchera eyio namakweri.
ⅩⅢ Nonya omonto ogoseka, enkoro yaye nabo eranyaare koba nomoichano, na omoerio bw’omogooko oyio nobororo.
ⅩⅣ Omonto ogure korwa ase okwegena kwaye, nabo araisaneke nechiinchera chiaye omonyene, na omonto omuya nigo agoisanekigwa n’omoyo oye.
ⅩⅤ Omonto otamanyeti amaange nigo akwegena kera ring’ana, korende omong’aini nigo agoitona aase agochia.
ⅩⅥ Omonto omong’aini nigo akwoboa na kwerenda korwa ase amabe, korende omoriri nigo akoba omoremu ase ogokora amabe.
ⅩⅦ Omonto okogechigwa bwango nigo agokora ay’oboriri, na omonto okoroberia ay’obobe nigo akogechwa.
ⅩⅧ Abatamanyeti amaange, oboriri nigo bokoba omwando obo, korende abare nokomanya nigo bakoegwa chiseemi chiaba eguutwa yabo.
ⅩⅨ Abanto ababe goika bekumbe ase amasio y’abanto abaya, na abakori amabe bekumbe ase ebiita bi’abanyene oboronge.
ⅩⅩ Omonto omotaka nigo agechire nonya nase abanto bamwabo, korende omonda nigo abwate abasani abange.
ⅩⅪ Omonto ogochaaya omonto onde nigo agokora ebibe, korende oyokororera abachaandegete amaabera ’nasesenigwe.
ⅩⅫ Inee! Abwo bakoroberia ayare amabe tibari gosira? Na abwo bakoroberia amaya, Inee, tibari konyora amaabera na ekeene?
ⅩⅩⅢ Emeremo ende yonsi egokorwa nigo ekorenta ebitoki, korende amang’ana amasa agokwanwa nigo gokorenta obotaka.
ⅩⅩⅣ Eguutwa y’abanto abang’aini n’obonda bwabo, korende oboriri bwoka nabwo okonyora kwa abanto abariri.
ⅩⅩⅤ Kirori ogokwana ekeene nigo agotooria obogima, korende oy’ogokwana oborimo nigo akorwa abande.
ⅩⅩⅥ Oyo okomoiroka Omonene nigo are n’ogosemeria gw’okorendwa bokong’u, na abana baye ’mbanyore aase bagotamera.
ⅩⅩⅦ Okomoiroka Omonene nakwo ensoko y’obogima eragere omonto eatanane korwa ase emetego y’amakweri.
ⅩⅩⅧ Obonge bw’abaanto nabwo bore esiko y’omorwoti, korende ase abanto bare abake omogambi obo nigo agosirigwa.
ⅩⅩⅨ Omonto otari kogechigwa bwango nigo abwate okomanya okonene, korende oyo okoberoroka bwango nigo agokinia oboriri bwaye.
ⅩⅩⅩ Enkoro y’omogooko nigo ere obogima bw’omobere, korende ogosanera nigo kore oboguundo bw’amauga.
ⅩⅩⅪ Omonto ogokorera omotaka bobe nigo agochaaya Omotongi bw’omotaka oyio, korende oyore omwororo ase oria oremeire nigo asigete Omotongi oye.
ⅩⅩⅫ Omokori amabe nigo agotugutwa riarua ase engencho y’ogokora kwaye okobe, korende omonyene oboronge nigo agokwa ore n’ogosemeria.
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Obong’aini nigo bomenyete ase enkoro y’omonto ore n’okomanya, korende ase enkoro y’omoriri tibomanyekaneti nonya ’ng’ake.
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Oboronge nigo bogokinia egesaku, korende ebibe nobosooku ase abanto bande bonsi.
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Omosomba ogokora emeremo n’obong’aini nigo agwancherwa n’omorwoti, korende endamwamu y’omorwoti nigo ekoba igoro ase oria okomorentera obosooku.
