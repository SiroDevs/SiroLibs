Ⅰ Onde bwensi bwanchete amorokererio yokomoorongeyia, oyio nigo anchete chiseemi, korende oyo ogechete ogokuurerwa n’omoriri.
Ⅱ Omonene nigo akomonyoorera monto omuya ogwancherwa, korende omonachera ekiina oyogokora emeroberio y’obobe.
Ⅲ Ogotamoiroka Nyasae tigokoagaacha omonto na komokong’ia, korende omori bw’omonyaboronge tokonyegerigwa.
Ⅳ Omokungu omuya nigo are eguutwa y’omosaacha oye, korende omokungu okorenta obosooku nigo are buna oboguundo ase amauga y’omosaacha oye.
Ⅴ Ebirengererio bi’omonyene oboronge nigo bire ebi’ekeene, korende ogoseemia kw’abakori amabe nigo kore okw’obong’ainereria.
Ⅵ Amang’ana yabakori amabe nigo akoboera na goitera amanyinga, korende aria abanyene oboronge bagokwana nigo agotooria abanto.
Ⅶ Abakori amabe nigo bakoiyerigwa basira kegima, korende enyomba y’omonyene oboronge ’nemenye na gotenena.
Ⅷ Omonto nigo agotogigwa buna obong’aini bwaye bore, korende oyore nebirengererio ebikorekanu nigo agochaaywa.
Ⅸ Omonto omoitongo ogwekorera emeremo yaye omonyene nigo are omuya kobua omonto ogwekora koba omonene, korende tabwati endagera.
Ⅹ Omonyene oboronge nigo agochaandekera nonya n’obogima bwe chitugo chiaye, korende oboseera bw’enkoro y’omokori amabe nigo bore ase ogotogonya.
Ⅺ Omonto ogokora emeremo ase omogondo oye ’natware ebinge akoria, korende oyo ogotunyana amang’ana atagwenereti, ere ’mbosa.
Ⅻ Omokori amabe nigo akogania ebionga bi’abaanto ababe, korende omori bw’omonyene oboronge nigo ogoseboka bwama.
ⅩⅢ Omonto omobe nigo agotegwa n’ogosaria gokorwa ase ebikoba biaye, korende omonyene oboronge natooreke korwa ase emechando.
ⅩⅣ Okwama kw’amang’ana y’omonto agokwana nigo gokomorentera amaya akomoisanekia, na emeremo y’amaboko ’omonto nigo ekomoiranera.
ⅩⅤ Enchera y’omonto omoriri nigo ekororekana ase amaiso aye ng’a nendonge, korende omonto omong’aini nigo akoigwera ogoseemigwa.
ⅩⅥ Okobeea kw’omonto omoriri nigo gokomanyekana rimo rioka, korende omonto omong’aini nigo akoombia otuba obobe.
ⅩⅦ Omonto bwanchete gokwana ekeene nigo akoorokia oboronge, korende kirori bw’oborimo nigo agokwana obong’ainereria.
ⅩⅧ Amang’ana omonto otari korengereria agokwana, nigo anga buna omoro ogokeba, korende oromeme rw’omonto omong’aini nigo rokorenta amang’ana akogwenia.
ⅩⅨ Ebikoba bigokwana ekeene ’mbigeenderere kobao botambe, korende oromeme rogokwana oborimo nigo rokobao ase engaki enke yoka.
ⅩⅩ Obong’ainereria nigo bore ase chinkoro chi’abwo bakoroberia amabe, korende abwo bakoroberia ay’omorembe nigo bakogera omogooko oba.
ⅩⅪ Amabe takobanyora abanyene oboronge, korende abakori amabe ’mbanyorwe n’amabe amaange.
ⅩⅫ Ebikoba bigokwana oborimo nigo bire okogechia ase Omonene, korende abare abegenwa ase ogokora kwabo nigo bakomogokia.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omonto omong’aini nigo akobisa ay’amanyete, korende abariri nigo bakoraria oboriri korwa ase chinkoro chiabo.
ⅩⅩⅣ Okoboko kw’omonto omonyamokia ’nkogambe, korende oyore omworo nabetererigwe akore emeremo y’obosomba.
ⅩⅩⅤ Ogoichana kore ase enkoro y’omonto nigo gokomoiyeria, korende ring’ana rigiya nigo rikomorentera omogooko.
ⅩⅩⅥ Omonyene oboronge nigo are omoraai omuya bw’omogisangio oye, korende chiinchera chi’abakori amabe nigo chikobatang’ana chiabasiria.
ⅩⅩⅦ Omonto omworo tari koiyeka nonya n’eki arusirie ase orotwari rw’aye, korende omonyamokia nigo akoyerioseria enibo yaye rigori rekerengo ekenene.
ⅩⅩⅧ Ase enchera y’oboronge obogima ’mboreo, na amakweri tari gotooka ase boeto obwo.
