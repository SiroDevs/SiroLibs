Okobwatana kw’ogotianania gokobekwa ekemanyererio
Ⅰ Eri’Omonene agateebia Musa, “Tiira gocha aa ase ’nde, aye amo na Aroni, na Nadabu, na Abihu, na abagaaka b’Abaisraeli emerongo etano na ebere. Mobeere aare ake, motugame inse monsasiime. Ⅱ Naye Musa bweka orachiche ong’ike ang’e, korende abwo bande tibang’ika ang’e. Boigo n’Abaisraeli barabwo tibachicha amo naye nonya ng’ake.”
Ⅲ Musa akagenda agateebia abanto amang’ana onsi ay’Omonene akwanete, na amachiiko aye onsi. Abanto bonsi bakamoiraneria ase eriogi erimo, bakaboora, “Amang’ana ayio onsi Omonene akwanire inatoyakore.” Ⅳ Erio Musa akariika amachiiko onsi ay’Omonene omoete. Erio akaimoka maambia chuni, akaagacha egesasiimero inse ye getunwa, agatenenia amagena ikomi na abere koreng’ana nebisaku ikomi na bibere bi’Abaisraeli. Ⅴ Agatoma abamura b’Abaisraeli bakarua ebing’wanso bi’ogosambwa, bakanyenyera Omonene chieri chi’ekeng’wanso ki’omorembe. Ⅵ Musa akabogoria ekeng’ese ki’amanyinga aya, akayabeeka egetaabo ime, na ekeng’ese kende akanyororokeria egesasiimero. Ⅶ Erio akaimokia egetabu gi’okobwatana kw’ogotianania agwo ase amariko a Omonene ariikire agasomera abanto bakaigwa, erio Barabwo bakaboora, “Natoigwere na gokora onsi ay’Omonene akwanire.”
Ⅷ 24:8: a Mat 26:28; Mar 14:24; Luk 22:20; 1 Abakr 11:25; Abaibr 10:29; b Abaibr 9:19,20Musa akaimokia amanyinga aria arenge egetaabo ime, akanyororokeria abanto, akabora, “Aya n’amanyinga okobwatana okw’Omonene akorire ase ogotianania nainwe ase okobaa amachiiko aya onsi.”
Ⅸ Erio Musa, na Aroni, na Nadabu, na Abihu, na abagaaka baria emerongo etano na ebere b’Abaisraeli bagatiira gochia egetunwa igoro, Ⅹ bakarora Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli. Inse y’amagoro aye nigo arenge buna amagena e rigori rinene akorokwa safiro, are nobokenonoku buna obore igoro. Ⅺ Omonene tarambora okoboko kwaye kobakorera bobe abagaaka baria b’Abaisraeli. Barabwo kobarorire Nyasae, bakaria na konywa.
Musa ase egetunwa gia Sinai
Ⅻ Omonene agateebia Musa, “Tiira gocha aa ase ’nde egetunwa igoro, oganye aa, na inche nkoe chibao chi’amagena chire namachiiko onsi aya narikireo, ayio ogochia kworokereria abanto.” ⅩⅢ Musa akaimoka amo nomosomba oye Yoshua, erio ere agatiira gochia egetunwa kia Nyasae. ⅩⅣ Agateebia abagaaka baria, “Motoganye aiga goika ekero torairane ase more. Rora Aroni na Huri nao bare aiga amo nainwe; onye omonto ore nobokong’u bonde, agende ababorie.”
ⅩⅤ Erio Musa agatiira gochia egetunwa igoro, ne riire rikagetuba. ⅩⅥ Obonene bw’Omonene bogaacha bokabeera igoro y’egetunwa gia Sinai, ne riire rikagetuba ase engaki y’amatuko atano na rimo. Rituko ria gatano na kabere Omonene akarangeria Musa korwa riire gati. ⅩⅦ Ase Abaisraeli obonene bw’Omonene bokarorekana buna omorero ogosiria, ase egetunwa igoro. ⅩⅧ 24:18: Okoir 9:9Musa agasoa riire ime, agatiira gochia egetunwa igoro, akabeera aroro ase engaki y’amatuko emerongo ene, omobaso na obotuko.
