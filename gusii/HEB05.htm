Ⅰ Naki kera Omokuani Omonene ore gochorwa korwa ase Mwanyabaanto nigo are kobekwa ase engencho ya Mwanyabaanto, kobatenenera ase ogokorera Nyasae, arue ebiegwa na ebing’wanso ase engencho y’ebibe. Ⅱ Na ere oranyare kobabogoreria ase obwororo abwo batamanyeti gento na batantanire, ekiagera nere omonyene nigo abuire noboreu. Ⅲ 5:3: Abal 9:7Ase engencho eyio yamogwenerete arue ekeng’wanso ase engencho y’ebibe biaye ere omonyene, buna are korua ase engencho y’ebibe bi’abaanto. Ⅳ 5:4: Okong’ 28:1Monto onde tari bwenyoreire amasikani ayio ere omonyene, korende abe orangerigwe na Nyasae, buna Aroni arangeretigwe.
Ⅴ 5:5: Zab 2:7Naboigo na Kristo, ere teimokereretie igoro ere omonyene koba Omokuani Omonene, korende nigo abegetwe nere oyomotebetie,
“Aye naye Omwana one,
reero nakoiboire.”
Ⅵ 5:6: Zab 110:4Naboigo akwanete aase aande,
“Aye n’Omokuani o kare na kare,
koreng’ana buna Melkizedeki.”
Ⅶ 5:7: Mat 26:36-46; Mar 14:32-42; Luk 22:39-46Ase amatuko ayio, ekero arenge ase omobere o Mwanyabaanto, Yeso agasaba na gosorora amo nokorera okonene, na amariga, asere oyorenge nokobua gw’okomotooria korwa ase amakweri, na akaigugwa ekiagera asigete Nyasae. Ⅷ Nonya orenge Omwana akeorokereria okoigwera ase emechando eyio yamonyorete, Ⅸ na gaikeranigwe akaba engencho y’ogotooreka gwa kare na kare ase abanto bonsi abamoigwerete, Ⅹ bwatoirwe na Nyasae ng’a n’Omokuani Omonene, koreng’ana buna Melkizedeki.
Ogokuurera kw’ogoteatanana korwa ase Okwegena
Ⅺ Igoro yaye ntore namang’ana amange tware gokwana korende naakong’u koyorokereria, ekiagera mwabeire aboro ase okoigwa. Ⅻ 5:12: 1 Abakr 3:2Naki ekeene, inwe anga mwabeire aborokia, ekiagera ebiro ebinge biaetire, korende nebagwenerete oyonde aborokererie amorokererio e ritang’ani ay’amang’ana a Nyasae. Inwe nebagwenerete moegwe amabeere, gose tari endagera enkong’u. ⅩⅢ Naki oyokonyora amabeere oka tamanyeti mono ring’ana ri’oboronge, ekiagera nomwana omoke. ⅩⅣ Korende endagera enkong’u ney’abaanto abanene, ekiagera barabwo mbanaretie ebirengererio biabo, na igo mbamanye ogwatanana amaya na amabe.
