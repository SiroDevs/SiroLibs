Oboitongo n’obonee bwa Kristo
Ⅰ Obogima bwaino ime ya Kristo nigo bokobaa chinguru, n’obwanchani bwaye bwabaremia. Okobwatana kw’Omoika, karabe amaabera ande onsi na amabeebe, Ⅱ ikerania omogooko one ase okoba nokorengereria okwo okwo na obwanchani obomo; mobe nomoyo oyomo, na okorengereria okomo. Ⅲ Timokora ing’ana rinde rionsi ase okwegwenera na ase okweenenia, korende ase oboitongo kera omonto abare abande ng’a nabaya kobua ere omonyene. Ⅳ Kera omonto tarigereria ebinto biaye omonyene, korende arigererie ebinto bi’abaanto bande. Ⅴ Naki mobe nebirengererio ebio ebio ime yaino buna ebio biarenge ime ya Kristo Yeso. Ⅵ Ere omochakano nigo arenge oyomo na Nyasae, na tabarete ng’a okoba koreng’ana buna Nyasae negento gi’okobwaterera, Ⅶ korende agekora buna tarenge nobonene, akaimokia ekieni ki’omosomba, akaba omogwekano o Mwanyabaanto. Ⅷ Naende agachia kororekana ore buna Mwanyabaanto omonyene, agekeyia, akaba omoigweri goika ogokwa, na korokwo nogokwa igoro ase omosalaba. Ⅸ Ase ayio Nyasae akamoimokereria igoro mono, akamoa erieta erio ribuete amarieta onsi, Ⅹ 2:10: Isaya 45:23erinde ase erieta ria Yeso kera riiru riringwe, ri’abwo bare igoro, na ase ense, na inse y’ense, Ⅺ na kera oromeme narobe kirori ng’a Yeso Kristo n’Omonene, ase obonene bwa Nyasae Tata.
Okobara buna Omebaso ase Ense
Ⅻ Ase ayio, abanchwa baane, buna moigwerete botambe, tari ekero indeo rioka, korende kobua mono ekero ntaiyo, korera ogotooreka kwaino abanyene ase okwoboa na okoiguswa, ⅩⅢ ekiagera Nyasae nere ogokora emeremo ime yaino ase okorigia, naende ase ogokora ogwancha kwaye okuya.
ⅩⅣ Mokore amang’ana onsi motari nokwemurungania na okoborania, ⅩⅤ 2:15: Okoir 32:5erinde monyare koba abatari na komocha, abachenu, abana ba Nyasae, motari na bobe egati y’ekegori eke ekebe kere bogeka. Ase egati y’abwo mobare buna emebaso ase ense. ⅩⅥ Bwata ring’ana ri’obogima, erinde inyore engencho y’ogwetogia ase rituko riria ria Kristo, ng’a tinaminyogete bosa, gose tinakorete emeremo bosa. ⅩⅦ Ee, kondaumorwe igoro y’ekeng’wanso na ogokora gw’okwegena kwaino, nagokire; ee, nagokire amo nainwe mwensi. ⅩⅧ Nainwe boigo, goka, ee, goka amo nainche.
Timotheo na Epafrodito
ⅩⅨ Korende nasemeirie ase Omonene Yeso gotoma Timotheo achiche bwango ase more, erinde inche ndemigwe, kondamanye buna more. ⅩⅩ Naki tindi na monto onde ore nomoyo oyomo nainche, oyoranyare gochandekera chingencho chiaino, ⅩⅪ ekiagera bonsi nigo bakorigia ebinto biabo abanyene, gose tari ebia Kristo Yeso. ⅩⅫ Korende momanyete Timotheo na ogokora kwaye, buna akorerete enchiri amo nainche. Igo are koreng’ana buna omwana okomokorera ise. ⅩⅩⅢ Ase ayio ninganetie komotoma ase obwango ekero ndamanye buna amang’ana arabe ase ’nde. ⅩⅩⅣ Na inche nasemeirie ase Omonene ng’a inche omonyene boigo ninchiche bwango.
ⅩⅩⅤ Korende nkarora ng’a ningwenerete gotoma Epafrodito oyominto, omokori emeremo amo nainche, omorwani amo nainche, oyo mwatomire gonkorera ase okoremerwa kwane. ⅩⅩⅥ Ere nigo arenge namaiga ase more mwensi, naende agaichana mono, ekiagera mwaigwete ng’a nigo arenge omorwaire. ⅩⅩⅦ Ere nomorwaire arenge ekeene, ang’e nogokwa, korende Nyasae akamororera amaabera, gose tari ere bweka, korende nonya ninche boigo, timba nomoichano igoro ase omoichano. ⅩⅩⅧ Ase ayio nakorire omokia mono komotoma, erinde ekero moramorore naende, monyare kogoka, na inche nkeeanerigwe omoichano one. ⅩⅩⅨ Momooroisie gaki ase Omonene ase omogooko bwonsi. Mosike abanto banga buna oyio, ⅩⅩⅩ ekiagera ase engencho y’emeremo ya Kristo akaba ang’e ase ogokwa, tarendete obogima bwaye ase ogoisanera ayaremete ase ogokora kwaino ase ’nde.
