Ⅲ
Ⅰ Esegi ase egati y’eamate ya Saulo na eya Daudi ekaba ekogenderera ase engaki entaambe. Daudi akagenderera komenteka chinguru chiaye, korende abanto b’eamate ya Saulo bakageenderera koba aboro goetania.
Abamura Ba Daudi
Ⅱ Aba nabwo abamura Daudi baiboreretwe agwo Heburoni: Aminoni nere orenge omwana oye omotangi, omwana bw’Ahinoamu korwa Yesireeli. Ⅲ Oyo o kabere Kileabu, omwana bw’Abigaeli, oyotigetwe omotakaanwa, orenge moka Nabali o Karimeli; na oyo o gatato Abisalomu o Maaka, mosubati o Talimai, omorwoti o Gesuri. Ⅳ Oyo o kane Adonia o Hagiti, na oyo o gatano Sefatia bw’Abitali; Ⅴ na oyo o gatano na rimo Iteramu bw’Egila, moga Daudi. Abamura aba nabwo Daudi aiborerete agwo Heburoni.
Abineri Okobwatana Na Daudi
Ⅵ Ekero esegi eyio yagendererete gati y’eamate ya Saulo na eya Daudi, Abineri akagenderera kwenyorera chinguru chinyinge ase egati y’eamate ya Saulo.
Ⅶ Saulo nabwate omokungu o sere‐sere ore korokwa Risipa, mosubati bw’Aya. Isi‐Boseti akaboria Abineri, “Nase ki mwamenyanire nomokuungu o sere‐sere o tata?” Ⅷ Erio Abineri akaba n’endamwamu enene ase engencho y’amang’ana ayio a Isi‐Boseti, akabora, “Inee! Inche nigo inde omotwe bw’esese korwa ase ense ya Yuda? Goika rituko ria reero ’ngendererete koba omwegenwa ase abanto b’eamate ya Saulo iso, na ase abamwabo, na ase abasani baye, ko tinakobeeka ase okoboko gwa Daudi, na bono aye gwansoereire na gonkora koba n’okomocha ase engencho y’omokuungu oyo. Ⅸ 3:9: 1 Sam 15:28Nyasae ang’e egesusuro ekenene onye tingoikeraneria Daudi ay’Omonene amorierete eira amokorere: Ⅹ Arusie oborwoti korwa ase enyomba ya Saulo, abobeeke ase Daudi, na Daudi abe omorwoti, agaambere Abaisraeli na Abayuda korwa Dani goika Beeri‐Seba.” Ⅺ Na Isi‐Boseti tanyara koiraneria Abineri ring’ana nonya nerimo, ekiagera amoirogete.
Ⅻ Abineri agatoma abanto ase Daudi agwo Heburoni, akabora, “Ense eye neyang’o? Kora okobwatana nainche, erio ngokoonye na nkorentere Abaisraeli bonsi babwatane naye.”
ⅩⅢ Daudi agatoma amairanerio, agateeba, “Ayio namaya, ninkore bo. Korende ninganetie onkorere egento ekemo. Kogocha aa ondetere Mikali, mosubati o Saulo. Onye togokora bo, rirorio tokonyara kondora.” ⅩⅣ 3:14: 1 Sam 18:27Erio Daudi agatoma abanto ase Isi‐Boseti, mosinto o Saulo, akabora, “Ing’iranerie Mikali mok’ane, oyio narierete boko, na ’ngaakana rigori ri’amasankwa a isiko ay’ebiimo bi’obosaacha ay’Abafilisti rigana erimo, erinde imonyuome.” ⅩⅤ Erio Isi‐Boseti agatoma abanto barusie omokungu oria korwa ase Palitieli, omosacha oye, mosinto o Laisi. ⅩⅥ Korende omosacha oye akamotunyana goika Bahurimu okona‐korera. Erio Abineri akamoteebia, “Irana!” Ere akairana.
ⅩⅦ Agwo Abineri agakwana nabagaaka b’Abaisraeli, akabora, “Korwa ase chingaki chinde chiaetire mwabeire mokogania ng’a Daudi abe omorwoti oino. ⅩⅧ Gaki bono, kora bo, ekiagera Omonene nariete eira igoro ya Daudi, agateeba: Nintoorie Abaisraeli, abanto bane, goetera ase okoboko gwa Daudi, omosomba one. Nimbarusie korwa ase amaboko y’Abafilisti, na korwa ase amaboko y’ababisa baabo bande bonsi.” ⅩⅨ Abineri agakwana n’abanto b’egesaku gia Benjamini boigo. Erio akageenda Heburoni, agateebia Daudi amang’ana ayio Abaisraeli na Ababenjamini bonsi banchete gokora ase are.
ⅩⅩ Ekero Abineri amo n’abanto bande emerongo ebere baigete Heburoni ase Daudi, ere akabakorera omoyega. ⅩⅪ Abineri agateebia Daudi, “Nkogeenda inde nsangererie Abaisraeli bonsi ase ensemo yao, aye omorwoti, omonene one, erio bakore okobwatana amo naye, erinde onyore eki kwaganetie, obagaambere bonsi pi.” Ase igo Daudi akaa Abineri ribaga, na Abineri akageenda nomorembe.
Abineri Ogoitwa
ⅩⅫ Kobachiire, Yoabu agacha korwa ase achiete gosakora amo nabasomba ba Daudi, nabarabwo mbabwate ebisakorwa ebinge. Korende Abineri konya ochiire, tarenge amo na Daudi agwo Heburoni, ekiagera Daudi konya omoeire ribaga, nere ogendire nomorembe. ⅩⅩⅢ Ekero Yoabu aigete amo nabarwani baye bonsi, ere agateebigwa ng’a Abineri, mosinto o Neri, nao arenge ase omorwoti, na omorwoti omoeire ribaga, ochiire ore nomorembe. ⅩⅩⅣ Erio Yoabu akageenda ase omorwoti, akamobooria, “Ninki eke gwakorire? Naigwa ng’a Abineri naacha aiga ase ore. Inaki gwakora kwamoa ribaga ogeenda? ⅩⅩⅤ Tomanyeti Abineri, mosinto o Neri? Ere igo acha aa gokong’aina, naende amanye chinsemo ase okogeenda na ebinto ebi ogokora.”
ⅩⅩⅥ Yoabu karuure ase Daudi agatoma abanto babwatie Abineri bamwoyie korwa ase engoro y’amaache ya Sira, korende Daudi tamanyete amang’ana ayio. ⅩⅩⅦ Ekero Abineri airanire Heburoni, Yoabu akamoira ensemo gati‐gati y’egeita, buna nigo arigetie gokwana nere bobisi, na agwo akamobeta enda goika agakwa. Nabo Abineri agaitwa ekiagera aitete Asaeli omwabo Yoabu. ⅩⅩⅧ Ekero Daudi aigwete amang’ana ayio, akabora, “Omonene namanyete ng’a inche na abanto bane titori na komocha ase obosio bwaye ase ogoiterwa kw’amanyinga y’Abineri, mosinto o Neri. ⅩⅩⅨ Tiga okomocha kw’ogoiterwa kw’amanyinga aye kobe igoro ya Yoabu, na igoro ase abanto b’eamate yaye: naende enyomba ya Yoabu teborwa omonto ogoiteka amaache anga buna chimengere, gose oyore noborwaire bwa ukoma, gose oyo ogwesiirera enyimbo, gose oyo oraitwe n’emioro, gose oyore omoremerwa bw’endagera.” ⅩⅩⅩ Naboigo Yoabu na Abisai omwabo baitete Abineri, ekiagera ere aitete Asaeli omwabo ase esegi eria barwanete aaria Gibeoni.
Ogotindekwa kw’Abineri
ⅩⅩⅪ Erio Daudi agachiika Yoabu amo n’abanto bonsi barenge amo nere batandore chianga chiabo na beboyie chigunia, erinde bareere Abineri. Omorwoti Daudi nere omonyene agatunyana oborere kobobogoirie. ⅩⅩⅫ Erio bagatindeka Abineri agwo Heburoni. Omorwoti akarera neriogi rinene agwo ase oboina bw’Abineri; na abanto bonsi bakarera. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Omorwoti akaba okweumia igoro y’Abineri, okobora,
“Inee! Nabo Abineri agwenerete gokwa buna omonto omoriri?
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Aye Abineri, amaboko ao tasibetwe,
gose amagoro ao tare kobeekwa chingataare.
Nigo kwagwa buna omonto ogoitwa nabakayaayu?”
Naende abanto bonsi bakagenderera komoreera.
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Abanto bonsi bagaacha koebereria Daudi arie endagera, korende Daudi agatiana, ogoteeba, “Nyasae ang’e egesusuru bono na kogenderera onye ’nkoria endagera erioba ritaragwa.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Abanto bonsi bakarora ng’a ayio nigo abagweneire buna amang’ana onsi omorwoti are gokora abagwenerete. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Ase rituko riria Abaisraeli bonsi bakamanya ng’a omorwoti tarenge na komocha ase ogoitwa kw’Abineri, mosinto o Neri. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Erio omorwoti agateebia abasomba baye, “Timomanyeti ng’a reero omonto omonene, omogambi bw’Abaisraeli, okuure? ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Nonya naagetwe amaguta ’nkaba omorwoti nachaandekire, tindi na nguru. Abamura aba b’omokuungu Seruya nabatindi, tinkobanyara. Omonene naakane egesiomba koreng’ana nobobe bwabo!”
