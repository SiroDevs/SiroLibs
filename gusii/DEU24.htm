ⅩⅩⅣ
Ogotigana na okonyuomana naende
Ⅰ 24:1: Mat 5:31; 19:7; Mar 10:4“Nenyaare koba ng’a omonto onyuomire omokungu, na magega y’ayio amwange, ekiagera anyorire ring’ana ase are ritamogogeti. Erio amoriikere riruube ri’ogotigana nere, ae omokungu oria na komoseeria korwa bwoye. Ⅱ Naende nenyaare koba ng’a omokungu oyio gatigire omosacha oria, agende anyuomwe n’omosaacha omoao, Ⅲ na oyo nere amwange, amoriikere riruube ri’ogotigana, amoseerie korwa bwoye, gose omosacha oyio o kabere akwe. Ⅳ Ase amang’ana ayio onsi, omosacha oria bw’eritang’ani omoseretie, tabaisa komoirania goocha bwoye abe mokaaye naende, ekiagera omokungu oria obeire oyotachenegeti; okwo nigo kore okogeechia ase obosio bw’Omonene, nainwe timobaisa korenta okomocha kobe ase ense eyi’Omonene Nyasae oino, agochia kobaa ebe omwando oino.
Amachiiko are ao ao
Ⅴ “Onye omonto onyuomire omokungu tageenda ase esegi amo n’abarwani bande, gose taegwa emeremo ende yonsi akorere egesaku, korende nigo araegwe ribaga abeere ’nka bwoye ase omwaka oyomo kogokia mokaaye.
Ⅵ “Onye omonto okoa omonto onde egento singo, taira orogena gose ensio bibe intenenera, ekiagera igo n’obogima bwaye akoira bobe intenenera.
Ⅶ 24:7: Okong’ 21:16“Omonto karanyorekane oibire Omoisraeli omwabo amokore buna omosomba, gose amoonie, omoibi oria goika aitwe akwe. Goika morusie obobe korwa ase more.
Ⅷ 24:8: Abal 13:1—14:54“Mwerende mono ekero mwarwarire oborwaire bwa ukoma, mokore buna abakuani Abalawi barabateebie. Ⅸ 24:9: Omob 12:10Ayio onsi inche nabachiigete ng’a babateebie, goika moyakore. Inyora ay’Omonene Nyasae oino, akorerete Miriamu ekero mwarenge ase orogeendo rwaino korwa Misiri.
Ⅹ 24:10: Okong’ 22:26,27“Ekero okoa omogisangio oo egento kende gionsi singo, tobaisa gosoa ase enyomba yaye koira egento keria abeekire kebe intenenera. Ⅺ Aye otenene isiko y’enyomba yaye, nere akorentere egento abeeka koba intenenera. Ⅻ Onye omonto oria n’omotaka, tobaisa kogeenda korara n’egento keria kiaye kere bwoo.
ⅩⅢ “Ekero bogochia koira omoiranerie egetambaa akwambara erinde ebisie, nario aragosesenie, na igo gwakorire ayaare boronge ase obosio bw’Omonene, Nyasae oo.
ⅩⅣ 24:14: Abal 19:13“Tobaisa gokorera bobe omonto omoriikwa ore omotaka na oremeire, karabe omonto omino Omoisraeli, gose omomenyi omenyete ase ense yaino. ⅩⅤ Rituko riria akorire emeremo, omoe eng’eria yaye ritang’ani risase ritaragwa, ekiagera ere n’omotaka, nigo asemeretie konyora eng’eria yaye. Onye tokomoakana nigo arabaise korera ase Omonene igoro yao, naende ayio nigo arabe ebibe ase ore.
ⅩⅥ 24:16: 2 Abar 14:6; 2 Amg 25:4; Ezek 18:20“Abaibori tibagoitwa ase engencho y’ebibe bi’abana babo, gose abana tibagoitwa ase engencho y’ebibe bi’abaibori babo; korende kera omonto nigo araitwe ase engencho y’ebibe biaye omonyene.
ⅩⅦ 24:17: Okong’ 23:9; Abal 19:33,34; Okoir 27:19“Tobaisa korina omonto omomenyi, gose entakaana, oboronge bwaye, na toira eanga y’ omoboraka ebe intenenera. ⅩⅧ Korende moinyore ing’a nainwe ’mwarenge abasomba ase ense ya Misiri, korende Omonene Nyasae oino, akababooria korwa aroro; ase ayio nabachiikire mobwatie amang’ana ayio.
ⅩⅨ 24:19: Abal 19:9,10; 23:22“Ekero mokogesa chindagera chi’emegondo yaino, na mwebe omosaima bw’endagera mogondo, timobaisa koirana gochia koywoyia, korende moyotige obe oyo bw’omonto omomenyi omenyete ase more, gose oyo bwe chintakana, n’ababoraka, erio nario Omonene Nyasae oino, arasesenie emeremo yaino yonsi mogokora. ⅩⅩ Ekero mwatorire amazeituni aino, timoirana naende gosangereria ase chinsagia aya mwamochete gotora. Moyatigere abamenyi, ne’chintakana, n’ababoraka. ⅩⅪ Ekero mwatorire amasabibu aino, timoirana naende gochia gosangereria ayatigaire, korende moyatigere abamenyi, ne’chintakana, n’ababoraka. ⅩⅫ Inyora ng’a inwe nigo mwarenge abasomba ase ense ya Misiri; ase ayio nabachiikire mobwatie amang’ana ayio.
