ⅩⅬⅥ
Ⅰ “Beli yaguure inse, na Nebo yekumbire;
emegwekano ye chinyasae chiria eberekire ne ching’iti ne chiombe;
ebinto ebio bibogoirie nigo bire chigurube chindito
ase ching’iti echio chiarosire.
Ⅱ Chinyasae echio nigo chiekumbete gochia inse amo,
tichikonyara gotooria chigurube chiria,
echio chinyene nigo chigwoswa chiairwa ase obosomba.
 
Ⅲ “Inwe ab’ororeeria rwa Yakobo,
inwe abatigari bonsi b’Abaisraeli,
inwe aba nabeire mbabogoretie korwa ekero mwarenge ase chinda chi’abanyoko,
na korwa ekero mwaagatire ase amaboko ’abanyoko,
inyigwere!
Ⅳ Ninche ndabe amo nainwe nonya ngoika engaki y’obogotu bwaino,
ekero morabe mwagotire kegima ne chimbuche chiarabire se,
nimbe nkobabogoria.
Ninche nabatongete, nimbarende,
nimbabogorie na kobatooria.
 
Ⅴ “Ning’o moranyare kondeng’ania ng’a imbe buna oyio?
Ning’o moranyare kongwekania ng’a togwekane nere?
Ⅵ Abanto nigo bakoumora chitaabu korwa emebuko yabo
na korenga chifeta nekerengero,
erio baakanera omoturi bw’etaabu,
nere oyeroisia yaba enyasae,
na abanto abwo batungama bosio bw’enyasae eyio bayesasiima.
Ⅶ Erio bayeimokia bayebeeka ase amareko abo, bayebogoria,
bayetereka aase aye, yabeera aroro;
tekonyara gotigera korwa aase agwo. Omonto naisa korera ase enyasae eria,
ero tekonyara komoigwa gose komotooria korwa ase emechando yaye.
 
Ⅷ “Inwe abasaria, inyora amang’ana ayio,
moyarengererie mono.
Ⅸ Inyora amang’ana abeete kare;
inche ninche Nyasae, onde tari;
ninche Nyasae, onde tari onga buna inche.
Ⅹ Korwa omochakano nigo naraaretie aya agochia koba;
agwo kare nigo nabaanete aya ataraba,
ngateeba: Emeroberio yane goika eikerane,
onsi aya nganetie ninyakore.
Ⅺ Nindangerie ensoti eche korwa moocha,
na omonto okorwa ense y’aare nache akore aya naroberetie.
Aya nakwanire, goika nyaikeranie,
na aya narobeirie, goika nyakore.
 
Ⅻ “Inwe abamore abanyabigoti bikong’u,
inwe abamore aare korwa ase oboronge,
inyigwera!
ⅩⅢ Narentire oboronge bwane ang’e, tibori aare,
na ogotooreka kwane tikogoteebana;
nimbeeke ogotooreka ase Sayoni;
ning’ee Abaisraeli obonene bwane.”
