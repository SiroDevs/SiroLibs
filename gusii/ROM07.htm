Okworokereria korwa ase okonyuomana
Ⅰ Timomanyeti, baminto (inche nigo ngokwana nabwo bamanyete amachiiko) ng’a amachiiko nigo akogaambera omonto ekero are moyo?
Ⅱ Inaki omokungu onyuomire igo asibire n’amachiiko ase omosacha oye, ekero ere are moyo, korende omosacha oye karakwe, rirorio osiboirwe korwa ase amachiiko aria amosibete ase omosacha oria. Ⅲ Naboigo ekero omosacha oye are moyo, aise koba nomosaacha onde nigo akorokwa omotomani; korende omosacha oye karakwe, rirorio osiboirwe korwa ase amachiiko ayio. Naisa konyuomwa nomosaacha onde, takorokwa omotomani.
Ⅳ Naboigo baminto, nainwe mwakwereire amachiiko ase enchera y’omobere o Kristo, erinde monyare koba ab’omoao, abaye ere oy’obogetigwe korwa ase abakuure, erinde tomwamere Nyasae okwama. Ⅴ Inaki ekero twarenge ime y’engencho yaito y’ebibe okogania gwaito kw’ebibe gokabooka ase engencho y’amachiiko, kogakora emeremo ase ebiimo biaito goika tokaamera amakweri okwama. Ⅵ Korende bono twasiboirwe korwa ase amachiiko, twakwereire ay’atosibete, erinde tonyare gokorera ase obogima oboyia bw’Omoika, tari inse y’amachiiko a kare ayariikire.
Amachiiko na ebibe
Ⅶ 7:7: Okong’ 20:17; Okoir 5:21Ninki rende torateebe? Amachiiko n’ebibe? Yaya, taibo! Korende tinare komanya ebibe onye amachiiko atarengeo; inaki tinare komanya okogania okobe, onye ogochika gotagoteeba, “Toba nokogania okobe.” Ⅷ Korende ebibe bikanyora ribaga ase ogochika koria, bikabokia ime ase ’nde chingencho chionsi chi’okogania okobe. Ase amachiiko atari, rirorio ebibe mbikwete. Ⅸ Inche iga narenge moyo agwo ritang’ani ekero ntarenge n’amachiiko, korende ekero ogochika koria gwachete, ebibe bikaboka, na inche ’ngakwa. Ⅹ Ogochika koria gw’okong’a obogima, gokarorekana gokondetera amakweri. Ⅺ 7:11: Omoch 3:13Inaki ebibe bikanyora ribaga ase ogochika koria, bikang’aina, na ase korokwo bikang’ita. Ⅻ Naboigo amachiiko namachenu, na ogochika nogochenu, na okwa boronge, na okuya.
ⅩⅢ Bono inee, riria erire rigiya ndiabeire amakweri ase ’nde? Yaya, taibo! Ebibe bikang’ita ase enchera y’erire rigiya, erio ebibe birorekane ng’a nebibe, erinde ase ogochika koria ebibe bimenteke koba bitari nekerengo.
Chingencho ibere chia Mwanyabaanto
ⅩⅣ Inaki twamanyire ng’a amachiiko nay’Omoika, korende inche n’omonto bw’engencho y’ebibe; nigo naonetigwe inse ase obosomba bw’ebibe. ⅩⅤ 7:15: Abagl 5:17Inaki timanyeti aya ngokora, ekiagera aya nyanchete, tindi koyakora, korende aya ngechete naro ngokora. ⅩⅥ Korende onye ngokora aya ntancheti, rirorio nanchire ng’a amachiiko n’amaya. ⅩⅦ Ase ayio, bono tari inche nkoyakora, korende n’ebibe biria bimenyete ime ase ’nde. ⅩⅧ Inaki namanyire ng’a ime ase ’nde, ase engencho y’ane y’obobe, kende kiya tikeiyo; okorigia kw’ogokora okuya nkoreo, korende tinkonyara kogokora. ⅩⅨ Inaki keria kere ekiya nyanchete, tindi kogekora, korende keria kere ekebe ntancheti, nakio ngokora. ⅩⅩ Onye ngokora keria ntancheti, bono tari inche nkogekora, korende n’ebibe biria bimenyete ime ase ’nde.
ⅩⅪ Narorire ogochika oko, ng’a kondigetie gokora ekere ekiya, ekere ekebe nigo kenyesungetie. ⅩⅫ Inaki nigo ngosasoka mono ase omoyo one ase engencho y’amachiiko a Nyasae, ⅩⅩⅢ korende ase ebiimo biane narorire ogochika konde nkoreo, gokorwana esegi n’ogochika gw’okomanya kw’ane na kogonkora koba omosakorwa bw’ogochika koria kw’ebibe kore ase ebiimo biane. ⅩⅩⅣ Obobe nobwane inche Mwanyabaanto! Ning’o oransibore korwa ase omobere oyo bw’amakweri? ⅩⅩⅤ Namoakeire Nyasae ng’a mbuya mono ase Yeso Kristo Omonene oito! Inaki inche omonyene ase okomanya kw’ane nigo ngokorera amachiiko a Nyasae, korende ase engencho y’obobe bwane nigo ngokorera amachiiko y’ebibe.
