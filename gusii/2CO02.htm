Ⅱ
Ⅰ Korende nkarengereria ase omoyo one, ng’a tingocha seino naende ase okobaichania. Ⅱ Naki onye inche nkobaichania, gaki ning’o orangokie, otatiga oria naichanirie? Ⅲ Inche nigo nabaiikerete ring’ana eri, erinde ngocha ting’ichanigwa na baria bagwenerete kongokia, naki ninsemeretie igoro yaino inwe mwensi, ng’a omogooko one noro oyoino mwensi. Ⅳ Naki ase emechando emenge na ase obororo bw’enkoro, naende ase amariga amange nigo nabarikerete, tari ng’a moichanigwe, korende monyare komanya obwanchani bwane obonge inde nabwo ase more.
Okwabera omokori amabe
Ⅴ Korende onye omonto orentire ogoichania, tang’ichaneti inche bweka, korende ase ensemo obaichanirie nainwe mwensi. Ase ogokwana igo, tinkoyakong’ia mono. Ⅵ Egesusuro eke omonto onga buna oyio aetwe nabange, nakio kiamoisanete. Ⅶ Ribaga ri’aya mbuya mwabere omonto buna oyio na komoremia, tabaisa kobugwa nogoichana, erinde erusie moyo. Ⅷ Ase ayio nabasoroire momworokie obwanchani bwaino. Ⅸ Eke nakio kiagerete nkabariikera, erinde mbateeme na komanya gose mwabeire abaigweri ase amang’ana onsi. Ⅹ Onde bwensi morabere inwe, nainche nimwabere. Ekero nabeire omonto, onye namwabeire egento igo namwabeire ase engencho yaino ase obosio bwa Kristo, Ⅺ Saitani tabaisa gotobua; naki intwe titokomocha komanya obong’ainereria bwaye.
Okogania gwa Paulo kogenda Troasi
Ⅻ 2:12: Ogok 20:1Gaki ekero naigete Teroa ase engencho y’okorandia enchiri ya Kristo, nkaigorerwa egesieri n’Omonene, ⅩⅢ korende tinanyora ogotimoka ase omoyo one, ekiagera ntanyorete oyominto Tito aroro. Erio nkabateebia ng’a batigare buya, nkaimoka nkagenda Makedonia.
Obobui goetera ase Kristo
ⅩⅣ Nyasae aakerwe ng’a mbuya mono, ere oyogotoraa ase okobua okonene botambe ase Kristo, naende oyogosokia rimisu rigiya goetera ase tore, erinde bamomanye kera aase. ⅩⅤ Naki intwe nigo tore ogotioka okuya gwa Kristo ase obosio bwa Nyasae, ase abwo bagotoorigwa, na ase abwo bagosira; ⅩⅥ ase abwo bagosira ogotioka kw’amakweri gokorenta amakweri; ase abwo bagotoorigwa ogotioka kw’obogima gokorenta obogima. Ning’o oraisane ase amang’ana aya? ⅩⅦ Naki intwe titori buna abange abagokora okoonchorana okobe ase ring’ana ria Nyasae; korende buna abanto bagokwana ase oborabu bw’omoyo, buna abanto batomirwe na Nyasae. Ase obosio bwa Nyasae, nigo togokwana, tore ime ase Kristo.
