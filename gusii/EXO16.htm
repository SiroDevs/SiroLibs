ⅩⅥ
Mana ne chinkware
Ⅰ Omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli okamanora na koimoka korwa Elimu, na ase rituko ria ikomi na gatano ri’omotienyi o kabere magega kobarure ense ya Misiri, bagaika ase erooro ya Sini, eyio ere egati y’Elimu na Sinai. Ⅱ Agwo ase erooro omoganda bwonsi bwa Abaisraeli bakemurunganeria Musa na Aroni, Ⅲ bakabateebia, “Nanga mbuya onye twakwerete ase amaboko a ’Omonene aaria ase ense ya Misiri. Agwo tware goikaransa ase chinyongo chie chinyama, tware koria endagera ende enyinge, twaigota. Korende bono mwatorentire aa ase erooro ase ogotoita intwe twensi nenchara.”
Ⅳ 16:4: Yoh 6:31Eri’Omonene agateebia Musa, “Rora, nimbatwerie endagera korwa igoro. Kera rituko abanto bagende isiko, basangererie eyekobaisana ase rituko erio. Ase enchera eyio inche mbateeme gose mbabwatie amachiiko aane. Ⅴ Korende rituko ria gatano na rimo goika basangererie na koroisia ara kabere buna bagosangereria amatuko ande.”
Ⅵ Erio Musa na Aroni bagateebia Abaisraeli bonsi, “Mogoroba reero inwe nario moramanye ng’a Omonene nere obarusetie korwa ase ense ya Misiri, Ⅶ na mambia inamorore obonene bw’Omonene, ekiagera ere oigure buna mwamwemurunganeirie. Ee, mwamwemurunganeirie Omonene, tari intwe. Intwe inki toisaine moratuemurunganerie?” Ⅷ Naende Musa agateeba, “inwe buna morore obonene bw’Omonene ekero arabae chinyama morie mogoroba, na mambia abae omogati morie, moigote ekiagera ere oigure buna mwamwemurunganeirie. Inki mokwemurunganeria ase tore? Intwe titoisaini gento. Tari intwe mwemurunganeirie, korende n’Omonene.”
Ⅸ Musa agateebia Aroni, “Bateebie omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli basuke gocha ase obosio bw’Omonene, ekiagera oigure okwemurungania kwabo.” Ⅹ Ekaba ekero Aroni arenge gokwana nomoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli, barabwo bakeonchora bakarigereria gochia ase erooro, bakarora obonene bw’Omonene bokooroka korwa ase riire. Ⅺ Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, Ⅻ “Naigure okwemurungania kw’Abaisraeli. Kwana nabarabwo, obateebie ng’a engaki ya mogoroba mbaegwe enyama barie, na mambia inabarie omogati. Erio bamanye ng’a ninche Omonene, Nyasae obo.”
ⅩⅢ Mogoroba chinkware chigaacha, chigaichora ase batoorete, na mambia yaye aase onsi aetanaine aase aria akanyorwa atoorire riimi. ⅩⅣ Na ekero riimi riakamokire, bakarora inse aria ase erooro ebinto binga buna amagoocho, bikarorekana buna obosie oborabu. ⅩⅤ 16:15: 1 Abakr 10:3Ekero Abaisraeli babiroche tibamanyete gose mbinto ki, bagachaaka koborania barabwo ase barabwo, “Mbinto ki biria?”
Musa akabateebia, “Oria nomogati oy’Omonene abaeire morie. ⅩⅥ Ring’ana erio nario Omonene achigete: Sangereria endagera eyio, kera oyomo buna agwenerete, ebimunu bibere ase kera omonto. Kera omonto asangererie buna abwate abanto bang’ana ase eema yaye.”
ⅩⅦ Abaisraeli bagakora bo, bagasangereria abande enyinge, na abande enke. ⅩⅧ 16:18: 2 Abakr 8:15Korende ekero bayerengete ase ebirengo, oyosangereretie enyinge tatigaria kende, na ere oyosangereretie enke taremeretwe gento. Kera oyomo konya osangereirie buna okoria kwaye kwang’ana. ⅩⅨ Musa akabateebia, “Monto onde tatigaria endagera ende goika mambia.” ⅩⅩ Korende tibaigwera Musa; abande bagatigaria eyende goika rituko ria kabere, na mambia yaye bakayenyora yagundire na yasoirwe namakonde, na Musa akababea mono. ⅩⅪ Ase igo kera emambia bakaba bagosangereria kera oyomo buna okoria kwaye kwang’ana; korende ekero omobaso oyesambire, yanyititoka.
ⅩⅫ Ase rituko ria gatano na rimo baba bagosangereria endagera eria ekerengo ara kabere, ase kera omonto ebimunu bine. Abagaaka bonsi b’omoganda bakagenda goteebia Musa amang’ana ayio. ⅩⅩⅢ 16:23: Okong’ 20:8-11Ere akabairaneria, “Ring’ana erio nario Omonene konya atebiire: Ankio nerituko richenu ri’ogotimoka, Esabato Enchenu yaatananirwe ase Omonene. Mosambe eki moganetie gosamba, na moiyeke eki moganetie koiyeka, na ebiratigare mobigache goika ankio.” ⅩⅩⅣ Na buna Musa abachigete bakagacha endagera eria yatigarete goika rituko ria kabere; ero tiyagunda etioke, gose tiyasogwa namakonde. ⅩⅩⅤ Musa akabateebia, “Karie eye reero, ekiagera reero n’Esabato yaatananirwe ase Omonene, ekiagera reero timokoyerora isiko ase erooro. ⅩⅩⅥ Ase amatuko atano na rimo mobe mokoyesangereria endagera eye, korende rituko ria gatano na kabere n’Esabato, endagera eyio tegotoka.”
ⅩⅩⅦ Ase rituko ria gatano na kabere abande ase abanto baria bakagenda koyesangereria, tibayenyora. ⅩⅩⅧ Omonene agateebia Musa, “Ngoika ririri morabe mokwanga ogotebereria kwane na amachiiko aane? ⅩⅩⅨ Inyora ng’a inche Omonene nabaeire Esabato. Ase rituko ria gatano na rimo nkobaa endagera y’amatuko abere. Ase ayio, ase rituko eri ria gatano na kabere mobeere kera omonto aase aye, onde tabaisa korua inka.” ⅩⅩⅩ Naboigo abanto bakaba bagotimoka ase rituko ria gatano na kabere.
ⅩⅩⅪ 16:31: Omob 11:7,8Abaisraeli bakaroka endagera eyio mana; ero nigo yare buna chinyeke chi’amaemba enkomba, chindabu, na obwansu bwaye nigo bware buna omogati ore nobooke. ⅩⅩⅫ Musa akabateebia, “Omonene otochikiire togache endagera eye inke ya Mana ase engencho ye chinderia chiaito, erinde banyare gocha korora endagera eye Omonene atoete torie ase erooro, ekero atorusetie korwa ase ense ya Misiri.” ⅩⅩⅩⅢ 16:33: Abaibr 9:4Musa agateebia Aroni, “Imokia ekeguuru, obeke ebimunu bibere bia mana imeo, oyebeke ase obosio bw’Omonene egachwe ase engencho y’ebigori bigochi‐gocha.” ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Buna Omonene achigete Musa, naboigo Aroni ayebegete bosio bw’Esanduki ere ne chibao chiriikire oborori, ekagachwao. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ 16:35: Yosh 5:12Abaisraeli bakagenderera koria mana engaki y’emiaka emerongo ene, goika bagaika aase ense abanto bamenyete; ense yarenge orobebe rw’ense ya Kaanani. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ (Ekerengo ki’ebimunu bibere ekio nensemo ya ikomi y’ekerengo gi’efa.)
