Ogotogia Obwanchani Bw’obomwabo
(Ogoteera kwa Daudi)
Ⅰ Rora buna ere buya na okogokia okonene
ekero abanto abamo bakomenya amo, baigwanaine.
Ⅱ Amang’ana ayio nigo anga buna amaguta akweakwa are amaya mono,
akonyororoka gochia ase chinderu chia Aroni,
aitekera omokoba bwe rigoti ri’omwambaro oye.
Ⅲ Naende nigo anga buna riime ria Herimoni,
rigotonya igoro y’ebitunwa bia Sayoni.
Aroro agwo Omonene ochiikire ogosesenigwa
na obogima botari nomoerio.
