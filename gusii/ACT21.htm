ⅩⅪ
Korwa Mileto goika Turo
Ⅰ Ekero twatananekire nabarabwo na komanora n’emeeri, tokagenda boronge goika Kosi, na ase rituko ria kabere togaika Rodo, na korwa aroro togaika Patara. Ⅱ Tokanyoora emeeri eyeranyaare kwamboka goika Foiniki, tokariina emeeri eria, tokagenda. Ⅲ Ekero twachia korora egesinka gekorokwa Kuporo, tokagetiga ensemo y’okobee; tokagenda goika Siiria, togaika Turo; na agwo chigurube chikarusigwa ase emeeri. Ⅳ Gatwanyorire aborokigwa, tokabeera aroro amatuko atano na abere. Na barabwo bagateebia Paulo ase okobua kw’Omoika Omochenu tatiira gochia Yerusalemu. Ⅴ Ekero amatuko aito achia koera, tokaimoka, tokagenda ase orogendo rwaito; na abwo bonsi, na abakungu babo, na abana, bagatokooba goika isiko y’omochie; togatung’ama ase engegu y’enyancha, togasaba. Ⅵ Gatwatebanirie ng’a motigare buya, intwe tokariina emeeri, na barabwo bakairana gochia sobo.
Goika Kaisaria
Ⅶ Gotwachia gokoora orogendo rwaito korwa Turo, togaika Tolemai, togakwania abaminto, togaikaransa nabarabwo rituko erimo. Ⅷ 21:8: Ogok 6:5; 8:5Mambia yaye tokaimoka tokagenda Kaisaria, togasoa enyomba ya Filipo, omorandia bw’enchiri. Nere orenge oyomo bwa baria batano na babere;*21:8 Rora Ogok 6:5 tokamenya bwoye. Ⅸ Omonto oyo narenge nabaiseke bane abekungi, baare kobana. Ⅹ 21:10: Ogok 11:28Gatwamenyire aroro amatuko atai make, omobani oyomo, erieta riaye Agabo, agatirimboka korwa Boyahudi. Ⅺ Agaacha ase tore, akabogoria orokini rwa Paulo, agesibera amagoro na amaboko, akabora: “Omoika Omochenu oteebire iga: Naboigo Abayahudi ba Yerusalemu barache gosiba omonto omonyene orokini oro, na barabwo nabamobeke ase amaboko ’abaanto b’Ebisaku.”
Ⅻ Togachia koigwa aya, intwe na abanto baria tokamosorora tabaisa gotiira gochia Yerusalemu. ⅩⅢ Erio Paulo akairaneria: “Inki eke mogokora, mokorera na kong’ita omoyo? Nigo inde ang’e, tari ase ogosibwa gwoka, korende nonya nogoitwa ase Yerusalemu ase engencho y’erieta ri’Omonene Yeso.”
ⅩⅣ Erio kangire amang’ana aito togakira togateeba: “Ogwancha kw’Omonene tiga gokoreke.”
Goika Yerusalemu
ⅩⅤ Magega ase amatuko ayio tokeebeka ang’e togatiira kogenda Yerusalemu. ⅩⅥ Abande baborokigwa ba Kaisaria bakagenda amo naintwe, bagatoira mwa Mnasoni, omonto o Kuporo, omworokigwa o kare, tokarara bwoye.
ⅩⅦ Ekero twaigete Yerusalemu abaminto bagatoroisia ase omogooko.
Omokia o Paulo bw’okobwatana n’Abakristo b’Abayahudi
ⅩⅧ Rituko rikobwatia Paulo agasoa mwa Yakobo amo naintwe, na abagaaka bonsi nao barenge. ⅩⅨ Kabakwanirie, akaborokia amang’ana onsi erimo erimo, aya Nyasae akorire ase egati y’abanto b’Ebisaku ase obosomba bwaye. ⅩⅩ Barabwo, ekero bachia koigwa, bagatogia Nyasae, bakamoteebia: “Oyominto, kwarorire ng’a Abayahudi chilifu chinyinge begenire; na barabwo bonsi nigo bare nomokia ng’a amachiiko abwatwe. ⅩⅪ Na barabwo mbateebetigwe ng’a kworokereirie Abayahudi bonsi abamenyete ase egati y’abanto b’Ebisaku ng’a batige Musa, gokobakania tibarokia abana babo, gose tibabwatia chingencho chiaito. ⅩⅫ Inki rende kegwenerete gekorwe? Ekeene, nabo baraigwe ng’a gwachire aiga. ⅩⅩⅢ 21:23: Omob 6:13-21Gaki kora ring’ana eri toragoteebie. Mbare aiga ase tore abanto baane abariete eira gete. ⅩⅩⅣ Ira abanto aba okore buna engencho y’ogwesibia ere amo nabarabwo, obaakanere chitaabu, erinde bakigite chitukia chiabo. Igo abanto bonsi banyare komanya ng’a amang’ana aria bateebetigwe igoro yao tari ay’ekeene, korende aye omonyene nigo okogenderera buya na kobwata amachiiko. ⅩⅩⅤ 21:25: Ogok 15:29Korende ase abanto b’Ebisaku abegenete, twatomire riruube buna twanacha, ng’a beatanane korwa ase ebinto biruure ebing’wanso ase emegwekano y’ogosasiimwa, na korwa ase amanyinga, na korwa ase enyama ende yonsi y’eng’iti eng’entire, na korwa ase obotomani.”
ⅩⅩⅥ Erio Paulo akaira abasacha baria, ne rituko ria kabere agakora buna engencho yare y’ogwesibia amo nabarabwo. Erio agasoa Hekalu ime, ase okoraria amang’ana ’ogwesibia na engaki y’okorua esadaka ase engencho ya kera oyomo obo.
Eburu‐buru yabeete Yerusalemu, na okobwatwa gwa Paulo
ⅩⅩⅦ Ekero amatuko aria atano na abere achia koba ang’e goikeranigwa Abayahudi abarwete Asia bakamorora ase Hekalu ime, bagaseegeta abanto bonsi, bakamobwata, ⅩⅩⅧ bagaaka eriogi, bagateeba: “Inwe abasacha ba Israeli, tokonye! Omonto oyo nere oria okworokereria abanto ase kera ensemo mamincha y’egesaku giaito, na amachiiko, na aase aiga. Kobua ase aya orentire Abagiriki ase Hekalu ime ogindirie Aase aiga Aachenu riko.” ⅩⅩⅨ 21:29: Ogok 20:4Ekiagera baroche Torofimo Omoefeso amo nere ase omochie, bagakaga ng’a Paulo namosoirie ase Hekalu ime.
ⅩⅩⅩ Omochie bwonsi ogatitira, na abanto bakaminyoka gocha amo, bakabwata Paulo, bakamokurura, bakamosokia korwa ase Hekalu. Erio ebisieri bigasiekwa. ⅩⅩⅪ Ekero bateemire komoita, amang’ana agaikera omotang’ani bw’abarwani elifu ng’a omochie omogima o Yerusalemu osarekire. ⅩⅩⅫ Erio bwango akaira abarwani na abatang’ani b’abarwani rigana, akaminyoka gochia ase bare. Na barabwo bagachia korora omotang’ani oria omonene na abarwani, bagatiga goaka Paulo. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Erio omotang’ani oria omonene agaika aroro, akamobwata, agachiika asibwe nebinyuri bibere. Akababoria: “Nere ng’o oyo, naende inki akora?” ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Abande ase omosangererekano bagaaka eriogi bagoteeba iga, na abande iga. Ekero atanyarete komanya oboronge bw’amang’ana aria ase engencho y’egeturi keria, agachiika arentwe ime ase enyomba y’abarwani. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Nabo agachia goika ase ebiriinero akabogorigwa nabarwani ase engencho y’obotindi bw’omoganda, ⅩⅩⅩⅥ ekiagera omoganda bw’abaanto omotunyanete, ogoaka egeturi, ogateba: “Morusieo oyo!”
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Na Paulo, ekero aikigwe ang’e gosoigwa ime ase enyomba y’abarwani, akaboria omotang’ani oria omonene: “Ninyore ribaga ngoteebie ring’ana?” Omotang’ani oria akabora: “Inee! Nomanyete Ekegiriki? ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Aye tori Omomisiri oria, ere reero iga oseegeta abanto baria Abaiti chilifu inye, akabatang’ana gochia ase erooro?”
ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Paulo akamoiraneria: “Inche n’Omoyahudi, omonto o Tariso, omochie o Kilikia, omobiare bw’omochie otari moke; nagosabire ong’e ribaga nkwane n’abanto aba.”
ⅩⅬ Erio ekero amoete ribaga, Paulo agatenena ase ebiriinero igoro, akabakiria nokoboko abanto baria. Kabakiririe kegima, agakwana nabarabwo ase omonwa bw’Ekeiberania, agateeba.
