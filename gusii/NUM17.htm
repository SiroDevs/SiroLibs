Enyimbo ya Aroni
Ⅰ Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, Ⅱ “Kwana n’Abaisraeli, obateebie ng’a bakoe chinyimbo chiabo, eyemo ase kera omotang’ani o kera egesaku kiabo, chinyimbo ikomi na ibere, Oriike erieta ria kera oyomo ase enyimbo yaye. Ⅲ Oriike erieta ria Aroni ase enyimbo y’egesaku kia Lawi. Kera omotang’ani bwe chiamate chia kera egesaku abe n’enyimbo eyemo. Ⅳ Erio obeke chinyimbo echio ime ase Eema y’Omosangererekano bosio bw’Esanduki eria ere ne chibao chiriikire oborori, agwo ase inche ndaumerane nainwe. Ⅴ Enyimbo y’omonto oria inche ’ndachoore neseboke. Ase enchera eyio ninkore Abaisraeli batige kwemurungania mamincha y’ane, naende ningere batige kwemurungania mamincha yaino.”
Ⅵ Musa agakwana n’Abaisraeli, akabateebia amang’ana ayio, na abaraai bonsi be chiamate chia kera egesaku bakamoa chinyimbo chiabo, chinyimbo ikomi na ibere, na enyimbo ya Aroni nigo yarenge ase egati ye chinyimbo chiria. Ⅶ Musa akaira chinyimbo chiria ime ase Eema y’Oborori, agachibekao ase obosio bw’Omonene.
Ⅷ 17:8: Abaibr 9:4Rituko ria kabere Musa agasoa ase Eema eria y’Oborori, akarora enyimbo ya Aroni, eyatenenerete egesaku kia Lawi, yasebokire, ne chinsagia echio chiasokirie amamumu na ebisicha, naende chiamire amatunda amalozi atobire. Ⅸ Erio Musa agachisokia isiko chinyimbo chiria chionsi korwa ase obosio bw’Omonene, agachirenta ase Abaisraeli bonsi. Abwo bagachirigereria, na kera omonto akabogoria enyimbo yaye. Ⅹ Omonene agateebia Musa, Irania enyimbo ya Aroni ebeere agwo bosio bw’Esanduki ere noborori, erinde egachwe ebe ekemanyererio ki’ogokuurera abanto batari koigwera. Nabo okirie okwemurungania kwabo ase ’nde, timbaisa koigwa amang’ana buna ayio, mbaise kobaita bakwe. Ⅺ Naboigo Musa akorete buna Omonene amochiigete.
Ⅻ Abaisraeli bagateebia Musa, “Rora, intwe twaikeire omoerio oito, tw akuure, twasirire intwe twensi. ⅩⅢ Kera omonto ogoika ang’e ase Obomenyo bw’Omonene, goika akwe. Inee! Ntwensi pi torakwe?”
