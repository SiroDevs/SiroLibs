Emebayeno ende ya Sulemani
Ⅰ Eye nero emebayeno ya Sulemani, eyio abanto ba Hezekia omorwoti bw’Abayuda, basangereretie.
Ⅱ Nigo bore obonene bwa Nyasae kobisa amang’ana; korende na obonene bw’abarwoti gokora obotuki ase amang’ana ayio.
Ⅲ Buna koreng’ana igoro ere aare mono etatukwe emanywe, na ayare ase ense inse boigo, ne chinkoro chi’abarwoti tichigotukwa chimanywe.
Ⅳ Rusia amabiria korwa ase efeta, na omoturi ’nanyore efeta akoroiseria egekorero ekiya. Ⅴ Rusia abakori amabe korwa ase obosio bw’omorwoti, erio ekerogo kiaye ki’obonene nigo kerageenderere gokong’igwa n’oboronge.
Ⅵ 25:6: Luk 14:8-10Tobaisa gwetang’anania ase obosio bw’omorwoti, gose tobaisa kwebeeka aase abanene bare. Ⅶ Nigo er’abe buya mono ekero orateebigwe, “Inchuo gocha aa”, kobua goseerigwa gochia goikaransa magega korwa ase ribaga ri’abaanto abasike.
Ⅷ Toba omwango kogeenda ase ekegambero. Inee! Inki orakore ase omoerio, ekero omonto oyio gwasoerete arakobue n’obe n’obosooku?
Ⅸ Onye aye omonyene kobwate amang’ana n’omonto onde, kwana nere, korende tobaisa koorokia obobisi bw’omonto omoao. Ⅹ Oise koboorokia, na omonto onde aise koigwa ayio, nigo aragosookie, na erieta ria’o risareke kogeenderera botambe.
Ⅺ Ring’ana rigokwanwa ase enchera egwenerete nigo ringa buna amatunda amororo amaya mono abekire ase egekabu keroisirie korwa ase efeta.
Ⅻ Omonto omong’aini ogokuurera oyokoigwera nigo are buna ebonyi ne chisonoi biroseirie chitaabu.
ⅩⅢ Omotomwa ore omwegenwa nigo are ase abwo bakomotoma buna amaache amakendu ase engaki ye rigesa, ekiagera nigo akogera emioyo yabo yaba emeyia.
ⅩⅣ Omonto ogwetogia ng’a nare nomoyo omorabu, ko tari korua ebiegwa, nigo anga amare na omwaga bitari korenta embura.
ⅩⅤ Oboremereria nigo bokwororobia omogambi, na oromeme rw’obwororo nigo rokobuna riuga.
ⅩⅥ Onye kwanyorire obooke, karie ekerengo kegoisaine; oise kogeetania nigo oraboroke. ⅩⅦ Amagoro ao tanara kona‐kogeenda ase enyomba y’omosani oo, nigo arabaise korosa naye, erinde akogeche.
ⅩⅧ Omonto okoba kirori bw’oborimo ase omonto onde nigo anga buna enungu y’eseegi, gose omoro, gose ensara enyonge.
ⅩⅨ Ogosemeria omonto otari omwegenwa ase engaki y’emechando, nigo enga buna erino ribunegete gose okogoro goteng’eretie.
ⅩⅩ Omonto ogoteera amatera y’omogooko ase omonto obwate enkoro ere nobororo nigo anga buna okorusia eanga ase engaki y’obokendu gose kobeeka egento ekeroro ase riote.
ⅩⅪ 25:21: Abar 12:20Onye omobisa oo ore nenchara, moe endagera arie; onye ore nerang’o, moe amaache anywe. ⅩⅫ Igo nobeke omorero bw’amakara ase omotwe oye, n’Omonene nakoe eng’eria yao.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omwaga okogusa korwa sugusu nigo okorenta embura, naboigo nomonto omogenki akorenta okobeeana.
ⅩⅩⅣ Mbuya komenya ase ekenunchi kia irongo igoro, kobua komenya ase enyomba amo n’omokungu omonyariomana.
ⅩⅩⅤ Amang’ana amaya akorentwa korwa ase ense y’aare nigo anga buna amaache amakendu ase omonto ore n’erang’o.
ⅩⅩⅥ Omonyene oboronge okorua ribaga ase omokori amabe nigo anga buna orooche rore netintira na buna ensoko yasarekire.
ⅩⅩⅦ Tari buya koria obooke obonge, na abwo bakwerigeria obonene, obonene bwabo nigo borabe bosa.
ⅩⅩⅧ Omonto otamanyeti kwegambera omonyene nigo anga buna omochie chindwaki chiaye chiatagoirwe.
