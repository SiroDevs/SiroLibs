Ekiina ki’Abakristo bosio bw’ekegambero
Ⅰ Omonto onde oino karabe ne ring’ana ase oyomwabo, Inee, nare noboremu amosoere ase abwo batari abaronge? Inki rende atari komosoera ase obosio bw’abachenu? Ⅱ Gose timomanyeti ng’a abachenu inabagaambie ense? Na onye ense egocha kogambigwa nainwe, Inee, timoisaini kogamba ebiina ebire ebike? Ⅲ Timomanyeti ng’a ntogambie abamalaika? Tari kobua mono amang’ana ’obogima obo? Ⅳ Gaki komorabe mogosoerana ase amang’ana buna ayio, naki rende mokoyarenta ase abwo batabariri buna ngento ase ekanisa? Ⅴ Nateebire aya ase okobasookia. Inee! Onde tari ase egati yaino ore ne chisemi chiisaine oranyare konacha amang’ana are ase egati y’abamwabo? Ⅵ Korende oyomwabo nigo agosoera oyomwabo, na ayio nase obosio bw’abwo bategeneti.
Ⅶ Amang’ana aya ng’a ngosoerana more inwe ase inwe nigo are ase okobugwa kwaino. Inki motanchereti gokorerwa bobe? Inki motanchereti kourwa ebi mobwate? Ⅷ Korende inwe abanyene mwakoreire abanto bobe, na kobaura ebi babwate, ee nonya nase abamino.
Ⅸ Gose timomanyeti ng’a abatari noboronge tibakoira omwando bw’oborwoti bwa Nyasae? Timong’aineka; abanyaka tibakoira omwando bw’oborwoti bwa Nyasae, nonya nabasasimi emegwekano, gose abatomani, gose abakwerua ase okogania kw’omobere, gose abasacha abagwesaria nabasaacha, Ⅹ gose abaibi, gose abegimeria, gose abagotinda, gose abarami, gose abauri. Ⅺ Abande ase more nigo mwarenge abanto buna aba, korende mogasibigwa na mogachenwa, mwanyorire oboronge ase erieta ri’Omonene Yeso Kristo, na ase Omoika o Nyasae oito.
Ebibe tibigwenereti ase obogima bw’obokristo
Ⅻ 6:12: 1 Abakr 10:23“Ninde nobosibore gokora kende gionsi”, korende tari bionsi birankonye. “Ninde nobosibore gokora kende gionsi”, korende tingwancha koba omosomba o gento kende gionsi. ⅩⅢ “Endagera ney’enda na enda ney’endagera”, korende Nyasae nasarie bionsi bibere, enda na endagera. Omobere tori ase obotomani, korende nase Omonene, n’Omonene ase omobere. ⅩⅣ Nyasae nabogetie Omonene, naintwe natobokie ase okobua kwaye. ⅩⅤ Timomanyeti ng’a emebere yaino nebiimo bia Kristo? Inee! Nimbogorie ebiimo bia Kristo na kobikora bibe ebiimo bi’omokuungu omotayayi? Yaya, nonya ng’ake! ⅩⅥ 6:16: Omoch 2:24Timomanyeti ng’a ere oyobwatanigwe nomokuungu omotayayi obeire omobere oyomo nere? Naki buna eriikire, “Baria babere nababe omobere oyomo.” ⅩⅦ Korende ere oyobwatanigwe n’Omonene igo obeire omoika oyomo nere. ⅩⅧ Tama obotomani! Kera ebibe binde bigokorwa na Mwanyabaanto nigo bire isiko y’omobere; korende ere oyogokora obotomani igo okorire ebibe ase omobere oye omonyene. ⅩⅨ 6:19: 1 Abakr 3:16; 2 Abakr 6:16Gose timomanyeti ng’a emebere yaino ne Hekalu y’Omoika Omochenu ore ime yaino, oyo mwaeirwe na Nyasae? Na inwe timori abaino abanyene, ⅩⅩ ekiagera mwagoretwe ase rigori rinene. Ase ayio, motogie Nyasae ase emebere yaino.
