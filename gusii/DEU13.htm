ⅩⅢ
Ⅰ “Nenyaare koba ng’a ase egati yaino omobani, gose omonyandoto, oratuboke na koorokia ebikone na amakumia, Ⅱ na amang’ana ayio aikeranigwe. Aise kobateebia: Togende tosasiime chinyasae ching’ao, (echio motana gosasiima agwo ritang’ani,) tochikorere, Ⅲ timobaisa koigwa amang’ana yomobani oyio gose ay’omonyandoto oyio. Omonene Nyasae oino, nigo akobateema, erinde arore gose ’momwanchete ne’chinkoro chiaino chionsi, na emioyo yaino yonsi. Ⅳ Mobwatie Omonene Nyasae oino, na momoiroke. Mobwate amachiiko aye na moigwere eriogi riaye, momokorere na komobwatia. Ⅴ Korende omobani oyio, gose omonyandoto oyio, naitwe akwe, ekiagera oyio nigo akoborokereria ng’a mobe abamotari koigwera Omonene Nyasae oino, oyio obarusetie korwa ase ense ya Misiri, na kobatooria korwa ase ense y’obosomba. Omonto oyio nigo arabasirie motige enchera eri’Omonene Nyasae oino, abachiigete kobwatia. Goika morusie obobe bwonsi korwa ase more.
Ⅵ “Boigo momura omino morwete enda eyemo, gose omwana oo, omomura gose omoiseke, gose omokungu oo bwanchete, gose omosani oo bwanchete buna bweanchete aye omonyene, nanyaare gokoebereria bobisi na kogoteebia: Togende tosasiime chinyasae ching’ao, chiria, aye otana gosasiima, gose chisokoro chiao chitarenge gosasiima. Ⅶ Oyomo obo nanyaare gokoebereria osasiime chinyasae chinde chi’ abanto abwo bagoetanaine, bamenyete aare korwa ase ore, gose ang’e, korwa ensinyo eyemo y’ense goika eyende. Ⅷ Tobaisa koebererigwa nere, gose komotegerera, gose komororera amabeebe, nonya n’okomororera amaabera gose komoegera. Ⅸ Momoite akwe. Aye obe oyo bw’eritang’ani komoaka amagena; magega y’ayio abanto bonsi bamanye komoaka amagena. Ⅹ Momoake amagena goika akwe ekiagera aganetie kobasiria ng’a motige Omonene Nyasae oino, oria obarusetie korwa ase ense ya Misiri, ense y’obosomba. Ⅺ Na Abaisraeli bonsi baigwe ayio akorekanire na bairoke, erinde obobe buna obwo tibokorwa ase egati yaino naende.
Ⅻ “Ekero momenyete ase emechie yaino, eyi’Omonene Nyasae oino abaeire, ande ’namoigwe ⅩⅢ ng’a abanto bande ababe batarochi inchera baruure ase egati yaino, bang’usire abanto b’omochie obo, na kobasiria, bagoteeba: Inchuo togende gosasiima chinyasae ching’ao konya motana gosasiima rinde. ⅩⅣ Mouna‐unie mono amang’ana ayio, erio momanye buya gose nabo are. Onye okogeechia buna okwo gwakorekanire ase egati yaino, ⅩⅤ moite abanto bonsi b’omochie oyio n’emioro*13:15oyio n’emioro: ase ogokwana konde nakwo ogokenya. Mosarie omochie oyio kegima amo n’ebinto bionsi bireo, ne’chitugo mochiite boigo. ⅩⅥ Mosangererie ebisakorwa bi’omochie oyio, mobibeeke bibe etui agwo ase eraaro aarabu, erio mosambe n’omorero omochie amo n’ebisakorwa bionsi, bibe ekeng’wanso ekegima ki’ogosambwa ase Omonene Nyasae oino. Noro obe ritombe goika kare na kare, tokoagachwa naende. ⅩⅦ Timobaisa koira gento kende gionsi keragereirie. Erio nario Omonene aratige endamwamu yaye enene, erinde abarorere amaabera. Ere nigo arabe n’amabeebe ase more, abakore momentekane, buna arierete chisokoro chiaino eira. ⅩⅧ Ayio nigo arakoreke onye mokoigwera Omonene Nyasae oino, na kobwata amachiiko aye onsi, aya inche nabaeire ase rituko ria reero, na komorakore ayaare amaronge ase obosio bw’Omonene Nyasae oino.
