Omorwani omuya o Kristo Yeso
Ⅰ Gaaki, aye omwana one, obe ne chinguru ase obuya bore ime ase Kristo Yeso. Ⅱ Na ayakwaigwete korwa ase ’nde ase amasio y’abarori abange oyabeeke ase abanto abegenwa, abaranyare kworokereria abaande boigo. Ⅲ Nonya naye oremererie emechando buna omorwani omuya o Kristo Yeso. Ⅳ Morwani onde tari, oyokogeenda ase esegi, orebeke ase amang’ana ande onsi y’ense eye, erinde amogwenere ere oyomoriigete. Ⅴ Omoamereria bwe chigosori takoegwa egutwa y’obobui ataamereraneti koreng’ana namachiiko. Ⅵ Omoremi oyobwechandeire emeremo, ere nemogwenerete abe oyo bw’eritang’ani konyora ensemo y’okwama. Ⅶ Rengereria mono ayio ngokwana, ekiagera Omonene nakoe okomanya ase amang’ana onsi.
Ⅷ Moinyore Yeso Kristo, oy’oboogete korwa ase abakuure, oyo bw’ororeria rwa Daudi, koreng’ana nenchiri eye nkorandia inche. Ⅸ Ase enchiri eyio ngachaandwa goika ngasibwa ne chingataare buna omokori amabe. Korende ring’ana ria Nyasae tirikobeegeka chingataare. Ⅹ Ase ayio naremereirie onsi ase engencho y’abachoore, erinde nabarabwo banyoore ogotooreka kore ase Kristo Yeso amo nobonene bwa kare na kare. Ⅺ Ring’ana eri neri’ekeene:
Onye twakwete amo nere, inatobe moyo amo nere boigo.
Ⅻ 2:12: Mat 10:33; Luk 12:9Onye tokoremereria, rirorio natogaambe amo nere boigo;
onye tokomokaana, nere natokaane boigo;
ⅩⅢ onye titori abegenwa, ere nigo are omwegenwa,
ekiagera takonyara gwekaana ere omonyene.
Omokori emeremo bwancheirwe
ⅩⅣ Obainyorie ayio, na kobakuurera ase obosio bwa Nyasae, tibaaba nokoamererania ase amang’ana, okwo gotanyaare gokoonya kende, korende nigo gokobasiria abagokoigwa. ⅩⅤ Kora buna oranyaare kweorokia ase obosio bwa Nyasae buna oyobwancheirwe nere, buna omokori emeremo oyotagwenereti gosookigwa, ogwatanana boronge ring’ana ri’ekeene. ⅩⅥ Bweatanane korwa ase amang’ana atari y’obonyasae, na atari nengeencho. Inaki ase ayio abanto nigo barageenderere komenteka ase chingeencho chitari chi’obonyasae. ⅩⅦ Amang’ana abo nigo akoria buna rikwege ri’esiria. Ase abwo mbareo Himenayo na Fileto. ⅩⅧ Abwo basireriigwe korwa ase ekeene, bagoteeba ng’a okobokigwa gwaikire kobao, na igo bakoonchorana okwegena kw’abaanto baande. ⅩⅨ 2:19: Omob 16:5Korende oboroso obo Nyasae abeegete bore ne chinguru, boteneine boronge, bore nekemanyererio eke: “Omonene namanyete abare abaye”, naende, “Kera oyogwatora erieta ri’Omonene, tiga eatanane korwa ase obobe.”
ⅩⅩ Ase enyomba enene tibirio ebinto bie chitaabu na ebie chifeta bioka, korende mbireo ebinto bi’emete na ebi’amaroba boigo; ebinde nebi’ogosika, na ebinde tibiri ebi’ogosika. ⅩⅪ Ase ayio, onye omonto origetie gwesibia ere omonyene ase ogweatanana korwa ase ebinto buna ebio, nabe egento ki’ogosika, giancheirwe, keragwenere Omonene, kiaroisiigwe ase kera emeremo emiya. ⅩⅫ Tama okogania okobe kw’obosae, rigia oboronge, na okwegena, na obwanchani, na omorembe, amo nabwo bakomorangeri’Omonene ase chinkoro chindabu. ⅩⅩⅢ Korende bwange amaborio y’oboriri atari aye chiseemi. Kwamanyire ng’a ayio nigo ararente eriomana. ⅩⅩⅣ Naende tegwenereti omosomba Omonene koba omonyariomana, korende abe omwororo ase abanto bonsi, abe oy’omanyete kworokereria buya, na abe omoremereria, ⅩⅩⅤ oyorabateebererie ase obwororo abwo bakoamererania nere. Aande Nyasae nabaonchore, erinde bamanye ekeene gionsi, ⅩⅩⅥ erio banyare gotaboboka na korua ase omoeto o Saitani kobasakoirwe nere goika bakore ogwancha kwaye.
