Ogosaba Gw’okonyora Oborendi
(Ogoteera kwa Daudi)
Ⅰ Aye Omonene, ’ntoorie korwa ase abanto ababe;
indende korwa ase abanto abatindi!
Ⅱ Abanto abwo nigo bakoroberia amabe ase chinkoro chiabo ime,
baba bakona‐korwana esegi botambe.
Ⅲ 140:3: Abar 3:13Chimeme chiabo nigo chire chinyoge buna echie ching’iti chingeenda inse;
obosongo bw’amarubi nigo bore ase ebikoba bi’emenwa yaabo.
 
Ⅳ Aye Omonene, indende korwa ase amaboko y’abakori amabe;
intoorie korwa ase abanto abatindi,
abwo barobeirie kobeeka ebigoocha ase ’ngoeta.
Ⅴ Abanto abaenenu babeekire oboreba bobisi ase ’ngoetera,
bantegeire ebichanga bimbwate,
babeekire emeeto ase oboeto bwane bwonsi.
 
Ⅵ Nateebirie Omonene: Aye Nyasae one,
aye Omonene, tegerera oigwe ogosaba kwane.
Ⅶ Aye Nyasae one, Omonene, Omotooria one omokong’u,
aye nigo ogotuba omotwe one ase engaki y’eseegi.
Ⅷ Omonene, tobaisa koa abakori amabe aya baganetie;
tobaisa gosesenia emeroberio yaabo emebe,
baabaise gwetogia.
 
Ⅸ Totiga ababisa abwo bang’etananire babue,
tiga obobe bw’amang’ana bagokwana bobairanere abanyene.
Ⅹ Tiga omorero bw’amakara obagwere,
barabwo batugutwe ase omorero,
barutwe ase chingoro chintambe tibaruao.
Ⅺ Totiga abagenki bagenderere koba ase ense.
Obobe nigo bogotwara abanto abatindi ase omoayerero bw’abasiria.
 
Ⅻ Nimanyete ng’a aye Omonene norwanerere abagokorerwa bobe,
noe abataka oboronge bwabo.
ⅩⅢ Ekeene, abanyene oboronge ’mbaake ng’a mbuya mono ase erieta riao,
abare ne chinkoro chindonge ’mbamenye ase obosio bwao.
