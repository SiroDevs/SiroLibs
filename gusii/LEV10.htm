Ebibe bia Nadabu na Abihu
Ⅰ Nadabu na Abihu, abamura ba Aroni, kera oyomo akaimokia orogio rwaye, akabeeka omorero ase orogio orwo, na akabeeka ekoroso igoro ase omorero oyio, bakarentera Omonene omorero orenge bw’engencho eng’ao, oyi’Omonene atabachiigete. Ⅱ Na omorero ogaika korwa ase Omonene, okabasamba, barabwo bakayia, bagakwera agwo ase obosio bw’Omonene. Ⅲ Erio Musa agateebia Aroni, “Aya nar’Omonene atebiire, ng’a ere neorokie koba omochenu ase abanto abwo bakomoika ang’e, na ase amaiso b’abaanto bonsi naorokie obonene bwaye.” Aroni agakira‐kiri.
Ⅳ Erio Musa akarangeria Mishaeli na Elisafani, basinto ba Usieli, ise moke Aroni, akabateebia, “Inchuo mobogorie emebere y’abamino, abana ba iso moke, moyerusie korwa ase Obomenyo Obochenu, moyeire isiko y’egetwori.” Ⅴ Barabwo bagaacha, bakabogoria emebere eria eboyirie chiganchwa chiabo, bakayeira isiko y’egetwori, buna Musa abateebeetie.
Ⅵ Musa agateebia Aroni amo n’Eleazari na Itamari, abamura ba Aroni, “Timobaisa gotiga chitukia chiaino chitachanuiri, gose timobaisa gotandora chianga chiaino ase okobaichanera. Moise gokora igo, goika mokwe, na endamwamu entindi y’Omonene echiche igoro y’omoganda bwonsi. Korende Abaisraeli bamino bonsi barabwo mbabe bakorera ase engencho y’ogosamba ogotindi Omonene arentire. Ⅶ Timobaisa gosoka isiko goetania egesoero ki’Eema y’Omosangererekano. Moise gokora igo, goika mokwe, ekiagera mwatananirwe ase ogoakwa amaguta ’Omonene.” Barabwo bagakora buna Musa abateebetie.
Amachiiko ase abakuani
Ⅷ Omonene agakwana na Aroni, akamoteebia, Ⅸ “Aye amo nabamura bao timobaisa konywa edivai, gose ekenyugwa kegotindia, ekero mogosoa ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. Moise gokora igo, goika mokwe. Okwo nogochika koragenderere ase more ebiaare ase ebiaare. Ⅹ Inwe goika momanye gwaatanana egento egechenu kere ekia Omonene korwa ase ekio getaatananiri koba egechenu, na egento gechenegete korwa ase egetachenegeti. Ⅺ Mworokererie Abaisraeli amachiiko onsi aya inche Omonene nabachiikire goetera ase Musa.”
Ⅻ 10:12: Abal 6:14-18Musa agateebia Aroni na abamura baye abatigarete, Eleazari na Itamari, “Imokia okoruegwa kw’endagera, okwo gotigarete korwa ase okwo gwasambirwe n’omorero ase Omonene, mokorie gotabeekiri memera, agwo ase egesasiimero ensemo, ekiagera okoruegwa okwo nogochenu mono. ⅩⅢ Mokoriere agwo Aase gete are aachenu. Ekio negetari kiao, na eki’abamura bao, gekorusigwa korwa ase okoruegwa kw’endagera kogosaambwa n’omorero ase Omonene. Ayio nar’Omonene anchiigete. ⅩⅣ 10:14: Abal 7:30-34Korende ritaana rigosungusigwa, na ekenama gekoruegwa, aye amo nabana bao, abamura na abaiseke, mwaeirwe ribaga kobiriera agwo aase achenire. Ebinto ebio aye na abana bao mwabieirwe korwa ase ebing’wanso bi’omorembe bikoruegwa n’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅤ Ekenama gekoruegwa, ne ritaana riria rigosungusigwa, birentwe amo nebitinya bikoruegwa ebing’wanso bigosaambwa n’omorero, bisungusigwe bibe ebing’wanso bi’ogosungusigwa ase obosio bw’Omonene. Ebio nabibe egetari kiao amo neki’abamura bao kogenderera botaambe, buna Omonene achiigete.”
ⅩⅥ Musa agatuka mono komanya inaki embori y’ekeng’wanso ki’ebibe yabeete, akamanya ng’a embori eria nigo yasaambetwe. Ase ayio akaba nendamwamu enene ase Eliezeri na Itamari, abamura ba Aroni, baria batigarete, akabateebia. ⅩⅦ 10:17: Abal 6:24-26“Nase ki motarierete ekeng’wanso keria ki’ebibe agwo aase are aachenu? Ekio negento egechenu mono mwaeirwe, erinde moruserie omoganda ebibe biabo, na gokora okobwatanigwa ase bare ase obosio bw’Omonene. ⅩⅧ Rora, amanyinga aye tairetwe agwo Aase Aachenu; nigo yabagwenerete mokeriere agwo aase achenire.”
ⅩⅨ Aroni akairaneria Musa, “Rora, rituko ria reero abanto barure ekeng’wanso kiabo ki’ebibe, na eki’ogosaambwa, ase obosio bw’Omonene, korende nonya naboigo nomanyete emechando eye nanyorire. Onye inaria ekeng’wanso ki’ebibe rituko ria reero, Inee, nanga nancherwa ase obosio bw’Omonene?” ⅩⅩ Ekero Musa aigwete amang’ana ayio agaisaneka.
