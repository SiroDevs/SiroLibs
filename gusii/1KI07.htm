Oboagacho bw’enyomba y’omorwoti Sulemani
Ⅰ Sulemani akaira emiaka ikomi na etato ase okoagacha enyomba yaye omonyene na koyekoora. Ⅱ Akaagacha enyomba ekorokwa Enyomba ye Rinani ria Lebanoni. Obotambe bwaye nigo bwareenge obw’amaboko rigana erimo, na obogare bwaye amaboko emerongo etano, na obotambe bwaye gochia igoro amaboko emerongo etato. Ero nigo yaagachetwe amo ne chisiiro chi’emecharake chindwaro inye. Igoro ase chisiiro echio emiamba y’emecharake yabaachetwe ekabekwa. Ⅲ Enyomba nigo yareenge nekerama ki’emecharake igoro y’emiamba yabegetwe ase chisiiro chiria emerongo ene na isano, ikomi na isano ase kera orwaro. Ⅳ Enyomba eyio nigo yareenge nebibao bire ase chindwaro isato, kera ekebao kerigereretie ekende. Ⅴ Ebisoero bionsi na ebibao nigo biareenge ne chikoona inye, na ebibao nigo biarigereranetie bire ase chindwaro isato.
Ⅵ Boigo akaagacha eero ye chisiiro. Obotambe bwaye nobw’amaboko emerongo etano, na obogare bwaye amaboko emerongo etato. Bosio bw’enyomba eyio akaagacha egesona, na bosio aroro chisiiro chire nekerama.
Ⅶ Naende akaagacha Enyomba y’ekerogo ki’Oborwoti, eye ekarokwa naende Enyomba y’ekegambero aase areenge kogaambera ebiina. Eye nigo yatubetwe ne chibao chi’emecharake korwa inse goika igoro.
Ⅷ 7:8: 1 Abar 3:1Naboigo enyomba yaye y’okomenya yaagachetwe magega y’enyomba eria y’ebiina, egwekaine neyio. Naende Sulemani akaagachera mokaye, mosubati o Farao, enyomba egwekaine eria yaye.
Ⅸ Echio chionsi nigo chiagachetwe namagena amaya ye rigori rinene, abaachetwe koreng’ana nekerengo, akanachwa nemesumeno chinsemo chionsi korwa oboroso goika rigasio ri’enyasi. Amagena onsi nigo aroisetigwe bo, nonya naya e risiko amo nay’orwaki rw’eraaro y’enyomba y’Omonene. Ⅹ Amagena ’oboroso nigo areenge aye rigori rinene, amagena amanene na amarito, ayande ay’obotambe bw’amaboko ikomi, na ayande ay’amaboko atano na atato. Ⅺ Igoro y’oboroso nareengeo amagena ande e rigori rinene, abaachetwe koreng’ana nekerengo, amo nemeriibo y’emecharake. Ⅻ Orwaki rw’eraaro enene igo rwaagachetwe namagena abaachire, abekire chindwaro isato gochia igoro, ne chibao chi’emecharake orwaro oromo. Naboigo norwaki rw’eraaro ya ime y’enyomba y’Omonene amo nenyomba yareenge bosio bw’aroro.
Emeremo ya Hiramu
ⅩⅢ Omorwoti Sulemani akarangeria Hiramu achiche korwa Turo. ⅩⅣ Hiramu nigo areenge omwana bw’omokuungu omoboraka bw’egesaku kia Nafutali, na ise nigo areenge omonto o Turo, omoturi bwe chitaai. Hiramu omonyene nigo areenge nobong’aini obonge amo ne chisemi, omanyete gotura ebinto binde bionsi bie chitaai. Ere agacha ase Sulemani, akamokorera emeremo yaye yonsi.
Chisiiro ibere chi’etai
(2 Amg 3:15-17)
ⅩⅤ Hiramu akaroisia chisiiro ibere chi’etai; obotambe bwa kera esiiro eyemo nigo bwareenge obw’amaboko ikomi na atato, na engori y’obotambe bw’amaboko ikomi na abere egaetanana kera esiiro. ⅩⅥ Naende akaroisia ebisure bibere bi’etai enyititoire na kobombwa, akabibeeka igoro ye chisiiro; obotambe gochia igoro bwa kera egesure nigo bwareenge obw’amaboko atano. ⅩⅦ Kera egesure igoro ye chisiiro nigo giachabeire nebichanga bitano na bibere biasisire goetanana. ⅩⅧ Akarosia emegwekano y’amakomomanga, akayebeeka ase chindwaro ibere igoro y’ebichanga biareenge ase ebisure biria.
ⅩⅨ Ebisure igoro ye chisiiro chiareenge ime y’enyomba ya bosio, nigo biabombire bigwekaine ebisicha; obotambe bwabo nobw’amaboko ane. ⅩⅩ Ebisure ebio nigo biareenge igoro ase chisiiro chiria ibere na igoro ye chinsemo chiatambeete gochia isiko. Emegwekano y’amakomomanga amagana abere yareenge ase chindwaro ibere ase kera egesure.
ⅩⅪ Hiramu akabeeka chisiiro chiria ase enyomba bosio bwe Hekalu; esiiro yabegetwe ensemo ya Irianyi akayeroka Yakini, na eyabegetwe ensemo ya sugusu akayeroka Boazi. ⅩⅫ Ebisure nigo biabombire buna ebisicha nigo biareenge igoro ase chisiiro.
Na igo emeremo ye chisiiro ekaera.
Egetuba ekenene keroisirie korwa ase etai
(1 Amg 4:2-5)
ⅩⅩⅢ Hiramu akaroisia egetuba ekenene korwa ase etai enyititoire na kobombwa, ekaba egesingo. Ekerengo korwa ensemo eyemo goika eyende nigo kiareenge eki’amaboko ikomi. Obotambe gochia igoro nigo bwareenge bw’ekerengo ki’amaboko atano. Engori y’obotambe bw’amaboko emerongo etato ekanyara goetanana egetuba ekio. ⅩⅩⅣ Ang’e nomogo emegwekano y’amasosa iyareengeo, ikomi ase kera ekerengo gi’okoboko okomo, yarandete ekeringero ekegima. Emegwekano eyio nigo yarenge chindwaro ibere, yabombire ase egento ekemo negetuba keria. ⅩⅩⅤ Egetuba keria giaterekereretwe igoro y’emegwekano ye chiombe ikomi na ibere. Chiombe isato chikarigereria gochia sugusu, na isato gochia bosongo, na isato gochia Irianyi, na isato gochia moocha; chinsemo chiabo chia magega chikarigereria gochia gati‐gati inse y’egetuba keria. ⅩⅩⅥ Obonetu bw’etai yaroiseretigwe egetubwa keria, nigo bwarenge obw’obogare bw’egesanyi gi’okoboko, na omogo okaroisigwa buna oyo bw’egekombe, gose obw’egesicha. Egetuba keria nigo kiarenge kobekwa amaache aisaine ang’e chilita chilifu emerongo etano (50,000).
Ebibekerero bi’ebituba bikoroisigwa korwa ase etai
ⅩⅩⅦ Naende Hiramu akaroisia ebibekerero ikomi bi’ebituba korwa ase chitaai. Obotambe bwa kera ekebekerero nigo bwarenge obw’amaboko ane, na obogare bwaye amaboko ane, na obotambe bwaye gochia igoro amaboko atato. ⅩⅩⅧ Ebibekerero biria nigo biakoretwe iga: Chinsingo nchiarenge ase chinsemo chiabo ase egati ye chinsingo chie chikoona. ⅩⅩⅨ Ase chinsingo echio yarengeo emegwekano ye chindo, na eye chieri, na ey’ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba; naboigo nase chinsingo chie chikoona igoro. Igoro na inse y’emegwekano eyio nigo yarenge emegwekano y’ebisicha biaroisirie chingata. ⅩⅩⅩ Kera ekebekerero nigo kiarenge namagoro ane aroisetigwe korwa ase etai. Ase chikoona chi’ekebekerero ebisiirero mbiarengeo. Ebisiirero ebio nigo biabombire nemegwekano y’ebisicha biaroisetigwe chingata ase kera ekemo. ⅩⅩⅪ Omonwa bw’ekebekerero nigo orenge igoro ime y’orosingo rw’obotambe gochia igoro okoboko okomo. Omonwa oyio orenge egesingo otambeete gochia inse, obotambe bw’okoboko okomo na ekeng’ese, nao ebiechabero biarenge. Emetobwa yaye nigo yarenge ne chikoona, tiyarenge egesingo. ⅩⅩⅫ Amagoro aye ane nigo abegetwe inse ye chinsingo chiaye, ne rinyongo riaye nigo ribwateranirie nekebekerero. Obotambe bwa kera okogoro nigo bwarenge obw’okoboko okomo na ekeng’ese. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Amagoro ayio nigo aroisetigwe buna aye chigari. Amagoro amagima nigo aroisetigwe korwa ase etai yanyititoire na kobombwa. Boigo amanyongo, ne chingata, na ebitingiro biaroisetigwe. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Ebisiirero bine mbiarenge ase kera ekebekerero ase chikoona chiaye; biaroisetigwe bikaba egento ekemo nekebekerero. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Igoro kegima ase kera ekebekerero nigo kiarenge orosingo rw’obogare bw’ekeng’ese gi’okoboko okomo; na igoro ase orosingo orwo nigo biarenge ebisiirero amo ne chinsingo chie chinsemo. Bionsi nigo biaroisetigwe bikaba egento ekemo nebisiirero. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Ase ebisiirero na ase chinsingo emegwekano y’ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba, na eye chindo, na ey’emekendo, amo ney’ebisicha, ekamang’wa, buna ribaga riarenge ase kera ensemo. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Naboigo akaroisia ebibekerero bionsi ikomi. Bionsi nigo biaroisetigwe korwa ase etai enyititoire na kobombwa; nigo biareng’aine na nigo biagwekaine.
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ 7:38: Okong’ 30:17-21Hiramu akaroisia na ebituba ikomi bie chitaai. Obogare bwa kera egetuba nigo bwarenge obw’ekerengo ki’amaboko ane, na nigo kiare koira amaache aisaine ang’e ne chilita amagana kianda. Naboigo aroisetie ebituba ikomi, ekemo ase kera ekebekerero. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Akabeeka ebibekerero bitano ensemo ya Irianyi ye Hekalu, na bitano ensemo ya sugusu, na egetuba keria ekenene agakebeeka ensemo ya Irianyi ya moocha.
Ebikorero bie Hekalu
(2 Amg 4:11—5:1)
ⅩⅬ Hiramu akaroisia ebituba, na ebichiko, na ebitaabo. Naboigo agakoora emeremo yonsi eyaetwe na Sulemani ng’a akore ase enyomba y’Omonene. ⅩⅬⅠ Akaroisia chisiiro ibere, na ebisure bibere biabegetwe igoro ye chisiiro, na ebichanga bisisire biaetanaine, bigatuba chinsemo chi’ebisure echiatambeete gochia isiko, ⅩⅬⅡ na emegwekano y’amakomomanga amagana ane eyabegetwe igoro y’ebichanga ebio, chindwaro ibere ase kera egesure kiarenge igoro ye chisiiro, erinde ensemo y’ebisure biria yatambeete etubwe. ⅩⅬⅢ Naende akaroisia ebibekerero bi’ebituba amo nebituba ikomi biaterekereireo, ⅩⅬⅣ na egetuba ekenene ekemo amo nemegwekano ye chiombe ikomi na ibere ekabekwa inse y’egetuba keria, ⅩⅬⅤ ne chindogio, na ebichiko, na ebitaabo. Ebinto ebio bionsi biaatoire nigo biare ebibekerero bi’enyomba y’Omonene, Hiramu aroiseretie omorwoti Sulemani. Bionsi nigo biaroisetigwe korwa ase chitaai chisibirie buya. ⅩⅬⅥ Ebinto ebio omorwoti akabibomba korwa ase etai enyititoire, ime ase esike ebombire agwo egati ya Sukoti na Saretani ase Riranya ria Yorodani. ⅩⅬⅦ Sulemani agatiga korenga ebinto ebi bionsi, ekiagera biarenge ebinge mono. Oborito bw’etai eria tibwamanyekanete buna bwang’ana.
ⅩⅬⅧ 7:48: a Okong’ 30:1-3; b Okong’ 25:23-30Boigo Sulemani akaroisia ebinto binde bionsi biare kobekwa ase enyomba y’Omonene; egesasiimero gi’etaabu, na emesa y’etaabu ase emegati yare koruegwa ase Nyasae yare kobekwa. ⅩⅬⅨ 7:49: Okong’ 25:31-40Ebibekerero bie chitaaya bitano biare kobekwa ensemo ya Irianyi, na bitano ensemo ya sugusu, bosio bw’Aase Aachenu Mono; na emegwekano y’ebisicha, ne chitaaya, amo nebiinachi biakorire korwa ase chitaabu. Ⅼ Naende akaroisia chisani ne chigami, na ebitaabo bi’ogosambera ubani, ne chindogio chi’okorenta omorero, ne chimbata chi’ebisieri bi’akanyomba ka ime, Aase Aachenu Mono, na echi’ebisieri bie Hekalu enyene. Ebinto ebio bionsi nigo biaroisetigwe korwa ase chitaabu chichenire.
ⅬⅠ 7:51: 2 Sam 8:11; 1 Amg 18:11Naboigo emeremo eye yonsi omorwoti Sulemani akorete ase enyomba y’Omonene ekaera. Na Sulemani agasoyia aroro ebinto ebi Daudi ise aatananete koba ebichenu: chifeta, ne chitaabu, na ebikorero; akabigacha ase chikungo chi’enyomba y’Omonene.
