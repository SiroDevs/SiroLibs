Emebayeno ya Sulemani
Ⅰ Eye nemebayeno ya Sulemani.
Omwana omomura omong’aini nigo akogokia ise, korende oyore omoriri nigo akorentera ng’ina omoichano.
Ⅱ Chinibo chi’ogokora okobe tichiri gokonya kende, korende oboronge nigo bogotooria korwa ase amakweri.
Ⅲ Omonene tari gotiga omonyene oboronge abe nenchara; korende nigo akwanga okogania kw’abakori amabe.
Ⅳ Omonto ogokora emeremo n’amaboko amoro nigo akoba omotaka, korende amaboko are nomokia nigo akorenta obotenenku.
Ⅴ Omwana omomura ogosangereria chindagera engaki ye rigesa nigo are omong’aini, korende oyo okorara engaki eyio nigo akorenta obosooku.
Ⅵ Ogosesenigwa nigo gokobera ase omotwe bw’omonyene oboronge, korende obochege nigo boichire ase emenwa y’abakori amabe.
Ⅶ Okoinyorwa kw’omonyene oboronge nigo gokorenta ogosesenigwa, korende erieta ri’omokori amabe nigo riragunde.
Ⅷ Omonto ore omong’aini ase enkoro yaye nigo anchete amachiiko, korende oyogokwana amang’ana atagwenereti nigo arasirigwe.
Ⅸ Omonto okobwatia chiinchera chi’oboronge nigo araarendwe buya, korende oyo okobwatia chiinchera chimbe nigo aramanyekane.
Ⅹ Omonto ogosumera amaiso ase okogenka nigo akorenta emechando, na oyogokwana amang’ana atagwenereti nigo arasirigwe.
Ⅺ Omonwa bw’omonyene oboronge nigo onga buna ensoko y’obogima, korende obochege nigo boichire ase emenwa y’abakori amabe.
Ⅻ 10:12: Yakb 5:20; 1 Petr 4:8Obogechani nigo bokorenta eriomana, korende obwanchani nigo bogotuba ebibe bionsi.
ⅩⅢ Obong’aini nigo bokonyorekana ase omonwa bw’omonto omomanyi, korende oyo otari n’okomanya nigo agwenerete aakwe enyimbo ase omogongo oye.
ⅩⅣ Abanto abang’aini nigo bakogaacha chiseemi, korende omonwa bw’omonto omoriri nigo okorenta ogosirigwa ang’e.
ⅩⅤ Obotenenku bw’omonto omonda nigo bore ase are buna omochie ogiteire chindwaki, korende obotaka nabwo ogosirigwa kw’abataka.
ⅩⅥ Okonyora kw’omonyene oboronge nigo gokomoraa gochia ase obogima, korende ebitoki bi’omokori amabe nigo bikomoraa gochia ase ebibe.
ⅩⅦ Omonto okoigwera okorongeigwa nigo akorobera ase enchera y’obogima, korende oyo okwanga korongeigwa nigo agosoa ase obosiru.
ⅩⅧ Omonto obisete okogecha ime yaye nigo agokwana oborimo korwa ase omonwa oye, na oyo ogotaaria obogenki nigo are omoriri.
ⅩⅨ Omonto ogokwana amang’ana amaange tari komocha korenta ogosaria, korende oyo orendete omonwa oye nigo are omong’aini.
ⅩⅩ Oromeme rw’omonyene oboronge nigo ronga buna efeta echenire korende ebirengererio bi’omokori amabe tibiri na igori rinde.
ⅩⅪ Ebikoba bi’omonyene oboronge nigo bikorenta amaya ase abanto abange, korende oboriri nigo bogoita.
ⅩⅫ Ogosesenigwa kw’Omonene nakwo gokorenta obotenenku, na ere tari kobeeka obororo ase ogosesenigwa okwo.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omonto omoriri nigo agokora ebibe buna egosori, korende omonto ore nokomanya nigo agokora ayare ay’obong’aini.
ⅩⅩⅣ Aria omokori amabe airogete naro akomochera, korende omonyene oboronge ’naikeranerigwe amagania aye.
ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero omwaga ekembugu‐mbugu ogoeta, omokori amabe nigo agosirigwa, korende omonyene oboronge nigo agotenena bokong’u kare na kare.
ⅩⅩⅥ Buna obokang’iti bokoba ase amaino na erioki rikoba ase amaiso, naboigo omonto omworo akoba ase abwo bakomotoma abakorere emeremo.
ⅩⅩⅦ Okomoiroka Omonene nigo gokomenta obomenyo ase obogima, korende emiaka y’omokori amabe nigo ekweng’eigwa.
ⅩⅩⅧ Ogosemeria kw’abanyene oboronge nigo kogoikeranigwa ase omogooko, korende okw’abakori amabe nokwabosa.
ⅩⅩⅨ Enchera y’Omonene nigo ere egekengero egekong’u ase abanyene oboronge, korende nigo ere obosiru ase abanyabibe.
ⅩⅩⅩ Omonyene oboronge takonyegerigwa, korende omokori amabe takogeenderera komenya ase ense.
ⅩⅩⅪ Omonwa bw’omonyene oboronge nigo ogokwana ay’obong’aini, korende oromeme rokong’ainereria ’ndonachwe.
ⅩⅩⅫ Ebikoba bi’omonyene oboronge nigo bimanyete ayagwenerete, korende omonwa bw’omokori amabe nigo ogokwana ay’atagwenereti.
