Omonene ogochika Abaisraeli barwe Sinai bagenderere norogendo rwabo
Ⅰ 33:1: a Omoch 12:7; b Omoch 26:3; c Omoch 28:13Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, Imoka korwa aa ogende, aye amo nabaanto aba kwang’anyetie korwa Misiri, obaire ase ense eria narierete Aburaamu na Isaka na Yakobo eira, ngateba “Ning’ee ororeria rwaino ense eyio. Ⅱ Nintome omomalaika agotang’ane, Niserie Abakanaani, na Abaamori, na Abahiti, na Abaperisi, na Abahivi na Abayebusi. Ⅲ Nimbaire ase ense ere entenenku y’amabeere na amooke. Inche omonyene tinkogenda amo nainwe, mbaise kobaitera inchera, ekiagera inwe nabanyabigoti bikong’u.” Ⅳ Ekero abanto baigwete amang’ana ayio amakong’u bakarera na onde taba obegete ebinto biaye bi’ogwechabera.
Ⅴ Omonene agateebia Musa “Bateebie Abaisraeli: Inwe nabanyabigoti bikong’u. Inse kogenda amo nainwe, nonya netagika eyemo, nigo ndabasirie kegima. Gaki bono rusia ebinto biaino ebi mwechaberete, erio ndore inki ndabakorere.” Ⅵ Ase igo Abaisraeli bakarusia ebinto biria bechaberete, na tibabibeka korwa ekero baruete egetunwa kia Horebu nakogenderera.
Eema y’Omosangererekano
Ⅶ Musa nigo anarete koira eema na koyetenenia are ake korwa ase egetwori akayeroka “Eema y’Omosangererekano.” Na onde bwensi oganetie kobori’Omonene ring’ana, goika are kogende ase Eema eria, isiko y’egetwori. Ⅷ Engaki ende yonsi Musa arenge kogenda ase Eema eria, abanto bonsi nigo baare koimoka na gotenena, kera omonto ase egesoero ki’eema yaye, na korigereria Musa goika osoire Eema eria ime. Ⅸ Ekero Musa asoire Eema eria, riire riare buna esiiro riba rigoika na gotenena ase egesoero ki’Eema eria, n’Omonene aba ogokwana na Musa. Ⅹ Ekero abanto bonsi barorire riire riria rigotenena ase egesoero ki’Eema, baba bakoimoka na gotung’ama inse ase ogosasiima, kera omonto ase egesoero ki’eema yaye.
Ⅺ Na Omonene aba ogokwana na Musa obosio ase obosio, buna omonto agokwana nomonto onde. Erio Musa aba okoirana gochia ase egetwori, korende Yoshua, omosomba oye omosae, mosinto o Nuni, tare korua ase Eema eria.
Eira y’Omonene y’okoba amo nabaanto baye
Ⅻ Musa agateebia Omonene, “Rora, aye nigo gwantebeetie: Baire abanto aba, korende tigwanteebia ng’a ning’o oratome agende amo nainche. Naende oganteebia: Ninkomanyete buya kegima, na buna okorokwa; naye kwanyorire ogwancherwa ase obosio bwane. ⅩⅢ Ase igo, onye nanyorire ogwancherwa ase obosio bwao, nagosabire, onyorokie chinchera chiao, erinde inyare gokomanya na konyora ogwancherwa ase obosio bwao, naende rigereria ng’a abanto b’egesaku eke nabaanto bao bare.”
ⅩⅣ Omonene akamoteebia, “Inche omonyene nigo ndagende amo naye, na ninkoe obotoereru.”
ⅩⅤ Musa akamoiraneria, “Onye aye omonyene tokogenda amo naintwe, rirorio totige, tobeere aa. ⅩⅥ Naki eramanyekane ng’a inche na abanto bao twanyorire ogwancherwa ase obosio bwao, onye tokogenda amo naintwe? Koragende amo naintwe, natororekane ng’a twachorirwe korwa ase abanto bande b’ense eye.”
ⅩⅦ Omonene akairaneria Musa, “Ninkore erio kwamboria, ekiagera kwanyorire ogwancherwa ase obosio bwane, na inche ninkomanyete buya kegima, na buna okorokwa.”
ⅩⅧ Musa akamosaba, “Koranche, onyorokie obonene bwao!”
ⅩⅨ 33:19: Abar 9:15Omonene akamoiraneria, “Ninkore obosiani bwane bwonsi boete ase obosio bwao, na erieta riane, inche Omonene, nindirarie ase obosio bwao. Nimororere amabeebe oyio ndarorere amabeebe, na nimororere amaabera oyio ndarorere amaabera.” ⅩⅩ Naende akamokania, akamoteebia, “Tokonyara korora obosio bwane, ekiagera Mwanyabaanto aise kondora, takonyara koba moyo.” ⅩⅪ Naende Omonene akagenderera komoteebia, “Rora, aase aiga are ang’e nainche, aye oranyare gotenena egetare igoro. ⅩⅫ Erio ekero obonene bwane borabe bogoeta, ninkobeke ase omotaro bw’egetare keria, na ngotube nokoboko kwane, goika imbe naetire. ⅩⅩⅢ Erio ndusie okoboko kwane, na aye ondore amagega aane, korende obosio bwane tokonyara koborora.”
