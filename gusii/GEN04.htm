Kaini na Abeli
Ⅰ Bono Adamu akaba na mokaye Hawa, na ere akaba morito, akaibora omwana omoisia, akabora: “Nanyorire omwana omomura ase ogokonywa kw’Omonene.” Ase igo akamoroka Kaini*4:1 Kaini: engencho yaye: okonyora (1 Amg 1:1-4). Ⅱ Naende akaibora omwana onde Abeli. Abeli akaba omorisia bwe ching’ondi, na Kaini akaba omoremi bw’omogondo. Ⅲ Engaki egaika Kaini akarenta okwama kw’omogondo oye erio kobe ekengwanso ase Omonene. Ⅳ 4:4: Abaibr 11:4Abeli nere akareenta emanwa entangi enoru korwa ase riicho riaye. Na Omonene aganchera ekengwanso ki’Abeli, Ⅴ korende tagogetigwe nekengwanso gia Kaini. Ase igo, ayio akagera Kaini akagechigwa mono kegima, na obosio bwaye bokaonchoka, bokairura. Ⅵ Na Omonene akamobooria: “Kaini, nase ki okobeera nendamwamu na obosio bwao bwairurire?
Ⅶ “Onye ndiria gwakora buya anga nigo agogete;
korende bono aye gwakorire ebibe, ebibe nigo bikoganyererete gesieri;
na birobio nigo bikoganetie.
Korende nekogwenerete obibue kegima.”
Ⅷ 4:8: Mat 23:35; Luk 11:51; 1 Yoh 3:12Erio Kaini agateebia Abeli omwabo: “Inchuo, togende getii.” Ekero baare agwo getii, Kaini akaimokera Abeli momura omwabo, akamoita.
Ⅸ Eri’Omonene akabooria Kaini: “Ng’ai Abeli momura omino are?”
Kaini akamoiraneria: “Inche tinkomanya. Inee! Ninche nkorenda oyominto?”
Ⅹ 4:10: Abaibr 12:24Naende Omonene akamoteebia: “Inki gekogera gwakora egookoroo eke ekebe iga? Eriogi ri’amanyinga a momura omino riarure ase riroba, riang’ikeire buna eriogi rikorigia egesiomba. Ⅺ Bono aye kwaragererigwe na tokogenderera korema korwa ase riroba. Rirorio nigo riaasamirie omonwa oye na konywa amanyinga y’omwana omino korwa ase okoboko kwao. Ⅻ Ekero orabe gokorema riroba tirigokwamera endagera nonya ng’ake. Igo orabe omoomboganu na omoetanani ase ense.”
ⅩⅢ Erio Kaini agateebia Omonene: “Egesusuro kiane nekerito, tinkonyara gokebogoria. ⅩⅣ Rora, reero gwanseririe korwa ase riroba eri, nimbiseke korwa ase obosio bwao, timborora naende. Nimbe omoomboganu na omoetanani ase ense, na onde bwensi orang’umere nigo arang’ite.”
ⅩⅤ Korende Omonene akamoteebia: “Taibo! Onde bwensi oraite Kaini nigo araakanigwe egesiomba ara gatano na kabere.” Na Omonene akabeekera Kaini ekemanyererio ng’a onde bwensi oramoumere tabaisa komoita. ⅩⅥ Erio Kaini akarwa ase obosio bw’Omonene, akageenda, akamenya ase ense ekorokwa okoombogana ere moocha ya Edeni.
Ororeeria rwa Kaini
ⅩⅦ Kaini akaba na mokaye, na ere akaba morito, akaibora Enoku; na Kaini akaagacha omochie, akayoroka Enoku, erieta ri’omwana oye. ⅩⅧ Enoku akaibora Iradi, Iradi akaibora Metusaeli, Metusaeli akaibora Lameki. ⅩⅨ Lameki akanyuoma abakungu babere. Omokungu oyomo nigo are korokwa Ada, n’oyonde Zila. ⅩⅩ Ada akaibora Yabeli; oyo nere sokoro y’abare komenya ase ebigutu na babwate ching’iti chigotugwa. ⅩⅪ Na momura omwabo nigo are korokwa Yubali; oyio nere sokoro y’abanto baria baare kobugia amakano na ebiroria. ⅩⅫ Zila akaibora Tubali‐Kaini. Ere nigo areenge omoturi bw’ebinto bi’ebioma na ebie chitaai. Na omosubati omwabo nere Naama.
ⅩⅩⅢ Lameki agateebia abakungu baye,
inwe “Ada na Zila, igwa eriogi riane,
inwe abakungu ba Lameki, tegerera buya moigwe buna ngoteeba,
naitire omomura omoke
ekiagera ong’akire na kombeeka rioote.
ⅩⅩⅣ Onye Kaini ogoakanerwa egesiomba ara gatano na kabere,
amaene, Lameki ere naakanerwe ara emerongo etano na ebere,
na gatano na kabere.”
Seti na Enosu
ⅩⅩⅤ Adamu na mokaye bakaibora omwana onde omomura, erio Hawa akamoroka Seti, agateeba: “Bono Nyasae ong’eire omwana onde omomura ribaga ri’Abeli, oria Kaini aitete”. Erio akamoroka Seti. ⅩⅩⅥ Seti nere akaibora omwana omomura, akamoroka Enosu. Erio agwo abanto bagachaaka korangeria erieta ri’Omonene ase okomosasiima.
