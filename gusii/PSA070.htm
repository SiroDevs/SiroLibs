Ogosaba gw’okonyora ogokonywa
(Zab 40:13-17)
(Ogosaba gwa Daudi)
Ⅰ Aye Nyasae, koranche ontoorie!
Omonene, oche bwango onkonye!
Ⅱ Abanto abwo barigetie gosiria obogima bwane,
tiga basookigwe na gotantanigwa,
na abwo baganetie ng’a obobe bonyore inche,
tiga bairanigwe magega na gochaywa.
Ⅲ Abwo bagonchaya na goteeba: “Kiamonyorire!”
Tiga bakirigwe-kiri ase obosooku bwabo.
 
Ⅳ Abwo bonsi bagokorigia,
tiga bakogokere na kogochengera;
abwo banchete ogotooreka kwao,
tiga bagenderere goteeba: “Nyasae atogigwe!”
 
Ⅴ Inche nigo inde omotaka na omoremerwa;
aye Nyasae, inchwo bwango onkonye!
Aye naye Omokonyi one, na Omotooria one.
Aye Omonene, totebana!
