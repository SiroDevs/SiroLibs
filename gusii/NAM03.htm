Ⅰ Oragererigwe omochie oyio omong’ainereria naende ogoita, oichire obotenenku boraibwe na gosakorwa! Ⅱ Tegerera ogwateka kw’emecharichi, okorasa kw’amagaari, ogocharara gw’echibarasi, na okonyenya gw’echigari chi’echibarasi chi’esegi!
Ⅲ Abariini be chibarasi nigo bagosiaroka, emioro ekobara amatimo akomeka meka!
Ebitondo bibangire amo gochia igoro, emebere ekwete etari na mobaro abasacha bakogochwa nero!
Ⅳ Ninawi omotomani nkoegwa are egesusuro. Akomeka meka na oichire n’ogwechabera kw’oborogi bw’ogoita, nigo aegete ebisaku na kobakora abasomba.
Ⅴ Omonene Nyasae nigo agoteeba, Ninyee Ninawi egesusuro! Ngochobore otigare getirianda erio ngotigere ebisaku bikorore, birore obosoku bwao bwonsi.
 
Ⅵ Nikobogorie ase ogochaaya naende ngotube ne eubi. Abanto mbakorigererie n’omondoko.
Ⅶ Onde bwensi orakorigererie natame gochia magega. Barobwo mbateebe, ’Ninawi yatigaire koba ritombe! Ing’o ore n’amabeebe ande onsi ase are? Ning’o oraganie komoremia?’
Ⅷ Aye Ninawi, Nigo ore gai kobwa Tebesi, omochie omonene o Misiri? Oyio boigo narenge n’oroche rokomokengera ronga enyasi Naeli nero yarenge egekengero kiaye. Ⅸ Ere nigo agamberete Etiopia na Misiri boigo, chinguru chiaye tichiarenge n’echimbebe; Libia nere orenge omosani oye. Ⅹ Abanto ba Tebesi bakabogorigwa gochia gooswa. Ase kera ensinyo y’agachie abana babo bagaitwa goika bagakwa. Abatang’ani babo bakabogorigwa babekire ebinyuri na kogabanwa ase abasakori babo.
Ⅺ Ninawi, aye boigo nogwe gochia ase ogosundoka kw’ogotindu! Aye boigo norigie gotama korwa ase ababisa bao. Ⅻ Chiburi chiao chionsi nigo chirabe buna emeko ere namako atobire: nyegeria emete, n’amatunda akogwere gocha ase omonwa oo! ⅩⅢ Abarwani bao nigo banga abakungu, n’ense yao yatenenire etabwati abarendi ase obosio bw’ababisa bao. Omorero nosarie emekorogoto y’ebiita biao. ⅩⅣ Gachie roche bweariganie ase okoboerwa, boigo kong’ia chindwaki chiao! Tiringa esike oroisie amatabori, naende obeke ang’e esike y’amatabori! ⅩⅤ Aye n’okora ekio orakore, goika osambwe okwe gose oitwe ase esegi. Aye nosirigwe buna endagera ekoriegwa n’echingige. Aye nigo gwaichorete buna chingige! ⅩⅥ Aye nigo kwaiborete abaonchoreria abange kobwa ching’enang’eni chire ase erioba! Korende bono bachiire, buna chingige chikorambora chimbaba chiabo chiairuruka chiagenda. ⅩⅦ Abanene bao nigo banga buna risaga ri’echingige echio chigotigara ase chinyasi ase rituko ri’obokendu. Korende ekero risase rigosoka, chirochio chiairuruka, na onde taiyo okomanya ase chikogenda!
ⅩⅧ Aye omorwoti bw’Abaasuri; abagabana bao bakuure, n’abanene bao nigo baraire kare na kare! Abanto bao basiarerwe ase ebitunwa igoro, na onde taiyo orabairanie gocha inka naende. ⅩⅨ Riogo tiriyo gose, amaote ao takonyara kobwena. Bonsi abakoigwa amang’ana ao y’ogosarigwa nigo bagwaka chinkobi n’omogoko. Nareo onde bwensi otambogete obotindi bwao botakoera?
