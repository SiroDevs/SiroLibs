Yeso okogwenia omonto oiboretwe ore omouko
Ⅰ Yeso agachia goeta akarora omonto orenge omouko korwa okoiborwa kwaye. Ⅱ Aborokigwa baye bakamobooria, “Rabii, ning’o okorete ebibe, nomonto oyo, gose nabaibori baye, goika akaiborwa ore omouko?”
Ⅲ Yeso akairaneria, “Oyo takorete ebibe, nonya nabaibori baye, korende aya akorekire, erinde emeremo ya Nyasae eorokigwe asare. Ⅳ Netogwenerete tokore emeremo yaye oyontomete ekero rire mobaso; obotuko ngocha bore, omonto atanyare gokora emeremo. Ⅴ 9:5: Mat 5:14; Yoh 8:12Ekero inde ase ense, inche nomobaso bw’ense.”
Ⅵ Agachia gokoora gokwana aya, agatwera amate inse, agakora etintira ase amate aria, agaaka omouko oria amaiso netintira eria, Ⅶ akamoteebia, “Genda bwesibie ase ritibo ria Siloamuu” (okoonchorwa kwaye, oyotomirwe). Erio akagenda agesibia, akairana okorora. Ⅷ Nabo abagisangio baye, na baria bamorooche ekero arenge omosabereri bakaboora, “Inee! Oyo tari ere oria orenge oikaransete gosaberera?” Ⅸ Abande bakaboora, “Nere”; abande bakaboora, “Yaya, nigo agwekaine nere.” Ere omonyene akabora, “Ninche ere.”
Ⅹ Nabo bakamobooria, “Amaiso ao naki rende aramogetigwe?”
Ⅺ Ere akairaneria, “Omonto okorokwa Yeso okorete etintira, akang’aka amaiso aane, aganteebia: Genda ase ritibo ria Siloamuu, bwesibie; nabo nkagenda ngesibia, nkanyara korora.”
Ⅻ Bakamobooria, “Ng’ai are oyio?” Akabora, “Timanyeti.”
ⅩⅢ Bakamoira oria orenge omouko ritang’ani ase Abafarisai.
ⅩⅣ Nigo riarenge rituko ri’Esabato ekero Yeso akorete etintira na komoramokia amaiso. ⅩⅤ Erio Abafarisai nabarabwo bakamobooria naende buna anyorete okorora. Akabateebia, “Nigo abegete etintira igoro ase amaiso aane, ngesibia, na bono nkorora nde.”
ⅩⅥ Erio abande b’Abafarisai bakaboora, “Omonto oyo tarwete ase Nyasae, ekiagera tabwateti Esabato.” Abande bakaboora, “Naki omonto omonyene ebibe aranyare gokora ebikone buna ebi?” Ogwatananekana gokaba ase egati yabo. ⅩⅦ Bakaboria omouko oria eria kabere, “Aye naki okobora asare, ekiagera akoramokia amaiso?”
Akabora, “Nigo are omobani.”
ⅩⅧ Erio Abayahudi tibeegena amang’ana aye, ng’a nigo arenge omouko, naende anyarete korora, goika bakarangeria abaibori baye oria onyorete okorora, ⅩⅨ bakababoria, “Oyo nere omwana oino mogoteeba ng’a nigo aiboretwe ore omouko? Naki akora orora bono?”
ⅩⅩ Abaibori baye bakairaneria, “Twamanyire ng’a oyo nomwana oito, naende ng’a nigo aiboretwe ore omouko; ⅩⅪ korende buna anyara korora bono, titomanyeti, gose ning’o omoramokia amaiso, titomanyeti. Ere nomonto omogima, moborie omonyene, nekwanere.” ⅩⅫ Abaibori baye nigo bakwanete aya ase okoiroka Abayahudi, ekiagera Abayahudi konya babwatanire, ng’a monto onde bwensi oramoorokie ng’a nere Kristo aserigwe korwa ase esinagogi. ⅩⅩⅢ Nero engencho yagerete abaibori baye bakaboora, “Ere nomonto omogima, moborie.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Erio eria kabere bakarangeria omonto oria orenge omouko, bakamoteebia, “Moe Nyasae ogotogia; intwe twamanyire ng’a omonto oyio nomonyene ebibe.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Erio omonto oria akairaneria, “Onye ere nomonyene ebibe, timanyeti; ring’ana erimo namanyire, inche nigo narenge omouko, korende bono nkorora nde.”
ⅩⅩⅥ Bakamobooria, “Naki agokorerete? Naki akoramogetie amaiso?”
ⅩⅩⅦ Akabairaneria, “Naikire kobateebia, ko timontegerereti. Naki morigetie koyaigwa eria kabere? Inwe boigo morigetie koba aborokigwa baye?”
ⅩⅩⅧ Erio bakamorama, bakaboora, “Naye ore omworokigwa oye, korerende intwe nigo tore aborokigwa ba Musa. ⅩⅩⅨ Intwe twamanyire ng’a Nyasae nigo arenge gokwana na Musa, korende oyo titomanyeti aase arure.”
ⅩⅩⅩ Omonto oria akairaneria, “Ring’ana eri neri’ogokumia, ng’a inwe timomanyeti aase arure, na ere ondamokirie amaiso. ⅩⅩⅪ Twamanyire ng’a Nyasae tari koigwa abanyene ebibe; korende omonto karabe omosasimi Nyasae, na gokora ogwancha kwaye, oyio nere Nyasae akoigwa. ⅩⅩⅫ Korwa omochakano teraigugwa ng’a omonto oramokirie amaiso ’omouko oiboretwe engencho eye. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Onye oyo tarwete ase Nyasae, anga takonyara gokora gento.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Bakamoiraneria, “Aye nigo kwaiboretwe ase ebibe ebinene, na intwe ngotworokereria ore?” Bakamosokia isiko.
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Yeso akaigwa ng’a bamosokirie isiko, akamorora, akamobooria, “Aye kwamwegenire Omwana‐o‐Mwanyabaanto?”
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Ere akairaneria, “Ning’o oyio, Omonene, inyare komwegena?”
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Yeso akamoteebia, “Kwamororire, naende nere oyogokwana naye nere oyio.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Akabora, “Omonene, negenire”; akamosasiima. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Yeso akabora, “Inche nigo nachire ase ense eye ase ekiina, erinde abatari korora barore, na barabwo abakorora babe emeuko.”
ⅩⅬ Abande ase Abafarisai abarenge amo nere bakaigwa aya, bakamobooria, “Inee! Naintwe nigo tore emeuko?”
ⅩⅬⅠ Yeso akabairaneria, “Onye mwarenge emeuko, anga timori nebibe; korende bono nigo mogoteeba: Nkorora tore; gaki ebibe biaino biagendereire kobao.
