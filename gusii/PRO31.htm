Ogosemeria kw’omorwoti
Ⅰ Amang’ana aya naro Lemweli omorwoti orokereretigwe na ng’ina.
Ⅱ Inaki aye omwana o’ne? Inaki aye omwana bw’enda y’ane? Ninki ndagoteebie, aye omwana bw’eira y’ane nariete? Ⅲ Tobaisa gochwara chinguru chiao ase abakungu, gose chiinchera chiao ase bare, ekiagera nigo bagosiria abarwoti. Ⅳ Aye Lemweli, okonywa edivai tikogwenereti abarwoti, gose abagaambi tibaabaisa kogania ebinyugwa bigotindia. Ⅴ Baise konywa, nigo barebe amachiiko, na baonchorane oboronge bwa abachaandegete bonsi. Ⅵ Moe ekenyugwa kegotindia oyo ogochia gosira, na edivai ase oyio oichanete ase omoyo oye. Ⅶ Tiga banywe, erinde beebe tibaabaisa kona‐koinyora emechando yabo n’obotaka bwabo.
Ⅷ Maanora omonwa oo, okwanere rimama, orwanerere oboronge bw’abwo batigirwe bachaandegete. Ⅸ Maanora omonwa oo, ogambe ebiina bi’ekeene, orwanerere oboronge bw’abataka na obaremeire.
Omokungu omuya
Ⅹ Ning’o oranyaare konyora omokungu omuya? Omokungu omuya nigo are n’engeencho enene kobua amagena ye rigori rinene.
Ⅺ Enkoro y’omosaacha oye nigo emwegenete, gose omosacha oyio takoremerwa konyora ebitoki.
Ⅻ Ase obogima bwaye bwonsi omokungu oyio nigo agokorera omosacha oye amaya, gose tari korenta amabe ase are.
ⅩⅢ Omokungu oyio nigo akorigia amarionya e ching’ondi ne chiusi chi’egatani, okora emeremo namaboko aye ore n’omoyo omuya.
ⅩⅣ Nigo anga chimeeri chi’abaonchoreria; nigo akorenta endagera korwa ase ense y’aare.
ⅩⅤ Nigo akoboka kobore botuko, oroiseria abanto b’enyomba yaye endagera, na oa abasomba baye abaiseke eyebatungetie.
ⅩⅥ Nigo akorigia omogondo oyogora, na korwa ase ebitoki bi’emeremo y’amaboko aye nigo agosimeka emesabibu.
ⅩⅦ Nigo akweboyia chinguru buna orokini, okora emeremo emekong’u n’amaboko aye.
ⅩⅧ Nigo akorora ng’a emeremo yaye nigo ekorenta ebitoki, na etaaya yaye teri korima nyomba mwaye botuko.
ⅩⅨ Nigo abwate egekorero akoroiseria chiusi namaboko aye na egete agochiranderera nebiara biaye.
ⅩⅩ Nigo akorambora okoboko kwaye okonya abataka, ee, nabaremeire nigo akobaramborera amaboko aye.
ⅩⅪ Ere tari kwoboa chingaki chi’obokendu, ekiagera abanto bonsi b’enyomba yaye nigo babwate chianga chie riberera chiroisirie korwa ase chiusi chimonyinga.
ⅩⅫ Omokungu oyio nigo akweroiseria ebirarero; chianga chiaye nigo chiroisirie korwa ase egatani engiya ne chiusi chimbariri.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omosacha bw’omokuungu oyio nigo amanyekanete ase ebiita bi’omochie, agwo ase agoikaransa ogaamba ebiina amo nabagaaka b’ense eyio.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omokungu oyio nigo akoroisia chianga chi’egatani engiya, ochioonia; naende nigo akoroisia chinkini, ochiooneria abaonchoreria bande.
ⅩⅩⅤ Nigo akweboyia chinguru na amasikani, na igo nabe nomogooko ase chingaki chigochi‐gocha.
ⅩⅩⅥ Amorokererio akorua ase omonwa oye nigo are ay’obong’aini n’obuya, amo n’obwororo nigo bore ase oromeme rw’aye.
ⅩⅩⅦ Nigo akoroberia buya amang’ana y’enyomba yaye, gose tari koria endagera atari gokorera emeremo.
ⅩⅩⅧ Abana baye nigo bakoboka bamoroka “Oyosesenirie”, naboigo n’omosaacha oye nigo akomotogia, oteeba.
ⅩⅩⅨ “Abakungu abange bakorire ayare amaya mono, korende aye kwababuire bonsi.”
ⅩⅩⅩ Obonyakieni nigo bokong’ainereria, na ekieni ekiya nigo kere bosa, korende omokungu omoirogete Omonene, oyio nere oranyaare gotogigwa.
ⅩⅩⅪ Kae omokungu oyio okwama kw’emeremo y’amaboko aye, na tiga atogigwe agwo ase ebiita ase ogokora kwaye gwonsi.
