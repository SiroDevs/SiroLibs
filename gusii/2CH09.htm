Enting’ana ya Seba okogenda gokwania Sulemani
(1 Abar 10:1-3)
Ⅰ 9:1: Mat 12:42; Luk 11:31Ekero eting’ana ya Seba aigwete chinkuma chia Sulemani, agacha Yerusalemu komoteema ase okomoboria amang’ana amakong’u, agacha amo nabasomba baye abange ne chingamia chiabogoretie ebinsari, ne chitaabu chinyinge, na amagena e rigori rinene. Na ekero aigete ase Sulemani, akamoteebia amang’ana onsi arenge ase ebirengererio biaye. Ⅱ Sulemani akamoiraneria amaborio aye onsi; nonya ne ring’ana erimo tiriabisekanete ase Sulemani; nigo amanyete onsi na komoyaorokia. Ⅲ Na ekero eting’ana ya Seba yaroche obong’aini bwonsi bwa Sulemani, na enyomba eria aagachete, Ⅳ na endagera yarenge korentwa ase emesa yaye, na akarora buna abakoonyi baye bamenyete aroro, na buna abasomba baye baare komokorera, ne chianga babegete, na ababogoria ebikombe baye, ne chianga babegete, na ebing’wanso biaye bi’ogosambwa aruete ase enyomba y’Omonene, agakumia na koumaata.
Ⅴ Eting’ana eria egateebia omorwoti, “Chinkuma nabeire nkoigwa ekero narenge ase ense yane igoro y’emeremo yao, na obong’aini bwao, nechi’ekeene kegima. Ⅵ Korende tinegenete amang’ana ayio, goika ekero nachire aa na neroreire namaiso aane inche omonyene. Bono narorire ng’a tinatebetigwe nonya nekeng’ese ki’obonge bw’obong’aini bwao. Nigo bwaetanirie amang’ana konya nkoigwa. Ⅶ Abanto bao, na abasomba bao, abagotenena botambe ase obosio bwao, na koigwa obong’aini bwao, mbare negesio ekenene. Ⅷ Omonene, Nyasae oo, atogigwe, oria ogokigwe asore na gokobeeka ase ekerogo kiaye ki’obonene, obe omorwoti ase obosio bwaye, ere Omonene, Nyasae oo! Ekiagera ere anchete Abaisraeli botambe, na akabakong’ia bakaba ne chinguru kare na kare, ogokorire aye koba omorwoti igoro ase bare, erinde onyare konacha ayare ay’ekeene na oboronge.”
Ⅸ Erio eting’ana eria ekaa omorwoti ebiegwa ebio arentete: Chitaabu ang’e chikiro chilifu isano (5,000), na ebinsari ebinge mono, na amagena e rigori rinene. Ebinsari binde tibiarentetwe ekero kende gionsi bireng’aine na biria eting’ana ya Seba yarenterete omorwoti Sulemani.
Ⅹ Abasomba ba Huramu na abasomba ba Sulemani, baria barentete chitaabu korwa Ofiri, bakarenta boigo korwa aroro chibao chi’emete ekorokwa alugumu, amo namagena e rigori rinene. Ⅺ Korwa ase chibao chi’alugumu omorwoti akaroisia ebiriinero bi’enyomba y’Omonene, na ebi’enyomba y’omorwoti, naende akaroisia amakano, na amakano ’ebirigisa akobugigwa nabateri. Ebinto buna ebio tibiana‐kororekana naende nonya ng’ake ase ense y’Abayuda.
Ⅻ Omorwoti Sulemani akaa eting’ana ya Seba ebinto bionsi aganetie, na ebio asabete aegwe, goetania ekerengo ki’ebio ere arenterete omorwoti. Erio eting’ana eria akairana, akagenda ase ense yaye amo nabasomba baye.
Obotenenku bw’omorwoti Sulemani
(1 Abar 10:14-25)
ⅩⅢ Chitaabu chiare korentwa ase Sulemani kera omwaka nigo chiarenge goetania chikiro chilifu emerongo ebere na isano (25,000). ⅩⅣ Boigo chitaabu n’echifeta chikaba chikorentwa nabaonchoreria b’ebinto, na baria barenge korenta ebinto korwa ase chinse chia isiko, naende chikarentwa nabarwoti b’Arabia na abagaambi bande abarenge goakana ebango ase omorwoti. ⅩⅤ Omorwoti Sulemani akaroisia chinguba amagana abere, agachiooma ne chitaabu chisunyuntire, na kera eng’aya ekaira etaabu esunyuntire ang’e chikiro isano na ibere. ⅩⅥ Naende akagoncha*9:16 akagoncha: engencho yaye okoroisia chinguba chinke amagana atato, agachioma ne chitaabu chisunyuntire, na kera enguba ekaira etaabu ang’e chikiro inye. Omorwoti agachibeka ime ase enyomba ekarokwa Enyomba ye Rinani ria Lebanoni.
ⅩⅦ Omorwoti akaroisia ekerogo ekenene ki’oborwoti, akagechabera amaino e chinchogu, na agakeooma netaabu echenire. ⅩⅧ Ekerogo keria ki’oborwoti nigo kiabwate ekeriinero kiarenge nebitambokero bitano na ekemo, na egeteni ki’amagoro kiaroisirie korwa ase etaabu; bionsi nigo biabwatanirie nekerogo keria. Kera ensemo y’ekerogo mbiarengeo ebisiirero ase arenge gotoorera amaboko, na emegwekano ye chindo ebere yatenenirieo, oyomo ase kera ensemo. ⅩⅨ Na emegwekano ye chindo ikomi na ibere yatenenirie aroro, oyomo ase kera ensemo ya kera ekeriinero. Omogwekano bw’ekerogo ekio tona koroisigwa ase obogambi bonde bwonsi.
ⅩⅩ Ebikombe bionsi bi’okonywera bi’omorwoti Sulemani nigo biarenge ebie chitaabu, na ebikorero bionsi bi’enyomba ye Rinani ria Lebanoni nigo biarenge bie chitaabu chichenire. Ase amatuko a Sulemani chifeta tichiabarire ng’a negento kie rigori rinene. ⅩⅪ Chimeeri chi’omorwoti nigo chiarenge kogenda Tarisisi chibogoretie abasomba ba Huramu. Ara rimo ase emiaka etato chimeeri chia Tarisisi nigo chiarenge gocha korenta chitaabu, ne chifeta, na amaino e chinchogu, naende chikaba chikorenta chingoge ne chinyoni chikorokwa chinchimbo.
ⅩⅫ Ase igo omorwoti Sulemani akaba omonene kobua abarwoti bonsi b’ense, ase obonda na obong’aini. ⅩⅩⅢ Abarwoti bonsi b’ense bagacha ase Sulemani, erinde baigwe obong’aini bwaye boria Nyasae abegete ime yaye. ⅩⅩⅣ Kera oyomo obo akaba okorenta ebiegwa biaye: ebinto bie chifeta na ebie chitaabu, ne chianga, na ebirwanero, na ebinsari, amo ne chibarasi ne chibarasi. Naboigo ekaba omwaka ase omwaka.
ⅩⅩⅤ 9:25: 1 Abar 4:26Sulemani nigo abwate ameeri chilifu inye (4,000) aye chibarasi, ne chigari, ne chibarasi chiare koriinwa chilifu ikomi na ibere (12,000). Echinde ase echio nigo chiabegetwe ase emechie ye chigari, na echinde ase omorwoti amenyete agwo Yerusalemu. ⅩⅩⅥ 9:26: Omoch 15:18; 1 Abar 4:21Akagambera abarwoti bonsi babegetwe kogamba korwa orooche rw’Eufarati goika ase ense y’Abafilisti, na goika orobebe rw’Abamisiri. ⅩⅩⅦ Omorwoti akarenta chifeta chinyinge agwo Yerusalemu, chikabucha buna amagena, ne chibao chi’emecharake nigo chiarenge chinyinge buna echi’emeko yarenge ase ense omweya. ⅩⅩⅧ 9:28: Okoir 17:16Chibarasi chikarentwa ase Sulemani korwa Misiri na korwa ase chinse chionsi.
Amang’ana ase obweng’e igoro y’obogambi bwa Sulemani
(1 Abar 11:41-43)
ⅩⅩⅨ Bono amang’ana ande onsi Sulemani akorete, korwa omochakano goika omoerio, nigo ariikire ase amariko a Nathani omobani, na ase obobani bw’Ahiya korwa Silo, na ase oborori bwa Ido omoorori igoro ya Yeroboamu, mosinto o Nebati. ⅩⅩⅩ Sulemani akagamba agwo Yerusalemu igoro y’Abaisraeli bonsi ase emiaka emerongo ene. ⅩⅩⅪ Erio Sulemani akarara ase chisokoro chiaye chiaraarete, agatindekwa ase Omochie o Daudi ise, na Rehoboamu, omwana oye, akaba okogamba ase ribaga riaye.
