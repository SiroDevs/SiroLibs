ⅩⅩⅣ
Yoshua ogokwana nabaanto agwo Sekemu
Ⅰ Yoshua agasangereria ebisaku bionsi bi’Abaisraeli agwo Sekemu. Akarangeria abagaaka b’Abaisraeli, na abatang’ani, na abagaambi, amo nabaraai babo, nabarabwo bagacha ase obosio bwa Nyasae. Ⅱ 24:2: Omoch 11:27Erio Yoshua agakwana nabaanto bonsi, akabateebia, “Aya nar’Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli; agokwana: Agwo kare chisokoro chiaino amo na Tera, ise Aburaamu, na Nahori, nigo bamenyete ng’umbu y’orooche rw’Eufarati bakaba bagokorera chinyasae ching’ao. Ⅲ 24:3: a Omoch 12:1-9; b Omoch 21:1-3Korende inche nkarusia Aburaamu, sokoro yaino, korwa ng’umbu y’orooche orwo, nkamotang’ana, ere agaeta ase ense engima ya Kaanani; nkamenta oroiboro rwaye. Nkamoa Isaka, Ⅳ 24:4: a Omoch 25:24-26; b Omoch 36:8; Okoir 2:5; c Omoch 46:1-7na Isaka nere nkamoa Yakobo na Esau. Esau nkamoa ense y’ebitunwa ya Edomu ekaba eyaye, korende Yakobo na abana baye bagatirimboka gochia Misiri. Ⅴ 24:5: Okong’ 3:1—12:42Erio ngatoma Musa na Aroni, ngaaka Abamisiri namaakwa aya nabarenterete, na magega y’ayio nkabarusia inwe aroro. Ⅵ 24:6: Okong’ 14:1-31Nkarusia chisokoro chiaino korwa Misiri, mogaika ase Enyancha Embariri, Abamisiri bakababwatia goikao ne chigari ne chibarasi. Ⅶ Erio chisokoro chiaino chikarera ase ’nde inche Omonene, mbakonye, na inche nkabeka omosunte ase egati yaino na Abamisiri. Nkaonchora enyancha egatuba Abamisiri ekabamera. Ee, ’mwaroche namaiso aino aya nakorete ase Abamisiri. Erio ase engaki entambe mokamenya ase erooro.
Ⅷ 24:8: Omob 21:21-35“Magega y’ayio nkabarenta ase ense y’Abaamori, baria bamenyete moocha ya Yorodani. Barabwo bakabarwania, korende inche nkababeka ase amaboko aino. Inwe mokaira ense yabo, na inche nkabasiria ase amasio aino. Ⅸ 24:9: Omob 22:1—24:25Erio Balaki, omorwoti o Moabu, mosinto o Sipori, akaimoka kobarwania inwe Abaisraeli. Akarangeria Baalamu, mosinto o Beori, achiche kobaragereria, Ⅹ korende tinancha koigwera Baalamu. Ase ayio akabasesenia, nainche nkabatooria korwa ase amaboko a Balaki. Ⅺ 24:11: a Yosh 3:14-17; b Yosh 6:1-21Mokaamboka Yorodani, mogaika Yeriko. Abanto ba Yeriko bakabarwania, naboigo Abaamori, na Abaperisi, na Abakanaani, na Abahiti, na Abagirigasi, na Abahivi, na Abayebusi bakorete, korende nkababeka ase amaboko aino. Ⅻ 24:12: Okong’ 23:28; Okoir 7:20Ngatoma ebiaramba bikabarasa, na ase ebio abarwoti babere b’Abaamori bagasirigwa bosio bwaino, tari ase emioro yaino, gose ase amata aino. ⅩⅢ 24:13: Okoir 6:10,11Nkabaa ense ase motakorete emeremo, na emechie motaagachete. Nigo momenyete ase emechie eyio na koria okwama kw’emesabibu na okw’emezeituni motasimegete.
ⅩⅣ “Ase igo moiroke Omonene na komokorera ase okoigwera na ase obwegenwa. Rusia korwa ase more chinyasae chiria chisokoro chiaino chiare gokorera aaria ng’umbu y’orooche rw’Eufarati na ase ense ya Misiri; mokorere Omonene bweka. ⅩⅤ Kerabe timogokiri gokorera Omonene, rirorio mwechorere reero oyo mwanchete gokorera, onye ne chinyasae chiria chisokoro chiaino chiakorerete ng’umbu y’orooche rw’Eufarati, gose chinyasae chi’Abaamori, baria momenyete ase ense yabo. Korende inche amo nabaanto b’enyomba yane ntomokorere Omonene.”
ⅩⅥ Abanto abwo bakairaneria, “Taibo. Titorigeti gotiga Omonene na gokorera chinyasae ching’ao. ⅩⅦ Naki Omonene, Nyasae oito, nere otong’anyetie intwe amo ne chisokoro chiaito korwa Misiri, ense eria ase twarenge abasomba. Nere okorete ebikone ebinene ase amasio aito, naende nere otorendete aande onsi twaetete ase egati y’abanto aba bonsi. ⅩⅧ Omonene agatosereria Abaamori aba bonsi abamenyete ase ense eye. Ase ayio, ntorigetie komokorera Omonene. Ere nere Nyasae oito.”
ⅩⅨ Yoshua agateebia abanto, “Timokonyara komokorera Omonene. Ere Nyasae omochenu, na Nyasae bwe risemi; takobaabera ogosaria kwaino na ebibe biaino. ⅩⅩ Moise gotiga Omonene na gokorera chinyasae ching’ao, nere neonchore abatige, nabae egesusuro na abasirie, magega kabakoreire amaya ritang’ani.”
ⅩⅪ Erio abanto abwo bakamoiraneria, “Taibo! Intwe inatomokorere Omonene.”
ⅩⅫ Yoshua akabateebia, “Inwe mwebereire birori ng’a mwachorire Omonene.”
Bakairaneria, “Ee, twebereire birori intwe abanyene.”
ⅩⅩⅢ Akabateebia, “Rusia chinyasae chingeni echire ase egati yaino, moonchore chinkoro chiaino ase Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Abanto bakairaneria Yoshua, “Omonene, Nyasae oito, nere torakorere, na amang’ana aye naro toraigwere.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Ase rituko riria Yoshua agakora okobwatana kw’ogotianania nabaanto abwo, na agwo Sekemu akabaa amachiiko na emeroberio barabwatie.
ⅩⅩⅥ Yoshua akarika amang’ana aria ase Egetabu ki’amachiiko a Nyasae. Erio agatenenia rigena rinene agwo inse y’Omoaluni, oteneine ang’e n’Obomenyo Obochenu bw’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅦ Agateebia abanto bonsi, “Rigena eri nigo rirabe kirori ase tore, riaigure amang’ana onsi ay’Omonene akwana naintwe. Ase igo riabeire kirori igoro yaino, erinde mobe abegenwa ase Nyasae oino.” ⅩⅩⅧ Erio Yoshua akaa abanto ribaga bakagenda kera oyomo ase omwando oye.
Yoshua na Eleazari bagokwa
ⅩⅩⅨ Magega y’ayio Yoshua, mosinto o Nuni, omosomba bw’Omonene, agakwa gakoorire emiaka rigana, na ikomi (110). ⅩⅩⅩ 24:30: Yosh 19:49,50Bakamotindeka ase omochie bw’omwando oye, agwo Timunati‐Sera, ase ense y’ebitunwa y’Efraimu, sugusu y’egetunwa kia Gaasi.
ⅩⅩⅪ Ase amatuko a Yoshua Abaisraeli bakaba bagokorera Omonene, naende bagakora boigo ase amatuko ’abagaaka baria batigarete magega y’ogokwa kwa Yoshua, abwo bamanyete amang’ana ay’Omonene akorete ase Abaisraeli.
ⅩⅩⅫ 24:32: Omoch 33:19; 50:24,25; Okong’ 13:19; Yoh 4:5; Ogok 7:16Amauga a Yusufu, aya Abaisraeli barentete korwa Misiri, agatindekwa agwo Sekemu, ase oboremo bw’ense eria Yakobo agorete korwa ase basinto ba Hamori, ise Sekemu, ase rigori rie chifeta rigana erimo (100). Oboremo obo bokaba omwando bw’ororeria rwa Yusufu.
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Na Eleazari, mosinto o Aroni, agakwa, agatindekwa Gibea, omochie oyio Finehasi, omwana oye, aetwe ase ense y’ebitunwa y’Efraimu.
