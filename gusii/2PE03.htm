Eira y’ogocha kw’Omonene
Ⅰ Abanchani, riruube eri neria kabere nabarikeire inwe. Ase onsi abere nabokirie ebirengererio biaino ebiya ase okobainyoria, Ⅱ monyare koinyora amang’ana aria akwanetwe kare nababani abachenu, na ogochika kw’Omonene na Omotooria abarenterete goetera ase abatomwa baino. Ⅲ 3:3: Yuda 1:18Ritang’ani momanye ring’ana eri, ng’a ase amatuko omoerio abanto abanyacheche b’ogosekereria inabache, baria bagotunyana okogania kwabo abanyene, Ⅳ bagoteeba, “Ng’ai bore oboikeranu bw’eira eria y’ogocha kwaye? Naki korwa agwo chisokoro chiaito chiaraarete ebinto bionsi nabo bigendererete engencho eyio eyio, buna biarenge korwa omochakano bw’ogotongwa.” Ⅴ 3:5: Omoch 1:6-9Naki nigo bamiametie amaiso abo tibarora ng’a igoro nao yarenge korwa kare, na ense egatongwa korwa ase amaache, na goetera ase amaache, ase chinguru chi’ering’ana ria Nyasae. Ⅵ 3:6: Omoch 7:11Ase ayio, ense eria y’ekero keria egasira ase etoigo ime. Ⅶ Korende igoro ya bono amo na ense eye biagacheirwe omorero ase ring’ana erio erio, birendwe goika rituko ri’ogosirigwa kw’abaanto abatamoirogeti Nyasae.
Ⅷ 3:8: Zab 90:4Korende abanchani, timweba ring’ana eri, ng’a ase Omonene rituko erimo nigo rire buna emiaka elifu, na emiaka elifu buna rituko erimo. Ⅸ Omonene tagoteebana goikerania, eira yaye, buna abande bakagete ng’a otebanire, korende nigo aremereretie ase engencho yaino, tancheti monto onde bwensi asire, korende bonsi baikere okoonchoka. Ⅹ 3:10: Mat 24:43; Luk 12:39; 1 Abats 5:2; Okom 16:15Korende rituko ri’Omonene nigo rirache buna omoibi agoocha. Ase rituko erio igoro neruo negeturi ekenene, na ebitongwa bia igoro inabisire nomorero ogotetenya, na ense na ebinto ebire aroro inabiyie.
Ⅺ Naboigo, ekiagera ebinto ebi bionsi nabisire, nebagwenerete inwe mobe abanto b’orogendo oroochenu na abe chingencho chi’obonyasae. Ⅻ Nebagwenerete gotengera na koayereria rituko ria Nyasae riiche bwango. Ase rituko riria igoro nesirigwe na koyia, na ebitongwa bia igoro inabinyititoke nomorero omonene. ⅩⅢ 3:13: Isaya 65:17; 66:22; Okom 21:1Korende buna atorierete eira ntotengerete igoro enyia na ense enyia, ase ebio oboronge mboreo ime yabo.
ⅩⅣ Ase ayio abanchani, ekiagera motengerete ebio, kora omokia, erio moche kororekana asare more nomorembe, motari na kemoa gose na komocha. ⅩⅤ Na bara okoremereria kw’Omonene oito ng’a nogotooreka. Naboigo noyominto omwanchwa, Paulo, abarikerete koreng’ana nobong’aini aetwe. ⅩⅥ Igoro y’amang’ana ayio ariikire ase amarube aye onsi. Imeo nare amang’ana ande ayare amakong’u koyamanya, ayio abanto batorokereretigwe buya na batateneini bokong’u bakoonchorana, buna bakoonchorana amariiko ande, na igo bakwerentera ogosira. ⅩⅦ Gaki, abanchani, komwatang’anire komanya aya, erende timobaisa gotantanigwa nogosira kw’abwo bagosaria amachiiko, na igo mobaise kogwa korwa ase ogwetenenera kwaino. ⅩⅧ Korende mokine ase obuya na ase okomomanya Omonene na Omotooria oito Yeso Kristo.
Obonene nobwaye bono na goika kare na kare. Amina.
