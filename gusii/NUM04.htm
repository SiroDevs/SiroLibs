Ⅳ
Emeremo Y’Abakohati
Ⅰ Omonene agakwana na Musa na Aroni, akabateebia, Ⅱ “Kora omobaro bw’Abalawi baria bare be chiamate chia Kohati, eamate ase eamate, na enyomba ase enyomba, Ⅲ abwo bare egati y’emiaka emerongo etato na merongo etano, abwo bonsi baranyare gokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. Ⅳ Emeremo Abakohati barakore agwo ase Eema eria nokorenda ebinto bire ebichenu mono.
Ⅴ “Ekero abanto barabe bakomanora na koimoka korwa ase ebitwori, Aroni amo n’abamura baye babe bagosoa ase Eema ime, basungore egetambaa keria getubete Esanduki eria ere ne chibao chiriikire oborori, na gotuba Esanduki eria negetambaa ekio. Ⅵ Naende batube egetambaa ekio ne risankwa ri’eng’iti ya rooche, na igoro ase risankwa erio baare egetambaa kere buru kegima. Erio basoyie emiaro y’okobogoreria Esanduki eria.
Ⅶ “Igoro ase emesa yemegati ekoruegwa ase Omonene baare egetambaa kere buru kegima, naende babeekeo chinteru, ne chisani ase ubani, amo n’ebitaabo n’ebitoono, ase ebio bagochia koumora okoruegwa kw’ebinyugwa. Emegati ekoruegwa botambe ase Omonene ebekweo boigo. Ⅷ Baare egetambaa ekebariri igoro ase ebinto ebio, bagetube ne risankwa ri’eng’iti ya rooche, na basoyie emiaro y’okobogoreria emesa eria.
Ⅸ “Erio baimokie egetambaa kere buru kegima, batube ekebeekerero gie chitaaya amo ne chitaaya chiaye, na ebinachi, ne chindogio chiaye, ne chichuba chire n’amaguta y’amazeituni, ebinto bigokorerwa ase emeremo y’obokuani. Ⅹ Nababeeke ekebeekerero ekio amo nebikorero biaye ase omobuko bwe risankwa ri’eng’iti ya rooche, na kobibeka ase ekebogorerio.
Ⅺ “Naende baare egetambaa kere buru kegima igoro ase egesasiimero keroisirie korwa ase etaabu, na bagetube ne risankwa ri’eng’iti ya rooche; erio basoyie emiaro y’okobogoreria egesasiimero keria. Ⅻ Nabaimokie ebinto bionsi bigokorerwa ase emeremo agwo ase Obomenyo Obochenu. Babibeke ime ase egetambaa kere buru kegima; erio babitube ne risankwa ri’eng’iti ya rooche na kobibeka ase ekebogorerio. ⅩⅢ Barabwo babuuche ribu, naende batube egesasiimero negetambaa kere monyinga. ⅩⅣ Igoro ase egesasiimero ekio babeeke ebikorero bionsi ebio bigokorerwa ase ogosasiima: chindogio chi’okorenta omorero, ne chigami, na ebichiiko, ne chisani, naende baare risankwa ri’eng’iti ya rooche igoro ase ebio bionsi, babitube. Erio basoyie emiaro y’okobogoreria egesasiimero keria. ⅩⅤ Ekero engaki eraike, abanto barabe bakomanora na koimoka korwa ase ebitwori, na ekero Aroni na abamura baye bakoorire gotuba Eema y’Omosangererekano, amo n’ebinto bionsi bireo, Abakohati bachiche babogorie ebinto ebi ebichenu, korende tibabikuna. Baise kobikuna, nigo barakwe.
“Emeremo y’okobogoria ebinto ebio bi’Eema y’Omosangererekano nigo erabe ey’Abakohati.
ⅩⅥ “Eleazari, omomura o Aroni Omokuani, nere oratenenere amaguta e chitaaya, na ubani egotioka buya, na okoruegwa kw’endagera gwa kera rituko, na amaguta akweakwa. Ase igo emeremo yaye ebe ey’okorenda Obomenyo Obochenu, na ebinto bionsi ebichenu, na ebinto bionsi ebichenu ebire imeo.”
ⅩⅦ Omonene agateebia Musa na Aroni, ⅩⅧ “Timotiga abanto be chiamate chia Kohati basirigwe korwa ase Abalawi. ⅩⅨ Ase ayio, ekero barabe bagoika ang’e n’ebinto biria ebichenu mono, Aroni na abamura baye basoe aroro, bae kera oyomo obo emeremo, na komoteebia keria arabe ogokora na kobogoria. Erio nario barabe moyo, tibakwa. ⅩⅩ Korende Abakohati baise gosoa na korora ebinto biria ebichenu, nonya netagika ensinini, nigo barakwe.”
Emeremo Y’Abagerisoni
ⅩⅪ Omonene agateebia Musa, ⅩⅫ “Kora omobaro bw’Abalawi baria bare b’ororeria rwa Gerisoni, eamate ase eamate, na enyomba ase enyomba. ⅩⅩⅢ Oriike abasacha bonsi abare egati y’emiaka emerongo etato na merongo etano, abwo bonsi baranyare gokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. ⅩⅩⅣ Eye nero emeremo abanto be chiamate chia Gerisoni barakore naende buna barabe bakobogoria ebinto ebi: ⅩⅩⅤ Barabwo mbabogorie ebitambaa bi’Obomenyo Obochenu amo nebitubo bi’Eema eria, ne risankwa ri’eng’iti ya rooche rikobituba, na egetambaa ki’egesoero ki’Eema eria, ⅩⅩⅥ na ebitambaa bi’eraaro eetanaine Obomenyo boria na egesasiimero, na ebitambaa bi’egesoero ki’eraaro, ne chingori chionsi chireo amo nebikorero bigokorerwao. Barabwo bakore emeremo ende yonsi eganeirie gokorwa ase ebinto ebio. ⅩⅩⅦ Aroni na abamura baye nabwo barachike Abagerisoni ebinto bionsi barabe bakobogoria, na onsi aya barakore; inwe mobatige batenenere ebinto bionsi barabe bakobogoria. ⅩⅩⅧ Itamari, omomura o Aroni Omokuani, nere oratenenere emeremo abanto be chiamate chi’Abagerisoni barabe bagokora agwo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano.”
Emeremo Y’Abamerari
ⅩⅩⅨ Omonene agateebia Musa, “Kora omobaro bw’Abalawi baria bare ab’ororeria rwa Merari, eamate ase eamate na enyomba ase enyomba. ⅩⅩⅩ Oriike abasacha bonsi abare egati y’emiaka emerongo etato na merongo etano, abwo bonsi baranyare gokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. ⅩⅩⅪ Emeremo yabo ase Eema eria ebe ey’okobogoria ebinto ebi: Chibao chi’Obomenyo Obochenu, na emeriibo, ne chisiiro amo ne chinterekerero chiabo, ⅩⅩⅫ ne chisiiro chi’eraaro amo ne chinterekerero chiabo, na ebirochi, ne chingori, amo nebikorero bionsi bigokorerwao. Obaatorere ebinto bionsi barabwo barabe bakobogoria. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Eye nero emeremo y’ abanto be chiamate chia Merari barabe bagokora agwo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano, inse y’oboteneneri bwa Itamari, omomura o Aroni Omokuani.”
Omobaro bw’Abalawi
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Musa, na Aroni, na abaraai b’omoganda bagakora omobaro bw’ororeria rwa Kohati, eamate ase eamate, na enyomba ase enyomba. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Bakariika abasacha bonsi abaare egati y’emiaka emerongo etato na merongo etano, abwo baare konyara gokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Abanto abwo barigetwe ase chiamate chiabo nigo barenge chilifu ibere, amagana atano abere, nemerongo etano (2,750). ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Oyio n’oro omobaro bwe chiamate chi’Abakohati abwo baare konyara gokora emeremo agwo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano, abwo Musa na Aroni baabarete, buna Omonene achiigete Musa.
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Oyo n’oro omobaro bw’ororeria rwa Gerisoni, eamate ase eamate, na enyomba ase enyomba. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Abanto abwo baare egati y’emiaka emerongo etato na merongo etano, naende barenge konyara gokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano, bakariikwa. ⅩⅬ Abwo nigo barenge abanto chilifu ibere, amagana atano nomo na merongo etato (2,630). ⅩⅬⅠ Oyo n’oro omobaro bwe chiamate chi’Abagerisoni, abarigetwe, abwo baare konyara gokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano, abwo Musa na Aroni baabarete, buna Omonene achiigete.
ⅩⅬⅡ Oyo n’oro omobaro bw’ororeria rwa Merari, eamate ase eamate, na enyomba ase enyomba. ⅩⅬⅢ Abasacha abwo baare egati y’emiaka emerongo etato na merongo etano, naende barenge konyara gokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano, bakariikwa. ⅩⅬⅣ Abwo nigo barenge abanto chilifu isato, n’amagana abere (3,200). ⅩⅬⅤ Oyo n’oro omobaro bwe chiamate chi’Abamerari, abwo Musa na Aroni baabarete, buna Omonene achiigete Musa.
ⅩⅬⅥ Musa, na Aroni, na abaraai b’Abaisraeli nigo baabarete Abalawi bonsi eamate ase eamate, na enyomba ase enyomba. ⅩⅬⅦ Abwo abaare egati y’emiaka emerongo etato na merongo etano, naende barenge konyara gokora emeremo ase Eema y’Omosangererekano, ⅩⅬⅧ nigo barenge abanto chilifu isano isato na amagana atano, na merongo etano netato (8,580).
ⅩⅬⅨ Abwo bonsi nigo baabaretwe buna Omonene achiigete goetera ase Musa. Kera omonto agateebigwa emeremo yaye arakore na ebinto arabogorie buna Omonene achiigete Musa.
