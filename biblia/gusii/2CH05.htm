Ⅰ 5:1: 2 Sam 8:11; 1 Amg 18:11Emeremo yonsi Sulemani akorete ase enyomba y’Omonene ekaera. Erio Sulemani akarenta ime ase enyomba eria ebinto Daudi ise aatananete: Chifeta ne chitaabu, na ebikorero bionsi, akabigacha ime ase ebigachero bi’enyomba ya Nyasae.
Esanduki y’Okobwatana ekorentwa ase Hekalu
(1 Abar 8:1)
Ⅱ 5:2: 2 Sam 6:12-15; 1 Amg 15:25-28Sulemani agasangereria abagaaka b’Abaisraeli, na abatang’ani bonsi b’ebisaku biabo, na abateneneri be chiamate chi’Abaisraeli, agwo Yerusalemu, erinde bagende korenta Esanduki ere ne chibao chiriikire okobwatana kw’ogotianania kw’Omonene, korwa ase Omochie o Daudi, noro Sayoni. Ⅲ Abasacha bonsi b’Abaisraeli bagasangererekana agwo ase omorwoti ekero kie Rituko Rinene ri’omotienyi o gatano na kabere. Ⅳ Abagaaka bonsi bagacha, na Abalawi bakaimokia Esanduki eria, Ⅴ bakayerenta amo n’Eema y’Omosangererekano, na ebikorero ebichenu bionsi biarenge ime ase Eema eria; abakuani Abalawi nabwo babirentete. Ⅵ Omorwoti Sulemani amo nomoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli, baria basangererekanete asare, bagatenena bosio bw’Esanduki eria, bakaruera aroro ebing’wanso bie ching’ondi ne chiombe chinyinge mono chitarenge konyara kobareka. Ⅶ Bono abakuani baria bakarenta Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana kw’Ogotianania kw’Omonene, bakayesoyia aase aye, ase akanyomba ka ime agwo Aase Aachenu Mono, bakayebeka inse ye chimbaaba chi’ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba. Ⅷ Ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba nigo baramborete chimbaaba chiabo igoro y’aase aria Esanduki eria yarenge, bagatuba Esanduki eria amo nemiaro yaye yare kobogorerigwa. Ⅸ Emiaro eria nigo yarenge emetambe, goika chinsinyo chiabo chikarorekana korwa Aase Aachenu bosio bw’Aase Aachenu Mono, korende tiyarenge kororekana korwa isiko; ero nao ere goika reero. Ⅹ 5:10: Okoir 10:5Egento kende ekeao tikiarenge ime ase Esanduki eria, otatiga chibao chiria ibere Musa abegete imeo agwo Horebu, aroro ase Omonene akorete okobwatana kw’ogotianania n’Abaisraeli, ekero barwete ase ense ya Misiri.
Obonene bw’Omonene
Ⅺ 5:11: 1 Amg 16:34; 2 Amg 7:3; Ezra 3:11; Zab 100:5; 106:1; 107:1; 118:1; 136:1; Yer 33:115:11: Okong’ 40:34,35Abakuani bonsi abarenge aroro ekero keria konya bechenire abanyene, nonya barenge b’ekeombe kende gionsi. Bono ekero abakuani bonsi basogete korwa ase Aase Aachenu, Ⅻ Abalawi bonsi abateri, nabwo Asafu, na Hemani, na Yedutuni, amo nabana babo abamura na abanto bamwabo babegete chianga chi’egatani engiya, babwate chisani barenge goakania na kwogia, na amakano, na amakano ’ebirigisa, bagatenena ensemo ya moocha y’egesasiimero, amo nabakuani rigana erimo, na emerongo ebere, ababugia ebitureeri. ⅩⅢ Nigo yarenge emeremo y’ababugia ebitureeri na abateri baigwekane bagoterera amo ase ogotogia Omonene na komoiraneria ng’a mbuya mono. Na amariogi ’amatera aimokereretigwe igoro, na ay’ebitureeri, na aye chisani chiarenge goakanigwa kwoga, na ay’ebinto binde biare kobugigwa, ekero baare gotogia Omonene, bagoteeba,
“Omonene nomuya,
ekiagera obwanchani bwaye bwa botambe
nigo bogendererete kare na kare.”
Agwo enyomba y’Omonene egaichorwa ne riire, ⅩⅣ goika abakuani tibarenge konyara kogenderera nobokoreri bwabo ase engencho ye riire riria, ekiagera obonene bw’Omonene bwaichorete ase enyomba ya Nyasae.
