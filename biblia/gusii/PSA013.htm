Ogosaba gw’okonyora obokonyi
(Ogoteera gwa Daudi)
Ⅰ Omonene, ngoika ririri oragenderere konyeba? Ngoika kare na kare?
Ngoika ririri orabe kobisete obosio bwao ng’a tondora?
Ⅱ Ngoika ririri ndabe ndemereretie emechando ase omoyo one,
imbe nobororo ase enkoro yane rituko ase rituko?
Ngoika ririri omobisa one arabe okogokera emechando yane?
 
Ⅲ Aye Omonene, aye Nyasae one, ondigererie,
ong’iranerie ase ogosaba kwane!
Ondamokie amaiso aane,
timbaisa korara chitoro chi’amakweri.
Ⅳ Totiga omobisa one ateebe ng’a ombuire,
na ababisa baane bonsi bachengere okogwa kwane.
 
Ⅴ Ninsemerie obwanchani bwao botari koera,
enkoro yane negokere ogotooreka gokorwa asore.
Ⅵ Ningoterere aye Omonene,
ekiagera gwankoreire amaya amange.
