Pilato okoa Abayahudi ribaga baire Yeso
(Mat 27:15-31; Mar 15:6-20; Luk 23:13-25)
Ⅰ Erio Pilato akaira Yeso, akamoaka emecharichi. Ⅱ Abarwani bagatwang’a egutwa y’amagwa, bakamobekera ase omotwe, bakamobekera na eanga embariri, Ⅲ bakamochiera, bakaboora, “Masoge, Omorwoti bw’Abayahudi!” Bakamoaka chindoe. Ⅳ Pilato akaoroka naende isiko, akabateebia, “Rora, inche namorentire ase more, monyare komanya ng’a tindochi komocha konde asare.”
Ⅴ Erio Yeso agasoka isiko, obekeire egutwa eria y’amagwa na eanga eria y’oborwoti embariri. Pilato akabateebia, “Rora omonto oyo!”
Ⅵ Erio abakuani abanene na abarendi bagachia komoorora, bagaaka eriogi, bakobora, “Mobambe! Mobambe!”
Pilato akabateebia, “Moire inwe, momobambe, ekiagera inche tindochi okomocha asare.”
Ⅶ Abayahudi bakamoiraneria, “Intwe ntore nogochika, naende ase ogochika okwo nagwenerete akwe, ekiagera agwekora koba Omwana o Nyasae.”
Ⅷ Erio Pilato agachia koigwa ring’ana erio, akaba nobwoba kobua. Ⅸ Agasoa ase ekegambero, akaboria Yeso naende, “Ing’ai kwarure aye?” Korende Yeso tamoiraneria. Ⅹ Erio Pilato akamobooria, “Togokwana nainche? Tomanyeti ng’a ninde nokobua gokobamba, naende ninde nokobua kogosibora?”
Ⅺ Yeso akamoiraneria, “Anga tori nokobua konde gwonsi, onye otaeiri korwa igoro; ase ayio ere oyombegete ase amaboko ao nare nebibe ebinene kobua.”
Ⅻ Korwa agwo Pilato akarigia komosibora, korende Abayahudi bagaaka eriogi, bakaboora, “Koramosibore, aye tori omosani o Kaisari; kera oyogwekora koba omorwoti nigo are mamincha ase Kaisari.” ⅩⅢ Erio ekero Pilato achia koigwa amang’ana aya, akarenta Yeso isiko, agaikaransa igoro ase ekerogo gi’ekina, aase akorokwa Atichire n’Amagena, naende ase Ekeiberania, Gabata. ⅩⅣ Nigo yarenge Okwearigania gw’Epasaka; naende nigo yarenge koreng’ana chinsa isano nemo. Agateebia Abayahudi, “Rora omorwoti oino!”
ⅩⅤ Korende barabwo bagaaka eriogi, “Morusie, morusie, mobambe!”
Pilato akababoria, “Imobambe omorwoti oino?”
Abakuani abanene bakairaneria,
“Titori nomorwoti otatiga Kaisari.”
ⅩⅥ Erio agwo akamobeka ase amaboko abo abambwe.
Okobambwa kwa Yeso
(Mat 27:32-44; Mar 15:21-32; Luk 23:26-43)
Barabwo bakabwata Yeso. ⅩⅦ Ere agasoka bwebogoreretie omosalaba oye, goika aase akorokwa Ekeore; ase Ekeiberania akorokwa Gologotha. ⅩⅧ Bakamobamba aroro amo nabaanto bande babere, oyonde ensemo, na oyonde ensemo, na Yeso gati‐gati. ⅩⅨ Pilato akarika ekemanyererio, agakebeka igoro ase omosalaba. Igo kiarigetwe, “YESO OMONAZARETI, OMORWOTI BW’ABAYAHUDI.” ⅩⅩ Nabo amariiko ayio abange b’Abayahudi mbayasomete, ekiagera aase aria Yeso abamberetwe nigo arenge ang’e nomochie; naro nigo arigetwe ase Ekeiberania, na Ekerumi, na Ekegiriki. ⅩⅪ Erio abakuani abanene na Abayahudi bagateebia Pilato, “Torika ng’a, n’Omorwoti bw’Abayahudi, korende ng’a nigo arenge kobora: Inche nomorwoti bw’Abayahudi.” ⅩⅫ Pilato akairaneria, “Aya nariikire, nariikire.”
Ogokwa kwa Yeso
(Mat 27:45-56; Mar 15:33-41; Luk 23:44-49)
ⅩⅩⅢ Erio abarwani kobaabambire Yeso, bakabogoria chianga chiaye, bagachiatanana chinsemo inye, kera omorwani anyore ensemo yaye. Bakabogoria eganchwa nero. Korende eganchwa eyio tiyasonire; igo yabwatanirie korwa igoro goika inse. ⅩⅩⅣ 19:24: Zab 22:18Erio bagateebania, “Titoyetandora, korende toyeakere obomera, torore gose neya ng’o ekoba.” Erio ririiko riikerane, eri rigoteeba,
“Mbaatananete chianga chiane,
naende eganchwa yane mbayekorerete obomera.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Naboigo abarwani bakorete. Korende ase omosalaba o Yeso ng’ina, na ng’ina moke, Mariamu moga Kiliopa, na Mariamu Magidalena bagatenenao. ⅩⅩⅥ Erio ekero Yeso achia korora ng’ina na omworokigwa oria anchete bateneine ang’e agateebia ng’ina, “Omong’ina, rora omwana oo!”
ⅩⅩⅦ Magega y’ayio agateebia omworokigwa oria, “Rora nyoko!” Erio korwa ensa eria omworokigwa oria akamoira bwoye.
ⅩⅩⅧ 19:28: Zab 69:21; 22:15Magega ase aya Yeso, kamanyete ng’a onsi aikire gokorwa, erinde ririiko riikeranigwe, akabora, “Ninde nerang’o.”
ⅩⅩⅨ Nkiarenge aroro egetaabo giaichire chinkama, erio bakabeeka esifunchi eichire chinkama igoro ase egetonto, bakamobekera monwa. ⅩⅩⅩ Erio Yeso agachia konyora chinkama, agateeba, “Yaikeranire!” Agatureka omotwe, agatweka mo.
Yeso okobetwa ritimo na koirwa gotindekwa
ⅩⅩⅪ Igo riarenge rituko ri’Okwearigania, na Abayahudi tibarigetie emebere ebe ase emesalaba igoro ase rituko ri’Esabato, ekiagera Esabato eria nigo yarenge enene. Ase ayio bagasaba Pilato ng’a amagoro y’abaanto abwo baabambetwe abunwe, na emebere yabo erusigweo. ⅩⅩⅫ Erio abarwani bakagenda, bakabuna amagoro abo, oyo bw’eritang’ani, na oyo o kabere, abwo baabambetwe amo na Yeso. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Bagachia gochiera Yeso, bakarora ng’a oikire gokwa, tibamobuna amagoro aye. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Korende oyomo bw’abarwani akamobeta ritimo rikere riaye, akaruao bwango amanyinga na amaache. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Nere oyoyaroche akaba kirori, na oborori bwaye nobw’ekeene, nere omanyire ng’a igo agokwana ekeene, erinde inwe monyare kwegena. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ 19:36: Okong’ 12:46; Omob 9:12; Zab 34:20Naki aya nigo akoregete erinde ririiko riikerane, eriateebete, “Riuga riaye tirikobunwa.” ⅩⅩⅩⅦ 19:37: Zak 12:10; Okom 1:7Naende rituko rinde ribora, “Nabamorigererie oyobabetete.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Magega ase aya Yusufu bw’Arimathaya agasaba Pilato ribaga arusie omobere o Yeso. Ere nigo arenge omworokigwa o Yeso, korende bobisi, ekiagera airogete Abayahudi. Pilato akamoa ribaga. Igo agacha, akarusia omobere oye. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ 19:39: Yoh 3:1,2Na Nikodemo oria omochierete botuko agwo ritang’ani, nere akagenda boigo, akarenta ebinto bisang’ananirie manemane na uudi, oborito bwaye chiratili rigana erimo. ⅩⅬ Erio bakabogoria omobere o Yeso, bakayoboyia egetambaa ki’egatani, bakayoaka amaguta aria amanukato, buna yarenge engencho y’Abayahudi ase ogotindeka. ⅩⅬⅠ Naende aase aria abamberetwe norengeo omogondo, na ime ase omogondo oboina oboyia, aase monto onde konya atarabekwa. ⅩⅬⅡ Na ase engencho y’Okwearigania kw’Abayahudi bakabeeka Yeso aroro, ekiagera oboina boria bwarenge ang’e.
