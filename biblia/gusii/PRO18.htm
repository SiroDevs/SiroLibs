ⅩⅧ
Ⅰ Omonto bweanchete omonyene, nigo akweganeria ayaye oka; nigo akwanga otindia nonya nase onsi ayare amaya.
Ⅱ Omoriri tari kogokigwa nay’obong’aini, korende nigo anchete gokwana ayare ase enkoro yaye omonyene.
Ⅲ Ekero ogokora amabe kogocha, ogochaaywa nigo kogocha nakorokwo, na ogochaaywa okwo nigo kogocha n’obosooku.
Ⅳ Amang’ana akorua ase omonwa bw’omonto nigo anga buna amaache ye chindiba. Ensoko y’obong’aini nigo enga buna amaache y’orooche akominyoka.
Ⅴ Tari buya gwanchereria omokori amabe na gosaria ekiina ki’omonto omooronge.
Ⅵ Chinkwana chi’omoriri nigo chikorenta eriomana, na omonwa oye nigo okorangeria ogoakwa.
Ⅶ Omonwa bw’omoriri nigo okomorentera ogosareka, ne chinkwana chiaye nigo chikoba omoeto ase are.
Ⅷ Amang’ana y’omogenki nigo are buna endagera enyansu; naro nigo agotirimboka gochia ase enkoro ime.
Ⅸ Omonto ogokora emeremo yaye nobworo nigo are oyomwabo omosaria.
Ⅹ Erieta ri’Omonene nigo rire orwaki orokong’u; omonyene oboronge nigo akorotamera oba orendire buya.
Ⅺ Obotenenku bw’omonto omonda nigo bonga buna omochie omokong’u, naende nigo bonga orwaki orotambe gochia igoro rokomorenda.
Ⅻ Oboenenu bw’enkoro y’omonto nigo bogotang’anera ogosarigwa, na oboitongo nigo bogotang’anera ogosikwa.
ⅩⅢ Omonto okoiraneria ring’ana atarariigwa buya, obwo noboriri ase are, na nigo akonyora obosooku.
ⅩⅣ Omoika bw’oboremu nigo okogera omonto oremereria ogochaandeka, korende ning’o orayoremererie omoika ore n’omoichano?
ⅩⅤ Enkoro y’omonto omong’aini nigo ekonyora chiseemi, na ogoto kwaye nigo kogochirigia.
ⅩⅥ Ebiegwa bi’omonto nigo bikomoigorera enchera na komobeeka amo n’abanto abanene.
ⅩⅦ Omonto gekwaneire ritang’ani nigo akororekana ng’a noboronge agokwana, korende omonto omoao gachire, nigo akoorokia amang’ana ayio buna are.
ⅩⅧ Obomera nabwo bogokoora okoamererania na gwatanana abanene abwo bakoamererania.
ⅩⅨ Nigo ere aakong’u koirania ase obosani omonto omocheirwe, kobua koirania omochie omokong’u, na eriomana nigo ringa buna emekorogoto y’ebiita bi’orwaki orokong’u.
ⅩⅩ Omonto nigo agoisanekia enda yaye n’ogokwana kw’omonwa oye, okwama kw’omonwa oye kwamoigotia.
ⅩⅪ Amakweri na obogima nigo bire ase chinguru chi’oromeme, na abwo banchete korokorera emeremo ’mbanyore okwama kwaye.
ⅩⅫ Omonto onyorire omokungu omuya, oyio onyorire ekeegwa ekiya na ogwancherwa korwa ase Omonene.
ⅩⅩⅢ Ekero omonto omotaka agokwana nigo agosorora, korende omonda nigo akoiraneria ase obotindi.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omonto obwate abasani abange nigo akwerentera emechando, korende n’are omosani bw’ekeene baigwanaine kobua oyomwabo.
