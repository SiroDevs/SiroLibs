Okworokereria igoro y’oboitongo
(Mar 9:33-37; Luk 9:46-48)
Ⅰ 18:1: Luk 22:24Ase engaki eria aborokigwa bagaacha ase Yeso, bakamobooria, “Ning’o ore omonene kobua abande ase oborwoti bwa igoro?” Ⅱ Akarangeria omwana, akamobeka gati‐gati yaabo, Ⅲ 18:3: Mar 10:15; Luk 18:17akabora, “Ekeene nabateebirie: Motaonchogeti mobe buna omwana, timogosoa ase oborwoti bwa igoro nonya ng’ake. Ⅳ Gaki, onde bwensi oyo orekeyie omonyene koba buna omwana oyo, oyio nere ore omonene kobua abande ase oborwoti bwa igoro.
Ⅴ “Onde bwensi oraariganie omwana oyomo buna oyo ase erieta riane, oyio onyariganirie inche.
Ogotemwa gokora ebibe
(Mar 9:42-48; Luk 17:1-2)
Ⅵ “Onde bwensi oramochere oyomo bw’aba abake, abanyegenete, anga mbuya kobua ase omonto oria asungerwe orogena rogosia ase rigoti riaye, arutwe ase endiba y’enyancha.
Ⅶ “Obobe nobw’ense ase engencho y’amateemwa ayakorenta ebibe! Inaki amateemwa takomocha kobao, korende obobe nobwaye omonto oyio okorenta ogoteemwa! Ⅷ 18:8: Mat 5:30Na onye okoboko kwao gokogera okore ebibe, gose nokogoro kwao, konache, ogotugute are; mbuya ase ore osoe ase obogima kore omonungu, gose egesegora, kobua koba namaboko abere, na amagoro abere, na gotugutwa ase omorero o kare na kare. Ⅸ 18:9: Mat 5:29Onye neriso riao rikogera okore ebibe, orirusie, oritugute are; mbuya ase ore osoe ase obogima kore egetong’o kobua koba namaiso abere na gotugutwa ase omorero o kare na kare.
Ⅹ 18:10: Luk 19:10“Rigereria, timobaisa gochaaya oyomo bw’aba abake; naki nabateebirie inwe, nonya nabamalaika babo igoro tibanagotiga korigereria obosio bwa Tata one ore igoro. [ Ⅺ Inaki Omwana‐o‐Mwanyabaanto nigo achete gotooria egiasirete.]
Omobayeeno bw’eng’ondi ensiru
(Luk 15:3-7)
Ⅻ “Inki mokorengereria? Omonto obwate ching’ondi rigana erimo, na eyemo kerasire; Inee, tagotiga chiria emerongo kianda na kianda ase ebitunwa, achie korigia eria yasirire? ⅩⅢ Kayenyorire, nekeene nabateebirie: Nagoke ase eria kobua ase chiria emerongo kianda na kianda chitasirete. ⅩⅣ Naboigo, tari ogwancha gwa iso oino ore igoro ng’a oyomo bw’aba abake asire.
Buna orakore ase oyomino okorire bobe
(Luk 17:3)
ⅩⅤ 18:15: Luk 17:3“Oyomino karakore ebibe, genda omoteebie okomocha kwaye, aye nere, inwe mweka. Karagotegerere, igo kwamonyorire. ⅩⅥ 18:16: Okoir 19:15Korende atagotegerereti, ira omonto oyomo, gose babere, mogende asare mwensi, ng’a kera ring’ana rinyare gwancheranwa ase oborori bwa babere gose batato. ⅩⅦ Ere atabategerereti abwo, teebia ekanisa; naende ataigwereti ekanisa nero, ase ore aye tiga abe buna omonto bw’ense*18:17 bw’ense: engencho yaye oy’otegeneti gose omosiru, na buna omorusia ebango. ⅩⅧ 18:18: Mat 16:19; Yoh 20:23Nekeene nabateebirie: Ande onsi morasibe ase ense, nabe asibirwe na igoro; na ande onsi morasibore ase ense, nabe asiboirwe na igoro.
Ogosaba kw’obomo
ⅩⅨ “Naende nabateebirie: Onye babere ase more bagwancherana ase ense igoro yering’ana rinde rionsi barasabe, inabakorerwe rirorio na Tata one ore igoro. ⅩⅩ Inaki ase bare babere gose batato basangererekanire ase erieta riane, nainche nao inde agwo gati‐gati yabo.”
Omobayeeno bw’omosomba otari koiraneria ng’a mbuya mono
(Luk 17:4)
ⅩⅪ 18:21: Luk 17:3,4Erio Petero agacha, akamobooria, “Omonene, ng’ara karenga oyominto arankorere bobe, na inche ’mwabere? Ngoika chintunda isano na ibere gose?” ⅩⅫ 18:22: Omoch 4:24Yeso akamoteebia, “Tindi goteeba omwabere chintunda isano na ibere, mwabere nonya ne chintunda isano na ibere ara emerongo etano n’ebere.
ⅩⅩⅢ “Ase ayio, oborwoti bwa igoro nigo bogwekaine nomorwoti origetie gokora omobaro bw’abasomba baye. ⅩⅩⅣ Ekero achaakire gokora omobaro akarenterwa omosomba oyomo omoriete chitalanta chilifu ikomi. ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero asinyetwe goakana, omonene oye agachiika aoonigwe, ere na mokaye, na abana baye, na ebi abwate bionsi, na esiira eria eakanwe. ⅩⅩⅥ Omosomba oria akagwa inse, akamosorora, agateeba: Omonene one, ing’ira ng’ora, na inche ningoakane bionsi. ⅩⅩⅦ Omonene bw’omosomba oria agasogwa namaabera, akamosibora, akamwabera ase esiira yaye. ⅩⅩⅧ Korende omosomba oyio, ekero achia korua aroro, akarora ase abasomba bande oyomo omoriete chidinari rigana erimo, akamobwata ebigoti, akabora: Ng’akane ebi kwariete. ⅩⅩⅨ Omosomba oria agatung’ama inse, akamosorora, agateeba: Ing’ira ng’ora, na inche ningoakane. ⅩⅩⅩ Akanga, agachia akamoruta echela ime goika aakane esira eria. ⅩⅩⅪ Ekero abasomba bande bachia korora aya akoreka, bagaichana mono, bakagenda, bagateebia omonene obo onsi ayakorekire. ⅩⅩⅫ Erio omonene oye akamorangeria, akamoteebia: Aye omosomba omobe! Inche ngakwabera esira yonsi, ekiagera gwansororete. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Inee! Aye tikwagwenerete kwabera omosomba oria onde, buna nainche nakwaberete? ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Omonene oye akabea mono, akamobeka ase abarendi b’echela, goika aakane ebi ariete bionsi. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Naboigo na Tata one ore igoro arabakorere nainwe, onye timokwabera abamino ase chinkoro chiaino ime.”
