Ⅰ Omonene agateebia Musa, “Rora nagokorire obe buna Nyasae ase Farao, na Aroni akwane nere buna omobani oo. Ⅱ Aye oteebie oyomino Aroni ayio onsi nagochikiire, na ere akwane na Farao, amoteebie ng’a ae Abaisraeli ribaga barue ase ense yaye. Ⅲ 7:3: Ogok 7:36Korende ninkong’ie enkoro ya Farao; nonya nabo ninkore ebikone ebinge na amakumia amange ase ense ya Misiri. Ⅳ Korende Farao takobaigwa. Inche nindambore okoboko kwane igoro y’Abamisiri na kobaa ebisusuro ebinene, na nindusie emeganda yane, abanto baane Abaisraeli, korwa ase ense ya Misiri. Ⅴ Erio Abamisiri mbamanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene, ekero ndarambore okoboko kwane ase barabwo na koruisia Abaisraeli korwa ase bare.” Ⅵ Musa na Aroni bagakora buna Omonene abachigete. Ⅶ Ekero keria bakwanete na Farao, Musa nigo arenge nemiaka emerongo etano na etato (80), na Aroni emiaka emerongo etano na etato, na etato (83).
Enyimbo ya Aroni
Ⅷ Omonene agateebia Musa na Aroni, Ⅸ “Ekero Farao arababorie ng’a mokore egekone, erio aye Musa oteebie Aroni abogorie enyimbo yaye, ayerute inse bosio bwa Farao, na ero neonchoke ebe eng’iti.” Ⅹ Musa na Aroni bakagenda ase Farao, bagakora buna Omonene abachiigete. Aroni akaruta enyimbo yaye bosio bwa Farao na bw’abasomba baye, na ero ekaonchoka ekaba eng’iti. Ⅺ Erio Farao akarangeria abanto abanyasemi na abaragori, na abwo abaringoria ba Misiri bagakora boigo ase oborogi bwabo bwa bobisi. Ⅻ Kera oyomo obo akaruta enyimbo yaye inse, na chirochio chikaonchoka, chikaba ching’iti; korende enyimbo ya Aroni ekamera chinyimbo chiabo. ⅩⅢ Enkoro ya Farao nigo yarenge enkong’u, na buna Omonene atebeete, taigwera Musa na Aroni.
Amaakwa ikomi
Ogoakwa kw’eritang’ani: Amache akoonchoka koba amanyinga
ⅩⅣ Omonene agateebia Musa, “Farao okong’irie enkoro yaye, bwangire koa abanto aba ribaga bagende. ⅩⅤ Ase igo, genda oumerane na Farao ekero aragende rooche mambia, ogende otenene ase engegu, omoganye aroro. Enyimbo eria yaonchogete ekaba eng’iti, oyebogorie koboko kwao. ⅩⅥ Omoteebie ng’a: Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaiberania, ontomire ngoteebie ng’a oe abanto baye ribaga bagende komokorera ase erooro. Naye goika bono toraigwa. ⅩⅦ 7:17: Okom 16:4Ase ig’Omonene oteebire iga: Ase ring’ana eri ndakore, nario oramanye ng’a ninche Omonene. Rigereria, ning’ake amaache y’orooche rwa Naeli nenyimbo eye mbwate koboko kwane, na amaache aya naonchoke abe amanyinga. ⅩⅧ Ne chinswe chire ase orooche ime inachikwe, chigunde, na orooche narotioke bobe, na Abamisiri nabasinywe konywa amaache aria.”
ⅩⅨ Eri’Omonene agateebia Musa moteebie Aroni, aimokie enyimbo yaye, arambore okoboko kwaye igoro ase amaache onsi ay’Abamisiri, ase chindooche chiabo na ase ebirooche, ase amatibo onsi ne chinsoko. Amache ayio onsi aonchoke abe amanyinga. Naboigo amanyinga abe ase ense yonsi ya Misiri, nonya nase chintuba na ase chinyongo chionsi.
ⅩⅩ Musa na Aroni bagakora buna Omonene abachigete. Ase obosio bwa Farao na abasomba baye, Aroni akaimokia enyimbo yaye, agaaka orooche, na amaache ayare imeo akaonchoka, akaba amanyinga. ⅩⅪ Ne chinswe chiare ase orooche chigakwa, na orooche rokagunda na gotioka, na Abamisiri bagasinywa konywa amaache aria. Amanyinga akaba ase ense yonsi ya Misiri. ⅩⅫ Erio abaringoria ba Misiri bagakora boigo ase oborogi bwabo. Farao akagenderera gokong’ia enkoro yaye, na buna Omonene konya atebiire, tabaigwa. ⅩⅩⅢ Erio Farao akairana akogenda bwoye, amang’ana aria tayabeka ase omoyo oye. ⅩⅩⅣ Abamisiri bonsi bagakunya‐kunya mbarabare y’orooche ase okorigia amaache bakonywa, ekiagera tibare konyara konywa amaache a rooche.
ⅩⅩⅤ Amatuko atano na abere akaera korwa riri’Omonene aagete orooche rwa Naeli.
