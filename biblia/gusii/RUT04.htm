Boazi ogotwara Rutu
Ⅰ Boazi akagenda agaikaransa ase etureti*4:1 Etureti: Ase emesangererekano egokorerwa agwo ase egeita ki’omochie. Ekaba ng’a omonto oria omonyamwando bw’Elimeleki ore ang’e kobua Boazi, oria Boazi akwanete igoro yaye, ngoeta arenge. Boazi akamorangeria, akamoteebia, “Oyominto, gobera gocha aa, oikaranse!” Omonto oria akagobera, agaikaransa. Ⅱ Naende Boazi akarangeria abagaaka ikomi b’omochie baikaranse aroro, ase igo bagaikaransa. Ⅲ Erio Boazi agateebia omonyamwando oria, “Naomi, oria oirana korwa ense y’Abamoabu, nkoonia are oboremo boria bwarenge omwando bw’Elimeleki ominto. Ⅳ Inche nigo narengereretie gokomanyia amang’ana aya. Bono nonyare kobogora aiga ase amasio abagaaka b’abanto baane aba, na ase aba bonsi baikaransete igaa. Onye kogwancha kobogora, bogore, korende onye taibo, ntebie imanye, ekiagera naye ore omonyamwando ore ang’e, na magega yao ninche.”
Omonto oria akairaneria, “Ee, nimbogore.”
Ⅴ Erio Boazi akamoteebia, “Korende onye gokogora oboremo korwa ase Naomi, rirorio oire Rutu, Omomoabu oria, moka oria okwete, abe mokao, naye omotenenerie oria okwete egesaku kerarie omwando oye.”
Ⅵ Omonyamwando oria akairaneria, akabora, “Onye naboigo, rirorio tinkobogora, mbaise gosiria omwando one. Ribaga riane ri’okobogora, aye riire; tinkonyara kobogora.”
Ⅶ 4:7: Okoir 25:9Ase amatuko a kare ase Abaisraeli, ekero enibo yare koboorigwa†4:7Koboorigwa: engencho yaye nokogora, gose koonchorerigwa, engencho yabo nigo yarenge ng’a oyomo bw’abwo nigo arenge gosomora egekoroto kiaye ekemo omoa oria onde. Ayio nigo are kogera aria agambwa takoonchoranwa, na ogokora okwo kwaba kirori.
Ⅷ Ase igo omonyamwando oria agateebia Boazi, “Aye bogore”, na akamosomorera egekoroto kiaye.
Ⅸ Erio Boazi agateebia abagaaka baria na abanto bonsi abarengeo, “Inwe mbirori more reero ng’a nagorire korwa ase amaboko a Naomi bionsi ebiarenge ebi’Elimeleki, na ebia Kilioni amo nebia Mahaloni. Ⅹ 4:10: Okoir 25:5,6Amo nayio Rutu, Omomoabu oria, oyorenge moka Mahaloni, namoirire abe mokane, erinde inyare goteneneria oria okwete egesaku kerarie omwando oye, nario ororeria rwaye rotasire ase abamwabo, gose ase abanto b’omochie oye. Ase amang’ana aya mbirori more reero.”
Ⅺ 4:11: Omoch 29:31Abagaaka baria na abanto bande bonsi abarenge ase etureti agwo ase egeita ki’omochie bakairaneria, “Ee, mbirori tore. Tiga Omonene asesenie omokungu oyio orache ase enka yao abe buna Raeli na Lea, abwo baiborerete Israeli abana abange bakaba egesaku. Aye osesenigwe ase egati y’Abaefarati na omanyekane ase Bethlehemu. Ⅻ 4:12: Omoch 38:27-30Naende enka yao ebe buna eya Peresi, oria Tamari aiborerete Yuda, goetera ase oroiboro rori’Omonene arakoe korwa ase omokungu oyio.”
ⅩⅢ Ase igo Boazi akaira Rutu, na ere akaba mokaye. Erio bakamenyana, n’Omonene aganchera Rutu koba morito, na ere akaibora omwana omoisia. ⅩⅣ Erio abakungu b’omochie oyio bagateebia Naomi, “Omonene atogigwe! Ere tagotigeti; reero okoeire omonyamwando, oyio erieta riaye riramanyekane ase egati y’Abaisraeli! ⅩⅤ Nere oragotimokie, abe chinguru chiao abe ogokorageria ase obogotu bwao. Moka momura oo nigo agwanchete; ere ogokoreire amaya kobua abamura batano na babere, na bono oiboire omwana oyio.” ⅩⅥ Naomi akaimokia omwana oria, akamoagata, akaba okomorera.
ⅩⅦ Abakungu abarenge abagisangio baye bakaboora, “Naomi oiboreirwe omwana omochokoro omomura”, bakamoroka Obedi.
Obedi oyio nere orenge ise Yese, na Yese akaba ise Daudi.
Chisokoro chia Daudi
ⅩⅧ Abanto aba nabwo ororeria rwa Peresi: Peresi akaibora Hezironi; ⅩⅨ Hezironi akaibora Ramu, na Ramu akaibora Aminadabu; ⅩⅩ Aminadabu akaibora Nahesoni, na Nahesoni akaibora Salimoni; ⅩⅪ Salimoni akaibora Boazi, na Boazi akaibora Obedi; ⅩⅫ Obedi akaibora Yese, na Yese akaibora Daudi.
