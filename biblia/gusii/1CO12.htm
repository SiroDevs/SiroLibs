Ebiegwa bio ’moika omochenu
Ⅰ Ase amang’ana y’ebiegwa ebikorwa ase Omoika, baminto, tindigeti momoche komanya. Ⅱ Mwamanyire ng’a ekero mwarenge ab’Ebisaku nigo mware koirwa ase emegwekano y’ogosasimwa, eyio etarenge gokwana, buna mware gotang’anwa. Ⅲ Ase ayio nindigetie kobamanyia, ng’a monto onde tari oyogokwana ase Omoika o Nyasae ogoteeba ng’a, “Yeso oragererigwe!” Gose onde takonyara goteeba, “Yeso n’Omonene”, otatiga ase Omoika Omochenu.
Ⅳ 12:4: Abar 12:6-8Bono mbireo ebiegwa ao ao, korende Omoika noro oria oyomo. Ⅴ Naboigo nkore ogokora ao ao, korende Omonene nere oria oyomo. Ⅵ Naende nkore ogokora kw’emeremo ase chinchera ao ao, korende Nyasae nere oria oria ogokora emeremo yonsi ase bonsi. Ⅶ Korende kera oyomo oeirwe okomaanorerwa kw’Omoika, erinde bonsi bakonywe. Ⅷ Naki omonto oyomo ase Omoika aegwa ogokwana kw’obong’aini, na oyonde ring’ana ri’okomanya ase Omoika oria oyomo. Ⅸ Oyonde aegwa okwegena ase Omoika oria oria, na oyonde aegwa ekeegwa gi’okogwenia ase Omoika oria oria. Ⅹ Oyonde aegwa gokora emeremo y’ebikone bie chinguru, na oyonde obobani, na oyonde okomanya kw’ogwatanana emeika, na oyonde ogokwana kw’emenwa ao ao, na oyonde okoonchora emenwa. Ⅺ Korende ebiegwa ebi bionsi nigo bikorwa ase Omoika oyo oyomo, oria oria okogabera kera oyomo buna bwanchete.
Omobere oyomo ore nebimo ebinge
Ⅻ 12:12: Abar 12:4,5Naki buna omobere ore oyomo, oro nore nebiimo ebinge, na ebiimo bionsi bi’omobere oria, nonya bire ebinge, nigo bire omobere oyomo; naboigo na Kristo are. ⅩⅢ Naki ase Omoika oyomo intwe twensi tokabatiswa, tokaba omobere oyomo, nonya tore Abayahudi, gose tore Abagiriki, onye tore abasomba, gose tore abasibore; intwe twensi nigo twanyoisetigwe Omoika oyomo.
ⅩⅣ Naki omobere tori nekiimo ekemo, korende nebinge. ⅩⅤ Onye okogoro kogoteeba, “Ekiagera inche tindi okoboko, tindi oyo bw’omobere”, Inee, igo tikori okw’omobere ase engencho eyio? ⅩⅥ Na onye ogoto kogoteeba, “Ekiagera inche tindi eriso, inche tindi oyo bw’omobere”, Inee, igo tikori okw’omobere ase engencho eyio? ⅩⅦ Onye omobere bwonsi oba eriso, anga okoigwa ng’ai kore? Onye bwonsi nogoto, anga okonyonka ng’ai kore? ⅩⅧ Korende bono Nyasae obekire ebiimo, kera ekemo ase omobere buna arigetie. ⅩⅨ Korende onye bionsi biba ekiimo ekemo, anga omobere ng’ai ore? ⅩⅩ Bono ebiimo nebinge bire, korende omobere noyomo. ⅩⅪ Eriso tirikonyara goteebia okoboko, “Tinkoganeti”, gose omotwe tokonyara goteebia amagoro, “Timbaganeti.” ⅩⅫ Korende kobua mono, ebiimo biria bi’omobere bigokagerwa ng’a tibiri ne chinguru, nigo biganeirie kobua. ⅩⅩⅢ Na ebiimo biria bi’omobere bigokagerwa ng’a tibiri nogosika twabieire ogosika kobua; na ebiimo biaito bitari nobuya, nigo tokobiboyia buya kobua, ⅩⅩⅣ naki ebiimo biaito bire nobuya tibiganeti ayio. Korende Nyasae obwatanirie omobere, na ekiimo keria keremeire ogosika okeire ogosika kobua, ⅩⅩⅤ erinde tikoba ogwatananekana ase omobere, korende ebiimo birendane. ⅩⅩⅥ Onye ekiimo ekemo gechandegete, ebiimo bionsi nigo bichandegete nabirobio; onye ekiimo ekemo kegotogigwa, ebiimo bionsi nigo bikogoka amo.
ⅩⅩⅦ Inwe nigo more omobere o Kristo na ebiimo bi’omobere oyio, kera ekemo aase aye. ⅩⅩⅧ 12:28: Abaef 4:11Na Nyasae obekire ase ekanisa ritang’ani abatomwa, eria kabere ababani, eria gatato aborokia. Naende akabeeka abakori emeremo y’ebikone bie chinguru, abanyorire ebiegwa bi’okogwenia amarwaire, abakoonyi, abaagambi, naende abagokwana emenwa ao ao. ⅩⅩⅨ Inee! Bonsi nabatomwa? Bonsi nababani? Bonsi naborokia? Bonsi ngokora bare ebikone bie chinguru? ⅩⅩⅩ Bonsi mbare nebiegwa bi’okogwenia abarwaire? Bonsi ngokwana bare emenwa ao ao? Bonsi nkoonchora bare? ⅩⅩⅪ Rigia mono ebiegwa ebiya kobua. Naende nimborokie enchera ere engiya mono.
