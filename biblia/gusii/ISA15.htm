Nyasae nasirie Abamoabu
Ⅰ 15:1: Isaya 25:10-12; Yer 48:1-47; Ezek 25:8-11; Amos 2:1-3; Sef 2:8-11Aya naro amang’ana y’ogokumia igoro y’ense ya Moabu.
Ekiagera Ari o Moabu nosarigwe ase obotuko obomo, nosirigwe kegima! Ee, ekiagera na Kiri o Moabu osarigwe ase obotuko obomo, osirigwe kegima! Ⅱ Abanto ba Diboni batiirire gochia aase aimokererete igoro agosasimerwa, barereo. Nigo bakweumia ase engencho ya Nebo na Medeba; kera omonto okiigitire etukia yaye, ogingire ne chinderu. Ⅲ Abanto nigo beboyetie chigunia baichanete agwo ase chinchera, abande nigo bariinete ebirama igoro, na abande bare ase ebiraario; kera omonto nigo akweumia na koiyeria amariga. Ⅳ Abanto ba Hesiboni na Eleale nigo bakorera neriogi rinene. Eriogi riabo nigo rikoigweka goikera Yahasi. Ase ayio abasikari ba Moabu bakorera neriogi rinene; chinkoro chiabo chiaondokire. Ⅴ Enkoro yane nkoreera ere ense ya Moabu. Abanto baye ngotamera bare Soari na Egilati‐Selisia; nigo bagotiira gochia Luhiti bakorera. Nigo bagoaka amariogi ay’okorera kw’ogosirigwa agwo ase enchera ekogenda Horonaimu. Ⅵ Orooche rwa Nimurimu rwakamire amaache; obonyansi bwomire, na bonde tibori komera, ekemeri kende ekebese tikeri aroro. Ⅶ Ebinto ebinge konya benyoreire, ne chinibo chionsi babeire bagachete mbabibogorie bamboke nabirobio ase Orooche rw’Emesobeti. Ⅷ Eriogi ri’okorera kwabo riaichoire ase ense ya Moabu; ekerero ekio giaikire Egilaimu na Beeri‐Elimu. Ⅸ Nonya nechindooche chia Dimoni chiaichoire namanyinga, ningenderere korenta amabe ande ase Dimoni. Nindete endo eite Abamoabu, abwo bagotama na abwo batigarete ase ense yabo.
