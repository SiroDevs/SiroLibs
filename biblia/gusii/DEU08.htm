Orogongo Oruya Rokoirwa
Ⅰ “Moigwere amachiiko aya onsi nabaeire ase rituko ria reero, moyabwate, erio nario moraabe moyo na komentekana, erinde mogende moire ense eri’Omonene arierete chisokoro chiaino eira ng’a n’abayee. Ⅱ Moinyore buna Omonene Nyasae oino, abeire okobaraa ase orogeendo rwaino rwonsi ase erooro, ase engaki y’emiaka emerongo ene yaetire, ase okobakora mobe abaitongo na kobateema ase okomanya ebirengerio biaino, arore gose ’moganetie korenda amachiiko aye, gose yaya. Ⅲ 8:3: Mat 4:4; Luk 4:4Akabakora mokaba abaitongo, akabatiga mokaba n’enchara. Erio akabaa mana, endagera gete eyio inwe motamanyete, nonya ne’chisokoro chiaino chitamanyete, erinde monyaare komanya ng’a omonto takonyaara koba moyo ase endagera yoka, korende nigo akoba moyo ase kera ring’ana riikorwa ase omonwa bw’Omonene. Ⅳ Ase emiaka eyio yonsi emerongo ene, chianga chiano tichiabagoterete, gose amagoro aino tabiimbete. Ⅴ Momanye ng’a, buna omonto akoa omwana oye egesusuro ase okomoorongeyia, naboigo n’Omonene Nyasae oino, akobaa egesusuro ase okobarongeyia. Ⅵ Ase ayio mobwate amachiiko a y’Omonene Nyasae oino, ase ogotaara ase chinchera chiaye na komoiroka. Ⅶ Omonene Nyasae oino, ’nkobaira are ase ense engiya ebwate chindooche ne’chinsooko, ne’chintuboka chigosookia amaache gochia ase amagoko na ase ebitunwa. Ⅷ Ense eyio ere n’engano n’esairi, n’emesabibu, n’emeko, n’emekomomanga, n’emezeituni, boigo n’amooke. Ⅸ Ase ense eyio inamorie endagera enyinge etari kobaerera; aroro timokoremerwa gento kende gionsi. Amagena areo nigo are n’ebioma, naende ’namoremere chitaai korwa ase ebitunwa biaye. Ⅹ ’Namonyoore endagera morie moisaneke. Erio motogie Omonene Nyasae oino, ase engencho y’ense eyio engiya abaeire.
Ogokuurerwa ase ogotamweba Omonene
Ⅺ 8:11: Hos 13:5,6“Mwerende mono, timobaisa kweba Omonene Nyasae oino. Timobaisa gotiga kobwata amachiiko aye, na emeroberio yaye yonsi echiikire, aya ’nkobaa ase rituko ria reero. Ⅻ Ekero moraabe mwanyorire endagera ebaisaine na koigota, naende mwaagachire chinyomba chingiya na komenyao, ⅩⅢ na ekero amaicho aino ye’chiombe na aye’ching’ondi arabe amentekanire, ne’chifeta, ne’chitaabu chiaino boigo, n’ebinto binde bionsi mobwate biramentekane, ⅩⅣ timobaisa koenena na kweba Omonene Nyasae oino, oy’obarusetie korwa ase ense ya Misiri, ense y’obosomba. ⅩⅤ Akabaraa na kobaetia ase erooro enene eyarenge ey’okoondokia, eyarenge ne’ching’iti chingeenda inse chire n’obosongo, n’ebiuge boigo: ase erooro eria enyomo etabwate amaache ere akababochorera amaache korwa ase egetare keria egekong’u. ⅩⅥ Agwo ase erooro akabaa mana mokaria, endagera chisokoro chiaino chitamanyete, anyare kobakora mobe abaitongo na kobateema, erinde ase omoerio oino abakorere amaya. ⅩⅦ Mwerende, timobaisa gokwana ase chinkoro chiaino ime: Okobua gwaito ne chinguru chi’amaboko aito nachio chiatonyoreire obotenenku obo. ⅩⅧ Moinyore ng’a Omonene Nyasae oino, nere okobaa chinguru chi’okonyoora obotenenku, ekiagera aganetie gokong’ia okobwatana kwaye kw’ogotianania okwo akorete ne’chisokoro chiaino buna akorire goika reero iga. ⅩⅨ Komoramwebe Omonene Nyasae oino, na koonchoka gochia ase chinyasae ching’ao ase ogochikorera, na gochisasiima, nabakuureire ase rituko ria reero ng’a, ekeene, goika mosire kegima. ⅩⅩ Buna ebisaku biria, Omonene arasirie korwa ase amasio aino, naboigo nainwe morasirigwe, ekiagera motaigwerete eriogi ri’Omonene Nyasae oino.”
