Ebibe bi’abaanto ba Sodomu
Ⅰ Abamalaika baria babere bakagenda, bagaika Sodomu mogoroba, na Loti nigo areenge oikaransete geita gia Sodomu. Ekero Loti abarooche, akaimoka kobarotota, agatugama inse, akaumama, Ⅱ akabora: “Abaanene baane, gobera gocha aa, mosoe mw’omosomba oino, monyore amaache mwesibie amagoro aino, moraare goika mambia bokie; erio moche moimoke mambia, mogende.”
Barabwo bagakana, bagateeba: “Yaya, nigo tokoba aa inchera obotuko obo bwonsi.”
Ⅲ Korende akabasorora mono, na barabwo bakagobera, erio bagasoa nyomba mwaye. Erio akabaroiseria endagera, na emegati etabekiri memera, na barabwo bakaria.
Ⅳ Ritang’ani batarachia korara, abanto bonsi b’omochie oria o Sodomu, abamura na abagaaka, bagacha bagaetanana enyomba, Ⅴ 19:5: Abag 19:22-24bakarangeria Loti, bakamobooria: “Abanto abwo bacha mwao bono botuko, ng’ai bare? Basokie isiko aa, tobakore abakungu.”
Ⅵ Loti agasoka isiko ase abanto baria, agasieka egesieri korwa magega yaye, Ⅶ akabateebia: “Baminto, nabasoroire, timokora obosooku buna obo. Ⅷ Rora, nimbwate abaiseke baane babere abekungi; tiga mbaabarentere, na mobakorere buna moranche, korende abanto aba iga timobakorera ing’ana rinde rionsi, ekiagera basoire nyomba mwane ime na nigo bare inse y’oborendi bwane.”
Ⅸ Korende barabwo bakamoteebia: “Torwereo!” Naende bagateebania: “Omonto oyo nomomenyi are aa seito, na bono nigo arigetie gototeebereria! Bono nigo toragokorere bobe mono kobua buna nanga tware gokorera abanto abwo.” Erio bagatwoma‐twoma Loti mono, bakarigia kobuna egesieri. Ⅹ Korende abanto baria baarenge ime bakarambora amaboko abo, bakang’usa Loti gochia nyomba ime, na erio kegima bagasieka egesieri. Ⅺ 19:11: 2 Abar 6:18Erio bagaukoria abanto baria baarenge korwa isiko, erinde bagasinywa konyora egesieri.
Ⅻ Erio abasacha baria babere bakabooria Loti: “Nore nonde omino aa? Nobwate abana bao banyuomete abaiseke bao, gose abamura na abaiseke bao, gose kobwate bande bamino ase omochie oyo, barusie korwa aase aa. ⅩⅢ Naki intwe nigo twachire gosaria aase aa, ekiagera ekerero kiaye kiamentekire ase obosio bw’Omonene; ase ayio Omonene ototoomire tosarie aase aa.”
ⅩⅣ Erio Loti agasooka, agateebia abamura aba konya bariereire abaiseke baye boko: “Imoka bwango, morue aase aa, ekiagera Omonene ngocha are gosaria omochie oyo,” Korende barabwo bagakaga ng’a negosori akobakorera.
Loti ogotoorigwa amo nabaiseke baye, korende mokaye akoomera inchera
ⅩⅤ Bogachia gokia mambia, abamalaika baria bakaayereria Loti, bakamoteebia: “Imoka bwango, ira mokao na abaiseke bao babere abare aa amo naye, tobaisa gosirigwa ase egesusuro keria omochie oyo ogochia konyora.” ⅩⅥ 19:16: 2 Petr 2:7Ekero abeete ogoteebana, abanto abwo bakamobwata okoboko, bakabwata na mokaye na abaiseke baye, ekiagera Omonene amororerete amaabera, akamosokia isiko y’omochie. ⅩⅦ Ekero baabasookirie isiko, oyomo obo akabateebia: “Tama, motoorie obogima bwaino; timorigereria magega, gose timotenena aande onsi ase ense eraire. Motame gochia ase ebitunwa, timobaisa gosira.”
ⅩⅧ Loti akabakania, akabateebia: “Taibo, Omonene one; ⅩⅨ rora omosomba oo onyorire ogwancherwa ase amaiso ao, kwamentire amaabera ao ase ’nde ase ogotooria obogima bwane; korende inche tinkonyara gotama ng’ikeere ebitunwa; amabe abaise konyora, ang’ite. ⅩⅩ Rora omochie oyo ore ang’e aiga, omochie omoke, noro ndaike bwango. Tiga ntame gochia ase omochie oria, ore omoke iga, ntoorie obogima bwane.”
ⅩⅪ Ere akamoteebia: “Rora, nagwancheire ase ring’ana erio boigo. Tingosaria omochie oyio gwateeba. ⅩⅫ Ayerera, bwetoorie, ekiagera tingokora ing’ana rinde rionsi goika gwasoire omochie oria ime.”
Ase ayio, omochie oyio okarokwa Soari.
Sodomu na Gomora egosambwa na gosarigwa
ⅩⅩⅢ Erioba konya riaorokire ekero Loti asoete Soari. ⅩⅩⅣ 19:24: Mat 10:15; 11:23,24; Luk 10:12; 17:29; 2 Petr 2:6; Yuda 1:7Eri’Omonene akaruta amagena ’omorero korwa igoro akonyititoka na omorero igoro ya Sodomu na Gomora, ⅩⅩⅤ agasamba emechie eyio, amo nense eyio yonsi eraire, na agasiria bonsi abamenyete imeo, na ebinto bionsi biare gokina ase ense eria. ⅩⅩⅥ 19:26: Luk 17:32Korende moka Loti nigo areenge korwa magega, akarigereria magega, akaonchoka, akooma, akaba buna rigena ri’omonyoo.
ⅩⅩⅦ Mambia kobwakia, Aburaamu akagenda aase aria atenenete ase obosio bw’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅧ Ere akarigereria maate gochia ensemo ya Sodomu na Gomora, na ense yonsi eraire, na agwo akarora erioki rigotiira korwa inse, buna erioki rikorwa ase eriko rinene.
ⅩⅩⅨ Ekaba ekero Nyasae asaretie emechie eyio y’ense eraire, akaba okoinyora Aburaamu, akarusia Loti ase ogosarigwa koria, ekero asambete emechie eyio ase Loti amenyete.
Omochaki bwe chinderia chi’Abamoabu na Abaamoni
ⅩⅩⅩ Loti agatiira korwa Soari, agacha akamenya ase ebitunwa amo nabaiseke baye babere, ekiagera nigo oboete komenya Soari; akamenya ase rikuruma amo nabaiseke baye babere. ⅩⅩⅪ Omoiseke oye omotangi agateebia oyomwabo omoke: “Tata ogotire, gose monto onde tari ase ense oyorache gotonyuoma buna engencho y’ense yonsi ere. ⅩⅩⅫ Bono, tiga toe tata edivai anywe, atinde, toraare nere, erinde tomotenenerie egesaku kiaye, tigesira.” ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Bakanyoinsia ise edivai ase obotuko boria; omoiseke oria omotangi agachia, akaraara nere, ise tamanya ekero araara, gose ekero aboka.
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Rituko rikobwatia omoiseke oria omotangi agateebia oria omoke omwabo: “Rora, obotuko bwa igoro nigo naraire na Tata; tomonyooinsie naende edivai ase obotuko obo, erio naye ochie, oraare nere, erinde tomotenenerie egesaku kiaye, tigesira.” ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Bakamonyoinsia naende edivai ase obotuko obwo. Omoiseke oria omoke akaimoka, akaraara nere, ise tamanya ekero araara, gose ekero aboka. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Naboigo abaiseke abwo babere ba Loti bakorete iga, ekagera bakaba barito korwa ase ise. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Ase igo omoiseke oria omotangi akaibora omwana omoisia, akamoroka Moabu; oyio nere sokoro bw’Abamoabu goika reero iga. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Na moiseke oria omwabo omoke, nere akaibora omwana omoisia, akamoroka Ben‐Ami; oyio nere sokoro bw’Abaamoni goika reero iga.
