Ogosaba ng’a Nyasae ae abanyabibe egesusuro
(Ogoteera gwa Daudi)
Ⅰ Inwe abamokogamba, Inee, ngokwana more amang’ana ’ekeene?
Inee! Nkonachera more abanto ebiina bire boronge?
Ⅱ Yaya, nigo more nemeroberio emekorekanu ime ase chinkoro chiaino,
amaboko aino nigo agokora obotindi bw’oborianania ase ense.
 
Ⅲ Abakori amabe nigo basirete korwa chinda chi’abang’ina,
abanyaborimo nigo bakoba abatantanu korwa engaki y’okoiborwa kwabo.
Ⅳ Barabwo nigo babwate obosongo bonga buna obw’eng’iti,
nigo banga buna rirubi rigoticha amato aye,
Ⅴ erinde tiriigwa amariogi abanyamosira,
gose ay’abaebia b’obong’aini.
 
Ⅵ Aye Nyasae, obune amaino ’emenwa yabo;
aye Omonene, gokora amagegu e chindo chinsae!
Ⅶ Abanto abwo tiga basirigwe kegima buna amaache omogooko agoeta asira;
ekero bakoruta emegwe yabo, eburoke.
Ⅷ Barabwo tiga babe buna amakorominyo akonyititoka amarenda aera,
na buna oborito bw’omokuungu bokogwa botaraika engaki yaye.
Ⅸ Omonene nabasirie bwango,
ee, bwango kobua chinyongo chiaino chitarabwata riberera ri’omorero bw’amagwa,
karabe amabese gose amasusanu.
 
Ⅹ Omonyene oboronge nagoke ekero egesiomba kegoakanwa,
natiringe amanyinga ’abaanto abanyabibe.
Ⅺ Erio abanto bateebe,
“Ekeene, abanyaboronge nigo bakonyora eng’eria yabo;
ekeene, Nyasae nareo okonacha ebiina ase ense.”
