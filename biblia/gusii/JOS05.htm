Ⅰ Abarwoti bonsi b’Abaamori, abamenyete bosongo ya Yorodani, na abarwoti bonsi b’Abakanaani, abamenyete ang’e n’Enyancha ya Mediterania*5:1 Enyancha ya Mediterania: Nero enyancha ya Meditarenia, bakaigwa buna Omonene akamogetie amaache a Yorodani bosio bw’Abaisraeli goika bakaamboka. Ase igo bakoboa mono na kwerusia moyo ase engencho y’Abaisraeli.
Okwaroka agwo Giligali
Ⅱ Ase engaki eyi’Omonene agateebia Yoshua, “Roisia emeyio emioge y’amagena ochake kwarokia Abaisraeli naende.” Ⅲ Erio Yoshua akaroisia emeyio y’amagena na akarokia Abaisraeli agwo ase Egetunwa gekorokwa Gibeati‐Haraloti.†5:3 Gibeati‐Haraloti: Nakio egetunwa kia Gibeati Haraloti Ⅳ 5:4: Omob 14:28-35Aya naro agerete Yoshua akarokia: Abasacha bonsi b’esegi, abwo bang’anyete korwa Misiri, konya bakwereire ase erooro kobare orogendo rwabo korwa Misiri. Ⅴ Abanto baria baruete Misiri konya barokigwe bonsi, korende ase abwo baiboreretwe ase orogendo rw’erooro, onde tarenge konya bwarokigwe, nonya noyomo. Ⅵ Abaisraeli konya bataarire ase erooro ase engaki y’emiaka emerongo ene, goika abanto bonsi abaare konyara korwana esegi konya bakuure, ekiagera bangete koigwera Omonene. Ase ig’Omonene agatiana ng’a takobaa ribaga ri’ogosoa ense eria konya ariereire chisokoro chiabo eira ase ogotiana ng’a natoe ense entenenku ere namabeere na amooke. Ⅶ Ase igo abana b’abaanto abwo baria baiboretwe korwa magega yabo nabw’Omone akinetie ribaga riabo nabwo konya batararokigwa kobaare orogendo ase erooro, abwo nabwo Yoshua arogetie.
Ⅷ Ekero abanto bonsi bakoorire kwarokigwa, abanto bonsi bakabeera aase abo ase egetwori goika bakagwena. Ⅸ Omonene agateebia Yoshua, “Rituko ria reero narusirie korwa ase more ogochaywa mwarenteretwe n’Abamisiri.” Ase ayio aase aaria akarokwa Giligali‡5:9Giligali: Engencho yaye ogoturungania gose amagena asangererekanete amo, naboigo akorokwa goika reero iga.
Epasaka agwo Giligali
Ⅹ 5:10: Okong’ 12:1-13Ekero Abaisraeli batoorete Giligali ase ebirubo ebiomo bia Yeriko bakaba bakorenda rituko rinene ri’Epasaka§5:10Epasaka: Omoyega bw’emegati etabekiri memera chinsa chia mogoroba, ase rituko ria ikomi na kane ri’omotienyi. Ⅺ Ase rituko ria kabere magega kobariire Epasaka, bakaria okwama gw’ense eria: emegati etabekiri memera, na engano ekarangire, ase rituko erio erio. Ⅻ 5:12: Okong’ 16:35Magega y’ayio mana ekaera, tiyagwa, na Abaisraeli tibanyora mana naende. Korwa ase omwaka oria oria, bariete okwama gwa Kaanani.
Yoshua na omonto ore nomoyio o birende
ⅩⅢ Ekero Yoshua aigete ang’e na Yeriko akarigereria akarora omonto oteneine obwate omoyio o birende koboko kwaye. Yoshua akamoika ang’e, akamobooria, “Inee! Aye nensemo yaito ore, gose nensemo y’ababisa baito?”
ⅩⅣ Omonto oria akairaneria, “Inche nigo inde omotang’ani bw’omoganda okorwana esegi y’Omonene, nakio bono nacheire aa.”
Erio Yoshua agatung’ama inse akaumama, agasasiima. Naende akamobooria. “Mang’ana ki obwate ogonteebia omonene one, inche omosomba oo?”
ⅩⅤ Omotang’ani oria bw’omoganda okorwana esegi y’Omonene agateebia Yoshua, “Rusia ebikoroto biao, ekiagera aase agwo oteneine naachenu.” Na Yoshua agakora bo.
