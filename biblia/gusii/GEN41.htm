ⅩⅬⅠ
Chindoto chia Farao
Ⅰ Magega y’emiaka ebere koyaetire Farao akaroota chindoto ng’a oteneine engegu y’orooche rwa Naeli. Ⅱ Akarora chiombe isano na ibere, chingiya mono chinoru, chikorwa rooche, chikorisia amasangaka. Ⅲ Naende akarora chiombe chinde isano na ibere, chimbe mono chindeu, chikorwa rooche, chigochitunyana korwa magega, chigatenena ase engegu y’orooche ang’e nase chiombe chiria chinde. Ⅳ Agwo chiombe chiria chimbe chindeu chikaria chiria chingiya chinoru. Erio Farao akaboka. Ⅴ Akaraara naende, akaroota eria kabere, akarora ebigara bitano na bibere ebinene ebinoru, bikinete ase oroboba oromo. Ⅵ Magega yabo ebigara binde ebireu, ebiasusanirie nomwaga ore korwa moocha, bikaoroka. Ⅶ Agwo ebigara ebio ebireu bitano na bibere bikaria biria binde ebinene ebinoru. Farao akaboka, akanyora ng’a nigo are koroota. Ⅷ 41:8: Dan 2:2Ekero bwachia gokia mambia, akaba oichaanete, agatoma abanto barangerie abaringoria bonsi ba Misiri na abanto abang’aini bonsi abaarengeo. Farao akabateebia chindoto chiaye, korende onde tatogete ore konyara komoteebia engencho ye chindoto echio.
Ⅸ Erio omoteneneri bw’ebinyugwa agateebia Farao: “Bono nainyoire okomocha kwane. Ⅹ Ekero aye Farao gwatobeete intwe abasomba bao, na gotosiekera, inche na omoteneneri bw’abaroisia emegati, ase echela eyere ase enyomba y’omotang’ani bw’abarendi, Ⅺ intwe ase obotuko obomo tokaroota chindoto twensi, kera oyomo okoroota kwaye kore nengencho yaye eng’ao. Ⅻ Agwo amo naintwe narengeo omomura oyomo Omoiberania, omosomba bw’omotang’ani bw’abarendi. Tokamoteebia chindoto chiaito, na ere agatoteebia engencho ye chindoto chiria, agatobanorera kera oyomo engencho y’okoroota kwaye. ⅩⅢ Na buna ere atoteebetie engencho ye chindoto chiaito, naboigo abeete; inche nkairanigwa ase emeremo yane, na oria onde agasungwa ase omote igoro.”
ⅩⅣ Erio Farao agatoma abanto korangeria Yusufu. Bakamorenta bwango korwa ase echela, na ere akagingirwa, akaonchoreria chianga chiaye, akagenda ase obosio bwa Farao. ⅩⅤ Farao agateebia Yusufu: “Nigo narota okoroota, gose onde tari oranyare gonteebia engencho y’okoroota kwane. Inche naigure amang’ana ao ng’a gokwaigure chindoto, nabo okomanya engencho ye chindoto echio.”
ⅩⅥ Yusufu akairaneria Farao, akabora: “Tari inche, korende Nyasae orakoe aye Farao okoiraneria kw’omorembe.”
ⅩⅦ Erio Farao agateebia Yusufu: “Ase okoroota kwane nigo narooche ng’a nteneine ase engegu y’orooche. ⅩⅧ Nkarora chiombe isano na ibere, chinoru chingiya mono, chikorwa rooche, chikorisia amasangaka; ⅩⅨ naende nkarora chiombe chinde isano na ibere, chimbe mono chindeu, chikorwa rooche, chigochitunyana korwa magega. Ase ense yonsi ya Misiri tinana korora chiombe chimbe buna echio. ⅩⅩ Agwo chiombe chiria chimbe chindeu chikaria chiombe chiria isano na ibere chi’eritang’ani, chiare chingiya chinoru, ⅩⅪ korende ekero chiachirire tichiarorekana ng’a nchiachiria, nkarora nabo chire buna chiare ritang’ani. Erio nkaboka. ⅩⅫ Naende konararire nkarora ase okoroota kwane ebigara ebinene ebinoru bitano na bibere, bikinete ase oroboba oromo; ⅩⅩⅢ na ebigara ebiomo ebireu, ebiasusanirie nomwaga ore korwa moocha, bikaoroka korwa magega yabo, ⅩⅩⅣ na ebigara biria ebireu bikaria biria ebinoru bitano na bibere. Inche ngateebia abaringoria, korende onde tari onyarete gonteebia engencho y’okoroota kwane.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Erio Yusufu agateebia Farao: “Chindoto chiao, aye Farao, nigo chire echimo. Nyasae okworokirie, aye Farao, aya agochia gokora iga bwango. ⅩⅩⅥ Chiombe chiria chingiya isano na ibere, eyio nemiaka etano na ebere, na ebigara biria ebiya bitano na bibere, eyio nemiaka etano na ebere; chindoto echio nechimo. ⅩⅩⅦ Ne chiombe chiria isano na ibere chimbe chindeu, chiachitunyanete chiria chinoru, eyio nemiaka etano na ebere, na ebigara biria bitano na bibere bitaamete, biasusanirie nomwaga ore korwa moocha, eyio nemiaka etano na ebere y’enchara. ⅩⅩⅧ Aya naro buna nagoteebia aye Farao, ng’a Nyasae okworokirie aya agochia gokora iga bwango. ⅩⅩⅨ Rora, ngochi-gocha ere emiaka etano na ebere y’omoigoto ase ense yonsi ya Misiri, ⅩⅩⅩ korende magega y’ayio neche emiaka ende etano na ebere y’enchara; na omoigoto oria bwonsi nigo ogochia kwebwa ase ense ya Misiri, ekiagera enchara eria nigo erarie ense eye. ⅩⅩⅪ Omoigoto oria tokoinyorwa ase ense eye, ase engencho y’enchara eye erache gocha korwa magega yaye, ekiagera nigo erabe endito mono. ⅩⅩⅫ Na bono Farao, okoroota kwao nigo gwachete eria kabere, aya nigo akoorokia ng’a Nyasae ochikiire amang’ana aya, na Nyasae nayaikeranie iga bwango.
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ “Ase ayio Farao, rigia omonto ore omomanyi na omong’aini, omobeke igoro y’ense yonsi ya Misiri. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Na bono Farao, aya naro ogwenerete okore: Obeke abateneneri igoro y’ense eye bachoge ensemo ya isano y’endagera ase ense ya Misiri ase emiaka eye etano na ebere y’omoigoto. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Tiga basangererie endagera eye yonsi y’emiaka eye emiya egocha, bayebeke erendwe ase emechie, ase okobua gwa Farao. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Na endagera eye egachwe eche koegwa abanto emiaka eye etano na ebere y’enchara, erache gocha ase ense ya Misiri, abanto tibaabaisa gocha gosira ase enchara eyio.”
Yusufu okoimokigwa konyora obonene ase Misiri
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Amang’ana ayio akagwenera Farao amo nabasomba baye bonsi. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Farao akabooria abasomba baye: “Inee! Ng’ai toranyore omonto onga buna oyo ore n’Omoika o Nyasae ime yaye?” ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Erio Farao agateebia Yusufu: “Ekiagera Nyasae okomanyirie amang’ana ayio onsi, onde tari aa omanyete, naende ore na obong’aini buna aye. ⅩⅬ 41:40: Ogok 7:10Bono naye orabe omoteneneri bw’enka yane, na abanto baane bonsi nabakoigwere buna orabe gokobachika. Ase ekerogo kiane ki’obogaambi oka, nao ndabe omonene gokobua.” ⅩⅬⅠ Farao agateebia Yusufu: “Rora nakobekire aye igoro y’ense yonsi ya Misiri.” ⅩⅬⅡ 41:42: Dan 5:29Erio Farao akarusia ebonyi yaye ere nekemanyererio korwa ase ekiara kiaye, akamoyebeekera Yusufu kiara kiaye, akamobekera na eanga engiya y’egatani, akamobekera ne chisonoi chi’etaabu igoti riaye. ⅩⅬⅢ Akamoriinia ase egari yaye ya kabere y’oborwoti. Abanto bagaaka eriogi, bakobora: “Tung’ama inse!” Naboigo akamobeka abe omogambi bw’ense yonsi ya Misiri. ⅩⅬⅣ Naende Farao agateebia Yusufu: “Ninche Farao. Otancheti aye, onde takoba ase ense yonsi ya Misiri oraimoke gokora gento.” ⅩⅬⅤ Farao Akaroka Yusufu Safenati‐Panea; akamonyuomeria omokungu, nere Asenati, omoiseke o Potifera, Omokuani bw’Oni. Yusufu akagenda aaria na aaria korigereria ense yonsi ya Misiri.
ⅩⅬⅥ Agwo Yusufu, ekero asoete gokorera Farao, omorwoti o Misiri, nigo arenge nemiaka emerongo etato. Na Yusufu akarua ase obosio bwa Farao, agaetanana ase ense yonsi ya Misri. ⅩⅬⅦ Emiaka eria etano na ebere y’omoigoto egaika, ense ekaama endagera enyinge, ⅩⅬⅧ na Yusufu agasangereria endagera yonsi ase emiaka eria etano na ebere, ase ense ya Misiri, akayegacha ase emechie. Ase kera omochie akagacha endagera yonsi y’emegondo eria yayoetanaine. ⅩⅬⅨ Yusufu akagacha endagera ase obonge mono, ekaba enyinge buna omokenye bw’Enyancha, goika agatiga koyerenga, ekiagera tiyare konyara korengeka.
Enchara egosoa Misiri
Ⅼ Ritang’ani konya emiaka eria y’enchara etaracha, Asenati, mosubati o Potifera, Omokuani bw’Oni, akaiborera Yusufu abana abaisia babere. ⅬⅠ Yusufu Akaaroka omwana oria omotangi Manase,*41:51 Manase: engencho yaye aande: okogera okweba agateeba: “Nyasae ogerire inyebe obokong’u bwonsi na abanto b’enka ya tata” ⅬⅡ Na omwana o kabere akamoroka Efraimu,†41:52 Efraimu: engencho yaye: okoibora ara kabere agateeba: “Nyasae ong’eire oroiboro ase ense y’emechando yane.”
ⅬⅢ Emiaka eria etano na ebere y’omoigoto ekaera ase ense ya Misiri, ⅬⅣ 41:54: Ogok 7:11na emiaka eria etano na ebere y’enchara egachaka gocha, buna Yusufu abaanete. Enchara egacha ase chinse chionsi, korende ase ense yonsi ya Misiri endagera n’yareengeo. ⅬⅤ 41:55: Yoh 2:5Ekero enchara yariete abanto bonsi ba Misiri, barabwo bakamorera Farao abae endagera; na Farao agateebia Abamisiri bonsi: “Genda ase Yusufu, na rinde rionsi arabateebie, goika morikore.” ⅬⅥ Ase igo, ekero enchara yasoete ase ense eria engima, Yusufu akaigora ebiage bionsi, akaooneria Abamisiri endagera, ekiagera enchara nigo yabeete endito ase ense ya Misiri. ⅬⅦ Abanto b’echinse chionsi bagacha Misiri kogora endagera korwa ase Yusufu, ekiagera enchara nigo yareenge endito ase ense engima.
