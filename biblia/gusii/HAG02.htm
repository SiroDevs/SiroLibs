Obomeka‐meku bwe Hekalu enyia
Ⅰ Ase rituko ri’emerongo ebere na rimo, omotienyi o gatano na kabere, ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha goetera ase Hagai omobani, rigateba: Ⅱ “Kwana na Serubabeli mosinto o Sealitieli, omogabana bw’Abayuda, na Yoshua Omokuani omonene, mosinto o Yehosadaki, na abatigari bonsi b’abaanto, obaborie: Ⅲ 2:3: Ezra 3:12Ning’o otigarete ase egati yaino oyo orooche obonene bw’enyomba eria entang’ani? Moise koreng’ania eye neria ya kare, naki eye ekororekana ase amaiso aino? Inee! Teri kororekana ase more ng’a negento bosa? Ⅳ Omonene nigo agoteeba iga: Aye Serubabeli, obe omokong’u na aye Yoshua Omokuani omonene, mosinto o Yehosadaki, obe omokong’u, nainwe abanto bonsi abamore ase ense eye, mobe abakong’u ase ogokora emeremo, ekiagera inche Omonene bw’Emeganda nigo inde amo nainwe. Ⅴ 2:5: Okong’ 29:45,46Ekero mwaruete ase ense ya Misiri nigo nabarierete eira ng’a botambe nigo ndabe amo nainwe; ase ayio Omoika one nigo ogendererete komenya ase egati yaino; timoiroka.
Ⅵ 2:6: Abaibr 12:26“Aya naro Omonene bw’Emeganda agoteeba: Ara rimo naende, engaki enke yatigaire nintengechie igoro chionsi, na ense, na enyancha, amo nense enyomo. Ⅶ Naende Omonene bw’Emeganda nigo agoteeba iga: Boigo nintengechie ebisaku bionsi, erinde ayaganeirie nabaanto b’ebisaku bionsi aoroke, na inche ing’ichorie enyomba yane nobonene. Ⅷ Chifeta nechiane, ne chitaabu nechiane. Nabo Omonene bw’Emeganda atebiire. Ⅸ Omonene bw’Emeganda nigo agoteeba ng’a obonene bw’enyomba eye ya bono nabobe obonene goetania obw’eria ya kare. Aase aiga nindete omorembe obe okogambao. Nabo Omonene bw’Emeganda atebiire.”
Ogosemia korwa ase abakuani
Ⅹ Ase rituko ri’emerongo ebere na kane, omotienyi o kianda, omwaka o kabere, bw’okogamba kw’omorwoti Dario, ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha ase Hagai omobani, rikamoteebia: Ⅺ “Aya naro Omonene bw’Emeganda agoteeba: Boria abakuani naki amachiiko agoteeba. Ⅻ Omonto aise kobogoria enyama enchenu ase omogo bw’eanga yaye akwambara, na akune nomogo bw’eanga eria omogati, gose endagera eiyekire, gose edivai, gose amaguta ’amazeituni, gose endagera ende yonsi, Inee, ekio giakunwa nkebe egechenu?”
Abakuani bakairaneria, “Yaya.”
ⅩⅢ 2:13: Omob 19:11-22Naende Hagai akaboria, “Onye omonto obeire oyotachenegeti ase ogokuna egetondo karakune ekemo ase ebinto ebio, Inee, ekio nakebe egetachenegeti?”
Abakuani bakairaneria, bagateeba, “Ee, nkebe egetachenegeti.”
ⅩⅣ Erio Hagai akairaneria, agateeba, “Omonene nigo agoteeba iga: Naboigo nabaanto aba, na egesaku eke, bare ase obosio bwane, na kera omoremo bagokora namaboko abo nabo ore, na kende gionsi eke bakorua aroro nigo kere egetachenegeti.
Omonene okoria eira y’okobasesenia
ⅩⅤ “Ase ayio nigo nkobaboria ng’a korwa rituko ria reero mobe mokorengereria buna yarenge ekero keria rigena konya ritarabekwa igoro ase rigena rinde, ase okoagacha Hekalu y’Omonene. ⅩⅥ Ninki kiabeete ase amatuko aria? Ekero omonto arenge kogenda ase etui y’emesaima ng’a anyore emesaima emerongo ebere, onyora ikomi yoka, na ekero arenge kogenda ase amasabibu akomiterwa ng’a anyore ebibekerero emerongo etano biichire edivai, onyora emerongo ebere bioka. ⅩⅦ Nigo nabaagete ase okobarentera oborwaire enyagetabo, na obw’okorigisia, na embura y’amagena ase chindagera chiaino mwakorerete emeremo. Nonya naboigo timwang’iranerete. Nabo Omonene atebiire. ⅩⅧ Korwa rituko ria reero, rituko ri’emerongo ebere na kane, omotienyi o kianda, ekero oboroso bwe Hekalu y’Omonene bwabegetwe, na kogenderera, morengererie buna arabe! ⅩⅨ Inee! Chimbusuro nchire ase ebiage? Inee! Emesabibu, na emeko, na emekomomanga, na emezeituni tiyangeti kwama? Korende korwa rituko ria reero nimbasesenie.”
Eira y’Omonene ase Serubabeli
ⅩⅩ Ase rituko riria ri’emerongo ebere na kane, omotienyi oria o kianda, ring’ana ri’Omonene rigacha ase Hagai naende, rikamoteebia, ⅩⅪ “Kwana na Serubabeli, omogabana bw’Abayuda, omoteebie ng’a inche nintengechie igoro chionsi amo nense. ⅩⅫ Ning’onchorane ebirogo bi’obonene bw’abarwoti, ninsirie chinguru chi’amarwoti ’abaanto b’ebisaku. Ning’onchorane chigari na abachiriini; chibarasi nchigwe amo nabachiriinete, kera omonto naite oyomwabo n’omoro. ⅩⅩⅢ Omonene bw’Emeganda nigo agoteeba iga: Ase rituko riria ninkoire aye Serubabeli omosomba one, mosinto o Sealitieli. Naende Omonene nigo agoteeba iga: Ningokore obe buna ebonyi ere nekemanyererio kiane, ekiagera nagochorire. Nabo Omonene bw’Emeganda agoteeba.”
