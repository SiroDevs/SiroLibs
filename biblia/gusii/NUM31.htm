Okorusia Egesiomba Ase Abamidiani
Ⅰ Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia. Ⅱ “Genda obaruserie Abaisraeli egesiomba ase Abamidiani ase engencho y’ayio abanto abwo baabakorerete, erio magega yaye nokwe, osangererekane amo n’abanto bao.”
Ⅲ Musa agateebia abanto, “Babeeke ang’e abanto bare ase egati yaino, mobae ebirwanero bagende korwania Abamidiani, erinde bamoruserie Omonene egesiomba. Ⅳ Korwa ase kera egesaku ki’Abaisraeli motome abanto elifu eyemo, bagende korwana esegi.”
Ⅴ Ase ayio korwa ase Abaisraeli chilifu chinyinge bagachora abanto elifu eyemo korwa ase kera egesaku. Abarwani bonsi bachoretwe kogeenda korwana esegi nigo baarenge chilifu ikomi na ibere. Ⅵ Musa agatoma abanto aba, elifu eyemo korwa ase kera egesaku, bakageenda korwana esegi. Amo nabarabwo agatoma Finehasi, mosinto bw’Eleazari Omokuani. Ere akaira ebikorero bi’Obomenyo Obochenu na chitarumbeta bi’okobugigwa. Ⅶ Barabwo bakaumokera Abamidiani, bakabarwania buna Omonene achiigete Musa, bagaita kera omosacha ase abanto abwo. Ⅷ Amo nabwo baitetwe nigo barenge abarwoti batano b’Abamidiani. Abarwoti abwo nabwo aba: Ebi, na Rekemu, na Suri, na Huri, na Reba. Boigo bagaita na Baalamu, mosinto o Beori n’omoro.
Ⅸ Abaisraeli bakaira abakungu b’Abamidiani amo nabana babo. Boigo bagasakora chiombe ne ching’ondi, n’ebinto bionsi bi’Abamidiani. Ⅹ Bagasamba emechie ase Abamidiani bamenyete, bagasamba nebitwori biabo bionsi bie chiema. Ⅺ Bakaira ebinto bionsi basakorete, bagoosa*31:11 bagoosa: engencho yaye nokoira abanto babe abasomba abanto, boigo bakaira chitugo chionsi. Ⅻ Erio bakarenta ebinto basakorete, na abanto boosete, ne chitugo, ase Musa na Eleazari Omokuani, na ase omoganda bw’Abaisraeli otoorete ase enyancha enyomo ya Moabu, agwo ase orooche rwa Yorodani, kororia Yeriko.
Okoirana Kw’abarwani B’esegi
ⅩⅢ Musa na Eleazari Omokuani, amo na abaraai bonsi b’omoganda bw’ abanto, bagasoka korwa ase ebitwori, bakageenda korotota abarwani baria b’esegi. ⅩⅣ Musa akagechigwa nabanene b’abarwani, abatang’ani b’abarwani chilifu, na ab’amagana, baria bairanete korwa ase okorwana esegi, ⅩⅤ akabora, “Inee! Ninki kiaba motaita abakungu? ⅩⅥ 31:16: Omob 25:1-9Inyora ng’a nabwo baigwerete ogosemia gwa Baalamu, bakagera Abaisraeli tibaaba abegenwa ase Omonene, ekero barenge agwo Peori, na ase ayio amaakwa agacha ase egati y’omoganda bw’ abanto ba Omonene. ⅩⅦ Ase ayio bono ita kera omwana omomura, naboigo moite kera omokungu obeire nomosaacha. ⅩⅧ Korende abaiseke bonsi abekungi timobaisa kobaita, mobaire babe abaino. ⅩⅨ Na bono, onde bwensi ore ase more oitire omonto, na onde bwensi okunire egetondo, goika abeere isiko y’egetwori ase engaki y’amatuko atano na abere. Ase rituko ria gatato, naende ase rituko ria gatano na kabere, mwechene inwe abanyene, amo nabooswa baino. ⅩⅩ Naende mochene kera eanga, na kera egento keroisirie korwa ase amasankwa, gose korwa ase amanya ye chimbori, na ebinto bionsi bi’emete.”
ⅩⅪ Na Eleazari Omokuani agateebia abanto abwo bairanete korwa ase okorwana esegi, “Oyo n’oro omoroberio Omonene achiigete Musa: ⅩⅫ Etaabu, na efeta, na etai, na ebioma ao ao, ⅩⅩⅢ na ebinto binde bionsi bitanyare gosarigwa nomorero, ebio mobietie ase omorero ime ase okobirura, nabirobio nabicheneke. Nonya naboigo goika mobisibie namaache yogochena, na kera egento getanyare kobua omorero goika mogeetie ime ase amaache ayio. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase rituko ria gatano na kabere mosibie chianga chiaino, nainwe ’namobe abachenekire; magega y’ayio monyare gosoa ase ebitwori.”
Ogwatananwa kw’ebisakorwa
ⅩⅩⅤ Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, ⅩⅩⅥ “Aye amo n’Eleazari Omokuani na abaraai b’ebisaku bi’omoganda bwonsi, mobare ebinto biasakorwa, na abanto abooswa, amo ne chitugo barenta. ⅩⅩⅦ Erio bwatanane ebinto biasakorwa, na abanto booswa amo ne ching’iti ase chinsemo ibere. Ensemo eyemo ebe ey’abarwani b’esegi, na eyende ey’omoganda. ⅩⅩⅧ Korwa ase ensemo etungetie abarwani orusie omonto oyomo korwa ase abanto amagana atano aboosetwe abe oyo bw’Omonene. Naboigo orusie eng’iti eyemo korwa ase ching’iti amagana atano ase chiombe, chitigere, ching’ondi ne chimbori, chibe echia Omonene. ⅩⅩⅨ Abanto abwo baatananwe korwa ase ekeng’ese ekio baegwe Eleazari Omokuani, erio babe ekeegwa ase Omonene, naboigo ne ching’iti chiria. ⅩⅩⅩ Naende korwa ase ekeng’ese egetungetie omoganda bw’Abaisraeli bwatanane omonto oyomo korwa ase abanto emerongo etano, na eyemo ye chiombe ase emerongo etano, naboigo ase chitigere, ne ching’ondi, ne chimbori. Abanto abwo amo ne ching’iti echio chionsi oe Abalawi abwo bagokora emeremo ase Obomenyo Obochenu bw’Omonene.” ⅩⅩⅪ Musa na Eleazari Omokuani bagakora buna Omonene konya achiikire Musa.
ⅩⅩⅫ Ebisakorwa ebiatigarete ase abarwani b’esegi, ebi barentete ekero babuete, nabio ebi: ching’ondi, amo ne chimbori, chilifu amagana atano nomo na merongo etano na ebere na isano (675,000), ⅩⅩⅩⅢ ne chiombe chilifu emerongo etano na ebere na ibere (72,000), ⅩⅩⅩⅣ ne chitigere chilifu emerongo etano nomo na eyemo (61,000) ⅩⅩⅩⅤ na abaiseke abekungi chilifu emerongo etato na ibere (32,000). ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Ekeng’ese egiatungetie abarwani b’esegi nigo kiarenge: Ching’ondi amo ne chimbori chilifu amagana atato na merongo etato na isano ibere, na amagana atano (337,500). ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Ebango y’Omonene yarusetigwe korwa ase ching’ondi echio, amo ne chimbori, nigo yarenge amagana atano rimo, na merongo etan’ebere, na isano (675). ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Chiombe baetwe nigo chiarenge chilifu emerongo etato na isano nemo (36,000), na echia Omonene nigo chiarenge emerongo etano na ebere, (72). ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Chitigere baetwe nigo chiarenge chilifu emerongo etato, na amagana atano (30,500), na echi’Omonene nigo chiarenge emerongo etano nomo, na eyemo (61). ⅩⅬ Abaiseke abekungi baetwe nigo barenge chilifu ikomi na isano nemo (16,000) na aba Omonene nigo barenge emerongo etato, na babere (32). ⅩⅬⅠ Musa akaa Eleazari Omokuani ensemo eria yaruetwe ase Omonene, buna ere konya achiikire Musa.
ⅩⅬⅡ Ekeng’ese giatungetie Abaisraeli, ekio Musa arusetie korwa ase abarwani baria b’esegi, ⅩⅬⅢ nigo kiarenge ching’ondi chilifu amagana atato, emerongo etato na isano na ibere, na amagana atano (337,500), ⅩⅬⅣ ne chiombe chilifu emerongo etato na isano nemo (36,000), ⅩⅬⅤ ne chitigere chilifu emerongo etato, na amagana atano (30,500), ⅩⅬⅥ na abanto chilifu ikomi na isano nemo (16,000). ⅩⅬⅦ Korwa ase ekeng’ese giatungetie omoganda bw’Abaisraeli, Musa agaatanana oyomo bw’ abanto emerongo etano, na eyemo ye ching’iti emerongo etano, akaa Abalawi baria barenge gokora emeremo ase Obomenyo Obochenu bw’Omonene, buna Omonene konya achiikire Musa.
ⅩⅬⅧ Erio abanene b’abarwani, abatang’ani b’abarwani chilifu, na ab’amagana, bagaacha ase Musa, ⅩⅬⅨ bakamoteebia, “Omonene, intwe abasomba bao, twabarire abarwani b’esegi abarenge inse y’oborendi bwaito, na twanyorire ng’a mbonsi bare, onde tasireti korwa ase tore. Ⅼ Ase ayio twarentire ebinto bie chitaabu bi’ogwechabera: ebitange bi’amaboko, na ebigera, ne chibonyi, na ebigera bi’amato, ne chisonoi. Ebinto ebio twasakorete kera oyomo oito, na bono twabirentire, twabirure ase Omonene, ase ogokora okobwatanigwa ase tore ase obosio bwaye.” ⅬⅠ Musa na Eleazari Omokuani bakanyora korwa ase abatang’ani abwo chitaabu chiria chiaroisetigwe ebinto bi’ogwechabera. ⅬⅡ Chitaabu chionsi chiaruetwe ase Omonene, chiria abatang’ani baria b’abarwani chilifu na ab’amagana barentete, nigo chiarenge chi’oborito koreng’ana ne chikiro amagana abere. ⅬⅢ Abarwani b’esegi konya basakoire ebinto, na kera oyomo obo konya oirire ebiaye bweka omonyene. ⅬⅣ Musa na Eleazari Omokuani bakanyora chitaabu chiria korwa ase abatang’ani b’abarwani chilifu na ab’amagana, bagachiira ime y’Eema y’Omosangererekano, na Abaisraeli bakainyorwa ase obosio bw’Omonene.
