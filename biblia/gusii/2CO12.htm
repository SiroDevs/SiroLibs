Amaroro na Okomaanorerwa
Ⅰ Ningwenerete gwetogia, nonya tigokongwenera gento; korende bono ninkwane igoro y’amaroro na okomaanorerwa bikorwa ase Omonene. Ⅱ Namanyire omonto oyomo ase Kristo, yaetire emiaka ikomi na ene, omonto oyio akabogorigwa gochia igoro ya gatato, (onye orenge ase omobere tinkomanya, onye orenge isiko y’omobere tinkomanya, Nyasae nere omanyete). Ⅲ Na inche namanyire omonto oyio, (onye orenge ase omobere tinkomanya, onye orenge isiko y’omobere tinkomanya, Nyasae nere omanyete); Ⅳ igo abogoretigwe goika Paradiso ya Nyasae, akaigwa amang’ana atagwatoreka, ayatagwenerete Mwanyabaanto ayakwane. Ⅴ Ase engencho y’omonto oyio ninyetogie; korende ase engencho yane tingwetogia, otatiga ase oboreu bwane. Ⅵ Naki onye ndigetie gwetogia anga tinaba omoriri, ekiagera nintebe ekeene. Korende ninyetange, omonto tambarera kobua aya aroche ase ’nde, gose koyaigwa korwa ase ’nde. Ⅶ Erinde timbaisa gocha koenena goetania ekerengo, ekiagera ase obonge bw’okomaanorerwa okwo, nkaegwa riigwa rimbetete ase omobere, omotomwa o Saitani, ang’ake, timbaisa gocha koenena goetania ekerengo. Ⅷ Ase engencho y’egento eke ngasaba Omonene ara gatato kendue. Ⅸ Korende aganteebia, “Obuya bwane bwagoisanire, ekiagera chinguru chiane chiaikeranirie ase oboreu.” Nabo ninyetogerie amareu aane ase omogooko, erio chinguru chia Kristo chiikaranse igoro ase ’nde. Ⅹ Ase engencho ya Kristo nagweneirwe namareu, na ogochaaywa, na obokong’u, na okorosigwa, na emechando yonsi; ekero inde omoreu, erio nario inde ne chinguru.
Ogwechanda gwa Paulo ase ekanisa ya Korinto
Ⅺ Nabeire omoriri, korende inwe mwambetereirie koba bo. Inche ningwenerete ntogigwe nainwe, ekiagera tindemeiri gento eki abatomwa baria abanene baare nakio, nonya tindi gento. Ⅻ Ekeene ebimanyererio bi’omotomwa bw’ekeene nigo biaorogetigwe egati yaino ase oboremereria bwonsi ase ebikone, na amakumia, na ogokora gwe chinguru. ⅩⅢ Naki ngento ki moremeire kobua chikanisa chinde, otatiga inche timbaraarereti? Monyabere okomocha okwo!
ⅩⅣ Rora, ekero eke nekia gatato inche nabeire ang’e gocha ase more. Tinkobaraarereria, naki tindi korigia ebinto biaino, korende inwe abanyene, ekiagera abana tibagwenereti kobagachera abaibori enibo, korende abaibori bagachere abana. ⅩⅤ Ase omogooko omonge ninyebeke, naende ninyerue ase engencho y’emioyo yaino. Onye nkomenta kobancha mono, Inee, ningwenerete koremerwa obwanchani? ⅩⅥ Korende abe bo, inche timbaraarereti, korende ekiagera inde omong’aini nkabanyora ase obong’ainereria. ⅩⅦ Inee! Nabaurete gento goetera ase onde bwensi natomete ase more? ⅩⅧ Ngasorora Tito agende; ngatoma oyominto oria amo nere. Inee! Tito nabaurete gento? Titwagendete ase Omoika oria oria? Titwataarete ase chimbaratero chiria chiria?
ⅩⅨ Inee! Mokagete ng’a iga togwekengera ase more naende? Ase obosio bwa Nyasae nigo togokwana ase Kristo. Na aya onsi akorirwe ase okobaagacha inwe abanchwa. ⅩⅩ Naki nairokire, ng’a inse gocha timbanyora buna ndigetie kobanyora; naende ndorekane ase more, tari buna morigetie inwe. Aande gose mbanyore more neriomana, ne ribero, na endamwamu, na okweancha, na ogoitania, na okogenkana, na oboenenu, ne rirorooche. ⅩⅪ Naende nairokire, ng’a inse gocha naende, Nyasae one nankeyie inse ase more, nimbaichanere mono ase abwo bamochete kare, abataonchogete korwa ase eubi, na obotomani, na obonyaka obwo bare kwenaria.
