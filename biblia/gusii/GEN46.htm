ⅩⅬⅥ
Israeli na abana baye bakong’anya gochia Misiri
Ⅰ Ase igo, Israeli akaimoka, akagenda, akaira ebinto bionsi ebi abwate, agaika Beeri‐Seba, akaruera Nyasae o Isaka ise ekeng’wanso. Ⅱ Nyasae akarangeria Israeli ase okorora ekero kia botuko, akabora: “Yakobo! Yakobo!”
Ere agaitaba: “Nao inde aa.”
Ⅲ Erio akamoteebia: “Inche ninche Nyasae, Nyasae o iso; tobaisa kwoboa gotirimboka gochia Misiri, ekiagera ningokore obe egesaku ekenene aroro. Ⅳ Inche nintirimboke amo naye goika Misiri, naende tinkomocha gokoirania; na Yusufu nabe okoirania amaiso ao kogw akuure.”
Ⅴ Erio Yakobo akaimoka korwa Beeri‐Seba, akagenda. Basinto ba Israeli bakabogoria Yakobo ise, amo nabana babo abake, na abakungu babo ase chigari chiria Farao abatomerete chibabogorie. Ⅵ 46:6: Ogok 7:15Bakaira chitugo chiabo ne chinibo chiabo echi baniberete ase ense ya Kaanani. Nabo bagaika Misiri, Yakobo na oroiboro rwaye rwonsi amo nere, Ⅶ abana baye abamura na abamura babo; na abana baye abaiseke, na abaiseke b’abamura babo. Naboigo akaira oroiboro rwaye rwonsi, akagenda nabarabwo goika Misiri.
Ororeeria rwa Israeli
Ⅷ Aya naro amarieta Abaisraeli abwo bagendete Misiri: Yakobo na abana baye, nabwo aba: Rubeni, nere omotangi o Yakobo. Ⅸ Abana abamura ba Rubeni nabwo aba: Hanoki, na Palu, na Hezironi, na Karimi. Ⅹ Na abana abamura ba Simioni nabwo aba: Yemueli, na Yamini, na Ohadi, na Yakini, na Sohari, na Saulo, omwana bw’omokuungu Omokaanani. Ⅺ Na abana abamura ba Lawi nabwo aba: Gerisoni, na Kohati, na Merari. Ⅻ Na abana abamura ba Yuda nabwo aba: Eri, na Onani, na Sela, na Peresu, na Zera; korende Eri na Onani bagakwera ase ense ya Kaanani. Na abana ba Peresu nabwo Hezironi na Hamuli. ⅩⅢ Na abana abamura ba Isakaru nabwo aba: Tola, na Pua, na Yobu, na Simuroni. ⅩⅣ Na abana abamura ba Sabuloni nabwo aba: Seredi na Eloni, na Yaleeli. ⅩⅤ Aba nabwo abana abamura Lea aiborerete Yakobo agwo Padan‐Aramu. Na omoiseke oyomo aiborete, nere Dina. Abana baye bonsi amo nabachokoro, abamura na abaiseke, nigo baareenge emerongo etato na batato.
ⅩⅥ Abana abamura ba Gadi nabwo aba: Sifioni, na Hagi, na Suni, na Esiboni, na Eri, na Arodi, na Areli. ⅩⅦ Na abana abamura b’Aseri nabwo aba: Imuna, na Isiva, na Isivi, na Beria, na omoiseke omwabo nere Sera. Na abamura ba Beria nabwo Heberi na Malikieli. ⅩⅧ Aba nabwo abamura na abachokoro ba Silpa, oyio Labani aete Lea, omwana oye omosubati. Abwo Silpa aiborerete Yakobo, abanto ikomi na batano nomo.
ⅩⅨ Abana abamura ba Raeli, moka Yakobo, nabwo Yusufu na Benjamini. ⅩⅩ 46:20: Omoch 41:50-52Yusufu akaiborerwa Manase na Efraimu, ase ense ya Misiri. Asenati, mosubati o Potifera, Omokuani bw’Oni, akamoiborera abana abwo abamura. ⅩⅪ Na abana abamura ba Benjamini nabwo aba: Bela, na Bekeri, na Asibeli, na Gera, na Naamani, na Eki, na Roshi, na Mupimu, na Hupimu, na Aridi. ⅩⅫ Aba nabwo abamura na abachokoro Raeli aiborerete Yakobo, abanto ikomi na baane.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omwana omomura o Dani nere Husimu. ⅩⅩⅣ Na abana abamura ba Nafutali nabwo aba: Yaseeli, na Guni, na Yaseri, na Silemu. ⅩⅩⅤ Aba nabwo abamura na abachokoro abamura Bilia aiborerete Yakobo, abanto batano na babere.
ⅩⅩⅥ Abanto bonsi abagendete amo na Yakobo ase ense ya Misiri, abwo baiboretwe korwa asare, nigo baareenge emerongo etano nomo, na batano nomo. Abakungu b’abana abamura ba Yakobo, barabwo tibaabariri. ⅩⅩⅦ 46:27: Ogok 7:14Na abana abamura ba Yusufu, abaiboreretwe agwo Misiri, mbabere baarenge. Abanto bonsi ba Yakobo abasoete Misiri nigo baarenge emerongo etano na ebere.
Yusufu okorotota Israeli ise
ⅩⅩⅧ Yakobo agatoma Yuda amotang’anere kogenda ase Yusufu, erinde aoroke ase obosio bwaye agwo Goseni, Naboigo bagaika ase ensemo ya Goseni. ⅩⅩⅨ Erio Yusufu akabeeka egari yaye ang’e, akayeriina, akagenda korotota Israeli aria Goseni. Ekero achia goika asare akamoboania rigoti riaye engaki etari nke. ⅩⅩⅩ Israeli agateebia Yusufu: “Bono tiga inkwe, ekiagera nakororire obosio bwao, naende narorire ng’a moyo ore.”
ⅩⅩⅪ Yusufu agateebia abamwabo na abanto b’enka ya ise: “Nkogenda inde ase Farao imoteebie: Abaminto na abanto b’enka ya tata, aba baarenge ase ense ya Kaanani, bachire ase ’nde. ⅩⅩⅫ Na abanto abwo nabatugi be chitugo, na bono barentire chitugo chiabo, ching’ondi ne chiombe, na binde bionsi bare nabio. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Bono, Farao karabarangerie na kobaboria meremo ki monarete gokora, ⅩⅩⅩⅣ nigo moramoiranerie: Intwe abasomba bao nigo tore abatugi be chitugo korwa obwana bwaito goika reero; buna tore igo, nabo ne chisokoro chiaito chiareenge. Na igo namonyore ribaga komenya ase ensemo ya Goseni, ekiagera kera omorisia bwe chitugo nigo are okogechia ase Abamisiri.”
