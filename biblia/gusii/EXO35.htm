Ogochika kw’erituko ri’Esabato
Ⅰ Musa akarangeria omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli amo, akabateebia, “Aya nar’Omonene achiikire morakore: Ⅱ 35:2: Okong’ 20:8-11; 23:12; 31:15; 34:21; Abal 23:3; Okoir 5:12-14Mokore emeremo ase amatuko atano na rimo, korende rituko ria gatano na kabere nigo rirabe richenu ase more, rituko ri’Esabato, riogotimoka ase Omonene. Onde bwensi aise gokora emeremo ase rituko erio goika aitwe akwe. Ⅲ Timobaisa gouta omorero ase rituko ri’Esabato, aande onsi ase momenyete.”
Ogosangererigwa kw’ebiegwa ase Obomenyo Obochenu
(Okong’ 25:1-9)
Ⅳ Musa agateebia omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli, “Aya nar’Omonene achiikire: Ⅴ Moruere Omonene ebiegwa. Kera omonto oyore nomoyo bw’ogwancha aruere Omonene ebinto ebi: Etaabu, na efeta, na etai; Ⅵ chiusi chi’amarionya chire buru, ne chimonyinga, ne chimbariri; egatani engiya na amanya e chimbori; Ⅶ amasankwa e chimingichi aakire etaago, na amasankwa amaterere e ching’iti chia rooche, ne chibao chi’emekonge; Ⅷ amaguta e chitaaya, na ebinto bi’okoroisia amaguta akweakwa, na ebi’okoroisia ubani; Ⅸ amagena e rigori rinene buna oiniki na ayande akobekwa ase egesibao na egesembe ki’obokuani.”
Ebinto bi’Obomenyo bwa Nyasae
(Okong’ 39:32-43)
Ⅹ “Abaroisia bonsi abang’aini abare ase egati yaino, tiga bache baroisie ebinto bionsi Omonene achiikire, Ⅺ nabio ebi: Obomenyo na eema yaye, na egietubo kiaye, ebigobanio ne chibao chiaye, na emeriibo, ne chisiiro amo ne chinterekerero chiabo; Ⅻ Esanduki na emiaro yaye, na Oboikaranso bw’amaabera, na etoto eria erabeekwe ebokenge; ⅩⅢ emesa na emiaro yaye, na ebikorero biaye bionsi, na emegati eria eraruegwe ase Nyasae; ⅩⅣ ekebekerero gie chitaaya na ebikorero biaye, ne chitaaya chiaye amo namaguta e chitaaya, ⅩⅤ na egesasiimero ase ubani egosamberwa amo nemiaro yaye, na amaguta akweakwa, na amanukato are ne rimisu rigiya, na ebitambaa bigosungwa ase egesoero ki’Obomenyo obwo; ⅩⅥ na egesasiimero aase ebing’wanso bigosamberwa amo negechungi kiaye gi’etai, na emiaro yaye, na ebikorero biaye bionsi, na egetuba amo nenterekerero yaye; ⅩⅦ ebitambaa ebirakenge eraaro, ne chisiiro amo ne chinterekerero chiaye, na egetambaa ki’egesoero ki’eraaro, ⅩⅧ na ebirochi bi’Obomenyo na ebi’eraaro amo ne chingori chiabo, ⅩⅨ ne chianga chiria chinseera chigokorerwa ime y’Ase Achenu, engencho yaye chianga chia Aroni Omokuani, amo nechi’abamura baye, barabe bakobeeka ekero bagokora emeremo yabo y’obokuani.”
Abanto bakorenta ebiegwa biabo
ⅩⅩ Erio omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli okaruao agwo ase obosio bwa Musa. ⅩⅪ Naende bakairana kera oyomo buna enkoro yaye yamosugetie, na buna anchete ere omonyene, bakarentera Omonene ebiegwa biabo ase okoroisia Eema y’Omosangererekano na ebinto bionsi ebiaganeirie, ase emeremo eria na ase chianga chiria chinchenu.
ⅩⅫ Barabwo bagacha, abasacha na abakungu, na ase ogwancha kwabo bakarenta amameka, na ebigera bi’amato abo, ne chibonyi chi’ebiara, ne chisonoi, na ebinto ao ao bie chitaabu bi’ogwechabera. Kera omonto akarenta ebinto bie chitaabu, ebi aatananete ase ogokorera Omonene. ⅩⅩⅢ Naende kera omonto oyo obwate chiusi chi’amarionya chire buru, ne chimonyinga, ne chimbariri, na egatani engiya, na amanya e chimbori, na amasankwa e chimingichi aakire etaago, na amasankwa e ching’iti chia rooche, akabirenta. ⅩⅩⅣ Bonsi abaare konyara korua chifeta ne chitaai bakabirenta bibe ebiegwa ase Omonene, na bonsi abarenge nemekonge eyere emiya ase emeremo ende yonsi, bakayerenta. ⅩⅩⅤ Na abakungu abaroisia abang’aini bagasisa namaboko abo chiusi chi’amarionya chire buru, ne chimonyinga, ne chimbariri, na echi’egatani engiya, bagachirenta. ⅩⅩⅥ Na abakungu bonsi, abasugetigwe ase emioyo yabo, naende bamanyete emeremo eye, bagasisa amanya e chimbori abe chiusi. ⅩⅩⅦ Abatang’ani babo bakarenta amagena e rigori rinene buna oiniki na ayande arabekwe ase egesibao na egesembe ki’obokuani, ⅩⅩⅧ naende ebinto bie rimisu rigiya amo namaguta e chitaaya, na amaguta akweakwa, na ubani ere ne rimisu rigiya. ⅩⅩⅨ Abaisraeli bonsi, abasacha na abakungu, abarenge nogwancha korenta ebinto bi’emeremo eye Omonene achigete goetera ase Musa, bakarenta ebiegwa biabo ase Omonene.
Abaroisia b’Obomenyo
(Okong’ 31:1-11)
ⅩⅩⅩ Musa agateebia Abaisraeli, Rora, Omonene ochorire Besaleli, oyore mosinto o Uri, mochokoro o Huri bw’egesaku kia Yuda. ⅩⅩⅪ Omoichoirie omoika o Nyasae, omoeire obong’aini ne chisemi anyare gokora emeremo ende yonsi y’oboroisia, ⅩⅩⅫ abe oyomanyete koroberia na koroisia ebinto bie chitaabu, ne chifeta, ne chitaai, ⅩⅩⅩⅢ abe okobaacha amagena e rigori rinene na koyabeeka ase ebikonde, na kobaacha emete, na gokora emeremo ende yonsi y’oboroisia. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Omonene omoeire ere amo na Oholiabu, mosinto bw’Ahisamaki bw’egesaku kia Dani, obong’aini bw’okworokereria abande oboroisia bwabo. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Obaichoirie obong’aini ase chinkoro chiabo, erinde banyare gokora emeremo ende yonsi buna okomang’a, na okoroberia, na koroisia ebitambaa bie chiusi chi’amarionya chire buru, ne chimonyinga, ne chimbariri, na echi’egatani engiya, na koroisia ebitambaa binde bionsi. Banyare gokora emeremo ende yonsi na bamanye koroberia oboroisia bonde bwonsi.
