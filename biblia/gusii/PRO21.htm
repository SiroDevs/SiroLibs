ⅩⅪ
Ⅰ Enkoro y’omorwoti nigo enga buna orooche rore ase okoboko kw’Omonene; n’Omonene nigo akomoraa gochia aande onsi buna aganetie.
Ⅱ Kera enchera y’omonto nigo ekororekana ng’a neya boronge ase are, korende Omonene nigo akorenga chinkoro.
Ⅲ Ogokora oboronge na ekeene, ayio naro Omonene agwancha kobua ebing’wanso.
Ⅳ Amaiso are noboenenu, na enkoro y’obomwenu, ebio nigo bire etaya y’abakori amabe, bikobaraa bakora ebibe.
Ⅴ Emeroberio y’omonto omong’aini nigo ekomorentera ebitoki ebinge, korende omonto okoayerera nigo akogeenderera koremerwa.
Ⅵ Chinibo chikonyorwa ase oromeme rw’obong’ainereria, nigo chikoera buna omooki, nigo chire n’obosongo buna emetego ya amakweri.
Ⅶ Obourereria bw’abakori amabe ’mbobasirie barueo, ekiagera bangire gokora ayare ay’oboronge.
Ⅷ Enchera y’omomochi nigo ere enkorekanu, korende ogokora kw’oyore n’enkoro endonge nigo kore okoronge.
Ⅸ Mbuya komenya ase ekenunchi kia irongo, kobua komenya amo ase enyomba n’omokuungu omonyariomana.
Ⅹ Omoyo bw’omokori amabe nigo okogania gokora ebibe, na omonto oyio tari kororera omonto onde amaabera.
Ⅺ Ekero omonto omonyacheche akonyora egesusuro, oria otamanyeti amaange nigo akoba omong’aini; na ekero omonto omong’aini akworokererigwa nigo akonyora chiseemi.
Ⅻ Nyasae omonyene oboronge nigo akorigereria enyomba y’omokori amabe; ere nigo akoiyeria abakori amabe inse basire.
ⅩⅢ Omonto oria ogosieka amato aye ng’a taigwa ekerero ki’omotaka, nere ekero ararere takoiguugwa.
ⅩⅣ Ekeegwa gekoruegwa bobisi nigo kegokendia endamwamu, ne riaaki ribisire ase eanga ime nigo rigokendia ekeririanda.
ⅩⅤ Ekero ekeene giakorirwe, ayio nigo akoba omogooko bw’abanyene oboronge, korende ase abakori amabe nomoichano.
ⅩⅥ Omonto ogotiga enchera y’obomanyi, oyio nabeere ase omosangererekano bw’abaanto bakwete.
ⅩⅦ Omonto bwanchete emegoko goika abe omotaka; na oyobwanchete kwegokia nedivai na amaguta takoba omonda.
ⅩⅧ Omokori amabe nigo agosoa ase emechando y’omonyene oboronge, oba embooria yaye, na oyo otari omwegenwa oba embooria y’oyore n’enkoro endonge.
ⅩⅨ Mbuya komenya ase ense y’erooro, kobua komenya n’omokuungu omonyariomana, naende omonyandamwamu.
ⅩⅩ Omonto omong’aini nigo akorenda chinibo chi’engeencho enene amo namaguta ase enka yaye, korende omoriri nigo agochiria bobe chiaera.
ⅩⅪ Omonto okorigia oboronge na obwororo, oyio ’nanyore obogima, na amasikani, na oboronge.
ⅩⅫ Omonto omong’aini nigo akoumokera omochie ore nabarwani esegi chituoni oyobua, erio otagora chindwaki chiria abanto abwo basemeretie.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omonto orendete omonwa oye, na oromeme rw’aye, oyio nigo etangerete emechando.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omonto ore omoenenu na omwerori, na ogokora kwaye kore okw’obomwenu, nigo akorokwa “Omochecheria”.
ⅩⅩⅤ Okogania kw’omonto omworo nigo gokomoita okwa, ekiagera nigo akwanga gokora emeremo namaboko aye.
ⅩⅩⅥ Omonto omong’iti nigo akweganeria kera egento botambe, korende omonyene oboronge nigo akorua na tari kong’itia.
ⅩⅩⅦ Ekeng’wanso ki’omokori amabe nigo kere okogechia, Inee, tari bobe mono goetania ekero agokerua nebirengererio ebibe?
ⅩⅩⅧ Kirori bw’oborimo nasire, korende omotegereri omuya nageenderere gokwana.
ⅩⅩⅨ Omokori amabe nigo agokong’ia obosio bwaye, korende oyore nenkoro endonge nigo akorenda orogendo rw’aye.
ⅩⅩⅩ Obong’aini tibori, gose okomanya, gose ogoseemia, biranyaare koamererania n’Omonene.
ⅩⅩⅪ Chibarasi nigo chikobekwa ang’e kogeenda korwana esegi, korende obobui nigo bokorua ase Omonene.
