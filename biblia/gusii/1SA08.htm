Abanto bagasaba baegwe omorwoti
Ⅰ Ekero Samweli konya agotire akabeeka abamura baye babe abagaambi igoro y’Abaisraeli. Ⅱ Omomura oye omotangi nigo are korokwa Yoel, na oyo o kabere Abiya. Barabwo nigo babete abagaambi agwo Beeri‐Seba. Ⅲ Korende abamura ba Samweli tibakorete buna ise areenge gokora ase okobwatia oboronge; bakabotiga na bakerigeria chinibo, bakarigia riaaki na bakaonchorana oboronge.
Ⅳ Erio abagaaka bonsi b’Abaisraeli bagasangererekana amo, bakagenda ase Samweli agwo Rama, Ⅴ 8:5: Okoir 17:14bakamoteebia: “Rora bono aye kwagotire, na abamura bao tibari kobwatia chinchera chiao. Ase ayio tochorere omorwoti oranyare gotogaambera buna ebisaku binde bionsi bibwate abarwoti.” Ⅵ Korende Samweli tancha amang’ana ayio bamoteebetie: “Toe omorwoti atogaambere.” Ase ayio Samweli agasaba Omonene. Ⅶ Eri’Omonene akamoteebia: “Otegerere amang’ana abo baragotebie. Tari aye bangire, korende ninche bangire, timba omorwoti obo. Ⅷ Korwa ekero keria nabarusetie Misiri goika reero iga bantigire inche, babeire bagokorera chinyasae ching’ao, na bono bagotigire aye buna bantigete inche. Ⅸ Gaki bono, igwera amang’ana abo, korende obakuurere mono, obateebie aya omorwoti orache kobagaambera arabe ogokora.”
Ⅹ Ase igo Samweli agateebia Abanto baria baganetie omorwoti amang’ana onsi Omonene amotebeetie. Ⅺ Akabateebia: “Aya naro omorwoti oyio orabagambere arabakorere: Naire abana baino abamura, ababeeke ase chigari chiaye, na abande ababeeke babe abariini be chibarasi chiaye bamokorere. Abande babetererigwe baminyoke bosio bwe chigari chiaye. Ⅻ Abande babekwe babe abatang’ani b’abarwani chilifu, na abande b’emerongo etano. Abande babe bakorema emegondo yaye na kogesa endagera yaye, abande babe bakoroisia ebirwanero bi’eseegi na ebikorero bie chigari chiaye. ⅩⅢ Naire abaiseke baino bachie koba abaroisia amaguta akweakwa, abande babe bakoruga endagera na koroisia emegati. ⅩⅣ Naire na emegondo yaino y’endagera, na ey’emesabibu, na ey’emezeituni eyere emiya mono, ayee abasomba baye. ⅩⅤ Narusie ensemo ya ikomi korwa ase rigesa riaino ri’emegondo y’endagera, na ey’emesabibu, ayee abwo bakomorenda, na abasomba baye. ⅩⅥ Boigo naire abasomba baino, abamura na abaiseke, na abasae baino abaya, babe bakomokorera, ne chitigere chiaino chibe chikomokorera boigo. ⅩⅦ Naende arusie ensemo ya ikomi y’amaicho aino, nainwe abanyene mobe abasomba baye. ⅩⅧ Ase engaki eyio inamobe mokorera nobororo ase engencho y’omorwoti oino, oyio mwechorerete, korende Omonene takoba ogotegerera okorera kwaino.”
ⅩⅨ Korende Abanto bakaanga gotegerera Samweli, bakaboora: “Yaya, taibo! Nigo toganetie omorwoti atogambere. ⅩⅩ Ntorigetie koba buna ebisaku binde; ntoganetie omorwoti ogotogaambera na gototang’ana, ekero togochia korwana chiseegi chiaito.” ⅩⅪ Ekero Samweli aigwete amang’ana aya Abanto bakwanire, akayaira ase Omonene. ⅩⅫ Omonene agateebia Samweli: “Igwera amang’ana abo, obachorere omorwoti.” Erio Samweli agateebia Abaisraeli bairane, kera omonto ase omochie oye.
