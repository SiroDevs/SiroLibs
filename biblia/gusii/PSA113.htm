Obuya bw’Omonene bogotogigwa
Ⅰ Motogie Omonene!
Inwe abasomba b’Omonene, motogie,
ee, ritogie erieta riaye!
Ⅱ Erieta ri’Omonene ritogigwe
korwa bono goika kare na kare!
Ⅲ Erieta ri’Omonene ribe rigotogigwa
korwa ekero risase rikooroka goika ekero rikorimera!
Ⅳ Omonene nigo are omonene igoro ase ebisaku bionsi,
na obonene bwaye nigo boetanaine igoro chionsi.
 
Ⅴ Ning’o onga buna Omonene, Nyasae oito,
oria omenyete aaria igoro mono,
Ⅵ na origereretie goocha inse aa arore igoro na ense.
Ⅶ Nigo akoimokia omotaka korwa ase obotaka obwere,
na koimokia omoremerwa korwa ase ribu,
Ⅷ obabeeka baikaransa amo n’abwo bare n’okobua,
baria bakogambera abanto baye.
Ⅸ Nigo akoa omokungu omogomba chinguru chi’okoibora,
oba n’omogooko ase obomenyo bwaye.
 
Omonene atogigwe!
