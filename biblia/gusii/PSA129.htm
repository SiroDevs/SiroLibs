Ogosaba ng’a ababisa b’Abaisraeli babugwe
Ⅰ “Ababisa nigo batochaandete korwa oboke bwaito.”
Nabo Abaisraeli bagoteeba.
 
Ⅱ “Ee, barabwo nigo batochaandete korwa oboke bwaito,
korende tibana‐gotobua.
Ⅲ Bakang’ororia emegongo yaito buna emegondo.
Bakayetambeyia emeng’ororia yaabo.”
Ⅳ Omonene nigo are omonyene oboronge,
ere obutoire chingori chi’abakori amabe.
 
Ⅴ Abwo bonsi bageechete Sayoni,
taga basookigwe na koiranigwa magega.
Ⅵ Barabwo tiga babe buna obonyansi bore kerama igoro,
bogosusana botarakina;
Ⅶ tibori goichora okoboko kw’omosangereria,
gose kobogwa omosaima okoboko.
Ⅷ Abanto bagoeta inchera tibakonyaara goteeba,
“Omonene abasesenie!”
Gose goteeba, “Nkobasesenia tore inwe ase erieta ri’Omonene!”
