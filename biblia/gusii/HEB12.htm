Okogambera kw’Omonene
Ⅰ Ase ayio, ekiagera intwe tore nabarori abange buna igo, batoetanaine buna riire, totugute are kera egurube, na ebibe biria bitobwatete. Tominyoke noboremereria ase okoamererania koria kobekire bosio bwaito, Ⅱ na korigereria Yeso, omochaki na omoikerania bw’okwegena gwaito. Ribaga ri’omogooko oyobegetwe bosio bwaye, akaremereria omosalaba na gochaaya obosooku, ere nigo aikaransete ensemo ya borio y’ekerogo ki’obonene kia Nyasae.
Ⅲ Gaki, morengererie mono ere oyoremereretie oboengereria buna obwo bokorwa ase abanyene ebibe igoro yaye ere omonyene, timocha kwebutokania na gokwa emioyo yaino. Ⅳ Timorarwana esegi goika mwaiteire amanyinga aino ase okoamererania nebibe, Ⅴ 12:5: Ayubu 5:17; Emeb 3:11,12naende mwebire ogosorora koria kogokwana nainwe buna abana,
“Omwana one, tochaya ogoakwa kw’Omonene kw’ogokorongeyia,
gose tokwa omoyo karakoe egesusuro.
Ⅵ Naki ere oyo Omonene anchete nigo akomoorongeyia,
na kong’wania bororo omwana oyo obeire oyoye.”
Ⅶ Ekero mokorongeigwa moremererie Nyasae nigo akobakorera buna abana baye. Naki, mwana ki otari korongeigwa na ise? Ⅷ Korende onye mokoba motari nokorongeigwa oko bonsi babwataine nakorokwo, rirorio nigo more abana ebimeru gose tari abana ba boronge. Ⅸ Amo nayio ntwarenge nabatata baito b’ense eye, baare gotorongeyia, na barabwo ntwabasigete. Inee! Tari buya mono kobua kwebeka inse ya Tata bw’emeika yaito, na koba moyo? Ⅹ Naki ekeene, abatata baito b’ense nigo batorongeetie ase amatuko amake buna baroche buya, korende ere nigo agotorongeyia ase ogotokonya, erinde tobe ababwatani b’obochenu bwaye. Ⅺ Kera okorongeigwa ase engaki yaye gokororekana ng’a tikori egento ki’omogooko, korende neki’omoichano. Korende magega yaye abakonarire banyore okwama kw’omorembe na okw’oboronge.
Ⅻ 12:12: Isaya 35:3Ase ayio, mobeke chinguru amaboko ayio ororobire, na amaru ayagataire. ⅩⅢ 12:13: Emeb 4:26Mokorere amagoro aino chinchera chindonge, erinde ekere ekerema tikeotoranwa, korende kobua mono kegwenigwe.
Ogokuurera kw’ogotaanga obuya bwa Nyasae
ⅩⅣ Kora omokia koba nomorembe nabaanto bonsi, na rigia obochenu, ekiagera onde tari orarore Omonene, atari naborobwo. ⅩⅤ 12:15: Okoir 29:18Morigererie mono ng’a onde taremerwa obuya bwa Nyasae, “omori bw’obororo” tocha gokumbaria na kobachanda, na abanto abange batambokerigwe ogotacheneka ase oyio. ⅩⅥ 12:16: Omoch 25:29-34Morigererie ng’a onde taba omotomani, gose oyotamosigeti Nyasae, buna Esau, oyoonetie omwando oye bw’okoiborwa kw’obotangi ase endagera eyemo. ⅩⅦ Naki mwamanyire buya ng’a ekero achiete korigia omwando bw’ogosesenigwa korwa magega, agakanigwa, naki tanyora ribaga ri’okoonchoka nonya oririgetie mono namariga.
ⅩⅧ 12:18: Okong’ 19:16-22; 20:18-21; Okoir 4:11,12; 5:22-27Naki timoraika ase egetunwa keranyare gokunwa, kegwoka omorero, gose omosunte, ne riire rimwamu, na ekembobe, ⅩⅨ na egeturi ki’egetureeri, na eriogi ri’amang’ana. Abwo bayaigwete bagasorora tibateebigwa ing’ana rinde naende. ⅩⅩ 12:20: Okong’ 19:12,13Naki tibanyarete koremereria ring’ana riria riachiigetwe, “Nonya neng’iti eise gokuna egetunwa nabo eraakwe amagena.” ⅩⅪ 12:21: Okoir 9:19Aya arorekanete igo arenge ay’okwoboyia, goika Musa agateeba, “Nabwatirwe nomoondoko na okoiguswa.” ⅩⅫ Korende inwe mwaikire egetunwa gekorokwa Sayoni, na omochie o Nyasae ore moyo, Yerusalemu ya igoro, na abamalaika abange batari kobareka, ⅩⅩⅢ na omosangererekano bw’omoyega, na omosangererekano bw’abaiborwa b’eritang’ani, abwo amarieta abo ariikire igoro, na Nyasae oyore omogambi bw’abaanto bonsi, na emeika y’abanto abanyene oboronge abaikeraniigwe, ⅩⅩⅣ 12:24: Omoch 4:10na Yeso oyore omobwatania bw’okobwatana okoyia, na amanyinga akonyororokerigwa ayagokwana buya kobua aria ay’Abeli.
ⅩⅩⅤ 12:25: Okong’ 20:22Rigereria, timomwanga ere oyogokwana. Naki onye baria tibetooretie baria bamwangete ere oyobakuurerete ase ense, kobua mono intwe titogwetooria onye tokomwanga ere oyogotokuurera korwa igoro. ⅩⅩⅥ 12:26: Hag 2:6Na eriogi riaye ndiatengechetie ense ekero keria, korende bono oriire eira, ogoteeba, “Ara rimo naende nintengechie ense, gose tari ense yoka, korende na igoro boigo.” ⅩⅩⅦ Korende ring’ana eri, “Ara rimo naende”, nigo rikoorokia, ng’a ebinto biria bigotengechigwa nigo birang’anyigwe ekiagera biatongetwe, erinde ebitanyare gotengechigwa bibeo. ⅩⅩⅧ Gaki, ekiagera twanyorire oborwoti obotanyare gotengechigwa, tiga toiranerie ng’a mbuya mono na komokorera Nyasae ase okomogokia amo namasikani na okoiroka. ⅩⅩⅨ 12:29: Okoir 4:24Naki Nyasae oito nomorero okoria na gosiria.
