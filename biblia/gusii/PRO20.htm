ⅩⅩ
Ⅰ Edivai nigo ekorenta echeche, ekenyugwa egetindi nigo gekorenta eriomana; na onde bwensi otari gokianga nigo akoborwa obong’aini.
Ⅱ Okogosa kw’omorwoti nigo konga buna okoruma kw’endo, na oyo okomogechia oba n’endamwamu, oyio nigo akogera obogima bwaye bosirigwe.
Ⅲ Nigo are amasikani ase omonto ogwetanga korwa ase eriomana, korende kera omonto ore omoriri nigo akona‐kwomana.
Ⅳ Omonto omworo tari korema omogondo ase engaki egwenerete, na ekero rigesa riaikire, ere gakogeenda korata, takonyora gento.
Ⅴ Emeroberio ere ase enkoro y’omonto nigo enga buna amaache are ase endiba, korende omonto ore nokomanya nigo akoyabuucha korwa aroro.
Ⅵ Abanto abange nigo bakweraria ng’a nabaya bare, korende ning’o oranyaare konyora omonto omwegenwa bori?
Ⅶ Omonto omonyene oboronge obwatetie chiinchera chindonge, abana baye ’mbasesenigwe korwa magega yaye.
Ⅷ Omorwoti ogoikaransa ase ekerogo kiaye gi’ekegaambero nigo agoserebania amabe onsi namaiso aye.
Ⅸ Ning’o oranyaare goteeba, “Nasibekire ase enkoro y’ane; nachenekire korwa ase ebibe biane”?
Ⅹ Ebirengero*20:10 Ebirengero: nabio ebipiimo bi’oborito, na ebi’obonge, bire ara kabere, nigo bire okogechia ase Omonene.
Ⅺ Nonya nomwana nigo amanyekanete ase ogokora kwaye, gose orogendo rw’aye nigo rorabe ororonge na orw’ekeene.
Ⅻ Ogoto gokoigwa, na eriiso rikorora, bionsi bibere ’Omonene obikorete.
ⅩⅢ Tobaisa gwancha chitoro, obaise koba omotaka; ramokia amaiso ao, naye ’nonyore endagera enyinge.
ⅩⅣ Omogori egento nigo agoteeba: “Eke tigeisaini gento”, na ekero akeirire bwetogia.
ⅩⅤ Etaabu nereo, na amagena amaange ye rigori rinene, korende ebikoba bigokwana ay’obong’aini nigo binga buna amagena ye rigori rinene akomeka‐meka.
ⅩⅥ Bogoria eanga y’omonto bwerure koba intenenera ase omonto onde, ira biria abwate, ekiagera bwerure ase engencho y’omonto otamanyekaneti.
ⅩⅦ Endagera ekonyorwa ase enchera y’obong’ainereria, nigo ekoba enyansu ase omonto, korende magega yaye omonwa bw’omonto oyio nigo ogoichorwa n’omokenye.
ⅩⅧ Emeroberio nigo egoikeranigwa goetera ase ogoseemia okuya na gokorwana esegi, yerwane n’obosemia obuya.
ⅩⅨ Omonto okona‐goeta‐etia obogenki nigo akoorokia obobisi, ase ayio tobaisa kobwatana n’omonto otari kweriita ase amang’ana aye.
ⅩⅩ Omonto oraragererie ise, gose ng’ina, oyio etaya yaye nerimigwe ekero ki’omosuunte ekerima.
ⅩⅪ Oise gosanera konyora omwando bwango korwa omochakano, omoerio oye tori gosesenigwa.
ⅩⅫ Tobaisa goteeba, “Goika ng’akane amabe nakoreirwe.” Oganye Omonene, nere oragokoonye.
ⅩⅩⅢ Ebirengo†20:23 rigereria 20:10 bi’oborito bire ara kabere nigo bire okogechia ase Omonene, na ekerengero kioboriania nigo kere ekebe.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omonene nere okoraa chiinchera chi’omonto, ase ayio ’naki omonto aranyare komanya chiinchera chiaye?
ⅩⅩⅤ Nigo ekoba omoeto ase omonto karabe omwango ase ogwatanana egento kebe egechenu, na magega yaye oba okorengereria aya arierete eira.
ⅩⅩⅥ Omorwoti ore omong’aini nigo akomanya abakori amabe obaatanana, erio obatacha n’amagoro ye chigaari.
ⅩⅩⅦ Omoika ore ase omonto, n’oro etaya y’Omonene; nigo ekomoreka na komanya amang’ana onsi are ime y’enkoro y’omonto.
ⅩⅩⅧ Obuya na obwegenwa, nabio bikorenda omorwoti, na ekerogo kiaye ki’obogaambi nigo kegokong’igwa namaabera.
ⅩⅩⅨ Chinguru nachio chire obonene bw’abamura, korende chimbuche nigo chire obwari bw’abaanto abagotu.
ⅩⅩⅩ Obororo bw’ogoakwa nenyimbo nigo bogosibia ebibe, boigo ogoakwa kw’ebiranya nigo kogosoa ase enkoro ime kwayesibia.
