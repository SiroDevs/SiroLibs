Daudi ogotama korwa ase Saulo
Ⅰ 21:1: Mat 12:3,4; Mar 2:25,26; Luk 6:3Daudi akagenda ase Ahimeleki Omokuani agwo Nobu. Ahimeleki agacha komoorotota ore nobwoba na okoiguswa, akamobooria: “Nase ki ore aye bweka otari na monto onde?”
Ⅱ Daudi akairaneria Ahimeleki Omokuani: “Omorwoti onchiikire gokora omoremo gete; ere nigo anteebia ng’a timbaisa goteebia monto onde, erinde amanye igoro y’omoremo oyio antomire na gonchiika ng’a nkore. Naende onchikiire ng’a ng’umerane nabasomba baane aase gete. Ⅲ Bono ndagera ki obwate aa? Ing’a emegati etano, gose kende gionsi obwate.”
Ⅳ Omokuani oria akairaneria Daudi: “Timbwati emegati eria ya botaambe aiga, korende emegati eria ere emechenu nereo, na nigo ndakoyee onye abamura abwo betangete na kwerita korwa ase abakungu.”
Ⅴ Daudi akairaneria Omokuani, akamoteebia: “Ekeene, twabeire twetangete korwa ase abakungu ase amatuko atato aerire, buna engencho yaito ere botaambe, ekero tokogenda gokora emeremo gete; boigo ebikorero bi’abamura aba nigo bire ebichenu botaambe, ekero tokogenda ase orogendo. Onye ayio are bo, naki rende reero ebikorero biabo tibiri ebichenu goetania?”
Ⅵ 21:6: Abal 24:5-9Ase ayio Omokuani akaa Daudi emegati eria emechenu, ekiagera mogati onde torengeo otatiga eria konya yarusigwe korwa ase obosio bw’Omonene, erinde eyere iberera ebekweo ase rituko erio.
Ⅶ Bono ase rituko erio omonto oyomo o Edomu, okorokwa Doegi, nao areenge ase obosio bw’Omonene osiekeireo. Ere nigo areenge omoteneneri bw’abarisia ba Saulo.
Ⅷ Daudi akabooria Ahimeleki: “Nobwate aa ritimo gose omoro? Inche tinarentanete omoro one, gose ebirwanero binde bionsi, ekiagera omoremo oyio omorwoti anchiigete gokora nigo oganetie okorwe ase obwango.”
Ⅸ 21:9: 1 Sam 17:51Omokuani oria akamoteebia: “Omoyio o Goliato, Omofilisti oria gwaitete agwo ase ense omweremo ya Ela, nore aa, oboire egetambaa na kogachwa magega y’egesibao ki’obokuani. Onye koyoganetie yoire, ekiagera onde omoao tori aa.”
Daudi agateeba: “Onde tori onga buna oyio, yong’e”.
Ⅹ Ase rituko erio Daudi akarua agwo, agatama korwa ase Saulo, akagenda ase Akisi, omorwoti o Gati, Ⅺ 21:11: 1 Sam 18:7; 29:5Abasomba b’Akisi bakamokwana: “Inee! Oyo tari Daudi Omorwoti? Abakungu tibaterete igoro yaye kobagotenga, bakobora; Saulo oitire chilifu chiaye, korende Daudi oitire chilifu chiaye emerongo na emerongo?”
Ⅻ 21:12: Zab 56Ekero Daudi aigwete amang’ana ayio, akayabeeka ase ebirengererio biaye, akairoka Akisi, omorwoti o Gati, mono. ⅩⅢ 21:13: Zab 34Ase igo akaonchora okoba gwe chingencho chiaye ase amasio abo, agekora buna ebarimo na kona‐koara ebisieri biabo; na agatiga amate akana‐konyororoka korwa monwa, agaitekera chinderu chiaye. ⅩⅣ Akisi agateebia abasomba baye: “Rora omonto oyo nebarimo. Nase ki mwamorenta ase ’nde? ⅩⅤ Inee! Inche nigo mborire Abanto chibarimo? Ninki kiagera mwarenta ebarimo eye gocha kogosoria obobarimo bwaye ase obosio bwane? Inee! Omonto buna oyo nanyore ribaga asoe nyomba mwane?”
