Ⅰ 3:1: Omoch 22:22Erio Sulemani agachaaka koagacha enyomba y’Omonene agwo Yerusalemu, igoro ase egetunwa kia Moria, agwo ase Omonene aorokerete Daudi ise, Daudi nigo achorete aase agwo ase ekeooreri ki’Orinani*3:1 Orinani: rora 1 Amg 21:15 Omoyebusi kiarenge. Ⅱ Omorwoti Solomoni agachaaka koagacha Hekalu omotienyi o kabere bw’omwaka o kane bw’okogamba kwaye. Ⅲ Ebirengo Sulemani aroseretie enyomba ya Nyasae, nigo biarenge obotambe bwaye nigo bwarenge chimita emerongo ebere na isano na ibere (27), na obogare bwaye nigo bwarenge chimita kianda (9). Ⅳ Enyomba eria yaagachetwe bosio bwe Hekalu nigo yarenge omotambe bw’echimita kianda (9) korengana no’obotambe bw’enyoma eyio, na obogare bwaye nigo bwarenge chimita emerongo etano na ene (54). Chinyasi chiaye chia ime nigo chiaomire ne chitaabu chichenire. Ⅴ Chinyasi chia ime chie Hekalu enyene agachituba ne chibao chi’emetarakwa, naende agachiooma ne chitaabu chingiya, agachichabera nemegwekano y’emekendo na ey’ebinyuri. Ⅵ Naende agachabera enyomba eria namagena e rigori rinene. Chitaabu chiria nigo chiarwete Parawaimu. Ⅶ Akaooma enyomba yonsi ne chitaabu; emiamba, na emetobwa, ne chinyasi, na ebisieri, ebinto ebio bionsi akabiooma ne chitaabu, akabaacha emegwekano y’ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba ase chinyasi. Ⅷ 3:8: Okong’ 26:33,34Akaroisia Aase Aachenu Mono, akanyomba karia ka ime. Obotambe bwaye nigo bwarenge chimita kianda (9), bwareng’aine n’obogare bwaye obwo bwarenge naborobwo chimita kianda (9). Agakaooma ne chitaabu chingiya chiarenge goetania chitani emerongo ebere (20). Ⅸ Oborito bw’emesumari nigo bwarenge chigiramu amagana atano n’etano ebere (570) chi’echitaabu. Ebinyomba bia irongo boigo bikaoomwa netaabu.
Ⅹ 3:10: Okong’ 25:18-20Ime y’Aase Achenu Mono akaroisia emegwekano ebere y’ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba, akayeooma ne chitaabu. Ⅺ Obotambe bwe chimbaaba chionsi chi’ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba nigo bwarenge chimita 4.4. Obotambe bw’orobaaba oromo rw’egetongwa kebwate chimbaba ekemo nigo bwarenge chimita 2.25 kera orobaba, orwo rwaramboire rogaika enyasi y’enyomba. Na orobaaba ronde rw’obotambe buna obwo rogakunana norobaaba rw’egetongwa kende kere n’echimbaba gekebwekaine. Ⅻ Boigo orobaba oromo rw’egetongwa kebwate chimbaba nigo rwakunete enyasi ase ensemo ya kabere ansemo y’enyasi y’enyomba, n’orwakabere rogakunana n’egetongwa keria kende kebwate chimbaba ase egati gati y’enyomba. ⅩⅢ Obotambe bw’echimbaba ibere bw’ebitongwa biria bibwate chimbaba nigo bwarenge bw’chimita 4.5 nigo chiateneine chirigereranetie n’amasio y’ebitongwa ebio bibwate chimbaba arigereretie eero. ⅩⅣ 3:14: Okong’ 26:31Akaroisia etoto y’egetambaa korwa ase chiusi chire buru, ne chimonyinga, ne chimbariri, amo ne chiusi chi’egatani engiya, akayeroisia ebe nemegwekano y’ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba.
Chisiiro ibere chi’etai
(1 Abar 7:15-22)
ⅩⅤ Omorwoti Solomoni akaroisia ase obosio bw’enyomba chisiiro ibere chire nobotambe bw’echibuti 52. Kera esiro nigo yabwate egutwa y’echibuti isano na ibere (7) igoro yaye. ⅩⅥ Akaroisia ebinyuri, akabibeka igoro ase chisiiro chiria; akaroisia nemegwekano y’amakomomanga†3:16 amakomomanga: engencho yaye n’amatunda akwama ase ense ya Asia agwansa rigana erimo (100), akayebeka igoro ase ebinyuri biria. ⅩⅦ Agatenenia chisiiro chiria bosio bwe Hekalu, eyemo ensemo ya Irianyi na eyende ensemo ya sugusu. Eria yarenge ensemo ya Irianyi akayeroka Yakini, na eria yarenge ensemo ya sugusu akayeroka Boazi.
