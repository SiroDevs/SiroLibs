Ⅰ Yeso akagenderera kobateebia, “Ekeene nabateebirie: Mbare abanto bande ase aba bateneine aiga, abataetere amakweri goika barorire oborwoti bwa Nyasae bogocha ne chinguru.”
Okoonchoka ekieni kwa Yeso
(Mat 17:1-13; Luk 9:28-36)
Ⅱ 9:2: 2 Petr 1:17,18Na magega ase amatuko atano na rimo Yeso akaira Petero, na Yakobo, na Yohana, ase egetunwa egetambe barabwo boka, akaonchoka ekieni kiaye ase bare. Ⅲ Chianga chiaye chikameka‐meka, chikaba chindabu mono, buna omosibia onde ase ense atanyare gochisibia chibe chindabu. Ⅳ Bakaorokerwa na Elija amo na Musa; na barabwo nigo barenge gokwana na Yeso. Ⅴ Petero agateebia Yeso, “Omwarimu, mbuya intwe koba aiga, tiga tokore ebigutu bitato: ekemo ekiao, ekemo kia Musa, na ekemo ki’Elija.” Ⅵ Inaki tamanyete eki agokwana, ekiagera babete nomoondoko omonene. Ⅶ 9:7: Mat 3:17; Mar 1:11; Luk 3:22Korende riire rigaacha rikababisia; eriogi rikarwa ase riire riria, rigateeba, “Oyo n’Omwana one omwanchwa, moigwere ere!” Ⅷ Erio bwango kobarigereirie aria na aaria, tibarora monto onde, otatiga Yeso bweka amo nabarabwo.
Ⅸ Kabagotirimboka korwa ase egetunwa akabakania tibatorera monto ayabaroche, goika Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto kabokire korwa ase abakuure. Ⅹ Bakabwata ring’ana riria, bakaboorania barabwo ase barabwo, ng’a okoboka korwa ase abakuure, engencho yaye ninki. Ⅺ 9:11: Malk 4:5; Mat 11:14Bakabooria Yeso, “Nase ki aborokia b’amariiko bagoteeba ng’a negwenerete Elija ache ritang’ani?” Ⅻ Akabateebia, “Nekeene Elija ogocha ritang’ani, na koroisia onsi; korende amo naya, ekiagera ariikire ng’a Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto nachandwe namange na gochaaywa? ⅩⅢ Korende nabateebirie: Elija oikire gocha, na barabwo bakamokorera onsi aya barigetie, buna ariikeretwe.”
Yeso Okogwenia omomura obwatire nekerecha
(Mat 17:14-21; Luk 9:37-43)
ⅩⅣ Kobaikire ase aborokigwa bande, bakarora omoganda omonene obaetanaine; aborokia b’amariiko bakaengererania nabarabwo. ⅩⅤ Erio omoganda bwonsi, ogachia korora Yeso, ogakumia mono; abwo bakamochiera bwango, bakamokwania. ⅩⅥ Yeso akaboria aborokigwa baye, “Ninki mokoengererania nabarabwo?” ⅩⅦ Omonto oyomo ase omoganda akamoiraneria, “Omworokia, namorenta omomura one ase ore, obwatire nekerecha kemokorete rimama; ⅩⅧ ekero kiamobokire nigo gekomogaragaria inse, asokia risombogoku, na koriania amaino; ase ayio chinguru chiaye chisira. Ngakwana naborokigwa bao bageserie, tibanyara.” ⅩⅨ Yeso akabairaneria, “Inwe ekegori getari nokwegena, nimbe nainwe goika ririri? Nindemererie nainwe goika ririri? Mondetere aa!” ⅩⅩ Bakamorenta asare. Ekero kiarora Yeso, bwango ekerecha keria gekaigusia omomura oria, na ere akagwa inse akegaragaria, agasokia risombogoku. ⅩⅪ Yeso akaboria ise, “Nkorwa kero ki anyoretwe naya?” Agateeba, “Nkorwa obwana bwaye. ⅩⅫ Ara aange kemoruta ase omorero na ase amaache, erinde kemosirie, korende koranyare gokora ring’ana, tororere amaabera, otokonye.” ⅩⅩⅢ Yeso akamoteebia, “Koranyare! Onsi nigo akonyarekana asare ere oyobwegenire.” ⅩⅩⅣ Bwango ise omwana oria agaaka eriogi, akarera, akabora, “Negenire, korende okwegena kwane n’ogoke nkoonye!” ⅩⅩⅤ Yeso agachia korora ng’a omoganda ngosangererekana ore bwango, agakania ekerecha keria ki’eubi, akageteebia, “Aye ekerecha rimama na egetiino, inche nagochiikire, morwe, tomoiranera naende!” ⅩⅩⅥ Erio gekarera, gekamogaragaria mono gekamorwa. Omwana oria akaba buna oyokuure, goika abange bakaboora, “Okuure.” ⅩⅩⅦ Korende Yeso akamobwata okoboko, akamoimokia, na ere agatenena. ⅩⅩⅧ Gasoire ase enyomba, aborokigwa baye bakamobooria bobisi, “Inaki intwe totanyara kogeseria?” ⅩⅩⅨ Akabateebia, “Ekerecha kenga buna eke tigekonyara korwa ase ring’ana rinde rionsi, otatiga ase ogosaba.”
Yeso ogokwana naende eriakabere igoro y’ogokwa kwaye
(Mat 17:22-23; Luk 9:43-45)
ⅩⅩⅩ Yeso akarua aria, na’borokigwa baye bagaeta gati‐gati ya Galili; Yeso tancha omonto amanye ase are, ⅩⅩⅪ ekiagera arenge kworokereria aborokigwa baye, akabateebia ng’a, “Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto nkogenda are kobekwa ase amaboko y’ abanto, na barabwo nabamoite, na ekero aitirwe naboke magega y’amatuko atato.” ⅩⅩⅫ Korende tibamanya ring’ana riria, bakairoka komoboria.
Ning’o ore omonene?
(Mat 18:1-5; Luk 9:46-48)
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Bagaika Kapernaumu. Ekero arenge nyomba ime, Yeso akaboria aborokigwa baye, “Ninki mware koamererania inchera?” ⅩⅩⅩⅣ 9:34: Luk 22:24Bagakira, ekiagera ekero barenge inchera baare koamererania barabwo ase barabwo ning’o ore omonene kobua abande. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ 9:35: Mat 20:26,27; 23:11; Mar 10:43,44; Luk 22:26Yeso agaikaransa inse, akarangeria baria ikomi na babere, akabateebia, “Omonto origetie koba oyo bw’eritang’ani nabe oyo bw’omoerio kobua bonsi, na boigo omosomba o bonsi.” ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Akabogoria omwana, akamotenenia gati‐gati yaabo, akamoagata, akabateebia, ⅩⅩⅩⅦ 9:37: Mat 10:40; Luk 10:16; Yoh 13:20“Onde bwensi oramwanche omwana oyomo buna oyo ase erieta riane, oyio onyanchire inche; na oyoranyanche inche, tanyancheti inche, korende ere oyontomete.”
Onde bwensi otari mamincha yaito nigo are naintwe
(Luk 9:49-50)
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Yohana akamoteebia, “Omworokia, ntwarora omonto ogoseria ebirecha ase erieta riao; tokamokania, ekiagera tare gototunyana intwe.” ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Yeso agateeba, “Timomokania, ekiagera monto onde tari oranyare gokora ebikone ase erieta riane, anyare erio bwango gonteeba bobe, ⅩⅬ 9:40: Mat 12:30; Luk 11:23ekiagera oyo otari mamincha yaito, oyio nenseemo yaito are. ⅩⅬⅠ 9:41: Mat 10:42Inaki onde bwensi orabae inwe amaache negekombe ase erieta riane, ekiagera inwe more abanto ba Kristo, nekeene nabateebirie: Tagosiria eng’eria yaye nonya ng’ake.
Ogotatanga abande
(Mat 18:6-9; Luk 17:1-2)
ⅩⅬⅡ “Onde bwensi oragere oyomo bw’aba abake abanyegenete akore ebibe, anga mbuya kobua ase omonto oria asungerwe orogena rogosia ase rigoti riaye, na gotugutwa ime y’enyancha. ⅩⅬⅢ 9:43: Mat 5:30Na onye okoboko kwao gokogera okore ebibe, konache; mbuya gosoa ase obogima kore ekerema, kobua koba namaboko abere, na kogenda Yeanamu [ase omorero o kare na kare oyo otakorimeka, ⅩⅬⅣ naboigo amakonde abo tagokwa]. ⅩⅬⅤ Na onye okogoro kwao gokogera okore ebibe, konache; mbuya gosoa ase obogima kore egesegora, kobua koba n’amagoro abere, na gotugutwa Yeanamu [ase omorero o kare na kare ⅩⅬⅥ aase amakonde abo atagokwa, na omorero otakorimeka]. ⅩⅬⅦ 9:47: Mat 5:29Na onye neriso riao rikogera okore ebibe, orirusie oritugute; mbuya gosoa ase oborwoti bwa Nyasae kore egetong’o, kobua koba namaiso abere, na gotugutwa Yeanamu [ase omorero o kare na kare, ⅩⅬⅧ 9:48: Isaya 66:24aase amakonde abo atagokwa na omorero otakorimeka].
Igoro y’omonto
(Mat 5:13; Luk 14:34-35)
ⅩⅬⅨ “Inaki kera omonto nabekwe omoonyo ase omorero. Ⅼ 9:50: Mat 5:13; Luk 14:34,35Omoonyo nomuya, korende omonyoo korasirie obwansu bwaye, ninki morayobeke bwanse? Mobe n’omoonyo ime ase more, momenye ase omorembe inwe ase inwe.”
