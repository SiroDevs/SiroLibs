Ogosaba Kw’oboitongo
(Ogoteera kwa Daudi)
Ⅰ Omonene, ase enkoro yane tindi kweimokereria igoro,
gose tindi omwerori.
Inche tinyechaandeti kwerigeria amang’ana amanene,
gose ayaare amakong’u mono ase ’nde.
Ⅱ Korende inche nigo intoereretie omoyo one;
buna omwana otigire okogonka oagatire koboko kwa ng’ina,
ee, naboigo omoyo one ore ime yane.
Ⅲ Inwe Abaisraeli, mosemerie Omonene
korwa bono na goika kare na kare.
