Yeso ogosoera aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai
(Mar 12:38-40; Luk 11:37-54; 20:45-47)
Ⅰ Naende Yeso agateebia emeganda na aborokigwa baye, Ⅱ “Aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai baikaransire ase ekerogo kia Musa. Ⅲ Gaki, amang’ana onsi bakobateebia, moyanare na koyabwata; korende, timokora buna barabwo bagokora. Ⅳ Inaki nigo bakoboa chigurube chindito, bachitweka ase amareko y’abanto, korende barabwo abanyene tibari gwancha gochitigeria nekiara kiabo. Ⅴ 23:5: a Mat 6:1; b Okoir 6:8; c Omob 15:38Na ogokora kwabo gwonsi nigo bagokora ng’a barorwe nabaanto; nigo bakogareyia ebitinti biabo biriikire amariiko, naende batambeyia emebareero y’ogochabera chianga chiabo. Ⅵ Naende nigo banchete aase amasikani ase emeyega, na goikaransa aase ebirogo bi’amasikani ase chisinagogi, Ⅶ na gokwanigwa n’amasikani ase chichiiro, na korokwa Omwarimu n’abanto. Ⅷ Korende inwe timobaisa korokwa Omworokia, ekiagera Omwarimu oino noyomo, na inwe mwensi nabamo. Ⅸ Naende timobaisa koroka monto ase ense tata, ekiagera Iso oino noyomo, oyore igoro. Ⅹ Gose timobaisa korokwa abanene, ekiagera Omonene oino noyomo, nere Kristo. Ⅺ 23:11: Mat 20:26,27; Mar 9:35; 10:43,44; Luk 22:26Nere oyore omonene mono kobua ase more, nigo arabe omokoreri oino, Ⅻ 23:12: Luk 14:11; 18:14na oyo okweimokereria igoro nairanigwe inse, na ere oyokweirania inse naimokererigwe igoro.
ⅩⅢ “Korende obobe nobwaino, aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai, abang’ainereria! Ekiagera mwasiekeire abanto oborwoti bwa igoro; naki inwe abanyene timori gosoa, na baria barigeti gosoa, timori kobaa ribaga basoe. [ ⅩⅣ Obobe nobwaino, aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai, abang’ainereria! Ekiagera mokounereria chinyomba chi’abatakaanwa, na ase obong’ainereria mwasaba ogosaba ogotambe. Ase ayio, inamonyore ekiina ekere ekenene kobua.] ⅩⅤ Obobe nobwaino, aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai, abang’ainereria! Ekiagera nigo mogosiongorokana enyancha na ense enyomo korigia omotunyani oyomo, na ekero abeire omotunyani, mwagera oba omwana bw’omorero o kare na kare ara kabere kobua inwe abanyene.
ⅩⅥ “Obobe nobwaino, abaraai emeuko, inwe abamogoteeba: Onde bwensi kararie emuma ase Hekalu, tari gento, korende onde bwensi kararie emuma ase etaabu ye Hekalu, bwesibire n’emuma yaye. ⅩⅦ Inwe abariri emeuko! Nkereki kere ekenene kobua: netaabu, gose ne Hekalu egokora etaabu ebe enchenu? ⅩⅧ Inwe nigo mogoteeba: Onde bwensi kararie emuma ase egesasiimero tari gento; korende onde bwensi kararie emuma ase ekeng’wanso kere igoro ase egesasiimero bwesibire nemuma yaye. ⅩⅨ Inwe emeuko! Nkere ki kere ekenene kobua: nesadaka, gose negesasiimero kegokora esadaka ebe enchenu? ⅩⅩ Gaki, oyorarie emuma ase egesasiimero nigo ararie ase ekio, na ase ebinto bionsi ebire igoro yaye. ⅩⅪ Na oyo orarie emuma ase Hekalu, nigo ararie ase eyio na ase oyo omenyete imeo. ⅩⅫ 23:22: Isaya 66:1; Mat 5:34Na oyo orarie emuma ase igoro, nigo ararie ase ekerogo ki’obonene kia Nyasae na ase oria ogeikaranserete.
ⅩⅩⅢ 23:23: Abal 27:30“Obobe nobwaino, aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai, abang’ainereria ekiagera nigo mokorua ensemo ya ikomi ye chiarare, na ebinsari, ne chinyeni chinde, na mwatiga amang’ana ayare amanene y’ogochika na oboronge, na amaabera, na okwegena. Naro ayio nabagwenerete moyakore, amo na gotatiga ayande. ⅩⅩⅣ Inwe abaraai emeuko, mwakenonora egechuni, mwamera engamia!
ⅩⅩⅤ “Obobe nobwaino, aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai, abang’ainereria! Ekiagera nigo mogosibia egekombe na esani korwa isiko, korende ime biaichoire nobouri na okogania gotari nekerengo. ⅩⅩⅥ Aye Omofarisai omouko! Sibia ritang’ani egekombe na esani ime, erio nonya nisiko binyare koba ebirabu.
ⅩⅩⅦ 23:27: Ogok 23:3“Obobe nobwaino, aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai, abang’ainereria! Ekiagera nigo mogwekaine nechimbeera chiakire echooka; na chirochio isiko chirorekana ng’a nechingiya, korende ime nigo chiichire namauga y’ebitondo, na euramba*23:27 Euramba: engencho yaye n’obochabu bwa kera engencho yonsi. ⅩⅩⅧ Nainwe nabo more, isiko mwarorekana ase abanto ng’a nabaronge more, korende ime yaino mwaichoirwe nobong’ainereria na ebibe ebinge.
ⅩⅩⅨ “Obobe nobwaino, aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai, abang’ainereria! Ekiagera nigo mokoagaacha chimbeera chi’ababani, na kobaria chimbeera chi’abaronge, ⅩⅩⅩ mogoteeba: Onye ntwarengeo kare ase amatuko ye chisokoro chiaito, anga titwabwatanete nabarabwo ase ogoitera amanyinga y’ababani. ⅩⅩⅪ Igo mwetebire inwe abanyene, ng’a inwe nabana babo more, baria baitete ababani. ⅩⅩⅫ Gaki bono, ichoria ekerengo gie chisokoro chiaino. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ 23:33: Mat 3:7; 12:34; Luk 3:7Inwe ching’iti, oroiboro rw’echinchoka, naki morakore motame ekiina ki’omorero o kare na kare! ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Ase ayio natomire ababani ase more, na abanto abang’aini, na aborokia b’amariiko; abande babo inamobaite na kobaabamba, na abande babo inamobang’wanie emecharichi ase chisinagogi chiaino, na kobachanda omochie ase omochie. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ 23:35: a Omoch 4:8; b 2 Amg 24:20,21Erio nario arabachere amanyinga onsi ayaboronge ayaitegete ase ense, korwa amanyinga y’Abeli, oria omonyene oboronge, goika amanyinga a Zakaria, omwana o Barakia, oria mwaiterete gati ye Hekalu na egesasiimero. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Ekeene nabateebirie ng’a amang’ana aya onsi naache igoro ase ekegori eke.
Yeso ogwechandera omochie o Yerusalemu
(Luk 13:34-35)
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ “Aye Yerusalemu, Yerusalemu, omochie ogoita ababani na kobaaka amagena abatomirwe ase ore! Ng’ara karenga narigirie gosangereria abana bao, buna engoko egosangereria ebichuchu biaye ase chimbaaba chiaye ime, korende timwancha! ⅩⅩⅩⅧ 23:38: Yer 22:5Rora, enyomba yaino mwatigeirwe ritombe†23:38 Ritombe: nenyomba etamenyiri. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ 23:39: Zab 118:26Na inche nabateebirie, timokondora naende nonya ng’ake goika ekero keria morache goteeba: Nasesenirie oyogocha ase erieta ri’Omonene.”
