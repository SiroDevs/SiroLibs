ⅩⅩⅥ
Isaka okomenya Gerari na Beeri‐Seba
Ⅰ Bono enchara yachete ase ense eria, nenchara y’ogokumia, tari eria yachete ritang’ani ase engaki ya Aburaamu. Erio Isaka agachiera Abimeleki, omorwoti bw’Abafilisti, aria Gerari. Ⅱ Na Omonene akaorokera Isaka, akamoteebia: “Totirimboka gochia Misiri, korende omenye ase ense eye ndakworokererie. Ⅲ 26:3: Omoch 22:16-18Obe buna omomenyi ase ense eye, na inche nimbe amo naye, ningosesenie; naki ninkoe aye na ororeeria rwao chinse echi chionsi, na ning’ikeranie eira yane eyio natianete ase Aburaamu iso. Ⅳ Nimente ororeeria rwao robe oronge buna ching’enang’eni chia igoro; ne chinse echi chionsi ninchiee ororeeria rwao; na ase ororeeria rwao ebisaku bionsi bi’ense inabisesenigwe, Ⅴ ekiagera Aburaamu naigwerete amang’ana aane, akabwata aya namochiigete, na amachiiko aane, na aya narigete, amo nogoteebereria kwane.”
Ⅵ Ase igo, Isaka akamenya Gerari. Ⅶ 26:7: Omoch 12:13; 20:2Ekero abanto b’aase aria baamoboretie amang’ana igoro ya mokaye, akabateebia: “Oyo ’mosubati ominto.” Naki nigo oboete goteeba ng’a ere ’mokaye, akarengereria ng’a abanto b’aase aria mbaabaise komoita ase engencho ya Rebeka, ekiagera ere areenge omonyakieni. Ⅷ Ekero konya bamenyire aria engaki entambe, Abimeleki, omorwoti bw’Abafilisti, akarigereria korwa ase ekebao, akarora Isaka bakogosoria na Rebeka mokaye. Ⅸ Erio Abimeleki akarangeria Isaka, akamobooria: “Amaene, oyo ’mokao? Naki gwakora ogateeba ng’a oyo mosubati omino?”
Isaka akamoiraneria: “Nigo nareengereretie ng’a, aande nimbaise goitwa ase engencho yaye.”
Ⅹ Abimeleki akamobooria: “Inki eke gwatokoreire iga? Aande monto onde oito areenge gochia araare na mokao, na aye anga nigo gwatobeka ase okomocha.” Ⅺ Ase ayio Abimeleki agatianera abanto bonsi, akabateebia: “Monto onde bwensi aise gokuna omonto oyo ore aa, gose mokaye, goika aitwe.”
Ⅻ Isaka agasimeka endagera ase ense eria, akagesa ase omwaka oyio ara rigana, n’Omonene akamosesenia. ⅩⅢ Omonto oyio akaba ne chinguru, akagenderera komenteka gokina, goika akaba nokobua okonene. ⅩⅣ Nigo abeete nenibo y’amaicho e ching’ondi ne chiombe, na abasomba abange. Ase igo, Abafirisiti bakamororera ribero. ⅩⅤ (Ne chinsoko chiria chionsi abasomba ba Aburaamu ise Isaka baremete, Abafirisiti konya bachitichire namaroba.)
ⅩⅥ Erio Abimeleki agateebia Isaka: “Karue ase tore, ekiagera aye kwamenyire ne chinguru chinyinge gotobua.”
ⅩⅦ Ase igo, Isaka akang’anya korwa aroro, agatoora ase ense eraire ya Gerari, akamenya aroro. ⅩⅧ Isaka akarubora chinsoko chiria naende, echio chiaremetwe ase engaki ya Aburaamu ise, ekiagera Abafirisiti konya bachitichire magega Aburaamu g akuure. Isaka agachiroka buna ise achirogete ritang’ani.
ⅩⅨ Abasomba ba Isaka bakarema agwo ase ense eraire, bakanyora entuboka y’amaache. ⅩⅩ Abarisia b’ensemo ya Gerari bakaba bakwomana nabarisia ba Isaka, bagoteeba: “Amache aya nayaito.” Ase igo, Isaka Akayeroka entuboka eria Eseki, ekiagera bamwomaneretie aroro.
ⅩⅪ Naende bakarema, bakanyora entuboka ende, nero bakayiomanera. Ase igo, akayeroka Sitina*26:21 Sitina: engencho yaye: eriomana. ⅩⅫ Akaimoka korwa agwo, akagenda akarema akanyora entuboka ende; eyio ero tibayiomanera. Ase ayio akayeroka Rehoboti†26:22 Rehoboti: engencho yaye: aase aagare, akabora: “Naki Omonene otoeire ense engare, na toragenderere komenteka ase ense eye.”
ⅩⅩⅢ Isaka akarua aroro, agatiira gochia Beeri‐Seba. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase obotuko obwo obw’Omonene akamoorokera, akamoteebia: “Inche ninche Nyasae o Aburaamu iso, tibwoboa, ekiagera nigo inde amo naye, ningosesenie aye amo na ororeeria rwao, ase engencho y’omosomba one Aburaamu.” ⅩⅩⅤ Na Isaka akaagacha egesasiimero aroro, akarangeria erieta ri’Omonene, agatenenia chiema chiaye aroro. Na agwo abasomba baye bakarema, bakanyora entuboka.
Isaka na Abimeleki bagokora okobwatana
ⅩⅩⅥ 26:26: Omoch 21:22Erio Abimeleki akarwa Gerari, amo n’Ahusati, omosemia oye ase obogaambi, na Pikoli, oria omochiki omonene bw’abarwani baye, bagachiera Isaka. ⅩⅩⅦ Na Isaka akababooria: “Inki mwacha korigia aa bwone, na ekero nare nainwe nigo mongechete, naende mwanseretie korwa seino?”
ⅩⅩⅧ Bakamoiraneria: “Amaene, twarorire maiso marore ng’a Omonene nigo are amo naye. Na nigo torengereretie ng’a totiananie na tokore okobwatana naye ng’a, ⅩⅩⅨ bono togotokorera bobe bonde, buna intwe totagokunete, gose totagokorerete bobe, otatiga amaya oka; tokagotiga okagenda bwoo nomorembe. Naye bono nomosesenigwa Omonene.” ⅩⅩⅩ Erio Isaka akabakorera omoyega, bakaria na bakanywa. ⅩⅩⅪ Bakaimoka mambia chuni, bagatianania barabwo ase barabwo; na Isaka akabakooba, na barabwo bakarua asare, bakagenda nomorembe.
ⅩⅩⅫ Ase rituko riria riria abasomba ba Isaka bagaacha, bakamorentera amang’ana igoro y’entuboka eria banyorete, bakamoteebia: “Twanyorire amaache.” ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Ere akayeroka Seba; na ase engencho eyio omochie oyio nigo okorokwa Beeri‐Seba goika reero iga.
Esau okonyuoma abakungu babere
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Ekero Esau gaika nemiaka emerongo ene akanyuoma Yuditi, omoiseke o Beeri, Omohiti, na Basemati, omoiseke o Eloni, nere n’Omohiti. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Barabwo babere bagakora obogima bwa Isaka na Rebeka koba nobororo obonge.
