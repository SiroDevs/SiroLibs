Ⅰ Ning’o onga buna omonto omong’aini? Ning’o omanyete engencho y’amang’ana? Obong’aini nigo bogokora obosio bw’omonto bwagirigenta, nigo bokoonchora obokong’u bw’okororekana kwaye.
Igwera Omorwoti
Ⅱ Inche nigo nkogosemia: Oigwere amang’ana omorwoti achiikire, ekiagera nkwarierete Nyasae eira. Ⅲ Toayerera gotiga obokoreri ase obosio bwaye, na tobwatana nogokora okobe, ekiagera ere nigo agokora onsi ayanchete. Ⅳ Ring’ana ri’omorwoti nigo rire nokobua. Ning’o oranyare komobooria, “Aye ngento ki eke ogokora?” Ⅴ Omonto okoigwera amachiiko takonyorwa namabe, na enkoro y’omonto omong’aini nigo ekomanya engaki na enchera y’okonacha boronge. Ⅵ Kera ring’ana ndibwate engaki yaye na enchera yaye y’okonacha boronge, na emechando y’omonto nigo ekomoritoera. Ⅶ Omonto tamanyeti ayagochia koba, na ning’o oranyare komoteebia buna amang’ana arabe? Ⅷ Monto onde tari obwate okobua igoro y’embeo, gose igoro ye rituko ri’ogokwa kwaye; ogotooreka tikori ase esegi, gose obobe tibogotooria abwo bakobokora.
Ababe na abaronge
Ⅸ Ayio onsi nigo nayaroche ekero nabegete ebirengererio biane kwegera aya onsi agokorwa aiga inse y’erioba, ekero omonto oyomo akogambera omonto onde na komokorera bobe ase okomochanda. Ⅹ Nkarora abakori amabe batindekirwe batimogete, na ekero abanto bairanete korwa ase achenu nigo bare kobatogia agwo ase omochie. Ayio boigo mbosa.
Ⅺ Ase engencho obobe botari konacherwa ekiina bwango, chikoro chia Mwanyabaanto nigo chikwerua gokora ayare amabe. Ⅻ Omonyabibe nakora ebibe ara rigana, naende agenderere koba moyo ase engaki entambe; nonya naboigo, nimanyete ng’a nigo erabe buya ase abwo bamoirogete Nyasae, ekiagera bamoirogete. ⅩⅢ Korende abakori amabe tibakonyora amaya ayio, gose obogima bwabo tibokoba obotambe. Nigo banga buna omorengari ogoeta, ekiagera tibamoirogete Nyasae. ⅩⅣ Nare amang’ana abosa kegima agokorekana aiga ase ense; mbareo abanto abanyaboronge bakonyorwa negesusuro ki’ogokora kw’abanyabibe, na boigo abanyabibe banyora obuya bw’ogokora kw’abanyaboronge. Ase ayio ngateeba, “Ayio boigo mbosa.”
ⅩⅤ Ngatogia omogooko, na nkarora ng’a Mwanyabaanto tabwati gento kende kere ekiya aiga inse y’erioba, otatiga arie na konywa, na kwegokia barabwo abanyene. Ayio naro aranyare kobeera asare ekero agendererete gokora emeremo emekong’u ase obogima obo are moyo, obo Nyasae amoeire aiga inse y’erioba.
ⅩⅥ Ekero nabeegete ebirengererio biane komanya obong’aini na korora emeremo emekong’u abanto bagokora aiga ase ense, batari korara chitoro omobaso gose obotuko. ⅩⅦ Erio nkarora ng’a omonto takonyara komanya emeremo ende yonsi ya Nyasae egokorekana aiga inse y’erioba. Omonto naisa gwechanda mono ase okorigia koyemanya, takonyara koyemanya. Nonya omonto omong’aini ogoteeba ng’a namanyete, takonyara koyemanya.
