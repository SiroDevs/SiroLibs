Ogosasimwa kw’emegwekano kw’Abaisraeli gokonacherwa ekiina
Ⅰ Omonto omonyene oboronge nigo agosira, na monto onde tari okorengereria igoro y’ayio; abegenwa ba Omonene nigo bakorusigwao, na monto onde takomanya engencho yaye. Omonyene oboronge nigo akorusigwa korwa ase obobe, Ⅱ osoa ase omorembe. Abwo bagotaara ase oboronge nigo bakoba batimogete ase chimbeera chiabo.
Ⅲ “Inwe abana b’omokuungu omoringoria, inwe ab’ororeeria rw’oyore omotomani na omotayayi, suka gocha ang’e aa. Ⅳ Inee! Inwe ning’o mogochecheria? Ning’o mwasameirie emenwa na mwasokirie chimeme? Inwe nigo more abanto b’ogosaria na oroiboro rw’abang’ainereria. Ⅴ Inwe nigo mogosambeeka nokogania okobe ase egati y’emete enga Emealuni, na inse ya kera omote omobese. Nigo mogoiteera abana baino agwo ase amagoko, inse y’ebitare. Ⅵ Amagena amaterere are ase chindooche, ayio naro egetari kiao; ayio naro agotungetie. Ayio naro kwabeire gokoumorera okoruegwa kw’ebinyugwa, na koyaruera okoruegwa kw’endagera. Inee! Nindemererie ogokora buna okwo? Ⅶ Nigo okobeeka ekerarero kiao ase egetunwa keimokererete igoro mono, erio gwatiira gochiao korua ekeng’wanso. Ⅷ Gwatenenia ekeinyorio kiao korwa ase egesieri moino y’emetobwa. Gwantigire inche, gwasioririe ekiebisio kiao, gwasoire aroro gwakegareeirie. Aye ogechorera oyomo, ogancha korara nere, ekero kwaroche okogosima nokoboko. Ⅸ Nigo kwaimogetie amaguta, okairera omorwoti, okamoirera na amaguta ao amange agotioka buya. Nigo gwatomete abanto bao gochia ase chinse chi’aare, ee, nonya ngochia aase abakuure. Ⅹ Nigo kwerosetie norogendo rwao rw’enchera entambe. Nonya naboigo tigwateebete ng’a ogosemeria tikori. Nigo kwamentetwe chinguru chinyia; ase ayio tikwagoyete.
Ⅺ “Ning’o kwoboete na okamoiroka goika ogakwana oborimo? Tikware kong’inyora gose ondengererie? Inee! Tinabeete ndemereretie ase engaki entambe, naye tikwabeete gokonyiroka? Ⅻ Ningoteebie buna oboronge bwao bore na buna ogokora kwao kore, birobio tibikonyara kogokonya. ⅩⅢ Ekero orabe gokorera, tiga emegwekano ogosasiima gwasangereirie egotoorie. Omwaga noyeire gochia aare, ee, embeo bosa neyeire. Korende oyo orantamere naire ense ebe omwando oye, ee, naire egetunwa kiane egechenu.”
Eira ya Nyasae ey’ogotooria na kogwenia
ⅩⅣ Amang’ana aya nakwanwe: “Ticha gocha igoro! Ticha gocha igoro! Oroisie enchera! Rusia kera ekegocha kere ase enchera y’abanto baane!”
ⅩⅤ Aya naro Oyio ore Igoro Mono, Oyio oragaambe goika kare na kare, Oyio erieta riaye n’Omochenu, agoteeba: “Inche nigo imenyete Aase aachenu are igoro mono, boigo nigo imenyete amo noyio enkoro yaye yeitirie na gwekeyia, erinde ninkore omoika bw’oyore omoitongo obe omoyia, na koa obogima ase oyio enkoro yaye yeitirie. ⅩⅥ Tinkogenderera koamererania gose koba nendamwamu kare na kare. Inse gokora bo, emeika yabo amo nemioyo eyio natongete, nigo eragireke ase obosio bwane. ⅩⅦ Abanto nigo babwate okogania okobe kwe ribero; ase ayio nigo bagokora ebibe bikagera nkaba nendamwamu ase bare, nkabaaka. Nigo nabisete obosio bwane, nkaba nendamwamu, korende barabwo nigo bagendererete gotiga obwegenwa na kobwatia chinchera chie chinkoro chiabo.
ⅩⅧ “Narorire chinchera echio chiabo, korende nimbagwenie. Nimbaraae na kobaremia, nindemie na abwo bakorera. ⅩⅨ 57:19: Abaef 2:17Nimbeeke ase ebikoba biabo okwama okuya, bikwane omorembe ase abwo bare aare na abare ang’e, na inche nimbagwenie.” Nab’Omonene agoteeba. ⅩⅩ “Korende abakori amabe nigo banga buna enyancha yaturubanigwe, etanyare gotoerera; amaache aye nigo agosiara etintira na eubi gocha igoro.” ⅩⅪ 57:21: Isaya 48:22Nyasae one nigo agoteeba iga: “Omokori amabe tari nomorembe.”
