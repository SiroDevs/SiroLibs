ⅩⅫ
Ogokwana gwa Paulo ase Abayahudi
(Ogok 9:1-19; 26:12-18)
Ⅰ “Inwe abaminto, na abatata, ntegerera aya ngwekengera omonyene ase more bono.”
Ⅱ Na ekero bachia koigwa ng’a nigo agokwana nabarabwo ase omonwa bw’Ekeiberania, bakamenta gokira. Agakwana.
Ⅲ 22:3: Ogok 5:34-39“Inche n’Omoyahudi, naiboreretwe Tariso, omochie o Kilikia, nkarererwa ase omochie oyo ase amagoro a Gamalieli, nkorokererigwa bokong’u amachiiko ye chisokoro chiaito. Nkaba omonto oyore nomokia ase Nyasae, buna inwe mwensi more reero iga. Ⅳ 22:4: Ogok 8:3; 26:9-11Ngachanda abanto b’Enchera eye goika nkabaita bagakwa, ngasiba abasacha na abakungu na kobabeeka echela ime, Ⅴ buna Omokuani omonene na abagaaka bonsi b’ekegambero bare kirori ase ’nde. Naetwe amarube nabarabwo ing’ire ase abaminto abarenge Damesiki, erio mbarente abarenge agwo, mbaire Yerusalemu basibire erinde baegwe egesusuro.
Ⅵ “Erio ekero nare kogenda, konaikire ang’e na Damesiki, ekero kia mobaso gati mobosokano amasase akarwa igoro akambarera chinsemo chionsi. Ⅶ Nkagwa inse, nkaigwa eriogi rigonteebia: Saulo, Saulo, nase ki gwanchandire? Ⅷ Nkairaneria: Naye ng’Omonene? Aganteebia: Ninche Yeso o Nazareti oyogwachandire aye. Ⅸ Na baria barenge amo nainche mbaroche amasase aria, korende tibaigwete eriogi ri’oria ore gokwana nainche. Ⅹ Nkaboria: Ninki ndakore Omonene? Omonene aganteebia: Imoka ogende goika Damesiki, agwo noteebigwe onsi aya gwachiikirwe oyakore. Ⅺ Na ekero nasinyetwe korora ase engencho y’oborabu bw’amasase aria, nkaraagwa namaboko abo abarenge amo nainche, ngaika Damesiki.
Ⅻ “Erio omonto oyomo ore korokwa Anania, omoiroki Nyasae ase ogotunyana amachiiko, ore gokwanwa buya n’Abayahudi bonsi abamenyete agwo, ⅩⅢ aganchichera agatenena ang’e nainche, aganteebia: Oyominto Saulo, nyora okorora. Ase ensa eria eria nkanyora okorora, nkamorora. ⅩⅣ Aganteebia: Nyasae bwe chisokoro chiaito ogochorire onyare komanya ogwancha kwaye, na korora Omonyene Oboronge, na koigwa eriogi rikorwa ase omonwa oye. ⅩⅤ Naki nomobeere kirori ase abanto bonsi igoro y’amang’ana aya kwarorire na koyaigwa. ⅩⅥ Gaki bono, inki ogotebanera? Tenena obatiswe, bwesibie ebibe biao, orangerie erieta riaye.
ⅩⅦ “Ekero nairanete Yerusalemu, ngosaba narenge ase Hekalu ime, nkabwatwa nobogondereria, ⅩⅧ nkarora Omonene aroro, ogonteebia: Ayerera, karue Yerusalemu bwango, ekiagera tibagwancha oborori bwao igoro yane. ⅩⅨ Na inche nkaboora: Omonene, barabwo mbamanyete ng’a nigo narenge gosiba baria bakwegenete na kobaaka ase chisinagogi chionsi. ⅩⅩ 22:20: Ogok 7:58Ekero amanyinga a Stefano, kirori oo, agoiterwa, inche nao narenge nateneine ang’e, ngancha aitwe, nkarenda chianga chiabo baria abamoitete. ⅩⅪ Nere aganteebia: Genda, ekiagera inche ningotome aare ase abanto b’Ebisaku.”
Paulo ogoteeba ng’a n’Omorumi are
ⅩⅫ Bagategerera Paulo goika ring’ana riria rigakwanwa. Erio bagaaka eriogi riabo, bagateeba: “Morusieo oyo ase ense, ekiagera tagwenereti abe moyo.” ⅩⅩⅢ Bagachia kona‐goaka egeturi na gotandora chianga chiabo na gosiara‐siara ebuse gochia igoro. ⅩⅩⅣ Omotang’ani bw’abarwani elifu agachiika arentwe ime ase enyomba enene y’abarwani, agachiika aborigwe amang’ana aye ase okong’wanigwa emecharichi, erinde amanye engencho bakomwogerera eriogi buna eri. ⅩⅩⅤ Korende ekero bamosibire engori, Paulo akaboria omotang’ani bw’abarwani rigana, oyo oteneine ang’e nere: “Inee! Mboronge kong’wania omonto Omorumi emecharichi, na ere ataranacherwa ekiina?”
ⅩⅩⅥ Omotang’ani oria ochia koigwa aya, akagenda akoorokia omotang’ani oria omonene, akabora: “Naki origetie gokora? Ekiagera omonto oyo n’Omorumi are.”
ⅩⅩⅦ Omotang’ani oria omonene agacha, akamoteebia: “Nteebia, n’Omorumi ore?” Agateeba: “Ee.” ⅩⅩⅧ Omotang’ani oria, omonene akairaneria: “Inche nigo nagorete obobiare obo ase chitaabu chinyinge.” Paulo agateeba: “Inche n’Omorumi omobiare.”
ⅩⅩⅨ Erio baria barenge ang’e komoboria bakaimoka, bakamotiga bwango, na oria omotang’ani omonene akoboa ekero aigwa ng’a n’Omorumi are, na konya omosibire.
Ogwekengera gwa Paulo bosio bw’ekegambero
ⅩⅩⅩ Rituko rikobwatia akarengereria komanya boronge nase engencho ki asoeirwe n’Abayahudi, akamosibora, agachiika abakuani abanene na abanto b’ekegambero bonsi bache. Agatirimbokia Paulo, akamobeka ase amasio abo.
