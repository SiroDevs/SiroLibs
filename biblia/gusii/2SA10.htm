Ⅹ
Daudi okobua Abaamoni na Abasiiria
(1 Amg 19:1-19)
Ⅰ Magega y’ayio omorwoti bw’Abaamoni agakwa, na Hanuni, omomura oye, akaba omorwoti ribaga riaye. Ⅱ Daudi akabora, “Goika imokorere Hanuni, omomura o Nahasi, amaya, buna ise arenge gonkorera amaya.” Ase igo Daudi agatoma abanto bageende komoremia ase engencho y’ogokwa gwa ise.
Ekero abasomba ba Daudi baigete ase ense y’Abaamoni, Ⅲ Abaamoni bakabooria Hanuni, omonene obo, “Inee! Igo okagete ng’a Daudi nigo asigete iso ase ogotoma abanto gocha gokoremia? Taibo. Nigo abatomire bache koroota omochie na koyosaria.”
Ⅳ Ase igo Hanuni akabwata abasomba abwo ba Daudi, akagingira chinderu chiabo ensemo eyemo, na akanacha chianga chiabo agachiikia ebinema, akabaseria bagende. Ⅴ Abasomba abwo bakarora obosooku obonene mono, na ekero Daudi ateebetigwe buna bakoretwe, agatoma abanto bachie kobarotota baabateebie, “Omorwoti oteebire ng’a mobeere agwo Yeriko goika chinderu chiaino chimere, erio monyare koirana ’nka.”
Ⅵ Abaamoni bakamanya ng’a bagechirie Daudi. Ase igo bagasaba Abasiiria ba Beti‐Rehobu na Soba baabae abarwani babo chilifu emerongo ebere (20,000), na omorwoti o Maaka ache amo nabarwani baye elifu eyemo (1,000); naende bagasaba baegwe abarwani chilifu ikomi na ibere (12,000) korwa Tobu. Ⅶ Ekero Daudi aigwete ayio, agatoma Yoabu amo ne chituoni chionsi chi’eseegi. Ⅷ Abaamoni bakageenda bakarobera ase egeita ki’omochie; Abasiiria ba Soba, na abwo ba Rehobu, amo nabaanto korwa Tobu na Maaka, barabwo bakarobera ao boka ase egetii.
Ⅸ Ekero Yoabu arooche ng’a esegi nigo ere bosio bwaye na magega, agachoora abanto abakong’u abamanyete korwana esegi, akabaroberia barwane n’Abasiiria. Ⅹ Abanto bande abatigari akababeeka inse y’oboteneneri bw’Abisai, momura omwabo, akabaroberia barwane n’Abaamoni. Ⅺ Erio agateebia Abisai, “Onye Abasiiria bakombua, rirorio oche onkonye; korende onye Abaamoni bagokobua, rirorio inche ninchiche ’ngokoonye. Ⅻ Otenene bokong’u obe omoremu! Torwanerere abanto baito na emechie ya Nyasae oito. Tiga Omonene akore ayarooche are amaya.”
ⅩⅢ Agwo Yoabu na abanto baarenge amo nere bagasuka gochia korwana n’Abasiiria, na barabwo bagatama korwa ase are. ⅩⅣ Abaamoni bagachia korora ng’a Abasiiria batamire, na barabwo boigo bagatama korwa ase Abisai, bagasoa omochie ime. Erio Yoabu agatiga korwana n’Abaamoni, akairana Yerusalemu.
ⅩⅤ Ekero Abasiiria barooche ng’a babuirwe n’Abaisraeli, bagasangererekana bonsi amo. ⅩⅥ Hadadeseri agatoma abanto barente Abasiiria abwo barenge moocha y’orooche rw’Eufarati. Barawo bagaacha Helamu, na Sobaki, omotang’ani bw’abarwani ba Hadadeseri, akabatang’ana. ⅩⅦ Ekero Daudi ateebetigwe amang’ana ayio agasangereria Abaisraeli bonsi, akaamboka Yorodani, akageenda Helamu. Aroro Abasiiria bakeroberia korwania Daudi esegi. ⅩⅧ Abasiiria bagatama korwa ase amasio y’Abaisraeli, na Daudi agaita Abasiiria abagendia chigaari amagana atano na abere (700), na abariini chibarasi chilifu emerongo ene (40,000), naende agaaka Sobaki, omotang’ani bw’abarwani, goika agakwerao. ⅩⅨ Ekero abarwani bonsi abwo barenge inse ya Hadadeseri barooche ng’a babuirwe n’Abaisraeli, bagakora omorembe nabarabwo na kwebeeka inse yabo. Ase ayio Abasiiria bakoboa gokonya Abaamoni naende.
