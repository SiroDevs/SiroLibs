Abanto abairanete korwa ase obosomba
Ⅰ Abaisraeli bonsi nigo baabaretwe na korikwa koreng’ana ne chinderia chiabo, na amang’ana abo nigo anyorekanete ase Egetabu ki’ogokora kw’Abarwoti b’Abaisraeli. Abayuda nigo baosetwe*9:1 baosetwe: engencho yaye bairetwe obosomba bakairwa Babiloni, ekiagera tibarenge abegenwa. Ⅱ 9:2: Ezra 2:27; Neh 7:73Abanto batang’anete koirana na komenya sobo ase emechie yabo n’Abaisraeli bande, na abakuani, na Abalawi, na abakori emeremo be Hekalu.
Ⅲ Abanto b’ororeria rwa Yuda, na orwa Benjamini, na orw’Efraimu, na orwa Manase, abwo bamenyete Yerusalemu nabwo aba.
Ⅳ Utai, omwana bw’Amihudi, mosinto bw’Omuri o Imuri, o Bani korwa ase ororeria rwa Peresi, mosinto o Yuda, Ⅴ na korwa Silo Asaia, omwana omotangi amo nabana baye abamura.
Ⅵ Ase ororeria rwa Zera Yeueli amo nabaanto bamwabo. Bonsi nigo barenge abanto amagana atano na rimo, na emerongo kianda (690).
Ⅶ Abanto b’ororeria rwa Benjamini nabwo aba: Salu, mosinto o Mesulamu o Hodawia, o Hasenua; Ⅷ naende Ibunia, mosinto o Yerohamu, na Ela, mosinto o Usi o Mikiri, na Mesulamu, mosinto o Sefatia o Reueli, o Ibunia, Ⅸ amo nabaanto bamwabo ase chinderia chiabo, nigo barenge amagana kianda, na emerongo etano, na batano nomo (956). Aba bonsi nigo barenge abateneneri be chiamate chiabo, kera oyomo ase eamate yaye.
Abakuani abamenyete Yerusalemu
Ⅹ Abakuani aba nabwo bamenyete Yerusalemu: Yedaya na Yehoyaribu, na Yakini; Ⅺ Asaria, mosinto o Hilikia o Mesulamu, o Zadoki, o Meraioti, bw’Ahitubu, omoteneneri omonene bw’enyomba ya Nyasae.
Ⅻ Naende Adaya, mosinto o Yerohamu o Pasuri, o Malikia; na Maasai, mosinto bw’Adieli o Yasera, o Mesulamu, o Mesilemiti, o Imeri.
ⅩⅢ Amo nabwo barenge abamwabo, abateneneri be chiamate chiabo, elifu eyemo, na amagana atano na abere, na emerongo etano nomo (1,760). Bonsi nigo barenge abamanyi ase ogokora emeremo ase enyomba ya Nyasae.
Abalawi abamenyete Yerusalemu
ⅩⅣ Abalawi aba nabwo bamenyete Yerusalemu: Semaya, mosinto o Hasubu bw’Asirikamu, o Hasabia korwa ase eamate y’Abamerari; ⅩⅤ Bakibakari na Heresi, Galali na Matania, mosinto o Mika o Sikiri, bw’Asafu; ⅩⅥ Obadia, mosinto o Semaya o Galali, o Yedutuni; na Berekia, mosinto bw’Asa bw’Elikana, oyomenyete ase emechie y’Abaanetofa.
Abarendi b’ebiita bie Hekalu abamenyete Yerusalemu
ⅩⅦ Aba nabwo abarendi b’ebiita bamenyete Yerusalemu: Salumu na Akubu, Talimoni na Ahimani, na abanto bande bamwabo. Salumu nere orenge omoteneneri obo. ⅩⅧ Abanto abwo bakaba bakorenda agwo ase Egeita ki’Omorwoti, ensemo ya moocha, goika engaki eye. Kare nabwo barenge abarendi b’egetwori ki’Abalawi.
ⅩⅨ Salumu, mosinto o Kore bw’Ebiasafu, o Kora, amo nabaanto bande b’eamate yaye, nabwo Abakora, nigo barenge abateneneri bokorenda ase Ebiita bi’Eema y’Omosangererekano, buna chisokoro chiabo chiarenge korenda ase egesoero ki’egetwori ki’Omonene. ⅩⅩ Finehasi, mosinto bw’Eleazari, nere orenge omogambi agwo kare, n’Omonene nigo arenge amo nere.
ⅩⅪ Zakaria, mosinto o Meselemia, nere orenge omorendi bw’egeita agwo ase egesoero ki Eema y’Omosangererekano.
ⅩⅫ Abanto aba bonsi amagana abere, na ikomi na babere (212) nigo bachoretwe bakaba abarendi b’ebisoero bi’ebiita. Bakabarwa na korikwa ase chinderia chiabo. Daudi na Samweli omoorori bakababeeka ase emeremo, ekiagera babegenete. ⅩⅩⅢ Ase igo barabwo amo nabamura be chinderia chiabo bakagenderera korenda ebiita bi’enyomba y’Omonene, nero Eema eria. ⅩⅩⅣ Abarendi baria b’ebiita bakabeera aria korenda chinsemo chia moocha, na bosongo, na sugusu, na Irianyi. ⅩⅩⅤ Abanto bamwabo abarenge ase emechie yabo bakabetererigwa gocha koba amo nabarabwo ase kera rituko ria gatano na kabere ase engaki yachikiire. ⅩⅩⅥ Abalawi baria baane abarenge abateneneri b’emeremo y’okorenda ebiita nabwo batenenerete ebinyomba ne chikungo chi’enyomba ya Nyasae. ⅩⅩⅦ Barabwo bakaba bakobeera isiko bagaetanana enyomba ya Nyasae, ekiagera emeremo y’oborendi nigo yabegetwe igoro ase bare, naende nabwo barenge koigora enyomba eria kera emambia.
Abalawi bande
ⅩⅩⅧ Abalawi bande nabwo batenenerete ebikorero bi’emeremo, ekiagera nigo baganeirie kobibara ekero biarenge korentwa ime, na ekero biarenge koirwa isiko ase emeremo. ⅩⅩⅨ Abande babo bagachorwa gotenenera chimesa na ebinto binde, na ebikorero ebichenu, na obosie obonuke, na edivai, na amaguta ’amazeituni, na ubani, na ebinsari. ⅩⅩⅩ Abande b’abamura b’abakuani nigo babegetwe kwearigania na gwochogania ebinsari.
ⅩⅩⅪ Matitia, oyomo bw’Abalawi, omomura omotangi o Salumu, omonto bw’eamate ya Kora, agatenenera emeremo y’okoroisia emegati ekoruegwa. ⅩⅩⅫ Boigo abande bamwabo b’eamate ya Kohati bagatenenera emeremo y’okoroisia emegati yarenge koruegwa ase Omonene kera Esabato.
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Abande nigo barenge abateri abanene, boigo nabwo barenge abateneneri be chiamate chi’Abalawi. Barabwo nigo bamenyete ase ebinyomba bie Hekalu; tibarenge gokora emeremo ende emeao, ekiagera omobaso na obotuko nigo barenge ase emeremo baetwe gokora.
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Aba nabwo barenge abateneneri be chiamate chiabo chi’Abalawi ase ebiare†9:34 ebiare: engencho yaye n’ebigori biabo. Abwo nigo barenge abaraai baria bamenyete Yerusalemu.
Chisokoro na ororeria rw’omorwoti Saulo
(1 Amg 8:29-38)
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Yeieli ise Abagibeoni, nigo amenyete agwo Gibeoni na mokaye nigo arenge korokwa Maaka. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Abidoni nere orenge omwana oye omomura omotangi, na abande nabwo aba: Suri na Kisi, Baali na Neri, na Nadabu, ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Gedori na Ahiyo, Zakaria na Mikiloti. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Mikiloti nere orenge ise Simeamu. Aba boigo nigo bamenyete bakabebekana nabamwabo agwo Yerusalemu.
ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Neri akaibora Kisi, na Kisi akaibora Saulo, na Saulo akaibora Yonathani na Malikisua, Abinadabu na Esi‐Baali. ⅩⅬ Yonathani akaibora Meribu‐Baali, na Meribu‐Baali akaibora Mika. ⅩⅬⅠ Abana abamura ba Mika nabwo aba: Pitoni na Meleki, Tarea na Ahasi. ⅩⅬⅡ Ahasi akaibora Yara, na Yara akaibora Alemeti na Asimaweti, na Simuri; nere Simuri akaibora Moza. ⅩⅬⅢ Moza akaibora Binea, na Binea akaibora Refaya, na Refaya akaibora Eleasa, na Eleasa akaibora Aseli. ⅩⅬⅣ Aseli nabwate abana abamura batano nomo, na amarieta abo naro aya: Asirikamu, na Bokeru, na Isumaeli, na Searia, na Obadia, na Hanani. Aba nabwo abana abamura b’Aseli.
