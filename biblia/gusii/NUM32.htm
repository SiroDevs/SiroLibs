ⅩⅩⅫ
Ebisaku Bia Moocha Ya Yorodani
(Okoir 3:12-22)
Ⅰ Abanto b’egesaku kia Rubeni, na ab’ekia Gadi, nigo babwate amaicho amaange mono. Ekero barooche ense ya Yaeseri na eya Gileadi ng’a nigo chire chingiya ase oborisia, Ⅱ bakageenda ase Musa, na Eleazari Omokuani, na ase abaraai b’omoganda bw’Abaisraeli, bakabateebia, Ⅲ “Ataroti, na Diboni, na Yaeseri, na Nimura, na Hesiboni, na Eleali, na Sibamu, na Nebo, na Beoni, Ⅳ nemechie y’ense eyi’Omonene atigete omoganda bw’Abaisraeli okayebua, na ense eye nigo ere engiya ase oborisia bwe chitugo, na intwe abasomba bao nigo tobwate amaicho amaange ye chitugo.” Ⅴ Naende bagateeba, “Onye twanyorire ogwancherwa korwa ase ore, tiga intwe, abasomba bao, toegwe ense eye ebe eyaito, korende tobaisa gotwambokia gochia ng’umbu ya Yorodani komenyao.”
Ⅵ Musa agakwana n’abanto ba Gadi na abareubeni, akabora, “Inee! Abanto bamino nigo bakogeenda korwana esegi, nainwe mobe motigarete aase aa? Ⅶ Nase ki inwe mogoita Abaisraeli emioyo, erinde tibaabaisa kwamboka gochia ng’umbu ya Yorodani, na koira ense eri’Omonene abaeire? Ⅷ 32:8: Omob 13:17-33Amang’ana aya naro chisokoro chiaino chiakorete ekero nabatomete korwa Kadeshi-Barinea gochia koroota ense eyio. Ⅸ Barabwo bakageenda bagaika ase ense omweya y’Esikoli, na ekero barooche ense eria, bakairana bagaita Abaisraeli emioyo ase ogotageenda ase ense eri’Omonene konya abaeire. Ⅹ 32:10: Omob 14:26-35Ase rituko riri’Omonene akaba nendamwamu enene, agatiana, akabora: Ⅺ Ekeene ase abanto bonsi baruete Misiri, aba bakoorire emiaka emerongo ebere na goetania, onde tari, nonya noyomo oranyare korora ense eria narierete Aburaamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo eira ng’a nimbae, ekiagera abanto aba bangire koba abegenwa asende. Ⅻ Ase barabwo onde tari oranyare goika aroro otatiga Kalebu, mosinto o Yefune Omokenisi, na Yoshua, mosinto o Nuni. Barabwo nabaike aroro, ekiagera babeire abegenwa ase ’nde, inche Omonene. ⅩⅢ Omonene akaba nendamwamu enene ase Abaisraeli, akabatiga bakana‐goetanana ase erooro ase engaki y’emiaka emerongo ene, goika abanto bonsi b’ekiaare keria, abwo konya bakorire ebibe ase Omonene, bagasirigwa kegima. ⅩⅣ Na bono, inwe oroiboro rw’ abanto abanyabibe, mwabeire mamincha y’Omonene, buna chisokoro chiaino chiarenge, na mwagerire Omonene omentire endamwamu yaye ase Abaisraeli. ⅩⅤ Inwe komoraange koigwera Omonene, ere nigo aratige abanto aba bonsi agwo ase erooro, nainwe mogere abanto aba bonsi basire.”
ⅩⅥ Abanto b’egesaku kia Rubeni, na ab’ekia Gadi bagaacha ase Musa bakaboora, “Toe ribaga toagache ameri amakong’u aiga ase chitugo chiaito chikobeera, naende toagache emechie egiteire chindwaki ase abana baito baramenye. ⅩⅦ Erio intwe toimokie ebirwanero bi’eseegi, twebeke ang’e totang’ane Abaisraeli korwana esegi goika tobaikie aase abo. Korende abana baito batigare aa, bamenyete ime yemechie egiteire chindwaki, erinde abanto b’ense eye tibaabaisa kobakorera bobe. ⅩⅧ Titokoirana ase chinka chiaito goika torore ng’a Abaisraeli baminto banyorire kera omonto orogongo rw’omwando oye. ⅩⅨ Intwe titokonyora omwando oito amo nabarabwo agwo ng’umbu ya Yorodani, ekiagera twanyorire omwando oito aiga moocha ya Yorodani.”
ⅩⅩ Musa akabairaneria, agateeba, “Onye mogochia gokora bo, rirorio bogoria ebirwanero biaino bi’eseegi ase obosio bw’Omonene, erio mogende korwana esegi. ⅩⅪ Kera omonto ore ase more, oyobogoretie ebirwanero bi’eseegi nigo aramboke gochia ng’umbu ya Yorodani, ase obosio bw’Omonene: mobeereo goika ere abue ababisa baye barue ase are, ⅩⅫ na ense eria ebe yabuirwe ase Omonene. Magega y’ayio nario moranyare koirana aiga motari na komocha, na igo monyore ense eye ebe eyaino, monyare komenya ase obosio bw’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅢ Korende motaikeraneti chiira chiaino mwarire, ekeene rirorio mwakorire ebibe ase Omonene, na mobe nobomaene ng’a nase engencho y’ebibe ebio biaino moranyore egesusuro. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase ayio agacha emechie ase abana baino baramenye na ameri ase ching’ondi chiaino, erio mokore buna mwariire chiira chiaino.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Erio abanto b’egesaku kia Gadi na ab’ekia Rubeni bakamoteebia Musa, “Omonene oito, intwe abasomba bao inatokore buna gwachiikire. ⅩⅩⅥ Abana baito na abakungu barabwo tiga batigare amo ne ching’ondi ne chiombe chiaito aiga ase emechie ya Gileadi. ⅩⅩⅦ Korende intwe abasomba bao, kera oyomo oito oyobogoretie ebirwanero bi’okorwana esegi agwo ase obosio bw’Omonene ’namboke gochia ng’umbu, buna aye omonene oito gwachiikire.”
ⅩⅩⅧ 32:28: Yosh 1:12-15Ase amang’ana y’ abanto abwo, Musa agachiika Eleazari Omokuani, na Yoshua, mosinto o Nuni, na abaraai be chiamate chi’ebisaku bi’Abaisraeli, ⅩⅩⅨ akabateebia, “Onye Abagadi na Abareubeni, abwo bonsi babogoretie ebirwanero bi’eseegi, bakwamboka Yorodani amo nainwe korwana esegi ase obosio bw’Omonene, na inwe mobue ense eria, na koyeira, erio mobae ense ya Gileadi ebe eyabo. ⅩⅩⅩ Korende onye tibakobogoria ebirwanero bi’eseegi bamboke gochia ng’umbu ya Yorodani, rirorio nigo baranyore orogongo rwabo amo nainwe agwo ase ense ya Kaanani.”
ⅩⅩⅪ Abagadi na Abareubeni bakairaneria, bagateeba, “Bun’Omonene atochiikire intwe abasomba bao, nabo torakore. ⅩⅩⅫ Intwe ’ntobogorie ebirwanero biaito bi’eseegi, na twamboke gochia ase ense ya Kaanani ase obosio bw’Omonene, erio nario toratware ense eye ere aa moocha ya Yorodani ebe eyaito.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Ase ayio Musa akaa abanto b’egesaku kia Gadi, na ab’ekia Rubeni, amo nekeng’ese ki’egesaku kia Manase, mosinto o Yusufu, orogongo rwa Sihoni, omorwoti bw’Abaamori, na orw’Ogi, omorwoti o Basani. Amo nechio akabaa boigo emechie y’ense eria amo ne chinse chiaetanaineo. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Abagadi bakaagacha emechie ya Diboni, na Ataroti, na Aroeri, ⅩⅩⅩⅤ na Ataroti‐Shofani, na Yaeseri, ne Yogibea, ⅩⅩⅩⅥ na Beti‐Nimura, na Beti‐Harani, emechie yagiteire chindwaki, naende yabwate ameri ye ching’ondi. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Abareubeni bakaagacha emechie ya Hesiboni, na Eleale, na Kiriataimu, ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Nebo, na Baali‐Meoni (amarieta abo akaonchorwa), na Sibuma. Boigo bakaroka emechie eyio baagachete ase amarieta ande amayia.
ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Abanto ba Makiri, mosinto o Manase, bakageenda bakaumokera ense ya Gileadi, bagaseria Abaamori bamenyeteo, erio bakaira ense eria. ⅩⅬ Musa akaa abanto ba Makiri, mosinto o Manase, ense ya Gileadi, na barabwo bakamenyao.
ⅩⅬⅠ Egesaku kia Yairi, mosinto o Manase, gekageenda, gekaumokera na koira emechie emeke y’ense ya Gileadi, bakayeroka Emechie ya Yairi. ⅩⅬⅡ Na Noba nere akageenda akaumokera na koira Kenati amo nebichie biaetanaineo, nere akaroka Kenati Noba, erieta riaye omonyene.
