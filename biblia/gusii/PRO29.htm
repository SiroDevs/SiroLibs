ⅩⅩⅨ
Ⅰ Omonto okona gokurerwa botambe ase okorongeigwa, korende okong’ia ebigoti, oyio nasirigwe mobosokano, tagokonywa.
Ⅱ Ekero abanyene oboronge bakogaamba, abanto nigo bakoba n’omogooko, korende ekero abakori amabe bakogaamba, abanto nigo bagochaandeka na kweumia.
Ⅲ Oyo bwanchete obong’aini nigo akomogokia ise, korende oyogotaara amo nabakungu abatayayi nigo agosiria chinibo chiaye.
Ⅳ Omorwoti ogokora ay’oboronge nigo akogera ense yaye yageenderera gotenena bokong’u, korende oyo okorigia amaaki nigo akoyeiyeria.
Ⅴ Omonto okong’aina omonto onde namang’ana amansu, oyio nigo agwetegera omotego ase amagoro aye.
Ⅵ Omonto omonyabibe nigo agotegwa nobosareku bwaye, korende omonyene oboronge nigo agoteera n’omogooko na gochenga.
Ⅶ Omonyene oboronge nigo agwechaandera oboronge bw’abataka, korende omokori amabe tamanyeti kende.
Ⅷ Abanyacheche nigo bakoburukania omochie, korende abanto abang’aini nigo bagokendia ekeririanda.
Ⅸ Ekero omonto omong’aini akoamererania nomoriri, oria omoriri nigo akogechigwa na goseka, tari gokira-kiri.
Ⅹ Abanto bare nerang’o y’ogoitera amanyinga nigo bakogecha oyo otari na komocha, korende abare ne chinkoro chindonge nigo bakomorenda.
Ⅺ Omonto omoriri nigo agosokia endamwamu yaye yonsi, korende oyore omong’aini nigo akoyetanga otoerera.
Ⅻ Omogambi aise gwancha amang’ana y’oborimo, abasomba baye bonsi nigo barabe abakori amabe.
ⅩⅢ Omotaka na omochaandi nigo bamenyete amo; Omonene nere okobaa bonsi babere oborabu bw’amaiso.
ⅩⅣ Omorwoti okogambera abataka ase oboronge, obogambi bwaye ’mbogeenderere gotenena bokong’u.
ⅩⅤ Ekeranya na ogotogonyera ase okorongeyia nigo bikorenta obong’aini, korende omwana ogotigwa okora buna anchete, nigo akorentera ng’ina obosooku.
ⅩⅥ Ekero abakori amabe bakomentekana, ogosaria nigo gokomentekana, korende abanyene oboronge ’mbarore okogwa kw’abwo.
ⅩⅦ Rongeyia omwana oo omomura, ere nakorentere ogotimoka, nigo araarente omosasoko omonge ase enkoro yao.
ⅩⅧ Ase okomaanorerwa kw’amang’ana gotari, abanto tibari gwetanga gokora amabe, korende nasesenirie oyo obwatete amachiiko.
ⅩⅨ Amang’ana oka amasa takorongeyia omosomba, ekiagera n’aba oyamanyete, takoyaigwera.
ⅩⅩ Oise korora omonto ore omwango ase ogokwana, ogosemeria ’nkore ase omonto omoriri kobua ase oyio.
ⅩⅪ Omonto ogotuga omosomba oye na omoa ebinto bionsi aganetie korwa obwana bwaye, omosomba oyio nabo araire omwando oye.
ⅩⅫ Omonto obwate endamwamu nigo agoseegeta eriomana, na oyo okogechigwa nigo akorenta ogosaria okonge.
ⅩⅩⅢ Oboenenu bw’omonto nigo bokomokeeyia, korende oyore n’Omoika bw’oboitongo nigo akonyoora ogosikwa okonene.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omonto ore omogisangio bw’omoibi nigo ageechete obogima bwaye obonyene; nigo akoigwa ogotianerwa gw’okoragereria, korende okira takoorokia gento.
ⅩⅩⅤ Okobairoka Mwanyabaanto nigo gokoba omotego, korende omonto omosemeretie Omonene nigo arendire buya.
ⅩⅩⅥ Abanto abange nigo bakorigia ogwancherwa gokorwa ase omogambi, korende kera omonto nigo akonyora oboronge korwa ase Omonene.
ⅩⅩⅦ Omonto otari omooronge nigo are okogechia ase oyore omooronge, na oyore n’enkoro endonge nigo are okogechia ase omokori amabe.
