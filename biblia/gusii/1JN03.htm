Abana ba Nyasae
Ⅰ 3:1: Yoh 1:12Rora, nobwanchani bore naki, obwo Tata atoeire, ng’a torokwe abana ba Nyasae, na naboigo tore! Naki ekegerete ense etatomanyeti neke, ng’a temomanyeti Nyasae. Ⅱ Abanchani, bono nabana ba Nyasae tore, korende teraorokigwa buna torache koba. Naki twamanyire ng’a ekero araorokigwe ntogwekane nere, ekiagera natomoorore buna are. Ⅲ Na monto onde bwensi omosemeretie nigo agwechena buna Kristo are omochenu.
Ⅳ Onde bwensi ogokora ebibe, nigo agosaria amachiiko, ekiagera ebibe nogosaria kw’amachiiko. Ⅴ 3:5: Yoh 1:29Na inwe mwamanyire ng’a Kristo nigo aorogetigwe erinde arusie ebibe biaito, na bibe tibiri ime yaye. Ⅵ Onde bwensi omenyete ime asare tagokora ebibe; onde bwensi ogokora ebibe tamorochi, nonya nkomomanya tamomanyeti. Ⅶ Abana baane, monto onde tabaisa kobang’aina. Ere oyogokora oboronge nigo are omonyene oboronge, buna Kristo are omonyene oboronge. Ⅷ Ere oyogokora ebibe noyo o Saitani, ekiagera Saitani nigo akorete ebibe korwa omochakano. Ekiagerete Omwana o Nyasae akaorokigwa neke, ng’a asarie emeremo ya Saitani. Ⅸ Kera omonto oiboirwe na Nyasae tagokora ebibe, ekiagera oroiboro rwa Nyasae ndotigarete ime yaye, gose takonyara gokora ebibe, ekiagera oiboirwe na Nyasae. Ⅹ Ase eri abana ba Nyasae nigo bare maiso marore, na abana ba Saitani boigo: Onde bwensi otari gokora boronge tarueti ase Nyasae, nonya noyo otancheti oyomwabo.
Twanchane intwe ase intwe
Ⅺ 3:11: Yoh 13:34Naki aya naro amang’ana mwaigwete korwa ritang’ani, ng’a twanchane intwe ase intwe. Ⅻ 3:12: Omoch 4:8Titoba buna Kaini, oyorenge bw’oria omobe, agaita oyomwabo. Ere nigo amoitete ase ki? Kaini nigo aitete oyomwabo ekiagera ogokora kwaye kwarenge okobe, na okw’oyomwabo nigo kwarenge okwa boronge. ⅩⅢ Timokumia, baminto, onye abanto b’ense bakobagecha. ⅩⅣ 3:14: Yoh 5:24Intwe twamanyire ng’a twaetire korwa ase amakweri, twasoire ase obogima, ekiagera twanchire abaminto. Oyotancheti nigo amenyete ase amakweri. ⅩⅤ Onde bwensi ogechete oyomwabo oyio nomoiti, na inwe momanyete ng’a omoiti tari nobogima bwa kare na kare ime yaye. ⅩⅥ Ase eri twamanyire obwanchani: Kristo nigo aruete obogima bwaye ase engencho yaito. Naboigo naintwe ntogwenerete korua obogima bwaito ase engencho y’abaminto. ⅩⅦ Korende oyore n’ebinto bi’ense, na arore oyomwabo oremeire, gotamororera amaabera, Inee, naki obwanchani bwa Nyasae borabe ime asare oyio? ⅩⅧ Abana baane, titwancha ase amang’ana gose ase ogokwana, korende ase ogokora, na ase ekeene.
Oboremu ase obosio bwa Nyasae
ⅩⅨ Ase eri natomanye ng’a nigo tore ab’ekeene, na ase obosio bwaye natoremie chinkoro chiaito. ⅩⅩ Onye chinkoro chiaito chigotogambia, Nyasae nomonene kobua chinkoro chiaito, na ere nigo amanyete onsi. ⅩⅪ Abanchani, onye chinkoro chiaito tichigotogambia, rirorio ntore noboremu ase obosio bwa Nyasae, ⅩⅫ na rinde rionsi togosaba nigo tokorinyora korwa asare, ekiagera ntobwatete amachiiko aye, naende igo tokoyakora ayakomogwenera. ⅩⅩⅢ 3:23: Yoh 13:34; 15:12,17Na oko nakwo ogochika kwaye, ng’a toriegene erieta ri’Omwana oye Yeso Kristo, na gwanchana intwe ase intwe buna atochiikerete. ⅩⅩⅣ Na bonsi ababwatete amachiiko aye nigo bamenyete ime asare na ere ime ase bare. Na igo twamanyire ng’a ere nigo amenyete ime ase tore, twayamanyire korwa ase Omoika oria atoeire.
