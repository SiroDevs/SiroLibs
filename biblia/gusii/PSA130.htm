Ogosaba Gw’okonyora Okwaberwa Na Ogotooreka
Ⅰ Aye Omonene, nigo nkorera asore, korwa ase chindiba ime.
Ⅱ Omonene, igwa eriogi riane;
tegerera oigwe eriogi ri’ogosaba kwane.
Ⅲ Aye Omonene, onye ’nkware gokora omobaro bw’ebibe,
ning’o ore konyaara gotenena ase obosio bwao?
Ⅳ Korende aye nigo ore omwaberi,
erinde onyaare koirokwa.
 
Ⅴ Inche nigo ’nganyete Omonene,
ee, omoyo one nigo omoganyete;
nigo ’nsemeretie ring’ana riaye.
Ⅵ Omoyo one nigo oganyete Omonene, kobua buna abarendi bakoganya ogokiamoka,
ee, kobua abarendi baganyete ng’a bokie.
 
Ⅶ Inwe Abaisraeli, mosemerie Omonene,
ekiagera Omonene nigo are nobwanchani bogendererete botambe,
na ogotooreka okonge nigo gokorwa asare.
Ⅷ 130:8: Mat 1:21; Tito 2:14Ere naboorie Abaisraeli korwa ase ebibe biabo bionsi.
