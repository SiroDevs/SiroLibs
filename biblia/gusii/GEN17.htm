Aburamu okorokwa Aburaamu, na okobwatana kwa Nyasae nere
Ⅰ Ekero Aburamu amenyire emiaka emerongo kianda, na kianda (99). Omonene akamoorokera, akamoteebia: “Inche ninche Nyasae Omobui; genderera ase obosio bwane, na obe omoikeranu. Ⅱ Inche ninkore okobwatana kw’ogotianania egati yane naye, ninkomentekanie mono.” Ⅲ Erio Aburamu agatugama inse akaumama, na Nyasae akamoteebia, Ⅳ “Rora, okobwatana kwane nabwatanire naye nakwo oko: Aye nobe sokoro bw’ebisaku ebinge. Ⅴ 17:5: Abar 4:17Bono erieta riao tirikoba naende Aburamu, korende nigo orarokwe Aburaamu*17:5 Aburaamu: engencho yaye: ise bw’abange, ekiagera nagokorire obe sokoro bw’ebisaku ebinge. Ⅵ Ninkomente obe na ororeeria oronge mono, ninkore ebisaku ebinge birue asore, na abarwoti nabarue asore boigo.
Ⅶ 17:7: Luk 1:55“Ning’ikeranie okobwatana kw’ogotianania egati yane naye, na ororeeria rwao korwa magega yao, kogenderera ekiaare ase ekiaare, kobe okobwatana gwa kare na kare; imbe Nyasae oo, na oyo bw’ororeeria rwao korwa magega yao. Ⅷ 17:8: Ogok 7:5Inche ninkoe aye, na ororeeria rwao rorache korwa magega yao, ense eye omenyete bono buna omomenyi, ense yonsi ya Kaanani, ebe eyaino kare na kare, na inche nimbe Nyasae obo.”
Okobwatana gw’okwarokigwa, na eira y’okoiborwa gwa Isaka
Ⅸ Nyasae agateebia Aburaamu: “Aye goika obwate okobwatana kwane kw’ogotianania, aye na ororeeria rwao rorache korwa magega yao, ekiaare ase ekiaare. Ⅹ 17:10: Ogok 7:8; Abar 4:11Oko nakwo okobwatana kwane, orabwate egati yane naye, na ororeeria rwao rorache korwa magega yao: Kera omosacha ore ase more arokigwe. Ⅺ Goika mwarokigwe risankwa ria isiko ri’ekiimo kiaino ki’obosacha, na ayio nabe ekemanyererio gi’okobwatana egati yane nainwe. Ⅻ Ekiaare ase ekiaare kera omwana omoisia ore ase egati yaino goika arokigwe ekero akoorire amatuko atano na atato, omobiare o inka amo nomogorwa chitaabu korwa ase abanto ba isiko, otari bw’ororeeria rwao. ⅩⅢ Omobiare o inka na omogorwa chitaabu, bonsi goika barokigwe. Ase igo, ekemanyererio gi’okobwatana kwane inakebe ase emebere yaino goika kare na kare. ⅩⅣ Omosacha onde bwensi otarokirie risankwa ria isiko ri’ekiimo kiaye ki’obosacha, omonto oyio nigo araatananwe korwa ase abanto bamwabo, aserigwe gochia isiko, ekiagera osaririe okobwatana kwane.”
ⅩⅤ Naende Nyasae agateebia Aburaamu: “Sarai mokao, tobaisa komooroka naende Sarai, korende erieta riaye nigo rirabe Sara.†17:15 Sara: engencho yaye: Omoiseke bw’omorwoti ⅩⅥ Nimosesenie, nkonyorere omwana omomura korwa asare, nimosesenie, abe ng’ina bw’ebisaku; na abarwoti b’abaanto inabaiborwe korwa asare.”
ⅩⅦ Erio Aburaamu agatugama inse, agaseka, akabora ase omoyo oye ime: “Inee! Omonto ore nemiaka rigana nanyare koibora omwana?” Sara nere oyo ore nemiaka emerongo kianda aibore omwana? ⅩⅧ Aburaamu agateebia Nyasae: “Koranche, Isumaeli abe moyo ase obosio bwao!”
ⅩⅨ Nyasae akamokania, akamoteebia: “Taibo, Sara mokao nakoiborere omwana omomura, na aye nomooroke Isaka‡17:19Isaka: engencho yaye ase omonwa bwe ’keiberania amaseko. Inche ning’ikeranie nere okobwatana kwane kw’ogotianania, kobe okobwatana gwa kare na kare ase ororeeria rwaye rorache korwa magega yaye. ⅩⅩ Ase amang’ana igoro ya Isumaeli nakoigure, rora, nimosesenie nimomentekanie mono, naibore abagaambi ikomi na babere; ninkore egesaku ekenene kerwe asare. ⅩⅪ Korende ning’ikeranie okobwatana kwane na Isaka, oyo Sara araibore ase engaki buna eye iga, ase omwaka ogocha.” ⅩⅫ Nyasae ekero akoorire gokwana na Aburaamu, akairana korwa asare, akagenda.
ⅩⅩⅢ Erio Aburaamu akabwata Isumaeli omwana oye, na abasomba aba baiboire ase enka yaye, na abagorwa chitaabu, kera omonto omosacha ore ase abanto b’enka ya Aburaamu, akabarokia risankwa ria isiko ri’ekiimo kiabo ki’obosacha, ase rituko riria riria, buna Nyasae amotebetie. ⅩⅩⅣ Aburaamu konya oikire nemiaka emerongo kianda, na kianda (99), ekero arogetigwe. ⅩⅩⅤ Na Isumaeli, omwana oye, nigo konya aikire emiaka ikomi, na etato, ekero arogetigwe. ⅩⅩⅥ Ase rituko erio erio Aburaamu akarokigwa amo na omwana oye Isumaeli. ⅩⅩⅦ Na abanto bonsi baiboire ase enka yaye, na abagorwa chitaabu korwa ase abanto ba isiko, bakarokigwa amo nere.
