Hagari na Isumaeli
Ⅰ Bono Sarai, moka Aburamu, tamoiborerete bana. Sarai nareenge nomoiseke omosomba Omomisiri, ore korokwa Hagari. Ⅱ Sarai agateebia Aburamu: “Norooche bono ng’a Omonene ontangire tinyora abana, menyana nomoiseke oyo one omosomba; aande nabo ndanyore abana korwa asare.” Aburamu akaigwera ogokwana gwa Sarai. Ⅲ Ase igo, magega Aburamu kamenyire ense ya Kaanani ase emiaka ikomi, Sarai akaimokia Hagari, omosomba oye Omomisiri, akamomoa Aburamu, omosacha oye, ng’a abe buna mokaye. Ⅳ Erio bakamenyana na Hagari, na ere akaba morito. Ekero Hagari achia korora ng’a obeire morito, agachaaka gochaaya Sarai moibori omwabo.
Ⅴ Sarai agateebia Aburamu: “Obobe obo bwanyorete, tiga naye bokonyore! Inakoa omoiseke one omosomba koboko kwao; ere, ekero achia korora ng’a obeire morito, onchayire. Tiga Omonene atwatanane inche naye!”
Ⅵ Aburamu nere agateebia Sarai: “Rora, omosomba oo oyio nase amaboko ao are; moonchie buna oranche.” Erio Sarai akamochanda bobe, na ere agatama korwa asare.
Ⅶ Omomalaika Omonene akarora Hagari ase erooro ase ensoko y’amaache yareenge ase enchera ekogenda Suri. Ⅷ Akamobooria: “Aye omosomba o Sarai, ng’ai okorwa, naende ng’ai okogenda?”
Akamoiraneria: “Nigo ngotama korwa ase moibori ominto Sarai.”
Ⅸ Erio omomalaika Omonene akamoteebia: “Irana gochia ase moibori omino, na bwekeyie asare.” Ⅹ Naende omomalaika Omonene akamoteebia: “Nimentekanie ororeeria rwao, na omobaro obo tokonyara kobareka, ekiagera nigo barabe abange mono.” Ⅺ Naende Omomalaika Omonene akamoteebia,
“Rora, morito ore, noibore omwana omoisia;
nomooroke Isumaeli,*16:11 Isumaeli: engencho yaye: Nyasae okoigwa
ekiagera Omonene oigure ekerero ki’emechando yao.
Ⅻ Ere nigo arabe buna etigere y’orosana ase egati y’abanto,
na okoboko kwaye nigo korabe igoro y’abanto bonsi,
na amaboko ’abaanto bonsi nigo arabe igoro yaye;
ere nigo aramenye bosio bw’abamwabo bonsi.”
ⅩⅢ Ase ayio Hagari Akaroka Omonene oyo okwanete nere Eli‐Roi, engencho yaye “Aye naye Nyasae bw’okorora”; naki akebooria: “Inee! Amaene, narorire Nyasae, na ere ontiga inde moyo magega konamororire?” ⅩⅣ Ase ayio ensoko eria ekarokwa Beeri‐Lahai‐Roi†16:14 Beeri‐Lahai‐Roi: engencho yaye; Nyasae okorora; ero nigo ere egati ya Kadeshi na Beredi.
ⅩⅤ 16:15: Abagl 4:22Erio Hagari akaiborera Aburamu omwana omoisia; nere Akaroka omwana oria oye, Isumaeli. ⅩⅥ Aburamu konya obeire nemiaka emerongo etano na etato, na etano nomo (86), ekero Hagari amoiborerete Isumaeli.
