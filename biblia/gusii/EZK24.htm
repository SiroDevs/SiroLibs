ⅩⅩⅣ
Oboboosu bw’enyongo
Ⅰ Ase rituko ria ikomi, omotienyi o ikomi, omwaka o kianda, ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: Ⅱ 24:2: 2 Abar 25:1; Yer 52:4“Aye Mwanyabaanto, riika erieta rie rituko ria reero, ekiagera rituko eri nario omorwoti o Babiloni achire korwania Yerusalemu. Ⅲ Kwana n’abanto aba batari koigwera, obateebie omoreng’anio. Otebe ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba:
“Tereka enyongo riko, ee, oyetereke,
oumore amaache ime yaye.
Ⅳ Obeke ebitari bie chinyama imeo,
bionsi ebire ebiya, ekenama ne rireko,
oyeichorie amauga, ochore ayare amaya.
Ⅴ Ebitari ebio obirusie korwa ase eng’ondi enchore ere ase riicho ria’o;
otumbe chinko chinyinge inse y’enyongo,
oute omorero obiyeke buya,
oiyekeranie amauga amo.
Ⅵ “Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Obobe nobw’omochie oyo oiterete amanyinga y’ abanto, oyio onga buna enyongo eroisirie korwa ase etai yaboosire, na oboboosu bwaye tibokorwao. Orusie korwa imeo egetari ase egetari otari gochora ng’a ’nkereki okorusia. Ⅶ Amanyinga y’ abanto b’omochie oyio oiterete nigo agendererete kobeera imeo. Tayaumorete ase riroba riyatube na koyabisa, korende nigo ayaumorete igoro ase egetare. Ⅷ Nigo natigete amanyinga ayio aiteretwe agenderere kobeera igoro ase egetare atatubiri, erio nario aragere imbe nendamwamu na ndusie egesiomba.
Ⅸ “Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Obobe nobw’omochie oyo oiterete amanyinga y’ abanto! Nainche boigo nintumbe chinko chibe chinyinge, ase omorero. Ⅹ Beka chinko chinyinge, na omorero ogenderere gwoka mono, erinde oiyeke chinyama chiyie kegima, na omosori oyie oere, na amauga ayie arigise. Ⅺ Erio otige enyongo eyio ere bosa igoro ase omorero bw’amakara, eyie esambwe, erinde etai y’enyongo eyio eyie ebariire burure; riko rire imeo rigokomoke, na oboboosu bw’enyongo eyio boyie bonyititoke boere. Ⅻ Inche nabeire nkwerosia ngokora emeremo bosa. Oboboosu bw’enyongo eyio tibokoruao neisa gosambwa nomorero. ⅩⅢ Kwabeire oyotachenegeti ase engencho y’obonyaka bwao. Nateemire kogochena, korende tochenegeti. Tokonyara gocheneka korwa ase ogotacheneka kwao goika inche ndete endamwamu y’ane igoro ase ore. ⅩⅣ Ninche Omonene nakwanire ayio. Goika aikerane. Ninkore ring’ana erio, tingotiga. Tinkoba n’amaabera, gose tingweitia. Goika onacherwe ekiina koreng’ana ne chinchera chiao, na ogokora kwao. Nabo Omonene ateebire.”
Ogokwa kw’omokuungu bw’omobani
ⅩⅤ Naende ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: ⅩⅥ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, rora, goetera ase ogokwa kwa mobosokano nindusie korwa ase ore oyore omogooko bw’amaiso ao; nonya naboigo tomoreera gose kobokia amagombo, gose amariga taiteka korwa ase amaiso ao. ⅩⅦ Oichane, korende oremererie torera; tobaisa koreera oria okuure. Bwetube egetambaa ase omotwe oo, obeeke ebikoroto ase amagoro ao. Tobaisa gotuba obosio bwao, gose tobaisa koria endagera y’ebirero.”
ⅩⅧ Mambia rituko ria kabere ngakwana nabaanto, na mogoroba yaye omokungu one agakwa. Rituko riare koribwatia ngakora buna konya nachiikirwe. ⅩⅨ Erio abanto bakamboria, “Nonyare gototeebia engencho y’amang’ana aya, na eyagera gwakora iga?”
ⅩⅩ Erio nkabairaneria, nkabateebia ng’a ring’ana ri’Omonene ndiacha asende, riganteebia: ⅩⅪ “Kwana n’Abaisraeli, obateebie ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Rora, ninkore Aase ane Aachenu abe atachenegeti, aase ayio are obonene bw’echinguru chiaino, na omogooko bw’amaiso aino, na okogania kw’emioyo yaino. Abana baino, abamura na abaiseke, abwo mwatigete magega, mbakenywe nemioro. ⅩⅫ Nainwe nabo morakore buna inche nakorire. Timobaisa gotuba chinderu na emenwa, gose timoria endagera y’ebirero. ⅩⅩⅢ Erio motige ebitambaa bibe ase emetwe yaino, na ebikoroto ase amagoro aino. Timobaisa kobokia amagombo gose korera, korende imoree ase engencho y’ebibe biaino, na mobe mokorererana. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase enchera eyio Ezekieli nabe ekiorokererio ase more. Inwe mokore kera egento agokora. Ekero amang’ana ayio araikeranigwe, erio imomanye ng’a ninche Omonene.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Erio Omonene agantebia, “Aye Mwanyabaanto, nomanye ng’a ase rituko erio ndarusie korwa ase bare ekio kere chinguru chiabo, na omogooko obo, na obonene bwabo, na omogooko bw’amaiso abo, na okogania gwe chinkoro chiabo, naende abana babo, abamura na abaiseke, ⅩⅩⅥ ase rituko erio omonto oratooreke natame gochia ase ore, akorentere amang’ana. ⅩⅩⅦ Erio omonwa oo nokunorwe ochaake gokwana nomotami oria, tokogenderera koba rimama; ase igo aye nobe ekiorokererio ase abanto abwo, na barabwo mbamanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.”
