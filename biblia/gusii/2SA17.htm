ⅩⅦ
Husai okong’aina Abisalomu
Ⅰ Magega y’ayio Ahitofeli agateebia Abisalomu, “Tiga ’nchoore abanto chilifu ikomi na ibere (12,000), erinde ’mbwatie Daudi ase obotuko obo. Ⅱ Inche imochiere mobosokano imogose ekero arosire na erusirie moyo. Abanto abwo bonsi bare komo mbatame, nainche ninyare goita omorwoti kare bweka. Ⅲ Erio ’mbarente abanto bonsi ase ore, buna omoriakari akorentwa ase omosacha oye. Aye nomonto oyomo origetie oite; naende abanto bonsi babe n’omorembe.” Ⅳ Ogoseemia okwo gokagwenera Abisalomu na abagaaka bonsi b’Abaisraeli.
Ⅴ Erio Abisalomu akabora, “Rangeria Husai Omoariki, toigwe nere, buna agototeebia.” Ⅵ Na ekero Husai achete ase Abisalomu, Abisalomu akamoteebia, “Aya naro Ahitofeli atoseemirie. Naro tokobwatia gose? Onye taibo, aye toteebie aya togokora.”
Ⅶ Husai akairaneria Abisalomu, “Ogoseemia okwo Ahitofeli abaeire tikori okuya ase ekero eke.” Ⅷ Naende Husai akamoteebia, “Nomanyete iso na abanto baye, ng’a n’abanto chituoni chi’eseegi, bare abakong’u, naende nigo bare abatindi buna riurugenyi*17:8 Riurugenyi: engencho yaye edubu yaurirwe chimanwa chiaye ase orosana. Iso nomonto omanyete korwana esegi, takoraara botuko, ere n’abanto baye. Ⅸ Ande nonya ’mbono nigo ebisete ase engoro gete, gose ase ribwago. Keraabe ng’a abanto ’mbaitwe ekero esegi egochaaka, onde bwensi karaigwe amang’ana ayio nigo arateebe ng’a abanto bare kobwatia Abisalomu babuirwe. Ⅹ Igo nonya noyore omoremu buna endo, nigo arairianyeke kegima, ekiagera Abaisraeli bonsi ’mbamanyete ng’a iso nigo are etuoni y’eseegi, na abanto baye nabatindi ase esegi. Ⅺ Ase ayio ogoseemia kwane n’oko: Orente Abaisraeli bonsi amo korwa Dani goika Beeri‐Seba babe abange buna omokenye ore engegu y’enyancha; naye omonyene ogende amo nabarabwo ase esegi. Ⅻ Erio toorokere Daudi aande onsi aranyorekane, tomogwere buna riime rikogwa igoro ase riroba, gose titotiga onde bwensi abe moyo, ere gose monto onde bwensi bare komo. ⅩⅢ Aise gosoa ase omochie onde bwensi, Abaisraeli bonsi nigo baraarente chingori bachietananie omochie oyio, bayokuruure gochia rooche ime, gose igena rinde tirigotigara nonya nerimo.”
ⅩⅣ Abisalomu na Abaisraeli bonsi bakaboora, “Ogoseemia kwa Husai Omoariki nokuya kobua koria kw’Ahitofeli.” Omonene nere ochiigete ogoseemia okwo okuya kw’Ahitofeli kwangwe, erinde arentere Abisalomu emechando.
Daudi ogokuurerwa atame etoorie
ⅩⅤ Husai agateebia Zadoki na Abiatari, abakuani, “Ahitofeli oseemirie Abisalomu na abagaaka b’Abaisraeli amang’ana aya na aya, korende inche nabaseemia iga na iga. ⅩⅥ Bono toma amang’ana bwango, mokuurere Daudi ng’a tabaisa koraara ase obotuko obo ase ebiambokero agwo ase erooro, korende goika amboke orooche, erinde tabaisa gosirigwa amo n’abanto baye bonsi.”
ⅩⅦ Bono Yonathani na Ahimasi nigo bareenge baganyete ase ensoko y’Eni‐Rogeli, ekiagera boboete gosoa omochie ime baabaise kororwa. Omoiseke omosomba oyomo nigo arenge okona‐kobairera amang’ana, nabarabwo bayairera omorwoti Daudi. ⅩⅧ Korende rituko erimo omosae oyomo akabarora, agateebia Abisalomu amang’ana ayio. Ase ayio Yonathani na Ahimasi bakageenda ase enka y’omonto gete aaria Bahurimu. Omonto oyio nabwate engoro y’amaache ase omochie oye, nabarabwo bakebisa imeo, ⅩⅨ na omokungu bw’omonto oria akaimokia egekuneko, akayekuneka, akanekera endagera yaye igoro aroro; gose omonto onde tamanyete amang’ana ayio. ⅩⅩ Ekero abasomba b’Abisalomu bachete ase omokungu oria nyomba ime, bakamobooria, “Ng’ai Yonathani na Ahimasi bare?”
Omokungu oria akabateebia, “Bambokire orooche orwo, bachiire.”
Ekero abanto baria baabarigetie tibaabarora, bakairana Yerusalemu. ⅩⅪ Magega barabwo kobachiire, baria babere bagasooka korwa ase engoro eria, bakaamboka orooche bwango, bakageenda ase omorwoti Daudi bakamoteebia, “Imoka mwamboke orooche bwango, morue aa, inaki aya na aya naro Ahitofeli arobeirie mamincha yaino.” ⅩⅫ Erio Daudi na abanto bonsi barenge komo bakaimoka, bakaamboka Yorodani. Bogachia gokia, bonsi konya bambokire; onde tatigarete otaambogete.
ⅩⅩⅢ Ekero Ahitofeli arooche ng’a ogosemia kwaye kwangirwe, akariina etigere yaye, akageenda omochie oye, na karobeirie amang’ana y’enyomba yaye, akageenda agesunga, agakwa. Gakuure agatindekwa ase oboina bwa ise.
ⅩⅩⅣ Daudi konya oikire Mahanaimu ekero Abisalomu ambogete Yorodani amo n’Abaisraeli bonsi barenge komo. ⅩⅩⅤ Abisalomu konya obeekire Amasa koba omotang’ani bw’abarwani b’esegi ribaga ria Yoabu. Amasa nigo arenge mosinto o Yitira Omoisumaeli. Yitira nere omeny’anete n’Abigaeli, omoiseke o Nahasi, mosubati omwabo Seruya, oyorenge ng’ina Yoabu. ⅩⅩⅥ Abisalomu amo n’Abaisraeli bagatoora ase ense ya Gileadi.
ⅩⅩⅦ Ekaba Daudi gaikire Mahanaimu, akanyorana na Sobi, mosinto o Nahasi, korwa Raba, ase ense y’Abaamoni, na Makiri, mosinto bw’Amieli, korwa Lo‐Debari, na Barisilai Omogileadi korwa Rogelimu. ⅩⅩⅧ Barabwo bakarenta ebiraarero, ne chisani, na ebikorero biroisirie korwa ase amaroba. Boigo bakarenta engano, na esairi, na obosie, na ebigara bisaambire, ne ching’ende ao ao, ⅩⅩⅨ na obooke, na amaguta, nemekera, na ebirandi bi’mabeere ye chiombe atundire bibe endagera ase Daudi amo n’abanto bareenge komo. Abwo babirentete nigo bateebete, “Abanto aba enchara yabanyarire, naende barang’ire na barosire aiga ase erooro.”
