ⅩⅩⅨ
Yakobo ogoika enka ya Labani
Ⅰ Erio Yakobo akagenderera norogendo rwaye, agaika ense y’abanto ba moocha. Ⅱ Agwo akarigereria akarora ensoko yaare ase egetii, na amaicho atato e ching’ondi araire ensoko eyio ensemo, ekiagera ase ensoko eyio nao amaicho are konywera amaache. Na ndiareenge aroro rigena rinene igoro y’omonwa bw’ensoko. Ⅲ Agwo nao amaicho onsi areenge gosangererekana, na abarisia nigo baare korusia rigena riria korwa ase omonwa bw’ensoko banyoinsia ching’ondi, naende bariirania ase omonwa bw’ensoko.
Ⅳ Yakobo akababooria: “Baminto, ng’ai seino?”
Abarisia baria bakamoiraneria: “Seito naiga Harani.”
Ⅴ Naende akababooria: “Inee! Momanyete Labani, mochokoro o Nahori?” Barabwo bakamoiraneria: “Ee, ntomomanyete.”
Ⅵ Naende akababooria: “Mbuya are?”
Abarisia bakamoiraneria: “Ee, mbuya are; na rigereria, Raeli, omoiseke oye oyio ogocha ne ching’ondi!”
Ⅶ Akabateebia: “Rigereria, bono mobaso, chinsa chi’ogosangereria amaicho tichiraika, nyoinsia ching’ondi, naende mochiire korisia.”
Ⅷ Korende barabwo bakamoiraneria: “Titokonyara gokora bo goika ekero amaicho onsi arache asangererekane amo, erinde abarisia bamanye gotigeria rigena korwa ase omonwa bw’ensoko; erio tomanye konyoinsia ching’ondi.”
Ⅸ Ekero areenge ogendererete gokwana nabarabwo, Raeli agacha obwate ching’ondi chia ise, naki nere orenge gochirisia. Ⅹ Bono ekero Yakobo achia korora Raeli, omoiseke o Labani, ng’ina rome, okorenta riicho ria Labani, akaimoka, agatigeria rigena riria korwa ase omonwa bw’ensoko, akanyoinsia ching’ondi chia Labani, ng’ina rome. Ⅺ Erio Yakobo akaagata Raeli, akarera na koiyeria amariga. Ⅻ Agateebia Raeli, “Inche nomoigwa o iso, omwana o Rebeka.”
Raeli akaminyoka, agateebia ise amang’ana ayio. ⅩⅢ Ekero Labani achia koigwa amang’ana a Yakobo, omoigwa oye, akaminyoka gochia komoorotota, akamoagata n’amaiga amanene, akamoira gochia nka bwoye. Yakobo agateebia Labani amang’ana onsi. ⅩⅣ Erio Labani akamoteebia: “Amaene, aye namanyinga omobere one.” Ere akamenya bwoye ase engaki y’omotienyi oyomo.
Yakobo ogokorera Labani ase okonyora Raeli na Lea
ⅩⅤ Labani agakwana na Yakobo, akamoteebia: “Nonya kore omoigwa one, tegwenereti onkorere emeremo bosa. Nteebia eng’eria yao ninki ndakoe!” ⅩⅥ Bono Labani nigo abwate abaiseke babere; omoiseke oria omonene nigo are korokwa Lea, na oria omoke Raeli. ⅩⅦ Lea amaiso aye nigo areenge amatu, korende Raeli nigo areenge omonyakieni, naende omwasioku.
ⅩⅧ Yakobo konya omwanchire Raeli, na ase ayio agateebia Labani, “Ningokorere emiaka etano na ebere, erinde ong’e Raeli, omoiseke oo omoke.”
ⅩⅨ Labani akamoiraneria, “Ayio mbuya. Tiga nkomoe aye kobua ’nkomoa omonto onde; menya aa bwone.” ⅩⅩ Ase igo, Yakobo akamokorera emiaka etano na ebere aegwe Raeli. Emiaka eyio ekarorekana asare buna namatuko makeigo, ekiagera nigo amwanchete Raeli mono.
ⅩⅪ Erio Yakobo agateebia Labani: “Bono naikeranirie engaki yane, ing’a mokane, tobe nere.” ⅩⅫ Ase igo, Labani akarangeria abanto bonsi b’ensemo eria, agakorera omoyega. ⅩⅩⅢ Rigachia koba mogoroba Labani akaimokia Lea, omoiseke oye omonene, akamorentera Yakobo, erio Yakobo akaba nere. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase ekero ekio Labani akarua omosomba oye omoiseke okorokwa Silpa, abe omokoreri o Lea, omwana oye. ⅩⅩⅤ Bogachia gokia mambia, Yakobo akanyora ng’a ’Lea aetwe. Erio akabooria Labani: “Inki eke gwankorerete? Tinagokorerete, erinde ong’e Raeli? Naki gwakorete okang’aina?”
ⅩⅩⅥ Labani akamoiraneria: “Aiga seito intwe titori gokora bo, gosokia omoiseke omoke, na gotiga omonene. ⅩⅩⅦ Tiga omoyega oyo bw’amatuko aya atano na abere oraera bw’oyio omonene, erio noegwe na oria omoke, naende aye onkorere emeremo emiaka ende etano na ebere.”
ⅩⅩⅧ Yakobo agancha ebe boigo, erio agaikerania amatuko ayio atano na abere. Erio Labani akamomoa Raeli, omoiseke oye omoke, abe mok’aye. ⅩⅩⅨ Labani akamoa Raeli, omwana oye, omokoreri, nere Bilia, omosomba oye omoiseke. ⅩⅩⅩ Ase igo, Yakobo akaba na Raeli; na agancha Raeli mono kegima kobua Lea. Erio Yakobo naende agakorera Labani emiaka ende etano na ebere.
Abana ba Yakobo
ⅩⅩⅪ Ekero Omonene arooche ng’a Lea tanchiri, akamosesenia ase oroiboro rwa abana, korende Raeli ere nigo areenge omogomba. ⅩⅩⅫ Lea akaba morito, akaibora omwana omoisia, akamoroka Rubeni,*29:32 Rubeni: engencho yaye: rora omomura naki nigo atebeete: “Omonene ororire buna ’nchayire. Korende ase engencho y’okoibora, bono omosacha one nanyanche.” ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Naende Lea akaba morito, akaibora omwana omoisia, akabora: “Omonene ororire ng’a tinyanchiri, naende ong’eire omwana onde omoisia boigo,” akamoroka Simioni.†29:33 Simioni: engencho yaye aande: okoigwa ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Naende akaba morito, akaibora omwana omoisia, akabora: “Bono engaki eye omosacha one nabwatane amo nainche, ekiagera namoiboreire abana abaisia batato.” Ase ayio akamoroka Lawi.‡29:34 Lawi: engencho yaye aande: obwateranirie ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Akaba morito naende, akaibora omwana omoisia, akabora: “Engaki eye nintogie Omonene.” Ase ayio akamoroka Yuda.§29:35Yuda: engencho yaye aande: ogotogigwa Erio Lea agatenena koibora.
