Ⅳ
Emeremo ya’ batomwa
Ⅰ Omonto natobare iga, ng’a nabasomba ba Kristo tore, na abarendi b’obobisi bwa Nyasae. Ⅱ Agwo naende ayarigirie ase abarendi naro aya: banyorekane ng’a nabegenwa bare. Ⅲ Asende inche, tai gento ngambigwe nainwe, gose na kegambero kende gionsi kia Mwanyabaanto. Nonya ninche omonyene tinkwegambia. Ⅳ Naki timanyeti engencho y’ogwesoera inche omonyene, korende timbareiri oboronge ase engencho y’ayio. Omonene nere orangambie. Ⅴ Ase ayio timogamba ing’ana rinde rionsi engaki etaraika, ekero Omonene ataracha; nere orabaorokerie ayabisegete ase omosunte, na koorokia amang’ana are ase chinkoro ime. Erio nario kera omonto aranyore ogotogigwa kwaye korwa ase Nyasae.
Ⅵ Amang’ana aya, baminto, nayakwanire igoro yane na igoro y’Apolo ase engencho yaino, erinde ase amang’ana aito monyare kweorokereria timotamboka ase amang’ana ariikire. Omonto taenena ase oyomo mamincha y’oria onde. Ⅶ Naki ning’o ogwatananire aye noyonde? Inki ore nakio otanyoreti buna ekeegwa? Onye gwakenyorire buna ekeegwa, inki rende ogwetogeria buna tikiarenge ekeegwa?
Ⅷ Mwaikire koigota! Mwaikire konyora obotenenku! Mwanyorire oborwoti intwe totaiyo! Anga mbuya onye mwagambete, erinde naintwe togambe amo nainwe! Ⅸ Naki nigo ndoche ng’a Nyasae otobekire intwe abatomwa tobe ab’omoerio ase bonsi, buna abanto bagambetigwe ng’a baitwe, ekiagera twabeire ekiorokererio ki’ogosekererwa ase ense, na ase abamalaika, na ase Mwanyabaanto. Ⅹ Intwe nigo tore abariri ase engencho ya Kristo, korende inwe nigo more abang’aini ase Kristo. Intwe nigo tore abareu, korende inwe nigo more ne chinguru. Inwe nigo mosikire, korende intwe tochayire. Ⅺ Nonya ngoika egeka eke kia bono ntore nenchara na erang’o, naende nigo tore gesobono; iga togoakwa chinkundi titori nobomenyo, Ⅻ 4:12: Ogok 18:3naende nigo togochandeka gatogokora emeremo namaboko aito abanyene. Ekero tokoramwa, twasesenia; ekero togochandwa, twaremereria; ⅩⅢ ekero togokwanwa bobe, intwe twaebereria. Twakorirwe buna eogoto y’ense na eubi y’ebinto bionsi goika bono.
Obotebereria bwa ise bw’obwanchani, na ogokuurera
ⅩⅣ Tinyarigeti aya ase okobasookia, korende nkobakuurera buna abana nyanchete. ⅩⅤ Naki nonya more nabateebereria chilifu ikomi ase Kristo, timori nabaiso abange. Naki ase Kristo Yeso nabeire buna iso, obaiboire ase enchiri. ⅩⅥ 4:16: 1 Abakr 11:1; Abafl 3:17Gaki nabasoroire, mobe abegi baane. ⅩⅦ Ase ayio natomire Timotheo ase more, oyore omwana one omwanchwa, na omwegenwa ase Omonene. Nere orabainyorie buna chingencho chiane chire ase Kristo, buna nkoborokereria kera aase ase kera ekanisa, ⅩⅧ Abande ase more baenenire ekiagera bakagire ng’a tindi gocha ase more. ⅩⅨ Korende ninchiche ase more bwango, Omonene karanche, na inche nimanye chinguru chiabo abwo bakoenena, gose tari ogokwana kwabo. ⅩⅩ Naki oborwoti bwa Nyasae tibori ase ogokwana, korende ase chinguru. ⅩⅪ Naki morigetie? Inchiche ase more nenyimbo gose nobwanchani ase omoika bw’obwororo?
