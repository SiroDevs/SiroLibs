ⅩⅪ
Amachiiko ase abwo babwate abasomba
(Okoir 15:12-18)
Ⅰ “Aya naro amachiiko orae Abaisraeli.”
Ⅱ 21:2: Abal 25:39-46Koragore omosomba Omoiberania, nigo aragokorere ase engaki y’emiaka etano nomo, korende omwaka o gatano na kabere mosibore agende ko tomoakana kende. Ⅲ Onye ochete bwoo tarenge na mokungu, rirorio nabo aragende bweka, korende onye orenge nomokuungu, omokungu oye nigo aragende amo nere. Ⅳ Onye omonene oye omonyuomeretie omokungu, na ere omoiboreire abana, abamura gose abaiseke, omokungu oria na abana baye nigo barabe enibo y’omonene oye; omosacha oria nigo ararueo ere bweka, Ⅴ Korende omosomba oria karatebe: Nimwanchete omonene one amo na mokane, na abana baane, tinganeti korua ase obosomba bwane, Ⅵ erio omonene oye amorente ase obosio bwa Nyasae, amoikie ase egesieri gose ase emetobwa ya gesieri na amotue*21:6 Amotue: engencho yaye amobochore ogoto kwaye n’esindake. Erio omonto oria abe omosomba oye ase obogima bwaye bwonsi.
Ⅶ “Omonto karaoonie omwana oye omoiseke koba omosomba, omoiseke oyio tagosiborwa buna abasomba abamura barasiborwe. Ⅷ Onye omoiseke oyio tagwenereti omonene oye, oyorengereretie komokora abe mokaye, rirorio nigo araboorigwe airane. Omonene oye takoba ne ribaga komoonia ase egesaku ekegeni, ekiagera among’ainete. Ⅸ Onye okomoa omomura oye abe mokaye, rirorio amwanche buna omwana oye omoiseke. Ⅹ Onye omonto oyio okonyuoma omokungu o kabere, takorina oria omotang’ani endagera yaye, ne chianga chiaye na kende gionsi aganetie buna mokaye. Ⅺ Atamokorereti amang’ana aya atato, goika aegwe ribaga arue bwoye ataakaneiri embooria.”
Amachiiko ase abaiti
Ⅻ 21:12: Abal 24:17“Monto onde bwensi oraake omonto na amoite akwe, nere goika aitwe akwe. ⅩⅢ 21:13: Omob 35:10-34; Okoir 19:1-13; Yosh 20:1-9Korende onye tamong’erete otatiga Nyasae omobegete ase amaboko aye, erio ninkworokie aase omoiti oria aratamere. ⅩⅣ Korende omonto ogoita oyomwabo mweno ase obong’ainereria, ere goika aitwe akwe, naisa gotamera egesasiimero kiane.
ⅩⅤ “Onde bwensi oraake ise gose ng’ina, nere goika aitwe akwe.
ⅩⅥ 21:16: Okoir 24:7“Onde bwensi oraibe omonto goika aitwe akwe, karabe omoonirie gose omobwate ase amaboko aye.
ⅩⅦ 21:17: Abal 20:9; Mat 15:4; Mar 7:10“Onde bwensi oraragererie ise gose ng’ina, nere goika aitwe akwe.”
Amachiiko ase abwo bagoaka abande
ⅩⅧ “Abanto babere kobarabe bakorwana, na oyomo aake oyonde rigena, gose enkundi, amoate chingoma, echio chiramokore omorwaire goika araare borere, ⅩⅨ karabe otimokire, naende asoke isiko gotaara ogwesiirera enyimbo, rirorio oria omoagete nigo aratigwe, otatiga amoakane ase engencho y’eng’eria eria asiririe ekero arwarete, na ere amorende goika agwene kegima.
ⅩⅩ “Omonto karaake omosomba oye, omomura gose omoiseke, nenyimbo, erinde akwere ase amaboko aye, goika aegwe egesusuro. ⅩⅪ Korende omosomba oyio karagenderere koba moyo rituko erimo gose abere, omonto oyio takoegwa egesusuro, ekiagera omosomba oyio nenibo yaye.
ⅩⅫ “Abanto abasacha kobarabe bakorwana, na kogwera omokungu ore morito, na oborito bwaye bogwe, korende atabeti na bobe bonde, omonto oria omogwerete goika aakane buna omosacha bw’omokuungu oria araborie magega abagaambi kobanachire ekiina. ⅩⅩⅢ Korende omokungu oria karakwe, rirorio egesusuro kebe obogima ase obogima, ⅩⅩⅣ 21:24: Abal 24:19,20; Okoir 19:21; Mat 5:38eriso ase eriso, na erino ase erino, okoboko ase okoboko, na okogoro ase okogoro, ⅩⅩⅤ ogosambwa ase ogosambwa, na eriote ase eriote, naende ogosionorwa ase ogosionorwa.
ⅩⅩⅥ “Omonto karaake omosomba oye, omomura gose omoiseke, na komwata eriso, rirorio goika amotige abe omosibore ase engencho y’eriso riaye. ⅩⅩⅦ Naende Karamoake omosomba oye omomura gose omoiseke amogookore erino, goika amotige abe omosibore ase engencho y’erino riaye.”
Amachiiko ase abanyene chitugo
ⅩⅩⅧ “Eeri eise goita omonto, omosacha gose omokungu, ne ching’unchara chiaye, goika eakwe amagena ekwe, ne chinyama chiaye tichiriegwa; korende omonyene eng’ombe eyio taegwa egesusuro. ⅩⅩⅨ Korende eeri eyio, onye enarete goita abanto, na omonyene eeri eyio okuureirwe, na ere tayesibeti, ero keraite omonto, omosacha gose omokungu, nigo eraakwe amagena ekwe, na omonyene nere aitwe akwe. ⅩⅩⅩ Onye embooria ekoborigwa korwa asare, goika arue chionsi echi chiraganigwe, chibe embooria ase obogima bwaye. ⅩⅩⅪ Onye eng’ombe egoita omomura gose omoiseke ne ching’unchara chiaye, ogochika nakwo okwo okomo. ⅩⅩⅫ Onye eeri egoita omosomba, omomura gose omoiseke, omonyene eng’ombe nigo araakane omonyene omosomba oria chisekeli chi’echifeta emerongo etato, na eeri eria eitwe ase ogoakwa amagena.
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ “Omonto karatige engoro yasie, gose onye okunyire engoro na tayekuneka, na eng’ombe gose etigere egwe imeo, ⅩⅩⅩⅣ rirorio omonyene engoro goika aakane omonyene etugo chitaabu. Erio eng’iti eria y akuure ebe eyaye. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Eeri y’omonto keraite ey’omonto onde ekwe, erio barabwo baoonie eeri eyio ere moyo, baatanane chitaabu echio, baatanane ne chinyama chi’eng’ombe eria y akuure boigo. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Korende onye emanyekanete ng’a eeri eria nigo enarete goita abanto, na omonyene tayesibeti, goika aakane omonyene eeri eria y akuure ase okomoa eeri eria ere moyo; na eria y akuure ebe eyaye.”
