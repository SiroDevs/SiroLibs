Yeso ogokwana na Nikodemo
Ⅰ Narengeo omonto oyomo bw’Abafarisai, erieta riaye Nikodemo, omonene bw’Abayahudi. Ⅱ Oyo nigo achete ase Yeso botuko, akamoteebia, “Rabii twamanyire ng’a nomworokia ore, kwarure ase Nyasae, ekiagera omonto onde tari oranyare gokora ebikone ebi ogokora aye, otatiga Nyasae abe amo nere.”
Ⅲ Yeso akamoiraneria, “Ekeene, ekeene, nagotebirie: Omonto ataiboiri eria kabere, takonyara korora oborwoti bwa Nyasae.”
Ⅳ Nikodemo akamobooria, “Naki omonto aranyare koiborwa kabeire omogaka? Nanyaare gosoa enda ya ng’ina eria kabere aiborwe?”
Ⅴ Yeso akamoiraneria, “Ekeene, ekeene, nagotebirie: Omonto ataiboiri ase amaache na Omoika, takonyaara gosoa ase oborwoti bwa Nyasae. Ⅵ Ekeiboire ase omobere n’omobere; na ekeiboire ase Omoika n’Omoika. Ⅶ Tokumia ekiagera nagoteebia: Goika oiborwe naende. Ⅷ Embeo nigo ekogusa aase erigetie, na egeturi kiaye nigo ogokeigwa, korende tomanyeti aase ekorwa gose aase ekogenda; naboigo na kera omonto oiboirwe ase Omoika.”
Ⅸ Nikodemo akamobooria, “Naki aranyare koba aya?”
Ⅹ Yeso akamoiraneria, akamoteebia, “Inee! Aye nomworokia bw’Abaisraeli, na aya toyamanyeti? Ⅺ Ekeene, nagotebirie: Eri tomanyete nario togokwana, na eri torooche twariorokia; na oborori bwaito timori kobwancha. Ⅻ Onye nkobateebia amang’ana ense, na inwe timokoyeegena, naki moreegene kondabateebie amang’ana a igoro? ⅩⅢ Naende monto onde tari ogendete igoro, otatiga ere oyoigete korwa igoro, Omwana‐o‐Mwanyabaanto. ⅩⅣ 3:14: Omob 21:9Naende buna Musa aimokereretie eng’iti ase erooro, naboigo na Omwana‐o‐Mwanyabaanto goika aimokererigwe igoro; ⅩⅤ erinde monto onde bwensi oyomwegenire tasira, korende abe nobogima bwa kare na kare.”
ⅩⅥ “Naki Nyasae ayianchete ense, goika akarua Omwana oye omomoima, erinde monto onde bwensi oyomwegenire tasira, korende abe nobogima bwa kare na kare. ⅩⅦ Naki Nyasae tatomete Omwana oye ase ense agambie ense, korende ense etoorigwe nere. ⅩⅧ Oyomwegenire takogambigwa oyotegeneti oikire kogambigwa, ekiagera atariegeneti erieta ri’Omwana omomoima o Nyasae. ⅩⅨ Na eke nakio ekiina ng’a omobaso ochire ase ense, korende abanto bagancha omosunte kobua omobaso, ekiagera ogokora kwabo kwarenge okobe. ⅩⅩ Naki kera oyogokora amabe nigo agechete omobaso, gose tagocha ase omobaso, ogokora kwaye tikobaisa koorokigwa. ⅩⅪ Korende oyogokora ekeene nigo agocha ase omobaso, ogokora kwaye kororekane ng’a nigo gwakorirwe ase Nyasae.”
Yohana Omobatisi na Yeso
ⅩⅫ Magega ase aya Yeso na aborokigwa baye bakagenda goika ense ya Boyahudi. Akabeera aroro amo nabarabwo, akabatisa. ⅩⅩⅢ Yohana nere nigo arenge kobatisera aria Ainoni, ang’e na Salimu, ekiagera amaache amange narengeo. Abanto bakamochiera bakabatiswa. ⅩⅩⅣ 3:24: Mat 14:3; Mar 6:17; Luk 3:19,20Naki Yohana konya tarabekwa echela.
ⅩⅩⅤ Nabo okoamererania gokaoroka ase egati yaborokigwa ba Yohana na Omoyahudi oyomo ase amang’ana ogwesibia. ⅩⅩⅥ Bagachiera Yohana, bakamoteebia, “Omworokia, ere oyorenge amo naye ng’umbu ya Yorodani, oyio kwaorogetie aye, oyio nao are akobatisa, na abanto bonsi nkomochiera bare.”
ⅩⅩⅦ Yohana akairaneria, “Omonto takonyara konyora ring’ana rinde rionsi otatiga aegwe korwa igoro. ⅩⅩⅧ 3:28: Yoh 1:20Inwe abanyene mwambereire kirori ng’a nateebete: Inche tari Kristo, korende natomirwe imotang’anere. ⅩⅩⅨ Oyore nomoriakari nere omonywomi, korende omong’wansi oye nigo agotenena na komoigwa, ogookera eriogi ri’omonywomi; naboigo omogooko one oikeranire bono.” ⅩⅩⅩ Ere goika amenteke, korende inche nkee.
Oyochire korwa igoro
ⅩⅩⅪ “Oyochire korwa igoro, oyio nigo are igoro ase onsi; oyore bw’ense noyo bw’ense, nigo agokwana amang’ana ’ense; ere oyochire korwa igoro nigo are igoro ase onsi. ⅩⅩⅫ Nigo akoba kirori ase aya arorire na koyaigwa; gose onde tari oyobwanchire oborori bwaye. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Oyobwanchire oborori bwaye oorokirie Nyasae maiso marore, ng’a noyo bw’ekeene. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Naki ere oyotometwe na Nyasae nigo agokwana amang’ana a Nyasae; naki Nyasae tari komoa Omoika ase ekerengo. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ 3:35: Mat 11:27; Luk 10:22Tata omwanchire Omwana, omoeire bionsi ase amaboko aye. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Oyoramwegene Omwana nare nobogima bwa kare na kare; oyotaigwereti Omwana takorora obogima, korende endamwamu ya Nyasae nigo emoikaranserete.”
