Omorendi otarenge omwegenwa
Ⅰ Naende agateebia aborokigwa baye, Narenge omonto omonda, orenge nomorendi. Oyio agasoerwa asare ng’a osiareire enibo yaye. Ⅱ Akamorangeria, akamoteebia: Ninki eke naigure ase ore? Orokia amang’ana y’oborendi bwao, ekiagera tokonyara kogenderera koba omorendi. Ⅲ Omorendi oria akabora ase omoyo oye: Ninki ndakore, ekiagera omonene one ondusirie ase oborendi bwane? Tindi ne chinguru chi’okorema; ogosaba nobosooku ase ’nde. Ⅳ Namanyire eke ndakore, erinde abanto bandoisie ase chinyomba chiabo, ekero nkorusigwa ase oborendi. Ⅴ Erio akarangeria abasiira b’omonene oye, oyomo ase oyomo, akaboria oyo bw’eritang’ani: Ninki oriete ki’omonene one? Ⅵ Akairaneria: Ebirengo rigana erimo bi’amaguta. Akamoteebia: Bogoria ekaratasi yao, ikaransa bwango, oriike emerongo etano. Ⅶ Naende akaboria oyonde: Naye ninki oriete? Akairaneria: Ebirengo rigana erimo bi’engano. Akamoteebia: Bogoria ekaratasi yao, orike emerongo etano na etato. Ⅷ Omonene oria agatogia omorendi otarenge omooronge, ekiagera akorete ase obong’aini. Naki abana b’ense eye nabang’aini kobua abana b’omobaso, ase ekegori kiabo abanyene.
Enchera engiya y’ogokorera enibo emeremo
Ⅸ Nkobateebia nde inwe: Ekorere abasani nenibo y’ense eye etari boronge, erinde ekero egochia gokea, baabaroisie inwe ase obomenyo bwa kare na kare.
Ⅹ Oyore omwegenwa ase ekere egeke, nabo are omwegenwa ase ekere ekenene; na oyotari omooronge ase ekere egeke, naboigo tari omooronge ase ekere ekenene. Ⅺ Onye timobeti abegenwa ase enibo y’ense eye etari boronge, ning’o oranyare kobegena koba nenibo y’ekeene? Ⅻ Onye timobeti abegenwa ase ekio ki’omonto onde, ning’o oranyare kobaa ekio ekere kiaino abanyene? ⅩⅢ 16:13: Mat 6:24Mosomba onde tari oranyaare gokorera abanene babere, ekiagera nageeche oyomo, na anche oyonde, gose nabwatane noyomo, na achaaye oyonde. Timokonyara gokorera Nyasae na enibo.
Ogotogonyerwa kw’Abafarisai
ⅩⅣ Abafarisai abarenge abanchi enibo bakaigwa amang’ana aya onsi, bakamosekereria. ⅩⅤ Akabateebia, “Inwe nabaanto mogwetogia ng’a nigo more abanyene oboronge ase obosio bwa Mwanyabaanto, korende Nyasae omanyire chinkoro chiaino, ekiagera ekeimokererete igoro ase Mwanyabaanto, Nyasae nigo akegechete.
ⅩⅥ 16:16: Mat 11:12,13“Amachiiko narengeo, na ababani boigo, goika ekero kia Yohana. Korwa ekero keria amang’ana amaya ay’oborwoti bwa Nyasae nkorandigwa are, na kera omonto nigo akobosoa ne chinguru. ⅩⅦ 16:17: Mat 5:18Korende naango kobua igoro na ense goeta, kobua enuguta eyemo y’amachiiko korua aroro.
ⅩⅧ 16:18: Mat 5:32; 1 Abakr 7:10,11“Kera oyogotiga mokaye na konyuoma oyonde otomanire, nere oyokonyuoma oyotigirwe nomosaacha oye, noyio otomanire.
Omonto omonda na omosabi Lazaro
ⅩⅨ “Narenge omonto gete omonda, orenge kweboyia chianga chimonyinga na echi’egatani engiya, na kwegokia kera rituko ase okoria. ⅩⅩ Na ase egesieri kiaye narengeo omonto oyomo omotaka, erieta riaye Lazaro, orenge namaote amange. ⅩⅪ Ere nigo arenge kogania kweigotia namatori are kogwa korwa ase emesa y’omonda oria, ne chisese nachirochio chicha chiamomena amaote aye. ⅩⅫ Omotaka oria agakwa, akabogorigwa nabamalaika, akabeekwa ase egekuba kia Aburaamu. Na oria omonda agakwa boigo, agatindekwa, ⅩⅩⅢ na ekero aare agwo aase Eora y’amakweri akaimokia amaiso aye, akarora Aburaamu okare aare, na Lazaro ore ase egekuba kiaye. ⅩⅩⅣ Akarera, akabora: Tata Aburaamu, indorera amaabera, toma Lazaro atobeke ekiara kiaye mesia ase amaache, akendie oromeme rwane, ekiagera nachandekire ase omorero oyo. ⅩⅩⅤ Aburaamu akabora: Omwana one, inyora buna kwanyorete amaya ao ase obogima bwao, na Lazaro boigo akanyora amabe, korende ere bono aiga oremiigwe, na aye ngochandeka ore. ⅩⅩⅥ Na kobua aya onsi, egati yaito na inwe eoromomo nereo yabeekirwe, erinde abanto abarigetie korua aiga bamboke gocha ase ore tibanyara, gose baria bare agwo tibanyara kwamboka gocha ase tore. ⅩⅩⅦ Akabora: Nagosabire tata, omotome ase enyomba ya tata, ⅩⅩⅧ ekiagera ninkare nabamura baminto batano; tiga agende abakuurere, erinde barabwo tibacha aa ase emechando. ⅩⅩⅨ Aburaamu akabora: Mbare na Musa na ababani; tiga baigwere barabwo. ⅩⅩⅩ Akabora: Yaya, tata Aburaamu, korende oyomo korwa ase abakuure aise kobachiera, inabaonchoke. ⅩⅩⅪ Akamoteebia: Bataigwereti Musa na ababani, naisa koboka omonto korwa ase abakuure, tibakoebererigwa banche.”
