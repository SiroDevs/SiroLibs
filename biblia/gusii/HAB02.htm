Omonene okoiraneria Habakuki
Ⅰ Inche nintenene korenda, ee, nintenene igoro ase omoorobiri bw’ogotengera, ndore ay’Omonene aranteebie, na buna ndairanerie igoro y’okorera kwane.
Ⅱ Omonene akang’iraneria, aganteebia, “Riika okorora okwo ase ebao namariko amaya, erinde oyo okoyasoma ayasome buya. Ⅲ 2:3: Abaibr 10:37Okorora okwo nigo koganyete engaki yaye eike. Nigo gokoayerera goikerane, tigokomocha goikerana. Nonya gokororekana ng’a gwatebanire, ekeene goika goikerane, tikogoteebana. Ⅳ 2:4: Abar 1:17; Abagl 3:11; Abaibr 10:38Rora, oyo okweimokereria igoro, omoyo oye tori omooronge, korende omonyene oboronge nabe moyo goetera ase okwegena kwaye.
Okogambigwa kw’abatari abanyaboronge
Ⅴ “Edivai nigo ere buna omoonchoreria omong’ainereria; omonto omwerori tari kogenderera koba n’obotoereru. Okogania kwaye nigo gokogarea kwaba buna aase abakuure; nigo konga buna amakweri atari goisaneka. Nigo agosangereria abanto b’ebisaku bionsi gocha asare na gokora abanto b’echinse chionsi koba abaye.” Ⅵ Abanto aba bonsi nigo baramokorere echeche baroisie engero y’okomochecheria bateebe, “Obobe nobwaye oyo‐gosangereria amo ebinto bitari ebiaye, na bwetweka ebinto airete bibe ntenenera. Ngoika ririri aragenderere gokora igo?”
Ⅶ Inee! Abanto baria kwairete ebinto biabo tibakogochera mobosokano, na bagochande, bakobwate bakoire ase obosomba. Ⅷ Ekiagera aye gwasakoire ebisaku ebinge, abanto b’ebisaku ebitigari mbagosakore aye; gwaiteire amanyinga ’abaanto abange, na ogakora ayare amabe ase ense na ase emechie, na ase bonsi abamenyeteo.
Ⅸ Obobe nobwaye omonto oria okonyora ebitoki bi’obobe na kobirenta ase enyomba yaye, erinde bweagachera egeswa kiaye aase aimokererete igoro ng’a abe noborendi korwa ase okobua kw’amabe. Ⅹ Aye kwarenteire enyomba yao obosooku ase ogoita abanto abange, na igo gwakorire ebibe bi’ogosiria obogima bwao aye omonyene. Ⅺ Amagena ’enyasi naake eriogi na emiamba ye rirongo neyairanerie.
Ⅻ Obobe nobw’omonto oyio okoagacha omochie ase ogoitera amanyinga na kobeeka oboroso bw’omochie ase ogokora ebibe. ⅩⅢ Abanto nigo bakwerosia gokora emeremo eyio erasambwe nomorero, na abanto b’ebisaku nigo bakwerosia gokora emeremo ere bosa. Rora, ayio nigo arure ase Nyasae bw’Emeganda. ⅩⅣ 2:14: Isaya 11:9Ense neichorigwe nokomanya kw’obonene o Omonene buna chinyancha chiichire namaache.
ⅩⅤ Obobe nobw’omonto oria okoa omonto onde ekenyugwa giochoganirie nobosongo kiamotindia, erinde anyare korigereria obogetirianda bwaye. ⅩⅥ Aye nigo gwaichoirwe nobosooku ribaga ri’ogosikwa. Aye kanywe, obe omonto otarogeti! Egekombe ki’egesusuro kere ase okoboko gw’okorio kw’Omonene nakegochere; na oboosoku mbotube obonene bwao. ⅩⅦ Obobe bwakoretwe ase Lebanoni mbogotube, na ogosirigwa gwe ching’iti koria gwachiondogetie; aye noegwe egesusuro, ekiagera gwaiterete amanyinga ’abaanto, na ogakora amabe ase ense, na ase emechie, na ase abanto bamenyeteo.
ⅩⅧ Omogwekano bw’ogosasimwa, mbitoki ki okorentera oyio okoyobomba? Oyio nomogwekano obombire ore omworokia omong’ainereria? Oyio oyoroisetie nigo asemeretie emeremo y’amaboko aye ere chinyasae chitari gokwana. ⅩⅨ Obobe nobwaye omonto oria ogoteebia egento kebaachire korwa ase omote: Boka! Na oteebia rigena ritanyare gokwana: Imoka! Inee! Ebinto ebio mbinyare gokworokereria enchera? Rora, ebio nigo bioomire ne chitaabu ne chifeta, korende omoika tori ime yabo.
ⅩⅩ “Korende Omonene nao are ase Hekalu yaye Enchenu; tiga ense yonsi ekire‐kiri ase obosio bwaye.”
