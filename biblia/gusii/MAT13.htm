Omerenganio bw’omobusuri
(Mar 4:1-9; Luk 8:4-8)
Ⅰ Ase rituko riria riria Yeso akarua nyomba agaikaransa ase enyancha mbarabare. Ⅱ 13:2: Luk 5:1-3Emeganda emenene ekamosangererekanera, goika ere akariina obwato ime, agaikaransa aroro, na omoganda bwonsi ogatenena ase engegu. Ⅲ Akabateebia amang’ana amange ase emebayeeno, okobora, “Omobusuri akamanora kogenda kobusura. Ⅳ Ekero are kobusura, chimbusuro chinde chikagwa ase enchera ensemo, chinyoni chigaacha, chigachiria, Ⅴ na echinde chikagwa ase egetare, aase amaroba arenge amake; chikamera bwango, ekiagera amaroba arenge amake. Ⅵ Omobaso ogachia kobara, chigasusana, na ekiagera chitarenge nemeri, chikooma. Ⅶ Na echinde chikagwa ase amagwa ime, na amagwa aria agakiina, agachimiga. Ⅷ Na echinde chikagwa ase amaroba amaya, chikaama echinde rigana, na echinde emerongo etano nomo, na echinde emerongo etato. Ⅸ Oyore namato akoigwera, tiga aigwe!”
Ekerenga ki’ogokwana ase emebayeeno
(Mar 4:10-12; Luk 8:9-10)
Ⅹ Aborokigwa baye bagaacha, bakamoboria, “Nase ki kware gokwana nabarabwo ase emebayeeno?” Ⅺ Akabairaneria, Inwe mwaeirwe komanya obobisi bw’oborwoti bwa igoro, korende barabwo tibaeiri. Ⅻ 13:12: Mat 25:29; Mar 4:25; Luk 8:18; 19:26Inaki oyore na gento naegwe, nere namenteranigwe; korende oyotabwati gento naurwe nonya nkeria abwate. ⅩⅢ Eke nakio gekogera ngokwana nabarabwo ase emebayeeno, ekiagera okorigereria ’nabarigererie, korende tibakorora, na ogotegerera nabategerere, korende tibakoigwa, nonya nokomanya, tibakomanya. ⅩⅣ 13:14: Isaya 6:9,10Ase barabwo obobani bwa Isaya bwaikeranire, obogoteeba:
Okoigwa namoigwe, korende timokomanya nonya ng’ake,
na okorigereria namorigererie, korende timokorora nonya ng’ake.
ⅩⅤ Inaki chinkoro chi’ abanto aba chiabeire chinyoro,
na amato abo nigo are amarito ase okoigwa,
na amaiso abo bayamiamirie,
tibacha korora namaiso abo
gose koigwa namato abo,
gose bamanye ne chinkoro chiabo,
baonchoke, na inche mbagwenie13:15 Isaya 6:9-10; Ogok 28:26-27.
ⅩⅥ 13:16: Luk 10:23,24Korende amaiso aino nasesenirie, ekiagera nkorora are, na amato aino, ekiagera nkoigwa are. ⅩⅦ Nekeene nabateebirie: Abaabani abange na abanyene oboronge mbaganetie korora aya morooche inwe, tibayarora, na koigwa aya mokoigwa inwe, tibayaigwa.
Ogotebereria kw’omobayeeno bw’omobusuri
(Mar 4:13-20; Luk 8:11-15)
ⅩⅧ “Gaki, igwa omobayeeno bw’omobusuri. ⅩⅨ Ekero omonto akoigwa ring’ana ri’oborwoti, na ere takorimanya, nigo agocha oria omobe, otaboria riria riasimekirwe ase enkoro yaye, oyo nere obusuirwe ase enchera ensemo. ⅩⅩ Na oyo obusuirwe ase egetare nere oria okoigwa ring’ana, erio kegima oribwata nomogooko, ⅩⅪ korende tari nemeri ime yaye, ogenderera ekero egeke; na ekero emechando egochi‐gocha, gose okorosigwa ase engencho ye ring’ana, erio bwango ogwa korwa ase okwegena kwaye. ⅩⅫ Na oyo obusuirwe ase amagwa, oyo nere okoigwa ring’ana; okorengereria gw’ense na obong’ainereria bw’eniibo biaba nkomiga bire ring’ana, riaba tirikwama. ⅩⅩⅢ Na oyo obusuirwe ase amaroba amaya, nere oria okoigwa ring’ana, orimanya; nere ekeene bwama okwama. Oyomo bwama rigana, na oyonde emerongo etano nomo, na oyonde emerongo etato.”
Omobayeeno bw’amabanchore
ⅩⅩⅣ Akabateebia omobayeeno onde, agateeba, “Oborwoti bwa igoro nigo bogwekaine nomonto obusurete embusuro engiya ase omogondo oye; ⅩⅩⅤ korende ekero abanto bararire, omobisa oye agacha akabusura amabanchore ime ase engano, akagenda. ⅩⅩⅥ Egachia komera ekama chinyeke, erio amabanchore naro akarorekana. ⅩⅩⅦ Erio abasomba b’omonyene enyomba bagaacha, bakamobooria: Omonene, tikwabusurete embusuro engiya ase omogondo oo? Inaki rende ororekanire namabanchore? ⅩⅩⅧ Akabateebia: Nomobisa okorete ayio. Abasomba bakamobooria: Inee! Norigetie tochie toyasimore? ⅩⅩⅨ Akabakania: Yaya; moise koyasimora amabanchore namoiye engano amo naro. ⅩⅩⅩ Bitige bikiine bionsi bibere goika rigesa, na ekero kie rigesa ninteebie abakogesa: Ritang’ani sangereria amabanchore, moyaboe emesaima monyare koyasamba; korende engano moyesangererie gochia kiage kiane.”
Omobayeeno bw’entetere y’Omosirapi na oyo bw’ememera
(Mar 4:30-34; Luk 13:18-21)
ⅩⅩⅪ Akabateebia omobayeeno onde, agateeba, “Oborwoti bwa igoro nigo bogwekaine nentetere y’omosirapi omonto abogoretie, akayesimeka ase omogondo oye; ⅩⅩⅫ ero nenke mono kobua chimbusuro chionsi, korende koyakiinire yaba enene kobua ebimeri bionsi, eba omote, goika chinyoni chia igoro chichia koroisia ebiswa ase chinsagia chiaye.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Akabateebia omobayeeno onde, “Oborwoti bwa igoro nigo bogwekaine nememera omokungu abogoretie, akayebeka ase ebirengo bitato bi’obosie, goika bionsi bikabota.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Aya onsi Yeso nigo ayakwanete ase emeganda eria ase emereng’anio; na tabateebeetie ring’ana otatiga ase emebayeeno, ⅩⅩⅩⅤ 13:35: Zab 78:2erinde rinyare goikeranigwa eriatebetwe nomobani,
“Nimaanore omonwa one ase emebayeeno,
inyatore ayabisegete korwa ogotongwa gw’ense.”
Omobayeeno bw’amabanchore ogotebererigwa
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Erio Yeso agatiga emeganda, akagenda nyomba. Aborokigwa baye bakamochiera, bakobora, “Totebererie omobayeeno bw’amabanchore a mogondo.” ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Akairaneria, “Oyogosimeka embusuro engiya n’Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto; ⅩⅩⅩⅧ omogondo nense; embusuro eyio engiya nabana b’oborwoti; na amabanchore nabwo abana boria omobe. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Omobisa oria oyasimegete nere Saitani; rigesa nomoerio bw’ense, na abaraache kogesa nabamalaika. ⅩⅬ Buna amabanchore agosangererigwa asambwa nomorero, naboigo nomoerio bw’ense orache koba. ⅩⅬⅠ Omwana-o-Mwanyabaanto natome abamalaika baye, na barabwo nabasangererie na kobarusia korwa ase oborwoti bwaye abakogera abande bagochwe, na abagokora ebibe bonsi, ⅩⅬⅡ nababatugute ase eriiko ri’omorero. Aroro agwo abanto inababe nekerero na okoriania amaino. ⅩⅬⅢ Erio abanyene oboronge nabagirigente buna erioba*13:43Erioba: nario “risase” ase oborwoti bwa Ise obo. Oyore namato akoigwera, tiga aigwe13:43 11:15; 13:9.
Emereng’anio y’eniibo, eruru, na ekeonga
ⅩⅬⅣ “Oborwoti bwa igoro nigo bogwekaine neniibo yabisire ase omogondo, eye omonto anyorete, akayebisa; erio ase omogooko oye akagenda, akaoonia ebi abwate bionsi, akagora omogondo oria.
ⅩⅬⅤ “Naende oborwoti bwa igoro mbogwekanirie nomonto omoonchoreria, okorigia chiruru chingiya. ⅩⅬⅥ Ere agachia korora eruru eyemo ye rigori rinene, akaimoka akagenda, akaoonia ebi abwate bionsi, akayegora.
ⅩⅬⅦ “Naende oborwoti bwa igoro nigo bonga buna ekeonga ekiarutetwe ase enyancha ime, kegasangereria chinswe chia kera egesaku. ⅩⅬⅧ Ekero giaichoire, abanto bagakeng’usa gocha engegu; bagaikaransa, bagasangereria chiria chingiya ase amakuru†13:48Amakuru: nebibekerero binga ebitonga, na chiria chimbe bagachituguta. ⅩⅬⅨ Naboigo eraache koba ase omoerio bw’ense. Abamalaika nabaoroke bache gwatanana baria ababe korwa ase abanyene oboronge, Ⅼ babatugute ase eriiko ri’omorero. Aroro agwo abanto inababe nekerero na okoriania amaino13:50 13:42; 22:12; 24:51; 25:30.”
ⅬⅠ Yeso akababoria, “Mwamanyire aya onsi?” Bakamoiraneria, “Ee.” ⅬⅡ Akabateebia, “Ase engencho eyio kera Omwarimu bw’amariiko oyokorirwe koba omworokigwa bw’oborwoti bwa igoro, obeire omogwekano bw’omonyene enyomba, oyokorua ase emonga yaye ebinto ebiyia na ebikoro.”
Yeso okwangwa Nazareti
(Mar 6:1-6; Luk 4:16-30)
ⅬⅢ Ekero Yeso akoorire emebayeeno eyio, akarua aroro, akagenda aase aande. ⅬⅣ Agachia goika ense yaye omonyene‡13:54 Agachia goika ense yaye omonyene: nao Yeso akiinerete na ekaba buna sobo nonya oiboreretwe Bethlehemu na gotamigwa gochia Misiri ekero kiobwana bwaye, akaborokereria ase esinagogi yabo, goika bagakumia, bagateebania, “Oyo ng’ai anyorire obong’aini obo na ogokora oko gwe chinguru? ⅬⅤ Oyo tari omwana bw’omobaachi? Ngi’na tari oria okorokwa Mariamu? Na bamura bamwabo tibari Yakobo, na Yusufu na Simioni, na Yuda? ⅬⅥ Na basubati bamwabo bonsi tibari aa seito? Gaki oyo ng’ai ayanyorire aya onsi?” ⅬⅦ 13:57: Yoh 4:44Bakagechigwa asare. Korende Yeso akabateebia, “Omobani takoborwa ogosikwa otatiga ase abanto baye na ime ase ense yaye.” ⅬⅧ Ere takorete amakumia amange agwo, ekiagera batarenge nokwegena.
