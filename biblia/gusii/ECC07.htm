Ebirengererio igoro y’obogima
Ⅰ 7:1: Emeb 22:1Erieta rigiya nigo ribuete amaguta amaya akweakwa, ne rituko ri’ogokwa nigo rire rigiya kobua eri’okoiborwa.
Ⅱ Mbuya kogenda ase enyomba y’ebirero kobua kogenda ase enyomba y’omoyega bw’okwegokia, ekiagera oyio noro omoerio o kera omonto, na abare moyo mbabeeke amang’ana aya ase chinkoro chiabo.
Ⅲ Omoichano nigo ore omuya kobua amaseko, ekiagera omoichano okororekana ase obosio nigo ogokora enkoro yaba nomogooko.
Ⅳ Chinkoro chi’abaanto abang’aini nigo chikoba ase enyomba y’ebirero, korende chinkoro chi’abariri nigo chire ase enyomba y’okwegokia.
Ⅴ Mbuya omonto gotegerera ogotogonyerwa kw’omonto omong’aini kobua gotegerera ogoteera kw’abariri.
Ⅵ Ogoseka kw’abariri nigo konga amagwa akoyia atoreka inse y’enyongo ere riko. Ayio boigo mbosa.
Ⅶ Ekeene, emoko nigo ekoonchora omonto omong’aini oba omoriri, ne riaaki nigo rigosaria ebirengererio.
Ⅷ Omoerio bw’egento nigo ore omuya kobua omochakano oye; na omonto omoremereria nigo are omuya kobua omonto omoenenu.
Ⅸ 7:9: Yakb 1:19Tobaisa koba omwango ase okogechigwa, ekiagera okogechigwa nigo komenyete ase chinkoro chi’abariri.
Ⅹ Tobaisa goteeba, “Nase ki amatuko a kare arenge amaya kobua aya bono?” Okoboria okwo tikori okw’obong’aini.
Ⅺ Obong’aini nigo bore obuya buna omwando*7:11 Omwando: Egento omonto akoegwa buna engeria, na nigo bogokonya abwo baroche erioba. Ⅻ Obong’aini nigo bogotokonya buna chitaabu chigotokonya, korende obuya bw’omonyasemi mboria ng’a obong’aini nigo bokorenda obogima bw’oyio ong’ainiire.
ⅩⅢ Rengereria emeremo ya Nyasae! Ning’o oranyare korongeyia egento Omonene akorire borogochi? ⅩⅣ Ekero amatuko ao are amaya kabe nomogooko, na ekero are amabe rengereria ng’a Nyasae nere okorire ayio onsi abere, erinde omonto tabaisa komanya erirache komobwatia korwa magega yaye.
ⅩⅤ Ase obogima obo bwane obore bosa narorire kera egento; narorire omonto omonyaboronge ogosira ore ase oboronge bwaye, na omonyabibe okogenderera koba moyo amatuko amange ore ase ebibe biaye. ⅩⅥ Tobaisa koba omooronge goetania, gose koba omong’aini goetania. Nase ki ogwesiria aye omonyene? ⅩⅦ Tobaisa koba omobe goetania, gose tobaisa koba omoriri. Nase ki oganetie gokwa engaki yao etaraika? ⅩⅧ Nigo ere buya ng’a obwate ring’ana eri erimo, na totiga neria kabere, ekiagera omonto omoirogete Nyasae namanye eki agokora ase kera ring’ana.
ⅩⅨ Obong’aini nigo bogokora abare abang’aini koba ne chinguru chibuete echi’abagaambi ikomi bare ase omochie oyomo.
ⅩⅩ Ekeene, omonto tari aiga ase ense ore omooronge ogokora amaya otari gokora ebibe.
ⅩⅪ Tobaisa gotegerera amang’ana onsi abanto bagokwana, nigo orabaise koigwa omosomba oo ogokoragereria. ⅩⅫ Aye omonyene nomanyete ase enkoro yao ng’a ase chingaki chinyinge kwabeire gokoragereria abanto bande.
ⅩⅩⅢ Aya onsi ngateema koyaenekia ase enchera y’obong’aini, ngateeba, “Goika imbe nobong’aini.” Korende obong’aini obwo bokabeera aare korwa ase ’nde. ⅩⅩⅣ Amang’ana aria nigo akare aare mono, naende akare aatambe gochia inse, aatambe mono, ning’o oranyare koyatuka? ⅩⅩⅤ Erio ngachaka korengereria imanye, na gotuka, na korigia obong’aini, na komanya buna ebinto bionsi bire; naende nkagania komanya obobe bw’oboriri, na oboriri obore obobarimo.
ⅩⅩⅥ Erio nkanyora egento ekeroro kebuete amakweri. Egento ekio nakio omokungu enkoro yaye yarenge omotego, naende amaboko aye are chingataare. Omonto ogogetie Nyasae tari kobwatwa nomokuungu oyio, korende omonyabibe ere nigo akobwatwa. ⅩⅩⅦ Omorandia nigo agoteeba iga: Naama! Amang’ana ayio nkayamanya, ekero nabeekeranetie ring’ana erimo ase erinde koyamanya onsi. ⅩⅩⅧ Aya nabeire nkona‐koyairanerera korigia komanya buna are, korende tindayamanya. Ase abanto elifu eyemo nkanyora omosacha oyomo, korende ase abwo bonsi tinanyorete omokungu nonya noyomo. ⅩⅩⅨ Naama! Egento gioka narigirie nanyorire neke: Nyasae nigo atongete Mwanyabaanto bare abaikeranu, korende barabwo batukire chinchera chinyinge chi’okobaisanekia.
