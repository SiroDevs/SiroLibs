Ogoakwa kwa gatano na gatato: Chingige chigocha
Ⅰ Eri’Omonene agateebia Musa, “Genda ase Farao ekiagera nakong’irie enkoro yaye na boigo ne chinkoro chi’abasomba baye, erinde inyare koorokia ebikone ebi biane ase egati yabo; Ⅱ erio naye nonyare goteebia abana bao na abachokoro bao amang’ana aya nakorire ase egati y’Abamisiri, na ebikone ebi nakorire ase egati yabo. Erio momanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.”
Ⅲ Musa na Aroni bakagenda ase Farao, bakamoteebia, “Aya nar’Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaiberania, agoteeba: Inee! Ngoika ririri orabe gokwanga gwekeyia ase obosio bwane? Bae abanto baane ribaga bagende bankorere. Ⅳ Onye gokwanga koa abanto baane ribaga bagende, rigereria ankio nindete chingige ase ense yao. Ⅴ Ne chingige echio inachitube ense eye yonsi, erio monto tanyara korora riroba, na chirochio inachisirie endagera eria etasaretigwe nembura y’amagena, amo na kera omote ore ase ense yao. Ⅵ Chinyomba chiaino amo nechi’abasomba bao, na echi’Abamisiri bonsi inachiichorwe ne chingige. Omogwekano bw’echio chisokoro chiao gose chisokoororia chitaroche nonya ng’ake korwa ekero bachaagete komenya ase ense eye goika reero iga.” Erio Musa akaonchoka, akarua o Farao.
Ⅶ Abasomba ba Farao bakamoteebia, “Ngoika ririri omonto oyo aratobeke ase emechando? Batige abanto aba bagende bakorere Omonene, Nyasae obo. Toramanya nonya mbono ng’a ense ya Misiri yasarekire?”
Ⅷ Erio Musa na Aroni bakarentwa ase Farao, Ere akabateebia, “Genda, mokorere Omonene, Nyasae oino. Korende ning’o nang’o moganetie mogende komo?”
Ⅸ Musa akamoiraneria, “Nigo toragende twensi, abake na abagotu, na abana baito, abamura na abaiseke, ching’ondi ne chitugo chiaito chinde chionsi, ekiagera ntobwate Rituko Rinene ri’Omonene.”
Ⅹ Korende Farao akabateebia, “Omonene abe amo nainwe onye nkobaa ribaga mogende amo nabana baino. Korende namanyire ng’a nigo more nebirengererio ebibe ase chinkoro chiaino. Ⅺ Yaya, taibo, genda inwe mweka abasacha, mokorere Omonene. Naki ayio naro konya moganetie.” Erio bagaserigwa korwa ase Farao.
Ⅻ Omonene agateebia Musa, “Rambora okoboko kwao igoro y’ense ya Misiri, erinde chingige chichiche chirie ebimeri binde bionsi ebitasaretigwe nembura y’amagena.” ⅩⅢ Agwo Musa akaimokia enyimbo yaye igoro y’ense ya Misiri, n’Omonene akarenta omwaga korwa moocha, rituko rigima, omobaso na obotuko. Ekero bwakia omwaga oria korwa moocha konya orentire chingige. ⅩⅣ 10:14: Okom 9:2,3Chingige chigaacha igoro y’ense yonsi ya Misiri, chikagwa chinsemo chionsi chi’aroro. Chingige chinyinge buna echio chitana gocha ase amatuko aetire, gose tichigochi‐gocha naende nonya ng’ake. ⅩⅤ Chingige echio chigatuba ense eria yonsi goika ekamwama ti, chikaria ebimeri bionsi pi ase ense eria, na amatunda onsi y’emete, ebio bionsi bitasaretigwe nembura y’amagena. Tigiatigarete kemeri kende gionsi ekebese gose amato y’emete ase ense ya Misiri engima.
ⅩⅥ Ase obwango mono Farao akarangeria Musa na Aroni, akabateebia, “Nakorire ebibe ase Omonene, Nyasae oino, na ase more. ⅩⅦ Gaki bono nasabire, monyabere ebibe biane ekero eke gioka. Monsabere ase Omonene, Nyasae oino, erio anduserie ogoakwa oko tikong’ita.” ⅩⅧ Musa akarua o Farao, agasaba Omonene. ⅩⅨ Omonene akaonchora omwaga okaba bw’echinguru, okagusa korwa bosongo, okaira chingige chionsi gochia ase Enyancha Embariri. Tiyatigara engige nonya neyemo ase ense ya Misiri engima. ⅩⅩ Korende Omonene agakong’ia enkoro ya Farao, na ere akaanga koa Abaisraeli ribaga bagende.
Ogoakwa gwa kianda: Omosunte ogocha
ⅩⅪ Eri’Omonene agateebia Musa, “Imokia okoboko kwao gochia igoro, erinde omosunte ekerima onyare koba ase ense ya Misiri, omosunte oranyare gotaba‐tabwa.” ⅩⅫ 10:22: Zab 105:28; Okom 16:10Musa akaimokia okoboko kwaye gochia igoro, na omosunte ekerima okaba ase ense ya Misiri engaki y’amatuko atato. ⅩⅩⅢ Abanto tibanyara kororana; ase amatuko atato onde tarenge gosuka kogenda aande. Korende Abaisraeli barabwo mbabwate omobaso aase onsi ase bamenyete.
ⅩⅩⅣ Farao akarangeria Musa, akamoteebia, “Genda mokorere Omonene. Moire abana baino, korende chitugo chiaino chirochio mochitige aiga.”
ⅩⅩⅤ Musa akamoiraneria, “Yaya, goika otoe ching’iti togochia koruera Omonene, Nyasae oito, ebing’wanso bi’ogosambwa, na ebinde bi’okonyenywa. ⅩⅩⅥ Naende goika toire chitugo chiaito chionsi, ende teri eratigare magega nonya neyemo. Korwa ase echio nao togochia korusia echi toraruere Omonene, Nyasae oito; intwe titomanyeti buna togochia gokorera Omonene goika toraika aroro.”
ⅩⅩⅦ Omonene agakong’ia enkoro ya Farao, na ere tancha kobaa ribaga bagende. ⅩⅩⅧ Erio Farao agateebia Musa, “Karue ase obosio bwane! Bwerende, tobaisa gocha kondora naende, naki rituko riria orache korora obosio bwane naende, nigo orakwe.”
ⅩⅩⅨ Musa akamoteebia, “Gwakwanire ekeene; inche tingocha gokorora naende.”
