Okobokigwa gwa Kristo
Ⅰ Bono baminto, nkobainyoria nde enchiri eyio nabaranderetie; nero mwanyorete, na ase eyio nao moteneine, Ⅱ na ase eyio mwatoorigwe, onye mwabwatire mono amang’ana nabaranderetie. Onye taibo, rirorio nigo mwegenete bosa.
Ⅲ 15:3: Isaya 53:5-12Naki ritang’ani nabarenterete amang’ana aya nanyorete inche omonyene, ng’a Kristo nigo akwete ase engencho y’ebibe biaito, buna amariiko agoteeba; Ⅳ 15:4: Zab 16:8-10; Mat 12:40; Ogok 2:24-32naende agatindekwa, erio akaboka rituko ria gatato, buna amariiko agoteeba; Ⅴ 15:5: a Luk 24:34; b Mat 28:16,17; Mar 16:14; Luk 24:36; Yoh 20:19naende akaorokera Kefa; naende na baria ikomi na babere. Ⅵ Magega akaorokera abaminto goetania amagana atano amo. Abange ase abwo moyo bare nonya mbono, abande bakuure. Ⅶ Magega y’ayio akaorokera Yakobo, erio abatomwa bonsi. Ⅷ 15:8: Ogok 9:3-6Na omoerio akang’orokera nainche. Inche nigo inde buna oyoiboretwe ase ekero getare boronge. Ⅸ 15:9: Ogok 8:3Naki inche nomoke ase abatomwa, ntagwenereti korokwa omotomwa, ekiagera nachandete ekanisa ya Nyasae. Ⅹ Korende ase obuya bwa Nyasae nabeire buna inde; na obuya bwaye bore ase ’nde tibwarenge bosa. Korende kobua mono nakorire emeremo goetania bonsi, gose tari inche, korende obuya bwa Nyasae obore amo nainche. Ⅺ Gaki onye ninche, gose mbaria, naboigo tokorandia, naboigo mwegenete.
Okobokigwa kw’abakuure
Ⅻ Bono onye Kristo orandiigwe ase more buna nabogetigwe korwa ase abakuure, naki rende abande ase more bagoteeba ng’a kobokigwa kw’abakuure tikori? ⅩⅢ Korende onye okobokigwa kw’abakuure tikori, rirorio Kristo nere tabogetigwe. ⅩⅣ Naende onye Kristo tabogetigwe, rirorio okorandia gwaito nokwa bosa, na okwegena kwaino nokwa bosa. ⅩⅤ Ee, naintwe rirorio twarorekanire koba birori b’oborimo ba Nyasae, ekiagera twateebete ng’a Nyasae nigo abogetie Kristo, oyo rirorio tamobogetie, onye abakuure tibakobokigwa. ⅩⅥ Naki onye abakuure tibakobokigwa, rirorio Kristo nere tabogetigwe. ⅩⅦ Na onye Kristo tabogetigwe, okwegena kwaino nokwa bosa; nigo more nonya mbono ase ebibe biaino. ⅩⅧ Naende nabwo bakwete ase Kristo basirire. ⅩⅨ Onye nase obogima obo bwoka tosemeretie Kristo, rirorio intwe nabataka tore kobua abanto bonsi.
ⅩⅩ Korende bono nekeene ng’a Kristo obokiigwe korwa ase abakuure buna ogotongorwa kw’abakuure. ⅩⅪ Naki buna amakweri arentetwe nomonto, naboigo nokobokigwa kw’abakuure kwarentirwe nomonto. ⅩⅫ Ekiagera buna ase Adamu bonsi bagokwa, naboigo nase Kristo bonsi nababe moyo, ⅩⅩⅢ korende kera oyomo aase aye; Kristo agatongora, naende ekero arachiche, abare abaye nababe moyo. ⅩⅩⅣ Erio agwo omoerio ochiche, ekero arae Nyasae Tata oborwoti, magega gasaririe kera obogambi na kera okobua ne chinguru. ⅩⅩⅤ 15:25: Zab 110:1Naki negwenerete ere agambe, goika abeeke ababisa bonsi inse ase amagoro aye. ⅩⅩⅥ Omobisa bw’omoerio orasirigwe namakweri. ⅩⅩⅦ 15:27: Zab 8:6“Naki Nyasae obekire ebinto bionsi inse ase amagoro aye.” Korende ekero agoteeba, “Bionsi biabekirwe inse asare”, nigo amanyekanete ng’a Nyasae, oyobegete ebinto bionsi inse asare, tabariri ase ebinto ebio. ⅩⅩⅧ Gaki ebinto bionsi kobiabekirwe inse asare, erio agwo Omwana omonyene nere nabekwe inse ase Nyasae, oyo obegete ebinto bionsi inse asare, erinde Nyasae abe nokobua ase bionsi.
ⅩⅩⅨ Inee! Aba bakobatiswa ase engencho y’abakuure, naki rende bagokora? Onye abakuure tibakoboka nonya ng’ake, nase ki bakobatiswa ase engencho yabo? ⅩⅩⅩ Nase ki tore nemechando kera engaki? ⅩⅩⅪ Ekeene baminto, ase ogwetogia kwane inde nakwo ase more ase Kristo Yeso Omonene oito, nigo ngokwa kera rituko. ⅩⅩⅫ 15:32: Isaya 22:13Onye, nase engencho ya Mwanyabaanto, nkarwana ne ching’iti chintindi aaria Efeso, inki erangwenere? Onye abakuure tibakoboka, “Tiga torie na konywa, ekiagera ankio natokwe.” ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Timong’aineka! Egosori embe nigo egosaria chingencho chingiya. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Boka buna egwenerete ase oboronge, timokora ebibe naende. Naki abande tibamomanyeti Nyasae. Natebiire aya ase okobasookia.
Omobere bwo’kobokigwa
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Korende omonto naborie, “Naki abakuure baraboke? Naende mobere ki barache noro?” ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Omoriri oyo, eke ogosimeka aye, tikeri komera getakweti. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Neke ogosimeka, tori gosimeka egento keria kegocha koba, korende embusuro yoka; onye nengano gose mbusuro ende yonsi. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Korende Nyasae ayea ekieni buna anchete, na kera embusuro ayea ekieni kiaye. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Chinyama chionsi tichiri echimo; korende echinde nechia Mwanyabaanto, echinde nechie ching’iti, echinde nechie chinyoni, na echinde nechie chinswe. ⅩⅬ Naende mbire igoro ebitongwa bitongire, na mbire ase ense ebitongwa bitongire, korende obonene bw’ebia igoro nigo bore ao, na obonene bw’ebia nse nigo bore ao. ⅩⅬⅠ Obonene bw’erioba nigo bore ao, na obonene bw’omotienyi nigo bore ao, na obonene bwe ching’enang’eni nigo bore ao, naende ogwatananekana nkore ase egati ye ching’enang’eni chinyene ase obonene.
ⅩⅬⅡ Naboigo nokobokigwa kw’abakuure. Osimekwa ase ogosareka, obokigwa ase ogotasareka. ⅩⅬⅢ Osimekwa ase obosooku, obokigwa ase obonene; osimekwa ase oboreu, obokigwa ase chinguru. ⅩⅬⅣ Omobere o Mwanyabaanto osimekwa, korende omobere bw’omoika obokigwa. Onye omobere o Mwanyabaanto oreo, naboigo noyo bw’omoika noreo. ⅩⅬⅤ 15:45: Omoch 2:7Naboigo eriikire, “Omonto bw’eritang’ani Adamu akaba omonto ore moyo”, Adamu bw’omoerio akaba omoika bw’okoa obogima. ⅩⅬⅥ Korende oria orenge bw’omoika, tari oro orenge bw’eritang’ani, korende noria bw’omobere o Mwanyabaanto orenge bw’eritang’ani; magega y’ayio oria bw’omoika. ⅩⅬⅦ Omonto bw’eritang’ani akarwa inse, agatongwa korwa ase amaroba; omonto o kabere akarwa igoro. ⅩⅬⅧ Buna omonto oyo bw’amaroba arenge, naboigo nab’amaroba bare; na buna omonto o igoro are, naboigo naba igoro bare. ⅩⅬⅨ Na buna tobogoretie ekieni ki’oria bw’amaroba, naboigo torabogorie ekieni ki’oria o igoro. Ⅼ Abaminto, aya nkobateebia naya, ng’a omobere na amanyinga tibikonyora omwando bw’oborwoti bwa Nyasae; gose ogosareka tigokonyora omwando bw’ogotasareka.
ⅬⅠ 15:51: 1 Abats 4:15-17Rigereria, aya nkobateebia nobobisi. Titogokwa intwe twensi, korende twensi natoonchorwe, ⅬⅡ ase ekero egesinini, okomiamia na okoramokia, ekero ki’egetureeri ki’omoerio. Naki egetureeri nakebuge, na abakuure nababokigwe, tibaaba nogosareka, na intwe natoonchorwe. ⅬⅢ Naki ogosareka kwagweneire koboigwe ogotasareka, na ogokwa koboigwe ogotakwa. ⅬⅣ 15:54: Isaya 25:8Ekero oyo bw’ogosareka oranyore ogotasareka, na oyo bw’ogokwa oranyore ogotakwa; erio naribe ring’ana riria ririikire,
“Amakweri amerirwe ase okobua.”
ⅬⅤ 15:55: Hos 13:14“Amakweri, ng’ai okobua kwao kore?
Amakweri, ng’ai obororo bwao bore?”
ⅬⅥ Obororo bw’amakweri nebibe, ne chinguru chi’ebibe namachiiko. ⅬⅦ Korende mbuya mono ase Nyasae, ere oyogotoa okobua goetera ase Omonene oito Yeso Kristo.
ⅬⅧ Gaki baminto, abanchwa, mobe ne chinguru, motari gotengechigwa, momente mono gokora emeremo y’Omonene ase amatuko onsi, momanye ng’a emeremo yaino ase Omonene teri bosa.
