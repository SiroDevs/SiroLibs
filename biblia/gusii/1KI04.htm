Ⅳ
Abakonyi ba Sulemani
Ⅰ Omorwoti Sulemani igo agamberete Abaisraeli bonsi, Ⅱ na abanene abaarenge komokoonya nabwo aba.
Asaria, mosinto o Zadoki, nere orenge Omokuani.
Ⅲ Elihorefu na Ahiya, basinto ba Sisa, nabwo baarenge abariki.
Yehosafati, mosinto bw’Ahiludi, nere orenge omoriki bw’amang’ana are gokoreka.
Ⅳ Benaya, mosinto o Yehoyada, nere orenge omochiki bw’abasikari.
Zadoki na Abiatari, nabwo baarenge abakuani.
Ⅴ Asaria, mosinto o Nathani, nere orenge omoteneneri igoro ase abanene baria.
Zabudi, mosinto o Nathani, nere orenge Omokuani, naende nigo areenge omosani bw’omorwoti.
Ⅵ Ahisari, nere otenenerete enyomba y’obogaambi.
Adoniramu, mosinto o Abuda, nere otenenerete emeremo yagwenerete gokorwa.
Ⅶ Sulemani nachorete abanto abanene ikomi na babere abaarenge gosangereria endagera na koyerentera omorwoti amo nab’enyomba yaye. Kera oyomo ase aba ikomi na babere nigo areenge korenta endagera eisaine koriegwa ase omotienyi oyomo ase omwaka. Ⅷ Aya naro amarieta ’abaanto abwo.
Beni‐Huri agatenenera ense y’ebigoro y’Efraimu.
Ⅸ Beni‐Dekeri agatenenera emechie ya Makasi, na Shaalabimu, na Beti‐Semesi, na Eloni, na Beti‐Hanani.
Ⅹ Beni‐Hesedi agatenenera emechie y’Aruboti na Soko, na ense yonsi ya Hefa.
Ⅺ Beni‐Abinadab agatenenera Nafati‐Dori yonsi (nere onyuomete Tafati, omoiseke o Sulemani).
Ⅻ Baana, mosinto bw’Ahiludi, agatenenera emechie ya Taanaki na Megido, na Beti‐Sehani yonsi, eyio eikerete Saretani, Irianyi ya Yesireeli, na korwa Beti‐Sehani goika Abeli‐Mehola, na ng’umbu ya Yoki‐Meamu.
ⅩⅢ Beni‐Geberi agatenenera Ramoti o Gileadi, na Emechie ya Yairi Omomanase agwo Gileadi, na orogongo rw’Arigobu agwo Basani. Igo atenenerete emechie emerongo etano nomo emenene yagiteire chindwaki, na ebiita biabo nigo biareenge nemekorogoto y’etai.
ⅩⅣ Ahinadabu, mosinto o Ido, agatenenera orogongo rwa Mahanaimu.
ⅩⅤ Ahimasi agatenenera orogongo rwa Nafutali (nere onyuomete Basemati, omoiseke o Sulemani).
ⅩⅥ Baana, mosinto o Husai, agatenenera orogongo rw’Aseri na omochie o Bealoti.
ⅩⅦ Yehosafati, mosinto o Parua, agatenenera orogongo rwa Isakaru.
ⅩⅧ Simei, mosinto bwa Ela, agatenenera orogongo rwa Benjamini.
ⅩⅨ Geberi, mosinto bwa Uri, agatenenera ense ya Gileadi yareenge kogamberwa na Sihoni, omorwoti bw’Abaamori, na Ogi, omorwoti o Basani. Nigo areenge omoteneneri oyomo bweka ase ense eyio yonsi.
Obogaambi bwa Sulemani bogosesenigwa
ⅩⅩ Abayuda na Abaisraeli nigo baarenge abange mono, buna omokenye o nyancha; barabwo bakaria na konywa, bakaba bakwegokia. ⅩⅪ 4:21: Omoch 15:18; 2 Amg 9:26Sulemani akaba okogaambera amarwoti onsi, gochakera orooche rw’Eufarati na goikera ense y’Abafilisti, na goikera orobebe rwa Misiri. Abanto bonsi b’oborwoti bwaye nigo baarenge komorentera ebango na bakaba bakomokorera ase obogima bwaye bwonsi.
ⅩⅫ Endagera Sulemani arenge korenterwa ase rituko erimo nigo yareenge: obosie obonuke, ebitonga amagana atato, na obosie bonde, ebitonga amagana atano na rimo, ⅩⅩⅢ ne chiombe ikomi chinoru chiare gotugerwa nyomba, ne chiombe chiare korisia getii, emerongo ebere, na ching’ondi rigana erimo, ne chinsune, ne chingabi, na amang’o, ne chinyoni chinoru.
ⅩⅩⅣ Sulemani nigo areenge kogaambera chinse chionsi chiare bosongo y’Eufarati, korwa Tifusa goika Gasa. Ase igo nigo areenge kogaambera abarwoti bonsi abaarenge bosongo y’Eufarati, nigo areenge nomorembe ne chinse chionsi chiamoetanaine. ⅩⅩⅤ Abayuda na Abaisraeli nigo bamenyete nomorembe korwa Dani goika Beeri‐Seba; kera omonto nigo areenge nemezeituni yaye na emeko yaye, ase emiaka yonsi Sulemani abagamberete.
ⅩⅩⅥ 4:26: 1 Abar 10:26; 2 Amg 1:14; 9:25Sulemani nabwate ameri e chibarasi chilifu emerongo ene chiareenge kong’usa chigari, ne chibarasi chiare koriinwa, chilifu ikomi na ibere. ⅩⅩⅦ Abateneneri baria abanene nigo baarenge korenta chindagera ase omorwoti Sulemani na ase abanto bonsi abaarenge koragera bwoye. Kera oyomo obo nigo are korenta endagera eisaine koriegwa ase omotienyi oyomo ase omwaka, endagera tiyarenge kobora. ⅩⅩⅧ Kera omonto nigo areenge korenta esairi na obonyansi aase biaganeirie ase chibarasi chiare kong’usa chigari amo ne chibarasi chinde.
ⅩⅩⅨ Nyasae akaa Sulemani obong’aini ne chisemi goetania ekerengo; okomanya kwaye nigo kwareenge okonge, koreng’ana buna obonge bw’omokenye ore ase engegu y’enyancha. ⅩⅩⅩ Obong’aini bwa Sulemani nigo bwabuete obw’abaanto bonsi ba Moocha na obw’Abamisiri bonsi. ⅩⅩⅪ 4:31: Zab 89Igo areenge omong’aini kobua abanto bonsi, kobua Etani Omoezara, na Hemani, na Kalikoli, na Darida, basinto ba Maholi; ne chinkuma chiaye chikagenda goika ase ebisaku bionsi biamoetanaine. ⅩⅩⅫ 4:32: Emeb 1:1; 10:1; 25:1; Ogot 1:1Boigo nakwanete emereng’anio chilifu isato; na amatera aye nigo areenge elifu eyemo, na atano. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Ere nigo areenge goteeba igoro y’emete yonsi, emenene buna emecharake ya Lebanoni na ebimeri ebike buna hisopo eyekomera ase chindwaki. Boigo agateeba igoro ye ching’iti, ne chinyoni, ne ching’iti chikwagura, ne chinswe. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Abanto bakarwa ase chinse chionsi, bagacha koigwa obong’aini bwa Sulemani, na abarwoti bonsi b’ense bagatoma abanto bagachia komoigwa.
