Ogoseerigwa korwa ase abanto ba Omonene
Ⅰ “Omosacha ogombairie, gose ekiimo kiaye ki’obosacha kebutogete, tabaisa gosoa ase omosangererekano Omonene.
Ⅱ “Ekerentane, gose omonto bw’ororeeria rw’aye, nonya ngoika ekiaare gia ikomi, tabaisa gosoa ase omosangererekano Omonene.
Ⅲ 23:3: Neh 13:1,2“Abaamoni, gose Abamoabu, gose ororeeria rwabo, nonya ngoika ekiaare gia ikomi, tibaabaisa gosoa ase omosangererekano bw’Omonene nonya ng’ake. Ⅳ 23:4: Omob 22:1-6Barabwo tibaabarototete na kobarentera endagera na amaache ekero mwarenge ase orogeendo korwa Misiri, naende nigo barangeretie Baalamu, mosinto o Beori, korwa Petori ya Mesopotamia achiche kobaragereria inwe. Ⅴ 23:5: Omob 23:7—24:9Nonya naboigo Omonene Nyasae oino, taigwerete Baalamu, korende akaonchora okoragereria koria gokaba ogosesenigwa ase more, ekiagera Omonene Nyasae oino, nigo abanchete. Ⅵ Ase amatuko aino onsi more moyo, timobaisa kobarigeria omorembe, gose ogosesenigwa.
Ⅶ “Timobaisa gochaaya Abaedomu, barabwo n’abanto bamino; gose timobaisa gochaaya Abamisiri, ekiagera inwe agwo kare nao mwarenge abamenyi ase ense yabo. Ⅷ Abanto b’ekiaare kia gatato ase ororeeria rwabo, bancherwe basoe ase omosangererekano Omonene.
Okorenda egetwori ki’abarwani b’esegi kebe gechenegete
Ⅸ “Ekero morabe mokogeenda korwania ababisa baino, na momenyete ase egetwori, mwerende mono korwa ase kera egento getachenegeti. Ⅹ Onye omonto ase egati yaino obeire oyotachenegeti ase ogosokia embusuro yaye y’oroiboro botuko, oyio asoke gochia isiko y’egetwori; tabaisa gosoa ime aroro.
Ⅺ “Ekero kia mogoroba, esibie omobere n’amaache, na ekero bwairiire kegima asoe ase egetwori.
Ⅻ “Mochoore aase isiko y’ebitwori ase mogochia gwekonyera. ⅩⅢ Mobe mokoimokia chinyimbo amo nebirwanero biaino; ekero mogochia rosana agwo isiko y’ebitwori, mokuunye chingoro ne chinyimbo chiria, na ekero mwekonyire motube obonyira n’amaroba. ⅩⅣ Ebitwori biaino goika bibe ebichenu, ekiagera Omonene Nyasae oino, nigo agotaara ase ebitwori biaino, erinde abakoonye, n’abeeke ababisa baino ase amaboko aino. Omonene tabaisa korora obonyinyeru ase more; aise koborora, nigo arabatige.
Amachiiko are ao ao
ⅩⅤ “Onye omosomba otamire korwa ase omonene oye, ochire ase more, timobaisa komoirania gochia ase omonene oye naende. ⅩⅥ Motige amenye amo nainwe ase omochie arachoore, oyio oramogookie agwo ase emechie yaino. Timobaisa komokorera bobe.
ⅩⅦ 23:17: Abal 19:29“Onde taaba ase Abaisraeli, omokungu gose omosacha, okwebeeka ase obonyaka bwe chie hekalu. ⅩⅧ Timobaisa korenta ase enyomba y’Omonene Nyasae oino, chitaabu chikonyorekana korwa ase obonyaka bw’abakuungu, gose obw’abasaacha banga buna chisese, ase ogoikerania chiira. Chibesa chikonyorekana korwa ase chinchera echio nigo chire okogeechia ase Omonene Nyasae oino.
ⅩⅨ 23:19: Okong’ 22:25; Abal 25:36,37; Okoir 15:7-11“Ekero okoa omonto bw’egesaku kiao chitaabu singo, gose endagera, gose gento kende gionsi, tobaisa komoboria ebitoki ekero agokoiraneria egento keria airete singo. ⅩⅩ Nonyare gosaba ebitoki korwa ase omonto omomenyi kwaete singo, korende tari korwa ase omonto omino Omoisraeli. Koraigwere amang’ana ayio, rirorio Omonene Nyasae oino, nagosesenie ase ense eria mogochia koira ebe eyaino.
ⅩⅪ 23:21: Omob 30:1-16; Mat 5:33“Ekero okoria eira ase Omonene, Nyasae oo, goika oikeranie eira eyio. Otayeikeraneti nigo Omonene arakobarere ebibe ebio. ⅩⅫ Korende koratige koria eira, tokobarerwa ebibe ase ayio. ⅩⅩⅢ Orende mono, oikeranie egento gwakwanete n’omonwa oo, ekiagera nigo kwariete eira y’ogwancha ase Omonene, Nyasae oo.
ⅩⅩⅣ “Ekero ogoeta ase omogondo bw’emesabibu bw’omogisangio oo norie amatunda y’emesabibu goika oigote, korende totwa nonya n’erimo obeeke ase esaaro yao na koriira. ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero oraabe kogoeta ase omogondo bw’omogisangio oo ore n’endagera eteneine, notabore ebigara n’okoboko kwao obunge otakune chinyeke, korende tobaisa kogesa n’omoyio oo ore enkuro*23:25 enkuro: sickle, omoyio egekobotio okonacherwa engano n’obonyansi endagera yaye eteneine.
