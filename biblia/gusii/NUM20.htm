ⅩⅩ
Aya akoregete agwo Kadeshi
(Okong’ 17:1-7)
Ⅰ Ase omotienyi bw’eritang’ani omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli ogaika ase erooro ya Sini na gotoora agwo Kadeshi. Miriamu agakwana agatindekwa aroro.
Ⅱ 20:2: Okong’ 17:1-7Amaache tarenge agwo ase batoorete; ase ayio omoganda ogasangererekana amo, okaba mamincha ya Musa na Aroni. Ⅲ Abanto baria bakaamerererania na Musa, bagakwana, “Nanga nigo yabeete buya kobua onye naintwe ’ntwakwete ekero abaminto baitetwe agwo ase obosio bw’Omonene. Ⅳ Ninki mwatwegegeretie*20:4 mwatwegegeretie: engencho yaye nokobogoria omosigo gose egurube ere enene kobua okwenyara kwao intwe omoganda Omonene gocha ase erooro eye, erio toche gokwera aiga, amo ne chitugo chiaito? Ⅴ Ninki mwatorusetie Misiri gocha ense eye yamakweri? Aase aa ataka atari kwama endagera, gose amako, gose amasabibu, gose amakomomanga. Nonya namaache tokonywa tari aa.” Ⅵ Musa na Aroni bakarua agwo ase omoganda oria, bakageenda ase Eema y’Omosangererekano, bagatung’ama inse bakaumama agwo ase egesoero. Erio obonene bw’Omonene bokabaorokera. Ⅶ Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, Ⅷ “Imokia enyimbo eria, erio aye na Aroni mosangererie omoganda bwonsi, na agwo ase amasio y’ abanto bonsi mokwane negetare keria, na amaache ’nasoke korwa aroro. Ase enchera eyio ’mosokerie abanto amaache yokonywa, ne chitugo chiabo ’nchiyanywe.” Ⅸ Musa akaimokia enyimbo eria korwa aase yarenge, agwo ase obosio bw’Omonene, buna Omonene konya amochiikire.
Ⅹ Musa na Aroni bagasangereria omoganda bwonsi bosio bw’egetare keria, akabateebia, “Igwa inwe abasaria aba! Inee! ’Nkobasokeria tore amaache korwa ase egetare eke?” Ⅺ Musa akarambora okoboko kwaye, agaaka egetare keria nenyimbo yaye ara kabere. Amaache agasoka amaange mono, na omoganda oria okanywa ne chitugo chiabo chikayanywa boigo.
Ⅻ Omonene agateebia Musa na Aroni, “Ekiagera inwe motanyegenete na koorokia ase amasio y’Abaisraeli ng’a inche nomochenu inde, timokoraa na goikia omosangererekano oyio ase ense eria ebe eyabo. ⅩⅢ Aya naro amaache a Meriba†20:13 Meriba: rora Okong’ 17:7 aase Abaisraeli baamereranetie n’Omonene, na ere akeorokia ng’a nomochenu are ase egati yabo.”
Omorwoti o Edomu okwanga koa Abaisraeli ribaga goeta ase ense yaye
ⅩⅣ Musa agatoma abanto korwa Kadeshi gochia ase omorwoti o Edomu bamoteebie, “Amang’ana aya naro intwe Abaisraeli bamino togoteeba: Nomanyete emechando yonsi yatonyorire, ⅩⅤ buna chisokoro chiaito chiagendete ase ense ya Misiri, chikamenyao emiaka emenge. Abamisiri bagachichanda ase obotindi obobe, bagatochanda naintwe. ⅩⅥ Ekero twarerete ase Omonene, ere akaigwa ebirero biaito, agatoma omomalaika agatorusia korwa ase ense ya Misiri. Bono nao tore aiga Kadeshi, omochie ore ase orobebe kegima rw’orogongo rwao. ⅩⅦ Koranche otoe ribaga toete ase egati y’ense yao. Titokogobera gochia ase emegondo y’endagera, gose ey’emesabibu, gose titokonywa amaache korwa ase chinsoko chiaino. Intwe nigo toraete ase enchera enene yoka, titokogobera gochia ensemo ya borio, gose eya bomosi, goika tobe twaetanirie orogongo rwao.”
ⅩⅧ Korende omorwoti o Edomu akamoiraneria, “Timobaisa goeta ase egati y’ense y’ane; moise goeta, nigo ndabarwanie esegi imbaite nemioro.”
ⅩⅨ Abaisraeli bakamoteebia, “Intwe nigo toraete ase enchera enene yoka, na gatoranywe amaache ao, intwe gose chitugo chiaito, rirorio ’natoakane amaache ayio. Toe ribaga toete rioka namagoro, titokogania gento kende goetania ekio.”
ⅩⅩ Abaedomu bakairaneria naende, “Timori ne ribaga goeta ase ense yaito.” Bagacha nabarwani barenge ne chinguru gocha korwania Abaisraeli. ⅩⅪ Ekiagera Abaedomu barinete Abaisraeli ribaga goeta ase ense yabo, Abaisraeli bakarua ase bare, bagaeta ensemo eng’ao.
Aroni Ogokwa
ⅩⅫ Omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli okarua Kadeshi, okageenda ogaika egetunwa kia Hori. ⅩⅩⅢ Agwo ase egetunwa keria kia Hori, ase orobebe rw’ense ya Edomu, Omonene agakwana na Musa na Aroni, akabateebia, ⅩⅩⅣ “Aroni nakwe, asangererekane amo n’abanto baye. Ere tagosoa ase ense eria naeire Abaisraeli, ekiagera inwe babere mwasaretie, timwanyigwera buna nabachiigete agwo ase amaache a Meriba. ⅩⅩⅤ Ira Aroni na Eleazari, momura oye, igoro ase egetunwa kia Hori. ⅩⅩⅥ Orusie Aroni chianga chiaye chi’obokuani, ochibekere Eleazari, momura oye. Aroni ere ’nakwe, na asangererekane amo n’abanto baye, ekero more aaria.” ⅩⅩⅦ Musa agakora buna Omonene amochiigete. Barabwo bagatiira gochia igoro as egetunwa keria kia Hori, ekero omoganda bwonsi origereretie. ⅩⅩⅧ 20:28: Okong’ 29:29; Omob 33:38; Okoir 10:6Musa akarusia Aroni chianga chiria chi’obokuani, agachibekera Eleazari, momura o Aroni. Aroni agakwera ase egetunwa keria, na Musa na Eleazari bagatirimboka korwao. ⅩⅩⅨ Ekero omoganda bw’Abaisraeli orooche ng’a Aroni okuure bakamoreera ase engaki y’amatuko emerongo etato.
