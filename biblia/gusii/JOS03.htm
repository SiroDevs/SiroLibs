Abaisraeli bakwamboka orooche rwa Yorodani
Ⅰ Yoshua akaimoka, mambia chuni, amo n’Abaisraeli bonsi bakaimoka korwa Sitimu, bakagenda, bagaika orooche rwa Yorodani. Bagatoora bakabeera aroro magega bataraamboka. Ⅱ Magega y’amatuko atato kaerire abateneneri bagaeta ase ebitwori, Ⅲ bagateebia abanto, “Ekero morarore Esanduki y’Omonene, Nyasae oino, ere ne chibao chiriikire oborori bw’okobwatana, ekobogorigwa nabakuani Abalawi, morue aase aa, moyetunyane. Ⅳ Timoyeika ang’e; mobeere magega ase oboare bw’ekiromita eyemo. Erio namomanye ase mogoeta, naki timwana goeta enchera eyio rinde.”
Ⅴ Yoshua agateebia abanto, “Mwechene, ekiagera anki’Omonene nakore amakumia ase egati yaino.” Ⅵ Magega y’ayio agateebia abakuani, “Imokia Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana, mogende motang’ane abanto.” Erio barabwo bakayeimokia, bakagenda, bagatang’ana abanto.
Ⅶ Omonene agateebia Yoshua, “Rituko ria reero ngochaka inde gokoimokererie igoro, ase amasio a ’baisraeli, nabarabwo mbamanye ng’a buna narenge amo na Musa, nabo ndabe amo naye boigo. Ⅷ Aye nochiike abakuani abwo babogoretie Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana ng’a ekero baraike engegu y’orocche rwa Yorodani goika bateneneo.”
Ⅸ Erio Yoshua agateebia Abaisraeli, “Inchuo ang’e aiga, motegerere amang’ana ay’Omonene, Nyasae oino, akobateebia.” Ⅹ Naende akabora, “Iga namomanye ng’a Nyasae ore moyo nigo are ase egati yaino, takomocha kobaseria korwa ase more Abakanaani, na Abahiti, na Abahivi, na Abaperisi, na Abagirigasi, na Abaamori, na Abayebusi. Ⅺ Rora, Esanduki ere ne chibao chiriikire oborori bw’okobwatana, eyio y’Omonene bw’ense engima, nkwambokigwa ere gochia rooche ng’umbu, bosio bwaino. Ⅻ Gaki bono chora abanto ikomi na babere korwa ase ebisaku bi’Abaisraeli, oyomo korwa ase kera egesaku. ⅩⅢ Igo erabe ekero abakuani abwo babogoretie Esanduki eria y’Omonene Nyasae bw’ense engima, baratache amaache a Yorodani namagoro abo, amaache akorwa rogoro ya Yorodani nanacheke, atenene buna enyasi.”
ⅩⅣ Ekero abanto baimogete korwa ase chiema chiabo erinde bamboke Yorodani, abakuani bakabatang’ana, babogoretie Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana. ⅩⅤ Bono orooche rwa Yorodani nigo rware goichora engaki yerigesa amaache aetanana chingegu. Ekero abakuani, abwo babogoretie Esanduki eria, baigete Yorodani na batachete amaache namagoro abo, ⅩⅥ erio amaache ayare gotirimboka korwa rogoro agatenena buna orwaki aare mono, agwo Adamu, omochie orenge ang’e na Saretani. Amache arenge gotirimboka gochia enyancha ya Araba*3:16Enyancha ya Araba: Nero enyancha y’omonyo nero enyancha y’omonyoo, akanacheka pi. Erio abanto bakaamboka kororia omochie o Yeriko. ⅩⅦ Ekero Abaisraeli baare kwamboka ase ense yakamokire, abakuani abwo babogoretie Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana kw’Omonene bagatenena gati‐gati ya Yorodani, ase ense yakamokire, goika abanto bonsi bakaera ase okwamboka Yorodani.
