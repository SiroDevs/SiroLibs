Ⅰ 23:1: 1 Abar 1:1-40Ekero Daudi konya agotire mono akabeeka Sulemani, omomura oye, akaba omorwoti igoro ase Abaisraeli.
Emeremo y’Abalawi
Ⅱ Daudi agasangereria abaraai bonsi b’Abaisraeli amo nabakuani na Abalawi. Ⅲ Abalawi abarenge nemiaka emerongo etato na goetania bakabarwa, na omobaro obo okaba chilifu emerongo etato na isano na isato (38,000). Ⅳ Daudi agateeba, “Abanto chilifu emerongo ebere na baane (24,000) ase aba mbabe bagotenenera emeremo ye nyomba y’Omonene, na abanto chilifu isano nemo (6,000) nababe abateneneri na abagaambi ebiina; Ⅴ abanto chilifu inye (4,000) nababe abarendi b’ebiita na abanto chilifu inye (4,000) babe bagotogia Omonene n’ebinto ebio bikobugigwa naroisirie ase okomotogia.”
Ⅵ Daudi agaatanana Abalawi ase ebiombe kobwatekana nabamura ba Lawi: Gerisoni na Kohati, na Merari.
Ⅶ Abana abamura ba Gerisoni nabwo Ladani na Simei. Ⅷ Abana abamura ba Ladani nabwo aba batato: Yehieli, omoteneneri obo, na Setamu, na Yoel. Ⅸ Abana abamura ba Simei nabwo aba batato: Selomoti na Hasieli na Harani. Aba nabwo barenge abateneneri be chiamate chia Ladani. Ⅹ Na abana abamura ba Simei nabwo aba baane: Yahati na Sina, Yeusi na Beria. Ⅺ Yahati nere orenge omoteneneri obo, na Sisa oyo o kabere, korende Yeusi na Beria tibabwate abana abamura abange; ase ayio bakabeekeranigwa amo ase okobarwa, bakaba eamate eyemo.
Ⅻ Abana abamura ba Kohati nabwo aba baane: Amuramu na Isari, Heburoni na Usieli. ⅩⅢ 23:13: Okong’ 28:1Abana abamura b’Amuramu nabwo Aroni na Musa. Aroni agaatananwa koba oyochenegete mono kogenderera botambe. Ere amo nabamura baye babe bagosamba ubani ase obosio bw’Omonene na komokorera, naende babe bagosesenia ase erieta riaye botambe. ⅩⅣ Abana abamura ba Musa, omonto o Nyasae, bakabareranigwa amo negesaku kia Lawi. ⅩⅤ Abana abamura ba Musa nabwo Gerisomu na Eliezeri. ⅩⅥ Ase abana ba Gerisomu Sebueli nere orenge omoteneneri obo. ⅩⅦ Ase abana b’Eliezeri Rehabia nere orenge omoteneneri obo. Eliezeri tabwate abana abamura bande; korende abana abamura ba Rehabia nigo barenge abange mono.
ⅩⅧ Ase abana ba Isari, Selomiti nere orenge omoteneneri obo. ⅩⅨ Abana abamura ba Heburoni nabwo aba: Yeria nere orenge omoteneneri obo, na Amaria, oyo o kabere, na Yahasieli, oyo o gatato, na Yekameamu, oyo o kane. ⅩⅩ Abana abamura ba Usieli nabwo Mika, omoteneneri obo, na Isia, oyo o kabere.
ⅩⅪ Abana abamura ba Merari nabwo Mali na Musi; na abana abamura ba Mali nabwo Eleazari na Kisi. ⅩⅫ Eleazari nigo akwete atabwati abana abamura, korende nabwate abana abaiseke. Abanto bamwabo, abana abamura ba Kisi, bakanyuoma abaiseke baria. ⅩⅩⅢ Abana abamura ba Musi nabwo aba batato: Mali, na Ederi, na Yeremoti.
ⅩⅩⅣ Abanto aba nabwo barenge ab’ororeria rwa Lawi ase chiamate chiabo amo nabateneneri be chiamate chiria, buna barigetwe kera oyomo ase erieta riaye. Abwo nigo barenge nemiaka emerongo ebere na goetania, na nigo baisanerete gokora emeremo y’obokoreri bw’enyomba y’Omonene.
ⅩⅩⅤ Daudi akabora, “Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli, oeire abanto baye obotoereru, na bono ere nigo amenyete Yerusalemu kare na kare. ⅩⅩⅥ 23:26: Okoir 10:8Ase ayio Abalawi tibaganeirie kobogoria Eema gose ebinto binde bionsi bi’obokoreri bw’Eema eria.” ⅩⅩⅦ Ase amatuko omoerio o Daudi, Abalawi bonsi bakarikwa, aba konya bakoorire emiaka emerongo ebere na goetania. ⅩⅩⅧ 23:28: Omob 3:5-9Emeremo yabo nebe ogokonya abwo b’ororeria rwa Aroni ase obokoreri bw’enyomba y’Omonene, na gotenenera chiraaro na ebinyomba ebike; boigo ogosibia ebinto bichenire, na gokora emeremo ende yonsi y’obokoreri bw’enyomba ya Nyasae. ⅩⅩⅨ Naende babe bagotenenera emeremo y’emegati ekoruegwa ase Nyasae, na ey’obosie obonuke bw’okoruegwa kw’endagera, na ey’emegati ere emeoo etabekiri memera, na ey’ekarangeire orogio, na ey’obosie bwochoganirie amaguta, na ey’ebirengo binde bionsi. ⅩⅩⅩ Barabwo babe bagotenenera kera rituko, mambia na mogoroba, bakoiraneri’Omonene ng’a mbuya mono na komotogia. ⅩⅩⅪ Nigo barenge gokonya ekero kende gionsi ebing’wanso bi’ogosambwa biarenge koruegwa ase Omonene, ase Chisabato na ase Emetienyi Emeyia, na ase Amatuko Amanene ande. Nigo baroberetigwe gokora emeremo eyio koreng’ana nomobaro oganeirie, bakagenderera koyekora botambe ase obosio bw’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅫ Ase enchera eye mbabe bakorenda Eema y’Omosangererekano na Aase Aachenu, na bakonye abanto bamwabo ab’ororeria rwa Aroni ase obokoreri bw’enyomba y’Omonene.
