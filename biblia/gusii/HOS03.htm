Hosea amo n’omokuungu otarenge omwegenwa
Ⅰ Omonene aganteebia, “Genda omwanche naende omokungu oyio nonya ogokora obotomani nomwanchi oye, omwanche buna Omonene anchete Abaisraeli, nonya baonchokeire chinyasae ching’ao, naende babeire n’okogania kwa amasabibu amomo.”
Ⅱ Ase igo nkamogora chisekeli chi’echifeta ikomi na isano, na esairi ebitonga ikomi na bitano. Ⅲ Nkamoteebia: “Ogenderere komenya nainche amatuko amange kore omweriti ase engencho yane. Tobaisa gokora obonyaka, gose koba oyo bw’omosaacha onde omoao, na inche ningenderere kwerita ase engencho yao.”
Ⅳ Naboigo Abaisraeli baragenderere komenya ase amatuko amange batabwati omorwoti gose omogambi, batari korua ebing’wanso, gose gosasiima amagena atenenirie, gose koba negesibao ki’obokuani, gose ebinyasae bibekire ase chinyomba. Ⅴ Nyuma y’ayio Abaisraeli nabairane, barigie Omonene, Nyasae obo, na oyo bw’ororeria rwa Daudi, omorwoti obo; mbamoiroke Omonene, na gosemeria obuya bwaye ase amatuko y’omoerio.
