ⅩⅩⅫ
Yakobo okoimoka kogenda koumerana na Esau
Ⅰ Yakobo akagenderera norogendo rwaye, bakaumerana nabamalaika ba Nyasae. Ⅱ Na ekero Yakobo abarooche akabora: “Oyo nomoganda o Nyasae!” Ase igo, Akaaroka aase aria Mahanaimu*32:2 Mahanaimu: engencho yaye: ebitwori bibere.
Ⅲ Yakobo agatoma abatomwa batang’ane kogenda ase Esau momura omwabo, baike ensemo ya Edomu, ase ense y’Edomu. Ⅳ Akabachiika bateebie Esau omonene oye iga: “Aya naro Yakobo, omosomba oo, agokwana: Nabeire nkomenya ase ense y’obogeni, ense ya Labani goika reero iga; Ⅴ nanyorire chiombe, ne chitigere, ne ching’ondi, na abasomba, abamura na abaiseke. Na bono, omonene one, nigo natomire abanto nkomanyie amang’ana ayio, inyoore ogwancherwa ase obosio bwao.”
Ⅵ Abatomwa baria, ekero bairanete ase Yakobo bakamoteebia: “Ntwaika o momura omino Esau, ere ngocha are gokorotota ore nabaanto amagana ane (400).” Ⅶ Erio Yakobo akaondoka mono na koumata, agaatanana abanto baria baarenge amo nere, ne ching’ondi, ne chiombe, ne chingamia, bikaba ebiombe bibere, Ⅷ akarengereria ng’a: “Esau karache aumokere ekeombe kie ritang’ani na kogesiria, keria kende nagetooreke.”
Ⅸ Erio Yakobo agasaba, akabora: “Aye Nyasae o sokoro yane Aburaamu, Nyasae o Isaka tata, aye Omonene, gwanteebetie ng’a: Oirane gochia seino, na eamate yao, na inche ningokorere amaya. Ⅹ Inche tingwenereti nonya ng’ake obuya na obwegenwa bwonsi gwankorerete inche, omosomba oo, naki engaki naambogeete orooche rwa Yorodani nigo nabwate enyimbo yoka koboko kwane, na bono nabeire ebiombe bibere. Ⅺ Koranche ontoorie bono korwa ase okoboko kw’oyominto Esau, ekiagera nairokire ng’a nabaise kong’ita, aite na abakungu na abana. Ⅻ 32:12: Omoch 22:17Korende aye ngwanteebetie: Ningokorere amaya, ninkore ororeeria rwao robe buna omokenye o nyancha, otanyare kobareka, ekiagera nomonge.”
ⅩⅢ Erio Yakobo akaraara aroro ase obotuko obwo; agachora korwa ase etugo eria abwate, echio aranyare koa Esau omwabo ebiegwa. ⅩⅣ Agachoora chimbori chiaberi amagana abere, ne chigoree emerongo ebere, ne ching’ondi chiaberi amagana abere, ne chimingichi emerongo ebere, ⅩⅤ ne chingamia chiare gokamwa emerongo etato, amo ne chimori chiabo, ne chiombe chiaberi emerongo ene, ne chieeri ikomi, ne chitigere chiaberi emerongo ebere, ne chitigere chinsacha ikomi. ⅩⅥ Akabeeka ching’iti echio ase amaboko ’abasomba baye, kera riicho akaribeeka ao, agateebia abasomba baye: “Eta motang’ane, na egati ya kera riicho motige ribaga.” ⅩⅦ Agachiika omosomba oria bw’eritang’ani, akamoteebia: “Esau ominto, ekero araumerane naye, na gokoboria: Noyo ng’o ore? Ng’ai ogochia? Etugo eye otang’anetie, neya ng’o?. ⅩⅧ Erio omoiranerie, omoteebie: Ney’omosomba oo Yakobo, nekeegwa akorenteire aye omonene one Esau; ere omonyene ngotobwatia are korwa magega,” ⅩⅨ Agachiika naende oyo o kabere, na oyo o gatato, na abande bonsi abaare gotunyana amaicho, akabateebia: “Moteebie Esau amang’ana ayio ayio ekero mokoumerana nere. ⅩⅩ Naende momoteebie: Omosomba oo Yakobo ngotobwatia are korwa magega.” Ere nigo arengereretie ng’a: “Nimwororobie nebiegwa ebi namotang’aneirie; magega yaye nindore obosio bwaye, aande nanyanchere.” ⅩⅪ Naboigo ebiegwa biria bigatang’anigwa gochia bosio bwaye, ere omonyene akaraara obotuko obwo aase aria atenenetie eema yaye.
Yakobo ogokinana na Nyasae, naende okorokwa Israeli
ⅩⅫ Ase obotuko obwo Yakobo akaimoka, akaira abakungu baye babere, na abasomba baye babere abakungu, na abana baye ikomi nomo, akaamboka orooche rwa Yaboki. ⅩⅩⅢ Akabaimokia, akabaambokia orooche, akaambokia na bionsi ebi areenge nabio. ⅩⅩⅣ 32:24: Hos 12:3,4Yakobo agatigara ere bweka.
Erio omonto oyomo agacha bagakinana nere, goika bogakiamoka. ⅩⅩⅤ Nere omonto oria, ekero arora ng’a tari kobua Yakobo, akamokuna enengo y’ebachwa, akamoteng’eria, kobagendererete gokinana. ⅩⅩⅥ Erio akamoteebia: “Ntige ngende, ekiagera bwakiire.”
Korende Yakobo akamoteebia: “Tingokoa ribaga ogende, otaransesenia.”
ⅩⅩⅦ Akamobooria: “Naki okorokwa?”
Akamoiraneria: “Yakobo.”
ⅩⅩⅧ 32:28: Omoch 35:10Erio akamoteebia: “Bono tokorokwa naende Yakobo, nigo orarokwe Israeli†32:28 Israeli: engencho yaye: ogokinana na Nyasae, ekiagera mwakinanire na Nyasae na abanto, na aye kwabuire.”
ⅩⅩⅨ 32:29: Abag 13:17,18Yakobo akamobooria: “Koranche, onteebie erieta riao.”
Korende akamoteebia: “Nase ki okomboria erieta riane?” Agwo akamosesenia.
ⅩⅩⅩ Yakobo Akaaroka aase aria Penieli‡32:30 Penieli: engencho yaye: obosio bwa Nyasae, naki nigo ateebete: “Naroranire na Nyasae obosio ase obosio, na obogima bwane bwatoorigwe.” ⅩⅩⅪ Erioba rikaorokera Yakobo kagoeta Penueli, akaba ogosegora, ekiagera ateng’eretigwe enengo y’ebachwa. ⅩⅩⅫ Ase ayio Abaisraeli tibari koria omoki bw’enengo y’ebachwa goika reero iga, ekiagera omonto oria namoteng’eretie Yakobo omoki bw’enengo y’ebachwa yaye.
