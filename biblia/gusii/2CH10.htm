Ⅹ
Ogotaigwera kw’ebisaku bia Sugusu
(1 Abar 12:1-20)
Ⅰ Rehoboamu akagenda Sekemu, ekiagera Abaisraeli bonsi konya bachiire aroro komobeka abe omorwoti. Ⅱ Na ekero Yeroboamu, mosinto o Nebati, aigwete amang’ana ayio, akairana korwa Misiri aaria ase atamete gochia kwebisa korwa ase omorwoti Sulemani. Ⅲ Abanto bakarangeria Yeroboamu; ere amo n’Abaisraeli bonsi bakagenda, bagakwana na Rehoboamu, bakamoteebia, Ⅳ “Iso nigo akorete rikorogoto riaito rikaba ririto. Ase ayio bono aye orikore ribe rigusu, bwororobie emeremo eria emekong’u iso atobekerete, naintwe ntogokorere.”
Ⅴ Rehoboamu akabateebia, “Genda moirane aa ase ’nde naende magega y’amatuko atato.” Ase ayio bakagenda.
Ⅵ Erio omorwoti Rehoboamu akaboria ogosemigwa korwa ase abagaaka baria barenge gosemia Sulemani ise, ekero arenge moyo, akababoria, “Ngosemia ki morang’e erinde ing’iranerie abanto aba?”
Ⅶ Abagaaka baria bakamoiraneria, “Korabe omwororo ase abanto aba na kobagwenera, naende okwane amang’ana amaya ase bare, barabwo nababe bakogokorera botambe.”
Ⅷ Korende Rehoboamu akaanga ogosemia kw’abagaaka baria, akarigia ogosemigwa korwa ase abamura b’ekegori kiaye, abwo barenge komokorera. Ⅸ Akababoria, “Ngosemia ki morang’e? Ninki ndairanerie abanto aba bamboretie ng’a nyororobie rikorogoto riria tata ababeekerete?”
Ⅹ Abamura baria b’ekegori kiaye bakamoteebia, “Aya naro orateebie abanto abwo bakoboretie ng’a bwororobie rikorogoto riria iso akorete ririto: Ekiara gi’okoboko kwane nigo kere ekenetu kobua ekebono gia tata Ⅺ Na bono rikorogoto riria rikong’u tata ababeekerete, inche nigo ndarimente ribe ririto goetania. Tata nigo arenge kobang’wania ebiranya, korende inche nigo ndabang’wanie ne chinkini chichomire chibonyi chinyoge chinga amagwa.”
Ⅻ Yeroboamu amo nabaanto bonsi bagacha ase Rehoboamu omorwoti rituko ria gatato buna abateebeetie bakore. ⅩⅢ Omorwoti akabairaneria amang’ana amatindi akaanga ogosemia kw’abagaaka baria. ⅩⅣ Agakwana nabarabwo buna abamura baria bamosemetie, agateeba, “Tata nigo akorete rikorogoto riaino rikaba ririto, korende inche nimbamentere ribe ririto goetania. Tata nigo arenge kobang’wania ebiranya, korende inche nigo ndabang’wanie ne chinkini chichomire chibonyi chinyoge chinga amagwa.” ⅩⅤ Ase ayio omorwoti taigwerete amang’ana ’abaanto. Ayio nigo akorekanete na Nyasae, erinde Omonene anyare goikerania ring’ana riria akwanete ase Yeroboamu, mosinto o Nebati, goetera ase Ahiya, omobani oria korwa Silo.
ⅩⅥ 10:16: 2 Sam 20:1Ekero Abaisraeli bonsi baroche ng’a omorwoti nigo angete kobaigwera, bakamoiraneria, bagateeba, “Ngetari ki intwe tobwate ase Daudi? Titori nomwando na mosinto o Yese. Inwe Abaisraeli, genda kera omonto bwoye! Inwe abanto ba Daudi, erende inwe abanyene!”
Ase igo Abaisraeli bonsi bakarua aroro agwo, bakagenda ase chinka chiabo. ⅩⅦ Rehoboamu agatigwa okogambera Abaisraeli baria bamenyete ase emechie y’Abayuda boka.
ⅩⅧ Erio omorwoti Rehoboamu agatoma Hadoramu, oria otenenerete emeremo y’okobetererigwa, korende Abaisraeli bakamoaka amagena agakwa. Na Rehoboamu akaimoka bwango, akariina egari yaye, agatama gochia Yerusalemu. ⅩⅨ Ase ayio Abaisraeli babeire bakwanga koigwera enyomba ya Daudi, goika reero iga.
