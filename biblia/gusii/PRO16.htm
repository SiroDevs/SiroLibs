ⅩⅥ
Ⅰ Mwanyabaanto nigo agokora emeroberio ase enkoro yaye, korende aya oromeme rokoiraneria nigo akorwa ase Omonene.
Ⅱ Chinchera chionsi chi’omonto nigo chikororekana ase are omonyene ng’a neching’ikeranu, korende Omonene nigo akorenga omoika o Mwanyabaanto.
Ⅲ Motweke Omonene emeremo yao, na emeroberio yao nesesenigwe.
Ⅳ Omonene okoreire kera egento engencho yaye, nonya nabakori amabe babeo ase engencho ye rituko ri’emechando.
Ⅴ Kera omonto ore omoenenu nigo are kogechia ase Omonene; omonto oyio goika aegwe egesusuro.
Ⅵ Ase amaabera, na obwegenwa, embooria yaakanirwe ase engencho y’ebibe, na ase okomoiroka Omonene, omonto nigo agweatanana korwa ase amabe.
Ⅶ Ekero chiinchera chi’omonto chiamogokirie Omonene Nyasae nigo agokora nonya nababisa b’omonto oyio babwatana nere.
Ⅷ Mbuya koba n’ebinto ebike ase oboronge kobua koba ne chinibo chinyinge chikonyorwa ase oborianania.
Ⅸ Ase enkoro yaye ime omonto nigo akoroberia chiinchera chiaye, korende Omonene nere okoraa amabarato aye.
Ⅹ Chinkwana chinchenu ’nchigwenerete koba ase ebikoba bi’omorwoti, na omonwa oye tomocha ekero akonacha ebiina.
Ⅺ Ekerengero*16:11 Ekerengero: Ekerengero engencho yaye n’egepimo korwa aase erieta ogopima gose okorenga obonge bwaye n’ebipimo ki’oborito gi’ekeene nigo gekorwa ase Omonene, na ebirengero bionsi bire ase chisaro nigo bire ogokora kwaye.
Ⅻ Ekero abarwoti bagokora amabe nigo bakorenta okogechia, ekiagera goetera ase oboronge ekerogo ki’obogambi nigo kegokong’igwa.
ⅩⅢ Amang’ana amaronge agokwanwa nigo akogokia omorwoti, nere nigo abanchete abwo bagokwana oboronge.
ⅩⅣ Endamwamu y’omorwoti nigo enga buna abatomwa bakorenta amakweri, korende omonto omong’aini nigo akoyekanya.
ⅩⅤ Omosasoko ore ase obosio bw’omorwoti nigo ore obogima, na ogwancherwa kwaye nigo konga buna amare agotwa embura y’okwamia endagera.
ⅩⅥ Konyora obong’aini, ayio namaya kobua konyora etaabu, na okoba nobomanyi nigo kogwenerete kobua koba nefeta.
ⅩⅦ Ogweatanana korwa ase amabe, eyio nero enchera enene y’abanyene oboronge; na omonto orendete enchera yaye, nigo arendete obogima bwaye.
ⅩⅧ Oboenenu nigo bogotang’anera obosiru, na omoika bw’obomwenu nigo ogotang’anera okogwa.
ⅩⅨ Nigo ere buya koba n’omoyo bw’oboitongo na komenya amo nabataka kobua koria ebisakorwa amo nabaenenu.
ⅩⅩ Omonto okoigwera Ring’ana nanyore amaya, na oyo okomosemeri’Omonene nasesenirie.
ⅩⅪ Omonto ore omong’aini ase enkoro yaye narokwe omomanyi, ne chinkwana chingiya nigo chikomenta chiseemi.
ⅩⅫ Obong’aini nigo bore ensoko y’obogima ase omong’aini, korende oboriri nigo bore egesusuro ase abariri.
ⅩⅩⅢ Enkoro y’omonto omong’aini nigo ekworokereria omonwa oye oboseemia, naende yamenta ogotegererwa kw’ebikoba biaye.
ⅩⅩⅣ Amang’ana y’okogokia nigo anga obooke bokorwa ase richoe, nigo are amansu ase omoyo naende nigo akogwenia omobere.
ⅩⅩⅤ 16:25: Emeb 14:12Enchera nere ekororekana ase omonto ng’a nengiya, korende omoerio bw’enchera eyio namakweri.
ⅩⅩⅥ Okogania kw’omonto nigo gokomobetereria gokora emeremo, ekiagera naganetie goisanekia enchara yaye omonyene.
ⅩⅩⅦ Omonto otaisaini nigo akona‐gotukoria amabe, ne chinkwana chiaye nigo chinga omorero okorura.
ⅩⅩⅧ Omonto omonyaborimo nigo akona‐gotaara‐taaria amang’ana akorenta eriomana, na omogenki nigo akorenta obobisa ase abanto banchaine.
ⅩⅩⅨ Omonto omotindi nigo akoebereria omonto onde omoraa gochia ase enchera embe.
ⅩⅩⅩ Omonto ogosumera ebioge bi’amaiso aye nigo akoroberia amabe, na oyo ogotakuna omonwa oye nigo agoikerania amabe.
ⅩⅩⅪ Chimbuche chire ase omotwe neguutwa y’obonene, eyio nigo ekonyorwa n’abanyaboronge.
ⅩⅩⅫ Omonto otari omwango ase okogechigwa nigo abuete omorwani egeata, na oyomanyete kogambera enkoro yaye nigo abuete oyo okobua omochie na koyoira.
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Obomera nigo bokorutwa ase esaro ime, korende Omonene nere ogochora oyo bokogwera.
