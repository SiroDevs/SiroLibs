Abaisraeli tibabete abegenwa
Ⅰ Omonene nigo agoteeba iga: “Omonto karatigane nomokuungu oye, na omokungu oria airwe nomonto omoao, inee, omosacha oria omotang’ani nairanere omokungu oria naende? Ense eria tekoba entwambu ase enchera eyio? Inwe mwakorire obotomani nabanchi abange. Na bono moganetie koirana ase ’nde? Ⅱ Rigereria gochia ebitunwa igoro, morore! Ng’aase ki atigarete motakorereti obonyaka bwaino? Mwabeire mogoikaransa ase chinchera mbarabare motengerete buna Omoarabu oganyete abanto ase erooro, moganyete abanchi baino. Mwakorire ense yabeire entwambu ase engencho y’obonyaka bwaino na obokayayu bwaino. Ⅲ Ase engencho eyio embura yatangirwe tegotwa, naboigo nembura y’okwamia endagera. Korende nonya naboigo, amasio aino nigo anga buna ay’abakungu abatayayi, timorochi obosooku.
Ⅳ “Na bono iga mwachakire kondangeria mogoteeba: Aye tata, aye naye gwatwanchete korwa obwana bwaito. Ⅴ Inee! Omonene nigo aratobee goika kare na kare, na endamwamu yaye nigo eragenderere kobao goika omoerio? Ee, inwe nabo mogoteeba, naende nigo mogendererete gokora amabe onsi buna moranyare.”
Abaisraeli na Abayuda goika baonchoke
Ⅵ 3:6: 2 Abar 22:1—23:30; 2 Amg 34:1—35:27Ekero omorwoti Yosia arenge kogamba, Omonene agakwana nainche, aganteebia, “Kwarorire amang’ana aya Abaisraeli batari abegenwa bakorire? Nigo batiirire gochia ase kera ekegoro egetambe, na bakagenda inse ya kera omote omobese, bagakorera obonyaka aroro. Ⅶ Inche nigo narengereretie ng’a ekero bakoorire gokora amang’ana ayio onsi, nigo barang’iranere, korende tibang’iranereti; na Abayuda, abwo batarenge abegenwa, abare abasubati bamwabo Abaisraeli, mbaroche amang’ana ayio. Ⅷ Inche nigo naseretie Abaisraeli, abwo bairanete magega, nkabaa riruube ri’ogotigana, ekiagera tibarenge abegenwa ase ’nde. Nonya naboigo Abayuda abanyaborimo, abwo bare abasubati bamwabo Israeli, tibaroche obosooku, korende barabwo boigo bakagenda gokora obonyaka. Ⅸ Tibaabarete obonyaka gose ngento; ase ayio bagakora ense eye ekaba entwambu ase ogotaba abegenwa ase ’nde, bakaba bagosasiima amagena na emete. Ⅹ Amo nayio onsi Abayuda, abwo bare abasubati bamwabo Abaisraeli, batarenge abegenwa, tibang’iranerete ne chinkoro chiabo chionsi, korende ase okong’ainereria gwoka.” Nab’Omonene atebiire.
Ⅺ Eri’Omonene aganteebia: “Abaisraeli batarenge abegenwa babeire abaronge kobua Abayuda baria bare abang’ainereria. Ⅻ Genda orarie amang’ana aya ase Abaisraeli, obateebie ng’a inche Omonene nigo ngoteeba iga: Inwe Abaisraeli motari abegenwa, ing’iranere; tinkogenderera koba nendamwamu ase more ekiagera ninde namaabera. ⅩⅢ Inwe ancha rioka ng’a nabamochi more, naende ng’a mwasaririe ase ’nde inche Omonene, Nyasae oino; mwabeire mokogenda gochia aaria na aaria inse y’emete yonsi emebese mogosasiima chinyasae ching’ao, timwaigwerete eriogi riane.” Omonene oteebire ayio.
ⅩⅣ Naende Omonene oteebire iga: Inwe abana baane motari abegenwa, ing’iranere, ekiagera ninche Omonene oino. Nindusie oyomo oino korwa ase kera omochie, na babere korwa ase kera eamate, mbarente ase egetunwa gia Sayoni. ⅩⅤ Nimbae inwe abarisia abaya, abarangokie ase enkoro yane, abarabaragerie bare nobong’ani na okomanya. ⅩⅥ Omonene nigo agoteeba ng’a ase amatuko ayio, ekero morabuche na komentekana ase ense eyio, abanto tibagokwana nonya ng’ake igoro y’Esanduki y’Okobwatana kw’Omonene. Eyio tekoinyorwa naende, gose korengererigwa; gose eyende tekoroisigwa. ⅩⅦ Ase engaki eyio Yerusalemu norokwe Oboikaranso bw’Omonene. Ebisaku bionsi nabisangererekane agwo Yerusalemu, ase obosio bw’Omonene, na tibikogania gotunyana naende oboenenu bwe chinkoro chi’obobe. ⅩⅧ Ase amatuko ayio, Abayuda na Abaisraeli nababe amo nabarabwo, bachiche korwa ase ense ya sugusu goika ase ense eria naete chisokoro chiaino ekaba omwando obo.
Abanto ba Nyasae bagosasiima emegwekano
ⅩⅨ “Inche Omonene nigo narengereretie
buna nare konyara kobabeeka inwe ase egati y’abana baane,
erinde mbae ense ere engiya mono,
ense y’omwando ere engiya kobua ey’ebisaku binde bionsi.
Nigo narengereretie ng’a inwe namondoke Tata,
na timware koonchoka montige.
ⅩⅩ Korende koreng’ana buna omokungu otari omwegenwa agotiga omosacha oye,
naboigo nainwe timori abegenwa ase ’nde, mogantiga.
Inche Omonene nakwanire.”
ⅩⅪ Eriogi riaigurwe igoro ase ebitunwa bire bosa,
eriogi ri’Abaisraeli ri’okorera na ogosorora,
ekiagera barubanirie chinchera chiabo,
banyebire inche Omonene, Nyasae obo.
ⅩⅫ Inwe abanto motari abegenwa, ing’iranere,
nainche ningwenie ogotegena kwaino.
Inwe nigo mogoteeba: Intwe twakoiraneire, ekiagera naye Omonene, Nyasae oito. ⅩⅩⅢ Amaene nigo ere bosa koba nogosemeria ase ebigoro na ase chigosori chimbe chire aroro. Omonene, Nyasae oito, nere ore ogotooreka kw’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅩⅣ Korwa amatuko a kare ay’obwana bwaito, enyasae eria y’obosooku Baal yasiririe emeremo eye chisokoro chiaito chiakorete; yasiririe chitugo chiabo, na abana babo, abamura na abaiseke. ⅩⅩⅤ Tiga toraare inse tobe nobosooku, na echayo ebe ase tore, ekiagera twasaririe ase Omonene, Nyasae oito, korwa obwana bwaito goika reero iga, intwe amo ne chisokoro chiaito. Titwamoigwerete Omonene, Nyasae Nyasae oito.
