Obobani bore mamincha y’omorwoti o Turo
Ⅰ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: Ⅱ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, teebia omorwoti o Turo ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Enkoro yao yeimokereirie igoro, aye nigo ogoteeba: Inche nigo inde Nyasae, nigo ing’ikaransete ase ekerogo kia Nyasae ase egati y’enyancha. Korende aye nigo ore Mwanyabaanto rioka, tori Nyasae, nonya kwarengereirie ase enkoro yao ng’a nigo ore Nyasae. Ⅲ Ee, nigo ore omong’aini kobua Danieli, na obobisi tibori bobisekanete korwa ase ore. Ⅳ Obong’aini bwao, na okomanya kwao, biakonyoreire obotenenku obonge; aye gwasangereirie chitaabu ne chifeta ase chikungo chiao. Ⅴ Goetera ase obong’aini bwao bw’oboonchoreria, obotenenku bwao bwamentekanire; aye bono kwaenenire ase enkoro yao ase engencho y’obotenenku bwao obonge.
Ⅵ “Ase ayio, aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Ekiagera aye kwarengereirie ng’a nigo ore omong’aini buna Nyasae, Ⅶ ninkorentere abanto b’egesaku ekegeni, egetindi kebuete ebisaku binde bionsi. Abwo mbakorwanie esegi, erinde basarie obwari obwo kwenyoreire ase obong’aini bwao, na bakore obonene bwao bokomeka‐meka bobe obotachenegeti. Ⅷ Mbagotugute gochia ase embeera ime, aye nokwe ogokwa buna okw’abwo baitetwe nemioro bakarutwa agwo ase enyancha ime. Ⅸ Ekero orabe ase amaboko abanto abwo, bakogoita, Inee, nogenderere gokwana ase amasio abo ng’a aye Nyasae ore? Aye nigo ore Mwanyabaanto, orabe ase amaboko ay’abwo bakogoita, tori Nyasae. Ⅹ Aye nokwere ase amaboko y abanto b’egesaku ekegeni, buna abanto batarogeti bagokwa. Ninche Omonene nakwanire ayio.”
Okogwa kw’omorwoti o Turo
Ⅺ Naende ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: Ⅻ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, bokia amagombo igoro y’omorwoti o Turo, omoteebie ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Aye nigo kwarenge omoikeranu goetania ebitongwa bionsi bi’obonene; nigo gwaichire nobong’aini, naende kwarenge omosiani omoikeranu. ⅩⅢ Nigo kwamenyete ime ya Edeni, omogondo o Nyasae. Nigo gwachaberetwe namagena ao ao e rigori rinene: Saridio, na topasio, na yasipi, na kurusolito, na berulo, na oiniki, na safiro, na sumaridi, na alimasi. Boigo nigo gwachaberetwe ne chitaabu. Ebinto biao bikobugigwa nigo biaroisetigwe ase rituko riria gwatongetwe. ⅩⅣ Aye nigo kwarenge egetongwa kebwate chimbaba oagetwe amaguta, ogatuba oboagare obonene. Inche nigo nakobegete ase egetunwa egechenu kia Nyasae, agwo ase kwarenge kona‐gotaara ase egati y’amagena are nomorero. ⅩⅤ Chingencho chiao nigo chiarenge ching’ikeranu, korwa rituko erio gwatongetwe goika ekero obobe bwanyorekanete ime yao. ⅩⅥ Ekero gwakorete oboonchoreria obonge, ogaichorwa nobobe; ase igo ogakora ebibe. Ase ayio nkagoseria buna egento getachenegeti, okarua ase egetunwa kia Nyasae, ee, aye egetongwa kebwate chimbaba ogatuba oboagare obonene, nagosiririe korwa ase amagena aria are nomorero. ⅩⅦ Enkoro yao nigo yeimokereretie igoro ase engencho y’obosiani bwao, aye ogasaria obong’aini bwao ase engencho y’obomeka‐meku bwao. Ase ayio ngakoiyeria inse, nkagotiga ase amaiso abarwoti bagosekerere. ⅩⅧ Ase engencho y’ebibe biao ebinge, na oboonchoreria bwao botari bw’oboronge, ogakora aase ao aachenegete akaba atachenegeti. Ngasokia omorero korwa ase egati yao, na omorero oyio okagosiria, ngakoonchora okaba ribu igoro ase ense, agwo ase amaiso y abanto bonsi bakorooche. ⅩⅨ Abanto bonsi abakomanyete nigo baragoichane. Aye gwaikire ase ogosirigwa gw’okoondokia, tokobao naende nonya ng’ake.”
Obobani bore mamincha ya Sidoni
ⅩⅩ 28:20: Yoel 3:4-8; Zak 9:1,2; Mat 11:21,22; Luk 10:13,14Naende ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha asende, riganteebia: ⅩⅪ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, rigereria gochia Sidoni, obaane mamincha yaye, ⅩⅫ otebe ng’a aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Aye Sidoni, rora, inche nigo inde mamincha yao, ning’orokie obonene bwane ase egati yao. Ekero ndakonachere ekiina na nkworokie obochenu bwane, erio abanto mbamanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene. ⅩⅩⅢ Nindete oborwaire bogoita ime yao, na amanyinga aiteke ase chinchera chiao. Norwanigwe esegi korwa ase chinsemo chionsi, na abanto baitwe bakwere ase egati yao. Erio abanto mbamanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.
Abaisraeli mbasesenigwe
ⅩⅩⅣ “Korende ase Abaisraeli, chimbambo gose amagwa tibikobao bibabete ekero bagendererete komenya ase egati y’ abanto abwo baabaetanaine baabachaayete. Erio mbamanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.
ⅩⅩⅤ “Aya naro Omonene agoteeba: Ninsangererie Abaisraeli, mbarusie korwa ase abanto b’ebisaku agwo ase basiareretwe gochia, na ning’orokie obochenu bwane ase bare ase amasio y abanto b’ebisaku; erio mbarente bamenye ase ense yabo eria naete Yakobo, omosomba one. ⅩⅩⅥ Mbamenye n’obotoereru ase ense eyio; baagache chinyomba, naende basimeke emesabibu ase emegondo. Barabwo mbamenye n’obotoereru, ekero ndanachere abanto bonsi baabaetanaine ekiina, abwo baabachaayete. Erio mbamanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene, Nyasae obo.”
