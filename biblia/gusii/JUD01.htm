RIRUUBE RIA
YUDA
Ogochaakera
Riruube korwa ase Yuda nigo riarigetwe gokurera igoro y’aborokia b’oborimo abwo baare goteeba buna barabwo n’abegeni. Ase riruube eri ereng’e erio ribwate amang’ana agoocherera 2 Petero, omoriki nigo akobeka abasomi baye chinguru bagenderere ase okwegena okwo ara rimo na botambe Nyasae aeire abanto baye
Okobagwa kw’egetabu eke
Ogochaakera 1-2
Igoro yaborokia b’oborimo, na okworokereria kwabo, na buna baragambigwe 3-16
Ogosorora gw’okobwata okwegena 17-23
Ogosesenigwa 24-25
Ⅰ
Ⅰ 1:1: Mat 13:55; Mar 6:3Korwa ase Yuda, omosomba o Yeso Kristo, oyomwabo Yakobo, nkobarikera nde inwe abamwarangerigwe, abamwanchire na Nyasae Tata, na morendire na Yeso Kristo.
Ⅱ Amaabera, na omorembe, na obwanchani, bimenteke ase more.
Aborokia b’oborimo
Ⅲ Abanchani, ekero narenge gokora omokia mono kobarikera inwe amang’ana ogotooreka gwaito, okwo tosangete nainwe, nkarora ng’a negwenerete mbarikere na kobasorora morwanerere okwegena okwo abachenu baetwe ara rimo. Ⅳ Naki mbare abanto besoirie bobisi, abwo barikeretwe kare okogambigwa oko, abanto batari ne chingencho chi’obonyasae. Barabwo nigo bakoonchorana obuya bwa Nyasae oito ase okobiserera ogokora kwabo kw’obonyaka, naende bagokana Omonene oito ore bweka, oyore Omonene oito Yeso Kristo. Korwa agwo kare amariiko nabanete okoragererigwa konya banyorire.
Ⅴ 1:5: a Okong’ 12:51; b Omob 14:29,30Nonya momanyete amang’ana aya onsi ninganetie kobainyoria buna Omonene atooretie abanto ba Israeli korwa ase ense ya Misiri, na magega y’ayio agasiria abwo batamwegenete. Ⅵ Na abamalaika baria batarendete okobua kwabo abanyene, korende bagatiga obomenyo bwabo, barendire nere ase ebisibo bia kare na kare inse y’omosunte omonene goika ekiina geche koba ase rituko riria rinene. Ⅶ 1:7: Omoch 19:1-24Naboigo nabaanto ba Sodomu na Gomora, na ab’emechie yaetanaine, bakorete obotomani bore engencho eyemo nobw’abwo, bakagenderera gotunyana okogania kw’omobere gotari kwa boronge. Bono nigo babekire koba ekiorokererio ki’ogokuurerwa ase okoegwa egesusuro ki’omorero o kare na kare.
Ⅷ Naboigo nabwo abanyandoto bakobeeka emebere yabo riko bagochaaya okobua, na korama abwo b’obonene. Ⅸ 1:9: a Dan 10:13,21; 12:1; Okom 12:7; b Okoir 34:6; c Zak 3:2Korende Mikaeli, omomalaika omonene, ekero riomanete na Saitani na koamererania nere igoro y’omobere o Musa, tarenge nomoyo bw’okomosoera ase amang’ana amabe, korende akamoteebia, “Omonene nagotogonyere.” Ⅹ Korende abanto aba nigo bakorama ase amang’ana onsi batamanyeti, na ase amang’ana bamanyete botambe koreng’ana buna ching’iti chitari nobong’aini chimanyete; ase aro besaria abanyene. Ⅺ 1:11: a Omoch 4:3-8; b Omob 22:1-35; c Omob 16:1-35Obobe nobwabo, ekiagera bagendererete ase enchera ya Kaini, na gwetuguta ase ogosaria kwa Baalamu ase engencho y’okonyora eng’eria, na barabwo basirire ase ogotaigwera gwa Kora. Ⅻ Abanto aba nebitare bikogocha ase emeyega yaino y’obwanchani, kobagokora emeyega amo nainwe batari nokoiroka, bakwerisia barabwo abanyene. Abanto aba namare atari nembura akoirwa nembeo, na emete yaarorokire amato etari namatunda, yakuure ara kabere, na gosimorwa kegima. ⅩⅢ Naende barabwo nemerindo y’enyancha etagotangeka, ekoorokia obosooku bwaye enyene buna riuro, ne ching’enang’eni chigoetanana na gosira, na bagacheire omosunte ekerima goika kare na kare.
ⅩⅣ 1:14: Omoch 5:18,21-24Na Enoku, omonto o gatano na kabere korwa ase Adamu, nakwanete amang’ana obobani igoro y’abwo, ogoteeba, “Rigereria, Omonene nache nabachenu baye chilifu ne chilifu, ⅩⅤ erinde agambie abanto bonsi ekiina na aenekie abwo bonsi batare ne chingencho chi’obonyasae ase engencho y’emeremo yabo yonsi etare ne chingencho chi’obonyasae, eye baare gokora ase ogotaba ne chingencho chi’obonyasae, na ase engencho y’amang’ana amakong’u abwo abanyene ebibe, abatare ne chingencho chi’obonyasae, baare gokwana igoro yaye.” ⅩⅥ Abanto aba nigo bare abemurungania na abereri, kobagotunyana okogania kwabo okobe, na emenwa yabo ekwana amang’ana ’oboenenu amanene mono, bagotogia abanto ase engencho y’okwenyorera.
Ogokuurera na ogotebereria
ⅩⅦ Korende inwe abanchani, moinyore amang’ana aria akwanetwe nabatomwa b’Omonene oito Yeso Kristo. ⅩⅧ 1:18: 2 Petr 3:3Naki igo babateebetie, “Ase engaki y’omoerio nababeo abanto abasekereria, abagotunyana okogania kwabo okobe kw’ogotaba ne chingencho chi’obonyasae.” ⅩⅨ Abanto abwo nabwo bakorenta ogwatananeka, bare abanto b’ense eye yoka, korende Omoika tibabwati. ⅩⅩ Korende inwe, abanchani, mweagache ase okwegena kwaino kore ogochenu mono, na gosaba ase Omoika Omochenu. ⅩⅪ Mwerende ase obwanchani bwa Nyasae, mobe moganyete amaabera Omonene oito Yeso Kristo goika monyore obogima bwa kare na kare. ⅩⅫ Mobarorere amaabera abwo bagotiting’ara. ⅩⅩⅢ Korende motoorie abande ase okobataboria korwa ase omorero. Abande mobarorere amaabera mobe nokoiroka, mogeche nonya neanga eria ebekire riko ri’okogania okobe kw’omobere.
Ogosaba kw’ogotogia
ⅩⅩⅣ Bono asare ere oyoranyare kobarenda inwe timotererigwa, na kobatenenia ase obonene bwaye, motari na komocha, ase omogooko omonene, ⅩⅩⅤ ere oyore Nyasae bweka, Omotooria oito goetera ase Yeso Kristo, Omonene oito, asare ere ogotogigwa, na obonene, n’echinguru, na okobua, korwa kare na kare, na bono, na goika kare na kare. Amina.
