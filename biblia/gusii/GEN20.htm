Aburaamu na Sara bakomenya Gerari
Ⅰ Erio Aburaamu akaimoka korwa agwo mamure, akagenda gochia ensemo y’ense ya Negebu, akaroisia obomenyo bwaye, egati ya Kadeshi na Suri agwo Gerari. Ⅱ 20:2: Omoch 12:13; 26:7Ekero are agwo, Aburaamu agakwana igoro ya Sara mokaye ogoteeba: “Oyo mosubati ominto.” Abimeleki, omorwoti o Gerari agatoma abanto kwoyia Sara. Ⅲ Korende Nyasae akaorokera Abimeleki botuko ase okorota, akamoteebia: “Aye goika okwe, ekiagera omokungu oyo kwarentire nomokuungu bw’omonto onde.”
Ⅳ Abimeleki konya ataraba nere, akabora: “Aye Omonene, Inee, nigo oraite nonya nabanyaboronge? Ⅴ Inee! Omonto oyio tantebeetie ere omonyene ng’a oyo mosubati omwabo? Na omokungu nere aganteebia ng’a oyo momura omwabo? Ase enkoro etari na komocha konde, na ase amaboko amarabu nakorire ayio.”
Ⅵ Erio Nyasae akamoteebia ase okoroota: “Inche namanyire ng’a ase enkoro etari na komocha gwakorire aya, na inche nkagotanga tigwankorera ebibe. Ase ayio, tinakoa ribaga omokune. Ⅶ Bono moiranerie omonto oria mokaye, ekiagera ere n’omobani, nagosabere onyare koba moyo. Korende otamoiranereti, manya amaene, goika okwe, aye amo na abanto bao bonsi ore nabarabwo.”
Ⅷ Abimeleki akaimoka mambia chuni akarangeria abasomba baye bonsi, akabateebia amang’ana aya onsi, na abanto abwo bakaondoka mono. Ⅸ Erio Abimeleki akarangeria Aburaamu, akamobooria: “Inki eke gwatokoreire iga? Inki nakomocheire goika kwarentire igoro yane na igoro y’oborwoti bwane ebibe ebinene buna ebi? Gwankoreire amang’ana atagwenereti gokorwa.” Ⅹ Naende Abimeleki akabooria Aburaamu: “Inki kwareengereretie goika ogakora ring’ana eri?”
Ⅺ Aburaamu akamoiraneria: “Nabo nakorete igo, ekiagera narooche ng’a abamoirogete Nyasae tibari aase aa, na barabwo nigo barang’ite ase engencho ya mokane. Ⅻ Ere amaene nomoiseke ominto, tata noyomo, korende ng’ina nomoao, na inche nkamonyuoma. ⅩⅢ Ekero Nyasae andusetie korwa ase omochie o tata kogenda gochia aaria na aaria ngateebia mokane: Obwo nabwo obwanchani orankorere: kera aase toragende oteebe igoro yane ng’a inche momura omino.”
ⅩⅣ Erio Abimeleki akaimokia ching’ondi ne chiombe, amo nabasomba, abamura na abaiseke, akaa Aburaamu, akamoiraneria na Sara mokaye. ⅩⅤ Abimeleki akamoteebia: “Rora, ense yane nigo ere ase obosio bwao; menya aande onsi ase oranche komenya.” ⅩⅥ Ere agateebia Sara: “Rora naeire momura omino chisekeli chi’echifeta elifu eyemo, chibe entorobi yao ase amaiso ’abaanto bonsi abare amo naye, erio omanyekane ng’a nomonto omooronge ore.”
ⅩⅦ Erio Aburaamu agasaba Nyasae; na Nyasae akagwenia Abimeleki amo na mokaye, na abasomba baye abakungu, na barabwo bakaibora abana. ⅩⅧ Naki Omonene konya okorire abakungu b’enka y’Abimeleki koba abagomba, erio tibaibora, ase engencho ya Sara moka Aburaamu.
