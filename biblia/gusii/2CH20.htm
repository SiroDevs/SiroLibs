ⅩⅩ
Esegi igoro y’Abaedomu
Ⅰ Ekaba magega y’ayio, Abamoabu na Abaamoni, na abanto bande bamenyete amo n’Abaamoni bagacha korwania Yehosafati esegi. Ⅱ Abanto bande bagacha bagateebia Yehosafati “Omoganda omonene mono bw’abaanto korwa Edomu, ng’umbu y’enyancha*20:2 Enyancha: nero Enyancha y’omonyo, erieta rinde Enyancha Ekwete enyancha yomonyoo ngocha ore gokorwania esegi; rora nao bare agwo Hasasoni‐Tamari” (noro Eni‐Gedi). Ⅲ Erio Yehosafati akaba nobwoba, akarigia Omonene; akararia ng’a Abayuda bonsi beng’ate. Ⅳ Abayuda bagasangererekana amo, bakarigia ogokonywa korwa ase Omonene. Ee, bagacha korwa ase emechie yonsi y’Abayuda korigia Omonene. Ⅴ Yehosafati agatenena ase egati y’omosangererekano oria bw’Abayuda na abanto ba Yerusalemu agwo ase eraaro enyia y’enyomba y’Omonene, Ⅵ agasaba, “Aye Omonene, Nyasae bwe chisokoro chiaito, Inee, aye tori Nyasae ore igoro? Tari aye okogamba igoro y’amarwoti onsi ay’ebisaku? Ase okoboko kwao okobua ne chinguru mbireo, goika onde tari oranyare gokobua. Ⅶ 20:7: Isaya 41:8; Yakb 2:23Aye Nyasae oito, tigwaseretie abanto ababiare b’ense eye ase amasio ’abaanto bao Abaisraeli, okayea ororeria rwa Aburaamu, omosani oo, ense eye ekaba eyabo kogenderera botambe? Ⅷ Bono barabwo nao bamenyete, naende bakoagacheire Obomenyo Obochenu ase engencho y’erieta riao, bagoteeba: Ⅸ Obobe koborachiche igoro ase tore, gose esegi, gose egesusuro, gose oborwaire bogoita, gose enchara, natotenene bosio bw’enyomba eye ase obosio bwao, ekiagera erieta riao nao rire ase enyomba eye. Igo ntonyare korera asore ase engencho y’emechando yaito, aye noigwe ototoorie.
Ⅹ 20:10: Okoir 2:4-19“Rora bono, Abaamoni na Abamoabu, na abanto b’ebigoro bia Edomu ngocha bare gotorwania. Ekero chisokoro chiaito Abaisraeli barwete ase ense ya Misiri, tikwabaete ribaga baete ase chingongo chiabo; ase ayio bakaba bakobaetanana. Ⅺ Bono kwarorire ng’a barabwo bachire gotosiria na gotoseria korwa ase ense eye yao gwatoeire ebe omwando oito. Ⅻ Aye Nyasae oito, Inee, aye tokobanachera ekiina? Intwe titobwati chinguru chi’okorwana nomoganda oyo omonene, ogocha gotorwania. Titomanyeti eki togokora, korende nigo tokorigereretie aye ase okonyora obosemia.”
ⅩⅢ Ase engaki eyio abasacha bonsi b’Abayuda bagatenena ase obosio bw’Omonene: barabwo amo nabana babo ebing’werere, na abakungu babo, na abana babo bande. ⅩⅣ Omoika bw’Omonene ogaacha igoro ase Yahasieli, mosinto o Zakaria o Benaya, o Yeieli, o Matania, Omolawi bw’ororeria rw’Asafu, ekero ateneine gati‐gati y’omosangererekano. ⅩⅤ 20:15: Okoir 20:1-4Ere agakwana, “Igwa inwe Abayuda mwensi, na abanto bamenyete Yerusalemu, na aye omorwoti Yehosafati. Aya nar’Omonene akobateebia: Timoiroka gose gokwa emioyo ase engencho y’omoganda oyo omonene; esegi teri eyaino, korende neya Nyasae. ⅩⅥ Ankio mogende mobarwanie; mobarore bagotiira ase egetiiro gia Sisi; namobanyore agwo ase ensinyo ye rikura, moocha y’erooro ya Yerueli. ⅩⅦ 20:17: Okong’ 14:13,14Inwe Abayuda na abanto ba Yerusalemu, timokorwana esegi eye; mobeere aase aino, motenene, mokire‐kiri na morore obobui bw’Omonene ase engencho yaino. Timoiroka, gose timokwa emioyo. Ankio mosoke mobachiere, n’Omonene nabe amo nainwe.”
ⅩⅧ Erio Yehosafati na Abayuda bonsi, amo nabaanto bamenyete Yerusalemu, bagatung’ama bakaumama ase obosio bw’Omonene, bakamosasiima. ⅩⅨ Abalawi baria barenge Abakohati na Abakora bagatenena gotogia Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli, ase eriogi rinene mono.
ⅩⅩ Bakaimoka mambia chuni†20:20 mambia chuni: engencho yaye mambia kobogokiamoka, bakagenda gochia ase erooro ya Tekoa; na ekero basogete isiko, Yehosafati agatenena agakwana, “Inwe Abayuda na abanto bamenyete Yerusalemu inyigwe! Mwegene Omonene, Nyasae oino, monyore chinguru; egene nababani baye, na inwe inamobue.” ⅩⅪ Ekero akoorete gosemania nabaanto, akabeeka abanto baare gochia goterera Omonene na komotogia beboetie chianga chinchenu. Barabwo bagatang’ana abasikari bagotera, “Moiranerie Omonene ng’a mbuya mono, ekiagera obwanchani bwaye bwa botambe nigo bogendererete kare na kare.”
ⅩⅫ Ekero bachaagete goteera na gotogia, Omonene agatoma abanto bakaumokera Abaamoni, na Abamoabu, na abanto b’ebigoro bia Edomu abwo bachete korwania Abayuda bagasirigwa kegima. ⅩⅩⅢ Abaamoni na Abamoabu bakaumokera korwania abanto b’ebigoro bia Edomu, bakabasiria kegima, na ekero basiretie abanto abwo, Abaamoni na Abamoabu bagaitana, bagakoorana. ⅩⅩⅣ Ekero Abayuda bachete ase omoorobiri bw’oborendi agwo ase erooro na bakarigereria, bakarora ebitondo birambereretie inse; onde tatooregete nonya noyomo.
ⅩⅩⅤ Na ekero Yehosafati na abanto baye bagendete koira ebisakorwa korwa ase emeganda eria bakanyora chinibo ne chitugo chinyinge,‡20:25 Chitugo chinyinge: amariko ande nigo agoteeba “emebere ekwete, na ayande chianga” na ebinto bi’erigori rinene; bakabibogoria na kobiira, goika bagasinywa koira goetania. Ase amatuko atato bakagenderera kobiira, ekiagera biarenge ebinge mono. ⅩⅩⅥ Ase rituko ria kane bagasangererekana ase Ebate ya Beraka,§20:26 Beraka: engencho yaye ogotogigwa ekiagera agwo nao batogeretie Omonene. Aase agwo abeire akorokwa Ebate ya Beraka goika reero iga. ⅩⅩⅦ Erio bakairana, kera oyomo bw’Abayuda na obw’abaanto ba Yerusalemu, na Yehosafati akabatang’ana. Bakairana Yerusalemu bare nomochengo, ekiagera Omonene abaete obobui igoro ase ababisa babo. ⅩⅩⅧ Bagacha Yerusalemu bakobugia amakano abo, na amakano abo ay’ebirigisa, na ebitureeri, bakagenda ase enyomba y’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅨ Nyasae akoboyia amarwoti onsi ay’ense, ekero abanto b’aroro baigwete ng’a Omonene narwanetie ababisa b’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅩⅩ Ense eria ase Yehosafati arenge kogambera ekaba n’obotoereru, ekiagera Nyasae oye amoete omorembe ase chinsemo chionsi.
Omoerio bw’okogamba kwa Yehosafati
(1 Abar 22:41-50)
ⅩⅩⅪ Ase ayio Yehosafati akaba okogambera Abayuda. Nigo arenge nemiaka emerongo etato, na etano, ekero achagete kogamba, na akagamba emiaka emerongo ebere na etano, agwo Yerusalemu. Ng’ina nigo arenge korokwa Asuba, mosubati o Silihi. ⅩⅩⅫ Yehosafati akabwatia chinchera chia Asa ise, taonchogete na gochitiga. Agakora ayare amaronge ase amaiso ’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Korende nonya naboigo, aase aimokererete igoro agosasimerwa tatagoretwe. Abanto konya tibaraonchokera Nyasae bwe chisokoro chiabo.
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Bono amang’ana ande onsi igoro ya Yehosafati, korwa omochakano goika omoerio, nigo ariikire ase amariko a Yehu, mosinto o Hanani, ayio are ime y’egetabu ki’ogokora kw’Abarwoti b’Abaisraeli.
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Magega y’ayio Yehosafati, omorwoti bw’Abayuda, akabwatana n’Ahasia, omorwoti bw’Abaisraeli, nonya omorwoti oria okorete amang’ana amabe mono. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Akabwatana nere ase okoagacha chimeeri chikogenda Tarisisi. Bagachiagachera agwo Esioni‐Geberi. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Erio Eliezeri, mosinto o Dodawahu korwa Maresa, akabana amabe igoro ya Yehosafati, akamoteebia, “Ekiagera kwabwatanire n’Ahasia, Omonene nasarie ebinto kwaroisirie.” Ne chimeeri chiria chikabochoka tichianyara kogenda Tarisisi.
