Amagena ’ekeinyorio akobekwa amo
Ⅰ Ekero abanto bonsi ba Israeli konya bambokire Yorodani, Omonene agateebia Yoshua, Ⅱ “Chora abanto ikomi na babere, oyomo korwa ase kera egesaku, Ⅲ obachike baimokie amagena ikomi na abere, korwa agwo gati‐gati ya Yorodani aase abakuani bateneine namagoro abo, bayaire na bayabeeke amo ase moraraare obotuko obo.”
Ⅳ Erio Yoshua akarangeria abanto baria ikomi na babere konya achorire korwa ase Abaisraeli, oyomo korwa ase kera egesaku, Ⅴ akabateebia, “Eta mogende bosio bw’Esanduki y’Omonene, Nyasae oino, goika gati‐gati ya Yorodani. Kera oyomo oino aimokie rigena erimo, aribeke ireko riaye, erimo ase kera egesaku ki’Abaisraeli. Ⅵ Amagena aya abe ekeinyorio ase more. Ekero abana baino barache kobabooria: Ninki engencho y’amagena aya? Ⅶ Mobateebie: Engencho yaye neye: Ekero Esanduki y’Okobwatana kw’Omonene yambogetigwe Yorodani, amaache nigo anachegete bosio bw’Esanduki eria. Ase ayio amagena aya nabe ekeinyorio ase Abaisraeli botambe.”
Ⅷ Abaisraeli bagakora buna Yoshua abachigete. Bakaimokia amagena ikomi na abere korwa Yorodani, erimo ase kera egesaku ki’Abaisraeli, buna Omonene konya atebereirie Yoshua, bakayaira ase baare gochia gotoora, bakayabeeka inse. Ⅸ Yoshua akabeeka amo amagena ande ikomi na abere gati‐gati ya Yorodani, aase abakuani batenenete, abwo babogoretie Esanduki eria y’Okobwatana. Amagena aria nao are goika reero iga. Ⅹ Abakuani abwo babogoretie Esanduki eria bakaba bateneine gati‐gati ya Yorodani, goika amang’ana onsi agaikeranigwa, ay’Omonene konya achiikire Yoshua goteebia abanto. Aya naro Yoshua achigetwe na Musa.
Abanto bakaayerera kwamboka. Ⅺ Ekero abanto bonsi konya bambokire, Abakuani bakamboka nesanduki y’Omonene bakagenda bosio bw’abaanto. Ⅻ Abanto ba Rubeni, na Abagadi, na ekeng’ese ki’egesaku kia Manase bagatang’ana Abaisraeli, babogoretie ebirwanero bi’esegi, buna Musa abateebeetie, ⅩⅢ Abanto koreng’ana chilifu emerongo ene babogoretie ebirwanero, baare ang’e korwana esegi, bagaetera ebirubo ebiomo ang’e na Yeriko, ase obosio bw’Omonene. ⅩⅣ Rituko riri’Omonene akaimokereria Yoshua igoro, ase amasio ’abaisraeli bonsi. Barabwo bakaba basigete Yoshua ase obogima bwaye bwonsi, buna basigete Musa.
ⅩⅤ Eri’Omonene agateebia Yoshua, ⅩⅥ “Bachike abakuani abwo babogoretie Esanduki eria ere ne chibao chiriikire oborori, ng’a batiire korwa Yorodani.” ⅩⅦ Erio Yoshua agachiika abakuani, akabateebia, “Karue agwo ase Yorodani.” ⅩⅧ Ekero abakuani abwo babogoretie Esanduki eria batiirete korwa gati gati ya Yorodani bagatacha ense enyomo. Erio bwango amaache a Yorodani akairanerana, agaetania chingegu chiaye chionsi akaba buna arenge agwo ritang’ani.
ⅩⅨ Abanto abwo bakamboka Yorodani rituko ria ikomi ri’omotienyi bw’eritang’ani, bagatoora Giligali, ase orobebe rwa moocha ya Yeriko. ⅩⅩ Agwo Giligali Yoshua akabeeka amo amagena aria ikomi na abere arusetigwe Yorodani. ⅩⅪ Agateebia Abaisraeli, “Ase engaki egochi‐gocha abana be chinderia chiaino kobaraborie abaise engencho y’amagena aya, ⅩⅫ mobateebie ng’a Abaisraeli nigo baambokerete Yorodani ase ense enkamoku. ⅩⅩⅢ Omonene, Nyasae oino, nigo akamogetie amaache a Yorodani bosio bwaino goika mokaamboka, naboigo buna akamogetie Enyancha Embariri bosio bwaito goika tokaamboka. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase ayio, abanto bonsi b’ense engima mbamanye ng’a okoboko kw’Omonene nkore ne chinguru, erinde bamoiroke Omonene, Nyasae oino, botambe.”
