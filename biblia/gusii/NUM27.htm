Abaiseke Ba Selofehadi
Ⅰ Ase engaki eyio abaiseke ba Selofehadi, mosinto o Heferi o Gileadi, o Makiri, o Manase bagaacha. Aba nigo barwete ase chiamate chia Manase, mosinto o Yusufu, na amarieta abo naro aya: Mala, na Nuhu, na Hogila, na Milka, na Tirisa. Ⅱ Barabwo bagaacha ase obosio bwa Musa, na obwa Eleazari Omokuani, na ase amasio y’abatang’ani, na ase omoganda bwonsi, bagatenena agwo ase egesoero ki’Eema y’Omosangererekano, bagateeba, Ⅲ “Tata nigo akwerete agwo ase erooro. Ere tarenge ase egati y’ekeombe keria giasangererekanete amo gekaba mamincha y’Omonene, ase abwo barenge ase ensemo ya Kora, korende ere nigo akwete ase engencho y’ebibe biaye omonyene. Ekero akwete tatigete abana abamura Ⅳ Nase ki erieta ria tata rigosirigwa korwa ase eamate yaye ekiagera tabwate abana abamura? Toe naintwe omwando ase egati y’abamura bamwabo tata.”
Ⅴ Musa akaira amang’ana abo ase obosio bw’Omonene, Ⅵ n’Omonene akamoteebia, Ⅶ 27:7: Omob 36:2“Abaiseke ba Selofehadi nekeene bakoboria. Bae omwando obo ase egati ya bamura bamwabo ise. Tiga omwando o ise obe oyobo. Ⅷ Naende oteebie Abaisraeli ng’a omonto karakwe, onye ere tabwati omwana omomura, omwando bw’omonto oyio omwana oye omoiseke ayoire. Ⅸ Onye omonto oyio tabwati nonya nomwana omoiseke, omwando oye abamura bamwabo bayoire. Ⅹ Onye omonto oyio tabwati abamura bamwabo, omwando oye oirwe nabamura bamwabo ise. Ⅺ Na onye omonto oyio tabwati abamura bamwabo ise, omwando oye oirwe nomoamate oye ore ang’e. Okwo nigo koraabe ogochika kw’oboronge ase Abaisraeli, buna inche Omonene nagochiikire.”
Yoshua Ogochorwa Koba Ribaga Ria Musa
(Okoir 31:1-8)
Ⅻ 27:12: Okoir 3:23-27; 32:48-52Omonene agakwana na Musa, akamoteebia, “Tiira gochia igoro ase egetunwa eke ki’Abarimu, na origererie ense naeire Abaisraeli. ⅩⅢ Ekero kwayerorire, aye nigo orasangererekane amo n’abanto bao buna Aroni omino asangererekanete amo n’abanto baye, ⅩⅣ ekiagera timwaigwerete ring’ana riane agwo ase erooro ya Sini, rituko riria omoganda bwonsi bwemurunganetie, gose timwaorogetie ng’a inche nomochenu inde, ekero mware kobochorera abanto amaache.” (Aya namaache a Meriba ya Kadeshi ase erooro ya Sini).
ⅩⅤ Musa agakwana n’Omonene akabora, ⅩⅥ “Aye Omonene Nyasae, aye oyokorua obogima ase abanto bonsi, chora omonto oraraae omoganda oyo, ⅩⅦ 27:17: 1 Abar 22:17; Ezek 34:5; Mat 9:36; Mar 6:34omonto oranyare gotang’ana abanto kobagosoka na kobakoirana, erinde omoganda bw’ abanto ba Omonene tobaisa koba buna ching’ondi chitari nomorisia.”
ⅩⅧ 27:18: Okong’ 24:13Omonene agateebia Musa, “Ira Yoshua, mosinto o Nuni, omonto oyio omoika one ore ime yaye, erio omobekere amaboko. ⅩⅨ Omorente atenene ase obosio bw’Eleazari Omokuani, na ase amasio y’omoganda bwonsi bw’ abanto, erio ase amasio abo omorarie ng’a nere oirire ribaga ria’o. ⅩⅩ Omoe okobua buna okwao, erio nario omoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli oramoigwere. ⅩⅪ 27:21: Okong’ 28:30; 1 Sam 14:41; 28:6Yoshua abe ogotenena ase obosio bw’Eleazari Omokuani, na Eleazari abe okomonogeri oye ase Urimu, agwo ase obosio bwane amanye ogwancha kwane. Yoshua amo nomoganda bwonsi bw’Abaisraeli nigo barakore emeremo yabo yonsi buna Eleazari arabachiike.” ⅩⅫ Musa akaira Yoshua buna Omonene konya amochiikire, akamorenta ase obosio bw’Eleazari Omokuani nomoganda bwonsi. ⅩⅩⅢ 27:23: Okoir 31:23Naende akabeeka amaboko aye igoro ase omotwe o Yoshua, akamwatanana, akamobeka ase ribaga riaye, buna Omonene konya amochikiire goetera ase Musa.
