Anania na Sabira
Ⅰ Korende narenge omosacha oyomo ore korokwa Anania, oyio amo na mokaye Sabira baoonetie ensemo y’omogondo obo. Ⅱ Korende ere ase ogwancherana na mokaye akabisa ensemo ye chitaabu echio baonetie, erio akaa abatomwa ensemo eria yatigarete. Ⅲ Erio Petero akamobooria: “Anania, nase ki ogwancha Saitani gokobogoria okong’aina Omoika Omochenu na kobisa ensemo ye chitaabu kwanyora korwa ase omogondo? Ⅳ Nyuma otaraonia omogondo oyo noyoo orenge; Na ekero oonetigwe chitaabu nechiao chiarenge. Nase ki kwarengereretie gokora ring’ana eri? Totebeti Mwanyabaanto oborimo, korende Nyasae.” Ⅴ Ekero Anania achia koigwa amang’ana aya, agaaka inse, agakwa. Omoondoko omonene ogasoa ase abanto bonsi abaigwete aya. Ⅵ Abamura bagacha bagatuba omobere oye egetambaa, bakamobogoria gochia isiko, bakamotindeka.
Ⅶ Magega ye chinsa isato mokaye agasoa, ere tamanyete aya akoregete. Ⅷ Petero akamobooria: “Nteebia, eri nario rigori aye n’omosacha oo mwaonetie omogondo ase?” Akairaneria: “Ee, ase rigori oino.”
Ⅸ Petero akamobooria: “Naki abeete aye n’omosacha oo mogancherana goteema Omoika bw’Omonene? Abasacha abwo batindeka omosacha oo nao bare agwo gesieri, na barabwo nabakobogorie aye gochia isiko.” Ⅹ Erio kegima akagwa ase amagoro aye, agakwa. Abamura baria bagasoa, bakamonyora okuure. Erio bakamobogoria gochia isiko, bakamotindeka ase omosacha oye ensemo. Ⅺ Omoondoko omonene okabwata ekanisa yonsi na abanto bonsi abaigwete aya.
Ebikone na amakumia
Ⅻ Ebikone ebinge na amakumia bikagenderera gokoreka ase egati y’abanto amo n’abatomwa. Ebegeeni bonsi bagasangererekana amo ase Egesona gia Sulemani. ⅩⅢ Onde tarenge korwa isiko obete noboremu kobwatana nabarabwo, korende abanto bakabasika ogosika okonene. ⅩⅣ Korende abanto abange bakamentwa ase abegeeni omoganda omonene bw’abasacha na’abakuungu abwo begenete omonene. ⅩⅤ Goetera ase emeremo eye abatomwa bare gokora, abarwaire nigo barenge kobogorigwa isiko ase amarere n’ebisero biabo erinde ekero Petero agoeta, omorengari oye okune abande b’abwo. ⅩⅥ Nonya n’abanto korwa ase emechie eria yaetanaine Yerusalemu bagesangereria, bakarenta abarwaire amo nabwo babwatire nebirecha bi’eubi, na bonsi bakagwenigwa.
Abatomwa bagochandwa
ⅩⅦ Omokuani omonene akaimoka, na bonsi abarenge amo nere; abwo nabwo barenge b’Abasadukai. Bagaichorwa ne ribero rinene ase Abatomwa ase igo bakaroberia kobaimokeria egetambokero. ⅩⅧ Bakabwata abatomwa, bakababeeka ime ase echela y’abanto bonsi ime. ⅩⅨ Korende ase obotuko boria omomalaika bw’Omonene akaigora ebisieri bi’echela, akabasokia isiko, erio akabateebia, ⅩⅩ “Genda, motenene ase Hekalu ime, moteebie abanto bonsi amang’ana onsi igoro y’Obogima obo oboyia.” ⅩⅪ Abatomwa bakaigwera, mambia kobogokiamoka bagasoa Hekalu ime bagachaaka kworokereria.
Omokuani omonene n’ababwatani baye bakarangeria abanene b’Abayahudi goika ase omosangererekano goika ase ekegambero ki’abaanto bonsi; erio bagatoma ogochika gochia ase echela erinde barente abatomwa baria ase ekegambero. ⅩⅫ Korende ekero abarendi bachia goika, tibaabanyora echela ime. Bakairana, bagateeba, ⅩⅩⅢ “Echela nigo twayenyora esiekire buya kegima, na abarendi bateneine bisieri, korende twachia koigora ebiita titwanyora monto onde ime.” ⅩⅩⅣ Na ekero omonene bw’abarendi be Hekalu na abakuani abanene bachia koigwa amang’ana aya, bagasogwa nomoichano omonene ase engencho yabo, naki arabe amang’ana aya. ⅩⅩⅤ Omonto oyomo agaacha, akabateebia: “Rora, abanto baria mwabeegete echela ime nigo bateneine ase Hekalu ime kworokereria abanto.” ⅩⅩⅥ Erio omonene bw’abarendi akagenda amo n’abanto baaye, bakabarenta, korende tari ase chinguru, ekiagera barenge nobwoba ng’a mbaakwe amagena n’abanto.
ⅩⅩⅦ Ekero baabarentire bakababeeka ase ekegambero. Omokuani omonene akababoria, ⅩⅩⅧ 5:28: Mat 27:25“Inee! Titwabakanetie bokong’u ng’a timworokereria ase erieta ri’omonto oyo? Korende rora aya mwakorire! Akababoria. Mwaichoirie Yerusalemu nokworokereria kwaino, naende morigetie gotororekania koba buna nintwe twaretete ogokwa kwaye.”
ⅩⅩⅨ Korende Petero na abatomwa bande bakairaneria: “Goika intwe toigwere Nyasae kobua Mwanyabaanto. ⅩⅩⅩ Nyasae bwe chisokoro chiaito nigo abogetie Yeso korwa ase amakweri, nyuma inwe mwamoitire ase okomosunga ase omosalaba. ⅩⅩⅪ Nyasae akamoimokereria igoro ase ensemo y’okoboko kwaye gw’okorio buna Omoraai na Omotooria, erinde abae abanto ba Israeli ribaga riokoonchoka erinde banyore okwaberwa kw’ebibe biabo. ⅩⅩⅫ Intwe nigo tore kirori ase amang’ana aya onsi—intwe amo n’Omoika Omochenu, oyio ore ekeegwa kia Nyasae ase baria bonsi bakomoigwera ere.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Ekero abanto baria b’ekegambero bachia koigwa aya bakagechigwa, bakarigia goita abatomwa bakwe. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Korende Omofarisai oyomo ore korokwa Gamalieli, oyomo bw’ekegambero, omworokia bw’amachiiko, omonto osikire n’abanto bonsi, agachiika ng’a abanto aba babekwe isiko ake, ⅩⅩⅩⅤ erio agakwana ase ekegambero keria, “Inwe abasacha b’Abaisraeli, erende buna morakorere abanto aba. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Naki ase amatuko aetire Theuda naimoka, agetogia ng’a ere nomonto omonene; abanto koreng’ana amagana ane bakabwatana nere, korende ere agaitwa, na abatunyani baye bonsi bagaserebekana, n’ekeombe kiaye kegasira. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Magega yaye, Yudasi Omogalili nere akaimoka ase amatuko aria ekero abanto baare koriikwa, akang’usa abanto abange bamotunyane. Nere boigo agasira, na bonsi abamotunyanete bagaserebekana. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Na bono nabateebirie, timobaisa koira getambokero kende gionsi ase abanto aba. Batige! Ekiagera onye ring’ana eri, gose emeremo eye, neya Mwanyabaanto, nesarigwe, ⅩⅩⅩⅨ korende onye neya Nyasae, timokonyaara koyesaria nonya ng’ake; mobaise kororekana ng’a Nyasae mokorwania!”
ⅩⅬ Bagancha amang’ana aye, na ekero barangeretie abatomwa, bakabachwata, bakabachiika tibakwana ase erieta ria Yeso. Erio bakabatiga bagende. ⅩⅬⅠ Abatomwa bakarua ase ekegambero keria, bagogete ekiagera konya baabarirwe ng’a bagweneire korenterwa obosooku ase engencho y’Erieta ria Yeso. ⅩⅬⅡ Na kera rituko, ase Hekalu ime na ase chinyomba chiabo tibatigete kworokereria na korandia amang’ana amaya igoro ya Yeso, oyio ore Kristo.
