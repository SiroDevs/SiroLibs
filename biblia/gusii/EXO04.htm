Nyasae okoa Musa okobua gw’ogokora ebikone
Ⅰ Erio Musa akamoiraneria Nyasae akamoteebia, “Eraba rende ng’a agwo tibanyegeti, gose koigwa eriogi riaane, barabwo nigo baratebe: Omonene takoorokereti.”
Ⅱ Omonene akamobooria, “Inki ekio obwate koboko kwao?”
Musa akairaneria, “Nenyimbo.”
Ⅲ Omonene akamoteebia, “Yerute inse!” Na ekero Musa ayeruta inse, ekaonchoka ekaba eng’iti, na Musa agatama korwa asere. Ⅳ Eri’Omonene akamoteebia Musa, “Rambora okoboko kwao, oyebwate ekemincha.” Erio ere akarambora okoboko kwaye, akayebwata, na ase okoboko kwaye ekaonchoka, ekaba enyimbo naende. Ⅴ Na Omonene akamoteebia, “Okore igo, na barabwo inabegene ng’a inche Omonene, Nyasae bwe chisokoro chiabo, Nyasae o Aburaamu, na o Isaka, na o Yakobo nakoorokera.”
Ⅵ Naende Omonene akamoteebia, “Soyia okoboko kwao eanga yao ime ase egekuba kiao.” Musa agakora boigo; na ekero akorusetie akanyora ng’a kwabeire okorabu se ase buna okwo omorwaire bwa ukoma. Ⅶ Naende Omonene akamoteebia, “Irania okoboko kwao eanga ime ase egekuba kiao.” Musa agakora bo, na ekero akorusetie akanyora ng’a kwabeire buya, buna koreng’ana omobere oye onde ore. Ⅷ Omonene akamoteebia, “Onye tibakwegena amang’ana ao, gose tibagwancha ase egekone kie ritang’ani, rirorio mbanche ase egia kabere. Ⅸ Onye tibakwegena nonya nase ebikone ebio bibere, gose gwancha amang’ana ao, rirorio obuuche amaache make korwa ase orooche rwa Naeli, oyaitere ase ense enyomo; amaache ayio orabuuche korwa ase orooche naonchoke abe amanyinga ase ense enyomo.”
Ⅹ Erio Musa agateebia Omonene, “Koranche, Omonene one, inche tindi Omokuani omuya, nonya nkorwa kare, gose korwa riria gwachaagete gokwana nainche, omosomba oo. Oromeme rwane nororito na nigo ingotutura.”
Ⅺ Omonene akamoiraneria, “Ning’o okoa Mwanyabaanto omonwa bw’ogokwana? Ning’o omokorete abe rimama, gose omotino, gose abe okorora, gose koba omouko? Inee! Tari inche Omonene? Ⅻ Bono genda! Nainche omonyene nimbe naye naende ninkoe amang’ana ogochia gokwana nomonwa oo na gokworokereria aya ogochia goteeba.”
ⅩⅢ Korende Musa akamoiraneria, “Omonene, koranche, toma monto onde bwensi oranche.”
ⅩⅣ Eri’Omonene akaba nendamwamu ekoberoroka ase Musa, akamoteebia, “Inee! Aroni momura omino, oria Omolawi, takaiyo? Nimanyete ng’a ere nere omonto omanyete gokwana buya. Naama! Ngocha are gokorotota; ere ekero arakorore nasasoke ase enkoro yaye. ⅩⅤ Aye nokwane nere, omoteebie aya agochia gokwana. Inche nimbae inwe babere amang’ana mogochia gokwana, boigo nimbaorokererie aya mogochi gokora. ⅩⅥ Nere orabe ogokwana nabaanto ribaga riao; ere abe Omokuani oo, na aye obe Enyasae asare ere. ⅩⅦ Aye obogorie enyimbo eyio koboko kwao, nero orakorere ebikone.”
Musa okoirana gochia Misiri
ⅩⅧ Musa akairana gochia ase Jethiro, ise biara, akamoteebia, “Koranche, tiga ing’irane gochia Misiri korora abaminto gose moyo bare.” Jethiro akamoteebia, “Ee, genda nomorembe.”
ⅩⅨ Ekero Musa konya atararwa aaria Midiani, Omonene akamoteebia, “Irana gochia Misiri, ekiagera baria bonsi barigetie obogima bwao bakuure.” ⅩⅩ Musa akaimokia mokaye na abana baye, akabariinia etigere igoro, na barabwo bakairana gochia ase ense ya Misiri. Ere akabogoria enyimbo ya Nyasae koboko kwaye.
ⅩⅪ Eri’Omonene agateebia Musa, “Bono ekero orabe gokoirana Misiri, origererie buya ng’a nokore ase obosio bwa Farao amakumia onsi aya nakoeire okobua gw’okoyakora. Korende ninkong’ie enkoro yaye, na ere tagotiga abanto bagende. ⅩⅫ Erio omoteebie Farao: Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba: Abaisraeli nomwana one omotangi. ⅩⅩⅢ 4:23: Okong’ 12:29Inche nagotebirie: Moe omwana one ribaga agende gonsasiima korende koraange, rirorio nigo ndaite omwana oo omotangi.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Kobare nchera kogenda, Omonene akaumerana na Musa aase baare gotimokera, akarigia komoita. ⅩⅩⅤ Erio Sipora akaimokia rigena riare erioge, agasara omwana oye risankwa ria isiko ri’ekiimo kiaye ki’obosacha akarikunia amagoro a Musa, akabora, “Amaene, nigo ore omonywomi one bw’amanyinga.” ⅩⅩⅥ Agw’Omonene agatiga Musa, tamoita. Erio omokungu oyio agateeba, “Omonyuomi bw’amanyinga ase engencho y’okwaroka.”
ⅩⅩⅦ Omonene agateebia Aroni, “Genda gochia ase erooro orotote Musa.” Erio Aroni akagenda, akaumerana na Musa ase egetunwa kia Nyasae, akamonyunyunta. ⅩⅩⅧ Musa agateebia Aroni amang’ana onsi Omonene amotebetie, na igoro y’ebikone bionsi amochigeete akore. ⅩⅩⅨ Erio Musa na Aroni bakagenda, bagasangereria abagaaka bonsi b’Abaisraeli. ⅩⅩⅩ Aroni akabateebia amang’ana onsi Omonene atebeetie Musa, na ere agakora ebikone biria maiso marore ase obosio bw’abaanto. ⅩⅩⅪ Erio abanto bakwegena; na ekero baigwete ng’a Omonene bwechandeire Abaisraeli, na ororire emechando yabo, barabwo bagatung’ama inse, bakamosasiima.
