Riborio igoro y’okorenda Esabato
(Mat 12:1-14; Mar 2:23—3:6)
Ⅰ 6:1: Okoir 23:25Rituko ri’Esabato Yeso ngoeta arenge ase emegondo y’engano; aborokigwa baye bakabutora ebigara, bakabibunga namaboko abo, bakaria. Ⅱ Korende abande ase Abafarisai bakababoria, “Naki mogokora aya’atagwenereti gokorwa ase rituko ri’Esabato?”
Ⅲ Yeso akairaneria, “Timwana gosoma aya Daudi akorete, ekero arenge nenchara, ere na abarenge amo nere, Ⅳ 6:4: Abal 24:96:4: 1 Sam 21:1-6buna asoete ase enyomba ya Nyasae, akabogoria na koria emegati yarenge koruegwa ase Nyasae, etare boronge koriegwa nonde otatiga nabakuani boka, naende akaa abwo barenge amo nere?” Ⅴ Akabateebia, “Omwana‐o‐Mwanyabaanto nere Omonene bw’Esabato.”
Ⅵ Esabato ende agasoa ase esinagogi, akaorokereria. Narengeo omonto agwo, okoboko kwaye gw’okorio kwagataete. Ⅶ Aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai bakamotega korora gose namogwenie ase rituko ri’Esabato, banyare komosoera. Ⅷ Korende akamanya ebirengererio biabo, agateebia omonto oria okoboko kwaye kwagataete, “Imoka, otenene gati aa!” Akaimoka agatenena. Ⅸ Yeso akabateebia, “Nkobaboria nde ring’ana erimo: Mboronge rituko ri’Esabato gokora amaya gose amabe, gotooria obogima gose kobosiria?” Ⅹ Akabarigereria bonsi, agateebia omonto oria, “Rambora okoboko kwao!” Agakora bo; okoboko kwaye gokagwenigwa. Ⅺ Na barabwo bagaichorwa nokobea, bagateebania barabwo ase barabwo, buna baramokore Yeso.
Yeso ogochora abatomwa ikomi na babere
(Mat 10:1-4; Mar 3:13-19)
Ⅻ Ase amatuko aria akaimoka akagenda ase egetunwa gosaba; akabao obotuko bwonsi ase ogosaba Nyasae. ⅩⅢ Bogachia gokia mambia akarangeria aborokigw baye, agachora korwa ase bare ikomi na babere, abwo arogete abatomwa. ⅩⅣ Simioni nere arogete Petero, na Anderea momura omwabo; Yakobo na Yohana, Filipo na Baritholomayo, ⅩⅤ na Matayo na Thomas, Yakobo bw’Alufayo, na Simioni oyomo bw’Abazelote, ⅩⅥ na Yuda o Yakobo, na Yudasi Iskariota, nere oyo omoruete.
ⅩⅦ Agatirimboka amo nabarabwo, agatenena aase ateremu, amo nomoganda bw’aborokigwa baye; na omoganda bw’abaanto abaruete Boyahudi yonsi, na Yerusalemu, na engegu ya Turo na Sidoni, bagacha komoigwa na kogwenigwa amarwaire abo. ⅩⅧ Bagacha koigwa amang’ana aye boigo nakogwenigwa amarwaire abo, na abwo bachandire nebirecha bi’eubi bakagwenigwa. ⅩⅨ Omoganda bwonsi okarigia komokuna, ekiagera chinguru chiare korua asare, chikabagwenia bonsi.
Ogosesenigwa na okoragererigwa
(Mat 5:1-12)
ⅩⅩ Yeso akaimokia amaiso aye ase aborokigwa baye, akabora,
Imosesenirie inwe abamore abataka,
ekiagera oborwoti bwa Nyasae nobwaino.
ⅩⅪ Imosesenirie inwe abamore nenchara bono,
ekiagera namoigotigwe.
“Imosesenirie inwe abamokorera bono,
ekiagera namoseke.
ⅩⅫ 6:22: 1 Petr 4:14“Imosesenirie inwe ekero Mwanyabaanto bakobagecha, na kobarusanania, na kobachaya, na gosaria amarieta aino buna nababe more, ase engencho y’Omwana‐o‐Mwanyabaanto. ⅩⅩⅢ 6:23: 2 Amg 36:16; Ogok 7:52Goka ase rituko riria, na gochenga, ekiagera ekeene eng’eria yaino nenene igoro; naki chisokoro chiabo nabo chiakorerete ababani engencho eyio eyio.
ⅩⅩⅣ “Korende obobe nobwaino inwe abanda, ekiagera mwanyorire omogooko oino.
ⅩⅩⅤ “Obobe nobwaino inwe abamoigotete bono, ekiagera namoche koba nenchara.
“Obobe nobwaino inwe abamogoseka bono, ekiagera namoche goichana na korera.
ⅩⅩⅥ “Obobe nobwaino inwe, ekero abanto bakobatogia, ekiagera chisokoro chiabo nabo chiakorerete ababani b’oborimo engencho eyio eyio.
Obwanchani ase ababisa
(Mat 5:38-48)
ⅩⅩⅦ “Korende nabateebirie inwe abamokoigwa: Ancha ababisa baino, bakorere amaya abwo baabagechete, ⅩⅩⅧ basesenie abwo bakobaragereria, basabere abwo bakobakorera bobe. ⅩⅩⅨ Oyo okogoaka orosea oromo, moonchorere noronde, na ere oyogokoura eanga ya isiko, tomorina neya ime. ⅩⅩⅩ Moe kera oyokogosaba; na oyogokoura ebinto biao tomoboria naende. ⅩⅩⅪ 6:31: Mat 7:12Naende buna morigetie abanto babakorere inwe, nainwe mobakorere bo.
ⅩⅩⅫ “Komoranche abaabanchete inwe, imbuya ki moranyore? Ekiagera nonya nabanyene ebibe nigo banchete abwo baabanchete. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Komorabakorere amaya abwo abakobakorera inwe amaya, imbuya ki moranyore? Ekiagera nonya nabanyene ebibe nabo bagokora. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Naende komorarue singo ase abanto mosemeretie konyora egento korwa ase bare naende, imbuya ki moranyore? Ekiagera nonya nabanyene ebibe nigo bakorua singo ase abanyene ebibe, erinde banyore koreng’ana. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Korende mwanche ababisa baino, bakorere amaya, na korua singo, motari korengereria konyora egento gi’okoiranerigwa, eng’eria yaino nebe enene, na inwe namobe abana b’Oyore Igoro Mono, ekiagera ere nomuya ase abanto batari nokoiraneria ng’a mbuya mono, nonya nase abang’iti. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Mobe namaabera, buna Iso oino are namaabera.
Igoro y’okogambia abande
(Mat 7:1-5)
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ “Timogambia, nainwe timokogambigwa; timonachera, nainwe timokonacherwa; abera, nainwe namwaberwe; ⅩⅩⅩⅧ karue, nainwe namoegwe. Ekerengo ekiya keigateire, keinyageire, geichire, nakio abanto barabae inwe ase amaboko aino, ekiagera ekerengo keria mokorengera, nakio morache korengerwa nainwe.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅨ 6:39: Mat 15:14Akabateebia omoreng’anio, “Inee! Omouko nonyare koraa omouko onde? Tibakogwa bonsi babere ase engoro? ⅩⅬ 6:40: Mat 10:24,25; Yoh 13:16; 15:20Omworokigwa tari omonene kobua omworokia oye, korende kera oyomo gakoorire kworokererigwa nabe buna omworokia oye. ⅩⅬⅠ Inaki okorigereria ekeogoto kere ase eriso ri’oyomino, na omoribo ore ase eriso riao ime toyorochi? ⅩⅬⅡ Gose naki orateebie oyomino: Oyominto, ntige nkorusie ekeogoto kere ase eriso riao ime, na aye omonyene torochi omoriibo ore ase eriso riao? Aye omong’ainereria, rusia ritang’ani omoribo ore ase eriso riao, nario orarore buya omanye korusia ekeogoto kere ase eriso ri’oyomino.
ⅩⅬⅢ “Naki tori omote omuya okwama amatunda amabe, gose omote omobe okwama amatunda amaya. ⅩⅬⅣ 6:44: Mat 12:33Kera omote nigo okomanyekana ase amatunda aye. Naki ase emetobo abanto tibari gotwa amako, gose ase emenyenya tibari gotwa amasabibu. ⅩⅬⅤ 6:45: Mat 12:34Omonto omuya ase emonga engiya y’enkoro yaye nigo akorua ayare amaya; na omonto omobe ase emonga yaye embe nigo akorua ayare amabe, ekiagera ebiichire ase enkoro, nabio omonwa ogokwana.
Amaroso abere
(Mat 7:24-27)
ⅩⅬⅥ “Nase ki mokondoka Omonene Nyasae, na inwe timori gokora aya ngoteeba? ⅩⅬⅦ Kera omonto ogocha ase ’nde, na koigwa amang’ana aane, na koyakora, nimborokie buna are: ⅩⅬⅧ Nigo agwekaine nomonto oagachete enyomba, agakunya gochia inse mono, akabeeka oboroso bwaye igoro ase egetare. Ekero etoigo yachia gocha, eooro egaaka enyomba eria ne chinguru, tiyanyara koyenyegeria, ekiagera oboroso bwaye bwaagachetwe igoro ase egetare. ⅩⅬⅨ Nere oyokoigwa otari gokora iga, nigo agwekaine nomonto oagachete enyomba igoro ase amaroba, etabegetwe oboroso. Nabo eooro ekayeaka ne chinguru, ekagwa bwango, na okogwa kw’enyomba eria gokaba okonene.”
