Ⅰ Abanchani baane, ekiagera tore ne chiira echio, twesibie intwe abanyene ogotacheneka kw’omobere na omoika, erio toikeranie obochenu ase okomoiroka Nyasae.
Omogoko o Paulo
Ⅱ Toigorere chinkoro chiaino; titokorereti monto onde bobe, titosareti monto, titorieti monto kiaboama. Ⅲ Tintebeti ring’ana eri erinde mbagambie inwe, ekiagera ritang’ani konya nabateebirie ng’a inwe nigo more ne ribaga ase chinkoro chiaito, ase ogokwa na okoba moyo amo. Ⅳ Ninde noboremu obonge kongokwana nainwe; ogwetogia kwane igoro ase more nokonge; naichoirwe nokoremigwa. Ase ogochandwa gwaito gwonsi naichoirwe nomogooko omonge mono.
Ⅴ 7:5: 2 Abakr 2:13Naki nonya nekero twaigete Makedonia, emebere yaito tiyanyorete ogotimoka, korende togaachandwa chinsemo chionsi; isiko ekaba esegi, na ime bokaba obwoba. Ⅵ Korende Nyasae, oyokoremia abaichanete, agatoremia ase ogocha gwa Tito, Ⅶ gose tari ase ogocha kwaye gwoka, korende nonya nase okoremigwa koria aremetigwe nainwe. Agatoteebia amang’ana y’amaiga aino, na okweng’ereria kwaino, na omokia oino ase ’nde, goika nkamenta kogoka. Ⅷ Naki nonya nabaichanetie ase riruube riria tinkweitia (nonya neitetie ritang’ani), ekiagera narorire ng’a riruube riria ndiabaichanetie, korende nase ekero egeke gioka. Ⅸ Bono nagokire, tari ekiagera mwaichanetigwe, korende ekiagera mwaichanetigwe goika mokaonchoka, ekiagera nigo mwaichanetigwe ase ogwancha kwa Nyasae, erinde timonyara gosira ase ogokora gwaito. Ⅹ Naki ogoichanigwa okore ase ogwancha kwa Nyasae nigo gokorenta okoonchoka gokoraa gochia ase ogotooreka, gotari na kweitia; korende ogoichanigwa gw’ense nigo gokorenta amakweri. Ⅺ Rora buna ogoichanigwa okwo kore ase ogwancha kwa Nyasae kwabarenteire omokia, na ogwekengera, na okobea, okoiroka na amaiga amange, ogwetigana na egesiomba! Ase kera enchera mweorokererie koba motari na komocha ase ring’ana erio. Ⅻ Nabo, nonya nabariikerete, tindigeti ase engencho yaye ere oyokorete bobe, gose tari ase engencho yaye ere oyokoreretwe bobe, korende ogwetigana kwaino ase tore konyare koorokigwa ase more ase obosio bwa Nyasae. ⅩⅢ Ase ayio twaremiigwe.
Na kobua ase okoremigwa okwo tokagookera mono omogooko o Tito, ekiagera omoyo oye otimokigwe nainwe mwensi. ⅩⅣ Naki onye ngwetogia asare ase engencho yaino, tinasoogetigwe; korende buna twabateebeetie amang’ana onsi ase ekeene, naboigo nogwetogia gwaito ase obosio bwa Tito nigo kwarenge ogw’ekeene. ⅩⅤ Obwanchani bwaye bw’omoyo ase more bwamentekanire, ekero akoinyora okoigwera kwaino inwe mwensi, na buna mwamoroisetie ase okoiroka na okoiguswa. ⅩⅥ Nagokire, ekiagera ase kera ring’ana ninde nomoyo bw’oboremu ase engencho yaino.
