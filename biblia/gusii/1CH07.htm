Ororeria rwa Isakaru
Ⅰ Abana abamura ba Isakaru nabwo aba: Tola, na Pua, na Yasubu, na Simuroni, bonsi mbaane.
Ⅱ Abana abamura ba Tola nabwo aba: Usi na Refaya, na Yerieli, na Yamai, na Ibusamu, na Semueli. Aba nabwo abateneneri be chiamate chia Tola, abanto chituoni ase okorwana esegi ase ebigori biabo. Ase amatuko ’okogamba gwa Daudi nigo barenge chilifu emerongo ebere na ibere, na amagana atano na rimo (22,600).
Ⅲ Abana abamura ba Usi nabwo aba: Isiraya amo nabana baye abamura: Mikaeli, na Obadia, na Yoel, na Isia, bonsi mbatano; na abwo nigo barenge abateneneri. Ⅳ Barabwo nigo babwate abakungu abange babaiborerete abana abamura abange. Ase igo barwete korwa ase chinderia chiabo abasikari abakong’u chilifu emerongo etato na isano nemo (36,000).
Ⅴ Abanto bamwabo be chiamate chionsi chia Isakaru nigo barenge abakong’u ase esegi. Bonsi nigo barenge chilifu emerongo etano na etato na isano na ibere (87,000), buna barigetwe ase chinderia chiabo.
Ororeria rwa Benjamini
Ⅵ Abana abamura ba Benjamini nabwo aba: Bela, na Bekeri, na Yediaeli, bonsi mbatato.
Ⅶ Abana abamura ba Bela nabwo aba: Esiboni, na Usi, na Usieli, na Yerimoti, na Iri. Aba bonsi batano nigo barenge abateneneri be chiamate chiabo, abanto abakong’u ase esegi. Abwo barikire ase omooroberio bwe chinderia chiabo nigo barenge chilifu emerongo ebere na ibere, na emerongo etato, na baane (22,034).
Ⅷ Abana abamura ba Bekeri nabwo aba: Semira, na Yoashi, na Eliezeri, na Elioenai, na Omuri, na Yerimoti, na Abiya, na Anatoti, na Alemeti. Aba bonsi nigo barenge abana abamura ba Bekeri. Ⅸ Abwo barikire ase omooroberio bwe chinderia chiabo, amo nabateneneri be chiamate chiabo, nigo barenge abanto abakong’u ase esegi, abanto chilifu emerongo ebere, na amagana abere (20,200).
Ⅹ Abana abamura ba Yediaeli nabwo aba: Bilihani amo nabana baye abamura: Yeusi, na Benjamini, na Ehudi, na Kenaana, na Setani, na Tarisisi, na Ahisahari, Ⅺ Aba bonsi nabwo ab’ororeria rwa Yediaeli. Barenge abateneneri be chiamate chiabo, naende nigo barenge abakong’u ase esegi. Omobaro obo nechilifu ikomi na isano na ibere, na amagana abere (17,200). Ⅻ Supimu na Hupimu nigo barenge ab’ororeria rwa Iri, naende Abahusi nab’ororeria rw’Aheri.
Ororeria rwa Nafutali
ⅩⅢ Abana abamura ba Nafutali nabwo aba: Yaseeli, na Guni, na Yeseri, na Salumu. Nabwo abanto b’ororeria rwa Bilia.
Ororeria rwa Manase
ⅩⅣ Abana abamura ba Manase nabwo aba: Asirieli, oria omokungu oye o sere‐sere, orenge Omoaramai amoiborerete. Boigo omokungu oria akaibora Makiri, ise Gileadi. ⅩⅤ Makiri akanywomeria Hupimu omokungu, boigo akanywomeria Supimu omokungu. Omoiseke omwabo nigo arenge korokwa Maaka. Omwana omomura o kabere o Manase nigo arenge korokwa Selofehadi. Selofehadi nigo abwate abana abaiseke boka.
ⅩⅥ Maaka, moka Makiri, akaibora omwana omomura, akamoroka Peresi; na momura omwabo Peresi akarokwa Seresi. Abana abamura babere ba Peresi nabwo Ulamu na Rakemu. ⅩⅦ Ulamu nabwate omwana omomura orenge korokwa Bedani. Aba nabwo barenge ab’ororeria rwa Gileadi, mosinto o Makiri o Manase.
ⅩⅧ Hamoleketi omosubati omwabo Gileadi, akaibora Isi‐Hodi na Abieseri, na Mahala. ⅩⅨ Abana ba Shemida nabwo aba: Ahiani, na Sekemu, na Liki, na Aniamu.
Ororeria rw’Efraimu
ⅩⅩ Oro narwo ororeria rw’Efraimu: Sutela omomura oye, na Beredi omomura oye, na Tahati omomura oye, na Eleada omomura oye, na Tahati omomura oye, ⅩⅪ na Sabadi omomura oye, na Sutela omomura oye. Naende Efraimu nabwate abana babere, Eseri na Eleadi; baria abanto baiboretwe ase ense ya Gadi baitete, ekiagera bachete koiba chiombe chiabo. ⅩⅫ Efraimu ise akaba okobareera ase amatuko amange, na abamura bamwabo bagacha komoremia. ⅩⅩⅢ Erio Efraimu akaba na mokaye, na ere akanyora oborito, akaibora omwana omomura. Efraimu akaroka omwana oria Beria, ekiagera amang’ana amabe achete ase enka yaye.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omwana oye omoiseke nigo arenge korokwa Seera, oria oagachete Beti‐Horoni ya rogoro na eya maate, na Useni‐Seera.
ⅩⅩⅤ Refa nigo arenge omomura bw’Efraimu, na Resefu omomura oye, na Tela omomura oye, na Tahani omomura oye, ⅩⅩⅥ na Ladani omomura oye, na Amihudi omomura oye, na Elisama omomura oye, ⅩⅩⅦ na Nuni omomura oye, na Yoshua omomura oye.
ⅩⅩⅧ Chingongo chiabo bamenyete nachio Beteli na ebichie biaye, na ensemo ya moocha Naarani, na ensemo ya bosongo Geseri na ebichie biaye, na Sekemu na ebichie biaye, na Aya na ebichie biaye.
ⅩⅩⅨ Boigo ase ribebe ri’abaanto ba Manase mbabwate Beti‐Sehani na ebichie biaye, na Taanaki na ebichie biaye, na Megido na ebichie biaye, na Dori na ebichie biaye.
Ase emechie eyio na ebichie nao abanto b’ororeria rwa Yusufu, mosinto o Israeli, bamenyete.
Ororeria rw’Aseri
ⅩⅩⅩ Abana abamura b’Aseri nabwo aba: Imuna, na Isiva, na Isivi na Beria, na omoiseke omwabo Sera.
ⅩⅩⅪ Abana abamura ba Beria nabwo Heberi, na Malikieli, oria orenge ise Birisaiti.
ⅩⅩⅫ Heberi nere orenge ise Yafileti, na Someri, na Hotamu, na ise omoiseke omwabo Sua.
ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Abana abamura ba Yafileti nabwo aba: Pasaki, na Bimuhali, na Asiwati. Aba nabwo abana abamura ba Yafileti.
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Abana abamura ba Someri, omomura omwabo, nabwo aba: Roga, na Yehuba, na Aramu.
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Abana abamura ba Helemu, omomura omwabo, nabwo aba: Sofa, na Imuna, na Selesi, na Amali.
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Abana abamura ba Sofa nabwo aba: Sua, na Hareneferi, na Suali, na Beri, na Imura, ⅩⅩⅩⅦ na Beseri, na Hodi, na Sama, na Silisa, na Itirani, na Beera.
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Abana abamura ba Yeteri nabwo aba: Yefune, na Pisipa, na Ara. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Abana ba Ula nabwo aba: Ara, na Hanieli, na Risia.
ⅩⅬ Aba bonsi nigo barenge abanto b’ororeria rw’Aseri. Nigo barenge abateneneri be chiamate chiabo, abanto bamanyekanete ng’a nabakong’u ase esegi, abatang’ani b’abaraai. Abwo barigetwe korwa ase chinderia chiabo bagende korwana esegi nigo barenge chilifu emerongo ebere na isano nemo (26,000).
