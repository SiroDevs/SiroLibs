Ogochika Okonene
Ⅰ “Aya naro amachiiko, na emeroberio echiikire, Omonene Nyasae oino, anchiigete imborokererie, erinde moyaigwere na koyakora ase ense eria mogochia koira ebe eyaino. Ⅱ Ase obogima bwaino bwonsi, inwe amo nabana baino, na abaachokoro baino, goika moiroke Omonene Nyasae oino, na kobwata emeroberio yaye yonsi yachiikirwe, n’amachiiko aye onsi ayio inche ’nkobachiika, erio nario amatuko aino arabe amaange. Ⅲ Ase ayio inwe Abaisraeli, tegerera moigwe, monyaare koyarenda; erio nario er’abe buya ase more, naende ’momentekane mono ase ense eria entenenku ere n’amabeere n’amooke, buna Omonene, Nyasae bw’echisokoro chiaino, abarierete eira.
Ⅳ 6:4: Mar 12:29“Inwe Abaisraeli, tegerera moigwe! Omonene Nyasae oito, nere bweka Omonene. Ⅴ 6:5: Mat 22:37; Mar 12:30; Luk 10:27Mwanche Omonene, Nyasae oo, n’enkoro yao yonsi, n’omoyo oo bwonsi, ne chinguru chiao chionsi. Ⅵ 6:6: Okoir 11:18-20Amang’ana aya ’nkobachiika rituko ria reero timobaisa koyeeba nonya ng’ake. Ⅶ Moyorokererie abana baino mono, mobe mokona‐koyairanerera botambe; moyakwane ekero moikaransete ase chinyomba chiaino, na ekero more ase orogeendo inchera, na ekero mogochia korara, na ekero mokobooka. Ⅷ Moyasiberanie ase amaboko aino, na moyabeeke ase amasio aino igoro, abe amariiko akobainyoria. Ⅸ Moyariike ase emetobwa na emeriibo y’ebisieri bi’echinyomba chiaino, na ase ebiita biaino.
Ogokuurerwa igoro y’ogotaigwera
Ⅹ 6:10: a Omoch 12:7; b Omoch 26:3; c Omoch 28:13“Omonene Nyasae oino, nabaire na kobaikia ase ense eria arierete chisokoro chiaino, Aburaamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo eira, ng’a nabae inwe ense eria ebwate emechie emenene naende emiya, eyio inwe motaagachete. Ⅺ Chinyomba nigo chiraabe chiichire n’ebinto ebiya, ebio inwe motabegeteo, ne chinsooko chi’amaache echio motakuunyete, na emegondo y’emesabibu na ey’emezeituni, eyio motasimegete. Ⅻ Komoraikeo mwerende mono, timobaisa kweba Omonene oria obarusetie korwa ase ense ya Misiri, ase mwarenge abasomba. ⅩⅢ 6:13: Mat 4:10; Luk 4:8Momoiroke Omonene Nyasae oino; momokorere ere bweka na motiane ase erieta riaye rioka. ⅩⅣ Timobaisa gosasiima chinyasae chinde chionsi chiria chi’ abanto baabaetanaine. ⅩⅤ Omonene Nyasae oino, oyomenyete ase egati yaino, nigo are Nyasae bwe risemi; mwerende, erinde endamwamu yaye tebaisa gwoka igoro ase more na kobasiria korwa ase ense.
ⅩⅥ 6:16: a Mat 4:7; Luk 4:12; b Okong’ 17:1-7“Timobaisa komoteema Omonene Nyasae oino, buna mwamoteemete aaria Masa. ⅩⅦ Mobe abeegenwa ase okobwata amachiiko a Omonene Nyasae oino, na oborori bwaye, na emeroberio echiikire, eyio abaeire. ⅩⅧ Mokore ayaare amaronge na’amaya ase obosio bw’Omonene, erio nario er’abe buya ase more, monyaare gosoa na koira ense eria engiy’Omonene arierete chisokoro chiaino eira ng’a n’abayee, ⅩⅨ na kobaseeria ababisa baino bonsi korwa ase more, buna Omonene ateebete.
ⅩⅩ “Ase chingaki chigochia‐goocha ekero abana baino barababorie: Ninki engencho y’oborori obo, n’amachiiko aya, na emeroberio eye, ebi Omonene Nyasae oito, abachiigete? Nase ki Omonene Nyasae oito, abachiigete amachiiko aya na emeroberio eye? ⅩⅪ Goika mobairanerie: Intwe nigo twarenge abasomba ba Farao ase ense ya Misiri, n’Omonene agatorusiao n’okoboko kwaye gwe’chinguru. ⅩⅫ Omonene nigo aorogetie ebikone na amakumia amanene, naende ay’okoondokia, aaria ase ense ya Misiri, ase Farao, na ase abanto bonsi b’enyomba yaye, naintwe tokayarora. ⅩⅩⅢ Omonene agatorusia ase ense eria, erio agatorenta aiga, agatoa ense eye arierete chisokoro chiaito eira ng’a n’abayee. ⅩⅩⅣ Erio agatochiika ng’a toigwere amachiiko aya onsi, na koimoroka ere Omonene Nyasae oito, erio nario er’abe buya ase tore botambe. Toise gokora bo ere natorende, buna atorendete goika reero iga, naintwe ’natogenderere koba moyo. ⅩⅩⅤ Onye tokorenda na koigwera amachiiko aya onsi, ase obosio bw’Omonene Nyasae, buna ere atochiikire, nigo torabarerwe oboronge.”
