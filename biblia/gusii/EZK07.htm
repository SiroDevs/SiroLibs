Omoerio nigo ore ang’e ase Abaisraeli
Ⅰ Ring’ana ri’Omonene rigaacha ase ’nde, riganteebia: Ⅱ “Aye Mwanyabaanto, aya naro Omonene agoteebia ense y’Abaisraeli: Oyo nomoerio! Omoerio ngocha ore ase chinsemo inye chi’ense eye.
Ⅲ “Bono omoerio ogocheire, na inche ’nkorenta ’nde endamwamu y’ane ebe igoro ase ore, na ninkonachere ekiina koreng’ana nogokora kwao; ninkoe egesusuro ase engencho y’ogokora kwao gwonsi gw’okogechia. Ⅳ Tinkoombia ng’a tinkoroochi, gose tingokororera amaabera, korende ninkoe egesusuro ase engencho y’ogokora kwao, na ogokora kwao gw’okogechia ’ngokoiranere. Erio nario oramanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.”
Ⅴ Aya naro Omonene agoteeba: “Rora, obobe ngoocha bore, obobe borabe bogoetania bonde bwonsi. Ⅵ Omoerio ’ngoocha ore, ee, omoerio ’ngoocha ore okonyore! Naama! Ngoocha ore! Ⅶ Inwe abanto momenyete ase ense eye, bono egeku nkobacheera kere. Engaki yao yaikire; rituko ri’omoichano ngocha rire; tirikoba rituko ri’ogoaka amariogi y’omogooko igoro ase ebitunwa.
Ⅷ “Bono yatigaire engaki enke, inche ng’umore okogechigwa kwane na goikerania endamwamu y’ane igoro ase more. Nimbanachere ekiina koreng’ana nogokora kwaino, nimbae egesusuro ase engencho y’okogechia kwaino gwonsi. Ⅸ Tinkoombia ng’a timbarochi, gose tinkobarorera amaabera, korende nimbae egesusuro ase engencho y’ogokora kwaino, na ogokora kwaino gw’okogechia nkobairanere. Erio nario moramanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene nabaakire.
Ⅹ “Naama! Rituko riria ngocha rire! Ngocha rire! Egeku nkobacheera kere! Enyimbo y’okobaaka yasebokire, na obomwenu bwareeretire. Ⅺ Okorega okwo gwakiinire, kwabeire enyimbo igoro y’abwo bagokora amabe. Omonto obo tagotigara nonya noyomo; obotenenku bwabo mbosirigwe amo na kende gionsi babwate, na obonene bwabo bwonsi mboere. Ⅻ Rituko riria riakire ang’e; omogori tabaisa kogooka, gose omoonia tabasa korera ekiagera endamwamu y’ane ⅩⅢ Omoonia tari gocha koiranerigwa egento ekio aoonetie nonya ogendererete koba moyo. Aya abaanetwe igoro y’omoganda bwonsi tagotigwa taikeranigwa. Onde tari ase abwo bagokora ebibe oranyare gotooria obogima bwaye. ⅩⅣ Babugirie ebitureeri na kobeeka ebinto bionsi ang’e, korende nonya n’oyomo tari oragende ase esegi eyio, ekiagera endamwamu y’ane nigo ere igoro y’omoganda bwonsi.
Egesusuro ase engencho y’ebibe bi’Abaisraeli
ⅩⅤ “Ogoitwa nemioro*7:15 Ogoitwa nemioro: ase Ekegusii kenarire na’abange n’ogokenywa nigo kore isiko, na oborwaire bogoita, na enchara, nigo bire ase omochie ime. Omonto okare isiko y’omochie naitwe n’omoro, na oyore ase omochie ime, enchara na oborwaire ’mbimoite. ⅩⅥ Korende abaratigare bare moyo mbatame gochia ase ebitunwa igoro, babe buna amaruma are ase amakuruma, bonsi mbabe bakorera, kera oyomo ase engencho y’ebibe biaye. ⅩⅦ Mbairianyeke amaboko abo aborwe chinguru, na amaru abo ororobe, abe buna amaache. ⅩⅧ Mbeboyie chigunia, batubwe nomoondoko. Amasio abo nabe nobosooku, na emetwe yabo esunyoke etukia ebe amasio. ⅩⅨ Mbatugute chifeta chiabo ase chinchera; chitaabu chiabo nchibarwe koba buna egento ki’eubi. Chifeta ne chitaabu chiabo tichikonyara kobatooria ase rituko ri’endamwamu y’Omonene. Ebinto ebio tibigoisanekia okogania kwabo, gose tibikobaigotia, ekiagera nigo biabete ebigocha bikagera bagakora ebibe. ⅩⅩ Nigo baenenerete obwari bw’ebinto ebio, na korwa ase ebio bakaroisia emegwekano yabo y’okogechia na ey’obosooku. Ase ayio ninkore ebinto ebio bibe buna eubi ase bare.
ⅩⅪ “Inche nindete abanto abaao basakore ebinto ebio, na abakori amabe b’ense mbabiegwe, babikore bibe ebitachenegeti. ⅩⅫ Ning’onchore obosio bwane korwa ase bare, erinde bakore ekungo y’ane ebe eyetachenegeti; abauri mbasoe aroro na koyekora ebe eyetachenegeti.
ⅩⅩⅢ “Roisia ebinyuri, ekiagera ense yaichoirwe nogoiterwa kw’amanyinga, na obotindi bwaichoire ase omochie ime. ⅩⅩⅣ Ase ayio nindete ebisaku bire ebibe mono biire chinyomba chiabo. Ninsirie obomwenu bw’abare ne chinguru, na aase abo aachenu abe aya atachenegeti. ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero ogosirigwa korabaachere mbarigie omorembe, korende tibakoyonyora. ⅩⅩⅥ Amaakwa nabe agotunyanwa na amaakwa ande, na amang’ana amabe nabe agotunyanwa n’ayande amabe. Abanto mbarigie amaroro korwa ase ababani; abakuani mbasinywe kworokereria amachiiko, na abagaaka mbasinywe gosemia. ⅩⅩⅦ Omorwoti nabe okweumia, na abagaambi mbatubwe nomoichano; amaboko y’ abanto b’ense eye nabe akoiguswa ase obwoba. Nimbakorere koreng’ana nogokora kwabo; nimbagaambie koreng’ana buna barenge kogaamba. Erio nario baramanye ng’a inche ninche Omonene.”
