Omogwekano bw’ogosasiimwa o Mika
Ⅰ Ase ense y’ebitunwa ya Efraimu nareengeo omonto oyomo ore korokwa Mika. Ⅱ Ere agateebia ng’ina, “Ekero omonto aibete chisekeli chi’echifeta amagana ikomi na rimo naiguure ng’a, nkwaragereretie omoibi bw’echifeta chiria. Rora aiga, ninche nchibwate. Ninche nachiirete.”
Ngi’na akamoteebia, “Omwana one, Nyasae agosesenie.” Ⅲ Omomura oria akairania chisekeli chiria chi’echifeta amagana ikomi na rimo ase ng’ina. Ng’ina agateeba, “Naatananire chifeta echi ase Omonene ase engencho y’omwana one. Chifeta echio ’nachibe echi’okoroisia omogwekano bw’ogosasiimwa oraabe obaachire na oomire ne chifeta. Ase ayio, omwana one, ningochiiranerie.” Ⅳ Korende ere akairaneria ng’ina chitaabu chiria. Ngi’na akaimokia chifeta amagana abere, agachiirera omoturi bw’echifeta, na ere akaroisia omogwekano obaachire na oomire ne chifeta. Omogwekano oyio okabeekwa ase enyomba ya Mika.
Ⅴ Omonto oyio Mika nabwate enyomba yaye aase arenge gosasiimera ebinyasae biaye. Akaroisia egesibao ki’obokuani na emegwekano y’ogosasiimwa, naende akabeeka oyomo bw’abamura baye abe Omokuani. Ⅵ 17:6: Abag 21:25Ase amatuko aria omorwoti tarenge ase Abaisraeli; kera omonto nigo arenge gokora kende gionsi arooche kere ekeronge aase okorora kwaye ere omonyene.
Ⅶ Bono nareenge omomura omosae, Omolawi omenyete agwo Bethlehemu o Yuda buna omomenyi. Ⅷ Omosae oria konya orure Bethlehemu ogendire korigia aase akomenya. Na ase orogeendo rw’aye agaika ase ense y’ebitunwa ya Efraimu, ase enyomba ya Mika. Ⅸ Mika akamobooria, “Ng’ai okorwa?”
Ere akairaneria, “Inche n’Omolawi o Bethlehemu korwa omochie o Yuda, naende nigo ’nkogeenda korigia aase ’nkomenya.”
Ⅹ Mika akamoteebia, “Menya aa amo nainche, obe ase ’nde tata, na obe Omokuani one, na inche ’nkoe chisekeli chi’echifeta ikomi kera omwaka, naende ’nkoe chianga amo n’endagera.” Ⅺ Omolawi oria agancha komenya amo n’omonto oria, na ere akamokora buna oyomo bw’abana baye abamura. Ⅻ Mika agaatanana omosae oria, nere akaba Omokuani oye, akamenya ase enyomba ya Mika. ⅩⅢ Mika agateeba “Bono namanyire ng’a Omonene nankorere amaya, ekiagera ’mbwate Omolawi ore Omokuani one.”
