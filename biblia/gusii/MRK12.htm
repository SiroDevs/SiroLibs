Omobayeeno bw’omogondo bw’emesabibu
(Mat 21:33-46; Luk 20:9-19)
Ⅰ 12:1: Isaya 5:1,2Yeso agachaaka gokwana nabarabwo ase emebayeeno. “Omonto naremete omogondo, agasimeka emesabibu imeo, akayogitera orobago, agakunya egetera akomitera amasabibu, akaagacha orwaki, akaywatananera abaremi, akamanora akagenda orogendo. Ⅱ Ekero kie rigesa agatoma omosomba ase abaremi baria, erinde anyore korwa ase abaremi ensemo y’amatunda y’emesabibu. Ⅲ Abaremi baria bakabwata omosomba oria, bakamoaka, bakamoseria atari na gento. Ⅳ Erio omonto oria agatoma naende ase bare omosomba onde, na oyio bakamoaka chingoma ase omotwe, bakamosokia bakamokora bobe. Ⅴ Omonto oria naende agatoma omosomba onde naende, na oyio bakamoita agakwa. Naboigo babakorerete abande abange; bagaaka abande, na abande bakabaita bagakwa. Ⅵ Narenge nomwana oyomo konya ataratoma, omwanchwa oye; oyo nere akamotoma ase bare magega, okobora: Inabasike omwana one. Ⅶ Abaremi baria bagateebania: Oyo nomonyamwando; inchwo tomoite, omwando oye obe oyoito. Ⅷ Bakamobwata, bakamoita agakwa, bakaimokia omobere oye bakayotuguta isiko korwa ase omogondo bw’emesabibu. Ⅸ Bono inki rende omonene oria bw’omogondo bw’emesabibu arakore? Nachiche asirie abaremi baria, na omogondo oria bw’emesabibu nayoe abande. Ⅹ 12:10: Zab 118:22,23Nonya neririiko eri timorarisoma:
“Rigena riria abaagachi bangete
riabeire rigena rinene ri’ekoona.
Ⅺ Ring’ana eri nigo riarure ase Omonene,
na rirorio nogokumia ase amaiso aito?”
Ⅻ Erio Abakuani abanene, aborokia b’amariiko n’abagaaka bakarigia kobwata Yeso ekiagera bamanyete ng’a omobayeeno oria nabwo ogoteeba; korende bakairoka omoganda, bakamotiga, bakagenda.
Riborio igoro y’okorwa ebango
(Mat 22:15-22; Luk 20:20-26)
ⅩⅢ Bagatoma asare abande b’Abafarisai na Abaherodia, erinde bamotege ase amang’ana. ⅩⅣ Bagachia goika bakamoteebia, “Omworokia, twamanyire ng’a aye nigo ore omonto bw’ekeene, gose tori koyiera monto, naki torengerereti obonene bw’ abanto, korende ase ekeene nigo okworokereria enchera ya Nyasae. Inee! Mboronge koa Kaisari ebango, gose taibo? ⅩⅤ Tomoe, gose titomoa?” Korende Yeso ere konya omanyire obong’ainereria bwabo, akabateebia: Nase ki mogonteema? Yendetere ebesa inyerore! ⅩⅥ Bakayerenta. Ere akababoria, “Ekieni eke na amariiko aya nebiang’o?” Bakamoiraneria, “Nebia Kaisari.” ⅩⅦ Erio Yeso akabateebia, “Ebire bia Kaisari, moe Kaisari, na ebia Nyasae, moe Nyasae.” Bakamokumia mono.
Riborio igoro y’okobokigwa
(Mat 22:23-33; Luk 20:27-40)
ⅩⅧ 12:18: Ogok 23:8Na Abasadukai bagachiera Yeso, baria bakoboora ng’a okobokigwa tikori, bakamobooria, ⅩⅨ 12:19: Okoir 25:5“Omworokia, Musa natoriikerete ng’a momura omwabo omonto karakwe atige omokungu oye; onye tari n’omwana, momura omwabo atware omokungu oria, atenenerie momura omwabo egesaku. ⅩⅩ Bono mbarenge abamura ba ise omo batano na babere. Oyo bw’eritang’ani akanyuoma omokungu, agakwa, tatiga abana. ⅩⅪ Na oyo o kabere akamotwara, agakwa; nonya nere tatiga abana; nonya noyo o gatato boigo. ⅩⅫ Bonsi batano na babere bakamotwara, tibatiga abana. Omoerio o bonsi omokungu oria nere agakwa. ⅩⅩⅢ Inee! Ase okobokigwa ’mokang’o arabe ase aba? Ekiagera bonsi batano na babere mbamotwarete.”
ⅩⅩⅣ Yeso akabateebia, “Inee! Timori gosira ase aya, ekiagera timomanyeti amariiko, nonya nokobua kwa Nyasae? ⅩⅩⅤ Inaki kobaraboke korwa ase abakuure, tibakonyuoma, korende nigo barabe buna abamalaika bare igoro. ⅩⅩⅥ 12:26: Okong’ 3:6Nase amang’ana y’abakuure, buna bakobokigwa, timwana gosoma ase egetabu ya Musa, ase ring’ana ri’egetutu, buna Nyasae amotebetie, akabora: Inche ninche Nyasae bw’Aburaamu, na Nyasae o Isaka, na Nyasae o Yakobo? ⅩⅩⅦ Ere tari Nyasae bw’abakuure, korende bw’abare moyo; ase ayio nigo mogosira mono.”
Ogochika okonene
(Mat 22:34-40)
ⅩⅩⅧ Oyomo ase aborokia b’amariiko agacha akabaigwa bakoamererania nere, akamanya ng’a obairaneirie buya, akamobooria, “Ase amachiiko onsi ndiriri rire rie ritang’ani?” ⅩⅩⅨ 12:29: Okoir 6:4,5Yeso akamoiraneria, “Erie ritang’ani nario eri: Igwa, Abaisraeli, Omonene oito, Nyasae, Nere Omonene oyomo; ⅩⅩⅩ naye mwanche Omonene oo nenkoro yao yonsi, na omoyo oo bwonsi, na obong’aini bwao bwonsi, ne chinguru chiao chionsi. ⅩⅩⅪ 12:31: Abal 19:18Na ogwa kabere nakwo oko: Mwanche omogisangio oo buna bweanchete. Amachiiko ande tari amanene kobua aya.” ⅩⅩⅫ 12:32: Okoir 4:35Omworokia oria bw’amariiko agateebia Yeso, “Ekeene, Omworokia, gwateebire buya ng’a Nyasae noyomo, na onde tari otatiga ere; ⅩⅩⅩⅢ 12:33: Hos 6:6na komwancha nenkoro yonsi, na okomanya gwonsi, ne chinguru chionsi, na gwancha omogisangio oo buna bweanchete, nkogwenerete kobua omosango bw’ebing’wanso bi’ogosambwa na ebing’wanso binde.” ⅩⅩⅩⅣ 12:34: Luk 10:25-28Yeso agachia korora ng’a nigo airaneria ase obong’aini, akamoteebia, “Aye tori are korwa ase oborwoti bwa Nyasae.” Korwa erio monto onde tanyara komoboria ring’ana naende.
Riborio igoro ya Kristo
(Mat 22:41-46; Luk 20:41-44)
ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Yeso akabora gakworokereria ime ase Hekalu, “Inaki aborokia b’amariiko bakobora ng’a Kristo nomwana o Daudi? ⅩⅩⅩⅥ 12:36: Zab 110:1Daudi omonyene nigo ateebete, buna arenge koraagwa n’Omoika Omochenu:
“Agateeba Omonene agakwana n’Omonene one
Ikaransa ensemo y’okoboko kwane gw’okorio,
goika mbeke ababisa bao
babe inse y’amagoro ao”.
ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Daudi omonyene nigo akomoroka Omonene; inaki rende Masihi aba omwana oye? Na omoganda oria omonene nigo orenge komoigwa ase omogooko.
Yeso ogokuurera igoro yaborokia b’amariiko
(Mat 23:1-36; Luk 11:37-54; 20:45-47)
ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Akabateebia ase okworokereria kwaye, “Mwerende korwa ase aborokia b’amariiko, abwo banchete gotaara beboyetie chianga chintambe, na gokwanigwa ase chichiiro, ⅩⅩⅩⅨ na goikaransa aase e ritang’ani ase chisinagogi, na aase e ritang’ani ase emeyega. ⅩⅬ Abwo bakounereria chinyomba ch’ababoraka, na ase obong’ainereria basaba ogosaba ogotambe; aba nabanyore ekiina ekere ekenene kobua.”
Esadaka y’omoboraka
(Luk 21:1-4)
ⅩⅬⅠ Yeso agaikaransa korigereria ekebekerero gi’esadaka, akarigereria buna omoganda okobeeka chitaabu ase ekebekerero. Abande abarenge abanda bakabeeka chinyinge. ⅩⅬⅡ Omokungu oyomo agacha, oyorenge omoboraka na omotaka, akabeeka ebisabwa bibere bie chitaai, rigori ri’ekeng’ese ki’ebesa eyemo. ⅩⅬⅢ Akarangeria aborokigwa baye, akabateebia, “Ekeene nabateebirie: Oyo omoboraka omotaka obekire chinyinge kobua bonsi ababeeka ase ekebeekerero gi’esadaka, ⅩⅬⅣ ekiagera aba bonsi nigo babeeka korwa ase ogoiteka kw’obonda bwabo; korende oyo ase obotaka bwaye obekire bionsi ebiarenge nabio, nonya n’enibo y’obogima bwaye bwonsi.”
