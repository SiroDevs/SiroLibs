ⅩⅩⅧ
Yakobo ogotama gochia Harani
Ⅰ Erio Isaka akarangeria Yakobo, akamosesenia, akamoteebia: “Tobaisa konyuoma omoiseke bw’Abakaanani. Ⅱ Imoka, ogende Padan‐Aramu ase omochie o Betueli, sokoro yao, onyuome omokungu oyomo korwa ase abaiseke ba Labani, nyoko-rome. Ⅲ Na Nyasae Omobui nagosesenie, agokinie, na oroiboro rwao naromente robe omoganda omonene bw’ebisaku. Ⅳ 28:4: Omoch 17:4-8Ere nakoe ogosesenigwa kwa Aburaamu, aye amo na ororeeria rwao, erinde onyare koira ense eye ore omomenyi bono, eye Nyasae aete Aburaamu!” Ⅴ Naboigo Isaka agateebia Yakobo agende, na ere akagenda Padan‐Aramu ase Labani, mosinto o Betueli Omoaramu, Labani nere momura omwabo Rebeka, ng’ina Yakobo na Esau.
Esau okonyuoma omokungu onde
Ⅵ Bono Esau akarora buna Isaka asesenirie Yakobo na komotoma agende Padan‐Aramu anyuome omokungu korwa aroro, ekiagera ekero amosesenetie, akamokania tabaisa konyuoma moiseke onde bwensi bw’Abakaanani!
Ⅶ Naende akarora ng’a Yakobo oigwereire ise na ng’ina ochiire Padan‐Aramu. Ⅷ Ase igo, ekero Esau arooche ng’a Isaka ise tancheti abaiseke abwo b’Abakaanani, Ⅸ nonya obwate abakungu bande, akagenda o Isumaeli, akanyuoma Mahalati, omoiseke o Isumaeli mosinto o Aburaamu, mosubati omwabo Nebayoti, abe omokungu oye amo nabakungu baria bande abwate ritang’ani.
Okoroota kwa Yakobo agwo Beteli
Ⅹ Yakobo akaimoka korwa Beeri‐Seba kogenda Harani. Ⅺ Agaika aase gete, akaraara agwo botuko, ekiagera erioba konya riagure. Akabogoria rigena erimo ri’aase aria, agatoorera omotwe oye ase rigena riria, karaaire. Ⅻ 28:12: Yoh 1:51Akaroota, akarora engaasi getenenirie korwa inse y’ense, keigete igoro, na abamalaika ba Nyasae bakoyeriinera na goikera. ⅩⅢ 28:13: Omoch 13:14,15Akarora Omonene oteneine ase obosio bwaye, okobora: “Inche ninche Omonene, Nyasae o Aburaamu, na Nyasae o Isaka. Ense eye oraire ninkoyee aye amo noroiboro rwao; ⅩⅣ 28:14: Omoch 12:3; 22:18narororwo inarobe oronge buna orotu rw’ense, naye norande gochia ensemo ya bosongo, na moocha, na sugusu, na Irianyi; na asore aye, na ase ororeeria rwao, chiamate chionsi chi’ense inachisesenigwe. ⅩⅤ Rora, inche nimbe amo naye, ninkorende aande onsi ase orabe gokogenda, naende ninkoiranie gocha ase ense eye; naki tinkogotiga goika nagokoreire ayio nagoteebirie.”
ⅩⅥ Yakobo akaboka korwa ase chitoro chiaye, akabora: “Amaene, Omonene nao are aase aa, na inche tinamanyete.” ⅩⅦ Ere akoboa, akabora: “Aa nase okoirokia! Aa takomocha koba enyomba ya Nyasae, aa nao egeita kia igoro.”
ⅩⅧ Yakobo akaboka mambia chuni, akaimokia rigena riria atoorerete omotwe rogoro, akaritenenia buna esiiro koba ekeinyorio, akaumora amaguta igoro yaye. ⅩⅨ Aase aria akaaroka Beteli.*28:19 Beteli: engencho yaye; enyomba ya Nyasae Korende ritang’ani omochie oria nigo ore korokwa Lusu. ⅩⅩ Erio Yakobo akaria eira, okobora: “Nyasae karabe amo nainche, andende ase orogendo rwane na kong’a endagera nkoria, ne chianga nkobeeka, ⅩⅪ na inche kondairane ase omochie o Tata nomorembe, eri’Omonene nabe Nyasae one; ⅩⅫ ne rigena eri natenenia aa buna esiiro nigo rirabe enyomba yao, Nyasae. Na kende gionsi orang’e inche, amaene tinkomocha gokoa ensemo ya ikomi.”
