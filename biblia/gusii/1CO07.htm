Obokong’u bw’okonyuomana, na Ogochika igoro yenywomo
Ⅰ Gaki ase amang’ana aya mwandikerete, mbuya omosacha takuna omokungu. Ⅱ Korende ase engencho y’amateemwa ay’ogokora obotomani kera omosacha abe na mokaye omonyene, na kera omokungu abe nomosaacha oye omonyene. Ⅲ Omosacha ae omokungu oye ekemogwenerete; naboigo nomokuungu ae omosacha oye ekemogwenerete. Ⅳ Omokungu tari nokobua igoro ase omobere oye, korende nomosaacha oye; naboigo nomosaacha tari nokobua igoro ase omobere oye, korende nomokuungu oye. Ⅴ Timorinana otatiga mwancheranire ase egeka egeke, erinde monyare kwerua ase ogosaba; naende mobe amo, Saitani tacha kobateema ekiagera motanyare gwetanga. Ⅵ Aya nigo nkobateebia buna ndoche; tindi korua ogochika. Ⅶ Ayanyanchete naro aya, ng’a abanto bonsi babe buna inche omonyene inde. Korende kera omonto nare nekeegwa kiaye omonyene gekorwa ase Nyasae, oyomo iga, na oyonde iga.
Ⅷ Bono nkobateebia nde baria bataranyuoma, na ababoraka, mbuya babe buna inche inde. Ⅸ Korende onye tibakonyara gwetanga, tiga banyuome, ekiagera mbuya konyuoma kobua gosambwa nokogania.
Ⅹ 7:10: Mat 5:32; 19:9; Mar 10:11,12; Luk 16:18Korende baria baikire konyuomana nabachiikire, gose tari inche, korende Omonene, ng’a omokungu tatiga omosacha oye, Ⅺ (korende, karabe otiganire nere, atige tanyuomwa gose abwatane nomosaacha oye naende), na omosacha tatiga mokaye.
Ⅻ Abwo bande nkobateebia inche, gose tari Omonene, ng’a onye onde ominto ore nomokuungu otegeneti na omokungu oyio bwanchire komenya nere, tamotiga. ⅩⅢ Na omokungu ore nomosaacha otegeneti, na omosacha oyo bwanchire komenya nere, tamotiga. ⅩⅣ Naki omosacha oria otegeneti ochenirwe ase engencho ya mokaye, na omokungu oria otegeneti ochenirwe ase engencho y’omosaacha oye. Onye taibo, abana baino anga tibaaba abachenu; korende buna ere bono, babeire abachenu. ⅩⅤ Korende oria otegeneti karaganie gotigana, tiga atigane. Oyominto omosacha gose oyominto omokungu tasibiri ase amang’ana buna ayio, korende Nyasae obarangeirie ase omorembe. ⅩⅥ Aye omokungu, ng’ai oramanyere gose notoorie omosacha oo? Gose aye omosacha, ng’ai oramanyere gose notoorie omokungu oo?
ⅩⅦ Kera omonto agenderere ase obogima obw’Omonene amogabeire na ase obwo Nyasae amorangereirie. Naboigo nachigete ase chikanisa chionsi. ⅩⅧ Monto onde bwensi orangerigwe na Nyasae ekero aikire kwaroka, tekora buna tarogeti, na monto onde bwensi orangerigwe otarogeti, taroka. ⅩⅨ Okwaroka tai gento, na ogotaroka tai gento, korende nokobwata amachiiko a Nyasae. ⅩⅩ Kera omonto tiga abe buna arenge ekero arangeretigwe. ⅩⅪ Inee! Igo kwarangeretigwe kore omosomba? Torora ng’a mbobe; korende koranyare gosiborwa, mbuya obe bo. ⅩⅫ Naki oyorangeretigwe ase Omonene kare omosomba, nomosibore ase Omonene. Naboigo noyorangeretigwe osiboire, nomosomba o Kristo. ⅩⅩⅢ Mokagorwa ase rigori, timoba abasomba b’abaanto. ⅩⅩⅣ Kera omonto, baminto, buna arenge ekero arangeretigwe, tiga aikaranse boigo ase Nyasae.
ⅩⅩⅤ Ase amang’ana ’abaiseke abekungi, tindi nogochika korwa ase Omonene, korende nabateebirie buna ndoche inche, buna oyonyorire amaabera korwa ase Omonene koba omwegenwa. ⅩⅩⅥ Narorire ng’a ring’ana eri nerigiya ase engencho y’emechando eye tore nero, ng’a mbuya omonto abe buna are. ⅩⅩⅦ Inee! Gwasibirwe ase omokungu? Torigia gosiborwa. Nosiboire? Torigia omokungu. ⅩⅩⅧ Korende koranyuome, tari ebibe gwakora; na omoiseke omwekungi karanyuomwe, tari ebibe akora. Korende abanto abwo nababe nemechando y’ense eye, na inche ninyanchete ng’a timosoa ase emechando eyio. ⅩⅩⅨ Korende baminto, nigo ngoteeba iga: Ekero getigarete negeke; gaki korwa bono baria bare nabakungu, tiga babe buna tibaabwati; ⅩⅩⅩ na baria bakorera babe buna tibari korera; na baria bagogete babe buna tibagogeti: na baria bakogora babe buna tibari na gento; ⅩⅩⅪ na baria bagokorera ense eye babe buna tibari koyekorera, ekiagera ense buna ere bono ngoeta ere.
ⅩⅩⅫ Korende nindigetie ng’a timoba nogwechanda. Oyotaranyuoma nigo arengereretie amang’ana ’Omonene, buna aragwenere Omonene: ⅩⅩⅩⅢ korende oyonyuomire nigo akorengereria amang’ana ’ense eye, buna aragwenere mokaye; ⅩⅩⅩⅣ igo obeire nogwatananekana. Omoiseke omwekungi nigo akorengereria amang’ana ’Omonene, buna aranyare koba omochenu ase omobere na ase omoika, korende oyonyuomirwe nigo akorengereria amang’ana ’ense, buna aragwenere omosacha oye. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Nateebire aya ase okobakonya; tari ng’a nkobabeekera nde egetango, korende ase engencho y’ayio abagwenerete, erinde monyare gokorera Omonene motang’usiri namang’ana ande.
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Korende monto onde bwensi kararore ng’a takorereti omoiseke oye omwekungi ayamogwenerete, na onye omoiseke oyio oeteire obuya bw’obosae bwaye, onye negwenerete, tiga omonto oyio akore buna arigetie; tari ebibe akora, amotige anyuomwe. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Korende oyotenenire noboremu ase enkoro yaye, na tari nokorigia, korende anyare kogambera okogania kwaye, naende orengereirie enkoro yaye ime amotige buna omoiseke oye omwekungi, nakore buya. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Gaki oyoranyuome omoiseke omwekungi nakore buya; na oyotamonyuome okorire buya kobua.
ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Omokungu nigo asibire ase omosacha oye ekero omosacha oye are moyo, korende omosacha oye karakwe, rirorio osiboirwe; nanyare konyuomwa na monto onde bwensi oyo aranche, korende nase Omonene oka. ⅩⅬ Korende anga mbuya kobua karabe buna are. Nigo ndoche inche; na inche narengereirie ng’a ninde n’Omoika o Nyasae.
