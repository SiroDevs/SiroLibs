Motogie Omonene nomogooko
(Ogoteera kw’ogoaka ng’a mbuya mono)
Ⅰ Inwe abanto b’ense yonsi,
moterere Omonene more nomochengo!
Ⅱ Mokorere Omonene more nomogooko,
moche ase obosio bwaye na komoterera amatera ’omochengo!
Ⅲ Manya ng’a Omonene nere Nyasae.
Nere ototongete, na intwe nabaye tore;
nigo tore abanto baye, ne ching’ondi chie riicho riaye.
 
Ⅳ Soa ase ebiita biaye na komoakera ng’a mbuya mono,
soa ase chigoora chiaye na komotogia!
Moakere ng’a mbuya mono,
motogie erieta riaye!
Ⅴ 100:5: 1 Amg 16:34; 2 Amg 5:13; 7:3; Ezra 3:11; Zab 106:1; 107:1; 118:1; 136:1; Yer 33:11Naki Omonene nomuya, na obwanchani bwaye nobwa kare na kare,
na obwegenwa bwaye nigo bogendererete ebiaare ase ebiaare.
