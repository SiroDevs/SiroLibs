Amang’ana igoro y’okobambwa gwa Kristo
Ⅰ Inche, ekero nachete ase more, baminto, tinachete kobaranderia oborori bwa Nyasae ase enchera y’obuya bwe chinkwana, gose ase obong’aini. Ⅱ Naki nigo narengereretie ng’a timanya ing’ana rinde rionsi ase more otatiga igoro ya Yeso Kristo, kobua mono okobambwa kwaye ase omosalaba. Ⅲ 2:3: Ogok 18:9Na inche nigo narenge amo nainwe ase oboreu na okoiroka, na okoiguswa. Ⅳ Enkwana yane na okorandia kwane tibiarenge amang’ana ay’okoebereria obong’aini bwa Mwanyabaanto, korende ase ekiorokererio ki’Omoika ne chinguru, Ⅴ erinde okwegena kwaino tikoba ase obong’aini bwa Mwanyabaanto korende ase chinguru chia Nyasae.
Obong’ani bwa Nyasae
Ⅵ Korende obong’aini mbore togoteeba ase egati y’abaikeranu, na borobwo tibori obong’aini bw’ense eye, gose obw’abagaambi b’ense eye abagendererete gochia gosira, Ⅶ korende nigo togokwana obong’aini bw’obobisi bwa Nyasae, obwo bwabisetwe, obwo Nyasae aroberetie korwa omochakano, erinde toegwe bobe obonene bwaito. Ⅷ Abagaambi b’ense eye tibabomanyete, nonya noyomo obo; naki onye mbabomanyete, anga tibaabambete Omonene bw’obonene; Ⅸ 2:9: Isaya 64:4korende buna eriikire,
“Aya eriso ritaroche, gose ogoto gotaigwete,
gose aya atarengereirie ase enkoro y’omonto,
ayio Nyasae aroiseretie abamwanchete.”
Ⅹ Korende Nyasae otoyaorokeirie ase Omoika oye, naki Omoika nigo okorigia onsi, nonya nobobisi bwa Nyasae. Ⅺ Naki ning’o ore ase Mwanyabaanto omanyete ebirengererio bi’omonto, otatiga omoika bw’omonto ore ime yaye? Naboigo nonde tari omanyete ebirengererio bia Nyasae otatiga Omoika o Nyasae. Ⅻ Na intwe titwanyorete omoika bw’ense eye, korende Omoika orwete ase Nyasae, erinde tonyare komanya ebiegwa ebitwaeirwe na Nyasae. ⅩⅢ Naro nigo tokoyakwana, tari ase amang’ana tworokererigwe ne chisemi chia Mwanyabaanto, korende aya tworokererigwe n’Omoika; igo togokwana amang’ana ’Omoika ase abare n’Omoika.
ⅩⅣ Nabo Mwanyabaanto ore ne chingencho chia kare, takonyora ebiegwa bi’Omoika o Nyasae, naki asare ere ebi nebiriri, gose takonyara kobimanya, ekiagera nigo bikomanyekana ase chinguru chi’Omoika. ⅩⅤ Omonto ore n’Omoika nigo akomanya onsi, korende ere takomanyigwa na monto onde. ⅩⅥ 2:16: Isaya 40:13“Naki ning’o omanyete ebirengererio bi’Omonene, erinde amworokererie?” Korende intwe ntore nebirengererio bia Kristo.
