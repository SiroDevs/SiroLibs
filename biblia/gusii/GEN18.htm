Aburaamu okorierwa eira naende konyora omwana
Ⅰ Omonene akaorokera Aburaamu ase emete enga buna Emealuni agwo Mamure, ekero Aburaamu aikaransete gesieri ki’eema yaye, engaki y’omobaso omoororo. Ⅱ 18:2: Abaibr 13:2Agwo akaimokereria amaiso aye igoro, akarora abanto batato bateneine bosio bwaye. Ekero abarora, akaminyoka korwa gesieri ki’eema, agachia kobarotota, agatung’ama inse, Ⅲ akabora: “Omonene one, onye nanyorire ogwancherwa ase obosio bwao, tong’etaania inche, omosomba oo. Ⅳ Tiga morenterwe amaache make, mwesibie amagoro, na motimokere aa ase ekiriiri ki’omote oyo. Ⅴ Inche imbarentere endagera inke morie, erio monyore chinguru; erio momanye kogenderera gochia ase mware kogenda, naki aase aiga mwaeta mbwone, inche omosomba oino.”
Bakamoteebia: “Gaki, kora buna gwateeba.”
Ⅵ Ase igo, Aburaamu akaayerera, agasoa eema ime agwo ase Sara arenge, akamoteebia: “Renta bwango obosie, ebirengo bitato, obokonye, oroisie emegati.” Ⅶ Erio Aburaamu akaminyoka getii ase riicho riaye, akarenta emori engiya, enoru, enterere, akayea omosomba oye, akamoteebia: “Yeroisie bwango, orente.” Ⅷ Akabogoria amaguta, na amabeere, ne chinyama chi’emori eria aroisetie, agaterekera abageni baria. Ere agatenena ase bare ensemo ase ekiriiri ki’omote, ekero baare koragera.
Ⅸ Barabwo bakamobooria: “Ng’ai Sara mokao are?”
Ere akabairaneria: “Neema ime are.”
Ⅹ 18:10: Abar 9:9Omonene akamoteebia: “Amaene, inche ning’irane asore omwaka ogocha engaki enga buna eye, na ninyore ng’a Sara mokao onyorire omwana omoisia.”
Ko erio Sara nigo ategererete gesieri ki’eema yareenge magega y’Omonene. Ⅺ Bono Aburaamu na Sara konya bagotire mono, baetanirie emiaka y’obosae, na Sara konya oeteire emiaka y’okonyora abana. Ⅻ 18:12: 1 Petr 3:6Ase ayio, Sara agaseka ase omoyo oye ime, ogoteeba: “Buna inde omogotu iga, na omosacha one nere ogotire, naki eranyarekane inyore omogooko oyio?”
ⅩⅢ Omonene akabooria Aburaamu: “Ninki Sara agosekera, ogoteeba: Inche amaene ninyibore omwana buna nagotire iga? ⅩⅣ 18:14: Luk 1:37Ing’ana ki rire rikong’u igo riramosinye Omonene? Engaki ndairane asore ase omwaka ogocha, engaki enga buna eye, ninyore ng’a Sara onyorire omwana omoisia.”
ⅩⅤ Sara agakana, agateeba: “Inche tinsegeti,” naki nigo noboete.
Omonene akamoteebia: “Taibo; korende aye ngwaseka.”
Aburaamu ogosabera abanto ba Sodomu
ⅩⅥ Erio abanto abwo bakaimoka korwa agwo, bakagenda, bakaba barigereretie gochia Sodomu, na Aburaamu akagenda amo nabarabwo, akabakooba. ⅩⅦ Omonene agateeba: “Inee! Imobise Aburaamu ring’ana ngochia gokora, ⅩⅧ ekero ndoche ng’a ngochia are koba egesaku ekenene gie chinguru, na ebisaku bionsi bi’ense inabisesenigwe goetera asare? ⅩⅨ Naki namochorire*18:19 namochorire: Ase Ekeiberania erieta ritumegete namomanyire, ng’a ere arue ogochika gochia ase abana baye na bonsi abagotoka enka yaye, na baria abarache koiborwa korwa magega yaye, babwatie enchera y’Omonene, bakore oboronge na ekeene, erinde Omonene aikeranerie Aburaamu, onsi aya amorierete eira.”
ⅩⅩ Naende Omonene agateeba: “Ekiagera ekerero gia Sodomu na Gomora nekenene, na ebibe biabo biamentekire mono, ⅩⅪ nigo ndaike gochia inse, indore onye bakorire amabe amange buna ekerero eke kiang’ikeire kegoteeba; onye taibo, nimanye ayio.”
ⅩⅫ Erio abanto baria bakarwa agwo, bakagenda gochia Sodomu, korende Aburaamu agatigara oteneine agwo ase obosio bw’Omonene. ⅩⅩⅢ Aburaamu agasuka gochia ase Omonene, akamobooria: “Inee! Amaene, nigo orasirie abanyene oboronge amo nabakori amabe? ⅩⅩⅣ Aande ase omochie oria abanyene oboronge emerongo etano mbakareo, nigo orayosirie, gose noyororere amaabera ase engencho y’abwo abanyene oboronge emerongo etano? ⅩⅩⅤ Yaya gaki teeba boigo, ekeene, nosirie abanyene oboronge nabakori amabe amo? Omonyene oboronge tagokorerwa buna abakori amabe! Inee! Aye Omogambi omonene bw’ense engima notige gokora ayare y’oboronge!”
ⅩⅩⅥ Omonene akamoteebia: “Inse konyora abanyene oboronge emerongo etano ase omochie oria o Sodomu, rirorio nigo ndatige gosiria omochie aria ase engencho y’abwo, abaronge.”
ⅩⅩⅦ Aburaamu akairaneria: “Rigereria, ningendererete gokwana n’Omonene, nonya inche inde orotu rw’amaroba, na eriibu bosa. ⅩⅩⅧ Aande ase baria emerongo etano baise koba batano tibaiyo, abanyene oboronge, Inee, nosarie omochie ekiagera batano tibaiyo?”
Nyasae akairaneria: “Tingosaria omochie, kondarore agwo emerongo ene, na batano.”
ⅩⅩⅨ Aburaamu akagenderera naende gokwana nere: “Aande baraisa kororekana rende emerongo ene?”
Nyasae akairaneria: “Ase engencho y’abwo emerongo ene, tingosaria omochie.”
ⅩⅩⅩ Aburaamu akabora: “Omonene, koranche, tobaisa kombea nainche, ningendererete gokwana naye. Aande barabaisa konyorekana rende emerongo etato?”
Omonene akairaneria: “Tingosaria omochie oria, inse konyora mbare emerongo etato aroro.”
ⅩⅩⅪ Aburaamu akabora: “Rora, ningendererete gokwana n’Omonene. Baise konyorekana rende emerongo ebere?”
Omonene akairaneria: “Ase engencho y’abwo emerongo ebere, tingosaria omochie.”
ⅩⅩⅫ Aburaamu akamobooria naende: “Omonene, tobaisa kombea, bono ndimo rioka ngokoboria, barachia konyorekana rende ikomi boka?”
Nyasae akairaneria: “Tingosaria omochie, inse konyora agwo ikomi boka.” ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Ekero Omonene akoorete gokwana na Aburaamu, akagenda; na Aburaamu akairana bwoye.
