Rutu ogokora emeremo ase omogondo o Boazi
Ⅰ Naomi nabwate omoamate oyomo ore korokwa Boazi, omonto omonda na bwesiko bw’eamate y’Elimeleki, omosacha oye. Ere nigo arenge omonto bwomong’uso, naende omonda. Ⅱ 2:2: Abal 19:9,10; Okoir 24:19Rutu, oria Omomoabu, agateebia Naomi, “Ntige ngende gochora ebigara ebitigari ase omogondo bw’omonto orang’e ribaga nkore bo.”
Naomi akamoiraneria, “Genda, mwana one!”
Ⅲ Ase igo Rutu akagenda ase omogondo gete, akaba ogochora ebigara ebitigari korwa magega y’abagesi. Na bono egatoka ng’a omogondo oyio nigo orenge oyo o Boazi, omonto oria bw’eamate y’Elimeleki.
Ⅳ Erio agwo Boazi agacha aroro korwa Bethlehemu, agakwania abagesi, ogoteeba, “Omonene abe amo nainwe!”
Nabarabwo bakairaneria, “Omonene agosesenie!”
Ⅴ Boazi akaboria omoteneneri bw’abagesi baye, “Inee! Omosubati oyo, noyo ng’o?”
Ⅵ Omoteneneri oria bw’abakori emeremo akamoiraneria, “Oyo nomosubati bw’Abamoabu, oria ochicha na Naomi korwa ase ense yabo. Ⅶ Nigo aboria ribaga ri’ogochora ebigara ebitigari korwa magega y’abagesi, na korwa mambia goika bono nigo agendererete gochora ebigara, taratimoka rinde otatiga bono ase engaki ensinini akendererete ase ekegutu keria.”
Ⅷ Erio Boazi agakwana na Rutu, akamoteebia, “Mwana one, ntegerera! Tobaisa kogenda gochora ebigara ebitigari ase omogondo omoao; obeere aa oka, obe kogokora emeremo na abaiseke aba. Ⅸ Origererie orore ase bakogesa obabwatie korwa magega! Nakanirie abanto baane tibakogochanda. Gokorang’a, ogende ase chinsiongo echio baichoirie amaache, otaae onywe!”
Ⅹ Rutu agatung’ama inse akaumama, akaboria Boazi, “Naki gwakora kwanyechandera na gonkorera buya iga, na inche nomomenyi inde aa?”
Ⅺ Boazi akamoiraneria, “Nateebigwe amang’ana onsi gwakoreire nyoko biara korwa ekero omosacha oo akwete. Namanyire buna gwatigire iso na nyoko, na ense yao kwaiboreretwe, ogaacha komenya nabaanto otamanyete agwo ritang’ani. Ⅻ Omonene agoakane ase aya gwakorire; ee, Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli, oyo gwatameire, erinde orendwe inse ye chimbaaba chiaye.”
ⅩⅢ Rutu akamoiraneria, “Omonene one, kwabeire omuya mono ase ’nde, kwandemirie ase ogokwana nobwororo nainche, omosomba oo, nonya tindenga’aini oyomo bw’abasomba bao abaiseke.”
ⅩⅣ Ekero abanto bachete koragera, Boazi agateebia Rutu, “Inchuo aa, orie omogati otongerie ase omosori ore nedivai.” Naboigo Rutu agaikaransa amo nabagesi baria, na Boazi akamoa ebigara bisambire. Rutu akaria, akaigota, naende eyende egatigara. ⅩⅤ Na ekero Rutu agendete gochora ebigara naende, Boazi agachiika abasomba baye, akabateebia, “Momotige achore ebigara nonya nase egati y’emesaima, timomokania. ⅩⅥ Naende momoruserie ebigara korwa ase emesaima, momotigere abichore, timobaisa komotanga.” ⅩⅦ Igo Rutu akagenderera gochora ebigara ase omogondo oria goika mogoroba, na ekero abibungete akanyora ng’a biaisanire ebitonga bibere.
ⅩⅧ Erio agetweka ebitonga biria, akairana gochia omochie, akaorokia ng’ina biara ebio achorete. Naende akamoa endagera eria yatigarete ekero ariete akaigota. ⅩⅨ Naomi akamobooria, “Ng’ai kware gochora ebigara reero? Nase omogondo o ng’o kware gokora emeremo? Asesenigwe omonto oyio ogwechandera kogokonya!”
Rutu agateebia Naomi ng’a nigo arenge gokora emeremo ase omogondo bw’omonto ore korokwa Boazi.
ⅩⅩ 2:20: Abal 25:25Naomi agateebia Rutu, moka momura oye, “Boazi oyio tiga asesenigwe n’Omonene, oyio ogoikerania chiira chiaye chi’obwanchani ase abwo bare moyo, na ase abwo bakuure.” Naende agateeba, “Omonto oyio nomonyamwando ominto, oyomo bw’abaamate baito.”
ⅩⅪ Naende Rutu, Omomoabu oria, akamoteebia, “Amo nayio ere nigo anteebia ng’a mbeere amo nabasomba baye goika bakoore rigesa riaye rionsi.”
ⅩⅫ Erio Naomi ageteebia Rutu, “Ee, omwana one, mbuya mono kobua asore okore emeremo nabaiseke abwo bakomokorera Boazi. Oise kogenda ase omogondo bw’omonto omoao, abanto bare aroro mbaabaise kogokorera bobe.”
ⅩⅩⅢ Ase ayio Rutu agakora emeremo amo nabaiseke baria, akaba ogochora ebigara goika bagakoora rigesa ri’esairi*2:23 Esairi: nendagera enga buna engano na eri’engano. Korende akagenderera komenya amo na ng’ina biara.
