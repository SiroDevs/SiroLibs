Petero na Kornelio
Ⅰ Ase Kaisaria narengeo omonto oyomo ore korokwa Kornelio, omotang’ani bw’abarwani rigana bw’ekeombe ekemo ki’abarwani kiare korokwa Abaitalia. Ⅱ Ere nigo arenge omonto omuya, nere amo n’abanto b’enyomba yaye bonsi nigo bamoirogete Nyasae. Nigo are koa abanto ebiegwa ebinge n’omoyo oyomo na kogenderera gosaba Nyasae botambe. Ⅲ Ang’e chinsa kianda iga chia mobaso akaba buna oy’okorota erio akarora omomalaika O Nyasae okomoochera, akamoteebia: “Kornelio!”
Ⅳ Akamorigereria mono, akairoka, akamobooria: “Naki Omonene?” Akamoiraneria: “Ogosaba kwao na ebiegwa biao biaikire igoro, na biabeire ekeinyorio ase obosio bwa Nyasae. Ⅴ Gaki, bono toma abanto Yopa barangerie Simioni, oyokorokwa Petero. Ⅵ Ere nomogeni bw’omonto oyomo okorokwa Simioni, omooroisia bw’amao; enyomba yaye nigo ere ang’e nengegu y’enyancha.”
Ⅶ Ekero omomalaika, oria okwanete nere, aimoka korwa aroro, Kornelio akarangeria abasomba baye babere na omorwani oyomo osigete Nyasae korwa ase baria bare komokorera botambe, Ⅷ akabateebia amang’ana onsi, erio akabatoma Yopa.
Ⅸ Rituko rikobwatia, ekero baare kogenda, kobaikire ang’e nomochie, Petero akariina kerama igoro gochia gosaba koreng’ana chinsa isano nemo chia mobaso. Ⅹ Akaba nenchara, akagania endagera arie. Korende ekero barenge koyeroisia, akabwatwa nobogondereria, Ⅺ akarora igoro amaanokire, na egento kegoika, kere buna egetambaa ekenene, kebwatire emekoba ene, kegoikigwa gocha inse. Ⅻ Ime yaye nigo biarengeo ebisaku bionsi bie ching’iti chire namagoro ane, ne ching’iti chi’orosana, na echikwagura, ne chinyoni chia igoro. ⅩⅢ Eriogi rikamocheera, rikamoteebia: “Petero, imoka onyenye orie!”
ⅩⅣ Korende Petero akabora: “Yay’Omonene, ekiagera tinana koria nonya ng’ake egento getachenegeti gose ekere neubi.”
ⅩⅤ Eriogi rikamochera eria kabere, rikamoteebia: “Eki Nyasae achenire tokeroka ng’a tikeri egechenu.” ⅩⅥ Eri rigakoreka ara gatato, na egento keria gekabogorigwa gochia igoro naende.
ⅩⅦ Ekero Petero arenge ne chitang’u‐tang’u ase omoyo oye ime ase okorora okwo aroche, engencho yaye inki, rora, abanto baria batometwe na Kornelio, kobaboririe enyomba ya Simioni, bagatenena geita. ⅩⅧ Bakarangeria neriogi bakabooria gose Simioni, oyokorokwa Petero, nao are ime.
ⅩⅨ Na Petero, karengereretie okorora koria, Omoika okamoteebia: “Rora, abanto batato bagokorigia. ⅩⅩ Imoka, oike, ogende amo nabarabwo, toba ne chitang’u‐tang’u ekiagera ninche nabatoma.” ⅩⅪ Petero agaika ase abanto baria, agateeba: “Ninche ere oyo mokorigia. Igo mwacha ase engencho ki?”
ⅩⅫ Bakamoteebia: “Nigo twatomirwe na Kornelio, omotang’ani bw’abarwani rigana, omonto omooronge, omoirogete Nyasae, oyogokwanwa buya negesaku gionsi ki’Abayahudi. Nateebigwa n’omomalaika omochenu ng’a atome abanto ase ore gokorangeria ogende bwoye, anyare koigwa amang’ana korwa ase ore.” ⅩⅩⅢ Erio Petero akabateebia basoe nyomba, babe abageni baye.
Rituko rikobwatia akaimoka akagenda amo nabarabwo, na abande bamwabo korwa Yopa bakagenda amo nere. ⅩⅩⅣ Rituko rigotunya erio bagasoa Kaisaria. Na Kornelio nigo arenge obaganyete, konya osangereirie eamate yaye amo nabanchi baye. ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero Petero achia gosoa omochie, Kornelio agasoka komoorotota, akagwa inse ase amagoro aye, akamosasiima. ⅩⅩⅥ Korende Petero akamoimokia, akamoteebia: “Tenena! Inche boigo Mwanyabaanto inde.” ⅩⅩⅦ Ekero are gokwana na Kornelio, agasoa ime, akanyora abanto abange basangererekaneteo. ⅩⅩⅧ Akabateebia: “Mwamanyire ng’a tari boronge omonto Omoyahudi abwatane nomonto ore bw’egesaku ekeao, nonya nokomochiera, korende Nyasae onyorokirie ng’a timoroka onde bwensi ng’a tari omochenu, gose tachenegeti. ⅩⅩⅨ Ase engencho eyio, ekero narangeretigwe ngacha, tinaanga. Nkoboria nde: Ing’ana ki mwandangereretie?”
ⅩⅩⅩ Kornelio akamoiraneria: “Reero nerituko ria kane, korwa ekero narenge gosaba nyomba mwane chinsa buna echi, chinsa kianda. Ani! Omonto nateneine bosio bwane obekeire chianga chikomeka‐meka. ⅩⅩⅪ Aganteebia: Kornelio, ogosaba kwao kwaigurwe, na ebiegwa biao biainyoirwe ase Nyasae. ⅩⅩⅫ Ase igo, toma abanto Yopa, orangerie Simioni, oyokorokwa Petero; ere nomogeni are nyomba mwa Simioni, omooroisia bw’amao, nigo amenyete ang’e nengegu y’enyancha. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Erio bwango ngatoma abanto orangerigwe, na aye gwakorire buya gwachire. Na bono twensi nao tore aiga ase obosio bwa Nyasae, tonyare koigwa aya onsi Omonene agochikiire gokwana.”
Ogokwana gwa Petero
ⅩⅩⅩⅣ 10:34: Okoir 10:17Erio Petero agachaaka gokwana, akabora: “Ase ekeene naikire komanya ng’a Nyasae tari nogwanchereria, ⅩⅩⅩⅤ korende ase kera egesaku omonto omoirogete na gokora oboronge nigo agwancherwa nere. ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Agatoma ring’ana eri ase Abaisraeli, akarandia amang’ana amaya ay’omorembe korwa ase Yeso Kristo, oyore Omonene o bonsi. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Inwe momanyete, ng’a ring’ana eri riaraaretigwe ase Boyahudi yonsi, rigachaakera Galili magega y’ebatiso eria Yohana arandetie, ⅩⅩⅩⅧ igoro ya Yeso o Nazareti, buna Nyasae amoete Omoika Omochenu ne chinguru, na buna ataarete na gokora emeremo emiya, na kogwenia bonsi abaregetwe na Saitani, ekiagera Nyasae nigo arenge amo nere. ⅩⅩⅩⅨ Na intwe nigo tore kirori b’amang’ana onsi aya arenge gokora ase ense y’Abayahudi na ime ya Yerusalemu. Bakamoita, bakamosunga ase omote, ⅩⅬ korende Nyasae akamobokia ase rituko ria gatato, akamwanchera kooroka, ⅩⅬⅠ tari ase abanto bonsi, korende nasetore, abatwachoretwe na Nyasae tobe kirori. Intwe nigo tware koria na konywa amo nere magega y’okoboka kwaye. ⅩⅬⅡ Agatochiika toranderie abanto, na tobe kirori ng’a nere ochoretwe na Nyasae abe omogambi bw’abare moyo na abakuure. ⅩⅬⅢ Oyo ababani bonsi baberete kirori, ng’a onde bwensi omwegenire nigo aranyore okwaberwa kw’ebibe ase erieta riaye.”
Abanto b’Ebisaku bakonyora Omoika Omochenu na ebatiso
ⅩⅬⅣ Ekero Petero arenge gokwana aya Omoika Omochenu ogaikera bonsi abaigwete amang’ana aya. ⅩⅬⅤ Abegenete korwa ase abaarogete abachete amo na Petero, bagakumia, ekiagera nonya n’abanto b’Ebisaku banyorire ekeegwa ki’Omoika Omochenu. ⅩⅬⅥ Naki baabaigwete bagokwana ase emenwa ao ao, bagotogia Nyasae. Erio Petero akabora, ⅩⅬⅦ “Inee! Omonto nanyare kobatanga abanto aba, tibaabatiswa ase amache? Abanto abwo nabarabwo banyorire Omoika Omochenu koreng’ana buna intwe?” ⅩⅬⅧ Agachiika baabatiswe ase erieta ria Yeso Kristo. Erio bakamobooria amente komenya amo nabarabwo amatuko atari make.
