ⅭⅩⅬⅢ
Ogosaba gw’okonyora ogokonywa
(Ogoteera gwa Daudi)
Ⅰ Aye Omonene, oigwe ogosaba kwane;
ase engencho y’oboronge bwao otegerere ogosorora kwane,
ong’iranerie ase engencho y’obwegenwa bwao.
Ⅱ 143:2: Abar 3:20; Abagl 2:16Tobaisa kong’ira inche omosomba oo ase ekegambero,
ekiagera ase abwo bare moyo onde tari ore omooronge asore.
 
Ⅲ Omobisa one obeire okorigia omoyo one;
obeire ogontiringera inse ase amaroba,
ong’ikaransirie ase omosunte,
nabeire buna omonto okwete kare.
Ⅳ Ase ayio omoika ore ime yane oborirwe chinguru,
na enkoro yane yairianyekire.
 
Ⅴ Nigo ’nkoinyora amatuko a kare,
na korengereria igoro y’ogokora kwao gwonsi,
naba ’nkona‐koiranerera emeremo y’amaboko ao.
Ⅵ Nigo ’nkorambora amaboko aane goocha asore,
omoyo one nigo ore nerang’o asore,
buna riroba rirang’ete embura.
 
Ⅶ Aye Omonene, ing’iranerie bwango!
Omoika ’nkong’erera ore!
Tombisa obosio bwao
mbaise koba buna abwo bakogeenda ase abakuure.
Ⅷ Kera emambia omanyie obwanchani bwao bogenderere botambe,
ekiagera naye ’nyegenete.
Inyorokererie enchera ’ngwenerete kobwatia,
ekiagera narure omoyo one asore.
 
Ⅸ Aye Omonene, ’ntoorie korwa ase ababisa baane;
nagotameire ombise.
Ⅹ Inyorokererie imanye gokora ogwancha kwao,
ekiagera naye ore Nyasae one!
Tiga Omoika oo omuya ondaae ase enchera ere endonge.
 
Ⅺ Aye Omonene, ing’a obogima oboyia ase engencho y’erieta riao;
na ase engencho y’oboronge bwao
rusia omoyo one korwa ase emechando.
Ⅻ Ase engencho y’obwanchani bwao bwa botambe, siria ababisa baane;
basirie kegima bonsi abagochaanda omoyo one,
ekiagera inche nigo ’nde omosomba oo.
