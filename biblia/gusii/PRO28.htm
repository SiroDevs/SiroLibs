ⅩⅩⅧ
Ⅰ Abakori amabe nigo bagotama nonya onde tari kobabwatia, korende abanyaboronge nigo bare abaremu buna chindo chinsae.
Ⅱ Ekero ense yasaririe nigo ekoba n’abagaambi abange, korende eye ebwate abanto bare nobong’aini na okomanya, obogambi nigo bokogeenderera gotenena bokong’u.
Ⅲ Omonto omotaka ore n’okobua ogokorera abataka bande bobe ase okobaurereria, nigo anga buna embura enyinge egosaria chindagera.
Ⅳ Abanto batigire amachiiko nigo bagotogia abakori amabe, korende abarendete amachiiko nigo bakoamererania nabwo.
Ⅴ Abakori amabe tibamanyeti ayare amaronge, korende abwo bakomorigia Omonene nigo bayamanyete onsi.
Ⅵ Omonto omotaka okobwatia oboronge nigo are omuya kobua omonda ore omokorekanu.
Ⅶ Omwana orendete amachiiko nigo are omong’aini, korende oyore omogisangio bwa baria bobe nigo akorenta obosooku ase ise.
Ⅷ Omonto okomenta chinibo chiaye ase enchera etari ey’ekeene ase okogania ebitoki, oyio nigo agochisangereria chibe echi’omonto okororera abataka amaabera.
Ⅸ Omonto okoonchora ogoto kwaye taigwera amachiiko, nonya n’ogosaba kwaye nigo kore okogechia.
Ⅹ Omonto ogosiria abare ne chinkoro chindonge, na kobaraa ase enchera ende, oyio nagwe ase eirima eria aremire ere omonyene, korende abwo batari na komocha ’mbanyore omwando omuya.
Ⅺ Omonto omonda nigo akwerengereria ng’a nomong’aini are, korende omonto omotaka ore n’okomanya nigo aramotuke amomanye.
Ⅻ Ekero abanyene oboronge babuire, okoiririata okonge nigo gokobao, korende ekero abakori amabe bakobua, abanto nigo bakwebisa.
ⅩⅢ Omonto okobisa ebibe biaye tagosesenigwa, korende oyo okobiorokia na kobitiga narorerwe amaabera.
ⅩⅣ Nasesenirie omonto omoirogete Omonene botambe, korende oyo okong’etie enkoro yaye nanyore emechando eramosirie.
ⅩⅤ Omogambi omokaayayu okogambera abanto abataka nigo anga buna endo ekoruma, gose edubu yagechiigwe.
ⅩⅥ Omogambi otari n’obong’aini nigo are omochaandi omotindi, korende oria ogechete okonyora ebitooki bitari bi’oboronge, nigo akogeenderera kogamba ase amatuko amaange.
ⅩⅦ Onye omonto oritoereirwe n’okomocha kw’ogoitera amanyinga y’omonto onde, tiga abe okona‐gotama goika akwe, na onde tagokoonya omonto oyio.
ⅩⅧ Omonto otari na komocha ase chiinchera chiaye natoorigwe; korende oyore omokorekanu ase chiinchera chiaye nasire ase eyemo y’echio.
ⅩⅨ Omonto ogokora emeremo ase omogondo oye ’nanyore endagera enyinge, korende oyokobwatia emeroberio etagwenereti nigo arabe omotaka omoraboku.
ⅩⅩ Omonto ore n’enkoro endonge ’nanyore ogosesenigwa okonge, korende oyo okoayerera ase okoba omonda takomocha konyora egesusuro.
ⅩⅪ Ase engencho y’agasamunye k’omogati omonto nigo agwesaria ase ogwanchereria. Korende, tari buya gokora ogwanchereria nonya ’ng’ake.
ⅩⅫ Omonto omwegimeria nigo akoayerera koba omonda, gose tari korengereria ng’a obotaka ’mbomonyooore.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omonto ogotogonyera oyo oiranire magega ase okomoorongeyia ’nanyoore ogwancherwa kobua oria okomong’aina namang’ana amansu.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omonto okoura ise gose ng’ina enibo yabo, erio oteeba, “Ebi tibiri ebibe,” oyio nigo are omogisangio bw’oyogosiria.
ⅩⅩⅤ Omonto ore n’endamwamu nigo agoseegeta eriomana, korende oyosemeretie Omonene ’natenenkigwe.
ⅩⅩⅥ Oyobwesemeretie ere omonyene, oyio nomoriri, korende oyo okobwatia obong’aini natoorigwe.
ⅩⅩⅦ Omonto ogokoonya abataka takoremerwa, korende oyo ogotuba amaiso aye ng’a tarora abataka ’nanyore okoragererigwa okonge.
ⅩⅩⅧ Ekero abakori amabe bakogamba, abanto nigo bakwebisa, korende ekero basiriigwe, abanyaboronge nigo bakomentekana.
