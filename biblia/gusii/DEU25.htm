ⅩⅩⅤ
Ⅰ “Onye abanto babere babwate eriomana ase egati yabo, rirorio bageende ase ekegaambero, erio abagaambi bagaambe ekiina ekio, na oyio ore n’oboronge aegwe oboronge bwaye, na oria ore n’okomocha abarerwe okomocha kwaye. Ⅱ Keraabe ng’a oria omomochi nigo agwenerete goakwa ebiranya, omogambi oria amochiike aramame inse agwo ase obosio bwaye na goakwa ebiranya koreng’ana buna okomocha kwaye kore. Ⅲ 25:3: 2 Abakr 11:24Omonto oyio aakwe ebiranya emerongo ene, korende tari goetania ebio; aise kogenderera goakwa goetania, nigo arasookigwe ase obosio bwao. Takoegwa ogoakwa goetania okwo gochiikire.
Ⅳ 25:4: 1 Abakr 9:9; 1 Tim 5:18“Tobaisa kobeekera eng’ombe ekeokio ekero ekoora endagera.
Buna omonto agwenerete gokora ekero momura omwabo akuure
Ⅴ 25:5: Mat 22:24; Mar 12:19; Luk 20:28“Onye abamura babere ba ise omo bamenyete aase aamo, na oyomo akwe, nere tabwate omwana, omokungu bw’omonto oria takonyuomwa n’omonto bw’eamate eng’ao, korende omomura omwabo omosacha oye nere oratware omokungu oria abe ribaga ria momura omwabo. Ⅵ Omwana omoisia omotangi omokungu oria araibore, arokwe erieta ri’oyomwabo ise oria okwete. Erio nario erieta riaye ritabaise gosira korwa ase Abaisraeli. Ⅶ 25:7: Rutu 4:7,8Korende onye omonto oria okwanga gotwara moka momura omwabo, rirorio omokungu oria agende ase abagaaka bare agwo ase egeita ki’omochie, akwane amang’ana aya: Omomura omwabo omosacha one bwangire goteneneria oyomwabo egesaku, erinde erieta riaye tirisira korwa ase Abaisraeli.
Ⅷ “Erio abagaaka baria b’omochie barangerie omonto oria, bakwane nere, na karagenderere gokaana ng’a tatageti gotwara omokungu oria, Ⅸ erio omokungu oria omoboraka aike ang’e ase omomura oria omwabo omosacha oye, asomore egekooroto korwa ase okogoro kw’omonto oria agwo ase amasio y’abagaaka, naende amotwere amate ase obosio bwaye, akwane: Nabo arakorwe omonto onde bwensi okwanga goteneneria omomura omwabo egesaku. Ⅹ Eamate y’omonto oyio emanyekane ase Abaisraeli, buna eamate y’omonto oyio osomoretwe egekooroto korwa ase okogoro kwaye.
Amachiiko ande
Ⅺ “Ekero abasacha babere bakorwana, na omokungu bw’omosaacha oyomo achiche gokoonya omosacha oye, na omokungu oria arambore okoboko abwate ekiimo ki’obosaacha ki’omonto oria onde, Ⅻ timororera omokungu oria amaabera; goika mobutore okoboko kwaye.
ⅩⅢ 25:13: Abal 19:35,36“Timobaisa koba n’ebirengo bibere ase chisaaro chiaino, ekemo ekenene na ekende egeke. ⅩⅣ Timobaisa gotwara ebimunu bikorenga bibere ase chinyomba chiaino, ekemo ekenene, na ekende egeke. ⅩⅤ Mokorere ebirengo bi’ekeene, naende ebiisaine bi’okomanya oborito na obonge; erio nario amatuko aino arabe amaange ase ense eri’Omonene Nyasae oino, agochia kobaa. ⅩⅥ Abanto bonsi bagokora oborianania buna obwo, nigo bare okogeechia ase Omonene Nyasae oino.
Amachiiko ay’ogoita Abaameleki
ⅩⅦ 25:17: Okong’ 17:8-14; 1 Sam 15:2-9“Moinyore aya Abaameleki baabakorerete ekero mwarenge ase orogeendo korwa Misiri. ⅩⅧ Barabwo tibamoirogete Nyasae. Ase ayio bagaacha, bakabarwania na goita abanto abatigarete magega konya bagoyire na korosa. ⅩⅨ Ase ayio, n’ebe ekero Omonene Nyasae oino, abaeire ense eria ebe omwando oino, naende ekero abaeire obotoereru korwa ase ababisa baino bonsi baabaetanaine, goika mosirie Abaameleki bonsi, tibainyorwa nonya ng’ake inse y’erioba. Timobaisa kweba amang’ana ayio.
