Ogosaba gw’okonyora obokonyi ase engaki y’emechando
(Ogoteera gwa Daudi)
Ⅰ 6:1: Zab 38:1Omonene, tontogonyera ekero ore nendamwamu;
tong’a egesusuro gi’okondongeyia ekero ore nekeririanda.
Ⅱ Omonene, ndorera amaabera, ekiagera ’nde omonyanyuru.
Aye Omonene, ’ngwenie, ekiagera amauga aane nigo akonyegerigwa.
Ⅲ Omoyo one ochandekire nomoondoko.
Omonene, ngoika ririri orenane otarankonya?
 
Ⅳ Aye Omonene, ing’iranere omboorie!
Ontoorie ase engencho y’obwanchani bwao botari koera.
Ⅴ Ase abwo bakuure onde tari oranyare gokoinyora.
Inee! Ning’o oranyare kogotogia korwa aase abakuure?
 
Ⅵ Nanyarekire ase okwerera kwane kw’obororo,
kera obotuko nigo ngoteremia namariga ase oborere bwane,
ebirarero biane biaichoirwe namariga.
Ⅶ Obosio bwane bwomire pa ase engencho y’emechando,
bwareeire ase engencho y’ababisa baane bonsi.
 
Ⅷ 6:8: Mat 7:23; Luk 13:27Inwe mwensi abakori amabe, karue
asende ekiagera Omonene oigure eriogi ria ebirero biane.
Ⅸ Omonene oigure okoboria kwane gw’okonyora amaabera,
nere bwancheire ogosaba kwane.
Ⅹ Ababisa baane bonsi mbarore obosooku,
bagoswe na koondokigwa mono kegima,
mbairane gochia magega bwango bare nobosooku.
