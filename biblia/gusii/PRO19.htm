ⅩⅨ
Ⅰ Omonto omotaka ore omwegenwa nigo are omuya kobua oyore omong’ainereria ase chinkwana chiaye naende omoriri.
Ⅱ Tari buya omonto aborwe okomanya ase omoyo oye, na oy’okegeenda bwango, nigo agosira.
Ⅲ Ase oboriri bw’omonto nigo agwesiria ase chiinchera chiaye omonyene, erio enkoro y’omonto oyio yamomochia Omonene.
Ⅳ Omonto omonda nigo akonyora abasani abange, korende omotaka nigo akwangwa nomosani oye.
Ⅴ Kirori bw’oborimo takomocha konyora egesusuro, na omonto ogokwana oborimo takomocha konyora egesusuro kiaye.
Ⅵ Abanto abange nigo bakorigia ogwanchwa na abanene, na abange nigo bakoba abasani b’omonto okorua ebiegwa n’omoyo omorabu.
Ⅶ Omonto omotaka nigo akogechwa nabaanto bamwabo bonsi, gose tikori ogokumia ng’a abasani baye bamotigire. Nigo egenete amang’ana abo, ko aba bosa ase are.
Ⅷ Omonto okonyora obong’aini, oyio nere obwanchire obogima bwaye, na oyo okorenda obomanyi bwaye nanyore amaya.
Ⅸ Kirori bw’oborimo takomocha konyora egesusuro, na oyo ogokwana oborimo goika asire.
Ⅹ Tari buya omonto omoriri amenye ase obogima bw’okwegokia; onye ere bo, tari buya nonya ng’ake ng’a omosomba agaambere abagaambi.
Ⅺ Obong’aini bw’omonto nigo bokogera oremereria okogechigwa, na okoombia okomocha nigo kore obonene bwaye.
Ⅻ Ekero omorwoti abeire nekeririanda nigo anga buna endo ekoruma, korende ogwancherwa kwaye nigo konga buna riime rire ase obonyansi.
ⅩⅢ Omwana omomura omoriri nigo akorentera ise amabe, na omokungu okona‐kwomana nigo anga buna amatoima y’embura akona‐gotonya.
ⅩⅣ Chinyomba ne chinibo nigo chire omwando abana bakonyora korwa ase abaise korende omokungu omong’aini nigo are ekeegwa korwa ase Omonene.
ⅩⅤ Obworo nigo bokorentera omonto chitoro chindito, na omonto buna oyio goika abe nenchara.
ⅩⅥ Omonto orendete amachiiko, oyio nigo arendete obogima bwaye, korende oyo otari korenda chiinchera chiaye nigo arakwe.
ⅩⅦ Omonto okororera omotaka amaabera, oyio nigo akorua singo ase Omonene, n’Omonene ’namoakane ase ogokora okwo kwaye.
ⅩⅧ Orokereria omwana oo omomura ase okomorongeyia ekero ogosemeria koreo, gose tobaisa komoganeria amakweri.
ⅩⅨ Omonto ore n’ekeririanda, ekeene goika anyore egesusuro; oise komotanga kagokora bobe nigo okomoseegeta.
ⅩⅩ Igwera ogosemigwa naende bwanche okworokererigwa, igo nario oranyore obong’aini boragokoonye ase chingaki chire bosio.
ⅩⅪ Emeroberio emenge nere ase enkoro y’omonto, korende nonya naboigo emeroberio y’Omonene nero eraikerane.
ⅩⅫ Okogania kw’omonto nigo gokworokia obuya bwaye, na omonto omotaka nigo are omuya mono kobua omonyaborimo.
ⅩⅩⅢ Okomoiroka Omonene nigo gokorenta obogima, na omonto ore nokoiroka okwo nigo agoisaneka, gose amabe takomonyoora.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omonto omworo nigo akobeeka okoboko kwaye ase esani y’endagera, gose tagokoimokia gocha ase omonwa oye.
ⅩⅩⅤ Aka omonto omonyacheche, na abanto batamanyeti amaange mbeorokererie korwa ase ayio; togonyera omonto ore n’okomanya, ere nigo aranyore chiseemi.
ⅩⅩⅥ Omwana ore omotindi ase ise na ogoseria ng’ina, nigo akorenta obosooku na ogochaaya.
ⅩⅩⅦ Omwana one, onye tokoigwera amorokererio nigo orasire korwa ase amang’ana akorenta chiseemi.
ⅩⅩⅧ Kirori omobe nigo agochecheria oboronge, na omonwa bw’omokori amabe nigo ogworerete ebibe.
ⅩⅩⅨ Ekiina ki’abanyacheche nigo kenachire, na abariri ’mbang’wanigwe ebiranya ase emegongo yabo.
