Nyasae okoorokera Sulemani naende
(2 Amg 7:11-22)
Ⅰ Ekero Sulemani konya akoorire koagacha enyomba y’Omonene, na enyomba y’oborwoti, na aande onsi ase anchete koagacha, Ⅱ 9:2: 1 Abar 3:5; 2 Amg 1:7Omonene akamoorokera eria kabere buna amoorokerete agwo Gibeoni ritang’ani. Ⅲ Omonene akamoteebia, “Naigure ogosaba kwao na okoboria kwao kw’oboitongo. Naatananire na gochena enyomba eye kwaagachire, nabekire erieta riane ribe aroro kare na kare. Amaiso aane na enkoro yane mbibe aroro ase chingaki chionsi. Ⅳ Korabwatie chinchera chiane, buna Daudi iso achibwatetie, ase enkoro endonge na ase obwegenwa; na onye kogokora onsi aya nagochikiire, na kobwata emeroberio yonsi echiikire; Ⅴ 9:5: 1 Abar 2:4rirorio inche ninkong’ie ekerogo kiao ki’oborwoti igoro ase Abaisraeli kare na kare, buna narierete Daudi iso eira, ngateeba: Takobora nonya ng’ake korwa ase ororeeria rwao omonto oraikaranse ase ekerogo ki’oborwoti ki’Abaisraeli. Ⅵ Korende inwe na abanto be chinderia chiaino komoreonchore na montige, naende timorenda amachiiko aane na emeroberio yane nabaeire, korende mogende gokorera chinyasae chinde na gochisasiima, Ⅶ rirorio ninsirie Abaisraeli korwa ase ense eria nabaeire, na enyomba eye naatananire na koyechena ase engencho y’erieta riane ninyetige, tinkoyerigereria namaiso aane. Abaisraeli nababe omorabaaro ne ring’ana rigokwanwa nabaanto b’ebisaku bionsi. Ⅷ 9:8: 2 Abar 25:9; 2 Amg 36:19Enyomba eye nebe rituuri riatagokire, na kera omonto ogoeta aase agwo naichanigwe na gokumia, na ekero monto onde akoboria: Nase ki Omonene akorire ayaiga ase ense eye na ase enyomba eye? Ⅸ Nigo barairanerie: Nekiagera barabwo batigete Omonene, Nyasae obo, oria orusetie chisokoro chiabo korwa ase ense ya Misiri, naende bakabwatana ne chinyasae ching’ao, bagachisasiima na gochikorera. Amang’ana ayio agerire Omonene orentire amabe aya onsi igoro ase bare.”
Sulemani okoa Hiramu emechie
(2 Amg 8:1-2)
Ⅹ Sulemani akaira emiaka emerongo ebere ase okoagacha chinyomba chiria ibere, enyomba y’Omonene na ey’oborwoti. Ⅺ Hiramu, omorwoti o Turo, konya oeire Sulemani chibao chi’emecharake, na echi’emetarakwa, amo ne chitaabu ase ekerengo aganetie. Magega y’ayio omorwoti Sulemani akaa Hiramu emechie emerongo ebere yarenge ase ense ya Galili. Ⅻ Korende ekero Hiramu achete korwa Turo korora emechie eria, tiyamogogetie. ⅩⅢ Akabora: “Emechie eye kwang’eire, oyominto, naki ere?” Ase eyio igo ekorokwa ense ya Kabuli goika reero iga. ⅩⅣ Hiramu konya otomeire omorwoti chitaabu chikiro chilifu inye (4,000).
Amang’ana ande Sulemani akorete
(1 Amg 8:3-18)
ⅩⅤ Oyo noro omooroberio bw’emeremo Sulemani abeetereretie abanto bakore: Akababeeka koagacha enyomba y’Omonene, na enyomba yaye omonyene, na Milo, na orwaki rwaetanaine Yerusalemu, na emechie ya Hasori, na Megido, na Geseri, ⅩⅥ (Farao, omorwoti o Misiri, konya ochire korwania Geseri na koyosakora, naende akayosamba nomorero. Agaita abanto ba Kaanani baria bamenyete aroro, na koyorua buna ekeegwa ki’ogosokia omoiseke oye abe moga Sulemani. ⅩⅦ Bono Sulemani akaagacha Geseri naende). Boigo akaagacha Beti‐Horoni ya maate, ⅩⅧ na Baalati, na Tamari ore ase erooro, ase ense eria, ⅩⅨ na emechie yonsi ye chikungo, eria Sulemani abwate, na emechie ye chigari chiaye, na eye chibarasi, na oboagacho bonde bwonsi Sulemani aganetie koagacha agwo Yerusalemu, na agwo Lebanoni na ase ense yonsi y’oborwoti bwaye. ⅩⅩ Abanto aba Sulemani abetereretie gokora emeremo, nigo baarenge abe chinderia chi’abaanto b’ense ya Kaanani, abwo Abaisraeli batanyarete kobua na gosiria kegima. ⅩⅪ Abanto abwo tibaarenge Abaisraeli. Nigo baarenge Abaamori, na Abahiti, na Abaperisi, na Abahivi, na Abayebusi. Abanto abwo bakagenderera koba abasomba, nabo bare goika reero iga. ⅩⅫ Korende Sulemani takorete Abaisraeli bakaba abasomba. Barabwo nigo baarenge abarwani b’esegi, na abakoonyi b’oborwoti, na abachiki baye b’abarwani, na abatang’ani b’abarwani, na abachiki baye be chigari ne chibarasi chikoriinwa.
ⅩⅩⅢ Abateneneri abanene igoro y’emeremo ya Sulemani nigo baarenge abanto amagana atano, na emerongo etano; abwo nigo batenenerete abanto baarenge gokora emeremo eria.
ⅩⅩⅣ Korende mosubati o Farao akarua ase Omochie o Daudi, agatiira gochia komenya ase enyomba yaye eria Sulemani konya amoagacheire; magega y’ayio Sulemani akaagacha Milo.
ⅩⅩⅤ 9:25: Okong’ 23:17; 34:23; Okoir 16:16Ara gatato ase omwaka Sulemani nigo arenge korua ebing’wanso bi’ogosambwa na ebing’wanso bi’omorembe ase egesasiimero keria aagacherete Omonene. Boigo aba ogosamba ubani ase Omonene. Ase igo agakoora emeremo y’okoagacha enyomba y’Omonene.
ⅩⅩⅥ Omorwoti Sulemani akaroisia chimeeri agwo Esioni‐Geberi ere ang’e na Eloti, ase omosomo bw’Enyancha Embariri, ase ense ya Edomu. ⅩⅩⅦ Hiramu agatoma abanto baye baria bamanyete emeremo ye chinyancha, bagasang’ana nabaanto ba Sulemani. ⅩⅩⅧ Barabwo bakagenda goika Ofiri, bakarenta korwa aroro chitaabu, chikiro chilifu ikomi na inye, na amagana atano (14,500), bakairera omorwoti Sulemani.
