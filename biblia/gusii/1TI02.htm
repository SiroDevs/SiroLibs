Okworokereria igoro y’ogosaba
Ⅰ Gaaki, ritang’ani ase amang’ana onsi nabasoroire ng’a okoboria, na ogosaba, na okogaramera amo nokoiraneria Nyasae ng’a mbuya mono, bikoreke ase engencho y’abanto bonsi, Ⅱ ase engencho y’abarwoti, na bonsi abaeire okobua, erinde tonyaare koba nobogima bw’oboitongo na obw’omorembe, na ase amang’ana onsi koba ne chingeencho chi’obonyasae, na echi’ogosikana. Ⅲ Eri nerigiya, naende ndiancheire ase obosio bwa Nyasae, Omotooria oito, Ⅳ oyorigetie abanto bonsi batoorigwe, banyaare komanya onsi ayare ay’ekeene. Ⅴ Inaki Nyasae noyoomo, na omobwatania gati ya Nyasae na Mwanyabaanto noyoomo, omonto oria Kristo Yeso Ⅵ ere oyobweruete omonyene koba embooria ase engencho ya bonsi. Igoro y’ayio oborori borentwe ekero engaki yaye eraike. Ⅶ 2:7: 2 Tim 1:11Ase engencho y’ayio nkaragwa imbe omworokia, na omotomwa, erinde inyorokie abanto b’Ebisaku amang’ana y’okwegena na ekeene; nigo ngokwana ekeene, tindi gokwana oborimo.
Ⅷ Nigo nganetie abasacha basabe kera aase, bakoimokia amaboko amachenu, batari nendamwamu, gose na tang’u‐tang’u. Ⅸ 2:9: 1 Petr 3:3Naboigo nabakungu beboyie chianga chi’ogosika, babe aberiiti na abaya, tari abakomina chituukia, gose kobeeka etaabu ne chiruuru, gose chianga chie rigori rinene, Ⅹ korende bakore ogokora okuya, buna egwenerete abakungu abakoorokia ng’a mbare ne chingeencho chi’obonyasae. Ⅺ Tiga omokungu eorokererie ase ogokira, na ase ogosika gwonsi. Ⅻ Ting’eti omokungu ribaga ri’okworokereria, gose kogaambera omosacha, korende nemogwenerete akire, ⅩⅢ 2:13: a Omoch 2:7; b Omoch 2:21,22ekiagera Adamu nere otongetwe ritang’ani, na magega yaye Hawa. ⅩⅣ 2:14: Omoch 3:1-6Na Adamu tang’ainetwe, korende nomokuungu ong’ainetwe, na igo akaba omomochi. ⅩⅤ Korende omokungu natoorigwe goetera ase okoibora abana onye okogeenderera ase okwegena, na obwanchani, na obochenu amo nobweriiti.
