Omonene okobea Abaisraeli
Ⅰ Inwe Abaisraeli, igwa ring’ana ri’Omonene, ekiagera obeeigwe nabamenyete ase ense eye: “Obwegenwa gose obwanchani tibori aroro, na abanto b’ense eye tibamomanyeti Nyasae. Ⅱ Nigo bagotiana na gokwana oborimo; nigo bagoita na koiba, naende bakora obotomani. Nigo bagosaria amachiiko onsi, na ogoiterwa kw’amanyinga nigo gokobwatigwa nokonde. Ⅲ Ase engencho eyio ense nigo ekorera ase okweumia, na kera omonto omenyeteo nigo achandegete na kogoya; boigo ching’iti chi’orosana, ne chinyoni, ne chinswe ngokwa chire.
Omonene okobea abakuani
Ⅳ “Monto onde tabaisa koba omoengereria, gose monto onde tasoera onde. Nonya nabo inwe nigo mogosoera abakuani. Ⅴ Aye nogochwa mobaso na botuko, na omobani agochwe amo naye. Inche ninsirie nyoko. Ⅵ Abanto baane nigo bagosirigwa, ekiagera batari nokomanya. Ekiagera aye kwangire komanya inche, nainche ninkwange, toba Omokuani one. Ekiagera kwangire koigwera amachiiko aane inche Nyasae oo, boigo nainche ninyange abana bao.
Ⅶ “Buna abakuani bagendererete kobucha, naboigo bagendererete gokora ebibe ase ’nde. Ase ayio ning’onchore obonene bwabo bobe obosooku. Ⅷ Nigo bakwerageria ebibe bi’abaanto baane, nigo bakogania bakore obobe. Ⅸ Buna eraabe ase abanto, naboigo eraabe nase Omokuani. Nindue egesusuro ase bare bonsi ase engencho ye chinchera chiabo, nimbaakane koreng’ana nogokora kwabo. Ⅹ Mbaragere, korende tibakoigota; mbabe bagokora obonyaka, korende tibakomentekana, ekiagera bantigire inche Omonene, na baonchokeire chinyasae ching’ao.
Omonene okwanga ogosasimwa kw’emegwekano
Ⅺ “Obotomani, na edivai, na ekenyugwa kegotindia, nigo bikoura abanto okomanya kwabo. Ⅻ Abanto baane nigo bakorigia ogosemigwa korwa ase ekeengwe ki’omote, na enyimbo yabo nigo ekobaorokeria aya baganetie komanya. Omoika bw’obotomani obasiririe, barabwo batigire Nyasae obo bachiire gotayaya. ⅩⅢ Nigo bakoruera ebing’wanso ase ebitunwa igoro, na basambera ubani ase ebigoro, inse y’emete ekorokwa Emialuni, na inse y’emete ekorokwa emesobeti, na ebirachuoki, ekiagera agwo nao ebiriri ebiya bire.
“Ase ayio, abaiseke baino nigo bagokora obonyaka, na baka bamura baino nigo bagokora obotomani. ⅩⅣ Ekero abaiseke baino barabe bagokora obonyaka na korua ebing’wanso, na baka bamura baino barabe bagokora obotomani, tinkobaa egesusuro, ekiagera abasacha abanyene nigo bakogenda ao bakora obonyaka nabatayayi be chie Hekalu, na abanto batari nokomanya goika basirigwe kegima.
ⅩⅤ “Inwe Abaisraeli, nonya mogokora obotomani, timobaisa gotiga Abayuda bakore ogosaria okwo. Timobaisa kogenda Giligali, gose gotiira gochia ase Beti‐Aveni,*4:15 Beti‐Aveni: engencho yaye: enyomba y’obobe gose timobaisa gotiana mogoteeba: Ekeene, buna Omonene are moyo. ⅩⅥ Abaisraeli babeire abaenenu buna ritororo rienenu ritari kogambereka. Inee! Omonene nanyare kobarisia buna chimanwa chi’eching’ondi chikorisigwa ase egetii ekegare? ⅩⅦ Egesaku gi’Efraimu kiabwatanire nemegwekano kegosasiima, tiga getigweo. ⅩⅧ Kobanyure batindire, nigo bakwerua kegima ase obotomani botambe; abwo bare chinguba chi’abaanto nigo banchete ay’obosooku. ⅩⅨ Ekembugu‐mbugu nkebaabuse ne chinguru chiaye, na barabwo mbasookigwe ase engencho y’ebing’wanso biabo.
