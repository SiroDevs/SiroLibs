Bilidadi
Ⅰ Bilidadi Omosua akairaneria, akabora:
Ⅱ “Obonene na okoirokia nigo bire ase Nyasae,
nigo agokora omorembe aaria igoro mono.
Ⅲ Inee! Ning’o oranyare kobara emeganda yaye?
Ning’o omobaso oye otari kobarera?
Ⅳ Naki rende omonto aranyare koba omonyaboronge ase obosio bwa Nyasae?
Naki omonto oiboire nomokuungu aranyare koba oyochenegete?
Ⅴ Rora, nonya nomotienyi tori noborabu asare,
gose ching’enang’eni tichiikeraneti ase amaiso aye.
Ⅵ Onye nabo, naki rende ase omonto oyio onga buna rikonde;
na Mwanyabaanto oyio onga buna enyongore.”
