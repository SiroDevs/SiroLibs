Ogosangererigwa kw’ebiegwa ase abataka ba Yerusalemu
Ⅰ 8:1: Abar 15:26Bono nkobamanyia tore inwe abaminto amang’ana y’obuya bwa Nyasae, obo chikanisa chia Makedonia chiaetwe. Ⅱ Naki baria bateemetwe nemechando emenge. Nonya barenge abataka mono, omogooko obo omonge okagera bakamenta okorua kwabo. Ⅲ Naki nababereire kirori ng’a nigo baruete ase omoyo bw’ogwancha, ase okonyara kwabo, na goetania okonyara kwabo. Ⅳ Barabwo bagatosaba na gotosorora mono tobae ribaga babwatane naintwe ase ogokorera abachenu. Ⅴ Naende bagakora buna totasemeretie; ritang’ani bakerua abanyene ase Omonene, erio ase tore ase ogwancha kwa Nyasae. Ⅵ Togasorora Tito aikeranie emeremo y’obuya obo buna ayechagete ase egati yaino. Ⅶ Korende buna more nobonge bw’ebinto bionsi: okwegena, na ogokwana, na obong’aini, na omokia bwonsi, na obwanchani bwaino ase tore buna tobanchete inwe, nabo mobe nobonge bw’obuya obo bwonsi.
Yeso bweruete koba omogwekano
Ⅷ Tinkwaneti aya ase okobachiika, korende ase omokia bw’abaanto bande nteeme ekeene ki’obwanchani bwaino. Ⅸ Naki mwamanyire obuya bw’Omonene oito Yeso Kristo buna ekorete omotaka ase engencho yaino, nonya orenge omonda, erinde inwe monyare koba abanda ase obotaka bwaye. Ⅹ Na inche ase ring’ana eri nabaeire ay’ogosemia kwane, mbuya ase more mokoore emeremo eyio eyio mwarenge abachaki koyekora omwaka oeta. Timware koyekora rioka, korende boigo mware nokogania gw’okoyekora. Ⅺ Bono moikeranie ogokora okwo, erinde buna mwarenge nokogania koroisia omooroberio, naboigo mobe ang’e koyoikerania ase ebi more nabio. Ⅻ Naki aase ogwancha kore, iga bigwancherwa koreng’ana nebi omonto are nabio, tari ase ebi atabwati. ⅩⅢ Naki tindi goteeba aya, erinde abande baabatigere na inwe moritoerwe, korende amang’ana abe areng’aine. ⅩⅣ Ekero eke kia bono obonge bwaino bobakonye ase okoremerwa kwabo; ekero kende obonge bwabo bobakonye inwe ase okoremerwa kwaino; erio amang’ana abe areng’aine. Esemba engencho yaye omogisangio ⅩⅤ Buna eriikire, “Oyosangereretie ebinge tamenteiri, nere oyosangereretie ebike taremeiri.”
Obotomwa bwa Tito na obw’abande
ⅩⅥ Korende Nyasae aakerwe ng’a mbuya mono, oyobekire enkoro ya Tito omokia gocha ase more. ⅩⅦ Naki igo abwatete ogosorora gwaito; kobua ayio, ere akamenta koba nomokia gocha ase more ase ogwancha kwaye omonyene. ⅩⅧ Amo nere twatomire oyominto oria omanyekanete ase chikanisa chionsi ase okorandia kwaye kw’amang’ana amaya. ⅩⅨ Gose tari ase ayio oka, korende nigo achoretwe ne chikanisa agende amo naintwe ase emeremo eye y’obuya obo togokora ase obonene bw’Omonene, na koorokia ng’a intwe nigo tore ang’e kobakorera. ⅩⅩ Twerende ne ring’ana eri, omonto tacha gotokwana bobe ase amang’ana y’ekeegwa eke tokogaba intwe. ⅩⅪ 8:21: Emeb 3:4Gatoraganie korengereria ayare amaya, tari ase obosio bw’Omonene bweka, korende nonya nase obosio bwa Mwanyabaanto. ⅩⅫ Amo nabarabwo twatomire oyominto oyotwarorire ara aange ase amang’ana amange ng’a nare nomokia, na bono nare nomokia kobua, ekiagera ase ogosemeria are nakorokwo ase more. ⅩⅩⅢ Nabo omonto karaganie komanya amang’ana igoro ya Tito; ere nesemba *8:23 nesemba: Engencho yaye omogisangio ase emeremo yane, naende nomokori emeremo amo nainche ase okobakorera inwe; naende karaganie komanya amang’ana igoro y’abaminto barabwo nabatomwa be chikanisa, naende obonene bwa Kristo. ⅩⅩⅣ Nabo moorokie ase obosio bwe chikanisa obwanchani bwaino, na ogwetogia gwaito ase engencho yaino ase bare.
