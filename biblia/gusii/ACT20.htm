Paulo ase Makedonia, na Bogiriki, na Teroa
Ⅰ Eburu‐buru eria egachia koera, Paulo akarangeria aborokigwa, akabaremia, agatigana nabarabwo, akaimoka akagenda Makedonia. Ⅱ Erio gaetire chinsemo chiria na gokwana amang’ana amange ase okobaremia, agaika Bogiriki. Ⅲ Akamenya aroro emetienyi etato. Ekiagera Abayahudi bamoboerete, konya omanoire kogenda ase enyancha gochia Siiria, agachoora koiranera goetera Makedonia. Ⅳ Abanto aba bakagenda nere: Sopater Omoberoya, mosinto o Piro, Aristariko na Sekundo, abanto ba Tesaloniki, na Gayo, omonto o Deribe, na Timotheo, na abanto b’Asia, Tukiko na Torofimo. Ⅴ Abanto aba bagatang’ana, bagatoganyera Teroa. Ⅵ Intwe tokarua Filipi n’emeeri magega y’amatuko y’Emegati etabekiri memera, tokagenda orogendo rw’amatuko atano, tokabaikeera Teroa. Tokabeera aroro amatuko atano na abere.
Eutiko
Ⅶ Ase rituko rie ritang’ani ri’echuma, ekero twasangererekanete ase ogosamunyora omogati, Paulo agakwana nabarabwo, agachiererera namang’ana aye goika botuko gati, ekiagera arengereretie komanora rituko ria kabere. Ⅷ Nchiarengeo chitaaya chinyinge ase enyomba ya irongo aase twasangererekanete. Ⅸ Omomura oyomo, erieta riaye Eutiko, oikaransete ase ekebao, akabwatwa ne chitoro chindito ekero Paulo agendererete gokwana; akabugwa ne chitoro chinyinge akagwa korwa rirongo ria gatato, akabogorigwa konya okuure. Ⅹ Korende Paulo agaika, akamoumamera, akamoagata, akabora: “Timoichana, ekiagera obogima bwaye nao bore ime yaye.” Ⅺ Paulo akariina igoro naende, agasamunyora omogati akaria, agakwana nabarabwo goika bogakia mambia. Erio akaimoka, akagenda. Ⅻ Bakarenta omomura oria, ore moyo, bakaba nokoremigwa okonene.
Orogendo gochia Mileto
ⅩⅢ Korende intwe togatang’ana kogenda n’emeeri, tokagenda goika Asosi. Tokarengereria ng’a ntonyore Paulo aria, ekiagera ere nabo achiigete. Ere omonyene nigo arengereretie kogenda namagoro. ⅩⅣ Ekero achia gotoikeera aaria Asosi, tokamosoyia ase emeeri, togaika Metilene. ⅩⅤ Tokamanora korwa agwo, ne rituko ria kabere togaika kororia Kio. Rituko ria gatato togaika Samo, tokabeera Torogilo, ne rituko ria kane togaika Mileto. ⅩⅥ Naki Paulo nigo arengereretie goeta Efeso mbarabare, tabaisa goteebana ase Asia, ekiagera nigo are koayerera kogenda Yerusalemu rituko ria Pentekosti, keranyarekane.
Paulo ogoakera abagaaka ba Efeso ng’a batigare buya
ⅩⅦ Korwa Mileto Paulo agatoma abanto kogenda Efeso barangerie abagaaka b’ekanisa. ⅩⅧ Bagachia goika asare, akabateebia.
“Inwe abanyene mwamanyire buna narenge egati yaino ekero gionsi korwa rituko rie ritang’ani nachaagete gotacha aiga Asia, ⅩⅨ buna nakorerete Omonene ase obokanyeku bwonsi, na ase amariga, na ase amateemwa nanyorete ase ogotega kw’Abayahudi. ⅩⅩ Inche tinabisete ring’ana rinde rionsi eriranyare kobakonya, korende inaborogetie inwe maiso marore enyomba ase enyomba, ⅩⅪ na ngakuurera Abayahudi na Abagiriki baonchokere Nyasae, na komwegen’Omonene oito Yeso Kristo. ⅩⅫ Gaki bono rora, nkogenda nde Yerusalemu, nasibirwe n’Omoika. Timanyeti ayarachie konyoora aaria, ⅩⅩⅢ otatiga ng’a Omoika Omochenu nigo okoorokia omochie ase omochie ng’a ogosibwa na emechando mbinganyete. ⅩⅩⅣ 20:24: 2 Tim 4:7Korende obogima bwane timbobareti ng’a negento kie rigori rinene ase ’nde, erinde nkoore orogendo rwane, na obosomba boria nanyorete ase Omonene Yeso koorokia enchiri y’amaabera a Nyasae. ⅩⅩⅤ Bono rora, namanyire ng’a timokondora obosio bwane naende, inwe mwensi aba nabeire ngotaara ase egati yaino na kobaranderia amang’ana ’oborwoti bwa Nyasae. ⅩⅩⅥ Ase ayio nabaorokeirie bonsi ase rituko ria reero, ng’a inche tindi na komocha ase amanyinga a monto onde bwensi, ⅩⅩⅦ ekiagera tinangete kobaranderia amang’ana ’okworokereria gwonsi kwa Nyasae. ⅩⅩⅧ Mwerende inwe abanyene amo ne riicho riria Omoika Omochenu obabegete mobe abarendi korisia ekanisa ya Nyasae, eye enyorerete namanyinga aye omonyene. ⅩⅩⅨ Namanyire inche ng’a magega ase okoimoka kwane chisese chi’orosana chintindi nachiche ase more, tichirorera riicho amaabera, ⅩⅩⅩ naende korwa ase egati yaino abanto mbaimoke bagokwana amang’ana ’obong’ainereria, bang’use aborokigwa babatunyane. ⅩⅩⅪ Ase ayio renda, moinyore ng’a ase emiaka etato, obotuko na omobaso, tinatigete gokuurera kera omonto ase amariga. ⅩⅩⅫ Gaki bono nababekire ase amaboko a Nyasae, na ase ring’ana ri’obuya bwaye, asare ere oyoranyaare kobaagacha na kobaa omwando amo nabwo bonsi abachenirwe. ⅩⅩⅩⅢ Tinaganetie efeta, nonya netaabu gose chianga. ⅩⅩⅩⅣ Inwe abanyene mwamanyire ng’a amaboko aya aane ankoreire okorigia kwane, na okw’abwo barenge amo nainche. ⅩⅩⅩⅤ Ase amang’ana aya onsi nabaorokeirie ng’a ase ogokora emeremo iga, nebagwenerete mokonye abatari ne chinguru, na koinyora amang’ana ’Omonene Yeso, buna akwanete omonyene: Nasesenirie oyokorua kobua oyokonyoora.”
ⅩⅩⅩⅥ Gakoorire gokwana aya agatung’ama inse, agasaba amo nabarabwo bonsi. ⅩⅩⅩⅦ Na bonsi bakaiyeria amariga, bakaboania Paulo rigoti riaye, bakamonyunyunta. ⅩⅩⅩⅧ Bagaichana kobua ase engencho ye ring’ana riaye, ateebete ng’a: “Timokondora obosio bwane naende.” Bakamokooba goika ase emeeri.
