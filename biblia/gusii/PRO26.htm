ⅩⅩⅥ
Ⅰ Buna koreng’ana obokendu bogocha ase engaki y’egurugura, gose embura egotwa ase engaki ye rigesa, naboigo ere bobe omonto omoriri konyora amasikani.
Ⅱ Buna enchiro ekona‐koong’anya, na buna egesingororo gekoiruruka, naboigo na okoragereria gotari nengeencho gotaikere oria oragererigwe.
Ⅲ Ekeranya nigo kere ase ogoaka ebarasi, na ekeokio ase okoraa etigere, naboigo n’enyimbo ere ase ogoaka omogongo bw’omonto omoriri.
Ⅳ Tobaisa koiraneria omonto omoriri buna oboriri bwaye bore, nigo orabaise koba omoriri buna are.
Ⅴ Moiranerie omonto omoriri buna oboriri bwaye bore, tabaisa gwekaga ng’a nomong’aini are.
Ⅵ Omonto ogotoma amang’ana ase okoboko kw’omonto omoriri, oyio nigo akobutora amaboko aye omonyene bwerentera emechando.
Ⅶ Omoreng’anio ogokwanwa n’omonwa bw’omoriri nigo onga buna omonto egesegora, okogoro kwaye gotari gokora emeremo.
Ⅷ Omonto okoa omoriri amasikani, nigo anga buna oyokoruta omobuko ore n’ebinto bi’erigori rinene ase etui y’amagena.
Ⅸ Omoreng’anio okorwa ase omonwa bw’omonto omoriri nigo onga buna rigwa rikobeta okoboko kw’omonto otindire amarwa.
Ⅹ Omonto omanyete emeremo nigo agwekorera emeremo emiya, korende oyore omoriri nigo akoriika onde bwensi ogoeta, na emeremo yasareka.
Ⅺ 26:11: 2 Petr 2:22Omonto omoriri okoiranerera gokora oboriri bwaye nigo anga esese ekoiranera amaroki aye enyene.
Ⅻ Oise korora omonto ogwekora omong’aini, ogosemeria ’nkore ase omonto omoriri kobua ase oyio.
ⅩⅢ Omonto omworo nigo agoteeba, “Endo nere isiko, ee, endo nere ase chiinchera chi’omochie.”
ⅩⅣ Omonto omworo nigo akonakweoncha‐onchora buna egesieri gekona‐koonchorwa ase chimbata gesibeire.
ⅩⅤ Omonto omworo nigo akobeeka okoboko kwaye ase esani y’endagera, orosa gokobogoria gocha ase omonwa oye.
ⅩⅥ Omonto omworo nigo akwerora ng’a n’omong’aini are abuete abanto batano na babere barakwane amang’ana are n’engeencho.
ⅩⅦ Omonto orenge goeta na gwesoyia ase eriomana ritarenge eriaye nigo anga buna oyo okobwata amato y’esese.
ⅩⅧ-ⅩⅨ Omonto okong’aina omonto onde, na magega yaye omoteebia, “Negosori yoka nare kogokorera,” oyio nigo anga buna ebarimo ekoruta ebigenga bi’omorero, ne chinsara, chia amakweri.
ⅩⅩ Ase chinko chitari, omorero nigo okorima, naboigo nase omogenki atari, eriomana nigo rigosira.
ⅩⅪ Omonyariomana ogoseegeta amariomana nigo anga buna amakara akogeenderera koyia, gose chinko chikogera omorero ogeenderera gwoka.
ⅩⅫ Amang’ana omonto omoitania nigo are buna endagera enyansu egotirimboka gochia ase enda inse.
ⅩⅩⅢ Omonto obwate amang’ana amansu ase ebikoba biaye, korende ore nobobe ase enkoro yaye, nigo anga buna egekorero ki’amaroba giatekire keoomire nefeta.
ⅩⅩⅣ Omonto omogechi nigo akobisa ase ebikoba biaye, obong’ainereria bore ase enkoro yaye. ⅩⅩⅤ Ekero agokwana amang’ana are amansu, tobaisa komwegena, ekiagera nabwate ay’okogechia atano na abere, ase enkoro yaye ime. ⅩⅩⅥ Naisa gotuba okogecha kwaye nobong’ainereria, obokayayu bwaye goika bomanyekane n’omosangererekano bwonsi.
ⅩⅩⅦ Omonto okorema eirima, nere omonyene oragwe imeo; na omonto ogoturunkania rigena, nere omonyene rirairanere rimogwere.
ⅩⅩⅧ Omonto ore noromeme rogokwana oborimo, nigo akogecha abanto abwo amang’ana aye achaandire bobe, na omonwa ogokwana amang’ana ay’okweansia nigo okorenta ogosirigwa.
