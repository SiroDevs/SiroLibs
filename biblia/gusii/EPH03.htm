Obotomwa bwa Paulo ase abanto b’Ebisaku
Ⅰ Inaki ase ayio inche Paulo nabeire omosibwa o Kristo Yeso ase engencho yaino, inwe abanto b’Ebisaku. Ⅱ Ase ekeene mwaigure amang’ana y’oborendi bw’obuya bwa Nyasae, obo naetwe ase engencho yaino. Ⅲ Ase okomaanorerwa nateebetigwe obobisi boria buna nabariikera reero iga ase amang’ana amake. Ⅳ 3:4: Abakol 1:26,27Ase ayio, ekero morayasome, inamonyaare korora okomanya kw’ane kw’obobisi bwa Kristo. Ⅴ Obobisi obo Nyasae tamanyetie Mwanyabaanto ase ebigori binde buna bono ase Omoika abaigoreire abatomwa baye abachenu na ababani. Ⅵ Obobisi boria nobo, ng’a abanto b’Ebisaku babeire abanyamwando amo naintwe, ebiimo bi’omobere oyomo, na ababwatani amo naintwe ase eira ere ase Kristo Yeso ase enchera y’enchiri.
Ⅶ Nabo ’ngakorwa koba omosomba bw’enchiri eye koreng’ana nekeegwa ki’obuya bwa Nyasae obo naetwe ase ogokora gw’okobua kwaye. Ⅷ Inche inde omoke kobua abachenu bonsi; nonya nabo ’nkaegwa obuya bw’okoranderia abanto b’Ebisaku obonda bwa Kristo, obo botari korengeka. Ⅸ Nkaegwa kobaorokereria abanto bonsi barore omooroberio bw’obobisi boria bwabisetwe korwa kare ase Nyasae, oyotongete ebinto bionsi; Ⅹ erinde bono, ase enchera y’ekanisa, obong’aini bwa Nyasae, obore ne chingeencho chinyinge, bomanyekane n’abagaambi na abare nokobua kwa igoro. Ⅺ Nabo kwareenge okorigia kwaye gwa kare na kare, okwo aikeranetie ase Kristo Yeso, Omonene oito. Ⅻ Asere ntore noboremu na ogosemeria kw’ogosoa ase enchera y’okomwegena. ⅩⅢ Gaki nabasabire, timokwa emioyo ase engencho y’ogochaandwa kw’ane inde nakwo ase engencho yaino, okore obonene bwaino.
Obwanchani bwa Kristo
ⅩⅣ Ase ayio namobekeire amaru aane inse Tata, ⅩⅤ ekiagera bonsi abakorwa ase Tata, abare igoro na abare ase ense, nigo bakorokwa ase erieta riaye. ⅩⅥ Nasabire ng’a koreng’ana nobonda bw’obonene bwaye, abanchere momentwe chinguru ase okobua goeteera ase Omoika oye ase omonto o ime; ⅩⅦ na Kristo amenye ase chinkoro chiaino komwamwegenire. Nasabire ng’a inwe komor’abe n’emeri na oboroso ase obwanchani, ⅩⅧ monyaare koba ne chinguru ase okomanya amo nabachenu bonsi obogare na obotambe, na gochia igoro, na gochia inse, ⅩⅨ na komanya obwanchani bwa Kristo oboetete okomanya, erinde monyaare goichorigwa ogoichorwa gwonsi kwa Nyasae.
ⅩⅩ Gaki, ase are ere oyoranyaare gokora goetania mono kobua aya togosaba gose aya tokorengereria, koreng’ana ne chinguru chigokora emeremo ime ase tore, ⅩⅪ ase are ere obonene ase ekanisa na ase Kristo Yeso goika ebigori bionsi kare na kare. Amina.
